# 中国白酒国际化

## 核心定义

The globalization of Chinese Baijiu faces high cultural and taste barriers, with Moutai as the primary vehicle for brand export.

## 新增事实

- The primary barrier to Moutai's global expansion is the high alcohol content and complex flavor profile (sauce aroma, charred aroma) which differs significantly from Western spirits.
- Moutai is developing derivative products such as Moutai ice cream and coffee to lower the entry threshold for international consumers.

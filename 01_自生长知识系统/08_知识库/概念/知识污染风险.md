# 知识污染风险

## 核心定义

The risk of incorrect associations between unrelated entities (e.g., AI news and educational events) leading to erroneous conclusions in automated knowledge systems.

## 新增事实

- Forcing association of unrelated data creates 'non-causal association' hallucinations that pollute downstream reasoning models.

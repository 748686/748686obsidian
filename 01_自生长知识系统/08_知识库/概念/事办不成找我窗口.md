# 事办不成找我窗口

## 核心定义

河北省邯郸市邱县政务服务大厅设立的一种专门处理跨部门、协调难度高、疑难复杂诉求的服务机制，由县纪委监委全程监督。

## 新增事实

- 该机制采用分级办理体系：一般事项由乡镇街道处理，跨部门复杂事项进入县级协调。
- 服务时限规定：当日事当日毕，多部门联动事项3日内办结，长期复杂事项7日内响应。
- 截至2026年11月报道时，该窗口累计受理并办结民生及企业类诉求超过3200件，办结率达99.8%。
- 该机制通过柔性执法（如前置预审、指导整改而非强制拆除）降低制度性交易成本。

### 本次变化

An administrative innovation in Qiu County, Hebei, where a dedicated government service window accepts and resolves complex or unresolved appeals from citizens and enterprises, supervised by the Commission for Discipline Inspection.

### 新增事实

- Qiu County established a '事办不成找我' (If things cannot be done, find me) window in its government service center.
- The mechanism is supervised by the County Commission for Discipline Inspection throughout the process of collection, routing, handling, and feedback.
- Timeline commitments: same-day for simple matters, 3 days for cross-departmental issues, and 7 days for complex historical issues.
- As of November 2026, the mechanism has resolved over 3,200 cases with a 99.8% closure rate.

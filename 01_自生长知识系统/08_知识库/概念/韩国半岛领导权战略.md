# 韩国半岛领导权战略

## 核心定义

South Korean President Yoon Suk-yeol's national strategy initiative to play a leading role in peninsula issues, relying on US alliance, economic/tech strength, and democratic values, while facing weaknesses in military deterrence against North Korea and economic dependence on China.

## 新增事实

- The strategy aims to position South Korea as a 'middle power' diplomatic model through US-ROK-Japan trilateral cooperation.
- Key strengths include US military protection umbrella, leadership in semiconductors/electronics/shipping, and democratic value output.
- Key weaknesses include geographic vulnerability of the capital region to North Korea and heavy economic dependence on China as its largest trade partner.

# Tajik Wedding Customs (塔吉克族婚俗)

## 核心定义

Unique customs observed in the wedding of Xiao Gongni Longji, including the Eagle Dance, the requirement for the bride to prepare 12 sets of wedding clothes, no gift money collected, and the tradition of the bride wearing red wedding attire for a full year after marriage.

## 新增事实

- Bride must prepare 12 sets of wedding clothes.
- No gift money is collected at weddings.
- Bride wears red wedding attire for one full year after marriage.
- Eagle Dance mimics the movements of an eagle (single wing, double wings, shoulder shaking).

# 数据管道源-事件映射完整性

## 核心定义

A systemic risk in the knowledge acquisition process where source articles are incorrectly mapped to event units. This specific instance demonstrates a 100% mismatch where unrelated content (domestic US security/9-11) was linked to a geopolitical event (Russia/Ukraine), rendering the event unverifiable and highlighting the need for strict cross-validation and source-content relevance checks before ingestion.

## 新增事实

- Event EVT-20260912-000213 was marked UNVERIFIABLE because the single source ARTICLE #284 was completely unrelated to the event title.
- Source ARTICLE #284 was in 'unresolved' and 'horizon_summary_only' status, meaning no full text was available for review.
- The system's internal rules prohibit treating unresolved summaries as reviewed original text for fact-checking purposes.

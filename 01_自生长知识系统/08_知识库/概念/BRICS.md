# BRICS

## 核心定义

BRICS cooperation is entering its third 'Golden Decade' following the 20th anniversary in 2026. China will assume the chairmanship in 2027, marking its fourth term. The coalition is expanding from economic trade to 'Big BRICS' models involving digital infrastructure, AI, and green energy.

## 新增事实

- China will host the 19th BRICS summit in 2027.
- China is assuming the BRICS Chairmanship for the fourth time.
- The 20th anniversary of BRICS cooperation occurred in 2026.

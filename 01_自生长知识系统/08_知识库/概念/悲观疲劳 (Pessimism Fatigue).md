# 悲观疲劳 (Pessimism Fatigue)

## 核心定义

A psychological state characterized by emotional desensitization, mental exhaustion, and a sense of learned helplessness resulting from prolonged or repeated exposure to disaster news and crises. It poses a risk to social resilience by dulling public reaction to subsequent threats.

## 新增事实

- Pessimism Fatigue is defined as a state of emotional numbness and burnout caused by long-term exposure to negative news and crises.
- Potential risks include weakened community response efficiency in future disasters and erosion of social cohesion.

# 算电协同 (Compute-Power Synergy)

## 核心定义

A policy concept defined by the State Council as a fundamental support for AI development, involving the integration of computing power networks with energy systems, including green power direct connection and 'source-grid-load-storage' projects.

## 新增事实

- Defined by the 2026-09-11 State Council Executive Meeting as the 'fundamental support' (根本支撑) for AI development.
- Involves building a multi-layered connected computing power system.
- Specific measures include strengthening trunk fiber optic network construction, promoting green power direct connection, and implementing 'source-grid-load-storage' (源网荷储) projects.
- Emphasizes the fusion of computing power and network (算力-网络融合).

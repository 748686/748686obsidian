# 曼德海峡航运安全风险评估框架

## 核心定义

A strategic assessment framework for the Bab el-Mandeb Strait involving Houthi asymmetric capabilities, three potential future scenarios (Deterrence Equilibrium, Status Quo Escalation, Total Chokepoint Closure), and associated monitoring indicators such as transit frequency and insurance premiums.

## 新增事实

- The framework defines three scenarios: Deterrence Equilibrium (15-20% cost increase, strait open), Status Quo Escalation (10-14 day delay via Cape of Good Hope), and Total Chokepoint Closure (>30 day closure, IEA emergency release).
- Key monitoring indicators include a 30% drop in daily transit frequency and a doubling of insurance premium rates as trigger signals.

# Kadeer Abulikemu (卡德尔·阿布都克力木)

## 核心定义

Grandfather of Xiao Gongni Longji. A veteran who served in 1951, wounded but continued fighting, awarded First Class Merit. He is considered a prototype for the character 'Amir' in the film 'Sender on the Ice Mountain'. He carved the family motto 'Follow the Party, Guard the Border, Love the Country' on a boulder at 4700m altitude.

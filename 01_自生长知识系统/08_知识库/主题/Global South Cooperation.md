# Global South Cooperation

## 核心定义

Global South nations are leveraging BRICS platforms to push for greater voice in international financial systems and cooperation in digital and green technologies, viewing China's 2027 chairmanship as a key opportunity for deepening these ties.

## 新增事实

- Observations from Russia, Malaysia, Egypt, and other nations highlight a shared desire to enhance Global South discourse in financial rules.
- Focus on trade and investment facilitation remains central, alongside new domains like AI and clean energy.

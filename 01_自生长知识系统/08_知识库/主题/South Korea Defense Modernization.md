# South Korea Defense Modernization

## 核心定义

South Korea is pursuing strategic autonomy through the Operational Control (OPCON) transfer and domestic defense industry development, which serves as a buffer against alliance volatility.

## 新增事实

- Concurrent efforts to complete OPCON transfer are underway.
- Advanced anti-drone systems have been unveiled, reflecting a growing self-reliant defense industrial base.

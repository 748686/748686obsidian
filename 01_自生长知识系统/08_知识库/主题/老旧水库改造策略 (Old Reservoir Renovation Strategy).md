# 老旧水库改造策略 (Old Reservoir Renovation Strategy)

## 核心定义

A policy approach to upgrading aging water infrastructure using 'one reservoir, one policy' (一库一策) customized renovation plans, combining short-term governance with long-term planning to improve flood control and water management.

## 新增事实

- The strategy employs 'one reservoir, one policy' (一库一策) for customized renovation.
- It combines short-term and long-term measures ('long-short combined', 'symptomatic and root-cause treatment').
- The goal is to enhance the safety capacity of old reservoirs and strengthen flood control functions.

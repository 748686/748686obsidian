# 数据管道异常处理策略

## 核心定义

A decision-making framework for handling source-event mismatches, prioritizing isolation and reclassification over forced association to maintain knowledge graph purity.

## 新增事实

- For source-event mismatches, 'Isolate & Reclassify' is recommended over 'Force Association' to prevent logical fallacies in the knowledge graph.
- Isolated source data should be preserved and remapped to correct categories rather than discarded, to maximize data utility.
- Events with mismatched data must be marked with specific tags (e.g., #Data_Mismatch, #Pending_Split) to trigger manual or automated correction workflows.

## Core Facts

### 1. Operational Performance of Hua Dian Xinjiang (Source: Article #386)
*   **Party Building Integration:** Since 2016, the company has utilized party building as a key driver for reform and development. It has established 120 grassroots party branches and mobilized over 2,800 party members.
*   **Project Milestones:**
    *   **Bazhou Hybrid Storage + Wind Power Project:** Overcame high chloride ion corrosion in salt-alkali swamps using "high anti-sulfate concrete + silane impregnation anticorrosion coating" technology.
    *   **Tianshan North Foot戈壁 (Gobi) Energy Base:** The first coal-fired power unit was completed and commissioned in just 23 months, setting a record for the shortest construction period for similar units domestically.
*   **Installed Capacity (as of June 2026):**
    *   Total installed capacity exceeded **43 million kilowatts**.
    *   New energy installed capacity accounts for **61.1%** of the total.
    *   The company developed national large wind/solar bases (batches 1–3) and power supply projects supporting "Xinjiang Electricity to Chongqing."
*   **Coal Power Flexibility:** Completed flexibility transformations for **41 coal-fired units**, accounting for nearly 50% of the region's total units. This represents the largest number of transformed units, highest investment, and greatest peak-shaving capacity among regional companies in China.
*   **Energy Supply Security:** During winter peaks and major holidays, 49 units generated full output. 18 heating enterprises provided heat to **2.52 million households** across 10 prefectures and 21 counties/districts.

### 2. Social Responsibility and Poverty Alleviation (Source: Article #386)
*   **Funding:** Cumulative assistance investment in Xinjiang reached **509 million RMB**.
*   **Personnel:** Dispatched **176** assistance cadres.
*   **Employment Impact:** Driven employment for **149,000 person-times**, creating 4,102 new job positions.
*   **Infrastructure:** Constructed "Hua Dian Golden Sun" distributed photovoltaic systems in **123 villages** to address clean and low-cost energy needs in Atush, Wuqia, and Habax counties.
*   **Public Services:** Built or supported **23 schools and health centers**. The Atash Hua Dian No. 1 Middle School opened in **August 2026**, completing a education assistance chain from preschool to junior high.
*   **Brand Development:** Supported local特色 (characteristic) industries, creating brands such as "Qionging Animal Husbandry" and "Haidekun," with consumer assistance amounts exceeding **150 million RMB**.
*   **Awards:** China Huadian Corporation received the highest rating ("Good") for centralized poverty alleviation assessment for **7 consecutive years**. Two assisted villages received national titles for "One Village, One Product" and rural governance demonstration.

### 3. Theoretical Framework: Modern Industrial System (Source: Article #388)
*   **Definition:** A modern industrial system is the material and technical foundation of Chinese modernization, composed of interconnected modern industries.
*   **Guangdong Case Study (2025 Data):**
    *   **GDP:** 14.58 trillion RMB (ranked 1st nationally for 37 consecutive years).
    *   **Manufacturing:** Advanced manufacturing and high-tech manufacturing accounted for **56.2%** and **34.7%** of above-scale industrial value-added, respectively.
    *   **Services:** Service sector value-added was 8.5 trillion RMB (58.3% of GDP). Modern services accounted for **64.9%** of service sector value-added.
    *   **Digital Economy:** Ranked 1st nationally in digital economy scale, industrial robot production, and service robot production.
*   **Policy Recommendations:** The article advocates for "chain master" systems, "揭榜挂帅" (open competition for selecting the best candidates) for bottleneck technologies, and the integration of innovation chains, industrial chains, capital chains, and talent chains.

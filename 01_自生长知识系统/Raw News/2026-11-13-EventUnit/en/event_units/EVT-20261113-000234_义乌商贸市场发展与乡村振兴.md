# Event E-20261113-000234: Yiwu Commercial Market Development and Rural Revitalization

## Event Overview

On November 13, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a coordinated editorial sequence spanning pages 07 and 08, covering two distinct but thematically linked macroeconomic narratives: the continued evolution of Yiwu's small commodity market into a digital-global hub, and the "15th Five-Year Plan" outlook for rural revitalization and agricultural modernization.

The event synthesis combines reporting on Yiwu's "small commodities, large market" prosperity model with expert commentary from the "China Economic Roundtable" on agricultural security, technological innovation, and rural governance.

## Core Facts

### I. Yiwu Commercial Market Development (Source: Article #382)
- **Market Scale:** Yiwu Global Small Commodity Market now averages ~300,000 daily visitors, with over 80,000 physical shops supporting 32 million jobs, supplying 233 countries and regions.
- **Trade Volume Metrics (2026 Jan–Oct):**
  - 63 trade promotion events held.
  - 1.055 million global purchasers attracted.
- **32nd China Yiwu International Small Commodities Fair (Late Oct 2026):**
  - 2,770 exhibiting enterprises.
  - 193,600 professional purchasers attended.
  - 14,700 overseas purchasers (+23.37% YoY).
- **Foreign Resident Population:** >38,000常驻 foreigners; >680,000 overseas businesspeople regularly engage in cross-border trade.
- **Digital Transformation:**
  - Yiwu Global Digital Trade Center (6th-generation market) opened October 2025.
  - Deployed "World Yiwu" trade large model and 25 digital applications (AI design, AI visual creation).
  - Cumulative platform visits >1 billion.
  - Chinagoods platform: 6.08 million registered purchasers, serving 60,000 operators.
- **Demographic Shift:** In the Digital Trade Center's 5,200+ stalls, 52% are operated by "second-generation" or "new-generation" entrepreneurs; 70% can independently design/manage IPs.
- **Brand Export:** Since 2023, 105 brand出海 projects deployed across 40 countries; annual export value driven by this initiative exceeds RMB 18.1 billion.
- **Logistics Infrastructure ("Four Ports Linkage"):**
  - China-Europe Freight Train (Yixinou) remains a stable overland trade link.
  - Yiwu-Ningbo Zhoushan Port maritime-rail联运: >1 million TEUs in 2025.
  - Average parcel dispatch: 26,000 items/minute globally.
- **Regulatory Innovation:** "Inspect before loading" model improved export efficiency by 30%, adopted nationally as a pilot replication experience.
- **Policy Burden:** 111 national-level and 216 provincial-level innovation pilot tasks undertaken by 2025.
- **Future Target:** Import-export volume aimed to reach RMB 1 trillion by 2030.
- **Industrial Growth:** Q1–Q3 2026规模以上工业增加值 +10.3% YoY.

### II. Rural Revitalization & Agricultural Modernization (Source: Article #384)
- **Policy Context:** "15th Five-Year Plan" opening year; Xi Jinping emphasized agricultural/rural modernization as the foundation of Chinese modernization.
- **Roundtable Participants:**
  - Zhao Changbao (Director, Rural Economic Research Center, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs).
  - Zhu Huasheng (Deputy Director, Heilongjiang Provincial Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs).
  - Wang Dongqing (Deputy Chief Engineer, First Tractor Co., Ltd.).
  - Hu Aiguo (Party Secretary, Dongjiao Village, Zaoyang City, Hubei Province).
- **Grain Security Status:**
  - Autumn harvest >90% complete; nationwide grain output expected to remain above 1.4 trillion jin for the 3rd consecutive year.
  - Heilongjiang grain output maintained >160 billion jin for two consecutive years; 2026 expected to set a new historical high.
  - Heilongjiang high-standard farmland: 125 million mu; mechanization rate: 99.34%.
  - Jiangxi Jishui County farmer Liu Song: 1,200 jin/mu late rice yield.
  - Hubei Dongjiao Village: Superior rice varieties yielding >1,500 jin/mu.
- **Technological Adoption:**
  - Rural internet penetration rate: 69.5%.
  - Agricultural production informatization rate growing ~2 percentage points annually.
  - China First Tractor released an intelligent tractor onboard operator agent at the 2026 China International Agricultural Machinery Exhibition (Tianjin, Oct 2026).
- **Rural Governance Innovation:** Dongjiao Village shifted to courtyard/field-level deliberation assemblies, improving citizen participation and mutual aid norms.
- **Industrial Integration Examples:**
  - Hubei Honghu: Lotus root as a富民产业.
  - Henan Yuzhou Dajian Village: Sweet potato deep processing (hot and sour noodles, rice sheets) sold via livestreaming.
  - Heilongjiang "Black Soil Excellent Products" provincial brand integrating定制 agriculture with cultural tourism.

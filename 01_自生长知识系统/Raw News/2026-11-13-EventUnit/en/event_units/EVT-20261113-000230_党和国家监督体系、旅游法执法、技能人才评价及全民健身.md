- **Source:** Article #374, reported by Deng Bo (Nov 12, 2026).

### 2. Release of *Skills Talent Evaluation Regulations (Draft for Comments)*
- **Issuing Body:** Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS)
- **Status:** Draft for Comments (征求意见稿)
- **Scope:** The draft comprehensively regulates skills talent evaluation activities, covering evaluation standards, institution management, exam affairs norms, certificate management, and协同监管 (coordinated supervision).
- **Objectives:**
  - Address structural contradictions between the supply and demand of high-skilled talents and industrial upgrade needs.
  - Standardize evaluation behaviors and establish unified national standards.
  - Resolve issues such as uneven quality of evaluation institutions, inconsistent credibility of certificates, and illegal subsidy fraud.
- **Key Provisions:**
  - Clarifies legal liabilities for evaluation institutions engaging in illegal evaluations.
  - Prohibits entities and individuals from conducting评价 activities under the guise of vocational qualification evaluation, skills level certification, or special vocational capacity assessment without filing.
  - Forbids forgery, alteration, or buying/selling of vocational qualification certificates, skills level certificates, and special vocational capacity certificates.
  - Establishes a协同监管 mechanism involving the MOHRSS, Cyberspace Administration, Public Security, and Market Regulation departments to combat cross-sectoral illegal acts such as false advertising and online certificate sales.
  - Supports industry associations, chambers of commerce, enterprises, and vocational schools in participating in the development of national occupational standards.
- **Expert Commentary:**
  - Cheng Xiezhong (China University of Political Science and Law): Legislation fills gaps in the legal system and supports building a first-class industrial technical workforce.
  - Li Fucheng (Beijing University of Science and Technology): Highlights the need to prevent the commercialization of talent evaluation through misleading宣传.
  - Chen Yujie (Chinese Academy of Labor and Social Security): Notes the协同监管 mechanism addresses the challenge of long regulatory chains.
  - Fan Wei (Chinese Academy of Personnel Sciences): Emphasizes the integration of diverse evaluation norms into a unified occupational standard system.
- **Source:** Article #375, reported by Qiu Chaoyi.

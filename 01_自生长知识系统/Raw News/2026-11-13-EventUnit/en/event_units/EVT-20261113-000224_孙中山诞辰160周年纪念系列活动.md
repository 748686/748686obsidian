# Event Name

Commemorative Activities for the 160th Anniversary of the Birth of Dr. Sun Yat-sen (孙中山诞辰160周年纪念系列活动)

## Event Overview

On November 12, 2026, a series of commemorative activities were held across mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Macao to mark the 160th anniversary of the birth of Dr. Sun Yat-sen (born November 12, 1866). The events centered on a major ceremony in Beijing, with parallel commemorations in Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Macao, reflecting the pan-Chinese nature of the occasion. The central theme emphasized Sun Yat-sen’s legacy as a national hero, patriot, and pioneer of the democratic revolution, linking his historical contributions to contemporary goals of national rejuvenation, modernization, and reunification.

## Core Facts

- **Date of Event:** November 12, 2026 (commemorating the birth date of Sun Yat-sen, November 12, 1866).
- **Main Ceremony:** A high-level commemorative conference was held in Beijing on November 12, 2026.
- **Key Speaker:** Xi Jinping delivered a major speech at the Beijing conference.
- **Other Key Participants in Beijing:** Zheng Jianbang (Vice Chairperson of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress and Chairman of the Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang) presided over the wreath-laying ceremony at the衣冠冢 (ceremonial tomb/memorial site) at Xiangshan碧云寺 (Biyun Temple in Fragrant Hills), Beijing.
- **Hong Kong Ceremony:** Held on November 12, 2026, at Sun Yat-sen Memorial Park. Li Jiazhao, Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (SAR), delivered a speech. Representatives from the Central Government Liaison Office, the National Security Corps, the Foreign Affairs Office, and the PLA garrison attended.
- **Taiwan Commemorations:** Multiple events were held across Taiwan, including at the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall in Taipei and in Taichung. Participants included representatives from the KMT (Kuomintang), New Party, and various civic groups.
- **Macao Exhibition:** The Macao SAR government hosted an image exhibition titled "Departing from Macao" (从澳门出发) at the Macao Cultural Centre, running from November 12 to November 27, 2026. Cen Hao hui, Chief Executive of the Macao SAR, delivered a致辞.
- **Philatelic Issue:** On November 12, 2026, China Post issued a miniature sheet commemorative stamp titled "Commemorating the 160th Anniversary of the Birth of Dr. Sun Yat-sen."
- **Historical Context:** Sun Yat-sen died in Beijing on March 12, 1925. His coffin was temporarily placed at Biyun Temple in the Xiangshan (Fragrant Hills) area. In 1929, his remains were moved to the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum in Nanjing. The clothes and hat he was buried in were placed in a楠木 (nanmu wood) coffin and sealed in the金刚宝座塔 (Vajra Throne Pagoda) behind the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall in Nanjing. The Beijing ceremony at Biyun Temple is a memorial rite for this衣冠冢.

## Unique Information by Source

**Article #343 (Xi Jinping's Speech):**
- Detailed evaluation of Sun Yat-sen's contributions: "great national hero, great patriot, great pioneer of China's democratic revolution."
- Six key spiritual legacies to inherit: patriotism, people-first orientation, pursuit of truth, perseverance, unity, and global vision.
- Explicit linkage of Sun's "Revitalize China" (振兴中华) slogan to the contemporary goal of Chinese modernization and national rejuvenation.
- Strong emphasis on national reunification: "Taiwan's future lies in national reunification," "We will never leave any space for any form of 'Taiwan independence' separatist activities."
- Reference to Sun's 《建国方略》 (State-Building Program) and how its blueprints (railways, roads, ports, etc.) have been realized and exceeded.
- Characterization of the Communist Party of China as "the most steadfast supporter, most loyal collaborator, and most faithful inheritor" of Sun's revolutionary cause.

**Article #357 (Beijing Wreath-Laying Ceremony):**
- Specific procedural details: two sessions of the ceremony; first session presided by Zheng Jianbang, second session for Taiwan representatives.
- Three-minute silence and three bows before Sun Yat-sen's statue.
- Attendees: He Baoxiang (Vice Chairperson of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference and Executive Vice Chairman of the RCCK), representatives from the CPPCC General Office, United Front Work Department, State Council Taiwan Affairs Office, RCCK, Beijing Municipal Government, Sun's relatives, overseas Chinese, and international friends.
- Historical detail: The nanmu coffin containing Sun's clothes and hat is sealed in the Vajra Throne Pagoda behind the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall in Nanjing.

**Article #358 (Commemorative Stamp):**
- Technical details: oil painting style, six-color offset printing, hot gold stamping, surface enhancement process.
- Border inscription: Sun Yat-sen's handwritten phrase "天下为公" (The World Belongs to All).
- Distribution channels: designated postal outlets, Philately Network Hall, China Post mobile app, WeChat mini-programs.

**Article #359 (Hong Kong Ceremony):**
- Location: Sun Yat-sen Memorial Park, Hong Kong.

- Attendees: Zhou Ji (Director of the Central Government Liaison Office to HK), Dong Jingwei (Director of the National Security Corps), Cui Jianchun (Commissioner of the Foreign Affairs Office), Tan Zhiwei (Deputy Commander of the PLA Garrison Force), principal officials, members of the Executive and Legislative Councils, NPC and CPPCC delegates, youth and student representatives.

**Article #360 (Taiwan Commemorations):**
- Specific venues: Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall (Taipei, Ren'ai Road) and Human · Taichung Cultural Exchange Garden.

- Gao Yongguang (former Dean of the Graduate Institute of Social Science at National Taiwan University) noted that Sun visited Taiwan three times and helped establish the Tongmenghui Taiwan Branch.
- Lin Guangrui (founder of the Association of Relatives of Taiwanese Anti-Japanese Patriots) presented on Taiwan precursor Lin Zumi's revolutionary activities under Sun.
- Citizen testimonials emphasized Sun's relevance to cross-strait unity and contemporary learning.

**Article #361 (Macao Exhibition):**

- Cen Hao hui's characterization of Macao as Sun's "important starting point to the world," "first stop for saving the people," "birthplace of republican planning," "harbor for seeking refuge," and "spiritual home for family ties."
- Exhibition runs through November 27, 2026.

## Event Overview

On November 13, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Renmin Ribao) published a coordinated block of content across its editorial and policy sections. The event encompasses three primary components published on the same day:
1.  A theoretical article in the Editorial Department section analyzing the precision and effectiveness of recent fiscal and tax policies, specifically focusing on the reduction of the VAT export rebate rate for new energy vehicles (NEVs).
2.  A formal call for submissions ("Welcome Contributions") for various columns in the editorial section.
3.  A major theoretical piece in the News section regarding the improvement of the Party and state supervision system, linked to the spirit of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee.

The synthesis focuses on the policy analysis and editorial administrative events as defined by the merge reason.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Publication:** November 13, 2026.
*   **Source Publication:** *People's Daily* (People's Daily).
*   **Policy Action:** The Ministry of Finance and the State Taxation Administration announced that effective April 1, 2027, the VAT export rebate rate for new energy vehicles will be reduced from 13% to 9%.
*   **Policy Rationale:** The article argues this adjustment guides enterprises to rely more on technology, quality, and service to expand overseas markets, thereby consolidating the competitive advantages of China's automotive industry.
*   **Contextual Policy History:** The article notes that NEV purchase subsidies were phased out at the end of 2022. Following this, policy focused on addressing infrastructure shortcomings, such as supporting pilot projects for county-level charging and swapping facilities in rural areas.
*   **Editorial Administration:** The editor-in-chief team for the editorial page (05 Edition) consists of Li Zheng, Chang Jin, and Yu Xuan.
*   **Submission Channels:** The "Welcome Contributions" notice invites submissions for the columns "General Discussion," "Warm News Hot Comments," "Chinese Path Chinese Dream," and "Letters to the Editor." Emails should be sent to `rmrbpl@peopledaily.cn` with the column name specified.

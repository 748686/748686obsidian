# Event: People's Daily Commentary Edition AI Skills Talent Discussion and Related Thematic Articles

## Event Overview

On November 13, 2026, the commentary section (Page 05) of the *People's Daily* published a collection of articles centered on development, education, and policy. The first-layer synthesis merged these items based on their simultaneous publication in the same daily commentary section. While the primary narrative focuses on the role of skilled laborers in the AI era and the historical significance of serving the people in the Lingnan revolution base area, the edition also featured articles on science education in ethnic regions and agricultural data regarding the "blue granary."

## Core Facts

**1. AI and Skilled Talent Discourse**
*   The People's Daily published a "Connected Commentator" interview titled "What Should Skilled Talents Do in the AI Era?" (Article #367).
*   The article discusses the definition and role of skilled talents as artificial intelligence accelerates, framing it within the context of national development needs.

**2. Historical Reflection on "Lingnan Spirit" and Public Service**
*   A "Live Comment" piece titled "Serving the People's Well-being is the Eternal落脚点 (Foothold/Final Goal)" was published (Article #367 content).
*   Author: Wang Jintao (Reporter for the Gansu Branch).
*   Location focus: Nanliang Town, Huachi County, Qingyang City, Gansu Province.
*   Historical context: The Nanliang area served as a critical foothold for the Long March and the site of the Shaan-Gan Border Revolution Base. The article cites Edgar Snow’s conclusion that the Communist Party survived because it represented the interests of the poor.
*   Current application: The article connects historical dedication to modern efforts in Qingyang, highlighting its role in the national "East Data West Computing" project.

**3. Qingyang’s Digital Economy Achievements**
*   Qingyang has become one of the eight national "East Data West Computing" hub nodes.
*   It is described as the benchmark cluster with the fastest computing power growth, largest increment, highest proportion of intelligent computing, and most sufficient consumption among the eight hubs.
*   The region undertakes 1/4 of the country’s intelligent computing demand.
*   Output has shifted from "selling racks" to "selling computing power" to "selling tokens" (词元).
*   Labor brands such as "Qingyang Data Calculators" have cumulatively driven employment for 550,000 people.

**4. Science Education in Ethnic Regions**
*   An article titled "Let the Seeds of Science Sprout in Questioning" (Article #368) was published under the series "Chinese Path Chinese Dream: Youth Shouldering Responsibility for Chinese Modernization No. 64."
*   Author: Wang Yushun (Official from Shangcheng District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang, serving in Yajiang County, Garzê Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, Sichuan).
*   The article details efforts to stimulate curiosity in high-altitude Tibetan children through hardware upgrades (smart blackboards, VR labs) and resource sharing (West Lake University博士科普团).

**5. Agricultural and Aquaculture Data**
*   An image report and accompanying text (Article #369) cited Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs data.
*   In the first three quarters of 2026, national aquatic product output reached 53.897 million tons.
*   Seawater aquaculture output was 20.297 million tons, maintaining high-speed growth.
*   A specific example was provided of Xiangshan County, Ningbo, Zhejiang, using digital empowerment for yellow croaker farming.

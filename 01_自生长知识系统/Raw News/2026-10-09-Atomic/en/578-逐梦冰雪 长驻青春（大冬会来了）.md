### [Zheng Qinwen advances to China Open quarterfinals](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184696.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On the evening of October 7, 2026, Chinese tennis player Zheng Qinwen defeated Armenia’s Margarita Gasparyan 6-1, 2-6, 6-4 to reach the fourth round of the China Open women’s singles, marking a return to the quarterfinals for the first time since 2024. The match tested Zheng’s physical and mental endurance as she overcame a second-set slump and a break in her service game at 5-2 in the decider. Zheng, who turned 23 earlier in October, reflected on her growth and ambition to keep improving, saying she is “working hard to upgrade” herself despite the rapid passage of time. Her performance has inspired younger Chinese players, including亚运会 gold medalist Wang Xiyu and silver medalist Wu Yifan.

# Event Name

Ningxia Helan County: Industrial Integration and Development of Fisheries and Wine Industries

## Event Overview

This event covers the industrial development and regional economic integration in Helan County, Yinchuan City, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, China. As reported by *People's Daily* on November 28, 2026, Helan County has focused on leveraging its resource endowments to promote the iterative development of its fisheries and wine industries toward a "Commerce, Culture, Tourism, Agriculture, Sports, and Wine" (commercial-cultural-tourism-agricultural-sport-wine) integrated model. The development aims to balance ecological protection with economic growth in the Yellow River basin, specifically targeting the improvement of local incomes through the transformation of saline-alkali wastelands into productive aquaculture areas and the expansion of the Helan Mountain East Foothills wine region. The article highlights specific achievements in "sea fish land rearing," wine branding, and the hosting of national-level sports events to drive tourism and consumer engagement.

## Core Facts

*   **Geographic Focus:** Helan County is located at the foot of the Helan Mountains and along the Yellow River, situated in the 38° north latitude golden wine industry belt.
*   **Aquaculture Statistics:**
    *   Current fishery breeding area is stable at 58,000 mu (approx. 3,866 hectares).
    *   Using 2.3% of the Northwest region's breeding water surface, Helan contributes 11.3% of the region's aquatic products.
    *   80% of products are sold to six surrounding provinces/regions.
    *   Average per capita disposable income of fishermen in 2025 exceeded 25,000 RMB, with a year-on-year growth rate of 7.31%.
*   **Aquaculture Innovations:**
    *   Helan County has been awarded titles such as National Aquatic Health Breeding and Ecological Breeding Demonstration Zone and National Safe Fisheries Demonstration County during the "14th Five-Year Plan" period.
    *   Collaborating with Fujian-Ningxia cooperation, the county developed a "sea fish land rearing" model, specifically inland simulated seawater breeding.
    *   The survival rate of large yellow croaker seedlings in transit exceeds 85%.
    *   The "He Zhi Xian" regional public brand has won 35 industry awards, including the "Golden Crab Award" and "Best Taste Award" at the National River Crab Competition and Shanghai International Crab Industry Expo.
*   **International Trade Milestone:**
    *   On October 28, 2026, "Sai Shang Yellow River Chinese Mitten Crabs" from Sanding Lake, Changxin Township, were exported to Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, via Xiamen, Fujian.
    *   This was the first independent export order for live crabs from Ningxia after obtaining export qualifications.
*   **Wine Industry Statistics:**
    *   The Helan Mountain East Foothills wine region has 17 operating wineries.
    *   7 wineries are classified (lieji) wineries.
    *   4 wineries are rated as National Class A Tourist Attractions.
    *   In 2026, Helan County secured 9.06 million RMB in project funds and allocated 1.7227 million RMB in county-level support funds.
    *   Local wines have won "Grand Gold Awards" at the Brussels International Wine Challenge and the China (Ningxia) Helan Mountain East Foothills International Wine Competition.
*   **Sports and Tourism Integration:**
    *   Helan County hosted the 2026 China Equestrian Show Jumping Championship Cup (Ningxia Station) and the 2026 China Equestrian Show Jumping Junior Championship Cup (Ningxia Station) in Changxin Township.
    *   The 2026 Chinese Fishing Championship "Fujian-Ningxia One Family" Ningxia Helan Station was held in the county.
    *   The county is developing a "one-hour leisure circle" for the Yinchuan metropolitan area, featuring IPs such as "Rock Painting + Wine" and "Starry Winery."

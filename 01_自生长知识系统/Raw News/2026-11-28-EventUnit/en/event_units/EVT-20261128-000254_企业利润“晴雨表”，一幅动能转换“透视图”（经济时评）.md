## Event Overview

On November 28, 2026, the *People's Daily* published an economic commentary titled "Corporate Profits 'Barometer,' A 'Perspective' of Kinetic Energy Transition" (企业利润“晴雨表”，一幅动能转换“透视图”) on Page 05. Authored by Liu Zhiqiang, the article analyzes recent data released by the National Bureau of Statistics regarding the profit growth of national above-scale industrial enterprises. The commentary highlights a dual trend in China's industrial sector: high-speed growth in high-tech manufacturing driven by emerging industries, contrasted with significant profit declines in traditional sectors such as steel and automotive manufacturing. The piece frames this divergence as a clear indicator of China’s economic transition towards new kinetic energy sources.

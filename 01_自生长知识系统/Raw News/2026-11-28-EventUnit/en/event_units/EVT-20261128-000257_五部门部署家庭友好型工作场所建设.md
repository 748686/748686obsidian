# Event Name

Five Departments Issue Guidelines for Family-Friendly Workplace Construction

## Event Overview

On November 27, 2026, the All-China Trade Union (ACTU) announced that five national departments jointly issued the *Guidelines for the Construction of Family-Friendly Workplaces (Trial)*. The issuing bodies are the All-China Trade Union, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, the National Health Commission, China Enterprise Confederation/China Association of Chambers of Commerce, and the All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce. This policy initiative is designed to optimize workplace management systems to support employees in balancing work, family, and personal life, while ensuring a safe, dignified, and health-promoting environment.

## Core Facts

*   **Document Title:** *Guidelines for the Construction of Family-Friendly Workplaces (Trial)*.
*   **Issuing Entities:**
    1.  All-China Trade Union (ACTU)
    2.  Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security
    3.  National Health Commission
    4.  China Enterprise Confederation / China Association of Chambers of Commerce
    5.  All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce
*   **Announcement Date:** November 27, 2026 (as reported by the All-China Trade Union).
*   **Publication Date:** November 28, 2026 (in the People's Daily).
*   **Definition:** A family-friendly workplace is defined as one where the employer optimizes work management systems to create a safe, dignified environment conducive to employee mental and physical health. It supports both male and female employees in balancing work, family, and personal life.
*   **Key Policy Innovation ("Six Friendlies"):** For the first time at a policy level, the guidelines propose six specific measures:
    1.  Friendly attitude and philosophy
    2.  Friendly career development
    3.  Friendly birth protection
    4.  Friendly care support
    5.  Friendly work arrangement
    6.  Friendly occupational health
*   **Mechanism:** The guidelines leverage the "tripartite mechanism" (employers, trade unions, and government) to promote harmonious labor relations.
*   **Responsibilities:** Joint responsibilities are established for policy supply, resource support, and service assurance among the above-mentioned departments and employers. Mechanisms for communication, information sharing, consultation, and coordinated promotion are mandated.
*   **Implementation Aid:** The guidelines provide specific practical case studies for different scenarios based on the "Six Friendlies" to serve as replicable operational samples for employers.

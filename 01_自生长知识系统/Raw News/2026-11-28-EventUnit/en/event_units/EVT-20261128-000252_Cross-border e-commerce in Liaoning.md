# Event Name
Development of Cross-Border E-Commerce in Liaoning Province

## Event Overview
As of November 2026, Liaoning Province has implemented a multi-pronged strategy to accelerate the development of its cross-border e-commerce sector. The provincial government has introduced specific policy packages, established infrastructure such as parks and incubation centers, and facilitated logistics and tax support for businesses. This initiative has led to significant growth in trade volume, the creation of new export channels (including for live animals and agricultural machinery), and a shift from individual breakthroughs to scaled, supply-chain integration.

## Core Facts
*   **Trade Growth:** In the first three quarters of 2026, cross-border e-commerce import/export volume in Liaoning Province increased by 70% year-on-year.
*   **Enterprise Registration:** The province registered 1,168 new cross-border e-commerce enterprises at customs authorities in the first three quarters of 2026.
*   **Innovative Metrics:** The province recorded over 80 "from 0 to 1" innovations in 2026, including first-time shipments and tax refunds in various categories.
*   **Infrastructure:** Liaoning has built 41 cross-border e-commerce industrial parks (including incubation centers) with over 1,700 resident enterprises.
*   **Specific Milestones:**
    *   April 2026: Completion of the first national export of live dogs and cats under the cross-border e-commerce B2B direct export model (Shenyang to South Korea).
    *   2026: Yingkou completed the province’s first "cross-border e-commerce + international road transport" business.
    *   2026: Dandong implemented the "no-invoice tax exemption" policy for small and medium-sized enterprises.
    *   2026: Tieling achieved "double-zero breakthroughs" in two cross-border e-commerce export models.
*   **Tax Support:** From January to September 2026, the number of enterprises receiving cross-border e-commerce export tax refunds increased by 145 (a 105.8% increase), with total tax refunds reaching 96.52 million RMB.
*   **Logistics Growth:** International postal and express delivery business in Liaoning grew by 37.15% year-on-year in the first eight months of 2026.
*   **Strategic Targets:** The 2026 cultivation plan aims to establish 10 industrial belts, 20 key industrial parks, 50 key enterprises, and 20 independent brands.

## Known Current Impact
*   **Economic Diversification:** Traditional industries (socks, jade/agate, swimwear, chemical equipment, agriculture) are diversifying into direct international e-commerce.
*   **Job Creation & Training:** Over 20,000 people have been covered by 411 cross-border e-commerce training sessions in the province. The Anshan park has directly employed over 20 people in e-commerce roles through university partnerships.
*   **Supply Chain Integration:** The shift from "point breakthroughs" to "full-chain development" is evidenced by the establishment of overseas warehouses (e.g., in Brazil) and coordinated group exports.
*   **Policy Effectiveness:** The reduction in tax refund thresholds and logistics costs has enabled small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to participate, evidenced by the 105.8% increase in firms claiming export tax refunds.

## Event Conclusion
Liaoning Province has established a robust framework for cross-border e-commerce growth in 2026, characterized by a 70% year-on-year trade increase and significant institutional innovations such as the first national live-pet B2B export channel. The provincial strategy successfully combines policy support (tax and logistics) with infrastructure development (41 parks), enabling a transition from fragmented SME participation to scaled industrial export. The initiative is currently focusing on transforming traditional manufacturing sectors into direct international supply chains, with measurable targets set for 2026 to further solidify Liaoning's position in the global market.

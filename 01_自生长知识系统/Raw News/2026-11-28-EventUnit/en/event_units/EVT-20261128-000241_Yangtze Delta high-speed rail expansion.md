# Event Name
Yangtze Delta High-Speed Rail Expansion: Three New Lines in Anhui Open

## Event Overview
On November 27, 2026, three new high-speed rail lines in Anhui Province were opened for operation: the Chaohu–Ma'anshan, Huaibei–Bengbu, and Huaibei–Fuyang lines. This milestone addition brought the total high-speed rail mileage in the Yangtze River Delta region to over 8,500 kilometers, while the total operating railway mileage in the region exceeded 15,800 kilometers. As a result, five counties in Anhui ended their lack of access to high-speed rail services, enhancing connectivity between the Hefei and Nanjing metropolitan areas and central cities in the Yangtze Delta.

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Event:** The new lines opened for operation on November 27, 2026.
*   **Specific Lines Opened:**
    *   Chaohu–Ma'anshan High-Speed Rail (巢湖—马鞍高铁)
    *   Huaibei–Bengbu High-Speed Rail (淮北—蚌埠高铁)
    *   Huaibei–Fuyang High-Speed Rail (淮北—阜阳高铁)
*   **Regional Milestones Achieved:**
    *   Yangtze Delta high-speed rail mileage surpassed 8,500 km.
    *   Total Yangtze Delta railway operating mileage surpassed 15,800 km.
*   **Counties Gaining High-Speed Access:**
    *   Hexian (Ma'anshan City)
    *   Suixi County (Huaibei City)
    *   Guzhen County (Bengbu City)
    *   Mengcheng County (Bozhou City)
    *   Lixin County (Bozhou City)
*   **Regional Impact:** The new lines created multi-path rapid channels linking the Hefei and Nanjing metropolitan areas to central Yangtze Delta cities, significantly compressing spatial-temporal distances between northern Anhui and the Yangtze River basin cities.

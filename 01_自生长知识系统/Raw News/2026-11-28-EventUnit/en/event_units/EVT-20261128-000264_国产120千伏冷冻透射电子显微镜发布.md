## Event Overview

On November 28, 2026, reports from *People's Daily* confirmed the release of China's domestic 120 kV cryo-electron microscope, designated TH—F120C, in Guangzhou. The event represents a breakthrough in autonomous high-end scientific instrumentation in China. The instrument is designed to provide tools for analyzing biological macromolecule structures and studying the microscopic characteristics of advanced materials. The development was a five-year effort led by the Bio Island Laboratory, involving multiple research institutions under the support of the Guangdong Provincial Department of Science and Technology.

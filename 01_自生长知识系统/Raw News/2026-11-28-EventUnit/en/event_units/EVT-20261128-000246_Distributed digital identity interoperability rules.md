# Event Name
Distributed Digital Identity Interoperability Rules

## Event Overview
On November 27, 2026, it was announced that eleven joint departments, including the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) and the Central Organization Department, published the *Provisions on the Application of Interoperability and Mutual Recognition of Distributed Digital Identities*. The regulation aims to standardize the roles of various entities in the distributed digital identity ecosystem, establish unified coding standards, and facilitate cross-platform identity recognition. The rules are scheduled to take effect on March 1, 2027.

## Core Facts
*   **Announcement Date:** November 27, 2026 (reported by People's Daily on November 28).
*   **Issuing Bodies:** Eleven departments jointly, specifically including the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) and the Central Organization Department.
*   **Document Title:** *Provisions on the Application of Interoperability and Mutual Recognition of Distributed Digital Identities* (《分布式数字身份互通互认应用规定》).
*   **Effective Date:** March 1, 2027.
*   **Regulated Entities:**
    *   Distributed digital identity chain operators
    *   Authoritative identity authentication institutions
    *   Distributed digital identity application service providers
    *   Verifiable credential issuance institutions
    *   Verifiable credential application institutions
*   **Standardized Processes:**
    *   Application and registration
    *   Credential issuance
    *   Usage management
    *   Verification workflows
    *   Identifier usage
    *   Key management
*   **Key Directives:**
    *   Encourages participation of multiple entities in building the distributed digital identity system.
    *   Establishes unified coding specifications and an identifier directory system.
    *   Reduces the cost of cross-platform and cross-industry identity mutual recognition.
*   **Target Application Sectors:**
    *   Finance
    *   Taxation
    *   Transportation
    *   Customs
    *   Public resource bidding
    *   Agent identity identification

With an effective date of March 1, 2027, this regulation sets the stage for the integration of distributed identity solutions in critical sectors such as finance, customs, and potentially AI agent identification.

## Event Overview
On November 28, 2026, *People's Daily* published a feature article titled "The Agricultural Revitalization Path of 'Gan Xiao Cheng'" (赣小橙的兴农路), describing the technological, industrial, and market advancements of the navel orange industry in the Gannan region of Jiangxi Province. The article, spanning from 1970 to the present, highlights the transformation of the orange industry from a poverty-alleviation tool to a high-value, integrated, and export-oriented sector. The narrative follows the journey of oranges, branded as "Gan Xiao Cheng," through four key stages of the modern supply chain: smart cultivation, automated sorting and grading, deep processing of by-products, and international export. The industry is reported to have an annual output value exceeding 23 billion RMB and a brand value of nearly 70 billion RMB, supporting over 130,000 households and 1.9 million mu of cultivation area.

## Core Facts
*   **Industry Scale:** The Gannan navel orange industry has a cultivation area of 1.9 million mu and involves over 130,000 farming households. The annual output value exceeds 23 billion RMB, with a brand value of nearly 70 billion RMB.
*   **Historical Origin:** In 1970, technician Yuan Shogen introduced navel orange saplings from Shaoyang, Hunan, to Xinfeng, Jiangxi. By 1976, initial exports to Hong Kong achieved a price of 36 HKD per kilogram.
*   **Technological Modernization:**
    *   **Cultivation:** Use of drones for transport and automated central control systems for water, fertilizer, and pest management.
    *   **Sorting:** Implementation of advanced sorting lines using near-infrared spectroscopy ("CT scan") to measure internal sugar content (e.g., 14.2% sugar degree) and size.
    *   **Processing:** "Five separations, five reconstructions" technology to process by-products (peel, albedo, pulp) into high-value items like orange oil, pectin, dietary fiber, and frozen orange pulp.
*   **Standardization:** A new national standard for navel oranges, incorporating intrinsic quality indicators like sugar content, took effect on July 1 of the current year (2026).
*   **Disease Management:** In 2022, Huanglongbing (citrus greening) disease forced the felling of trees (e.g., 2,000 mu in one case). The central, provincial, and municipal governments invested over 500 million RMB in special funds for disease prevention and control.
*   **Export Growth:** In 2025, Gannan navel orange exports in the Longnan Customs jurisdiction reached 23,100 tons (a 77% YoY increase). In the first 10 months of 2026, exports reached 22,900 tons (a 120% YoY increase), distributed across 24 countries and regions.
*   **Economic Impact:** Deep processing has increased profit margins from 20% for fresh fruit to over 50% for processed goods. By-products that were previously waste are now valued at approximately 5,940 RMB per ton after processing, compared to a purchase price of 1,000 RMB per ton.

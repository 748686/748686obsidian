# Li Qiang Chairs State Council Meeting on High-Quality Development, Capital Markets, and Safety

## Event Overview
On November 27, 2026, Premier Li Qiang chaired a meeting of the State Council. The agenda covered four primary areas: reviewing reports on comprehensive supervision of high-quality development, studying reforms in capital market investment and financing, hearing reports on the national standard system, and deliberating on the "Regulations on Investigation and Management of Production Safety Accident Hidden Risks (Draft)." The meeting emphasized rectification of issues found during supervision, stabilization of the capital market through specific financial mechanisms, optimization of national standards, and the prevention of major safety accidents.

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Meeting:** November 27, 2026.
*   **Chairman:** Li Qiang, Premier of the State Council.
*   **Key Agenda Items:**
    1.  Heard the report on the promotion of high-quality development comprehensive supervision and rectification.
    2.  Studied work to continuously deepen the comprehensive reform of investment and financing in the capital market.
    3.  Heard the report on the construction of the national standard system.
    4.  Deliberated and passed the "Regulations on Investigation and Management of Production Safety Accident Hidden Risks (Draft)."
*   **Capital Market Policy Measures:**
    *   Improve capital market stabilization mechanisms and implement existing policies while planning incremental policies.
    *   Encourage listed companies to increase dividends and share buybacks.
    *   Strengthen the role of "national team" funds in stabilizing the market.
    *   Optimize the function of the central Huijin Company as a quasi-"stability fund" to provide liquidity support.
    *   Improve policies guiding medium- and long-term funds (insurance, corporate annuities, social security funds) to enter the market.
    *   Strengthen whole-chain regulation and crack down on financial fraud and illegal share sales.
*   **Safety Production Policy:**
    *   Shift the governance model towards ex-ante prevention.
    *   Implement closed-loop management for rectifying hidden risks in key industries.
    *   Refine standards for determining major accident hidden risks.
    *   Promote the application of new digital and intelligent technologies/equipment.

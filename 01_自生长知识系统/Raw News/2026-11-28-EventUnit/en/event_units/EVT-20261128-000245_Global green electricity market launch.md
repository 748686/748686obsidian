# Event Name

Global Launch of the Largest Green Electricity Trading Market: Northwest-East China Joint Transaction

## Event Overview

On November 26, 2026, China completed its first joint green electricity transaction between the Northwest and East China regions, marking the official launch of a unified green electricity market that integrates inter-provincial and intra-provincial trading. According to *People's Daily*, this new "one-time application, joint transaction" model allows wind and solar power generators and electricity consumers in both regions to trade through a single platform under unified rules. The initial transaction involved 1,615 market entities and traded 6.2 billion kilowatt-hours of electricity. Authorities describe this event as a major milestone in the construction of China's national unified electricity market, specifically fulfilling policy goals set in February 2026 to transition from fragmented provincial trading to a nationwide joint matching system.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Transaction:** November 26, 2026.
*   **Nature of Event:** The first joint green electricity transaction between Northwest and East China regions.
*   **Market Structure:** A unified market merging inter-provincial and intra-provincial green electricity trading channels.
*   **Operational Model:** "One-time application, joint transaction" (一次申报、联合交易), where entities submit quantity and price demands once, and the market matches supply and demand across the combined regions.
*   **Participants:** 1,615 business entities in total.
    *   427 electricity retail companies and direct power users (demand side).
    *   1,188 power generation companies (supply side).
*   **Transaction Volume:** 6.2 billion kilowatt-hours (kWh) of green electricity.
*   **Regional Flow:** 1.9 billion kWh of green electricity was transmitted from the Northwest region to East China.
*   **Environmental Impact:** The transaction is reported to reduce carbon dioxide emissions by approximately 5 million tons.
*   **Policy Context:** This launch implements the "Implementation Opinions on Improving the National Unified Electricity Market System" issued by the State Council General Office in February 2026.

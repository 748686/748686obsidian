# Xin Changxing Elected as Secretary of the Jiangsu Provincial Party Committee

**标题：** Xin Changxing Elected as Secretary of the Jiangsu Provincial Party Committee

**作者：** Deng Jiansheng, Yin Xiaoyu (People's Daily)

**标签：** Politics, Chinese Communist Party, Jiangsu Province, Leadership Transition, Personnel Appointment

**一句话总结这篇文文章:** On November 27, 2026, Xin Changxing was elected Secretary of the Jiangsu Provincial Committee of the CPC, alongside a newly formed leadership team including Vice Secretaries Liu Xiaotao and Fan Bo, and a Standing Committee and Discipline Inspection Commission.

**总结文章内容并写成摘要:**
On November 27, 2026, the First Plenary Session of the 15th Jiangsu Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC) was held to elect the provincial leadership. Xin Changxing was elected as the Secretary of the Jiangsu Provincial Committee. The session also elected two Vice Secretaries: Liu Xiaotao and Fan Bo. Additionally, a ten-member Standing Committee was elected, comprising Zhou Hongbo, Zhang Wenbing, Hu Lijie (female), Bian Xuewen, Li Yaoguang, Chen Zhongwei, Ma Shiguang, Shen Jianrong, Jing Jian, and Wu Qingwen. Concurrently, the First Plenary Session of the 15th Jiangsu Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission elected Bian Xuewen as the Provincial Discipline Inspector, with Lu Ye, Huang Chuzhong, Qi Xin, and Wang Jubing serving as Deputy Inspectors. This event marks a standard procedural leadership transition within the CPC hierarchy in Jiangsu Province.

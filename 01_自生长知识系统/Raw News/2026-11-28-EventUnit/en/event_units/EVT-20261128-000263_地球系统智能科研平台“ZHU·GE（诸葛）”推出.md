# Earth System Intelligent Scientific Research Platform “ZHU·GE (Zhuge)” Launch

## Event Overview

On November 28, 2026, the Earth System Intelligent Scientific Research Platform named “ZHU·GE” (诸葛) was officially unveiled at the 2026 Xiongan Meteorological Artificial Intelligence International Seminar. The platform was co-led in research and development by the China Meteorological Administration (CMA) and the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. Its primary function is to connect massive meteorological data and atmospheric scientific knowledge, modify research frameworks, and conduct scenario experiments, thereby rapidly converting research ideas into data-supported, analyzable, and verifiable scientific conclusions.

## Core Facts

*   **Event:** Official launch of the “ZHU·GE” Earth System Intelligent Scientific Research Platform.
*   **Date:** November 28, 2026.
*   **Location:** Beijing, China (reported via a Beijing dispatch).
*   **Venue/Occasion:** 2026 Xiongan Meteorological Artificial Intelligence International Seminar.
*   **Primary Developers:** China Meteorological Administration (CMA) and Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory.
*   **Platform Purpose:** To facilitate the integration of massive meteorological data and atmospheric scientific knowledge, transform research frameworks, and run scenario experiments.
*   **Capabilities:** Transforms research ideas into scientific conclusions backed by large-scale data; converts scientific understanding into business capabilities.
*   **Data Resources:**
    *   Approximately 10 PB (petabytes) of public scientific data.
    *   First batch of 1 PB of CMA-authorized data.
    *   Approximately 2 million literature records.
*   **Built-in Tools:** Over 5,000 code tools and more than 10 general scientific research skills.
*   **Future Plans:**
    *   Full coverage of scientific research and business units within the meteorological department.
    *   Opening to members of the National Meteorological Science and Education Integration Innovation Alliance.
    *   Exploration of providing international services.

## Event Conclusion

On November 28, 2026, the China Meteorological Administration and the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory jointly launched the “ZHU·GE” Earth System Intelligent Scientific Research Platform at the 2026 Xiongan Meteorological Artificial Intelligence International Seminar. The platform aggregates approximately 11 PB of data (including 10 PB public and 1 PB CMA-authorized data) and over 2 million literature records, equipped with more than 5,000 code tools. It is designed to accelerate the transition from research concepts to data-verified scientific conclusions. Future developments will extend access to the national meteorological science and education innovation alliance and potentially to international users.

## Core Facts

*   **Publication Context**: The article appeared in the "Cultural Heritage" section of *People's Daily* on November 28, 2026. The authors are Li Qingjun and Lin Junming.
*   **Geographical Focus**: The narrative centers on Quanzhou City, Fujian Province, specifically the areas of Shishi City, Anxi County, and Nan'an City.
*   **Subject 1: Li Zifang**:
    *   Born in 1910 in Jinjiang (now Shishi), Quanzhou.
    *   Joined the Red Army in May 1932 in Shima, Zhangzhou.
    *   Joined the Communist Party of China in April 1933.
    *   Participated in the Long March with the First Front Army in October 1934.
    *   During the All-National War of Resistance against Japan, served as Head of the Organization Department of the New Fourth Army Political Department.
    *   Captured during the 1941 "Wannan Incident" (Southern Anhui Incident) after refusing to withdraw despite illness.
    *   Served as secretary of a clandestine Party branch in prison; reportedly recited Wen Tianxiang's *Song of Upright Spirit* to encourage fellow prisoners.
    *   Executed by enemies in prison in May 1942, at the age of 32.
    *   His former residence (a stone-walled, tiled house built in 1910, the 2nd year of the Xuantong reign) in Ziying Village, Yongning Town, Shishi City, was listed as a municipal-level protected cultural relic in 1991, named "Li Zifang Former Residence."
*   **Subject 2: Li Gang**:
    *   A Nantai native residing in Malaysia.
    *   Returned to China to join the revolution following the "September 18th Incident" of 1931.
    *   Participated in the three-year southern guerrilla war that supported the Long March.
    *   Left a letter to his parents in winter 1938, stating, "I know one should store grain for hunger and raise sons for old age, but now the nation faces a crisis; I must attend to the nation's crisis and cannot attend to my family. I ask my parents' forgiveness!"
    *   Died in action in February 1941 during a breakthrough attempt, at the age of 27.
    *   His former residence ("Dongshan Courtyard"), a Qing-dynasty style house in Hongqi Village, Shishan Town, Nan'an City, was registered as a newly discovered cultural relic during the Fourth National Cultural Relic Census.
    *   The residence retains a slogan handwritten by Li Gang: "Overthrow Local Dungs."
*   **Historical Site: Zhongxin Courtyard (Fozige Village, Kuidou Town, Anxi County)**:
    *   Serves as the site of the former CCP Anxi Central County Committee.
    *   Listed as a county-level protected cultural relic in 1985.
    *   In November 1932, the CCP Anxi County Committee was elevated to the CCP Anxi Central County Committee here to coordinate revolutionary struggles in Anxi, Nan'an, Yongchun, and Dehua.
    *   This effort included establishing a Soviet government and conducting land reform, which helped distract enemy forces and support the Anti-Encirclement Campaigns of the Central Soviet Area and the Long March.

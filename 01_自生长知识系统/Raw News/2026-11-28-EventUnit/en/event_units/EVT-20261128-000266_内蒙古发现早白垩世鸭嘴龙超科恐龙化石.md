# Event Name
Inner Mongolia Discovery of Early Cretaceous Duck-billed Dinosaur Fossils

## Event Overview
On November 28, 2026, reports emerged from a joint scientific team that early Cretaceous hadrosauroid dinosaur fossils were discovered in the Ordos Basin (referred to as Erlian Basin in the text) in Inner Mongolia. This discovery, published in the international journal *Fossil Record*, represents the first record of hadrosauroid fossils from the Lower Cretaceous strata of the Erlian Basin. The fossils were excavated in Inner Mongolia and indicate the presence of this dinosaur group in the region during the early Cretaceous period.

## Core Facts
*   **Fossil Type**: Early Cretaceous hadrosauroid (duck-billed dinosaur superfamily) fossils.
*   **Discovery Location**: Eji Na'er Town, Dong Ujimqin Banner, Xilingol League, Inner Mongolia, China.
*   **Stratigraphic Context**: Lower Cretaceous strata of the Erlian Basin (二连盆地).
*   **Significance**: This is the first hadrosauroid fossil record from the Lower Cretaceous of the Erlian Basin.
*   **Publication**: Published in the international paleontology journal *Fossil Record*.
*   **Research Team**: A joint team consisting of the Inner Mongolia Museum of Natural History, Yunnan University, the Institute of Vertebrate Paleontology and Paleoanthropology (Chinese Academy of Sciences), and Shenyang Normal University.
*   **Morphological Characteristics**: The specimen exhibits both primitive traits of early ornithopods and progressive evolutionary features approaching the Hadrosauridae family.
*   **Report Date**: November 28, 2026.

### [Songhua bing: a traditional pine pollen pastry from Sanmen](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/23/content_30182674.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A September 23, 2026 People&\#x27;s Daily feature describes songhua bing, a regional snack from Sanmen, Zhejiang, made with pollen from male flowers of Masson pine. The article outlines how the pollen is harvested, dried, sifted, and stored, then mixed with glutinous rice flour at a 5:1 ratio, formed into oval cakes, and pan-fried over low heat until golden. It highlights that the pollen is gathered once a year with low yield, is used in other local foods and as a natural skin powder, and has historical literary associations dating to the Northern Song period. With local tourism growth, the pastry is increasingly sold prepared fresh to visitors.

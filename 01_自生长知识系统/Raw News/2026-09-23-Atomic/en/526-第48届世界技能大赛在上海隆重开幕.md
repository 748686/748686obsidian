### [August 2026 anti-corruption enforcement figures published](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/23/content_30182628.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 22, 2026, China&\#x27;s Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and National Supervisory Commission published August statistics on enforcement of the Central Eight-Point Regulation. Authorities investigated 29,171 violations nationwide in August, resulting in criticism and education or other handling of 35,901 people, including disciplinary and administrative sanctions against 24,494 individuals. Formalism and bureaucracy issues accounted for 15,431 cases, with dereliction affecting high-quality development being the largest category at 12,971 cases. Hedonism and extravagance violations totaled 13,740 cases, where illicit gift and specialty receipt was the most common issue at 8,603 cases. Enforcement involved 117 department-level officials, 1,568 county-level officials, and 27,486 township-level and below officials.

**「Background」** The Central Eight-Point Regulation, introduced in 2012, sets standards for official conduct to combat waste, privilege, and bureaucratic excess in the Chinese Communist Party and government institutions. The Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and National Supervisory Commission routinely publishes monthly violation statistics to demonstrate accountability enforcement and deter corruption.

**「Impact」** Government officials, party members, and state institution employees are subject to disciplinary review for compliance failures, with August data showing the majority of enforcement actions targeting grassroots cadres at the township level and below.

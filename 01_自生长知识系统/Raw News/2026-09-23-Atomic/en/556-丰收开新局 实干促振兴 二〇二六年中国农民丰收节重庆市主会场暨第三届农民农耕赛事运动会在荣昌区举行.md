### [China warns elderly consumers about净水器 pyramid schemes](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/23/content_30182666.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Chinese consumer protection authorities have issued warnings about deceptive water purifier sales targeting elderly people in rural and community settings. Between January and July 2026, complaints exceeded the total from all of 2024, with July alone showing a 40.17% increase over January. Scammers use tactics like free gifts, health seminars, and water electrolysis demos to create fear about tap water quality, then sell low-cost units at prices of thousands to tens of thousands of yuan while falsely claiming therapeutic benefits. The China Consumers Association advises buyers to verify proper certification marks including CCC certification and provincial health permits, purchase from licensed retailers with formal invoices, understand ongoing filter costs, and preserve receipts for potential threefold damage claims under consumer protection law.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 23, 00:26

**「Background」** Water purifiers are domestic filtration devices that remove contaminants from tap water; they require periodic filter replacements and must comply with Chinese safety and hygiene regulations. The national compulsory certification \(CCC\) system mandates safety testing for electrical appliances, while health departments issue hygiene permits for products claiming water treatment functions.

**「Impact」** Elderly consumers in rural areas and communities face heightened risk of financial loss and safety hazards from unregulated water purifier sales. Buyers should seek products with documented CCC certification, official hygiene permits, and written warranty terms before purchasing.

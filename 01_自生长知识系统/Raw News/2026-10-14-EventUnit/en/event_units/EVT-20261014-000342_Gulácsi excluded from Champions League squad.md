# Gulácsi Excluded from Champions League Squad

## Event Overview

On 2026-10-14, it was reported that goalkeeper Péter Gulácsi (RB Leipzig) was excluded from the club’s UEFA Champions League squad. The exclusion follows a criminal incident in which Gulácsi was the victim of an armed robbery outside a Berlin nightclub.

## Core Facts

- **Date of incident:** Early morning of 4 October 2026.
- **Event type:** Armed robbery (described as “gang rape and hold-up” by German outlets).
- **Location:** Outside a Berlin nightclub.
- **Victim:** Péter Gulácsi, RB Leipzig goalkeeper.
- **Consequence for Gulácsi:** Treated at Berlin’s Charité hospital; sustained injuries.
- **Consequence for teammate:** Andreas Lukenbach (RB Leipzig youth player) also injured, underwent surgery, and was in critical condition (as of 6 October).
- **Squad exclusion:** Gulácsi omitted from RB Leipzig’s UEFA Champions League squad after the incident.
- **Match context:** The exclusion is linked to a scheduled Champions League match against Galatasaray (reported as upcoming on 6 October).
- **Public response:** RB Leipzig players displayed a black armband before their 5–0 Bundesliga win over Paderborn on 3 October to show solidarity with Gulácsi.
- **Criminal status:** Three suspects in their 20s are in police custody, charged with rape and attempted robbery.
- **Medical status:** Gulácsi is recovering from his injuries.

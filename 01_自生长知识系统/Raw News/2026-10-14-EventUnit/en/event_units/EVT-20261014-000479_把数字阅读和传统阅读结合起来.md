*   **Claim:** The editors posit that the core theme connecting the day's cultural reports is the concept of "questioning." The article asserts that education should not merely be about "teaching" but about inspiring students to "ask questions" and "explore problems."
*   **Purpose:** The note serves as a meta-commentary, indicating that the accompanying articles on university libraries demonstrate how institutions are facilitating this environment of inquiry.

**2. Jiangsu University Library Initiatives**
*   **Source:** "Opening Treasure Libraries in Ways Young People Like" (Article #567).
*   **Author:** Wang Hanchao (People's Daily).
*   **Scope:** The report covers three specific institutions: Soochow University, Hohai University, and Nanjing University.

    *   **Soochow University:**
        *   **Resource Scale:** Four districts, four libraries, total construction area over 80,000 square meters, holding over 4.35 million volumes.
        *   **Initiative:** "Walking Reading" project. Students visit off-campus locations such as Suzhou gardens and former residences of famous individuals with specific research topics.
        *   **Mechanism:** Real-world exploration generates curiosity and questions, which students then pursue by returning to the library for specialized deep-reading and resource retrieval.
        *   **Exhibitions:** Displays red school history and patriotic deeds of predecessors (e.g., exhibition celebrating the 100th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China) to serve as practical classrooms for new student education.
        *   **Statement:** Tang Wenyue (Librarian) emphasizes that libraries must use methods appealing to young people to cultivate lifelong reading habits.

    *   **Hohai University:**
        *   **Specialty:** Water conservancy. Founded in 1915 by Zhang Jian.
        *   **Initiative:** Integration of professional characteristics into moral education.
        *   **Activities:** "Yugong Culture Reading Festival" and thematic exhibitions on the "Century of Water Control."
        *   **Curriculum Innovation:** Librarians collaborated with faculty to develop a labor education course syllabus titled *Ancient Book Conservation and Restoration*. Students used a reprint of the ancient text *Illustrated Manual of River Engineering Tools*, filled in blank spaces after consulting materials, and produced personalized thread-bound books.
        *   **Statement:** Wang Zhifeng (Secretary of the Library Party Committee) states the goal is to help students understand national needs behind their majors.

    *   **Nanjing University:**
        *   **Resource Scale:** Total holdings of 6.95 million volumes, including nearly 400,000 ancient books (many rare/single-copy editions).
        *   **Initiative:** Interactive and creative engagement with ancient texts.
        *   **Activity 1 (Script Murder Game):** Students analyzed *Investiture of the Gods* to deconstruct and reconstruct a "script murder" (mystery role-playing) game. This required rigorous textual close-reading to understand character psychology and plot lines.
        *   **Activity 2 (Red Dream Society):** Students examined a facsimile of a残缺 (fragmented) Cheng Jiaben edition of *Dream of the Red Chamber*. To repair/complement the volume, students had to cross-reference multiple editions in the collection, leading to a version exhibition.
        *   **Statement:** Huang Xianjin (Librarian) argues that young people are not just readers but creators; activating library value requires贴近 (closeness to) young people's interests.

## Event Conclusion

The event highlights a strategic shift in Chinese higher education libraries, particularly in Jiangsu Province, toward **inquiry-based learning** and **experiential engagement**. By moving beyond traditional lending models, institutions like Soochow, Hohai, and Nanjing Universities are leveraging their unique collections and local cultural assets to stimulate student curiosity. The editorial framing confirms that this approach is intended to counteract the fragmentation of attention caused by digital media (short videos) by returning to the root of deep learning: the ability to ask meaningful questions and seek answers through rigorous reading and research. The discrepancy in the source merge (pairing a university library report with a primary school agriculture photo) indicates a metadata error in the first-layer processing but does not detract from the validity of the primary source material regarding library innovations.

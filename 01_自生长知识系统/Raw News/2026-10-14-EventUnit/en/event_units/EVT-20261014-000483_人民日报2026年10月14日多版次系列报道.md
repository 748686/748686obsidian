## Event Overview
On October 14, 2026, *People's Daily* published a series of coordinated reports across its social, ecological, and international sections (Pages 13–15). These articles document simultaneous developments in domestic social governance, ecological conservation, green energy transition, and international relations. The event covers: (1) consumer protection guidance for health products; (2) urban space optimization and social welfare improvements; (3) ecological heritage preservation and biodiversity protection; (4) green energy infrastructure and data governance; and (5) diplomatic positions on multilateral trade reform and Sino-European cooperation.

## Core Facts

### Social Governance & Consumer Protection
- **Health Product Consumer Guidance**: The State Administration for Market Regulation and the China Consumers Association jointly issued consumer guidelines on identifying authentic health products. Key measures include: recognizing the "Blue Hat" certification mark, verifying approval numbers via the SAMR special food information query platform, avoiding "free lecture" marketing traps, and using the national consumer association's smart 315 platform for complaints. The guidelines emphasize that health products are not medicines and cannot replace pharmaceutical treatment.
- **Xicheng District Urban Renewal**: Beijing's Xicheng District implemented a "hutong space micro-renewal" initiative, converting unused边角地 (edge/corner lands) into multi-story parking structures. The pilot project increased parking capacity by approximately 2x on the same land area, added new energy slow-charging spaces, installed noise-reduction equipment, and offered subsidized parking rates for residents. By the reporting date, the district had added 2,052 new parking spaces in 2026.
- **Anhui Social Insurance Reform**: Anhui Province's tax authorities established a "social insurance payment one-stop service" zone across all 118 county-level administrative units. The integrated system combines tax, human resources, and medical insurance services, reducing required documentation by over 40% and cutting average processing time to 3 minutes per transaction.
- **Changsha Courier Service Stations**: Changsha commissioned 611 courier "love stations" (600 municipal-standard + 11 provincial-demonstration) providing rest, hydration, charging, and climate control facilities for delivery workers and outdoor laborers.
- **Gansu Occupational Rehabilitation**: Gansu Province extended occupational injury rehabilitation services to grassroots levels.
- **Xiongan New Area Individual Case**: Resident Li Yuan, originally from Liucun Village in Dacheng Town, transitioned from operating a courier station to opening a yoga studio in Rongdong District after relocating in 2023. She and her partner obtained senior yoga instructor certification in late 2024, opened their studio in November 2025, and now offer free community classes.
- **Zhejiang Elderly Care Event**: Xin'anjiang Subdistrict in Jiande City hosted a Double Ninth Festival celebration with traditional food and activities for elderly residents.

### Ecological Conservation & Green Energy
- **Yunnan Shilin World Heritage Protection**: Shilin Yi Autonomous County implemented a four-tier ecological protection network ("county-bureau-township-village") combining professional patrol teams with local村民巡护 members. The county has completed calibration of 798 boundary marker coordinates and deployed digital monitoring systems. In the first 7 months of 2026, Shilin County received 9.3476 million visitors (+7.63%) with tourism revenue of 11.324 billion yuan (+5.98%). Local tourism employment exceeds 90% local residents; Wukeshu Village generates over 8 million yuan annual collective income from approximately 500,000 annual visitors.
- **Guangxi Beibu Bay Marine Protection**: The Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs designated the Weizhou Island area as China's first Bryde's whale important habitat. The 335.43 km² marine protected area implements an 8.5-month annual fishing ban (December–April专项禁渔叠加 May 1–August 16 South China Sea fishing moratorium). Over 70 individual whales have been identified since monitoring began in 2018.
- **Hunan Bird Migration Protection**: Yanling County established a "Millennium Bird Way" protection mechanism through the Niutou'ao Ecological Police Joint Duty Station, coordinating government forces, social volunteers (炎陵护鸟营), and cross-provincial cooperation during migration season.
- **Meteorological Data Governance**: The China Meteorological Administration promulgated the "Meteorological Data Management Measures," effective November 1, 2026. The framework covers data supply, circulation, security, and supervision. The meteorological sector has shared 12 categories and 100+ data products, aggregated nearly 200,000 industry monitoring stations, and serves 1.3 million users across 21 domestic industries and 153 countries/regions.
- **Shenzhen Green Power Infrastructure**: Southern Power Grid Shenzhen Power Supply Bureau promoted zero-carbon transition initiatives:
  - **Chunhui Substation**: China's first zero-carbon smart science popularization substation, utilizing装配式钢结构, natural ester insulating oil (biodegradable), and mixed-gas insulated equipment reducing GHG emissions by 60% (over 25,000 tons carbon savings). Annual green power generation exceeds 40,000 kWh via wind-solar-storage microgrid.
  - **Virtual Power Plant**: Shenzhen's virtual power plant management platform integrates over 5.1 GW of resources with 1.4 GW dispatchable capacity.
  - **Green Certificate Trading**: The Shenzhen Green Power and Certificate Service Center facilitated 50.31 billion kWh of green power/certificate transactions through May 2026, the highest in the Southern Grid region.
  - **Charging Infrastructure**: 14 supercharging stations supporting "green vehicle charging green electricity," serving over 100,000 new energy vehicle owners with 23 million kWh consumed, equivalent to ~12,200 tons CO₂ reduction.

### International Affairs & Diplomacy
- **Tunisia World Heritage Site**: The UNESCO World Heritage Committee inscribed Sidi Bou Said as Tunisia's 10th World Heritage Site at its 48th session in Busan, South Korea. The town's value lies in the integration of Andalusian-style architecture, urban planning, and natural topography. Protection mechanisms include the 1915 heritage law, a dedicated World Heritage management authority, and a phased protection plan (2025–2030). Key challenges include landslide risk management and sustainable tourism.
- **Sino-European Cooperation Discourse**: Commentary at the 8th Giverny World Forum discussed EU-China economic relations. French media outlets expressed wariness about Chinese technological advancement, while European industry representatives advocated partnership-based cooperation. The article cited IEA's 2026 Electricity Report showing EU energy-intensive industrial electricity prices exceeding US levels by over 2x and Chinese levels by ~50%.
- **WTO Reform Position**: At a WTO special meeting on October 12, 2026, Chinese Permanent Representative Li Yongxia presented China's proposal on WTO reform fundamentals. China emphasized maintaining most-favored-nation (MFN) treatment as a cornerstone principle, opposing selective application or conditionality. The African Group, Africa-Caribbean-Pacific Group, Pakistan, India, and Indonesia expressed support for China's reform-oriented, development-focused approach.
- **Shanghai IP Recognition**: Shanghai received the inaugural WIPO "Innovative City" award.
- **Iran Strait Issue**: Iran stated it is reviewing US positions while maintaining unchanged conditions for strait reopening.
- **Indonesia Wildfire Smoke**: Regional countries enhanced coordination to address recurrent forest fire haze.
- **Saudi Aramco CEO Warning**: Global crude oil inventory approaching "dangerously low" levels.

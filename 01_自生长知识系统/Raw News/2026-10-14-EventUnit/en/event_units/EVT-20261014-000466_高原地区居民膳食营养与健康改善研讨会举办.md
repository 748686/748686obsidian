## Event Overview

On October 13, 2026, the "High-Altitude Region Residents' Dietary Nutrition and Health Improvement Symposium" was held in Lhasa, Tibet Autonomous Region. The event was co-organized by the Institute of Food and Nutrition Development of the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (MARA) and the Tibet Academy of Agriculture and Animal Husbandry Sciences. The symposium focused on evaluating the effectiveness of special projects aimed at improving the dietary nutrition and health of residents in high-altitude areas, sharing experiences, addressing challenges, and outlining future implementation strategies.

The event underscores a systematic approach to public health in high-altitude regions, emphasizing a cyclical model linking food supply, nutrition, health outcomes, consumer behavior, and agricultural production.

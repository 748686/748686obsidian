## Event Overview

On October 13, 2026, the 2nd CASS Youth Academic Conference was held in Beijing. Gao Xiang, President and Party Secretary of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences (CASS), attended the event and delivered an academic report. The conference focused on themes of "Inheritance, Innovation, and Standardization" and included a main forum alongside eight specialized sub-forums covering topics such as AI-assisted research, global civilization studies, and high-quality development. The event also involved the recognition of notable research achievements by CASS scholars since the 20th CPC National Congress and the awarding of honors for youth social science research initiatives.

## Core Facts

- **Event**: 2nd Chinese Academy of Social Sciences (CASS) Youth Academic Conference.
- **Date**: October 13, 2026 (Reported in the press on October 14, 2026).
- **Location**: Beijing, China.
- **Key Figure**: Gao Xiang, President and Party Secretary of CASS, who attended and gave an academic report.
- **Theme**: "Inheritance, Innovation, and Standardization" (传承·创新·规范).
- **Structure**: A main forum and eight sub-forums:
  1. Studying and implementing Xi Jinping Thought on Party Building.
  2. Artificial Intelligence assisting scientific research.
  3. Global civilization research.
  4. Promoting theoretical innovation through the "Two Integrations."
  5. High-quality development.
  6. Comprehensive law-based governance.
  7. Area and country studies.
  8. Integration of science and education.
- **Key Activities**:
  - Release of important research achievements by CASS youth scholars since the 20th CPC National Congress.

- **Organization**: Hosted by CASS; co-organized by the Party Committee of the Directly Affiliated Organs, the Scientific Research Bureau, and the Personnel and Education Bureau of CASS.

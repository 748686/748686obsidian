## Event Overview

This event documents the forty-year career of Li Quanmin (李全民), a rural public welfare film projectionist in Hebi City, Henan Province. Reported by *People's Daily* on October 14, 2026, the article highlights Li's dedication to maintaining public cultural services in rural areas despite technological shifts, market decline, and personal hardships. From starting in 1986 with heavy film equipment to adopting digital systems under national public cultural programs, Li has累计 projected over 12,000 screenings, serving more than 300,000 residents.

## Core Facts

*   **Subject:** Li Quanmin, male, aged 62 as of 2026.
*   **Location:** Hedong Village, Hebi Collection Town, Heshan District, Hebi City, Henan Province, China.
*   **Career Start:** 1986.
*   **Duration:** 40 years of continuous service (as of October 2026).
*   **Total Screenings:** Over 12,000 films.
*   **Beneficiaries:** More than 300,000 people.
*   **Current Responsibilities:** Covers 24 villages (previously 29 construction villages).
*   **Equipment Evolution:**
    *   1986–~1999: Heavy film projectors (approx. 100+ jin / 50+ kg). High failure rate; required manual repair of broken films and sound issues.
    *   Post-2007: Government-issued digital projection equipment (clearer picture, better sound, lower failure rate, subtitles available).
*   **Income Model:**
    *   Pre-2000: Projection work alone was insufficient for livelihood. Opened an agricultural supplies store in 1999 to subsidize projection costs.
    *   Post-2007: Subsidies increased under the "Rural Film Screening Project" (national public cultural service system).
*   **Peak Period:** The 1990s saw the highest attendance, with nearly 400 screenings in a single year and crowds of 300–500 villagers per showing.
*   **Political Status:** Joined the Communist Party of China in October 2025.
*   **Local Cultural Adaptation:** Liaised with the Hebi City Yu Opera Niu School Art Research Institute to produce regional opera films such as *Taihang Hometown Sentiments* (*Taihang Guxiang Qing*) and *New Seven Smart Magistrate* (*Xin Qiqi Zhima Guan*) to better suit elderly rural audiences.

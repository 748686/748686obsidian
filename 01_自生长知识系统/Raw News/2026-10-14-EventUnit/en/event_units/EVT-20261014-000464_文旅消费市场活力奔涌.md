# Event Name
**National Day 2026 Cultural Tourism Consumption Trends: Immersive Experiences, County Tourism, and Night Economy Surge**

## Event Overview
During the National Day holiday in 2026, China’s cultural tourism consumption market demonstrated significant vitality. The market witnessed a concentration of new consumption scenarios, formats, and models, characterized by a shift from simple "check-in" tourism to immersive experiences, a surge in travel to smaller counties ("奔县游"), and a robust night economy that extended into consumption activities. These trends were supported by targeted government policies and subsidy programs aimed at stimulating domestic demand.

## Core Facts
*   **Immersive Tourism Shift**: Consumers are moving from passive sightseeing to active participation.
    *   In Mengzi City, Yunnan Province, the Southwest Associated University (蒙自分校) Memorial Hall introduced a real-scene script tour where tourists act as story participants and interact with historical figures across time.
    *   In Chaotian District, Guangyuan City, Sichuan Province, the Mingyue Gorge scenic area launched a闯关 (challenge/obstacle course) experience allowing tourists to wear Hanfu and carry "documents" to simulate traveling back a millennium on ancient plank roads.
*   **County and Small-City Tourism ("奔县游")**: There is a notable trend of tourists directing traffic toward lower-tier cities.
    *   Meituan data indicates that visits to entertainment locations in cities at the third tier and below grew by **258%** compared to before the National Day holiday.
    *   This growth rate was approximately **1.9 times faster** than that of first- and second-tier cities.
    *   High-star hotel booking hotspots included Lijiang, Dali, Yan’an, Huangshan, and Ili.
*   **Night Economy Expansion**: Consumption has evolved from "night tours" to "night shopping" and integrated evening entertainment.
    *   **Rural Example**: In Nankeng Town, Luxi County, Jiangxi Province, the 34th "Nanxi Cup" basketball tournament attracted over 50,000 local citizens and tourists. This event boosted local snack shop profits by over **200,000 RMB** and generated daily sales of over **5,000 RMB** for street vendors near the court. Additionally, a hot air balloon music carnival was held at the Xianfeng Sanbaoyuan scenic area.
    *   **Urban Example**: In Sucheng District, Suqian City, Jiangsu Province, the upgraded Gubei · 1996 Block became a vibrant nighttime consumption cluster featuring diverse formats such as specialty snacks, trendy dining, stage performances, and leisure entertainment.
*   **Policy Support and Subsidies**:
    *   The fourth batch of **62.5 billion RMB** in ultra-long special sovereign bonds for consumer goods trade-in programs was disbursed just before the holiday, supplemented by local matching subsidies.
    *   During the National Day Culture and Tourism Consumption Month, over **20,000** cultural and tourism events were held nationwide.
    *   More than **310 million RMB** in consumption vouchers and subsidies were issued to consumers.

## Event Conclusion
The National Day 2026 period highlighted a dynamic evolution in China's cultural tourism sector. The market is moving beyond traditional sightseeing toward immersive, experiential, and localized consumption. Key drivers include the popularization of "county tourism," the expansion of the night economy into retail and entertainment, and strong policy support through bond-funded subsidies and voucher distributions. Data from Meituan and reported case studies from provinces including Yunnan, Sichuan, Hunan, Jiangxi, and Jiangsu confirm a broad-based increase in consumption vitality, particularly in lower-tier cities and rural areas.

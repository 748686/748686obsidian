## Event Overview
On October 13, 2026, senior Chinese leadership conducted a series of diplomatic engagements with foreign dignitaries visiting Beijing and hosted domestic political activities. The events reflect China’s diplomatic priorities in Africa, multilateral parliamentary engagement, and its domestic health policy agenda at the start of the “15th Five-Year Plan” period.

The primary activities included:
- State Visit of Botswana President Duma Sydion Boko and meeting with General Secretary Xi Jinping.
- Meeting with Togo’s Minister of the Presidency (referred to as "Minister of State/Chairman of the Council of Ministers" in reports), Faure Gnassingbe.
- Domestic review of the National Health and Wellbeing Conference held on October 12–13, emphasizing "Healthy China 2030" goals.
- Auxiliary diplomatic meetings by other senior officials (Wang Yi, He Lifeng, Li Haixing) and parliamentary exchanges.

## Core Facts

### 1. Xi Jinping Meets Botswana President Duma Sydion Boko
**Date:** October 13, 2026  
**Location:** Great Hall of the People, Beijing  
**Source:** Articles #505, #506, #510

- Botswana President Duma Sydion Boko arrived for an official state visit (October 12–17).
- Xi Jinping and President Boko held talks and announced the upgrade of bilateral relations to a **Comprehensive Strategic Partnership**.
- A joint statement titled *"Joint Statement of the People's Republic of China and the Republic of Botswana on the Establishment of a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership"* was issued.
- Multiple cooperation documents were signed covering: Global Security Initiative implementation, Belt and Road Initiative, green minerals, health/medical cooperation, and human resources.
- Xi emphasized four principles for China-Africa mining cooperation:
  1. African resources belong to Africans.
  2. Combining justice with mutual benefit.
  3. High-quality development orientation.
  4. Fair and open cooperation environment.
- Boko reaffirmed adherence to the One-China Principle, opposition to "Taiwan independence," and support for China’s development path. He praised Xi’s Four Global Initiatives.
- Ceremony details: Welcome ceremony at the North Hall of the Great Hall of the People; 21-gun salute at Tiananmen Square; inspection of the Honor Guard; welcome banquet in the Golden Hall.
- Wang Yi participated in the activities.

### 2. Xi Jinping Meets Togo’s Faure Gnassingbe
**Date:** October 13, 2026  
**Location:** Great Hall of the People, Beijing  
**Source:** Article #506

- Togo’s Minister of the Presidency (Chairman of the Council of Ministers), Faure Gnassingbe, visited China for formal talks.
- Both sides announced the elevation of bilateral relations to a **New Era China-Togo Community with a Shared Future**.
- Xi highlighted:
  - Zero-tariff measures for African countries as a special arrangement to support African自主发展.
  - Encouraging Togo to use the "green channel" for exports to China.
  - Praise for Togo’s promotion of cross-border RMB use.
  - Cooperation in new energy and digital intelligence.
- Xi proposed four principles for constructive participation in resolving African hotspot issues:
  1. African ownership.
  2. Maintenance of fairness and justice.
  3. Promotion of peace through dialogue.
  4. Addressing both symptoms and root causes.
- Faure expressed admiration for China’s modernization achievements and the Chinese governance model.
- Wang Yi participated in the meeting.

### 3. National Health and Wellbeing Conference Review
**Date:** October 14, 2026 (reporting on Oct 12–13 conference)  
**Source:** Article #507

- The National Health and Wellbeing Conference was held in Beijing on October 12–13.
- Xi Jinping attended and delivered a speech.
- The *People’s Daily* commentary emphasized:
  - Goal: Build a "Healthy China" by 2035, with the "15th Five-Year Plan" period being critical for decisive progress.
  - Achievements since the 18th CPC National Congress:
    - Per capita life expectancy reached 79.25 years (2025 data).
    - Infant mortality: 3.8‰.
    - Maternal mortality: 13.4 per 100,000.
    - WHO recognition as a high-performing country in maternal and child health.
  - Global health contributions: Medical teams sent to 59 countries/regions; hospital partnerships with 43 countries; 29 key clinical specialty centers.
- The article underscored the importance of party leadership, public welfare in healthcare, and the integration of traditional and Western medicine.

### 4. Other Diplomatic Activities (October 12–13)
**Sources:** Articles #511–#516

- **He Lifeng** (Vice Premier) met with representatives of the National Committee for US-China Relations on October 12. Referenced Xi’s recent state visit to the US and the agreement to build a "constructive strategic stability relationship based on respect, fairness, and reciprocity."
- **Wang Yi** (State Councilor) met with Khalid bin Mohammed Al Qasimi, Special Envoy for China Affairs to the UAE President, on October 13.
- **Li Haixing** (Director, International Department of the CPC Central Committee) met separately with:
  - Peruvian Senator Second Vice President Chacon.
  - French Presidential Advisor Bonnet.
- **Ba Erte** (Vice Chairman, CPPCC) led a delegation to Suriname (Oct 11–12), meeting President David A. Simpson and Speaker Adhin. Celebrated 50th anniversary of diplomatic ties.
- **Peng Qinghua** (Vice Chairman, NPC Standing Committee) opened the 2026 International Parliamentary Friendly Exchange Forum in Tianjin (Oct 13), themed "Sharing High-Quality Development, Building a Sustainable Future Together." 66 legislators from 52 countries/regions attended.
- **Both Botswana President Boko and Togo’s Faure** laid wreaths at the Monument to the People’s Heroes in Tiananmen Square on October 13.

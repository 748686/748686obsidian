## Core Facts

### 1. Red Tourism and Rural Development in Sichuan
*   **Banyou Village, Ruoergai County, Aba Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture:**
    *   Located at the site where the Red Army's Right Route Army arrived in August 1935 after a 7-day, 7-night march across the grasslands.
    *   The "Brazil Meeting Site Teaching Base" was put into operation in **September 2025**. It utilizes 28 revolutionary sites to create 5 red education routes, 3 green ecological practice routes, and 22特色 (special characteristic) courses.
    *   **Impact:** As of the report date, the base has hosted 73 training classes, 44 inspection/research batches, and 25 study tours,累计 (cumulatively) serving over **17,000 people**.
    *   **Economic Shift:** Banyou Village has developed red tourism to support rural revitalization. A company uniformly operates village collective resources (red stations, auditoriums), spawning homestays, pastoral handicrafts, and folk interactions.
    *   **Results:** In the past two years, 5 new agricultural households and homestays and 6特色餐饮 (specialty catering) shops were added. Deng Haotian, a homestay operator, provides over 20 annual jobs for herders and purchases local yak meat and yogurt. Herder Ben Wangjia reports stable monthly wages compared to unstable herding income.
    *   *Source: Article #520*

*   **Yihai Village, Mianning County, Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture:**
    *   Site of the 1935 "Yihai Alliance" between Liu Bocheng (Central Red Army Pioneer Team Commander) and Yi leader Xiao Yedan.
    *   The scenic area developed red culture corridors, exhibition centers, and a "Red Trail" hiking route, cooperating with travel agencies for immersive red study tours.
    *   **Impact:** The scenic area received **130,000 tourists** in the previous year. Over 30 villagers are employed in the scenic area. Villager Ji Gen Shuomu’s family earns rental income of over 10,000 RMB annually from housing rented to the scenic area.
    *   **Agricultural Integration:** The village cultivates over 10,000 mu of花椒 (Sichuan pepper) and develops特色 fruits (western plums, cherries). Direct sales to tourists and live-streaming have generated over **100,000 RMB** in specialty agricultural product sales this year.
    *   **Collective Economy:** Village collective economic annual income exceeds **300,000 RMB**.
    *   **County-Level Industry:** Mianning County is developing three major特色 industries (rice, cili/rosehip, Mianning ham) and accelerating oil olive, cherry, and beef cattle industries.
    *   *Source: Article #521*

### 2. Ecological Protection in the Northeast Tiger and Leopard National Park
*   **Location:** Dongning City, Heilongjiang Province (China-Russia border).
*   **Facility:** The Dongning Science and Education Exhibition Center opened on **July 29, 2026** (Global Tiger Day). It features interactive displays (e.g., slowed-speed leopard projections for children) and sounds of Amur tigers.
*   **Tourism Data:** Received **24,000 tourists** since opening, including a notable number of Russian visitors.
*   **Conservation Data (Since 2021):**
    *   Northeast Tiger: 310 monitoring events, 9 identified individuals.
    *   Northeast Leopard: 1,313 monitoring events, 18 identified individuals (including 5 cubs).
*   **Notable Individual:** "Wandaishan No. 1," a male tiger rescued and released in April 2021 in Misshan City, Heilongjiang. It has been continuously active in the Dongning area since December 2022. Recent infrared camera footage (September 2026) shows it healthy; overlapping ranges with two female tigers suggest potential mating activity.
*   **Conservation Strategy:** Emphasis on "non-disturbance." Rangers review data to avoid tiger/leopard areas. Infrared cameras are motion-activated to minimize human intrusion. 164 forest rangers and 2,464 village ecological rangers are deployed. 30 supplementary feeding points are set up.
*   **Ecological Theory:** The presence of tigers confirms a robust ecosystem, citing the "1/10 law" of energy transfer. Tigers and leopards act as "umbrella species," protecting the entire forest ecosystem and biodiversity.
*   *Source: Article #522*

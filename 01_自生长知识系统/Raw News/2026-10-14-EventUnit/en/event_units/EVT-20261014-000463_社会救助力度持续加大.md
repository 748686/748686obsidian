# Event Name
**Continued Strengthening of Social Assistance in China During the 14th Five-Year Plan Period**

## Event Overview
On October 14, 2026, *People's Daily* published an article titled "Two Figures Exceeding 20% Witness the Strength of Social Assistance" as part of its "Big Data Observation" series. The report synthesizes Ministry of Civil Affairs data and policy developments to highlight the expansion and refinement of China's social assistance system during the 14th Five-Year Plan (2021–2025). Key themes include the formal implementation of the *Social Assistance Law* on July 1, 2026; a shift toward "material + service" comprehensive assistance models; and the integration of big data monitoring with grassroots verification ("big data + iron feet") to proactively identify beneficiaries.

## Core Facts

**1. Statistical Data on Beneficiaries (as of first half of 2026)**
*   **Urban and Rural Minimum Living Standard Guarantee (Dibao) Recipients:** 39.354 million people.
*   **People in Extreme Poverty (Tekun):** 4.937 million people.
*   **Disabled Persons Receiving Living Subsidies for Difficulties:** 12.027 million people.
*   **Disabled Persons Receiving Nursing Subsidies for Severe Disabilities:** 16.871 million people.

**2. Financial Expenditure and Standard Adjustments (14th Five-Year Plan Period)**
*   **Cumulative Basic Living Assistance Expenditure:** Exceeded 1.38 trillion RMB.
*   **Growth in Dibao Average Standards:**
    *   Urban standards increased by 22.8%.
    *   Rural standards increased by 25%.
*   **Growth in Disabled Person Subsidy Standards:**
    *   Living subsidies for difficult circumstances increased by 34.58%.
    *   Nursing subsidies for severe disabilities increased by 25.09%.

**3. Legislative and Policy Framework**
*   **Social Assistance Law:** The *Social Assistance Law of the People's Republic of China* came into effect on July 1, 2026. It legally establishes dynamic monitoring of low-income populations, expands the scope of assistance to include marginal families and those with rigid expenditure difficulties, and mandates proactive discovery of困难群众 (people in difficulty).
*   **Service-Oriented Assistance:** The Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Ministry of Finance recently issued the *Guiding Opinions on Actively Developing Service-Oriented Social Assistance*. This policy pushes for a transition from single material aid to a "material + service" comprehensive model, providing care, daily life support, and psychological companionship.
*   **Administrative Convenience:** Qualification recognition and application for disability subsidies have achieved "cross-provincial processing" and "full online handling."

**4. Operational Mechanisms**
*   **"Policy Finds People" Model:** Authorities are shifting from passive applications to proactive identification. Systems such as Zhejiang Province’s "Big Assistance Information System" generate alerts for high medical expenses or income drops, triggering automatic verification.
*   **Precedent Cases:** In Yunhe County, Zhejiang Province, 135 households were proactively identified and included in assistance this year through system warnings. A specific case involved a 67-year-old man who became paralyzed after a stroke; through the "one-stop" online service, his family received Dibao benefits within 15 days (1,210 RMB/month) and subsequent medical support.

## Event Conclusion

The *People's Daily* report from October 14, 2026, confirms a significant intensification of China's social assistance efforts during the 14th Five-Year Plan period. Supported by the newly enacted *Social Assistance Law* (effective July 2026), the government has moved beyond simple financial transfers toward a "material + service" model that integrates digital monitoring with grassroots human intervention. Official statistics indicate substantial growth in both the number of beneficiaries (totaling over 60 million across key categories) and the financial value of subsidies, with urban and rural Dibao standards increasing by over 20%. The narrative emphasizes a shift toward proactive identification ("policy finds people") and comprehensive support for vulnerable groups, including the disabled and the elderly.

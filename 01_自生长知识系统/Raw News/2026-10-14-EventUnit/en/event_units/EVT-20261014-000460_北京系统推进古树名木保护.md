## Event Overview

On or around October 13, 2026, the Capital Greening Committee (首都绿化委员会) issued the *"Action Plan for High-Quality Development of Ancient and Famous Tree Protection in the Capital (2026–2030)"* (《首都古树名木保护高质量发展行动方案（2026—2030年）》). This document initiates a comprehensive optimization and upgrade of Beijing’s ancient and famous tree protection management system, establishing specific targets and implementation pathways for the next five years. The initiative aims to advance the scientific, digital, and systematic protection of these trees, with a notable focus on integrating tree conservation with cultural heritage preservation in the city’s core functional areas.

## Core Facts

- **Issuing Authority:** Capital Greening Committee (首都绿化委员会).
- **Document:** *Action Plan for High-Quality Development of Ancient and Famous Tree Protection in the Capital (2026–2030)*.
- **Announcement Date:** Reported as of October 13, 2026 (published in *People’s Daily* on October 14, 2026).
- **Reported By:** Pan Junqiang (潘俊强), *People’s Daily*.
- **Key Actions Initiated:**
  1. Optimization and upgrade of the ancient and famous tree protection management system.
  2. Precision quality improvement and upgrade of protection measures.
  3. Scientific and technological innovation empowerment for protection.
  4. Cultural inheritance and active utilization of ancient and famous trees.
- **Quantitative Targets for 2030:**
  - Establish 1 standardized database for ancient and famous tree resources.
  - Create 10 ancient ecological culture landmarks in the old city.
  - Build 100 ancient tree habitat gardens and ancient tree villages.
  - Implement preventive protection and rescue rejuvenation for over 3,000 ancient and famous trees.
  - Collect and conserve germplasm resources of 100 rare and endangered ancient and famous trees.
- **Methodological Shifts:**
  - Launch of the **third municipal survey** of ancient and famous tree resources.
  - Expansion of survey scope to include trees within **nature reserves**.
  - Establishment of an **annual health check-up mechanism** for millennium-old trees (千年古树).
  - Integration of ancient tree protection with the **Central Axis Cultural Heritage**, historical and cultural blocks, cultural relics, ancient architecture, and traditional courtyards in the capital’s core functional area.

*   **Conference Theme and Focus:** The theme was "Innovation and Perseverance: Key Choices." The event focused on two main lines: the protection and revitalization of the ancient Lingqu Canal ("a pearl of ancient water conservancy architecture in the world") and the demonstration of high-quality development in modern inland water transport via the Pinglu Canal.
*   **Participants:** Representatives from canal cities, research institutions, universities, and industry organizations from over 20 countries attended.
*   **CIIE Imports:** On October 13, the first batch of air-freighted exhibition goods for the 9th China International Import Expo arrived at Shanghai Pudong International Airport from Lyon, France.
*   **Exhibition Goods:** The specific goods were Michelin tires made from 75% renewable and recycled materials, making their Asian debut at the CIIE.
*   **Customs Processing:** The goods were successfully cleared after review by Shanghai Customs.

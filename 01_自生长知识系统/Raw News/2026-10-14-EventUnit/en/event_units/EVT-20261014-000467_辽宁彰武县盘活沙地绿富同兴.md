EVT-20261014-000467: Zhangwu County’s Integration of Desertification Control and Industrial Development (Liaoning Province)

## Event Overview
On October 14, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a feature article in its 7th section (News) titled "Revitalizing Sandy Lands, Achieving Both Green and Wealth" (盘活沙地 绿富同兴). The report details how Zhangwu County in Fuxin City, Liaoning Province, has transitioned from passive sand control to active utilization of sandy land resources. This strategic shift has resulted in significant ecological restoration and the development of diverse industries, including agriculture, silicon sand processing, and eco-tourism.

## Core Facts

**1. Ecological Restoration**
*   **Historical Context:** For over 70 years, residents have engaged in接力治沙 (relayed desertification control). Zhangwu County previously had a desertification area accounting for 96% of its total land area.
*   **Current Status:** Forest coverage has increased from 2.9% to over 30%. Annual sandstorm days have dropped from more than 200 to approximately 5.
*   **Case Study:** Li Dongkui, a 62-year-old forest ranger at the Zhanggutai Forest Farm, has patrolled over 8,500 mu of Korean pine forests for 39 years.

**2. Agricultural Industry ("Sandy Land + Agriculture")**
*   **Sweet Potatoes:** The county has stabilized sweet potato planting at approximately 100,000 mu, with an annual output exceeding 250,000 tons. The product is nicknamed "golden lump in sandy land" (沙地金疙瘩) due to its high quality resulting from sufficient sunlight and large day-night temperature differences.
*   **Crops:** Focus on high-sugar peanuts,特色 (characteristic) fruits and vegetables, and premium coarse grains.
*   **Grain Production:** In the previous year, grain output reached over 2.4 billion jin (1.2 billion kilograms), aided by innovative farming models such as "wheat + soybean" rotation.
*   **Village Economy:** In Alcun Village, under the leadership of Party Secretary Li Mingming (who returned home after graduating from a forestry college), village collective economic income has exceeded 1 million yuan annually for four consecutive years.

**3. Industrial Development ("Sandy Land + Industry")**
*   **Silicon Sand Resources:** Proved reserves are 850 million tons, with prospective reserves exceeding 3 billion tons.
*   **Processing Chain:** The county has established a full-chain green industry system covering mining, fine processing, and waste sand recycling.
*   **Output Value:** The silicon sand industry cluster has an annual output value exceeding 3.3 billion yuan.
*   **Products:** High-end casting sand, permeable building materials sand, 3D printing sand, and petroleum fracturing sand.
*   **Value Add:** Deep processing has increased the value of fine sand by more than 10 times compared to simple sieving and low-price sales.

**4. Eco-Tourism ("Sandy Land + Tourism")**
*   **Attraction:** Delige'er Grassland Scenic Area in Dade Town transforms former barren sandy areas into scenic landscapes.
*   **Visitor Numbers:** In 2025, the county received over 1.1 million tourists.
*   **Economic Impact:** Integrated tourism, research travel, and health/wellness leisure industries have provided new income paths. Villagers, such as Liu Jun from Huanghua Village, report annual employment incomes exceeding 100,000 yuan.

## Event Conclusion
Liaoning Province's Zhangwu County has successfully transformed its ecological劣势 (disadvantage) of vast sandy lands into an economic advantage through a diversified strategy of "Sand Control + Industry." By leveraging natural endowments for specialized agriculture (sweet potatoes, peanuts), developing a high-value silicon sand processing cluster, and promoting eco-tourism, the county has achieved both environmental restoration (forest coverage >30%) and economic growth. The case illustrates a shift from passive ecological protection to active resource utilization, serving as a notable example of rural revitalization and green development reported by the *People's Daily*.

## Event Overview
On October 5, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a comprehensive multi-page special covering the mid-point of China's National Day holiday (Golden Week). The reporting synthesizes domestic consumer data, cultural tourism trends, political commemoration of the Long March's 90th anniversary, international sports results (the closing of the 20th Asian Games), agricultural modernization, and Belt and Road Initiative infrastructure projects. The event represents a coordinated media narrative highlighting economic stability, cultural confidence, and national achievement during a key patriotic holiday period.

Trade-in programs for consumer goods drove sales of 19.63 billion RMB, benefiting 3.483 million person-times (Article 384).

### 2. 20th Asian Games Closing (Nagoya, Japan)
*   **Event:** The 20th Asian Games closed on the evening of October 4, 2026, at Nagoya, Aichi Prefecture (Article 387).
*   **China's Performance:** The Chinese delegation won 169 gold, 89 silver, and 83 bronze medals (341 total), ranking first in the gold medal tally for the 12th consecutive Asian Games. The gold count set a new high for China at an Asian Games held outside its borders (Article 387).
*   **Records:** Chinese athletes broke 8 world records, 11 Asian records, and 22 Asian Games records (Article 387).
*   **Notable Athletes:**
    *   **Bi Kun:** Won gold in windsurfing iQFOiL class and served as the flagbearer at the closing ceremony (Article 395). He is the only athlete to win gold in this event at three consecutive Asian Games (2018, 2023, 2026) (Article 395).

    *   **Zhang Zhanshuo:** 19-year-old swimmer won 7 gold medals, tying the record for most golds by a single athlete at one Asian Games (Article 387).
    *   **Chen Yujie:** 17-year-old became Asia's youngest female "100m sprint queen" (Article 387).
    *   **Men's Football Team:** Reached the podium for the first time in 28 years, achieving the best result under the U-23 age limit rule (Article 387).

### 3. Red Tourism and Long March 90th Anniversary
*   **Commemoration:** 2026 marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March (Article 385, 389).
*   **Exhibition:** The "Great Expedition – Theme Exhibition Commemorating the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March" opened at the Chinese People's Revolutionary Military Museum on September 25, 2026 (Article 389).
    *   **Scale:** ~3,300 sqm exhibition area, featuring 357 photo/chart items, 1,061 artifact/literature items (over 300 displayed for the first time) (Article 389).
    *   **Key Artifacts:** A half belt eaten by Xiao Zhaozhong (Zhou Enlai's bodyguard) during the grassland crossing; Chen Shuxiang's sculpture with the inscription "Shed the last drop of blood for the Soviet"; a 1.5-meter recruitment height mark (resembling a Han Yang造的 rifle + bayonet) (Article 389).
    *   **Status:** Will be retained as a permanent basic exhibit due to high public demand (Article 389).
*   **Zunyi Case Study:** Zunyi's "Red Army Street" and the 1935 Block (opened in 2025 for the 90th anniversary of the Zunyi Conference) formed a 15-minute cultural tourism ecosystem. In 2025, the block received over 2 million visitors, and 140+ merchant households increased income steadily (Article 388).

### 4. Smart Agriculture in Heilongjiang
*   **Context:** Heilongjiang Province is advancing smart agriculture to ensure national food security (Article 390).
*   **Jiamusi National Agricultural Hi-Tech Demonstration Zone:**
    *   **Breeding:** AI algorithms reduced rice breeding cycles from 5–8 years to 3–5 years (Article 390).
    *   **Platform:** The "Jiajia Nong" (佳家农) APP, developed with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, serves over 60,000 farmers across 7 million mu, achieving ~3% yield increase and ~10% reduction in input costs (Article 390).
*   **Binhua County:** Custom agriculture cooperative signed contracts worth 33.18 million RMB, increasing per-capita income for members by 2,300 RMB (Article 390).
*   **Fujin City:** Variable fertilization maps using satellite remote sensing and drone monitoring reduced base fertilizer use by 10% while increasing yield by over 3% (Article 390).
*   **Mechanization:** Crop mechanization rate exceeds 99%, ranking first nationally for multiple years; drone保有量 (保有量) ranks first nationally (Article 390).

### 5. Belt and Road Initiative: Saudi Arabia Natural Gas Project
*   **Project:** Sinopec Engineering (Group) Company (石工建) is constructing Phase III of the Saudi National Natural Gas Pipeline Network ("East-West Gas Transportation Artery") (Article 393).
*   **Scope:** Sections 6 and 7, totaling ~700 km, aimed at boosting gas supply to western Saudi Arabia (King Abdullah Economic City and power stations) (Article 393).
*   **Progress:** Over 80% complete, with >678 km of pipeline welding finished (Article 393).
*   **Technology:** Use of domestic automatic welding machines; welding time optimized to <15 minutes per joint (vs. 18 minutes for competitors) (Article 393).
*   **Local Impact:** Trained over 5,000 local standardized pipeline construction skills personnel; achieved "zero accidents" throughout construction; won Sinopec's "Management Innovation Award" (Article 393).

### 6. Rural Revitalization: Pomegranate Industry in Xinjiang
*   **Location:** Yecheng County, Kashgar Prefecture, Xinjiang (Article 391).
*   **Scale:** Planting area reached 8,301 mu, annual output >2,300 tons (Article 391).
*   **Value Chain:** Shift from raw fruit sales to deep processing (pomegranate juice, wine) using a 5-million-RMB production line funded by Shanghai aid (Article 391).
*   **Tourism Integration:** Development of a pomegranate-themed scenic area with water parks and glamping, driving rural tourism revenue (Article 391).

### 7. Institutional Reform: Marriage Registration
*   **Policy:** Implementation of the revised "Marriage Registration Regulations" allowing mainland residents to register marriage nationwide regardless of household registration (hukou) (Article 397).
*   **Trends:** Rise of "travel + marriage registration" model.
    *   **Saiwu Lake (Xinjiang):** Slogan "520km from Urumqi, 2073m altitude, 1314 sq km" (homophones for "I love you, love you deeply, forever"). Processed >20,000 couples since April 2024, 80% from outside Xinjiang (Article 397).
    *   **Changbaishan (Jilin):** Capacity increased from 30 to 180 couples/day after expanding facilities in February 2026 (Article 397).
    *   **Shaoxing (Zhejiang):** Marriage registration center moved to Huangjiu Town (Yellow Wine Town) in May 2026, creating a "sweet economy" chain including boat weddings and cultural tourism (Article 397).

### 8. Industrial Tourism Growth
*   **China FAW (Changchun):** China FAW Museum opened on July 21, 2026. Auto industry tourism route (launched July 2024) has received ~400,000 visitors in two years (Article 401).
*   **Jiangnan Shipyard (Shanghai):** Accumulated >330,000 visitors since 2019; features tours of the Fujian (Fujian) aircraft carrier construction site (Article 401).
*   **Hainan Commercial Spacecraft Launch Site (Wenchang):** Has achieved 21 consecutive successful launches since Nov 2024. "Monthly launches" have turned sporadic viewing into scheduled tourism. Longlou Town earned 60 million RMB in tourism revenue in 2025 from >400,000 visitors (Article 401).

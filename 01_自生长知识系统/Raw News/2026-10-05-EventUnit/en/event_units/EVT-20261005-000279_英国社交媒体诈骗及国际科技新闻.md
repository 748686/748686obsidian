- Fuzhou’s annual municipal cultural relic protection funding increased to 15 million yuan starting in 2024.
- During the 14th Five-Year Plan period (2021–2025), Fujian cumulatively carried out over 1,200 cultural relic protection and restoration projects.
- Fujian has 12 historical and cultural cities, 63 historical and cultural blocks, 200 historical and cultural towns/villages, and 1,193 traditional villages with completed protection planning. The province has over 40,000 immovable cultural relics.
- Fujian Province has piloted a “cultural relic insurance + service” model, investing over 30 million yuan. More than 30% of national-level key cultural relic protection units and over 20% of provincial-level units are covered.
- Since 2022, Fujian has held an annual Houguan Forum in Fuzhou to explore the spiritual connotations and educational value of Houguan culture, linked to moral education at the Fuzhou University City.
- Quanzhou dangling-string puppetry, with over 1,300 years of history, performed 140 shows during the 2026 National Day holiday (7 days). Performances are sold out全年.
- Fujian has created immersive cultural tourism projects including *Moon Over Wuyi* and *Return to Southern Fujian*.
- During the 14th Five-Year Plan period, 19 works from Fujian won the “Five Ones Project” Award, and 64 individuals/works received national awards such as the “Wenhua Prize” and “Plum Blossom Prize.”

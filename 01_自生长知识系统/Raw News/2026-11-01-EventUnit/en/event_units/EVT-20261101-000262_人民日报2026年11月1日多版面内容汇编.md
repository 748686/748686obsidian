## Core Facts

**1. Political Leadership and Party Governance**
*   The Party's 20th Central Committee Fifth Plenary Session was held, adopting the *Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Continuously Advancing Comprehensive Strict Governance of the Party in All Respects* [ARTICLE #363].
*   The session emphasized the "Two Answers" for escaping the historical cycle of rise and fall: "let the people supervise the government" and "self-revolution" [ARTICLE #363].
*   The Plenary Session outlined the "Seven Adherences" principles for comprehensive strict governance [ARTICLE #363].
*   Central and state organs, as well as people's organizations, have launched enthusiastic study campaigns to implement the spirit of the Fifth Plenary Session, aiming to act as pioneers ("first方阵") in implementing Party discipline [ARTICLE #371].
*   The 21st National Congress of the CPC is scheduled to be held in the second half of 2027 [ARTICLE #371].

**2. Yellow River Governance (80th Anniversary Commemoration)**
*   The article marks the 80th anniversary of the Communist Party of China leading the people in governing the Yellow River, noting that governance began with the establishment of the Yellow River Old Course Management Committee in the Jiluyu Area in 1946 [ARTICLE #364].
*   **Achievements:** Since the new era, the Yellow River has achieved continuous non-interruption for 27 years. Water and sediment regulation projects have discharged 3.74 billion tons of sediment into the sea, rewriting the history of downstream riverbed siltation [ARTICLE #364].
*   **Ecological Data:** By 2025, the累计治理 (cumulative governance) of soil erosion in the Yellow River Basin reached 290,000 square kilometers. The Wuliangsu Lake now hosts 263 wild bird species [ARTICLE #364].
*   **Economic Impact:** Preliminary calculations indicate that 80 years of governance and protection of the Yellow River have generated cumulative economic benefits of 24.2 trillion yuan [ARTICLE #364].
*   **Future Projects:** Construction of the Yellow River Guixian Water Conservancy Hub is progressing toward a dam closure target of 2027 [ARTICLE #364].

**3. Internet and AI Governance**
*   General Secretary Xi Jinping raised "questions of the times" at the opening ceremony of the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference: how to coexist with thinking machines, ensure algorithmic security, match governance to ethical challenges, and achieve universal benefit amidst growing divides [ARTICLE #365].
*   The article asserts: "If we cannot pass the test of the internet, we cannot pass the test of long-term governance" and "To realize Chinese modernization, we must pass this internet test" [ARTICLE #365].
*   The Cybersecurity Law was significantly amended on October 28, 2025, to address AI development needs [ARTICLE #365].
*   In May-June 2025, during the drafting of the 15th Five-Year Plan, over 3.113 million suggestions were received from netizens [ARTICLE #365].

**4. Red Army Long March (90th Anniversary)**
*   The Central Publicity Department organized the theme interview activity "Continuing the Long March Spirit, Forging Ahead on the Path to Rejuvenation—Reporters Retrace the Long March Road," which concluded on October 31, 2026 [ARTICLE #366].
*   On September 6, 2026, the G3998 Fuxing High-Speed Train departed from Ruijin (Jiangxi), marking the first time the departure and destination points of the Central Red Army's Long March were connected by direct high-speed rail [ARTICLE #366].
*   The high-speed rail connection takes approximately 11 hours and 32 minutes [ARTICLE #366].
*   In Huaihua (Hunan), a node of the New International Land-Sea Trade Corridor, the port shipped 1,051 freight trains worth approximately 7.9 billion yuan in the first three quarters of the year [ARTICLE #366].
*   Luochuan (Yan'an) exported 3,307.09 tons of apples with a value of 29.2459 million yuan in the current year; total output in 2025 was 1.1685 million tons [ARTICLE #366].

*   **Switzerland:** China and Switzerland signed the Protocol to Upgrade the Free Trade Agreement on October 30, 2026, in Beijing [ARTICLE #375].
*   **Germany:** Xi Jinping met with German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier on October 30, 2026 [ARTICLE #373].

*   **World Cities Day:** Vice Premier He Lifeng attended the 2026 World Cities Day Global Main Venue Event in Fuzhou on October 31, 2026, and met with UN-Habitat Executive Director Maimunah Mohd Sharif [ARTICLE #376].

**6. Science and Agriculture**
*   **Aerospace Rice:** A new variety, "HuaHang XiangYinZhen" (华航香银针), was developed by researchers at South China Agricultural University. It has a yield of 723.5 kg per 100-mu demonstration field and is resistant to rice blast and lodging. Dongguan Heyuan has planted 50,000 mu of aerospace rice [ARTICLE #369].
*   **Shenzhen Hetao Academy:** The academy, co-built by 31 mainland and 6 Hong Kong/Macao universities, completed the full-parameter post-training of a 1.6-trillion-parameter large model (DeepSeek-V4 series) using Ascend 910C domestic computing power in June 2026 [ARTICLE #380].

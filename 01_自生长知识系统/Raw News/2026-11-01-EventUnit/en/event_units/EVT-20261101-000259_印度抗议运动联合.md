Indian Protest Movements Unite: New Challenge for Modi Government

## Event Overview

On November 1, 2026, multiple protest movements in India merged into a coordinated front, presenting a significant new challenge to the Modi government. This development was triggered by the failure of India's space mission (*Chandrayaan-4* / GSAT-N2), which reignited public anger over unemployment and agricultural distress.

## Core Facts

- **Date of Event:** November 1, 2026.
- **Key Development:** Various protest groups across India united their efforts, combining demonstrations against agricultural policies with broader anti-government sentiment.
- **Immediate Catalyst:** The failure of India's space mission (referred to in sources as *Chandrayaan-4* and GSAT-N2) served as a "spark" that intensified existing frustrations regarding the economy and farmers' livelihoods.
- **Government Response:** Prime Minister Narendra Modi stated that the administration would work to resolve farmers' problems, framing the situation as a challenge of leadership.
- **Nature of Opposition:** Observations suggest the opposition is no longer limited to traditional political rivals but has evolved into a broader social discontent involving farmers and general citizens facing economic hardships.

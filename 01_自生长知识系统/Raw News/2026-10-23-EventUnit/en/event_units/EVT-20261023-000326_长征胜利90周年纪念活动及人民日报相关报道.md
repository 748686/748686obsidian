## Core Facts

**The Commemoration Ceremony:**
- **Date:** October 22, 2026.
- **Location:** Great Hall of the People, Beijing.
- **Key Figure:** Xi Jinping delivered the principal address.
- **Attendees:** The ceremony was attended by Party, military, and civilian representatives.

**Xi Jinping's Speech Content:**
- **Historical Recap:** The speech reviewed the Long March (October 1934–October 1936), describing it as a "revolutionary feat that shook the world" and an "heroic epic." It cited statistics: the Red Army traversed over ten provinces, covered 25,000 li (approx. 12,500 km), crossed nearly 100 rivers, climbed snow-capped mountains, crossed grasslands, fought over 600 battles, and defeated over one million enemy troops.
- **Significance:** The speech asserted that the Long March victory was inevitable and蕴含 (contained) the "winning之道 (way)" of the Party and the army. It emphasized four key factors for victory:
    1. Reliance on the Party's correct leadership (highlighting the Zunyi Conference in January 1935 as a turning point that established Mao Zedong's leadership).
    2. Reliance on firm revolutionary will and belief.
    3. Reliance on independence, self-reliance, and seeking truth from facts.
    4. Reliance on the support of the broad masses of the people (citing stories such as "Half a Quilt" and "Yihai Alliance").
- **Great Long March Spirit:** Defined as placing the fundamental interests of the nation above all else, maintaining firm ideals and beliefs, fearing no hardship or sacrifice, adhering to independence and实事求是 (seeking truth from facts), maintaining overall大局 (the big picture) and discipline, and relying closely on the people.
- **"Five Musts" for the New Era:** Xi proposed five requirements for carrying forward the Long March spirit and walking the new Long March road:
    1. Must adhere to the Party's ideals, beliefs, and purpose.
    2. Must unswervingly promote high-quality development and meet people's growing needs for a better life.
    3. Must courageously engage in great struggles with many new historical features.
    4. Must accelerate the building of the people's military into a world-class force.
    5. Must adhere to Party leadership and strengthen Party building.
- **Conclusion:** The speech called for unity around the CPC Central Committee with Xi Jinping at the core, urging the nation to create new brilliance on the new Long March road toward national rejuvenation.

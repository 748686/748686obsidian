# Event Name
**Chinese Political and Legislative Update: Premier Li Qiang's Return and NPC Standing Committee Agenda**

## Event Overview
On October 22–23, 2026, significant political and legislative activities took place in Beijing. Premier Li Qiang returned to Beijing following an official overseas visit. Concurrently, Zhao Leji, Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (NPC), presided over the 74th meeting of the Council of Chairpersons. This meeting set the agenda and schedule for the 25th session of the 14th NPC Standing Committee, scheduled for November 2–6, 2026. The upcoming session will review a broad range of legislative drafts, including laws related to artificial intelligence cooperation, judicial procedures, finance, and environmental protection.

## Core Facts
*   **Return of Premier Li Qiang:** Premier Li Qiang concluded an overseas visit and returned to Beijing. This event was reported in the *People's Daily* on October 23, 2026 (Page 01).
*   **NPC Council of Chairpersons Meeting:** The 74th meeting of the Council of Chairpersons of the 14th NPC Standing Committee was held on the afternoon of October 22, 2026, at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing.
*   **Presiding Official:** Zhao Leji, Chairman of the NPC Standing Committee, presided over the meeting.
*   **Legislative Session Schedule:** The meeting decided that the 25th session of the 14th NPC Standing Committee will convene from November 2 to November 6, 2026, in Beijing.
*   **Proposed Legislative Agenda:** The Council of Chairpersons recommended that the November session review the following drafts and reports:
    *   Draft Law on Prosecutorial Public Interest Litigation.
    *   Draft Law on Antarctic Activities and Environmental Protection.
    *   Revised Draft Water Law.
    *   Draft Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law.
    *   Draft State-Owned Assets Law.
    *   Draft Financial Law.
    *   Proposal to revise the Criminal Procedure Law.
    *   Revised Draft Renewable Energy Law.
    *   Revised Draft Law on the Administration of Tax Collection.
    *   Amendments to 15 other laws, including the Emergency Response Law.
    *   **AI-Specific Item:** A proposal to approve the "Agreement on the Establishment of the World AI Cooperation Organization."
    *   Reports on financial work (2025) and state-owned asset management (2025).
    *   Reports on the implementation of the Food Security Assurance Law, Hainan Free Trade Port Law, and Accessible Environment Construction Law.
*   **Attendees:** Vice Chairpersons Li Hongzhong, Wang Dongming, Xiao Jie, Zheng Jianbang, Ding Zhongli, Hao Mingjin, Cai Daping, He Wei, Wu Weihua, Tie Ning, Peng Qinghua, Zhang Qingwei, Luosang Jiangcun, and Xu Kekelite Zha'ke'er attended the meeting. Liu Qi, Secretary-General of the NPC Standing Committee, reported on the agenda and schedule.

# Event Name

The 24th China International Equipment Manufacturing Expo (CIME) held in Shenyang, Liaoning Province.

## Event Overview

From September 1 to September 4, 2026, the 24th China International Equipment Manufacturing Expo was held at the Shenyang International Exhibition Center in Shenyang, Liaoning Province. Themed "Intelligent New Equipment · New Quality Productive Forces," the event served as an international industrial platform for full-chain display, multi-dimensional exchange, and high-level cooperation.

The expo marks a significant evolution for Shenyang, a historic old industrial base, shifting its manufacturing identity from "manufacturing" to "intelligent manufacturing." It also highlights the city's strategic pivot to become a new hub for manufacturing cooperation in Northeast Asia. The event is part of the third Shenyang Manufacturing Economic and Trade Activity Week, which integrated the expo with trade matchmaking, technology exchange, and investment promotion activities.

## Core Facts

*   **Dates:** September 1–4, 2026.
*   **Location:** Shenyang International Exhibition Center, Shenyang, Liaoning Province.
*   **Theme:** Intelligent New Equipment · New Quality Productive Forces.
*   **Scale:** Exhibition area of 100,000 square meters.
*   **Participants:** Over 1,000 units participated, including Fortune 500 companies, multinational corporations, international renowned machine tool manufacturers, domestic equipment manufacturing enterprises, and research institutes.
*   **Exhibits:** Coverage included industrial mother machines, industrial robots and automation systems, new energy materials, new generation information technology, and new intelligent equipment with independent intellectual property rights.
*   **Growth:** The number of participating enterprises increased by 11% compared to the previous session.
*   **Online Presence:** The online cloud exhibition platform hosted over 12,000 resident enterprises, enabling all-weather supply and demand matching.
*   **International Participation:** Overseas participation reached a record high, with over 150 overseas delegation groups attending.

The 24th China International Equipment Manufacturing Expo, held in Shenyang from September 1-4, 2026, successfully demonstrated the city's transition from a traditional heavy industrial base to a hub for intelligent manufacturing and innovation.

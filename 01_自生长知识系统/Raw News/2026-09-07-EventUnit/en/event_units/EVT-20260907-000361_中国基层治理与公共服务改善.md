## Event Overview

On September 7, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Page 01) published two distinct reports detailing improvements in grassroots governance and public service delivery in China. The events focus on (1) the integration of gig economy workers, specifically couriers, into urban governance systems in Beijing, and (2) the下沉 (sinking/downward shift) of medical resources to rural areas in Sichuan Province through the consolidation of county-level medical consortia. Both initiatives are framed within the context of implementing directives from the Communist Party of China regarding people-centric governance.

## Core Facts

**1. Beijing: Integration of New Employment Groups into Urban Governance**
*   **Demographics:** Beijing has approximately 100,000 courier/express delivery workers, of whom 65,000 are frontline delivery staff ([Article #449](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/07/content_30179382.html)).
*   **Policy Driver:** The initiative follows Xi Jinping's directive to pay attention to new business formats and improve ideological guidance and cohesion services for new employment groups.
*   **Mechanisms:**
    *   **"Urban Responders" (Chengshi Xiangyingren):** Xicheng District has incorporated 10 types of new employment groups into this team, focusing on six scenarios including safety monitoring and elderly care.
    *   **"Dual Station Master" System:** ChaoYang District established a partnership where enterprise station masters and community party members jointly manage hubs. This addresses worker needs (rest, meals) while mobilizing workers for community reporting.
*   **Statistical Outcomes (2025):**
    *   Xicheng District: Over 2,000 additional couriers registered with communities; 17,000 clues reported regarding issues like damaged manhole covers and flying wires.
    *   ChaoYang District: 690+ "Dual Station Masters" selected across 400+ outlets; 800+ appeals responded to.
    *   City-wide: Over 2,300 couriers participated in friendly scene construction; over 4,000 served as "civilization propagandists"; volunteer services reached over 6,000 residents.
*   **Political Representation:** Gao Feng, a Meituan rider and Beijing Municipal People's Congress representative, utilized party branch meetings to collect suggestions (e.g., building numbering, accessible ramps, rental guarantees) which were incorporated into policy measures.

**2. Sichuan: Downward Shift of Medical Resources**
*   **Location:** Bazhong City (Pingchang County, Tongjiang County, Nanjiang County, Enyang District), located in the northern part of Sichuan province in the Chuanyue Revolutionary Base Area.
*   **Mechanism:** Promotion of "Tightly-knit County-level Medical Consortia" (Yi Gong Ti).
    *   Pingzhou General Hospital (led by Pingchang County People's Hospital) encompasses 20 former township health centers.
    *   Implementation of an information sharing platform enabling "Grassroots examination, county-level diagnosis, mutual recognition of results."
*   **Operational Changes:**
    *   Senior physicians (intermediate title and above) from county hospitals settle in township branches for permanent service and training.
    *   Deployment of smart chronic disease management systems for remote monitoring (e.g., blood pressure, blood sugar).
*   **Statistical Outcomes (2025):**
    *   Bazhong City's grassroots diagnosis and treatment volume accounted for 68.18%, ranking among the highest in Sichuan Province.
*   **Future Goals:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" period aims to further ensure "common illnesses are solved at the city/county level, and daily illnesses are solved at the grassroots level."

## Event Conclusion

The September 7, 2026, coverage in *People's Daily* highlights a coordinated national strategy to strengthen grassroots governance and public service provision through two distinct regional models. In Beijing, the strategy involves co-opting and supporting the gig economy workforce (couriers) to act as extensions of the state's urban governance and social safety nets. In Sichuan's mountainous regions, the strategy focuses on structural integration of healthcare resources (medical consortia) and digital infrastructure to overcome geographical barriers to essential services. Both initiatives align with the central directive of "people-centric" development and shifting resources/workflows downward to the county and community levels.

# EVT-20260907-000362: First Direct High-Speed Train Between Ruijin and Yan'an

## Event Overview

On September 6, 2026, the first direct Fuxing high-speed train G3998 departed from Ruijin Station in Jiangxi Province, arriving at Yan'an Station in Shaanxi Province. The service connects the Central Red Army's Long March departure point with its final destination, spanning approximately 1,800 kilometers in 11 hours and 32 minutes. This marks the first direct high-speed rail link between these two historically significant cities associated with the Long March.

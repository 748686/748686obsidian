# Event Name

**Revision and Promulgation of the Regulation on Emergency Response and Investigation of Power Safety Accidents (State Council Decree No. 845)**

## Event Overview

On September 7, 2026, *People's Daily* published the full text of the revised "Regulation on Emergency Response and Investigation of Power Safety Accidents" (《电力安全事故应急处置和调查处理条例》), issued as State Council Decree No. 845. The revision was approved during the 94th Executive Meeting of the State Council on August 21, 2026, and signed by Premier Li Qiang on August 30, 2026. The regulation is scheduled to come into effect on January 1, 2027.

This event marks a significant update to China's legal framework for power grid safety, superseding the original 2011 version (State Council Decree No. 599). While Source Article #445 contains the complete legal text, Source Article #446 was retrieved from the same publication date but pertains to an unrelated topic regarding RV manufacturing in Rongcheng and is excluded from this synthesis.

## Core Facts

**1. Legal Authority and Timeline**
*   **Decree:** State Council Decree No. 845.
*   **Approval Date:** August 21, 2026 (94th Executive Meeting of the State Council).
*   **Promulgation Date:** August 30, 2026.
*   **Effective Date:** January 1, 2027.
*   **Publisher:** *People's Daily*, Page 13, dated September 7, 2026.

**2. Legislative Purpose (Article 1)**
The regulation aims to strengthen emergency response to power safety accidents, standardize investigation and handling procedures, control and mitigate damage, and ensure the safe operation of power systems and reliable power supply.

**3. Definition and Classification of Accidents (Articles 2-3)**
*   **Definition:** An accident affecting the safe/stable operation of the power system or normal power supply during power production or grid operation.
*   **Classification:** Four levels: Extraordinarily Serious, Major, Larger, and General.
*   **Standards:** Grade classification standards are listed in the attached schedule. Adjustments to these standards must be proposed by the energy department under the State Council and approved by the State Council.
*   **Special Clause:** For areas supplied by a single transmission line or substation, specific classification standards for outages caused by failures of that single asset are to be separately formulated by the energy department.

**4. Regulatory Responsibility (Article 4)**
*   The energy department under the State Council is responsible for strengthening supervision, establishing emergency/investigation systems, and organizing/participating in investigations.
*   Other departments and local governments cooperate within their statutory authorities.

**5. Obligations of Entities (Article 5)**
*   **Power Enterprises/Users/Other Grid-connected Entities:** Must comply with safety regulations, obey unified dispatch, implement preventive measures in planning/construction/operation, and inspect for hidden dangers.
*   **Dispatch Institutions:** Must strictly conduct grid operation control according to laws and regulations.
*   **Important Power Users:** Must configure self-provided emergency power sources as required.

**6. Accident Reporting Procedures (Articles 8-11)**
*   **Immediate Reporting:** Personnel on site report to plant/grid dispatch/unit heads; unit heads report immediately to the local dispatch agency and county-level emergency management department.
*   **Specific Reports:**
    *   Thermal plants must also report to heating management departments if heat supply is affected.
    *   Hydropower plants must report to water authorities if dam safety is at risk.
    *   Provincial-level+ dispatch agencies must immediately report to the State Council energy department or dispatched regulatory agency.
*   **Prohibitions:** Late reporting, concealment, and false reporting are prohibited.
*   **Content:** Time, location, unit, brief process, equipment damage, load loss/generation loss, preliminary cause, and measures taken.

**7. Emergency Response Measures (Articles 12-20)**
*   **Preparedness:** Power enterprises must formulate emergency rescue plans, file them with regulatory agencies, and conduct regular drills.
*   **Immediate Action:** Entities must take emergency measures to prevent systemic collapse. Dispatch institutions can order the start/stop of generation equipment or adjust grid modes.
*   **Islanding/Rollover:** In case of total regional system failure, dispatch institutions must determine restoration schemes, potentially utilizing independent startup capabilities or external power sources.
*   **Priority Restoration:** Important plant auxiliary power, key transmission equipment, backbone grids, important users, and key cities/regions take priority.
*   **Government Role:** Local governments must organize security, hazard elimination, rescue/resettlement, traffic/communication保障, and supply of emergency materials.

**8. Investigation and Handling Authority (Article 21)**
*   **Extraordinarily Serious/Major:** Investigated by the State Council or authorized departments (Energy Department).
*   **Larger/General:** Investigated by dispatched energy regulatory agencies. The State Council energy department may investigate larger accidents if deemed necessary.
*   **Delegation:** Dispatched agencies may commission the accident unit to investigate general accidents that did not cause power outages to users.

**9. Investigation Timelines (Article 23)**
*   **Extraordinarily Serious/Major:** 60 days (extendable by up to 60 days).
*   **Larger/General:** 45 days (extendable by up to 45 days).

**10. Legal Liability and Fines (Articles 27-30)**
*   **Unit Fines (Article 29):**
    *   General: 300,000 – 1,000,000 RMB.
    *   Larger: 1,000,000 – 2,000,000 RMB.
    *   Major: 2,000,000 – 10,000,000 RMB.
    *   Extraordinarily Serious: 10,000,000 – 20,000,000 RMB.
    *   *Aggravated circumstances:* 2x to 5x the above amounts.
*   **Principal Offender Fines (Article 30):** Based on previous year's income:
    *   General: 40%.
    *   Larger: 60%.
    *   Major: 80%.
    *   Extraordinarily Serious: 100%.
*   **Other Responsible Personnel:** 20% – 50% of previous year's income.
*   **Obstruction Penalties (Article 28):** Fines of 1,000,000 – 5,000,000 RMB for the unit; 60%-100% of income for individuals; criminal liability for serious offenses.
*   **Career Ban (Article 31):** Managers receiving dismissal or criminal penalties cannot serve as principals of any production/business unit for 5 years.

**11. Coordination with Other Laws (Articles 35-36)**
*   If an accident causes casualties or direct economic loss, the higher severity level between this Regulation and the "Regulation on the Reporting and Investigation of Production Safety Accidents" applies.
*   If the accident constitutes a major/extraordinarily serious production safety accident (based on casualties), the "Regulation on the Reporting and Investigation of Production Safety Accidents" governs the investigation.
*   Nuclear power plant accidents follow the "Regulation on Emergency Management of Nuclear Power Plant Accidents."

## Event Conclusion

On September 7, 2026, the *People's Daily* officially published the revised **Regulation on Emergency Response and Investigation of Power Safety Accidents** (State Council Decree No. 845). Revised at the 94th Executive Meeting of the State Council on August 21, 2026, and signed by Premier Li Qiang on August 30, 2026, the regulation establishes a rigorous legal framework for power safety management in China. It defines four levels of power safety accidents, mandates specific reporting hierarchies involving energy, emergency management, and specialized departments (heating/water), and outlines emergency response priorities. The regulation introduces substantial penalties, including fines up to 20 million RMB for entities and up to 100% of annual income for principals, alongside a 5-year ban on executive roles for serious offenders. It harmonizes with the *Production Safety Accident Reporting and Investigation Regulation* and takes effect on **January 1, 2027**, repealing the 2011 version (Decree No. 599).

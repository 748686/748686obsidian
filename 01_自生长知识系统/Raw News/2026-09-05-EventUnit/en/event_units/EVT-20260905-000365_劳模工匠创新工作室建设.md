# Event Name
Funding and Expansion of National Model Worker and Craftsman Innovation Studios (2026-2030)

## Event Overview
On September 4, 2026, the All-China Federation of Trade Unions (ACFTU) issued a notice announcing support for the construction of 600 national model worker and craftsman innovation studios for the year 2026. Each studio will receive a subsidy of 100,000 yuan. This initiative aims to stimulate employees' innovation and creativity and enhance the示范引领 (demonstration and guidance) role of model workers and craftsmen. This announcement coincides with the launch of the 2026 National "Quality Month" activities and aligns with the long-term targets set by the ACFTU's October 2025 policy opinion.

## Core Facts
*   **Policy Action:** The All-China Federation of Trade Unions (ACFTU) issued a notice on September 4, 2026.
*   **2026 Target:** Support the construction of 600 national model worker and craftsman innovation studios.
*   **Financial Support:** A subsidy of 100,000 yuan per innovation studio.
*   **Purpose:** To further stimulate职工 (employees') innovation and creative vitality, and better play the示范引领作用 (demonstration and leading role) of model workers and craftsmen.
*   **Historical Performance (Last 3 Years):**
    *   Innovation achievements: 32,000.
    *   Patents obtained: 19,200.
    *   Skilled talents cultivated: 137,000.
    *   High-skilled talents cultivated: 41,000.
*   **Long-term Targets (ACFTU Opinion, Oct 2025):**
    *   By 2030, ACFTU-supported innovation studios to reach 3,000.
    *   By 2030, innovation studios at all levels to reach 160,000.
*   **Related Activities:** Members actively participate in "Model Worker and Craftsman Helping Enterprises" and "Model Worker and Craftsman Entering Campuses" activities to promote the spirit of model workers, labor, and craftsmanship.

## Event Conclusion
The All-China Federation of Trade Unions has formally announced its 2026 plan to support 600 national model worker and craftsman innovation studios, each receiving 100,000 yuan in subsidies. This action operationalizes the broader strategy outlined in the October 2025 ACFTU opinion, which aims for 3,000 nationally supported studios and 160,000 total studios at all levels by 2030. Previous three-year data shows these studios have generated 32,000 innovation achievements, 19,200 patents, and trained 137,000 skilled talents (41,000 high-skilled), demonstrating their effectiveness in fostering industrial innovation and talent development.

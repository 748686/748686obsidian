This EventUnit synthesizes two independent scientific breakthroughs reported by *People's Daily* (Science & Knowledge section) on September 5, 2026. The first involves a Chinese research team synthesizing the world’s longest single-atom-diameter metal wire. The second involves a British-led international team achieving synchronized room-temperature micro-lasers. ## Core Facts

**1. Synthesis of Longest Single-Atom Metal Wire (China)**
*   **Institution:** Beijing National High Magnetic Field Center (led by Li Kuo’s team).
*   **Achievement:** First synthesis of micrometer-scale, atom-thick, structurally stable carbon-wrapped single-metal-atom chains.
*   **Specifications:** The single atomic chain length exceeds 1 micrometer, consisting of more than 4,000 continuous copper atoms.
*   **Comparison:** This represents an improvement of more than two orders of magnitude in chain length compared to previous international records.
*   **Methodology:** Innovative application of high-pressure solid-phase topological polymerization strategy using a Paris-Edinburgh press.
*   **Versatility:** The preparation method is extensible to other metals, including cobalt, nickel, and zinc.
*   **Publication:** Results published in the international journal *Science*.
*   **Implication:** Potential new material options for next-generation nanocircuits and flexible wearable electronics; opens new paths for low-dimensional functional materials.
*   **Commentary:** Mao He Guang (Academician, Chinese Academy of Sciences, World High Pressure Scientist) endorsed the work.

**2. Room-Temperature Synchronized Micro-Lasers (UK)**
*   **Institution:** University of Southampton, UK (led by an international team).
*   **Achievement:** First spontaneous synchronization of multiple micro-lasers at room temperature, forming a coherent and reconfigurable light source.
*   **Mechanism:** Micro-lasers are generated in optical microcavities based on liquid crystals and organic dye molecules. They operate at the same frequency and phase as a unified coherent light emitter, forming a macroscopic collective state called a "supermode."
*   **Advantage over prior tech:** Previous observations of similar laser control phenomena required specialized semiconductor microcavities cooled to below -250°C; this new platform operates at room temperature.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Electrically Tunable:** Applying small voltages reorients liquid crystal molecules, adjusting light propagation and laser modes.
    *   **Optically Reconfigurable:** The relative positions of micro-lasers within the same device can be adjusted.
*   **Publication:** Research paper published in *Nature Communications* (UK).
*   **Implication:** A step toward lower-cost, reconfigurable, and more accessible photonic computing technologies.

## Event Conclusion

On September 5, 2026, two significant scientific breakthroughs were reported in *People's Daily*:

1.  **Chinese Researchers Achieve Record-Breaking Single-Atom Metal Wire:** A team at the Beijing National High Magnetic Field Center, led by Li Kuo, successfully synthesized the world's longest single-atom-diameter metal wire. Using a high-pressure solid-phase topological polymerization strategy, they produced carbon-wrapped copper atomic chains exceeding 1 micrometer in length (over 4,000 atoms), surpassing previous records by two orders of magnitude. This platform is electrically tunable and optically reconfigurable, offering a promising path for future photonic computing. The findings were published in *Nature Communications*.

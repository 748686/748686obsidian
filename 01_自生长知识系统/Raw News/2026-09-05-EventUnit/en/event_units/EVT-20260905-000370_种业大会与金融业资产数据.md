## Event Overview

Simultaneously, the People's Bank of China (PBOC) released preliminary statistical data revealing that China's financial institutions recorded total assets exceeding 560 trillion yuan as of the end of the second quarter of 2026, reflecting continued growth across banking, securities, and insurance sectors.

## Core Facts

- **Event Date**: September 4–5, 2026
- **Seed Industry Conference**: The Third Thirty-Third China Beijing Seed Industry Conference opened in Beijing.
- **Financial Asset Data**: According to preliminary statistics from the People's Bank of China as of the end of Q2 2026:
  - **Total Financial Sector Assets**: 562.2 trillion yuan, up 7.7% year-on-year.
  - **Banking Sector Assets**: 497.98 trillion yuan, up 6.6% year-on-year.
  - **Securities Sector Assets**: 20.37 trillion yuan, up 29.8% year-on-year.

    - Banking Liabilities: 458.37 trillion yuan, up 6.8% year-on-year.
    - Securities Liabilities: 16.32 trillion yuan, up 36.6% year-on-year.
    - Insurance Liabilities: 39.79 trillion yuan, up 12.2% year-on-year.

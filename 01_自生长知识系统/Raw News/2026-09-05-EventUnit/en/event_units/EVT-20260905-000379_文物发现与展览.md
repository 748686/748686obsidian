# Event Name
Cultural Heritage Discoveries and Exhibitions in China (September 2026)

## Event Overview
On September 5, 2026, *People's Daily* published coverage regarding two distinct cultural heritage developments in China: the discovery of a stone stele inscribed by Bao Zheng in Kaifeng, and the launch of a major exhibition featuring Western Han Dynasty lacquered wooden artifacts from the Guangling region at the Nanjing Museum.

## Core Facts
*   **Exhibition Launch:** The Nanjing Museum launched an annual special exhibition titled "Clouds Soaring: Culture and Art of Western Han Guangling Lacquered Wooden Ware" (云飞扬：西汉广陵漆木器文化与艺术). The exhibition is divided into seven chapters presenting the daily life and spiritual world of the Western Han period.
*   **Artifacts:** The exhibition features extensive collections of Guangling lacquered ware, characterized by red/brown bases, cloud-pattern motifs, and gold/silver inlay techniques. Notable items include silver-rimmed wine vessels (zhi) and a seven-piece lacquered toiletry set (zhuanglian) featuring gold and silver paste decorations.
*   **Geographic Scope:** The exhibition defines "Guangling" broadly as the "Eastern Chu" region identified by Sima Qian, encompassing the Jianghuai area of Jiangsu and eastern Anhui, rather than just modern-day Yangzhou.
*   **Archaeological Context:** The article notes that after the reduction of feudal domains, Guangling lacquer production shifted from royal workshops to market-driven local artisans, making Guangling a national design and production center with exports reaching overseas.
*   **Kaifeng Stele:** A stele inscribed by Bao Zheng was discovered in Kaifeng, Henan.

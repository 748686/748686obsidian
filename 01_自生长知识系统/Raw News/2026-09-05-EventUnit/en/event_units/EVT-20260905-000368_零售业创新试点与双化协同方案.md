# Event Name

**Retail Industry Innovation Pilot Programs and Digital-Green Synergy Policy Initiative**

## Event Overview

On September 5, 2026, the Chinese government announced two significant policy developments concerning economic transformation. First, the Ministry of Commerce published the list of cities for the second batch of national retail industry innovation and improvement pilot programs. Second, seven central departments jointly issued the "Implementation Plan for Promoting the Coordinated Development of Digitalization and Green Transformation (2026–2030)," outlining the strategic goals and key tasks for the "15th Five-Year Plan" period. These initiatives signal a coordinated state effort to modernize retail sectors and enforce sustainability standards across key economic pillars such as agriculture, manufacturing, and urban operations.

## Core Facts

*   **Retail Innovation Pilots:** The Ministry of Commerce released the official list for the second batch of national retail industry innovation and improvement pilot cities.
*   **Policy Issuance:** On September 4, 2026, the Cyberspace Administration of China, along with six other ministries (National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Ecology and Environment, Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, and Ministry of Commerce), jointly issued the *Implementation Plan for Promoting the Coordinated Development of Digitalization and Green Transformation (2026–2030)*.
*   **Strategic Timeline:** The plan targets the "15th Five-Year Plan" period, setting benchmarks for 2030.
*   **2030 Goals:**
    *   AI low-cost, high-efficiency advantages to be more prominent in empowering green/low-carbon tech.
    *   Significant improvement in energy efficiency for emerging fields like computing power facilities and 5G base stations.
    *   Renewable energy consumption in key facilities (e.g., computing power centers) to reach the renewable energy consumption responsibility weight level of their respective provinces.
    *   Deep integration of digital-green transformation in agriculture, manufacturing, trade, consumption, and urban operations.
    *   Enhanced ecological environment governance via digital-intelligent technologies.
*   **Task Framework:** The plan deploys 14 key tasks across four areas:
    1.  Integration of digital-intelligent and green technologies (including green AI).
    2.  Improving energy efficiency in emerging sectors (computing, communications, digital enterprises).
    3.  Deepening applications in key sectors (agriculture, manufacturing, trade, consumption, urban ops, and ecological governance).
    4.  Consolidating foundational support (optimizing development environment, digital carbon management, international cooperation).
*   **Contextual Economic Indicator:** Data released alongside these policy announcements indicates that Yantai Port’s breakbulk ro-ro shipment volume reached 16.22 million tons in the first eight months of 2026, a 52.9% year-on-year increase, exceeding the total shipment volume of 2025. A specific vessel carrying 23,800 tons of machinery and engineering vehicles departed Yantai Port for Ghana on September 3, 2026.

## Event Overview
On November 3, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a series of articles across its commentary (Page 05) and news (Pages 06-07) sections. The coverage spans multiple high-priority national themes: the implementation of the 15th Five-Year Plan, the governance and regulation of Artificial Intelligence, deep-sea strategic development, the advancement of the hydrogen economy, and the preparations for the 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai. Additionally, the day features significant political reporting on the outcomes of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC).

## Core Facts

**1. Childcare Infrastructure and the 15th Five-Year Plan**
*   **Status:** As of the end of 2025, China had 126,000 childcare service institutions and 6.66 million childcare spaces.
*   **Policy Direction:** During the "15th Five-Year" period (2026–2030), the government will continue to promote the integration of childcare and early education.
*   **Implementation:** Support will be directed toward communities and employers to establish childcare services, ensuring residents can access affordable, nearby care to balance family and career responsibilities.
*   **Source:** Article #365.

**2. AI Governance and Ethics**
*   **Context:** A song titled "Overcomer" (Guailairen) featuring AI-generated faces of ancient figures went viral, contrasting with criminal cases where individuals used AI face-swapping to bypass facial recognition systems and steal account information.
*   **Analysis:** The commentary argues that while AI can liberate humans from repetitive labor, it risks weakening human thinking, increasing social inertia, and blurring responsibility if not used properly.
*   **Framework:** The article proposes a three-step approach based on ancient horse-taming wisdom:
    1.  **"Xiangma" (Assessing the Horse):** Understanding AI's origins to ensure it extends human capability rather than replacing human agency.
    2.  **"Tiaosheng" (Training the Horse):** Ensuring AI products enhance happiness and efficiency rather than anxiety.
    3.  **"Bianji" (Treating Illness):** Establishing legal red lines and ethical bottom lines to prevent fraud and infringement.
*   **Source:** Article #367.

**3. Deep-Sea Strategic Development**
*   **Milestone:** The "Dream" (Mengxiang) ocean drilling ship completed trial drilling in the South China Sea, reaching a maximum depth of 4,929.17 meters, setting a new record for China's self-implemented deep-water drilling.
*   **Future Projections:** In 2026, the three manned submersibles—"Jiaolong," "Shenhai Yongshi," and "Fendouzhe"—are expected to exceed 2,000 cumulative dives. The "deep-sea all-weather resident floating research facility" project has entered a new construction phase.
*   **Resource Data:** Globally over the last 10 years, 67% of discovered oil and gas blocks were in deep-sea areas, accounting for 68% of reserves. Multi-metal nodules rich in manganese, copper, cobalt, and nickel are found at greater purity than land-based deposits.
*   **Innovation:** The "Shenhai Yihao" (Deep Sea No. 1) energy station was developed after two years of analysis to solve storage issues without precedent. Qingdao Pengpai Ocean Exploration Technology Co. is using "simulation training + AI forecasting models + multiple protections" to improve deep-sea robot reliability.
*   **Policy:** The "15th Five-Year" plan outlines implementing deep-sea engineering to enhance entry, exploration, development, and safety capabilities.
*   **Source:** Article #368.

**4. Hydrogen Economy Industrialization**
*   **Event:** The 2026 China Hydrogen Energy Industry Innovation Conference was held in Inner Mongolia.
*   **Participants:** Over 230 enterprises participated in a "hydrogen practice" initiative.
*   **Goal:** To demonstrate industrial cluster effects and accelerate the commercialization and large-scale application of hydrogen energy.
*   **Source:** Article #369.

**5. 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE) Preparations**
*   **Location:** Shanghai (November 2026).
*   **Logistics:** 32 municipal保障 (support) groups are managing 257 key service tasks. Customs offers "CIIE Express" bonded warehouse services allowing goods to enter warehouses immediately upon arrival for pre-exhibition display.
*   **Technology:** The venue deployed 1,400 sets of Wi-Fi 7 equipment with 5G-A 10-gigabit network coverage, supporting 200,000 simultaneous online users.
*   **Services:** A new AI-powered travel assistant "Xiao Jiao" was launched. 4,561 volunteers ("Little Leaves") are stationed, including over 100 fluent in French, German, and Spanish.
*   **Economic Strategy:** Shanghai aims to transform CIIE traffic into 365-day "consumption flow" and "development increment," positioning itself as a global debut site for new products.
*   **Source:** Article #370.

**6. CPC Twenty-Fifth Plenary Session (Political Report)**
*   **Date of Adoption:** October 29, 2026.
*   **Document:** "Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Several Major Issues Concerning the Persistent Promotion of Comprehensively Strict Governance of the Party."
*   **Significance:** Described as a milestone document guiding the party's self-revolution. It addresses the "unique difficulties of a major party" and consolidates the experience of the past decade.
*   **Drafting Process:** The drafting group held 5 plenary sessions and 35 special meetings over 8+ months, producing 36 draft versions.
*   **Feedback Statistics:**
    *   1,173修改意见 (revision opinions) were received from various sectors; after removing duplicates, 1,076 remained.
    *   208 opinions were absorbed, representing a 17.7% absorption rate.
    *   81 additions, rewrites, or精简 (simplifications) were made to the text.
*   **Key Theoretical Contributions:** The document systematizes "Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building," outlining "Fourteen Adherences" and seven principles for persistent strict governance (e.g., adhering to a strict tone, problem orientation, and institutional governance).
*   **Source:** Article #369 (Partial content included in feed).

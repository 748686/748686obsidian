## Event Overview
On November 3, 2026, the People's Daily (paper.people.com.cn) published a feature article titled **"Staying Behind is Also an 'Expedition'"** (*Shou Shou, Ye Shi Yi Chang "Yuan Zheng"*). The article, part of the "Frontier Observation" series on the Party Building (Dangjian) page, revisits the history of the Long March to highlight the contributions of Red Army troops and游击队 (guerrilla units) that remained behind after the main force departed.

The synthesis connects historical events from the 1930s (specifically the aftermath of the Central Red Army's departure in 1934 and the earlier Northern Route Anti-Japanese Vanguard's entry into Zhejiang in 1934) with contemporary examples of economic transformation, ecological conservation, and social welfare in China. The core argument posits that the "Long March Spirit" is not merely a geographical concept but a continuous spiritual lineage characterized by resilience, sacrifice for the greater good, and close ties with the people.

**Specific Historical Cases Cited**
1.  **Northern Route Anti-Japanese Vanguard (Zhejiang):** Entered Qingyuan County, Zhejiang, in 1934. Fought for 7 days, marching over 140 kilometers on foot,牵制ing KMT forces in the Fujian-Zhejiang border region.
2.  **Zhang Ligui (Qingyuan County):** Former Deputy Commander of the Shou-Tai-Jing-Qing Independent Battalion. Captured in May 1937 after being betrayed. Executed at age 27 after refusing to reveal Party secrets under torture; his family members were also taken hostage.
3.  **Southern Jiangxi Three-Year Guerrilla War:** Central Red Army departed in 1934. Leftbehind forces faced enemies dozens of times larger, blockades, and isolation.
4.  **Liu Bojian:** Political Director of the Gannan Military Region. Wounded and captured during a breakout. Refused to renounce the Party, stating "Life is for China, death is for China." Executed.
5.  **Qiongya Column (Hainan):** Chen Yulan (now 106 years old as of 2026) served as a traffic officer. Survived severe leg injuries sustained in 1934 due to aid from local villager Huang Yunfa.

**Contemporary Applications of the Spirit**
1.  **Economic Resilience (Qingyuan County):** The Zhenan Pencil Factory transformed from struggling pencil production to manufacturing wooden eyeliner pencils. After investing over 5 million yuan and testing thousands of formulas, they exported their first batch in January 2002. Current annual output exceeds 100 million sticks, with over 50% of the European market share.
2.  **Ecological Sacrifice (Xunwu County, Jiangxi):** As the source of the Dongshen Water Diversion Project, Xunwu closed all mining points in 2008 to protect water quality for Shenzhen and Hong Kong. This required relocating 592 households (2,297 people) from Sanbiao Township. Vegetation coverage in abandoned mines rose from 10.2% to 95% over more than a decade.
3.  **Social Welfare (Wenchang City, Hainan):** Implementation of elderly meal halls and delivery services for high-age/infirm seniors (e.g., 106-year-old veteran Chen Yulan receives lunches for 10 yuan).

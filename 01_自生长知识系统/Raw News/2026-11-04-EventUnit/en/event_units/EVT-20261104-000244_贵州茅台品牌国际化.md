# Event: Kweichow Moutai Brand Internationalization Strategy

## Event Overview

On November 3, 2026, *People's Daily* published an article titled "Kweichow Moutai: Using Culture as a Bridge to Promote Brand Globalization." The report details the strategic pivot of China's largest liquor producer from simple product export to comprehensive brand internationalization. The narrative emphasizes a methodology where cultural heritage and modern quality management serve as the foundation for entering global markets.

The event covers Moutai's historical context (referencing the 1915 Panama-Pacific International Exposition), its recent commercial performance data up to late 2025/early 2026, its technological research initiatives, and specific marketing activities such as "Moutai Night" events in Seoul and San Francisco.

## Core Facts

**Commercial Performance (Source-Reported)**
*   **Revenue:** In 2025, Kweichow Moutai Co., Ltd.'s foreign market operating income was approximately 4.85 billion RMB [Source 1].
*   **Geographic Reach:** As of September 2026, Moutai products covered 67 countries and regions [Source 1].
*   **Regional Growth:** In 2025, sales in the Southeast Asian market grew by more than 4 times year-on-year [Source 1].
*   **Channel Performance:** Duty-free channel sales doubled in 2025 [Source 1].
*   **Digital Exposure:** From 2025 to the present (as of Nov 2026), cumulative exposure on Moutai's overseas social media platforms exceeded 510 million person-times [Source 1].

**Strategic Framework**
*   **Three-Step Strategy:** "Product Export" -> "Brand Going Global" -> "Internationalization" [Source 1].
*   **Six Supporting Systems:** Expression, Product, Channel, Price, Compliance, and Policy [Source 1].
*   **Operational Shift:** Transitioning from "channel-driven" to "consumer-driven" [Source 1].
*   **Product Matrix:** Development of "International Edition," regional limited editions, and low-alcohol products to enhance overseas adaptability [Source 1].
*   **Channel Focus:** Wholesale, online retail, offline retail, F&B, and private domains; prioritizing Asia as the center and North America as a support, with a focus on Southeast Asia [Source 1].

**Quality and Production Standards**
*   **Core Production Area:** 15.03 square kilometers along the Chishui River, recognized as a Geographical Indication product protection scope [Source 1].
*   **Craftsmanship:** Listed as a National Intangible Cultural Heritage item in 2006. Production involves 30 processes, 165 craft links, and requires at least 5 years before export [Source 1].
*   **Technological Research:**
    *   Establishment of Moutai Science and Technology Research Institute in 2022 [Source 1].
    *   Establishment of Guizhou Moutai Biotechnology R&D Co., Ltd. in 2025 [Source 1].
    *   Focus on the "Time-Space Method" brewing system, microbial genomics, flavor chemistry, and metabolite analysis [Source 1].
*   **Awards:**
    *   National Quality Award: Received in 2003, 2011, and 2023 [Source 1].
    *   EFQM Global Award: Received the 2023 award (presented June 2024) with a 7-diamond rating, becoming the first Chinese enterprise to achieve this under the new assessment standards [Source 1].
*   **ESG:** Maintains an MSCI ESG rating of A [Source 1].

**Cultural and Diplomatic Activities**
*   **2025 Events:**
    *   Held "Moutai Night" during the World Expo, releasing five new products in the "Walking Into Series" tailored to different nations [Source 1].
    *   Celebrated the 110th anniversary of the Panama-Pacific International Exposition award and the 10th anniversary of San Francisco "Moutai Day" [Source 1].
*   **2026 Events:**
    *   October 2026: "Moutai Night" held for the first time in Seoul, South Korea. Released the first "Feitian Series" promotional film specifically for overseas markets, titled *Warmth, Connecting the World* [Source 1].
*   **Platform Presence:** Regular appearances at the Davos Forum and the World Chinese Entrepreneurs Convention [Source 1].
*   **Invitation Programs:**
    *   "Chishui River Forum": Held for two consecutive years [Source 1].
    *   "Super Factory Tour": In 2025, hosted guests from 28 countries/regions, totaling over 400 individuals [Source 1].

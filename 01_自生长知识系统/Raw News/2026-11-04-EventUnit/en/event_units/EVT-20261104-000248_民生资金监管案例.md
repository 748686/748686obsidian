## Event Overview
On November 3, 2026, *People's Daily* reported on a case handled by the Discipline Inspection and Supervision Working Committee of the Xinyu High-tech Zone in Jiangxi Province. The report details how a village group leader misappropriated surplus funds from a rural water supply project. The incident was identified through a "small incision" special supervision campaign focused on rural water supply, leading to the recovery of the funds and administrative disciplinary action against the responsible official.

## Core Facts
*   **Location:** A village group within the Xinyu High-tech Zone, Jiangxi Province.
*   **Subject:** He Moumou (何某某), the leader of the village group.
*   **Misconduct:** In 2024, He Moumou collected tap water installation fees from 91 households. After the installation was completed, a surplus of 42,300 RMB remained. Instead of refunding the villagers as required, He Moumou retained the funds for personal use.
*   **Duration:** The funds were occupied for a prolonged period following the 2024 installation completion.
*   **Discovery Mechanism:** The issue was surfaced by villager complaints ("The tap water has been connected for almost two years, but what happened to the extra installation fees we paid?") during a special supervision campaign in the rural water supply sector.
*   **Investigation Method:** The Xinyu High-tech Zone Discipline Inspection and Supervision Working Committee activated a "upgrade-level handling + regional collaboration" mechanism. They utilized a combination of data comparison, household verification, and fund tracing to verify accounts against physical realities.
*   **Outcome:** Upon intervention by the discipline inspection department, He Moumou voluntarily returned the misappropriated surplus. He received an administrative sanction (政务处分) for infringing upon the interests of the masses and violating work requirements.
*   **Official Commentary:** The article emphasizes that civilian funds are not "private money" and calls for standardized operation of grassroots micro-power, clarified boundaries, normalized disclosure of village affairs and finances, and source prevention of "micro-corruption" around the masses.

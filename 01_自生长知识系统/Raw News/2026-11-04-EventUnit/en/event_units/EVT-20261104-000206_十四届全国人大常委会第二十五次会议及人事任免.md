## Event Overview

On November 2, 2026, the 25th session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress convened in Beijing. During this session, the Standing Committee reviewed multiple draft laws and initiated measures to deepen the study of the spirit of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China.

## Core Facts

*   **Event Timing:** The session took place on November 2, 2026.
*   **Legal Review - Criminal Procedure Law:** The draft amendment to the Criminal Procedure Law was submitted for review. The 55-article draft aims to improve basic principles, personal coercive measures, the handling of涉案财物 (case-related property), and the protection of litigation participants' rights. Key changes include strengthening the protection of victims' rights (e.g., legal aid access), enhancing the defense rights of suspects and defendants, and refining procedures for minors and foreign-related criminal cases.
*   **Legal Review - Renewable Energy Law:** The draft revision of the Renewable Energy Law was submitted for initial review. The 10-chapter, 76-article draft introduces environmental thermal energy and hydrogen energy into its scope. It establishes principles of prioritizing the development and utilization of renewable energy and mandates the construction of a unified national electricity market system.
*   **Legal Review - Tax Collection and Administration Law:** The draft revision of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection was submitted for review. The 6-chapter, 100-article draft focuses on improving tax collection measures, standardizing law enforcement, and protecting taxpayers' legitimate rights. Notable provisions include regulating internet platform enterprises' tax cooperation obligations and limiting tax late payment penalties to no more than twice the principal tax amount.
*   **Political Study Materials:** Two authoritative輔導材料 (study guides) were published to help the Party and the public understand the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee. These include the "Study Guide" (published by the People's Publishing House) and "One Hundred Questions on Learning" (published by the Party Building Literature Publishing House and Learning Publishing House).

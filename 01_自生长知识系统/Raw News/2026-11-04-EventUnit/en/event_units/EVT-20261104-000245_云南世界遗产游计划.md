# Event: Yunnan's "World Heritage Tour Plan" for Civilizational Exchange

## Event Overview

This EventUnit synthesizes reporting on the "World Heritage Tour Plan" (世界遗产游计划), a cross-border cultural and tourism initiative launched by Yunnan Province, China. The plan utilizes Yunnan's six UNESCO World Heritage sites as a foundational platform to foster international cooperation, specifically targeting countries in South and Southeast Asia, as well as broader global connections. The initiative aims to transform world heritage sites from mere观光 (sightseeing) destinations into active spaces for civilizational dialogue, promoting cross-border tourism routes, heritage protection strategies, and people-to-people exchanges.

## Core Facts

**Initiation and Leadership:**
*   **Launch Date:** November 2024.
*   **Launching Bodies:** The plan was jointly initiated by the Publicity Department of the Yunnan Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China, the Yunnan Provincial Department of Culture and Tourism, and the Yunnan Provincial Association for Promoting International Friendship.
*   **Source:** Article #403 (People's Daily).

**Strategic Recognition:**
*   In July 2025, the plan was selected and included in the "Action Plan List of the Ministerial Meeting on Global Civilization Dialogue" as one of 110 specific projects.
*   **Source:** Article #403 (People's Daily).

**Core Assets:**
*   The plan leverages Yunnan's six World Heritage sites, the second-highest number in China:
    1.  Ancient City of Lijiang
    2.  Sanjiang Passing Rivers
    3.  China South Karst - Yunnan Stone Forest
    4.  Chengjiang Fossil Site
    5.  Honghe Hani Rice Terraces Cultural Landscape
    6.  Pu'er Jingmai Mountain Ancient Tea Forest Cultural Landscape
*   **Source:** Article #403 (People's Daily).

**Cross-Border Tourism Routes Launched:**
*   **"Starlight Roaming, Buddha Realm Secrets":** A China-Laos fantasy tour starting from Kunming, passing through the Mohan port, and ending in Luang Prabang and Vientiane, Laos.
*   **"Tour Yunnan, See Angkor":** A China-Cambodia natural and cultural dual-heritage tour linking Yunnan’s natural heritage sites with the Angkor Wat heritage in Cambodia.
*   **Infrastructure Context:** These routes are facilitated by improved transport networks, including the China-Laos Railway, the Kunming-Manila corridor, and the Mekong River transportation network. Additionally, a 240-hour visa-free transit policy applies to nine prefectures/cities in Yunnan, including Kunming, Lijiang, Yuxi, and Pu'er.
*   **Source:** Article #403 (People's Daily).

**Key Activities (2025–2026):**
*   **September 7, 2025:** The "2025–2026 World Heritage Tour Plan" launched at the Chengjiang Fossil Site World Natural Heritage Museum in Yunnan, accompanied by a "World Heritage Photo Exhibition."
*   **July 2026:** During the China-Myanmar Media Twin-City Forum and "Mountain has Emotion, Wind has Intent" China-Myanmar Cultural Exchange Week, a collaborative painting activity connected Lijiang (China) and Bagan (Myanmar).
*   **September 2026:** A pop-up event in Galle, Sri Lanka, featuring imagery, coffee, tea, and handicrafts to showcase Yunnan’s heritage.
*   **September 2026:** An event in Ninh Binh, Vietnam, titled "One Cup of Coffee Brings Scenery," focusing on the collaboration between coffee estates and world heritage sites, leveraging Yunnan’s status as China’s largest coffee producer and Vietnam’s significance in global coffee production.
*   **June 2026:** A photo exhibition titled "World Heritage Face-to-Face, Civilizational Mutual Learning Hand-in-Hand" was held at Kunming Changshui International Airport, displaying 50 works linking Yunnan sites with Angkor Wat (Cambodia), Luang Prabang (Laos), and Sukhothai (Thailand).
*   **Source:** Article #403 (People's Daily).

**Institutional Framework & Expansion:**
*   The plan focuses on three main lines: operating overseas social media accounts, launching a large-scale converged media special website for global promotion, and organizing "Yunnan-Africa Humanistic Dialogue" themed activities in conjunction with the "China-Africa Year of Humanistic Exchange" in 2026.
*   The Yunnan Center for International Communication to South and Southeast Asia has disseminated content to Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, and Saudi Arabia.
*   **Source:** Article #403 (People's Daily).

## Event Conclusion

The "World Heritage Tour Plan" represents a structured, state-led soft power initiative by Yunnan Province to leverage UNESCO World Heritage assets for geopolitical and cultural diplomacy. By anchoring its strategy in six significant heritage sites and integrating them with tangible cross-border infrastructure (railways, visa policies) and experiential diplomacy (coffee, art, photography), the plan seeks to position Yunnan as a central node in a civilizational dialogue network extending from Southeast Asia to Africa. The inclusion of the plan in the *Global Civilization Dialogue* action list validates its alignment with broader national diplomatic goals.

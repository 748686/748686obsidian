# Event: Cultural Export of Chinese Culture and Brand Internationalization (Xifeng Liquor Case)

## Event Overview

This event synthesizes information regarding the international expansion of Chinese cultural brands, specifically focusing on the "Going Global" strategy of Xifeng Liquor (Shaanxi). The coverage highlights the intersection of intangible cultural heritage preservation and commercial internationalization. The primary narrative centers on how Xifeng Liquor leverages its status as a holder of both "National Intangible Cultural Heritage" and "National Industrial Heritage" to promote Chinese liquor culture worldwide. While the provided source material is a corporate press release/advertisement rather than an independent analytical article on general cultural export theory, it serves as a concrete case study for the mechanisms of Chinese cultural branding abroad.

## Core Facts

*   **Entity:** Xifeng Liquor (Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Factory Group Co., Ltd.).
*   **Heritage Status:**
    *   In 2021, the "Xifeng Liquor Brewing Technique" was included in the fifth batch of National Intangible Cultural Heritage Representative Project List.
    *   In 2021, the "Xifeng Liquor Factory" was included in the fifth batch of National Industrial Heritage List.
*   **Policy Context:** The article situates these efforts within the opening year of China’s "15th Five-Year Plan," citing the *15th Five-Year Plan for Cultural Heritage Protection and Inheritance*. This plan emphasizes "protection first," "rational utilization," "minimum intervention," and the synergistic protection of material and intangible cultural heritage.
*   **Intangible Heritage Preservation Methods:**
    *   **Museum:** The Xifeng Liquor Cultural Museum opened in September 2025, utilizing historical artifacts, realistic reconstruction, and digital technology to display 3,000 years of brewing history.
    *   **Production:** Utilization of 10,000-ton "Jiuhai" (wine sea) storage warehouses where ancient techniques using秦岭 (Qinling) Thorn branches hand-woven and pasted with hundreds of layers of paper are still actively used for aging liquor.
    *   **Tourism:** Transformation of industrial tourism from simple factory visits into a "cultural tourism + industry" experiential model, integrating museums, brewing workshops, and Jiuhai warehouses into daily public experience.
*   **Internationalization Metrics (Claimed):**
    *   Trademarks successfully registered in 20 countries and regions.
    *   Products exported to more than 30 countries and regions.
    *   Sales networks established in Southeast Asia, Europe, and Africa.
*   **Strategic Goal:** To "tell the story of Chinese liquor" and promote the "Feng-flavor" (Xifeng) brand globally by guarding cultural roots and continuing millennial inheritance.

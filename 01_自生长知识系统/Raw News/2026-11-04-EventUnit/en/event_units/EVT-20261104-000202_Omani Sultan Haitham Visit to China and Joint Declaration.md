## Event Overview

On November 2, 2026, Sultan Haitham bin Tariq of the Sultanate of Oman conducted an official state visit to China. During the visit, he held meetings with top Chinese leadership, including President Xi Jinping and Premier Li Qiang. The diplomatic engagement culminated in the issuance of a joint declaration titled *Joint Statement of the People's Republic of China and the Sultanate of Oman on Deepening the China-Oman Strategic Partnership*. The visit underscored the maturity of bilateral ties, described as spanning 48 years of diplomatic relations, and highlighted cooperation across energy, technology, and multilateral security frameworks.

## Core Facts

- **Event Date:** November 2, 2026.
- **Key Participants:**
  - **Oman:** Sultan Haitham bin Tariq.
  - **China:** President Xi Jinping; Premier Li Qiang; State Councilor Wu Zhenglong (present at the meeting with Premier Li); NPC Standing Committee Chairman Zhao Leji (hosted parallel diplomatic engagements); Foreign Ministry Vice Minister Hua Chunying.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Release of the *Joint Statement of the People's Republic of China and the Sultanate of Oman on Deepening the China-Oman Strategic Partnership* (Article #342).
  - High-level bilateral meetings between Chinese leadership and the Omani Sultan.
- **Contextual Markers:**

  - It followed the signing of the Protocol to Amend the China-Peru Free Trade Agreement (effective November 1, 2026), indicating a broader period of diplomatic activity for China in early November 2026.

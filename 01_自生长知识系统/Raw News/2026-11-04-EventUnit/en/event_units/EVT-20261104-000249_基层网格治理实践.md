# Event Name
Grassroots Grid Governance Practice in Zhoukou, Henan

## Event Overview
On November 3, 2026, the *People's Daily* published an article titled "Grid Frontline Shows Responsibility," reporting on Henan Province's Zhoukou City. The report highlights Zhoukou's experience in using grid-based governance to resolve grassroots conflicts and stabilize social contradictions. The article uses a specific case study involving a land dispute in Shenqiu County to illustrate the effectiveness of the city's "3345" grid worker methodology and its broader efforts to strengthen grassroots governance infrastructure.

## Core Facts

**1. Governance Mechanism**
*   Zhoukou City has optimized the setup of rural, community, and exclusive grids, totaling **27,832 grids**.
*   The city has developed the **"3345" grid worker methodology**:
    *   **3 Tasks**: Inspection, reporting, and disposal.
    *   **3 Time Periods**: Mandatory checks during morning, noon, and evening.
    *   **4 Key Items**: Mandatory inspection of four categories of key issues.
    *   **5 Bottom-Line Issues**: Mandatory reporting of five types of bottom-line problems.
*   Zhoukou has built a "Digital Grid" relying on a high-efficiency governance platform, achieving dynamic collection and real-time updates of six elements: people, land, affairs, objects, emotions, and organizations.

**2. Human Resources**
*   Zhoukou is an agricultural city with a large population and many grassroots contradictions.
*   To address this, the city deepened organizational improvement actions, rectifying weak and lax village party organizations on a "one village, one policy" basis.
*   **32,301** full-time and part-time grid workers are active at the grassroots level, led by party members.
*   **968** rural revitalization village assistants have entered villages for posts, conducting routine household visits and conflict排查 (troubleshooting/排查).

**3. Case Study: Shenqiu County Dispute**
*   **Location**: Yangzhuang Village, Shenqiu County, Zhoukou City.
*   **Incident**: A neighbor felled a tree onto Grandpa Yang's arable land and damaged crops while transporting it. A verbal altercation escalated when Yang's son, who works elsewhere, threatened to return immediately via high-speed rail, promising to "teach the neighbor a lesson."
*   **Intervention**: Party grid worker Chen Dan, who was visiting households in the first grid (covering 127 households), heard the commotion. She reported to Village Party Secretary Yang Bing.
*   **Resolution**: Secretary Yang Bing and Chen Dan visited Yang's home the same day. They:
    *   Criticized and educated the neighbor who felled the tree.
    *   Conducted ideological work on Grandpa Yang to de-escalate his anger.
    *   Contacted Yang's son to persuade him not to return.
    *   Coordinated the restoration of the damaged land and an apology from the neighbor.
    *   Helped coordinate a rototiller since the neighbor could not find one.
*   **Outcome**: The dispute was resolved the same day, preventing a potential escalation involving the returning son.

**4. Statistical Outcomes**
*   In the current year (as of Nov 2026), the grid system in Zhoukou has resolved **34,000** (3.4万) various types of conflicts and contradictions.

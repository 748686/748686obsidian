# Event Name

Implementation of the Degree Law Reform: Practice Outcomes as Substitutes for Traditional Theses in Graduate Degree Applications

## Event Overview

Following the implementation of the revised Degree Law in 2025, which legally established "practice outcomes" (实践成果) as an alternative basis for conferring master’s and doctoral degrees alongside traditional academic theses, several Chinese universities have initiated pilot programs allowing students to apply for degrees using professional or creative practice outputs. As of September 2026, first cohorts of graduates have successfully defended under this new framework across disciplines including creative writing, engineering, and civil infrastructure.

## Core Facts

- The 2025 Degree Law was the first to legally recognize practice outcomes as co-equal with学位论文 (academic dissertations) for degree conferral.
- East China Normal University (ECNU) Chinese Creative Writing program: 36 master’s students, including Guo Yige, submitted “literary works + creative statements” instead of traditional theses. The program, approved in 2022, shifted from requiring both创作 (creative work) and学术论文 (academic papers) to offering a practice-outcome track. Evaluation includes 开题, 预答辩, plagiarism checks, blind peer review by writers/editors, and a defense panel including industry professionals. Graduates reported diverse career paths, including internet-sector scriptwriting and cultural creative industries.
- Lanzhou University College of Nuclear Science and Technology: Wang Haixi and Li Yang became the first engineering doctoral candidates to defend via practice outcomes, based on their contributions to the AB-BNCT (accelerator-driven boron neutron capture therapy) demonstration device—the first domestically autonomous system of its kind. Their work addressed key technical bottlenecks: high-power rotating lithium target (Wang) and radiation shielding (Li). The university’s revised degree standards require practice outcomes to align with national/industrial needs, involve real engineering problems, and demonstrate technological breakthroughs or industrial upgrading. Pre-defense validation included on-site expert inspection and certification.
- Nanjing University School of Modern Engineering and Applied Sciences: PhD student Wang Haoran defended using his independent contribution to a distributed fiber-optic sensing monitoring system for prestressed steel筒 concrete pipes in the South-to-North Water Diversion Project. The system detects wire fractures via high-frequency acoustic signals to prevent pipe bursts. Third-party technical testing, user-application reports, and expert科技成果鉴定 were required. The technology was later deployed in Henan’s Zhengkai Eastern Water Supply Project and exported to similar overseas projects.
- A People’s Daily expert commentary (September 4, 2026) by Wan Anlun (BNU professor, Fujian Thought Center researcher) supported the reform, arguing that practice-outcome evaluation is discipline-appropriate for applied/professional fields (medicine, engineering machinery, publishing) but that theoretical theses remain essential for research-oriented disciplines. The commentary emphasized that the reform raises—not lowers—standards by demanding higher实践能力 benchmarks tied to real industry needs, and called for continued refinement of recognition criteria and quality oversight.

## Cross-Source Verification

- **Legal basis**: Both the reporting article and the commentary confirm the 2025 Degree Law as the enabling legislation. This is corroborated across both sources.
- **Non-equivalence with lowered standards**: The reporting article explicitly states that practice-outcome defense “does not mean lowering thresholds”; the commentary reinforces this by calling it “绝非‘降格毕业’” (absolutely not “graduation by lowering standards”). Consistent across both.
- **Disciplinary distinction**: The commentary distinguishes research-oriented vs. applied/professional fields; the reporting article illustrates this through three cases (creative writing, nuclear engineering, hydraulic infrastructure), all professionally oriented. No contradiction.
- **Procedural rigor**: All three university cases describe multi-stage evaluation (proposal, pre-defense, blind review, industry-expert panels, third-party testing where applicable), confirming that the reform maintains rigorous assessment.

- First cohorts of graduates have successfully obtained degrees via practice outcomes at ECNU, Lanzhou University, and Nanjing University.
- Degree evaluation systems are being recalibrated to include practice-outcome channels alongside traditional theses.
- Industry-academia collaboration is being formalized through requirements for enterprise/expert participation in defense panels and third-party validation.
- Public and editorial discourse (as reflected in People’s Daily) supports the reform as aligned with high-quality talent development for industrial upgrading.

## Event Conclusion

The 2025 Degree Law reform has enabled Chinese universities to recognize practice outcomes as valid alternatives to traditional academic theses for graduate degree conferral. Early implementations at East China Normal University, Lanzhou University, and Nanjing University demonstrate that the pathway maintains rigorous, multi-stage evaluation involving industry experts and third-party validation, and is tailored to professionally oriented disciplines. Expert commentary supports the reform as a necessary evolution toward discipline-appropriate, application-focused evaluation while affirming the enduring value of theoretical theses for research-oriented fields. No contradictions exist between sources; however, broader systemic impact, nationwide adoption scope, and long-term outcomes remain undetermined pending further reporting.

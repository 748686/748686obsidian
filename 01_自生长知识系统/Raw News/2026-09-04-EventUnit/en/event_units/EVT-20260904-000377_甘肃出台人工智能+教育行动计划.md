# Event Name
Gansu Province Issued "Artificial Intelligence + Education" Action Plan (2026–2030)

## Event Overview
On September 3, 2026, the Gansu Provincial Department of Education and four other departments jointly issued the *Gansu Province "Artificial Intelligence + Education" Action Plan (2026–2030)*. The initiative aims to advance the national education digitization strategy, accelerate the digital-intelligent transformation of education through AI, and build a high-quality education system. The plan outlines five key areas comprising 15 specific tasks.

## Core Facts
*   **Issuing Authorities:** Gansu Provincial Department of Education and four other departments (totaling five departments).
*   **Document:** *Gansu Province "Artificial Intelligence + Education" Action Plan (2026–2030)*.
*   **Publication Date:** Reported on September 4, 2026, in *People's Daily*.
*   **Primary Objective:** To promote national education digitization and construct a high-quality education system via AI-driven transformation.
*   **Structural Framework:** The plan consists of 5 main tasks with 15 specific clauses/items.
*   **Higher Education Initiatives:**
    *   Universities are to collaborate with leading enterprises and research institutes to establish joint innovation mechanisms and communities.
    *   Supply-demand matchmaking platforms are to be built to strengthen the incubation of AI achievement transformations.
    *   AI is to be promoted as a compulsory public foundation course or a designated elective course in universities.
    *   Universities meeting specific conditions may establish AI-related minor majors or micro-majors.
*   **K-12 and Infrastructure Initiatives:**
    *   Deployment of a provincial education computing power platform integrating supercomputing and intelligent computing.
    *   Construction of smart classrooms and upgrading of traditional classrooms to intelligent standards.
    *   Support for junior and senior high schools to purchase AI laboratory equipment.
    *   Support for higher education institutions in constructing teaching and research facilities, updating training equipment, and building industry-education integration internship bases.
*   **Industrial Integration:** Around key industries such as new energy, new materials, and equipment manufacturing, a batch of AI industrialization demonstration projects will be cultivated.

The Gansu Provincial government has formally introduced the *Gansu Province "Artificial Intelligence + Education" Action Plan (2026–2030)*. The plan seeks to digitize and intelligently transform the regional education system by integrating AI into university curricula as a foundational requirement, establishing joint innovation ecosystems between academia and industry, and upgrading physical and computational infrastructure across K-12 and higher education sectors.

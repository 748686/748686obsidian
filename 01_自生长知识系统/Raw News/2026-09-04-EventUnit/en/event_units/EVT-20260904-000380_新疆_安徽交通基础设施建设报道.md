## Event Overview
On September 3, 2026, two distinct infrastructure developments were reported in China, highlighting both legislative advances and construction progress in the transportation sector. In Xinjiang, the Standing Committee of the People's Congress announced the promulgation of the "Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region Highway Regulation," establishing a legal framework for the full lifecycle governance of highways. Concurrently, in Anhui Province, the main span of the Yan'an Road Bridge in Bengbu achieved successful closure, marking a critical milestone toward the completion of the Huai River basin's largest span cable-stayed bridge.

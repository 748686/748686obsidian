## Event Overview
On September 3, 2026, the "Bloom – The Third National College Students Fine Arts Exhibition (Beijing Exhibition)" (绽放——第三届全国大学生美术作品展览（进京展）) opened in Beijing. The exhibition features over 400 selected works from eight regional divisions, showcasing creations across various fine arts disciplines including Chinese painting, oil painting, prints, sculpture, animation, digital art, and crafts. The event aims to present the diverse explorations of new-generation creators and the educational outcomes of higher arts institutions in the new era. A symposium on national higher arts education was also held on the same day.

The "Bloom – The Third National College Students Fine Arts Exhibition (Beijing Exhibition)" officially opened in Beijing on September 3, 2026.

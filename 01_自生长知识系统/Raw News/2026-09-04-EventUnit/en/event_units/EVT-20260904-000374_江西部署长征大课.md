# Event Name
Jiangxi Deploys "Long March Grand Lesson" for 2026 Fall Semester

## Event Overview
On September 3, 2026, Jiangxi Province announced the deployment of a province-wide educational initiative for the 2026 fall semester. The initiative, titled the "Long March Grand Lesson," requires students across all levels of education (primary, junior high, senior high/vocational, and university) to attend a unified lesson simultaneously. The theme is "Carry Forward the Great Long March Spirit and Walk the New Long March Road Well." The curriculum is designed to integrate historical facts with age-appropriate moral and political education, drawing on the cognitive characteristics of different student groups.

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Announcement:** September 3, 2026.
*   **Location:** Jiangxi Province, China.
*   **Initiative:** 2026 Fall Semester First Lesson of the School Year ("Kai Xue Di Yi Ke").
*   **Scope:** All students in primary schools, junior high schools, senior high schools (including vocational high schools), and universities within Jiangxi Province.
*   **Theme:** "Carry Forward the Great Long March Spirit, Walk the New Long March Road Well."
*   **Organizing Bodies:** The lesson is jointly produced by the Publicity Department of the Jiangxi Provincial Committee of the CPC, the Working Committee of the Jiangxi Provincial Committee of the CPC for Education, the Jiangxi Provincial Department of Education, the Jiangxi Provincial Committee Party History Research Office, and Jiangxi Radio and Television Station.
*   **Pedagogical Approach:** The curriculum is segmented by educational level:
    *   **Primary School:** Theme "Heroic Epic." Focuses on vivid stories from the Long March to help students understand the basic historical course.
    *   **Junior High School:** Theme "Great Expedition." Aims to help students comprehend the great significance of the Long March and its profound impact on the historical process of the Chinese nation.
    *   **Senior High/Vocational School:** Theme "Great Long March Spirit." Focuses on understanding the rich connotations of the Long March Spirit and appreciating the ideals and responsibilities of revolutionary predecessors.
    *   **University:** Theme "New Long March, Setting Sail Again." Connects the inheritance of the Long March Spirit with personal growth stories, practical answers to high-quality development in Jiangxi's "red soil," and the struggle journey of youth in the new era, encouraging students to integrate personal pursuits into the national development overall situation.
*   **Source Publication:** People's Daily (Paper), September 4, 2026, Page 06 (News).

Party Building Collaboration Promotes Common Prosperity in Xiaoshan District, Zhejiang

## Event Overview

On September 3, 2026, *People's Daily* reported on the development strategies of Xiaoshan District in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province. The district is utilizing "party building collaboration" (党建联建) as a core mechanism to activate new drivers for development and explore pathways toward common prosperity. Under the guidance of the "Thousand Villages Demonstration, Ten Thousand Villages Renovation" project (千万工程), the district focuses on organizational revitalization to drive comprehensive rural revitalization, establishing a model of "party leadership, government-enterprise-village-farmer linkage, and household income generation."

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Report:** September 3, 2026 (Published in *People's Daily*, September 4, 2026 edition).
*   **Location:** Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province.
*   **Key Mechanism:** Party building collaboration serving as a bond, with market-oriented operation as the guide.
*   **Initiative:** Construction of "Common Prosperity Workshops" (共富工坊).
*   **Quantified Outcome:** As of the report date, 101 "Common Prosperity Workshops" have been established across the district, cumulatively absorbing 11,900 individuals into employment.
*   **Social Security Model:** The district has constructed a guarantee system covering daily life, medical care, and elderly care, ensuring development outcomes are shared by villagers.
*   **Key Personnel Mentioned:**
    *   Wo Aomao (沃阿毛): A villager from Fenghuang Village, Qianxia Town, cited as an example of improved livelihood.
    *   Unnamed Official: A responsible person from the Organization Department of the Xiaoshan District Committee.
*   **Strategic Goal:** To contribute to the high-quality construction of the Common Prosperity Demonstration Zone.

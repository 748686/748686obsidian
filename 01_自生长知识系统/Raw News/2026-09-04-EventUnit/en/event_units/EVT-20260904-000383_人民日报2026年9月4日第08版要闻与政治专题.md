## Core Facts

### 1. Grassroots Governance and Community Development (Beijing)
*   **Location:** Shiyuan Village East Community, Baiquan Street, Yanqing District, Beijing.
*   **Issue:** Residents of the new residential complex (5 courtyards, 39 buildings) faced a lack of ground-level space for fitness and cultural activities.
*   **Intervention:** The community Party General Branch utilized the unused rooftop of the community committee building as a solution.
*   **Process:** A professional institution conducted structural safety assessments. The "Red Building Gate议事厅" (autonomy platform) was used to gather resident opinions. Modifications included anti-slip flooring, solar lighting, noise control measures, and staggered activity scheduling. Professional dance instructors were recruited via the sub-district office.
*   **Source:** Article #516 (Author: Yin Xingyun).

### 2. Market Regulation Enforcement ("Ghost Takeaway"专项整治)
*   **Event:** A national symposium on market regulation law enforcement inspection and investigating illegal acts related to "ghost takeaways" was held in Beijing on September 3, 2026.
*   **Context:** During the first half of 2026, market regulatory systems nationwide handled 641,700 cases with confiscations totaling 6.982 billion RMB.
*   **Directives:**
    *   Deepen the special rectification of "ghost takeaways."
    *   Coordinate efforts with anti-gang/evil campaigns and anti-telecom fraud measures.
    *   Rectify "involutionary" (cutthroat) market competition.
    *   Implement the "Guarding Consumption" (Shouhu Xiaofei) iron fist action targeting food, electric bicycles, and metrology violations.
*   **Source:** Article #517 (Reported by Lin Lili).

### 3. National Fire Safety Action Plan (Seven Departments)
*   **Event:** Press conference held on September 3, 2026, regarding the *National Fire Safety Literacy Improvement Action Work Plan (2026–2030)* (issued July 24, 2026).
*   **Participating Bodies:**
    1.  National Fire Rescue Bureau (Host)
    2.  Central Social Work Department
    3.  All-China Federation of Trade Unions
    4.  Ministry of Education
    5.  Ministry of Civil Affairs
    6.  Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security
    7.  Ministry of Culture and Tourism
*   **Key Measures:**
    *   **Trade Unions:** Promote "One Letter, Two Books" (一函两书), encourage workers to report hazards via "Hidden Danger Snap" apps, and organize evacuation drills.
    *   **Education:** Expand the "1530" safety education model in primary/secondary schools (1 min daily, 5 mins weekly, 30 mins pre-holiday).
    *   **Civil Affairs:** Mandate coverage training for elderly care facility staff (to "report fires, handle initial outbreaks, organize evacuation"). As of end-August 2026, 431,000 personnel trained.
    *   **Culture & Tourism:** Joint inspections during peak holiday seasons against blocked exits and illegal electricity usage.
*   **Source:** Article #518 (Reported by Qi Yukun).

### 4. Anti-Drug Operations (Beijing)
*   **Data:** In 2026, Beijing police seized over 60 kg of various drugs.
*   **Special Crackdown:** Since July 2026, authorities targeted "Thiofentanil" (Ti-lai-ta-ming) – a small-animal anesthetic disguised in electronic cigarette cartridges.
*   **Results:** 6 drug syndicates dismantled, 20+ cases solved, and 170+ suspects apprehended.
*   **Source:** Article #519 (Reported by Wang Zhou).

### 5. Theoretical Exposition (Party Leadership)
*   **Author:** Ji Zhengju (Deputy Dean, Central Institute of Party History and Literature).
*   **Topic:** "Upholding the Centralized and Unified Leadership of the Party Central Committee."
*   **Key Arguments:**
    *   Described as the highest principle of Party leadership and a core Marxist organizing principle.
    *   Emphasizes "体用贯通" (integration of essence and application), where central leadership is the foundational "body" (essence) enabling the "application" (governance tools).
    *   Cites Engels' *On Authority* and Lenin's democratic centralism as theoretical origins.
    *   Argues that centralized leadership is essential for governing a super-large country and ensuring long-term strategic continuity (contrasting with Western electoral cycles).
*   **Source:** Article #522.

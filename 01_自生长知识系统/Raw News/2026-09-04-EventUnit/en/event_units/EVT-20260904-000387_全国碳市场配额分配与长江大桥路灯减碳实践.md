## Core Facts

### National Carbon Market Quota Allocation
*   **Issuer:** Ministry of Ecology and Environment of China.
*   **Document:** "2025 and 2026 Quota Total and Allocation Schemes for the Power Generation Industry, and 2026 Schemes for the Steel, Cement, and Aluminum Smelting Industries in the National Carbon Emissions Trading Market" (Source: Article #533).
*   **Date of Report:** September 3, 2026 (reported from Beijing).
*   **Scope:** Covers four industries: power generation, steel, cement, and aluminum smelting.
*   **Allocation Method:** Free allocation based on carbon emission intensity control. The policy aims to lay the groundwork for future free and paid allocation combinations.
*   **Incentive Mechanism:** "Reward the advanced, urge the backward." Units with lower carbon emissions per product unit receive higher quota surplus rates.
*   **Clearing Rules:** Key emission units may use quotas from other key emission units within or across industries. They may also use certified voluntary emission reductions registered after the启动 of the national greenhouse gas voluntary emission reduction trading market on January 22, 2024, to offset 2025 and 2026 quota clearing.
*   **Greenhouse Gases Covered:**
    *   Power, Steel, Cement: Carbon dioxide.
    *   Aluminum Smelting: Carbon dioxide, carbon tetrafluoride, and hexafluoride.
*   **Exclusions:** Indirect emissions from consuming electricity and heat are not covered.
*   **Policy Alignment:** Schemes coordinate with macroeconomic conditions, historical emissions, and policies on capacity replacement and output regulation.

### Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge Streetlight Carbon Reduction
*   **Location:** Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge, Hubei Province.
*   **Timeline:**
    *   1957: Bridge opened with 512 incandescent bulb spherical lamps (60W).
    *   1970s: Upgraded to 80–125W high-pressure mercury lamps.
    *   Early 2000s: Steel lamp posts replaced cement ones; upgraded to 250W high-pressure sodium lamps.
    *   2016: Pilot test of 5000K cold white LED lamps; paused due to public controversy regarding light刺眼 (glare) and impact on the bridge's warm atmosphere.
    *   November 2025: Full lighting system upgrade initiated.
    *   2019: Second Seven Yangtze River Bridge completed LED upgrade.
    *   2020–2021: LED lamps推广 in main urban roads.
*   **Upgrade Specifications:** 150 old 250W high-pressure sodium lamps replaced with 200W LED lamps.
*   **Energy Savings:** Daily electricity consumption dropped from 450 kWh to 144 kWh. Comprehensive energy saving rate is 68%. Annual savings estimated at approximately 110,000 kWh.
*   **Technology:** Smart lighting control platform utilizing IoT for single-lamp control, time-sharing dimming, and light control combined with time control (threshold: 55 lux).
*   **Future Targets:** By the end of 2027, five cross-river bridges (Erx, Second, Yingwuzhou, Yangsigang, and Wuhan Yangtze River Bridges) will achieve full smart lighting coverage.

These two narratives highlight the scaling of carbon reduction from high-level policy design to tangible local implementation.

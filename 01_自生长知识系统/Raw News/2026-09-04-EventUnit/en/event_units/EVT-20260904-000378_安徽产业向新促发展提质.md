# Event: EVT-20260904-000378 — Anhui Drives Industrial Upgrading Through Innovation and Quality Development

## Event Overview

On September 4, 2026, *People's Daily* published an in-depth report (Page 06, "Industry Moving Toward 'New' — Development with Quality Improvement") documenting how the Anhui Province has been advancing its strategic industries through sustained investment in science and technology innovation. The report positions the "artificial intelligence + education" initiative in Gansu Province as a concurrent regional development story, while focusing primarily on Anhui's concrete achievements in quantum technology, commercial aerospace, brain-computer interfaces, and future industry clustering.

The primary source of factual claims in this EventUnit is **Article #509**, a People's Daily reporter dispatch by Zhang Zhifeng and Li Junjie, retrieved from `paper.people.com.cn` with `source_status: fetched` and `content_status: full`. No other independently corroborating articles were provided for this event.

## Core Facts

**Confirmed Fact — Launch event**
On September 1, 2026 at 10:00, at the Jiuquan Dongfeng Commercial Aerospace Innovation Test Area, the "Zhishenxing-1" (智神星一号) launch vehicle for its first orbital test was ignited and successfully placed its payload into the designated orbit. According to the report, this marks the first liquid oxygen–kerosene launch vehicle manufactured in Anhui, launched from the city of Chizhou.¹

**Confirmed Fact — Policy background**
In October 2024, General Secretary Xi Jinping inspected Anhui Province and emphasized: "Guard the foundation of the real economy, accelerate the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries, expand strategic emerging industries, make forward-looking arrangements for future industries, develop new quality productive forces suited to local conditions, and build advanced manufacturing clusters with international competitiveness."¹

**Confirmed Fact — High-tech enterprise statistics (report year 2025)**
- New high-tech enterprises added in 2025: 3,200
- Total high-tech enterprise stock: 23,500¹

**Confirmed Fact — H1 2026 industrial growth figures**
- Equipment manufacturing value-added: +22.7% year-on-year
- High-tech manufacturing value-added: +44.6% year-on-year¹

**Confirmed Fact — Brain–computer interface (BCI) development in Bengbu**
- Bengbu "China Sensor Valley" has attracted over 200 upstream and downstream enterprises in the intelligent sensing industry.
- The Anhui North Microelectronics Research Institute Group Co., Ltd. (安徽北方微电子研究院集团有限公司) reports that its BCI device — enabling stroke patients to control a robotic glove via EEG-captured intent — was independently developed in-house.
- Longhu Laboratory (龙湖实验室) has been officially opened and put into operation.
- The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) BCI Testing and Certification Public Service Platform has landed in Anhui.
- Anhui's BCI action plan targets a "one core, two wings" layout (Hefei–Bengbu–Wuhu), aiming to enter the national first tier by 2028.¹

**Confirmed Fact — Commercial aerospace cluster**
- In May 2026, the first "Anqing-made" liquid launch vehicle, "Zhihang-1" (致航一号), completed full-stack integration and testing.
- A four-city commercial aerospace layout was described:
  - Hefei: satellite platforms, constellation operations, data applications
  - Bengbu & Chizhou: reusable launch vehicles, engines, shared testing services
  - Anqing: complete satellite manufacturing¹
- 2024 Anhui policy target: aerospace industry scale to exceed RMB 100 billion by 2027.
- 2026 three-year commercial aerospace action plan target: by 2028, annual capacity of 200 commercial launches plus engines, and 100 commercial satellites.¹

**Confirmed Fact — Technology transfer mechanism ("沿途下蛋")**
- Anhui piloted a "harvesting along the way" (沿途下蛋) mechanism allowing results from large science facilities to be commercialized incrementally rather than only after project completion.
- Example: Anhui Zhongke Terahertz Technology Co., Ltd. developed next-generation terahertz security scanners derived from high-precision monitoring technology on the Strong Magnetic Field facility, now deployed in high-speed rail and metro stations.¹

**Confirmed Fact — Future industry clustering**
- Quantum technology: over 100 chain enterprises across the province.
- Fusion energy: over 70 chain enterprises settled in Hefei, covering superconducting wires (upstream), host equipment manufacturing (midstream), and design/operation (downstream).¹

## Event Conclusion

Anhui Province is advancing a coordinated strategy to upgrade traditional industries and cultivate emerging and future sectors — specifically quantum technology, fusion energy, brain-computer interfaces, and commercial aerospace — guided by the October 2024 directives of Xi Jinping and operationalized through provincial action plans targeting 2027–2028. Key milestones reported include the successful first orbital launch of the Zhishenxing-1 vehicle from Chizhou-manufactured components, the establishment of a four-city aerospace cluster, the opening of the Longhu Laboratory and MIIT BCI testing platform, and the piloting of a "harvesting along the way" technology-transfer mechanism.

## Event Overview
On September 4, 2026, *People's Daily* published its 09th edition (Theory section), featuring articles that address fundamental political principles and the construction of autonomous academic knowledge systems in China. The event centers on two primary theoretical contributions: the imperative of upholding the Party Central Committee's centralized and unified leadership, and a detailed framework for building a self-reliant knowledge system for Chinese Business Administration (工商管理学). Additionally, the column includes an excerpt from *Economic Daily* regarding financial support for the artificial intelligence industry.

*   **Key Article 2:** Excerpt "Using Financial Power to Support High-Quality Development of the AI Industry" (以金融力量支撑人工智能产业高质量发展), originally published in *Economic Daily* on August 4, 2026.

*   **Theoretical Framework:** The construction of the Chinese business administration knowledge system is grounded in Dialectical Materialism and Historical Materialism, aiming to answer "China's questions, the world's questions, the people's questions, and the era's questions."

## Unique Information by Source

### Source: Article #523 (Business Administration & Party Leadership)
*   **Definition of Discipline:** Defines Business Administration as studying management activities and operational laws of micro-economic organizations, serving both micro-level (resource allocation, value creation) and macro-level (industrial transformation, strategic implementation) needs.
*   **Critique of Western Theory:** Asserts that Western business administration theories are predicated on capitalist private ownership logic and shareholder value maximization, making them insufficient to explain unique phenomena in Chinese enterprises.
*   **Specific Chinese Practices:**
    *   State-owned enterprises (SOEs) integrating Party building into corporate governance to tackle key core technologies.
    *   Government-enterprise synergy creating a governance pattern of "effective market and capable government."
    *   Traditional culture empowering management innovation (e.g., "righteousness and profit," "harmony and coexistence").
*   **Four Directional Pillars:**
    1.  Rooting in local practice to refine Chinese management laws (via "Two Integrations").
    2.  Openness and inclusiveness to solve cross-border operation challenges.
    3.  Social responsibility回馈 (giving back) to ensure development benefits are shared fairly.
    4.  Embracing technological change (AI, quantum info, IoT, blockchain) to expand theoretical innovation space.
*   **Three Focus Areas for Research:**
    1.  Characteristics of Chinese modern enterprise systems (leadership, property rights, governance).
    2.  Strengthening enterprises' role as innovation subjects (new quality productive forces).
    3.  Integration of technology and culture to catalyze market innovation (AI/VR activating traditional culture).

### Source: Article #524 (Financial Support for AI)
*   **Original Source:** Excerpt from *Economic Daily*, August 4, 2026.
*   **Industry Characteristics:** Identifies AI industry traits as asset-light, high investment, long cycle, and high risk.
*   **Structural Financial Issues:**
    *   Mismatch in credit models.
    *   Insufficient supply of long-term "patient capital."
    *   Inadequate empowerment from multi-level capital markets.
*   **Policy Recommendations:**
    1.  Develop long-term capital and leverage government funds to nurture patient capital.
    2.  Innovate financial products (equity investment, credit, bonds) for one-stop comprehensive services.
    3.  Broaden direct financing channels to optimize capital structures.
    4.  Improve risk-sharing mechanisms to balance security and vitality.

## Event Conclusion
The September 4, 2026, Theory column of *People's Daily* represents a coordinated push to consolidate ideological leadership and academic autonomy in China. The event marks a continued deepening of the "Chinese school" of thought in social sciences and economic policy.

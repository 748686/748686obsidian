## Event Overview

On September 4, 2026, multiple significant events occurred in China, as reported by official media sources, primarily *People's Daily*. ## Core Facts

### Diplomatic Activities
*   **President Xi Jinping's Return:** President Xi Jinping returned to Beijing on September 4, 2026, concluding a diplomatic tour from August 30 to September 3. The tour included visits to Kyrgyzstan and Egypt and participation in the SCO summit.
*   **SCO Summit Outcomes:** During the 26th Meeting of the Council of Heads of State of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, President Xi emphasized the "Shanghai Spirit" and proposed four "persistences": development priority, security focus, people-centered approach, and dialogue and consultation. The summit resulted in the revision of the SCO Charter, the adoption of the "Bishkek Declaration," and nearly 30 cooperation documents. New initiatives included the establishment of the China-SCO Port Economy Cooperation Center and the Basic Education Cooperation Center, along with a commitment to implement 100 new scientific and technological cooperation projects over the next three years.
*   **China-Egypt Relations:** President Xi visited Egypt from September 1 to 2, meeting with President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi. They signed agreements on building a community with a shared future, covering areas such as artificial intelligence, trade, and cultural exchange. Xi also proposed four initiatives for constructing a new Middle East security architecture.
*   **Other Diplomatic Engagements:** Xian Hui, Special Envoy of President Xi and Vice Chairperson of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC), attended the presidential inauguration ceremony in Zambia. The Mexican Minister of Foreign Affairs, Velasco, is scheduled to visit China. The Chinese Guest of Honor event at the 39th Moscow International Book Fair opened.

### Domestic Commemorations and Ceremonies
*   **WWII Victory Anniversary:** A symposium was held in Beijing on September 3, 2026, to commemorate the 81st anniversary of the victory of the Chinese People's War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression and the World Anti-Fascist War. Li Shulei, a member of the Politburo and Minister of Publicity, attended the event.
*   **Science Popularization Month:** The 2026 National Science Popularization Month kicked off on September 4.

### Economic and Industrial Developments
*   **New Quality Productive Forces:** Anhui Province reported that its "Zhishenxing-1" liquid oxygen-kerosene launch vehicle successfully reached orbit on September 1, marking the province's first locally manufactured rocket of its kind. High-tech manufacturing value-added in Anhui grew by 44.6% year-on-year in the first half of 2026.
*   **Youth in Tech Innovation:** A *People's Daily* article highlighted that researchers under 45 now lead over 40% of National Key R&D Program projects and 80% of National Natural Science Foundation projects, driving technological independence during the "15th Five-Year" plan period.
*   **Electricity Generation Capacity:** As of the end of July 2026, China's cumulative power generation capacity reached 4.08 billion kilowatts, an 11.0% increase year-on-year. Solar power capacity was 1.29 billion kilowatts (+16.1%), and wind power capacity was 690 million kilowatts (+19.5%).
*   **New Industry Standards:** In 2026, over 1,400 national standards for emerging industries have been released, accounting for approximately half of the total 2,800+ national standards issued. These cover sectors such as next-generation information technology, new materials, high-end equipment manufacturing, new energy, and environmental protection.
*   **High-Tech Manufacturing Profits:** From January to July 2026, profits of above-average-sized high-tech manufacturing enterprises increased by 50.1% year-on-year, pulling overall industrial profit growth by 9.6 percentage points.

### Sports and Culture
*   **Sports Powerhouse Construction:** At a press conference on September 3, the General Administration of Sport of China announced goals for the "15th Five-Year" plan, including reaching a per capita sports venue area of 4 square meters and a regular exercise participation rate of 40% by 2030, with a target sports industry scale exceeding 7 trillion yuan. The administration also outlined plans to open up competitive sports to diverse entities and reform governance.
*   **China Post and Rural Revitalization:** China Post integrated e-commerce, logistics, and finance to support rural revitalization. Agricultural-related loans grew by over 1 trillion yuan during the 14th Five-Year Plan period, and online agricultural sales reached 73.2 billion yuan.
*   **Beijing Cultural Forum:** The 2026 Beijing Cultural Forum is scheduled to take place from September 22 to 23.

### Disaster Relief
*   **Tibet Qulong Debris Flow Response:** Rescue operations continued in Qulong, Tibet, following a debris flow triggered by rainfall from Nepal on August 26. By September 3, temporary power supply was restored to the disaster core area. Rescue efforts involved firefighters, engineering teams, medical personnel, and psychological support teams. Cross-border assistance was provided to Nepali citizens.

### Regulatory and Financial Updates
*   **Insurance Asset-Liability Management:** The National Financial Regulatory Administration released the "Measures for the Administration of Asset and Liability Management of Insurance Companies" and related implementation notices. Insurance companies are required to establish governance structures for asset-liability management and conduct stress tests. A three-year transition period is allowed for institutions not initially meeting regulatory indicators.

### Obituaries
*   **Liu Jiyuan:** Former General Manager and Party Secretary of China Aerospace Industry Corporation, passed away on August 17, 2026, at age 93.
*   **Li Fashen:** Former President of Lanzhou University, passed away on August 13, 2026, at age 86.
*   **Zhang Shengzuo:** Former Vice Minister of the United Front Work Department of the CPC Central Committee and former Director of the State Administration of Religious Affairs, passed away on August 1, 2026, at age 93.

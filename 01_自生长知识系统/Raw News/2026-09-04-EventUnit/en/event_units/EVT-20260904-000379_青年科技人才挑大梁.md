Young Scientific and Technological Talents Taking on Key Roles in China's Innovation Drive (EVT-20260904-000379)

## Event Overview
On September 4, 2026, *People's Daily* published an article titled "Youth Taking the Lead, Innovation Adding Momentum" (part of the "15th Five-Year Plan" series). The report highlights the increasing prominence of young scientists and technicians in China's strategic tech sectors, particularly in artificial intelligence, aerospace, deep-sea exploration, and advanced manufacturing. The article frames this trend as a direct response to policy directives supporting youth leadership in scientific innovation.

## Core Facts

### Policy and Strategic Context
*   **Leadership Directive:** President Xi Jinping emphasized that policy重心 (focus) for cultivating strategic national talent should be placed on young scientific and technological talents, granting them more trust, support, and opportunities to "take the lead and play a starring role" (Article #510).
*   **15th Five-Year Plan:** The draft plan calls for expanding youth science and technology innovation actions and encouraging young people to serve as vanguards in various fields (Article #510).
*   **Institutional Support:** The General Offices of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council issued measures to strengthen the cultivation and use of young scientific talent (Article #510). The Communist Youth League has深化 (deepened) the construction of youth commandos for scientific and technological innovation (Article #510).

### Statistical Evidence
*   In the National Key R&D Program, over 40% of project leaders are young scientists aged 45 and under (Article #510).
*   80% of National Natural Science Foundation projects are undertaken by young scientists aged 45 and under (Article #510).

### Case Studies of Youth Leadership

**1. Superconducting Target Materials (Shanghai)**
*   **Entity:** Shanghai Superconductor Technologies Co., Ltd. (上海超导科技股份有限公司).
*   **Team:** A youth commando team.
*   **Achievement:** Developed high-performance superconducting target materials, breaking long-standing import dependencies and technical barriers.
*   **Key Personnel:** Qian Han (钱晗), team member, who noted the difficulty in process precision and the establishment of proprietary quality control systems (Article #510).
*   **Result:** Mass production stabilized; capacity increased 20-fold within two years (Article #510).

**2. Tunnel Engineering Equipment Remanufacturing (Changsha)**
*   **Event:** Winner of the 2025 China Youth Science and Technology Innovation "Open Competition" (揭榜挂帅)擂台赛 (Arena Match).
*   **Entity:** Changsha University of Science and Technology (长沙理工大学).
*   **Project:** "'Rebirth' Pioneer – Leader in Tunnel Engineering Equipment Remanufacturing."
*   **Key Personnel:** Xiong Jianda (熊健达), project leader.
*   **Challenge:** Addressed material performance deficiencies and poor process controllability in tunnel equipment remanufacturing.
*   **Industry Collaboration:** China Railway Fifth Group First Engineering Company Limited (中铁五局一公司) provided lab access, engineering sites, and technical mentors.
*   **Result:** Developed high-hardness ultra-wear-resistant wire specifically for extreme conditions; verified in actual engineering projects (Article #510).

**3. Super-Resolution Microscopy Imaging (Beijing)**
*   **Entity:** Tsinghua University, Department of Automation (清华大学自动化系).
*   **Event:** The 19th "Challenge Cup" National College Student Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Works Competition (第十九届“挑战杯”全国大学生课外学术科技作品竞赛).
*   **Key Personnel:** Liu Shuran (刘书然), doctoral student; Academician Dai Qionghai (戴琼海), advisor.
*   **Achievement:** Project "Reliable and Trustworthy Ultrafast, Ultra-long Time程, Ultra-high Resolution Microscopy Imaging Technology" won the Special Prize in the main competition.
*   **Technical Breakthrough:** Proposed a deformable phase space calibration mechanism and corresponding neural network, extending the imaging duration of multi-color live-cell super-resolution imaging by over 30 times (up to tens of thousands of time points) (Article #510).

### Broader Sector Involvement
*   **Aerospace:** Large numbers of young researchers participate in core system R&D for space station construction (Article #510).
*   **Deep Sea:** Young technicians play important roles in the "Jiaolong" (蛟龙) manned submersible R&D team (Article #510).
*   **AI:** A batch of youth entrepreneurship teams have achieved breakthroughs in algorithms and large models (Article #510).

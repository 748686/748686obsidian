# Event Name
**Li Qiang Chairs State Council Executive Meeting to Deploy Multiple Work Items**

## Event Overview
On September 11, 2026, Chinese Premier Li Qiang chaired an executive meeting of the State Council. The meeting addressed five primary areas:
1.  **Work Safety:** Implementing further deployments to address recent production safety accidents and strengthen regulations.
2.  **Computing Power Network (AI Infrastructure):** Researching the construction of a computing power network to support artificial intelligence development.
3.  **Reservoir Rehabilitation:** Studying the comprehensive renovation and upgrading of aging reservoirs to enhance flood control and water management.
4.  **Education:** Reviewing progress on building a strong education country and addressing student demographic changes.
5.  **Foundations Management:** Deliberating on the revision of the *Regulation on the Management of Foundations* to improve public trust and oversight.

The event was reported by Xinhua News Agency and published in the *People's Daily* on September 12, 2026.

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Meeting:** September 11, 2026.
*   **Chairperson:** Li Qiang, Premier of the State Council.
*   **Key Policy Directives (Work Safety):**
    *   The meeting noted that recent production safety accidents in various regions served as a "profound lesson."
    *   Directives included implementing "strict territorial management" and the "Three Responsibilities" (three musts) for safety.
    *   Focus on investigating causes related to lax enforcement of rules, subcontracting of projects, and inadequate safety supervision.
*   **Key Policy Directives (Computing Power/AI):**
    *   Defined the computing power network as the "fundamental support" for AI development.
    *   Goals include rational layout, standardization, and the construction of a multi-tiered networked computing system.
    *   Emphasis on "computing-electricity coordination" and "computing-network integration."
    *   Specific initiatives: Strengthening trunk optical fiber network construction, promoting green power direct connection, and landing "source-grid-load-storage" projects.
*   **Key Policy Directives (Reservoirs):**
    *   Approach: "Combining long-term and short-term goals," "treating both symptoms and root causes," and "categorized policies."
    *   Method: "One reservoir, one policy" for renovation plans.
    *   Objective: Enhance safety capabilities of aging reservoirs to safeguard flood control and river management.
*   **Key Policy Directives (Education):**
    *   Resource allocation adjusted for changes in the school-age population to meet peak demand.
    *   Focus on cultivating innovative talent aligned with national strategic needs.
    *   Implementation of a "health first" educational philosophy, integrating moral, intellectual, physical, aesthetic, and labor education.
*   **Regulatory Action:**
    *   The *Regulation on the Management of Foundations (Revised Draft)* was reviewed and passed during the meeting.

# Event Name
Fujian Province Advances Construction and Utilization of the Long March National Cultural Park

## Event Overview
On September 12, 2026, *People's Daily* reported on the progress of the Long March National Cultural Park (Fujian Section) construction project. The project is implemented in response to instructions given by General Secretary Xi Jinping during a visit to Fujian in October 2024, which emphasized the inheritance of red culture and the establishment of Long March National Cultural Parks in Changting and Ninghua. The event highlights the physical construction of preservation sites, the enactment of protective legislation, and the integration of red cultural resources into tourism and educational practices within the provinces' Long March departure zones.

## Core Facts
*   **Political Mandate:** In October 2024, Xi Jinping, during an inspection in Fujian, explicitly instructed to "inherit and promote red culture, properly build the Long March National Cultural Parks in Changting and Ninghua, and deepen the research and interpretation of revolutionary history and revolutionary cultural relics."
*   **Investment:** The Fujian provincial government has completed a cumulative investment of 5.525 billion yuan in the construction and management of the Long March National Cultural Park (Fujian Section).
*   **Structural Layout:** The park is organized around the "One Axis, Two Zones" concept:
    *   **Axis:** The central route of the Central Red Army’s Long March.
    *   **Main Construction Zones:** Changting and Ninghua, identified as primary departure points for the Long March.
    *   **Extension Zones:** Areas associated with Long March events.
*   **Theme:** The thematic positioning is defined as "The Departure of the Long March and Withdrawal from the Soviet Areas."
*   **Legal Framework:** The implementation of the *Fujian Provincial Regulations on the Protection of Red Cultural Relics* and the *Fujian Provincial Plan for the Protection and Utilization of Revolutionary Cultural Relics* has established a "nation-province-county" three-level cultural relic protection linkage mechanism.
*   **Preservation Metrics:**
    *   Sanming City has achieved full dynamic monitoring coverage for 227 Long March cultural relics.
    *   A new site for the Ninghua County Revolutionary Memorial Hall has been built.
    *   Ninety-one municipal-level or higher primary and secondary school study tour practice bases have been established.
    *   Over 200 on-site teaching points have been created.
*   **Educational Reach:** Since 2022, the province has trained 330,000 person-times of students and organized 1.68 million person-times of red study tour activities.
*   **Tourism Impact (Specific to Zhongfu Village):** In the previous year, Zhongfu Village in Changting County received over 300,000 tourists, generating a total tourism revenue of over 10 million yuan.

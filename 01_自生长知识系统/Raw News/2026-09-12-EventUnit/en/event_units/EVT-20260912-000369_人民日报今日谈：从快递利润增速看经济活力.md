## Event Overview
On September 12, 2026, *People's Daily* published a commentary piece in its "Today's Talk" (今日谈) column titled "Viewing Vitality Through Express Delivery Profit Growth Rates." Authored by Yu Xuan and appearing on Page 1, the article analyzes the recent semi-annual reports of major listed express delivery companies. The core thesis argues that the express delivery industry is moving away from a pattern of "volume growth with profit decline" ("量增利减") as profit growth rates have generally surpassed parcel volume growth rates. The commentary attributes this shift to three main factors: the implementation of "new quality productive forces" (digital and AI transformation), improved economic order through the combination of effective markets and active government regulation (specifically curbing "involution"-style competition), and the robust resilience of China's internal demand within its ultra-large-scale market.

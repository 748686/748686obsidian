## Event Overview

On September 12, 2026, *People's Daily* published a major feature article marking the 10th anniversary of the establishment of the Chinese People's Liberation Army Joint Support Force (Jianqin Baozhang Bufei). The article, authored by reporters Jin Zhengbo and Li Longyi with contribution from Yu Zhengxing, comprehensively reviews the force's transformation over the past decade. It highlights the shift from fragmented, regional logistics to an integrated, technologically empowered joint support system. The publication appears in the "News" section of the newspaper, specifically on both Page 01 and Page 02, indicating its significance as a primary news item for that date. The core narrative focuses on the force's evolution from its founding on September 13, 2016, to its current role in ensuring combat readiness through "smart logistics," unmanned systems, and enhanced medical and transport capabilities.

## Core Facts

*   **Founding Date:** The Central Military Commission Joint Support Force was officially established on September 13, 2016, in Beijing.
*   **Annette Event:** President Xi Jinping presented military flags to the Wuhan Joint Support Base and the Wuxi, Guilin, Xining, Shenyang, and Zhengzhou Joint Support Centers at the founding ceremony and delivered a training speech.
*   **Core Mission:** The force operates under the guidance of the "Four Requirements": listening to the Party's command, focusing on winning battles, pursuing innovation, and ensuring high-quality, efficient support.
*   **Operational Transformation:** Over the last 10 years, the force transitioned from "regionally dispersed" to "three-arm integration," from "fixed-point support" to "global linkage," and from "personnel-intensive" to "technology-empowered."
*   **Key Capabilities Highlighted:**
    *   **Unmanned Systems:** Use of drones for high-altitude supply, robotic "machine wolves" for medical supply delivery, and tracked unmanned ambulances.
    *   **Logistics Integration:** Multi-modal transport (road, rail, air, sea) for deploying main combat equipment; smart vertical warehouses with automated sorting.
    *   **Medical Innovation:** Development of the "10x formula for fluid replacement" by Dr. Shen Chuan'an’s team; remote medical consultations integrated into field training.
    *   **International Operations:** Medical teams deployed to Lebanon, Democratic Republic of Congo, Mali, and South Sudan for peacekeeping; rapid response logistics during the 2020 early stages of the COVID-19 pandemic.
*   **Personnel Highlights:**
    *   Shen Chuanan: Head of Burn Plastic Surgery at PLA General Hospital No. 4 Medical Center.
    *   Jiang Xinxin: Professor at the Joint Support Force Engineering University; developer of high-speed explosion-suppression fire-fighting systems for oil/gas storage.
    *   Zhang Junkai: Senior First-Class Warrant Officer in a certain motorized transport regiment, specializing in field equipment maintenance.
    *   Zhang Ying: The first female military doctor in the PLA to reach a mission point at 5,681 meters altitude.
*   **Ceremonial Milestones:**
    *   2019: The Joint Support Force marched in the National Day Parade for the first time.
    *   2025: The force carried its branch flag for the first time during the parade commemorating the 80th anniversary of the victory of the World Anti-Fascist War.

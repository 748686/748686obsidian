## Event Overview

On September 12, 2026, *People's Daily* reported that the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI) and the National Supervisory Commission (NSC) of China expelled Li Xu, former Standing Committee Member and Deputy Commander of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, from the Communist Party of China and removed him from public office. This disciplinary action was taken following a case-filing review and investigation into serious violations of discipline and law. Li Xu is accused of engaging in political speculation, violating organizational principles, and accepting bribes in exchange for favors in areas such as equipment procurement, project contracting, and land-use changes. His case has been transferred to procuratorial authorities for criminal prosecution. This event is distinct from the concurrent anti-corruption action taken against Lei Siwei, a former Vice Governor of Gansu Province, which was also reported in the same edition of *People's Daily*.

## Core Facts

*   **Subject:** Li Xu, former Standing Committee Member of the Party Committee and Deputy Commander of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps.
*   **Disciplinary Actions:**
    *   Expulsion from the Communist Party of China.
    *   Removal from public office by the National Supervisory Commission.
    *   Termination of qualifications as a delegate to the 10th Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region Party Congress, the 8th Corps Party Congress, and the 8th Party Congress of the 9th Division of the Corps.
    *   Confiscation of illegal gains.
*   **Alleged Violations:**
    *   Loss of ideals and beliefs; political speculation; associating with "political scammers"; long-term engagement in superstitious activities.
    *   Violations of the Central Eight-Point Regulations, including accepting banquets that could affect fair performance of duties and receiving free vehicle and driver services from private business owners.
    *   Violation of organizational principles, including failure to report personal matters as required and seeking benefits for others in job promotions and hiring.
    *   Compromising the bottom line of integrity by illegally receiving gifts and cash.
    *   Using official power for personal gain, specifically in equipment procurement, project contracting, and land nature changes, resulting in the illegal acceptance of huge sums of wealth.
*   **Legal Basis:** Actions taken pursuant to the *Regulations on Disciplinary Actions of the Communist Party of China*, the *Supervision Law of the People's Republic of China*, and the *Law of the People's Republic of China on Sanctions and Administrative Duties of Public Office Holders*.
*   **Approvals:** The decision was studied by the Standing Committee of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and approved by the Communist Party of China Central Committee.
*   **Status:** The case and associated materials have been transferred to the procuratorial organ for legal review and prosecution.
*   **Timing:** The announcement was published on September 12, 2026, reporting on a decision made following an investigation concluded around September 11, 2026.

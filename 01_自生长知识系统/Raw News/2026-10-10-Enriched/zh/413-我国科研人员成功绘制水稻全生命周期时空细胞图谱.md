President

Donald Trump

was in his home state of New York, this time in Syracuse, to campaign for Bruce Blakeman, the Republican candidate for the state's gubernatorial election.

Trump though had a special guest in

Mike Tyson

, who visited the White House earlier in the day and took the trip to Syracuse on Air Force One alongside Trump.

While Trump was campaigning for Blakeman, he shouted Tyson and his physical stature out.

He was a very dangerous person actually, and he was one of the greatest fighters in history."

Trump then cracked a joke that Tyson was there to protect Blakeman.

"I see him standing right next to Bruce Blakeman. Bruce wants a little protection, he’s got the right guy. Bruce knows who to stand near," Trump said.

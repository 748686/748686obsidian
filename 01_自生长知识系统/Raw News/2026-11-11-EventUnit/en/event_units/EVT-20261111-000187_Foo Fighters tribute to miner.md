# Event Name

Foo Fighters Tribute to Beaconsfield Miner in Sydney

## Event Overview

On the evening of May 5, 2006, during a live performance in Sydney, Australia, the Foo Fighters interrupted their concert to pay tribute to Todd Russell, one of the two miners rescued from the collapsed Beaconsfield mine in Tasmania. The tribute was delivered by Australian journalist and radio personality Ray Martin, who addressed the crowd and explained the significance of the moment.

## Core Facts

- **Date:** May 5, 2006
- **Location:** Sydney, Australia
- **Artist:** Foo Fighters
- **Subject of Tribute:** Todd Russell (Beaconsfield miner)
- **Context:** The miners, Todd Russell and Brant Webb, had been trapped underground for approximately 14 days following a cave-in on April 8, 2006. They were rescued on May 5, 2006, with Todd Russell emerging first, followed by Brant Webb several hours later.
- **Key Figure:** Ray Martin, an Australian journalist and radio host, spoke to the crowd during the concert interruption.
- **Performance Details:** The band performed their song "Times Like These" as part of the tribute. Dave Grohl acknowledged Todd Russell by name during the performance.

**Summary:**
The 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE) opened its doors on November 5, 2026, in Shanghai. The opening day was characterized by a significant increase in professional visitors, the arrival of first-time participating countries, and the debut of numerous global products and technologies. Key highlights included the expansion of the "CIIE Friendship" to 69 countries and international organizations, the introduction of a new coffee activity zone, and strong participation from global medical and consumer goods companies demonstrating China's opening-up strategy.

*(Note: While the Event Title mentions Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang attending the opening ceremony and giving a speech, the provided source articles (Articles #362-#409) do not contain specific text or details regarding his attendance at the opening ceremony or the content of any speech. Consequently, this specific detail is excluded from the "Core Facts" below to maintain factual accuracy based strictly on the provided text.)*

## Core Facts

### 1. Event Scale and Participation
*   **Participating Entities:** A total of **69 countries and international organizations** participated in the National Pavilion (Source: Article #402).
*   **First-Time Participants:** Five countries and one organization made their debut at the CIIE:
    *   Moldova
    *   Romania
    *   Seychelles
    *   Gabon
    *   Somalia
    *   United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) (Source: Article #402).
*   **Visitor Profile:** The opening day saw a noticeable increase in professional visitor groups touring by specialized routes. Visitors were described as "experts who know how to read the room" (Source: Article #401).

### 2. Key Exhibitions and Zones
*   **Agricultural and Food Products Zone:**
    *   Featured an upgraded "Asia-Africa Products Zone," where free exhibition space for Least Developed Countries (LDCs) increased by **17%** (Source: Article #397).
    *   **Products:** Cream pineapple from Benin, saffron from Afghanistan, New Zealand kiwis, bananas from various countries, and a white-fleshed durian from Penang, Malaysia (Source: Articles #397, #399).
    *   **Innovation:** This was the first year the CIIE established a **Coffee Activity Zone**, hosted in collaboration with the Kunshan Municipal People's Government, Jiansu Province (Source: Article #405).
*   **Medical Devices and Pharmaceutical Health Zone:**
    *   Described as a "star" zone with high density of multinational enterprises (Source: Article #404).
    *   **Siemens Healthineers:** Debuted the world's first photon-counting CT-guided PCI solution (China premiere) and the Asia's first native AI spectral angiography system. Also showcased a smart ring-shaped radiotherapy platform (Source: Article #404).
    *   **Fosun Pharma:** Showcased the "Da Vinci 5" surgical robot (China premiere). The company noted that its artemether injection has saved over 92 million severe malaria patients globally (Source: Article #404).
    *   **Gilead Sciences:** Showcased the progress of "CIIE Babies" (products debuted at CIIE) moving from exhibition to clinical application (Source: Article #401).
    *   **Danone:** Exhibited over 150 innovative products, including 9 global/national premieres, such as "Nutra Infant" powdered milk for Chinese babies and patent Akkermansia muciniphila (Akk) strain technology (Source: Article #407).
*   **Consumer Goods Zone:**
    *   **Wilkinson Sword (Switzerland):** Showcased 6 different types of toothpaste, highlighting direct consumer interaction (Source: Article #398).
    *   **Yihai Kerry (Wilmar International):** Showcased health-oriented products like diacylglycerol oil and grain rice balls. Identified as a "full attendance" participant for 9 consecutive sessions (Source: Article #398).
    *   **Adidas:** Expanded its exhibition space from 300 sqm to 500 sqm. Showcased sports apparel for the Chinese women's volleyball team and the "Super Cup." Noted that over **65%** of products sold in China are designed by local teams, **95%** are made in China, and **25%** of local designs are sold globally (Source: Article #406).
*   **Service Trade Zone:**
    *   Focus on intangible services and data.
    *   **Dun & Bradstreet (D&B):** Debuted a new intelligent open platform in Asia, providing AI decision-making infrastructure and global data services for Chinese enterprises going global (Source: Article #403).
    *   **Informa:** Highlighted plans to host the 2026 Hongqiao International Science and Technology Week in Shanghai immediately after the CIIE (Source: Article #403).
*   **Innovation Incubation Zone:**
    *   Featured small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and startups.
    *   **Livos Group (Italy):** Partnered with Yigao Group (China) to bring 10 Italian tech startups to their CIIE debut (Source: Article #401).

### 3. Cultural and Public Activities
*   **"CIIE文艺荟" (CIIE Arts Festival):** Held in the central plaza of the "Four-Leaf Clover" on the afternoon of November 5. Featured ethnic dances, opera, and fashion shows from domestic provinces and foreign countries including Canada, Spain, and Kazakhstan (Source: Article #401).
*   **China Pavilion:** Featured humanoid robots making tea, cross-border e-commerce technology demonstrations, and models of the domestically built large cruise ships *"Adora Magic City"* and *"Adora Flower City"* (Source: Article #402).
*   **Humanities Exchange:** Over **1,300 institutions** participated in cultural exchange activities, allowing visitors to experience intangible cultural heritage and folk customs from around the world (Source: Article #397).

### 4. Historical Context and Statistics
*   **CIIE History:** This is the 9th edition of the CIIE.
*   **Cumulative Data (Pre-9th):**
    *   Total overseas exhibitors: **27,000+** enterprise-times (Source: Article #397).
    *   Cumulative intent transaction volume: Over **$580 billion** (Source: Article #397).
    *   Cumulative new products/technologies/services launched: Over **3,400** (Source: Article #397).
    *   Siemens Healthineers cumulative exhibits: Over 200 products/solutions since the 1st CIIE; approx. **85%** launched in China; approx. **60%** produced locally (Source: Article #404).

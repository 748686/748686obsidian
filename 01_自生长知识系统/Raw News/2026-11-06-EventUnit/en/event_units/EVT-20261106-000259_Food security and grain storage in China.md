## Event Overview
On November 6, 2026, *People's Daily* published editorial content contextualizing China's food security efforts. The publication featured an editorial note titled "Let Our Granaries Be Fuller" alongside on-the-ground reporting from Xi'an, Shaanxi Province. The coverage links the political agenda of the 20th CPC Central Committee's Fifth Plenum—specifically regarding comprehensive strict Party governance—to tangible outcomes in rural collective asset management. The narrative frames the rectification of rural "three assets" (funds, assets, and resources) as a foundational component of broader grain security and rural revitalization strategies.

## Core Facts

**1. Political Context and Policy Framework**
*   The reporting is anchored in the spirit of the 20th CPC Central Committee's Fifth Plenum.
*   General Secretary Xi Jinping is cited emphasizing that "handling China's affairs well depends on the Party and comprehensive strict governance of the Party" (*People's Daily*, Nov 6, 2026).
*   The Plenum's decision calls for "normalizing the rectification of misconduct and corruption around the masses," with rural collective funds, assets, and resources ("three assets") identified as a key entry point for this governance.

**2. Xi'an Rural "Three Assets" Special Rectification**
*   **Scope:** The Xi'an Municipal Commission for Discipline Inspection and Supervision, in collaboration with relevant departments, conducted a special rectification of rural collective assets.
*   **Statistics:** As of the report date, 79,000 contracts were investigated, and 69,000 problematic contracts were rectified.
*   **Shibanyu New Village Case:** In Chang'an District's Wutai Subdistrict, a民宿 (B&B) named "Shijianfang" had a contract issue where the Village Committee was listed as Party A. This was corrected to comply with the *Rural Collective Economic Organization Law*, changing the contracting party to the "Shibanyu New Village Share Cooperative Economic Cooperative."

**3. Digital Governance and Transparency**
*   **Platform:** The Xi'an Rural Three Assets Management Monitoring Platform was developed with oversight from the municipal discipline inspection commission.
*   **Adoption:** The associated "Three Assets Public" mobile application has over 2.09 million registered users.
*   **Impact:** Rural petition and reporting rates dropped from 45.1% in 2021 to 26.7% in 2025.
*   **Upcoming Feature:** A "Cadre Early Warning" module is planned, which will cross-reference contracts signed by cooperative chairpersons, supervisors, and village committee members.

**4. Economic Outcomes of Asset Rectification**
*   **Case Study:** A 6-mu land lease, previously contracted for 50 years at only 720 RMB annually, was renegotiated. The new terms set a 20-year term with an annual rent of 40,000 RMB (increasing by 5% every 5 years), generating over 800,000 RMB in additional collective income.
*   **Market Integration:** Xi'an established 177 sub-district/town rural collective asset regulation and operation service centers and a urban-rural integration factor trading market.
*   **Financials:** The trading market has recorded transaction volumes of 9.075 billion RMB, resulting in 5.1 billion RMB in increased collective income and 220 million RMB in cost savings.

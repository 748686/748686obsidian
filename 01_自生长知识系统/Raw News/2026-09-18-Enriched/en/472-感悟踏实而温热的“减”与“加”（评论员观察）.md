### [China Releases Statistical Reform Plan for 15th Five-Year Period](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/18/content_30181830.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 16, 2026, China&\#x27;s National Bureau of Statistics released the Statistical Reform and Development &\#x27;15th Five-Year&\#x27; Plan to advance the modernization of statistics and support the country&\#x27;s broader modernization goals. The plan focuses on the period of the 15th Five-Year Plan, deploying key initiatives such as improving the statistical institutional and methodological framework, refining the national economic accounting system, and establishing more modern statistical measures for evolving industries. Specific objectives include enriching statistical indicators to better reflect the development of new sectors and new quality productive forces, strengthening monitoring of agriculture, manufacturing, and services, and enhancing the scientific rigor and international comparability of macroeconomic accounting data.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 18, 00:26

**「Background」** The 15th Five-Year Plan is part of China&\#x27;s rolling five-year planning cycle, which directs national economic and social development. The national economic accounting system refers to the set of rules and methods used to measure and record a country&\#x27;s economic activity, such as its Gross Domestic Product \(GDP\). Modernizing this system is a key part of the country&\#x27;s broader economic policy.

**「Impact」** The plan will directly impact government policymakers, economic analysts, and standard setters who rely on official Chinese economic data, as it commits to strengthening data integrity through stricter enforcement against fraudulent reporting and improving the international comparability of the country&\#x27;s macroeconomic figures. It also signals a continued shift in economic tracking priorities toward emerging sectors and social welfare indicators.

据新华社北京9月16日电  （记者王雨萧）为进一步全面深化统计改革，以统计现代化服务中国式现代化，国家统计局16日对外发布《统计改革发展“十五五”规划》。

规划聚焦“十五五”时期统计改革发展目标，从健全统计制度方法体系、完善国民经济核算制度、健全现代化产业统计、深化统计重点领域改革等方面作出多项重要部署。

规划提出，丰富反映新经济新领域发展、新质生产力发展、社会民生改善等方面的统计指标，健全支撑高质量发展的统计指标体系。进一步深化统计核算制度方法改革，巩固地区生产总值统一核算改革成果，提高统计核算的科学性规范性，增强核算数据的国际可比性，为国家宏观经济治理提供高质量核算结果。加强对农业、制造业、服务业及三次产业融合发展的统计和监测，更好反映现代化产业体系建设情况。

规划明确，围绕发展新质生产力、加快构建新发展格局、加快全面绿色转型，锚定建设科技强国、建设强大国内市场、建设美丽中国，完善调查制度，加强监测分析，为推动高质量发展提供坚实统计保障。围绕惠民利民、投资于人加强统计监测，优化相关领域统计调查制度方法和指标体系，科学客观反映人民生活改善和社会事业发展情况，为推动人的全面发展、全体人民共同富裕提供统计依据。严查严治数据造假行为，加大重大统计违法案件查处力度，统筹纠治统计数据虚报、瞒报、拒报行为，通报典型案件，深化警示教育。

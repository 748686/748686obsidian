Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Crisis and Corporate Controversy Involving Megaspeed

## Event Overview

Between November 12 and 16, 2026, multiple news reports surfaced regarding a crisis involving Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. The events include allegations linking Huang to a company named Megaspeed, calls for his removal from Nvidia, and legislative actions by the U.S. Congress to tighten AI chip export rules in response to the scandal. Reports indicate that Huang is actively seeking support from allies to maintain his position despite public criticism and government scrutiny.

## Core Facts

- **Allegations and Corporate Link**: A company named Megaspeed has been linked to the downfall of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Megaspeed has publicly stated that it operates as a "legitimate and lawful business" (Source: Article #198).
- **Calls for Leadership Change**: U.S. Secretary of the Treasury Scott Bessent has called for Nvidia to undergo a "shake-up," specifically suggesting the appointment of an external CEO (Source: Article #222).
- **Huang’s Response**: Jensen Huang is discussing his comeback as Nvidia CEO with his allies (Source: Article #221).
- **Legislative Action**: The U.S. Congress is moving to pass tighter AI chip export rules following the Nvidia scandal (Source: Article #228).

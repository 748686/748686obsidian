## Core Facts
*   **Date of Publication:** October 13, 2026.
*   **Source:** *People's Daily*, Page 08 (Economy).
*   **Policy Announcement:** On October 12, 2026, the Ministry of Finance and the State Taxation Administration issued an announcement regarding the adjustment of export tax rebate policies for certain products.
*   **Effective Date of Policy:** April 1, 2027.
*   **Specific Policy Changes:**
    *   The value-added tax (VAT) export tax rebate rate for new energy vehicles (NEVs) is reduced from 13% to 9%.
    *   VAT export tax rebates are canceled for electronic cigarette equipment and devices, among other products.
*   **Tax Scope:** For products subject to consumption tax within the listed categories, the export consumption tax policy remains unchanged and continues to apply the consumption tax rebate/exemption policy.
*   **Determination Basis:** The applicable export tax rebate rates for the listed products are defined by the export date noted on the export goods customs declaration form.
*   **Reported Context:** The news report cited an analysis titled "Why China's Innovation Clusters Have Continuously Ranked First," which was published on the same newspaper page.

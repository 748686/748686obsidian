## Event Conclusion
On October 13, 2026, *People's Daily* published commentaries asserting that the transition from traditional to new energy drivers in China’s historic industrial regions is a complementary relay rather than a zero-sum opposition. Using Ningxia as the primary case study, the articles highlight how photovoltaic and wind energy can be integrated with traditional coal and thermal power through storage, grid transmission, and industrial diversification (e.g., green hydrogen, data centers). The consensus presented is that "optimizing the old" (coal/thermal) and "cultivating the new" (renewables) must occur simultaneously to ensure energy security and industrial competitiveness. Specific technical models in Zhongwei and Ningdong are cited as replicable paths for modern industrial system construction.

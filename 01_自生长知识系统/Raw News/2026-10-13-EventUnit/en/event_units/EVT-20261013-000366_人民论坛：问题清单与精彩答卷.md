## Event Overview

On October 13, 2026, the *People's Daily* published articles on its front pages and in its commentary sections detailing significant developments in Sino-Russian cross-border ecological cooperation and Hong Kong-Shenzhen border infrastructure. The events center on two primary announcements: the successful joint population census of wild Amur tigers and leopards across the China-Russia border, and the official opening of the newly reconstructed Huanggang Port, which has streamlined cross-boundary travel procedures between Shenzhen and Hong Kong.

The editorial framing within the newspaper ("把问题清单变成精彩答卷" / "Turning the Problem List into an Excellent Answer Sheet") highlights a broader narrative of resolving developmental challenges through innovative policy and technological implementation (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).

## Core Facts

*   **Sino-Russian Cross-Border Wildlife Conservation:**
    *   Northeast Tiger and Leopard National Park (spanning Jilin and Heilongjiang provinces) and Russia's Land of the Leopard National Park have established a cooperative protection zone covering approximately 17,000 square kilometers (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   A joint individual identification and statistical survey utilizing a shared methodology (pattern matching to create "fingerprint IDs") has confirmed that 21 Amur tigers and 27 Amur leopards regularly migrate across the border (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   A specific female tiger, termed the "heroic tiger mother," was recorded in November 2025 with five cubs—the highest recorded rate of single-litter survival in China—having given birth to 13 cubs across four litters since crossing from Russia in 2018 (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   Since 2023, 290 wildlife migration corridors have been opened along the border fencing, with infrared cameras recording over 30,000 crossings by various species (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   Within the past five years of the park's establishment, 156 mining rights were closed, 276 contracting projects were cleared, and 81,500 cattle were removed from core habitats (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   This joint monitoring effort is described as the first instance of unified standards and protocols for transboundary wildlife in the international conservation field (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).

*   **Huanggang Port Reopening:**
    *   The Huanggang Port (Huanggang Kou岸) in Shenzhen officially reopened on October 12, 2026, at 06:30, following a seven-year reconstruction and upgrade (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   The port now operates under a "cooperative inspection, single release" (合作查验、一次放行) model, allowing travelers to clear both Shenzhen and Hong Kong immigration and customs checks simultaneously in a single queue (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   For small passenger vehicles, the integration of five regulatory agencies (Shenzhen Immigration, Shenzhen Customs, Hong Kong Immigration, Hong Kong Agriculture/Fisheries/Conservation Department, and Hong Kong Customs) has reduced clearance time from approximately 10 minutes to 2 minutes (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).
    *   The facility is described as the largest single-building land port in the world (People's Daily, Oct 13, 2026).

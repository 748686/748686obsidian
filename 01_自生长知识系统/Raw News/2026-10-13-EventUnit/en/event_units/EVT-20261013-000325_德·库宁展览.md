## Event Overview

On 13 October 2026, the Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam launched "Museumama," a retrospective exhibition featuring the work of Willem de Kooning (1904–1997). The exhibition is framed within the context of de Kooning’s long-standing association with Amsterdam and his relationship to the city, where he lived for 15 years during his youth and returned periodically throughout his career. The exhibit highlights his engagement with the city as both an inspiration and a subject, positioning the museum's collections alongside works from international lenders to explore themes of memory, loss, and artistic development.

## Core Facts

- **Exhibition Title:** Museumama
- **Artist:** Willem de Kooning (1904–1997)
- **Institution:** Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam
- **Launch Date:** 13 October 2026
- **Scale:** One of the most extensive de Kooning exhibitions in over a decade.
- **Curatorial Focus:** De Kooning’s relationship with Amsterdam; the tension between memory and reality in his work, particularly regarding his childhood memories of the city which contrasted with his later perceptions.
- **Historical Context:** De Kooning lived in the Netherlands from age 16 to 21. The exhibition explores his early years there, including his service with the German army in Ukraine during World War I, and his eventual emigration to the United States in 1926.

- **Loan Sources:** Works are sourced from the Musée National d'Art Moderne (Centre Pompidou), Kunstmuseum Den Haag, MoMA, and various private collectors.

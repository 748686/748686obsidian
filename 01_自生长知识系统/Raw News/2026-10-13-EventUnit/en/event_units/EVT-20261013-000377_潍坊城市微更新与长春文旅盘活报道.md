## Core Facts
Based on the supplied articles, the following facts are established:

### Changchun, Jilin (Source: Article #491)
1.  **China FAW Museum:** Opened in July 2026 in Changchun. It is converted from the Tool Factory of the First Automobile Works (established 1953). In its first month, it received over 100,000 visitors. It displays nearly 150 physical vehicles and over 1,000 historical artifacts.
2.  **Route 54 Tram:** Originally an industrial commuter line, now a cultural tourism special train. It utilizes a "leaf cleanup not swept" (落叶不扫) fine management approach to create scenic views in autumn.
3.  **Xinmin Avenue:** A century-old street and Jilin's first "China Historical and Cultural Street." It underwent a protection and upgrading project that removed walls, opened closed historical courtyards, and implemented pedestrianization without major demolition. "Xinmin Towards New Celebrating the Birthday" themed activities were held during the National Day holiday.
4.  **Xiguilin Block:** Idle boiler rooms and old laundry rooms were upgraded into trendy leisure landmarks featuring dessert baking and music.
5.  **Changchun Zoo:** Transformed into a popular cultural tourism check-in spot with immersive performances available day and night.
6.  **Nanhu Area:** The park implemented a "demolish perimeter, reveal green" project to integrate the garden with the city. The "Nanhu Spring Islet" area hosts regular university band residencies, flash mobs, and magic shows.
7.  **Other Transformations:** 
    *   Nanling Water Purification Plant → Changchun Water Culture Ecological Park (preserving industrial heritage).
    *   Beihu Mud Depression → Beihu National Wetland Park (ecological restoration).

### Hainan Maona Village (Source: Article #492)
1.  **Location:** Maona Village, Shuiman Township, Wuzhishan City, Hainan Province. Located at the highest altitude township on Hainan Island.
2.  **Demographics:** 165 households, 646 permanent residents. Terrain described as "nine mountains, half water, and half a field."
3.  **Economic Shift:** Previously impoverished (per capita income 3,000 RMB/year). Pivot to tea industry after 80% of village area was designated part of the Hainan Tropical Rainforest National Park (established 2021), prohibiting old livelihoods (firewood, medicinal herb foraging).
4.  **Tea Industry Development:**
    *   2024: Three group standards for Wuzhishan Tropical Rainforest Large-leaf Tea released; confirmed as a new Camellia species.
    *   June 2025: "Wuzhishan Black Tea" obtained Geographical Indication product recognition.
    *   Model: "Village Committee + Enterprise + Cooperative + Farmer." Tea garden area expanded from ~600 mu to 3,388 mu.
    *   2025 Tea Green Revenue: 3.5 million RMB.
5.  **Agritourism:** Maona Village Industry Development Co., Ltd. (village collective控股) established in Sept 2023. Adheres to底线 (bottom line): no demolition of houses, no cutting of trees, no occupation of farmland. Built picking trails and viewing platforms. Hosts "Early Spring Tea Opening Festival" and "Rainforest Time" concerts. 6 agritainment homes and 28 homestay rooms operational.
6.  **Cultural Heritage Integration:** 
    *   Li Pottery Intangible Cultural Heritage Transmission Center: Tourist experiences generate sales.
    *   Li Medicine Intangible Cultural Heritage Proprietor Wang Yuzhen: Sold over 100,000 RMB worth of products (e.g., sprain plaster) over 3 years.
7.  **2025 Economic Data:**
    *   Tourists: 220,000.
    *   Tourism Revenue: >11 million RMB.
    *   Village Collective Economy Income: 3.06 million RMB (nearly 20x increase since 2021).
    *   Per Capita Disposable Income of Farmers: >28,000 RMB.
8.  **Recognition:** Maona Village Party Branch awarded "National Advanced Grassroots Party Organization" in July 2026.

## Event Overview
On October 13, 2026, *People's Daily* reported that Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region has introduced innovative regulatory service models for street food vendors. The regional initiative focuses on implementing code management (assignment of unique identification codes) and a "compensation-first" mechanism for consumer complaints. These measures aim to improve food safety oversight while enhancing consumer protection in the informal food sector.

## Core Facts
*   **Location:** Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, China.
*   **Date of Report:** October 13, 2026 (*People's Daily*, Page 10).
*   **Key Measure 1: Code Management:** Vendors are assigned unique codes to facilitate traceability and regulatory tracking.
*   **Key Measure 2: Compensation-First:** A mechanism where consumer compensation is processed prior to the final determination of vendor liability, ensuring quicker redress for consumers.
*   **Outcome Metric:** Following the implementation of these measures, complaints and reports regarding food vendors decreased by 26.9%.

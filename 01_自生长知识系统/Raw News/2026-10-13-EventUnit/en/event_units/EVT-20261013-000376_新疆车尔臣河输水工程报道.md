### 2. Weifang Urban Micro-Renewal Initiative (Reported Oct 13, 2026)
*   **Source:** *People's Daily*, Page 07, Article ID 30185280.
*   **Status:** Substantive report available.
*   **Authors:** Li Bo, Hou Linliang.
*   **Editorial Credits:** Managed by editors Guan Xuanyue, Lu Tao, and Zhai Qinqi.
*   **Location:** Weifang City, Shandong Province, China.
*   **Case Study 1: Bailanghe Eco-Park (Ecological River Management)**
    *   **Scale:** 6.7 km length; area >10 sq km. It is a National Urban Wetland Park.
    *   **Methodology:** "Water purification by fish" strategy involving introduction of bighead carp, silver carp, and grass carp.
    *   **Infrastructure:** 11 fixed pump stations in the mid-to-upper reaches recycle treated wastewater from downstream sewage plants back upstream to Yuandu Lake for year-round ecological replenishment.
    *   **Outcomes:** Biodiversity increased (30+ bird species including little terns and egrets; dozens of mammals including squirrels and martens). Economic and ecological benefits realized through引入 (introduction) of研学 (educational tours), team-building, wedding, and film industry formats.
    *   **Key Figure:** Ma Gang (Park Management Office Director); Fan Xiangxin (Head of Cultural Tourism Development, Weifang Cultural Tourism Group).

*   **Case Study 2: Xiyuan Street (Old District Renovation)**
    *   **Profile:** Historic street with 230 shops and 120 convenient stalls. Known for traditional foods like Chaotian pot and meat火烧 (huoshao).
    *   **Problems Prior to Renovation:** Potholed roads, flooding during rain, chaotic parking, affecting resident and merchant livelihoods.
    *   **Intervention:**
        *   Infrastructure: Full road reconstruction, re-planning of non-motorized vehicle parking, 2,600-meter yellow operating line marking to resolve pavement damage and parking issues.
        *   Cultural Elements: "Xiyuan Ruisiang" (Western Garden auspicious elephant) pillar as a landmark; "Xiyuan Seal Rhyme · Market Long Song" landscape belt with hand-painted murals of old Weixian life.
    *   **Outcomes:**
        *   Foot traffic doubled compared to pre-renovation levels.
        *   Some characteristic stores saw revenue growth exceeding 100%.
    *   **Governance Model:** Formation of a self-governance alliance of 16 merchant representatives; implementation of star-rating assessments and "maha (stool) discussions" (community dialogue). Shift from passive management to active participation.
    *   **Key Figures:** Yu Haige (Market Responsible Person); Qin Aihui (Merchant resident for 20+ years).

*   **Case Study 3: Yuqing Street Bailang River Bridge Underpass (Micro-Space Activation)**
    *   **Intervention:** Cleared debris and "zombie vehicles"; painted murals featuring local icons (Bohai Eye, Kuiwen Gate, Weifang kites) on bridge piers; installed varied flooring (cobblestones, colored rubber running tracks); added smart parking and charging piles.
    *   **Zoning:** East bank features chess-patterned seating; west bank features dedicated areas for table tennis, square dancing, and ballroom dancing.
    *   **Outcome:** Transformed into a composite space for tourism, sports, and social interaction with long-term maintenance responsibilities assigned to local authorities.
    *   **Key Figure:** Liu Yun (Secretary of the Weifang Municipal Party Committee).

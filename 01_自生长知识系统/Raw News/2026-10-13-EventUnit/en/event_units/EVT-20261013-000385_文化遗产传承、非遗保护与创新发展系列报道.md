## Event Overview
On October 13, 2026, *People's Daily* published a series of reports across its Theory (Page 09), Political (Page 10), and Cultural (Page 11) sections focusing on the transmission of Chinese cultural lineage, the innovation of intangible cultural heritage (ICH) protection, and specific case studies of cultural preservation. The coverage encompasses theoretical foundations, policy deployments for the "15th Five-Year Plan," financial consumer protection (as a parallel governance topic in the same section), local governance innovations, and diverse on-the-ground examples of cultural and technological integration.

## Core Facts
*   **Policy Framework:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" for Intangible Cultural Heritage Protection and Inheritance explicitly deploys the exploration of new forms of expression and utilization for ICH, aiming to cultivate new ICH inheritance experience scenes. This marks the first time "ICH inheritance experience new scenes" have been incorporated into national economic and social development top-level design (Article #508).
*   **Theoretical Basis:** Xi Jinping Diplomacy Thought is rooted in 5,000+ years of Chinese civilization, realizing the creative transformation and innovative development of fine traditional Chinese culture. Concepts such as "harmony without uniformity" and "universal peace" underpin the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind (Article #507).
*   **New Scene Mechanism:** ICH experience new scenes are characterized by two shifts: from large-scale facility construction to refined, distributed layout in public spaces (museums, malls, communities), and from passive protection to active empowerment via market and social co-construction (Article #508).
*   **Financial Governance Context:** On October 12, 2026, the Supreme People's Court, People's Bank of China, National Financial Regulatory Administration, and Securities Regulatory Commission jointly released six typical cases protecting financial consumers, emphasizing "seller due diligence" as a prerequisite for "buyer bears the risk" (Article #509).
*   **Tourism Regulation:** The Anhui Provincial Department of Culture and Tourism canceled the 4A rating for six scenic spots and downgraded one due to issues like long-term closure and safety hazards, shifting from passive removal to active clearance (Article #510).
*   **Specific Cases:**
    *   **Hongjun Bridge:** A village generation protects a Red Army bridge, linking local history with red culture preservation (mentioned in Page 10 index, Article #509/510 context).
    *   **Qingbai Ware/Qing Kiln:** In Hangzhou, inheritor Jin Xin developed a digital cultural research system for Southern Song official kiln porcelain, using 3D modeling and AI to assist young learners (Article #521).
    *   **Changshengqiao Aesthetic Market:** In Chongqing's Qianjiang District, 20+ ICH items (shadow puppetry, paper-cutting, etc.) are integrated into a permanent market摊位 (stall) model (Article #523).
    *   **Zhongnan University:** The library launched a "Food in Classics" short video campaign with over 100 million views, and an "AI Micro-teaching Room" serving 2,100+ normal students (Article #520).
    *   **Hongjava Village:** Uses art to revitalize rural areas (referenced in the prompt's cluster reasoning).
    *   **Red Army Bridge:** A village generation guards the Red Army bridge (referenced in the prompt's cluster reasoning).

**2. Long March Spirit and Red Culture in Yunxi County**
*   **Location:** Yunxi County, Shiyan City, Hubei Province.
*   **Key Artifact:** A yellowed mimeographed leaflet titled *"What is the Red Army"* (《什么是红军》), printed on December 10, 1934, by the Political Department of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Northward Anti-Japanese Vanguard.
*   **Historical Context:** 
    *   In December 1934, the 25th Red Army entered the Yunxi area to create the E-Yu-Shan (Hubei-Henan-Shaanxi) Revolutionary Base.
    *   On February 19, 1935, the "Yunxi Conference" was held at Lachi Courtyard, Huping Village, Yunxi County.
    *   Local youth Li Yucai (from Hutouyan Village) joined the Red Army after hearing of the conference. Due to his mother's illness, he returned home, carrying the leaflet to propagate revolutionary ideas.
    *   After the 25th Red Army left for the rest of the Long March in July 1935, Li Yucai remained. His wife, Liu Liying, an illiterate peasant woman, concealed the leaflet in a genealogy book, wrapped it in red cloth, and hid it in a wall crevice covered with thick mud.
*   **Preservation and Current Status:**
    *   Liu Liying surrendered the leaflet to the state in 1981 during a revolutionary cultural relic collection drive; it is now stored in the Yunxi County Archives.
    *   In August 2024, the Long March National Cultural Park (Yunxi Section) officially opened, including the former site of the 25th Red Army Corps headquarters, the 223rd Regiment Political Department, and the "Yunxi Conference" site.
    *   Yunxi County has 75 red heritage sites, accounting for one-third of the province's Long March-related remains.
    *   A 260-member宣讲队 (propaganda team) named "Hong Sheng Liao Liang" tells the story of Liu Liying.
    *   Cultural products based on this IP include plays such as *Spark on Paper* and *The Leaflet Spark Illuminates Yunxi*. Over 180 cultural creative products have been developed, with annual sales exceeding 50 million RMB.

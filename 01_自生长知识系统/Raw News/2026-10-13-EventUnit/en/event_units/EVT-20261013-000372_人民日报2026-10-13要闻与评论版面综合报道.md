# Event Name
2026 Beijing "Respond and Act Immediately" Reform Forum Opening and Central Political and Legal Affairs Commission Food Safety Policy Release

## Event Overview
On October 12, 2026, two significant policy and reform events occurred in China. First, the 2026 Beijing "Respond and Act Immediately" (Jiesu Jiban) Reform Forum opened at the National Conference Center in Beijing. Second, Central Political and Legal Affairs Commission (CPLAC) officials provided a detailed interpretation of a new opinion titled "On Strictly Investigating and Dealing with Crimes Endangering Food Safety in Accordance with the Law," issued recently following a September 24, 2026, special meeting. Both events were reported by *People's Daily* on October 13, 2026, highlighting the Chinese government's focus on urban governance innovation and food safety regulation.

## Core Facts

### 1. 2026 Beijing "Respond and Act Immediately" Reform Forum
*   **Date:** October 12, 2026.
*   **Location:** National Conference Center, Beijing.
*   **Theme:** The permanent theme is "People's Cities, Co-creating a Better Future." The 2026 annual theme is "Digital Intelligence Era and People's Cities."
*   **Organizers:** Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, China Media Group, Beijing Municipal Committee of the Communist Party of China, and Beijing Municipal People's Government.
*   **Scale:** Over 3,200 guests attended, including more than 240 international guests from over 70 countries.
*   **Structure:** The forum included an opening ceremony, main forum, 6 parallel forums, 4 salons, and a closing ceremony. Supporting activities included the screening of the documentary "Your Voice 2," "Walking into 12345" events, and city tours.
*   **Key Outputs:** Several research成果 (achievements) were released:
    *   *Beijing "Respond and Act Immediately" Reform Theoretical Research Results (2026)*
    *   *Why is Urban Governance So: Research Based on Beijing's "Respond and Act Immediately" Reform Practice*
    *   *Global City Hotline Service and Governance Efficiency Evaluation Report (2026)*
    *   *National 12345 Hotline Service and Governance Efficiency Evaluation Report (2026)*
    *   *Global Urban Governance Typical Cases (2026)*
*   **Significance:** Aimed at promoting the transition of Beijing's reform from empirical summary to theoretical explanation and from Chinese practice to international comparison, providing experience and theoretical support for global urban governance. The forum is held biennially.

### 2. CPLAC Food Safety Policy Interpretation
*   **Policy Document:** *Opinion on Strictly Investigating and Dealing with Crimes Endangering Food Safety in Accordance with the Law* (issued by CPLAC).
*   **Background:** A CPLAC special meeting was held on September 24, 2026, to study the deployment of work on strictly investigating food safety crimes. An official interpretation was given by a CPLAC responsible comrade on October 12, 2026.
*   **Context:** While China's food safety situation is stable and improving, new issues have emerged:
    *   Crimes are becoming networked, professionalized, and chain-like.
    *   Gaps exist in the connection between administrative law enforcement and criminal justice.
    *   High-frequency incidents occurred recently, specifically involving "formaldehyde cabbage," "dichlorvos disinfection," and "medicine-water bayberries," which shocked the food safety bottom line.
*   **Core Principles:**
    1.  **People-centered:** Focus on meat products, campus food, health foods, vegetables, and fruits.
    2.  **Strict tone:** Maintain high pressure, increase criminal costs, and deter offenders.
    3.  **Rule of law:** Use legal thinking to govern food safety, improving laws, cases, and industry supervision.
*   **Specific Measures:**
    *   **Strict Investigation:** Focus on key links (production, online sales, dining, imports). Strictly control probation and exemption from criminal punishment. Strengthen supervision of major cases.
    *   **Administrative-Criminal Connection:** Resolve unclear case standards and irregular transfer procedures. Establish an information-sharing platform for administrative-criminal connection. Improve the reverse connection mechanism between administrative penalties and criminal penalties.
    *   **Legal Policy Improvement:** Accelerate the comprehensive revision of the Food Safety Law to address new formats like fresh produce e-commerce and live-streaming sales. Update lists of illegal additives.
    *   **Source Governance:** Issue judicial/procuratorial recommendations to improve full-chain supervision. Use big data legal supervision models and public interest litigation. Promote pre-event prevention.

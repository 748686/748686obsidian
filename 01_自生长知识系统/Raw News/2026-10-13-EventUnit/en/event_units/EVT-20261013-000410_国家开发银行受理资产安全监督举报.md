## Core Facts

### Establishment and Scope
- On October 12, 2021, China formally established five national parks: Sanjiangyuan, Giant Panda, Northeast Tiger and Leopard, Hainan Tropical Rainforest, and Wuyishan.
- The total area of these five national parks is 230,000 square kilometers.
- They integrate and optimize over 120 various types of nature reserves, covering representative ecosystems such as river headwaters, alpine forests, temperate mixed forests, tropical rainforests, and subtropical forests.

### Ecological Performance (5-Year Assessment)
- **Overall Evaluation:** 75% of ecological benefit assessment indicators are rated "excellent."
- **Core Services:** Functions such as water conservation, carbon sequestration and oxygen release, and soil and water conservation have been effectively enhanced.
- **Sanjiangyuan National Park:** Acts as the "Water Tower of China," delivering an average of 80 billion cubic meters of high-quality water downstream annually. The source notes that the landscape of "thousands of lakes competing for beauty" has reappeared in Maduo County, Guoluo Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, at the source of the Yellow River.
- **Giant Panda National Park:** 
    - Opened 13 ecological corridors for local wild giant panda populations, significantly improving habitat connectivity.
    - The Nibaba Mountain ecological corridor (connecting the Qionglai and Daxiangling mountain ranges) has restored over 7,000 mu of damaged habitat, replanted more than 120,000 native trees, and 60,000 clumps of bamboo. Infrared cameras have captured giant pandas with cubs traversing the corridor.
- **Northeast Tiger and Leopard National Park:** 
    - Implemented ecological protection and restoration projects covering 600,000 mu.
    - Established an integrated space-air-ground ecological monitoring system with over 20,000 infrared cameras.
    - Cumulative monitoring records since establishment: over 30,000 instances of Northeast tiger activity and over 40,000 instances of Northeast leopard activity.
- **Hainan Tropical Rainforest National Park:** 
    - Strengthened strict management of natural forests and implemented rainforest ecological restoration.
    - Selected as a pilot for the national ecological product value realization mechanism.
    - The ecosystem gross product reached 208.966 billion yuan.
- **Wuyishan National Park:** 
    - Implemented a redemption mechanism for commercial forests in key locations.
    - Discovered new species including the *Yushen horned toad* (雨神角蟾), *Wuyi tree frog* (武夷林蛙), and *Fujian ma-tai orchid* (福建天麻), enriching the biodiversity baseline.

### Flagship Species Protection Milestones
- **Tibetan Antelope (Sanjiangyuan):** Population recovered to over 70,000.
- **Northeast Tiger (Northeast Tiger and Leopard NP):** Increased from 27 at establishment to 72.
- **Northeast Leopard (Northeast Tiger and Leopard NP):** Increased from 42 at establishment to 115.
- **Hainan Gibbon (Hainan Tropical Rainforest NP):** Expanded to 7 groups and 44 individuals; noted as the only gibbon population globally showing sustained growth.
- **Giant Panda (Giant Panda NP):** Protects over 70% of China's wild giant panda population.

### Community and Livelihood Impact
- Nearly 50,000 local residents are employed as ecological patrol officers across the national parks.
- **Income:** Patrol officers earn an average annual wage income of 12,000 to 21,000 yuan.
- **Sanjiangyuan Specifics:** Approximately 27,000 ecological patrol officers are stationed in Sanjiangyuan National Park.
- **Public Engagement:** Over 14 million visits by the public were recorded over the five-year period through nature education and ecological tourism.

### Future Outlook
- During the "15th Five-Year Plan" period, China plans to continue high-quality construction of national parks and steadily establish new ones.
- Priorities include habitat and corridor construction, biodiversity protection projects, and improving policies for ecological compensation and wildlife damage compensation.

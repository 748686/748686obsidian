### 2. Nanjing Luhe District Aerospace Industry Development
*   **Strategic Shift:** Luhe District, located approximately 40 kilometers from downtown Nanjing, is transitioning from an industry focused on supporting the Nanjing Chemical Industrial Park to a hub for the aerospace sector.
*   **Key Infrastructure ("Aerospace Creation Corridor"):** A new industrial layout has emerged comprising:
    *   Nanhang International Innovation Port
    *   Aerospace Digital Intelligence Industrial Park
    *   Aerospace Industry Park
    *   Luhe Aerospace Port
    *   **Pattern:** Described as "one body, two wings, one port," linking R&D, tech transfer, manufacturing, and commercial applications.
*   **Major Project:** Starry Sky Dynamics (Galaxy Dynamics) base in Luhe District is preparing for the production of the "Pallas Athena-2" (Zhishenxing-2) rocket.
    *   Total Phase I Investment: Approximately **2 billion RMB**.
    *   Capacity: Designed for 10 rockets annually.
    *   Function: Final assembly and testing of large reusable liquid rockets.
*   **Research & Innovation Ecosystem:**
    *   Collaborative establishment of the Nanhang International Innovation Port with Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA) in 2021.
    *   Lab facilities include a National Key Laboratory for Complex Product Intelligent Manufacturing System Technology.
    *   Metrics: 6 National Key Laboratories, 10 University-Enterprise Joint Labs, and 13 Provincial/Ministerial Innovation Platforms introduced.
    *   A company, Nanjing Yangtze River Industrial Technology Research Institute Co., Ltd., facilitates concept validation, pilot-scale熟化 (maturation), and enterprise incubation.
*   **Industrial Spillover:**
    *   **CNC Machine Tools:** The high-end CNC machine tool industrial park has attracted over 130 enterprises; more than 90% of parts can be sourced within the park.
    *   **Specific Tech:** A positioning device co-developed by Nanjing Sufeng CNC Technology Co., Ltd. and NUAA is now used in the C919 large passenger aircraft.
    *   **Investment:** Attraction of **General Technology Group** in the previous year to tackle core precision CNC technology bottlenecks.
*   **Economic Performance:**
    *   2025: Above-scale industrial output value achieved double-digit growth.
    *   First half of 2026: Continued significant growth.
*   **Official Statements:**
    *   **Rong Fei (District Mayor):** Emphasized finding the intersection between local endowments and industrial needs rather than chasing trends.
    *   **Li Kai (Deputy Director of Science and Technology Bureau):** Provided data on lab facilities.
    *   **Zhou Yong (Party Secretary):** Outlined future goals to build an important position for Jiangsu's commercial aerospace industry and synergize four advanced manufacturing clusters (New Energy, High-end New Materials, High-end Intelligent Manufacturing Equipment, Aerospace).

# Event Unit: Synthesis of Chinese Ecological and Environmental Policy Milestones

## Event Overview

On October 13, 2026, *People's Daily* published a report from Urumqi detailing the release of findings from the Third Comprehensive Scientific Expedition of Xinjiang. This event marks the culmination of a systematic assessment of Xinjiang's resource environment conducted between 2021 and 2026. The published results highlight significant ecological improvements, including climate warming and wetting trends, reduced desertification, and expanded green zones. Concurrently, this report was part of a broader editorial page in *People's Daily* (Page 14, Ecology) that also referenced the five-year achievements of China's first batch of national parks and the initiation of the third round, seventh batch of central ecological and environmental protection inspections.

## Core Facts

**Ecological and Climatic Trends in Xinjiang:**
*   **Climate Shift:** Over the past 30 years, Xinjiang has exhibited a持续 (sustained) trend of warming and wetting.
*   **Hydrology:** Annual average river runoff over the last 30 years has increased by approximately 10 billion cubic meters compared to the preceding 40 years. Projections indicate that mountain water inflow will remain at a high oscillation and increasing trend for the next 20 to 40 years.
*   **Land Rehabilitation:** Desertification and land sandification have achieved a "dual reduction." The oases area has newly increased by 45,500 square kilometers.
*   **Disaster Mitigation:** The area at high risk for sandstorm disasters has shrunk by one-third.

**Biodiversity and Biological Resources:**
*   **New Discoveries:** The expedition discovered 33 new species.
*   **Record Additions:** 243 new species were added to the records for China or Xinjiang.
*   **Genetic Resources:** Over 300 stress-resistance genes were obtained.
*   **Flora:** 315 types of halophytes (salt-tolerant plants) were identified, leading to proposed agricultural development paths for saline soils based on these plants.

**Agricultural and Geological Findings:**
*   **Soil Quality:** A contiguous selenium-rich soil belt covering over 22 million mu was identified in the Tarim Basin.
*   **Energy Minerals:** Theoretical and exploration breakthroughs occurred in energy minerals. Ten mineral exploration target areas were delineated in the昆仑山 (Kunlun Mountains) Huoshaoxia mining district.
*   **Coal Reserves:** An oil-bearing coal resource quantity of 77.5 billion tons was verified in the Turpan-Hami Basin.

**Policy Context (From Page 14 Layout):**
*   **National Parks:** The first batch of national parks (including Sanjiangyuan and four others) has achieved significant results in ecological restoration, flagship species protection, and community co-construction over their first five years.
*   **Inspection Initiation:** The third round, seventh batch of central ecological and environmental protection inspections has been fully launched.

**Expedition Scope:**
*   The Third Comprehensive Scientific Expedition of Xinjiang started in 2021.
*   It covers five key areas: the Tarim River, Ili River, Ertix River, northern slope of the Tianshan Mountains, and the Turpan-Hami Basin.
*   It serves as a systematic baseline assessment of Xinjiang’s全域 (whole region) resources and environment after many years.

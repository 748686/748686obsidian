# Event: People's Daily Cultural and Social Reporting (November 2, 2026)

## Event Overview

This synthesis covers content from the 11th (Culture) and 12th (Society) pages of the *People's Daily* published on November 2, 2026. The report aggregates four distinct articles focusing on cultural preservation, educational-industrial integration, agricultural security, and vocational skills development. The events are geographically distributed across Jiangsu, Chongqing, and Henan provinces, with policy context provided at the national level for disability prevention.

## Core Facts

### 1. Preservation of Rare Opera: Liyuan Drama
*   **Subject:** The article highlights the rejuvenation of Liyuan drama, referred to as a "living fossil of ancient southern opera."
*   **Theme:** Focuses on the "youthful" generation taking up this rare art form (Article #334).

### 2. School-Enterprise Cooperation in Yangzhou, Jiangsu
*   **Location:** Yangzhou, Jiangsu Province.
*   **Mechanism:** The "Science and Technology Innovation Navigation" (Ke Chuang Zhu Hang) special envoy group facilitates cooperation between universities and enterprises.
*   **Key Figures:**
    *   **Zhu Yadong:** Head of the Mechanical Technology Department at Yangzhou Vocational University of Technology. He uses real-world industrial cases (e.g., multi-axis coordinated intelligent control optimization derived from 3 months of joint debugging) in his smart processing production line technology classes (Article #334, Article #335).
    *   **Guo Yongqiang:** Head of Planning and Operations at the Beijing Electromechanical Research Institute. He serves as a science and technology mayor brigade member selected by Yangzhou and teaches on-site at the institute's Yangtze River Delta Innovation Center in Yangzhou (Article #335).
*   **Data Points:**
    *   Zhu Yadong visited over 20 local enterprises to address "difficulty in recruiting and retaining workers" (Article #334).
    *   The "Ke Chuang Zhu Hang" team has sent 5 batches, 106人次 (person-times) of university experts/professors.
    *   Outcomes include 232 school-enterprise cooperation projects, 528 introduced talents, and 57 enterprise R&D platforms established (Article #334).
    *   Yangzhou has selected 17 batches totaling 868 university experts to serve in science and technology mayoral brigades (Article #335).
    *   A specific "Joint Training Class" for Yangzhou University's School of Mechanical Engineering enrolled students in 2025, benefiting from the Beijing Electromechanical Research Institute's relocation of its high-end precision stamping project to Yangzhou in 2021 (Article #335).
*   **Policy Context:** Yangzhou is exploring a "High-Level Talent Sharing Plan" with a tripartite model (government support, university staffing, enterprise待遇/treatment). The "Lüyang Jinphoenix Plan" includes a talent attack consortium special fund, with subsidies up to 5 million yuan per consortium (Article #334).

### 3. Food Security in Chongqing
*   **Location:** Chongqing Municipality.
*   **Strategy:** Implementation of "Storing Grain in Land and Storing Grain in Technology" (Article #331, Article #336).
*   **2025 Performance:**
    *   Grain sowing area: 30.509 million mu.
    *   Grain output: 22.25 billion jin (111.25 billion kg), a 20-year high.
    *   Achieved "Six Consecutive Years of Abundant Harvest" in total output and "Ten Consecutive Years of Increase" in yield per unit area (Article #331, Article #336).
*   **Current Year (2026) Data:**
    *   Summer grain sowing area: 5.661 million mu.
    *   Summer grain output: 1.279 million tons (Article #331, Article #336).
*   **Key Measures:**
    *   **"Three-Party Co-cultivation":** A model involving supply and marketing cooperatives, village collectives, and农户 (farmers) to reclaim wasteland and utilize winter fallow fields (e.g., rapeseed-rice or rapeseed-sorghum rotation) (Article #331, Article #336).
    *   **Digital Management:** Use of an "AI + Food Security" digital base, including a farmland planting purpose control platform and "greenhouse house" smart management platform (Article #331, Article #336).
    *   **"Four Good" Integration:** Integration of good methods, fields, seeds, and machinery (Article #331, Article #336).
    *   **Demonstration Areas:** 700,000 mu of "Hundred-Thousand-Ten Thousand" demonstration areas built across 16 ministerial-level whole-county promotion districts (Article #331, Article #36).
    *   **Subsidies:** 100 million yuan invested in soybean-corn strip composite planting (200 yuan/mu direct subsidy); scale planting subjects receive approx. 70 yuan/mu; high-yield demonstration subjects can receive 20,000 yuan rewards (Article #331, Article #336).
*   **Sources Attribution:** The article text for this topic appears in both Article #335 (title metadata lists "Chongqing...") and Article #336 (content body contains the full article).

### 4. National Disability Prevention Action Plan (2026-2030)
*   **Source of Policy:** Press conference held by the Office of the State Council Leading Group on Disabled Persons' Affairs in Beijing on October 30, 2026 (Article #333, Article #337).
*   **Plan Structure:** 18 main indicators; 6 major actions and 19 task measures covering science popularization, birth defect control, disease chain prevention, injury prevention, rehabilitation service improvement, and empowerment (Article #333, Article #337).
*   **Key Demographic Risks:**
    *   **Children:** Road traffic injuries, falls, drowning, suffocation, burns, poisoning. Experts note uneven coverage of safety seats/helmets and need for improved product standards (Article #333, Article #337).
    *   **Elderly:** Fall-related disability remains high-risk. Issues include wet floors, thresholds, poor lighting, and weak care support for solitary/frail elderly. The plan requires high-level, standardized fall prevention services and home environment适老化 (age-friendly)改造 (renovation) (Article #333, Article #337).
*   **Occupational Disease Prevention:**
    *   "14th Five-Year" period new occupational disease cases decreased by 49% compared to the "13th Five-Year" period (Article #333, Article #337).
    *   Focus on small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and key industries due to funding/technical limitations in prevention (Article #333, Article #337).
    *   **2030 Target:** 90%+ coverage of county-level occupational health examinations and municipal-level occupational disease diagnosis services (Article #333, Article #337).
*   **Chronic Disease Prevention:**
    *   Approx. 450 million chronic disease patients in China (hypertension, diabetes, cardiovascular/cerebrovascular disease, chronic respiratory disease, chronic kidney disease) (Article #333, Article #337).
    *   Current grassroots standardized management rate for hypertension and diabetes exceeds 60%, but high-risk population screening coverage remains uneven (Article #333, Article #337).
    *   Plan emphasizes stroke and老年期痴呆 (senile dementia) screening, early intervention, and building a dementia-friendly social environment (Article #333, Article #337).

### 5. Second National Civil Affairs Industry Vocational Skills Competition
*   **Location:** Zhengzhou, Henan Province (Finals) (Article #332, Article #338).
*   **Scale:** 32 representative teams, 317 contestants. Preliminary stages included 400+ city-level and 32 provincial-level selection contests with over 20,000 participants since May 2026 (Article #332, Article #338).
*   **Competitions:**
    *   Elderly Caregiver (养老护理员).
    *   Orphan and Disabled Child Caregiver (孤残儿童护理员) - First time included in national class-one skills competition.
    *   Funeral Service Provider (殡仪服务员) - First time included.
    *  遗体防腐整容师 (Body Preservation and Cosmetics Specialist) - First time included.
*   **Features:**
    *   **Technology Integration:** Nearly 20 smart products deployed, including companion robots, patrol robots, smart SOS watches, and one-button call terminals (Article #332, Article #338).
    *   **Safety Assessment:** Fire emergency response is part of the exam (e.g., contestants reported a fire, sealed doors with wet towels, cooled doors, and comforted the "elderly client") (Article #332, Article #338).
    *   **Humanistic Care:** Emphasis on empathy, non-confrontational communication, and怀旧疗法 (reminiscence therapy) using equipment like vintage tape recorders (Article #332, Article #338).
*   **Contestant Profile:** Chen Qiufang (48, Liuzhou, Guangxi) noted her goal was learning advanced skills to serve children better, not just winning (Article #332, Article #338).

*   **Jiangsu (Yangzhou):** Focuses on the **education-technology-talent integration** model. The perspective is developmental, highlighting how local government mechanisms ("Science and Technology Innovation Navigation," "High-Level Talent Sharing") bridge the gap between academic theory and industrial reality (AI, intelligent manufacturing).
*   **Chongqing:** Focuses on **agricultural resilience and food security** in a challenging geographical context (90% mountainous/hilly). The perspective emphasizes technological empowerment ("AI + Food Security") and policy innovation ("Three-Party Co-cultivation") to overcome natural resource constraints.
*   **National (Policy Level):** Focuses on **social welfare and public health infrastructure**. The Disability Prevention Action Plan reflects a shift toward proactive, preventive healthcare and the integration of disability prevention into chronic disease and occupational health management.
*   **Henan (Zhengzhou):** Focuses on **vocational standards and professionalization** of the civil affairs sector. The competition highlights the modernization of caregiving and funeral services through technology (robots, smart devices) and the emphasis on "humanistic care" alongside technical skill.

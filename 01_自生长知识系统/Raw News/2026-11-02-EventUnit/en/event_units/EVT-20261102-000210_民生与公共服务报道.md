## Core Facts

### 1. Grassroots Medical Service Improvement in Yuecheng District, Shaoxing, Zhejiang
- A resident, Li Xiangqing, reported convenience in accessing cardiac CT scans at Taoyan Street Community Health Service Center, stating: “Regular check-ups at my doorstep are convenient and reassuring.” (*Article #339*, Source: paper.people.com.cn)
- Yuecheng District has implemented a “one hospital, one specialty” differentiated development strategy, written into the district government work report for two consecutive years.
- The district has established 24 municipal-level grassroots specialty departments and full coverage of district-level specialty departments.
- Taoyan Street Community Health Service Center receives over one million yuan annually in special funding for upgrades (CT, AI imaging) and early screening for tumors and cardiovascular diseases.
- Over three years, more than 30,000 key-population early screenings were conducted; an abnormal tumor indicator detected by physician Huang Qinqin led to timely referral and diagnosis.
- The district built a hierarchical diagnosis and treatment network: “Provincial People’s Hospital as leader, district hospital as backbone, 17 town/street health centers as主体的主体, village clinics as safety net.”
- Over RMB 11 million was invested in the past three years for standardized and smart renovation of all grassroots stations, creating a “15-minute medical service circle.”

### 2. National Quality Certification Market Regulation
- On November 1, 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issued the *Interim Measures for the Administration of Nationally Unified Voluntary Certification Systems* to规范 (standardize) the quality certification market and support national unified market construction. (*Article #340*, Source: paper.people.com.cn)
- China currently implements 37 nationally promoted voluntary certification systems, with approximately 2.416 million valid certificates issued, accounting for nearly 56% of total quality certification certificates nationwide.
- Green building materials: 5,725 certified enterprises drive industry revenue nearing RMB 130 billion and reduce carbon emissions by over 30 million tons annually.
- Rail transit equipment: Domestic production rate of urban rail vehicles exceeds 95%; self-reliance rate for permanent magnet traction system core control devices reaches 100%; market size exceeds RMB 1.2 trillion.
- Livelihood保障 (guarantee): 34 buildings, including Beijing Daxing International Airport and the National Barrier-Free Environment Exhibition Hall, obtained barrier-free environment certification, serving over 250 million person-times annually.

### 3. First Nanjing Talent Week Opening

- Theme: “Gather in Jinling, Create the Future.”

### 4. Yunan Yongren County “Small County, Large City” Urbanization Strategy
- Since 2025, Yongren County (Chuxiong Yi Autonomous Prefecture, Yunnan), with a total population under 100,000, has pursued urbanization through “small county, large city” development. (*Article #342*, Source: paper.people.com.cn)
- Data highlights:
  - New agricultural transfer population settling in urban areas: 1,905 persons.
  - Forestry and fruit industry output value exceeded RMB 1.9 billion.
  - Seven “Xingfuli Communities” provided employment services 175,900 person-times.
  - Permanent resident urbanization rate rose to 42.98%.
- Policy pillars:
  - Housing security:进城购房 (urban home purchase subsidies), co-owned property housing, forest rights protection.
  - Employment: 21 standardized forestry workshops, 536,000 mu of forestry and fruit planting area.
  - Logistics: Selected as provincial logistics hub.
  - Energy: Photovoltaic installed capacity reached 1.778 million kilowatts; all-vanadium flow storage project Phase I completed.
  - Public services: Optimized school layouts, medical networks, “rural affairs handled in city” service stations.
- Conflict resolution rate: 98.4%.
- Urbanization rate increased from 38.68% to 42.98% during the 14th Five-Year Plan period.
- Target: Reach 60%+ permanent resident urbanization rate by 2030.

### 5. Ecological Restoration Case Studies
#### a. Liaoning Jinzhou — Daling River Estuary Restoration
- Formerly degraded by aquaculture enclosures cutting off tidal channels, causing salt marsh vegetation die-off.
- Starting 2018: Bohai comprehensive治理 campaign; 2022: Marine ecological protection and restoration工程 (project).
- Measures: Demolished enclosures, dredged tidal channels, replanted cordgrass and salicornia.
- Result: Red beach restored; red-crowned cranes returned in spring 2022 after 10+ years absence.
- Metrics:滨海湿地水动力提升30% (coastal wetland hydrodynamic efficiency improved by 30%); bird species significantly increased.
- Tourism: Eco-tourism base hosts over 500,000 visitors annually; some former fishermen became ecological bird guards.

#### b. Gansu Minqin — Qingtu Lake Revival
- Once dried up in 1959 due to climate change and overuse, becoming a major sandsource.
- Solution: Jingdian Phase II Extension Water Diversion Project (completed 2000, water delivery started 2001).
- Yellow River water pumped via 13-stage pumps (~500m elevation gain) over 260km to Minqin.
- 2010: First ecological water injection (12.9 million m³) created 3 sq km water surface.
- Current: Water surface area 27.65 sq km; 380km oasis fringe forest belt closed loop formed.
- Economic benefit: Under-forest carnivorous herbs (Cistanche) and seedling cultivation provide stable income.

#### c. Yunnan Xichou — Karst Desertification Control
- Sandaog Village (Xingjie Town) transformed from barren rock-strewn land to productive orchards.
- Approach: Integrated治理 of “mountain, water, forest, field, road, village.”
- “Cooperative + base + household” model introduced kiwifruit, ginger, cucumber, and golden cabbage.
- Villager Gao Shixian’s annual income grew from tens of thousands to over RMB 300,000 via farming + agritainment.
- Village now functions as eco-tourism综合体 (complex) combining fruit picking, sightseeing, and cultural display.

### 6. Editorial Commentary on Ecological Governance

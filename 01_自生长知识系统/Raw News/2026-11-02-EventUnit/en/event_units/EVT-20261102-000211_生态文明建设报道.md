## Core Facts

### 1. National Water Environment Quality (Jan–Sep 2026)
*   **Source:** Article #346 (based on data released by the Ministry of Ecology and Environment on Nov 1, 2026).
*   **National Surface Water:** The proportion of Class I–III water quality was **86.1%**, a year-on-year increase of **1.7 percentage points**. The proportion of inferior Class V water quality was **0.8%**, a year-on-year decrease of **0.4 percentage points** (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Major River Basins:** The seven major river basins (Yangtze, Yellow, Zhujiang, Songhua, Huai, Hai, Liao) plus Northwest and Southwest rivers and Zhejiang-Fujian coastal rivers showed a Class I–III proportion of **87.1%** (+1.7 pp YoY) and inferior Class V at **0.7%** (-0.4 pp YoY) (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
    *   **Water Quality Ratings:**
        *   Excellent: Southwest Rivers, Zhejiang-Fujian Coastal Rivers, Yangtze River Basin, Northwest Rivers.
        *   Good: Zhujiang, Yellow, Liao, Huai, and Hai River Basins.
        *   Lightly Polluted: Songhua River Basin (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Key Lakes and Reservoirs (145 monitored):**
    *   Class I–III proportion: **71.7%** (+2.0 pp YoY).
    *   Inferior Class V proportion: **3.4%** (-0.7 pp YoY).
    *   Trophic State: 2 lakes (1.4%) were moderately eutrophic; 43 lakes (29.7%) were lightly eutrophic; the rest were mesotrophic or oligotrophic (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
    *   **Specific Lake Status:**
        *   Taihu: Good water quality, lightly eutrophic.
        *   Chaohu: Lightly polluted, lightly eutrophic.
        *   Dianchi: Lightly polluted, moderately eutrophic.
        *   Erhai and Danjiangkou Reservoir: Excellent water quality, mesotrophic.
        *   Baiyangdian: Good water quality, mesotrophic (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).

### 2. Hainan Tropical Rainforest National Park Ancient Tree Census
*   **Event:** The census work officially launched on Nov 1, 2026, in Wuzhishan City, Hainan Province (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Methodology:** Utilization of satellite remote sensing and UAVs to survey species, quantity, age, distribution, growth status, and historical/cultural significance (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Purpose:** To establish a resource management database, identify endangered ancient trees/key protected famous trees, and support scientific protection and sustainable management (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).

### 3. Inner Mongolia Desert- crossing Highway Construction
*   **Progress:** As of the end of September 2026, **2,667 km** of desert-crossing highways were under construction, completing the annual target ahead of schedule (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Cumulative Data (Since 2024):** Total **4,468 km** started, including:
    *   Korqin and Hunshandake Sandland Annihilation Zone: 3,827 km.
    *   Yellow River "Ji" Bend Assault Zone: 641 km (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Ecological Impact:** Expected to drive desert control covering **787.45 million mu** (Annihilation Zone: 750.25 million mu; Assault Zone: 37.2 million mu) (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Policy Framework:** Implements the "Road-Based Sand Control" model, aligned with the Sixth Phase of the "Three Norths" Project. Key planning documents include the 2024–2030 Development Plan for the Korqin and Hunshandake Sandlands and the 2025–2030 Implementation Plan for the Yellow River "Ji" Bend and Hexi Corridor–Taklamakan Desert Edge zones (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).

### 4. Launch of Domestic "Chinese Core" Rose Varieties
*   **Event:** Two domestically bred fresh-cut rose varieties, **"Wenxiu"** and **"Jiaolong"**, went on sale on Oct 29, 2026, at the Dawan Modern Agricultural Ecology Demonstration Park in Kunming, Yunnan (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Breeder:** Yunnan Academy of Agricultural Sciences Flower Research Institute (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Series:** Part of the "Heroines" (Jinguo Yingxiong) series within the "Chinese Style" (Guofeng) rose system,定位为 "Chinese Core, Chinese Style, Chinese Made" (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Variety Details:**
    *   **"Wenxiu":** Bright鹅-yellow (goose-yellow),挺拔 (upright) plant type. Named after Huang Wenxiu (July 1 Medal recipient, Times Model), symbolizing selfless dedication to rural revitalization (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
    *   **"Jiaolong":** Full flower shape, elegant fragrance. Named after He Jiaolong (grassroots cadre in border areas), symbolizing practical担当 and growth towards the sun (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).
*   **Commercialization:** Initial sales on the "Hua Wu" (花伍) flower trading platform saw first batches sell out in minutes; bulk supply expected in late November (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 13).

### 5. Xu Jiayu’s Short-Course Swimming Records
*   **Event:** 2026 National Swimming Championships (25m) concluded on Oct 28, 2026, at the Beijing National Aquatics Center (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 14).
*   **Records Broken by Xu Jiayu (Zhejiang Team):**
    *   Men’s 50m Backstroke (Short Course) World Record: **22.03 seconds** (in 4x50m Mixed Relay leg) (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 14).
    *   Men’s 100m Backstroke (Short Course) Asian Record: **48.54 seconds** (in 4x100m Mixed Relay leg); also swam 48.77s in the individual final, breaking the Asian record then (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 14).
*   **Context:** Xu Jiayu (age 31) adjusted his training regimen to "less volume, more intensity," focusing on分段 (segmented) techniques and adequate rest. He aims for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 14).
*   **Coach:** Li Zhuhao (retired swimmer, now coach), who helped restructure Xu’s training system (Source: *The People's Daily*, Nov 2, 2026, p. 14).

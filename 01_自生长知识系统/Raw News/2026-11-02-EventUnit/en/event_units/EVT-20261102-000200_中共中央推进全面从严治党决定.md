# Event: CPC Central Committee Advances Comprehensive Strict Governance of the Party

## Event Overview

On October 26–29, 2026, the Fifth Plenum of the 20th CPC Central Committee was held in Beijing. The Plenum reviewed and adopted the *"Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persevering in the Comprehensive and Strict Governance of the Party"* and its explanatory statement. The decision and related commentary were published in *People's Daily* on November 2, 2026. The single-volume edition of the decision was announced by Xinhua on November 1, 2026, and made available for public sale starting November 2, 2026.

The event sits at the intersection of several significant contextual markers identified by sources: the 105th anniversary of the founding of the CPC (2021 + 105 = 2026), a period described as critical to the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation, and the opening year of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period.

## Core Facts

### 1. The Decision and Its Explanatory Statement
- **Official Title**: *Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persevering in the Comprehensive and Strict Governance of the Party* (《中共中央关于持之以恒推进全面从严治党若干重大问题的决定》).
- **Explanatory Statement**: Delivered/attached as an accompanying document explaining the decision.
- **Adopting Body**: The Fifth Plenum of the 20th CPC Central Committee.
- **Source (Official Text)**: *People's Daily*, Page 02, November 2, 2026 (Article #300, Article #303).
- **Publication Format**: Single-volume edition published by People's Publishing House (人民出版社), sold nationwide through Xinhua Bookstores starting November 2, 2026 (Article #302, citing Xinhua, November 1, 2026).

### 2. Guidance and Principles
The decision outlines the guiding ideology and seven principles for persevering in comprehensive strict governance of the party:
- **Guiding Ideology**: Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Theory of Three Represents, the Important Thought of Scientific Development, and fully implementing Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, including deep study and implementation of **Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building** (习近平党建思想).
- **Seven Principles**:
  1. Adhere to serving the party's mission and tasks.
  2. Adhere to taking party political construction as the overarching guide.
  3. Adhere to comprehensive strictness.
  4. Adhere to problem orientation.
  5. Adhere to keeping integrity while innovating (守正创新).
  6. Adhere to leading by example from the top (以上率下).
  7. Adhere to governing the party according to regulations (依规治党).

### 3. Key Theoretical Formulations
- **Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building** (习近平党建思想): Described as a major theoretical innovation formed through the practice of comprehensive strict party governance in the new era. It is characterized as enriching Marxist party-building theory, resulting from combining Marxist basic principles with China's specific realities and fine traditional Chinese culture. It is identified as an important component of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era.
- **"Two Answers" for Breaking the Historical Cycle**: The decision states that the CPC has found two answers to escaping the historical cycle of rise and fall: "letting the people supervise the government" and "self-revolution."
- **Three "More Arduous" Strategic Judgments**: The decision assesses that the tasks of safeguarding national sovereignty/security/development interests, promoting reform/development/stability, and maintaining party先进性/purity are all "more arduous" in the current international, domestic, and intra-party contexts.
- **The "Seven Has-Its" (七个有之)**: Political discipline explicitly forbids clique-building, spreading rumors, buying influence, acting independently against the center, and other specified behaviors.

### 4. Structural Content of the Decision
Based on the full text provided in Articles #300 and #303, the decision covers at minimum:
- **Section I**: The importance, urgency, and general requirements of persevering in comprehensive strict party governance.
- **Section II**: Strengthening the party spirit (党性) of communists, including loyalty education, theoretical study, traditional/cultural cultivation, practical tempering, and party spirit assessment.
- **Section III**: Strictly implementing democratic centralism, including central committee leadership, intra-party democracy, and leadership team decision-making rules.

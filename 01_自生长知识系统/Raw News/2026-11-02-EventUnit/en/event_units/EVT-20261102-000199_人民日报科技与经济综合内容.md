# Event E20261102-000199: Xi Jinping Presents Explanation for "Decision" on Comprehensively and Rigorously Governing the Party at the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee

## Event Overview
On November 1, 2026, in Beijing, Xi Jinping delivered an explanation to the Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC) regarding the drafting of the *Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persistently Advancing Comprehensive and Rigorous Party Governance on Several Major Issues* (hereinafter referred to as the "Decision"). 

The event took place ahead of the Second Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee, which was convened to study and advance comprehensive and rigorous Party governance. The presentation covered the rationale for the plenary session's agenda, the drafting process, and the basic framework and core content of the Decision document.

## Core Facts

**Event Details:**
*   **Date of Delivery:** November 1, 2026
*   **Location:** Beijing
*   **Speaker:** Xi Jinping
*   **Capacity:** Acting on behalf of the Central Political Bureau
*   **Audience:** Members of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee

**Document Status:**
*   **Title:** *Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persistently Advancing Comprehensive and Rigorous Party Governance on Several Major Issues*
*   **Purpose:** To serve as a programmatic document for advancing the new great project of Party building in the new era.

**Drafting Process:**
*   **Initiation:** In January 2026, the CPC Central Committee established a drafting team for the Fifth Plenary Session documents.
*   **Leadership:** Xi Jinping served as Head of the drafting team; Wang Huning, Cai Qi, and Li Xi served as Deputy Heads.
*   **Start Date:** Formal work commenced with the first full meeting of the drafting team on February 6, 2026.
*   **Consultation:**
    *   On January 31, 2026, the Central Committee issued a notice soliciting opinions from all regions and departments.
    *   16 units were commissioned to conduct research on 23 key topics.
    *   On May 6, 2026, Xi Jinping presided over a symposium in Shanghai to hear opinions from responsible comrades of some provinces and autonomous regions.
    *   On August 3, 2026, the draft Decision was issued for internal consultation within the Party, including opinions from retired senior Party leaders, as well as opinions from heads of democratic parties, the All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce, and non-affiliated persons.
*   **Review:** The Central Political Bureau Standing Committee held 3 meetings, and the Central Political Bureau held 2 meetings to review and revise the draft.

**Key Rationales for the Plenary Session Agenda:**
1.  **Historical Context:** 2026 marks the 105th anniversary of the Party's founding.
2.  **Goal of Self-Revolution:** To answer how to escape the historical cycle of rise and fall. Mao Zedong provided the first answer ("let the people supervise the government") in the Yan'an cave dwellings; the Party has found the second answer through comprehensive and rigorous Party governance ("self-revolution").
3.  **Current Challenges:** Issues such as ideological impurity, organizational impurity, and conduct impurity have not been fundamentally resolved, and new problems continue to emerge. The proportion of Party members and cadres born after the Reform and Opening Up is increasing, presenting new requirements for education and management.
4.  **Strategic Necessity:** To consolidate the hard-won situation of high unity and cohesion among the Party, military, and people, and to provide strong guarantees for Chinese-style modernization and national rejuvenation amidst a "profound changes unseen in a century" and domestic developmental challenges.

**Framework of the Decision:**
The Decision consists of three main sections:
1.  **General Theory (Part I):** Addresses the importance, urgency, and general requirements, including the 7 guiding principles.
2.  **Specific Tasks (Parts II–VIII):**
    *   Part II: Strengthening the Party spirit of Communists.
    *   Part III: Strictly implementing democratic centralism.
    *   Part IV: Forging a high-quality cadre team capable of shouldering the重任 of national rejuvenation.
    *   Part V: Building a tight organizational system with smooth transmission and effective execution.
    *   Part VI: Unremittingly rectifying conduct and enforcing discipline.
    *   Part VII: Resolutely advancing the integrated promotion of "不敢腐" (dare not corrupt), "不能腐" (cannot corrupt), and "不想腐" (do not want to corrupt).
    *   Part VIII: Adhering to rule-based Party governance.
3.  **Supervision and Responsibility:** Deploys measures for perfecting the Party and state supervision system and implementing political responsibility for comprehensive and rigorous Party governance.

**Key Content Highlights:**
*   **"Self-Revolution":** Defined strictly as turning the blade inward, conducting comprehensive and rigorous Party governance, and achieving self-purification, self-improvement, self-renewal, and self-enhancement.
*   **Anti-Corruption Strategy:** Emphasizes maintaining a high-pressure stance on punishing corruption while strengthening the restriction and supervision of power operation and laying the ideological foundation for rejecting corruption.
*   **Discipline:** Highlights the "Eight-Point Regulation" as an iron law and the need for normalized and long-term conduct rectification.
*   **Cadre Management:** Focuses on correct employment orientations, precise selection, and enhancing the ability of cadres to pursue practical achievements.

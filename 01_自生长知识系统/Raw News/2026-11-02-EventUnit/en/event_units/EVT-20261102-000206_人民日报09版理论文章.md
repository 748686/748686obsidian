People's Daily Theoretical Section (November 2, 2026): Synthesis of Party Building, Governance, and Historical Discourse

## Event Overview

On November 2, 2026, *People's Daily* (paper.people.com.cn) published a series of theoretical and comprehensive articles across its 09th and 10th sections. The event encompasses four distinct articles addressing: (1) the scientific methodology within Xi Jinping's thought on Party building; (2) the dialectical relationship between phased work and long-term goals; (3) the construction of a higher-level "Safe China" (Ping’an Zhongguo) through grassroots social governance; and (4) the strategic position of Hong Kong and Macau in national rejuvenation under the "One Country, Two Systems" framework. Additionally, an article on Page 10 examined the contemporary narrative techniques used in commemorating the 90th anniversary of the Red Army's Long March.

These articles collectively reflect the Communist Party of China’s (CPC) current theoretical priorities: methodological discipline in governance, people-centric social stability, and the integration of regional prosperity with national rejuvenation.

## Core Facts

**1. Methodological Discipline in Governance (Page 09)**
*   **Source:** Article #326 and #327.
*   **Key Thesis:** Success requires combining long-term strategic goals with phased, practical work.
*   **Long-term Goals:** These guide direction,凝聚 (coalesce) social consensus, ensure policy stability across generations ("one blueprint to the end"), and prevent short-sighted resource exhaustion.
*   **Phased Work:** Involves breaking down grand visions into tangible, verifiable tasks. Examples cited include Zhejiang’s "Thousand Villagers Demonstration, Ten Thousand Villagers Renovation" project (20+ years of progression from local to regional beauty) and the planning phase of the Xiong’an New Area ("millennium plan") to avoid historical legacy issues.
*   **Dialectic:** Long-term goals牵引 (lead) phased work; phased work支撑 (support) long-term goals.

**2. Social Governance and "Safe China" (Page 09)**
*   **Source:** Article #327 and #328.
*   **Key Thesis:** Constructing a higher-level "Safe China" requires shifting from rigid management to service-oriented governance, prioritizing prevention over reaction.
*   **Author:** Zhang Chengang (President, Institute for Social Governance and Development, Tsinghua University).
*   **Shift in Logic:** Moving from "herding cats" (management/control) to "serving well" (service/governance). Governance subjects diversify; methods become flexible;重心 (focus) shifts from terminal handling to frontend prevention.
*   **Pre-emptive Governance:** Emphasizes "front-loading" risk resolution through grid systems and community consultation (New Era "Fengqiao Experience") to prevent small conflicts from escalating.
*   **Goal:** Achieve "共建共治共享" (co-construction, co-governance, and shared benefits), transforming public participation from passive ("要我参与") to active ("我要参与").

**3. "One Country, Two Systems" and National Rejuvenation (Page 10)**
*   **Source:** Article #328 and #329.
*   **Author:** Theory Study Center Group of the Working Committee of the Central Leading Group for Hong Kong and Macau Affairs.
*   **Context:** Commentary on Xi Jinping’s speech at the 105th Anniversary of the Founding of the CPC.
*   **Hong Kong’s Status:** Described as transitioning from "chaos to order" to "order to prosperity." Claims include: Hong Kong retains global top rankings in freedom and competitiveness (surpassing Switzerland as the largest cross-border wealth management center) and has a world-class business environment.
*   **Macau’s Status:** Described as achieving historical economic leaps, with per capita GDP ranking among the highest globally. Focus on "1+4" economic diversification.
*   **Strategic Imperative:** Hong Kong and Macau must integrate into the national "15th Five-Year Plan" and the 2035 modernization goals.
*   **Governance Principle:** Emphasizes "Patriots governing Hong Kong/Macau" as an absolute prerequisite for stability, linking it to the rejection of external interference.

**4. Long March Commemoration and Media Strategy (Page 10)**
*   **Source:** Article #329.
*   **Context:** 90th Anniversary of the victory of the Long March.
*   **Media Properties:** CCTV documentaries *The Great Expedition* (伟大远征) and *Ten Thousand Rivers and Mountains* (万水千山).
*   **Performance Metrics:** *The Great Expedition* reached over 800 million views online; *Ten Thousand Rivers and Mountains* ranked first in ratings for its time slot.
*   **Narrative Technique:** Utilizes "material evidence" (bones, inscriptions, artifacts) rather than pure narration. Uses technology (virtual production, satellite remote sensing) strictly as a service to historical accuracy ("reverence for history").
*   **Target Audience:** Aiming to resonate with younger demographics by connecting historical sacrifice to contemporary "new Long Marches" (space exploration, rural revitalization).

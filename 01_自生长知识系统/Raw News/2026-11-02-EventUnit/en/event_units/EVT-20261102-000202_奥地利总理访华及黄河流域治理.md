### 2. Yellow River Governance Anniversary
*   **Milestone:** The year 2026 marks the 80th anniversary of the Communist Party of China (CPC) leading the governance of the Yellow River.
*   **Historical Start:** In 1946, the Jin-Ji-Lu-Yu Border Region Government established the Yellow River Old Course Management Committee (later the Jin-Lu-Yu Yellow River Water Affairs Commission), marking the beginning of CPC-led governance.
*   **Strategic Framework:** President Xi Jinping proposed the "Yellow River Basin Ecological Protection and High-Quality Development" national strategy. The framework has evolved from "deepening promotion" (2021) to "comprehensive promotion" (2024).
*   **Key Statistics (as reported in Article #307):**
    *   Yellow River length: 5,464 km.
    *   Historical floods: Over 1,500 breaches and 26 course changes in the 2,500+ years prior to 1949.
    *   Water resource per capita: 27% of the national average.
    *   Soil erosion治理 (treatment): Cumulative preliminary treatment area exceeded 290,000 square kilometers by 2025; soil erosion area reduced by nearly 50% compared to the 1990s.
    *   Water Quality: The main stream of the Yellow River achieved Class II water quality for the first time in 2022 and has maintained this status since.
    *   Biodiversity: In the Shandong Yellow River Delta National Nature Reserve, bird species increased from 187 at establishment to approximately 400.
*   **Legal/Policy Instruments:**
    *   October 2020: "Outline of the Yellow River Basin Ecological Protection and High-Quality Development Plan."
    *   June 2022: "Yellow River Basin Ecological Environment Protection Plan."
    *   April 1, 2023: Implementation of the "Yellow River Protection Law of the People's Republic of China."

## Core Facts

**1. Signing of the UN Convention on Transferable Cargo Documents**
*   **Event:** The signing ceremony for the *United Nations Convention on Transferable Cargo Documents* took place on October 26, 2026, in Accra, Ghana.
*   **Participants:** 26 countries, including China and Ghana, became the first batch of signatories. Approximately 400 delegates from over 60 countries and regions attended.
*   **Chinese Representation:** Jiang Chenghua, Vice Minister of Commerce, attended the ceremony and signed the convention on behalf of the Chinese government.
*   **Legal Significance:** Initiated and led by China, the convention was adopted by the 80th Session of the UN General Assembly in December 2025. It creates an international legal framework for "transferable cargo documents" applicable to all cross-border transport modes, breaking the centuries-old institutional barrier that only sea freight bills of lading could represent cargo rights.
*   **Official Statements:**
    *   **Jiang Chenghua (China):** Described the convention as a milestone in international commercial rule innovation and an action to build a community with a shared future for mankind. He expressed willingness to complete domestic approval procedures promptly and invited more countries to join.
    *   **Elizabeth Oforsoa-Agyeiware (Ghana):** As a首批 signatory, Ghana will follow up with legislative actions and pilot programs for transferable cargo documents.
    *   **Anna Joabin-Blair (UNCITRAL Secretary):** Stated the convention reduces uncertainty, enhances resilience, promotes trade finance, supports trade digitization, and improves supply chain efficiency. She thanked China for submitting the project to UNCITRAL.

**2. Diplomatic Meeting with European Parliament Delegation**

**3. Public Cultural Service Integration in Xi'an**
*   **Location:** "215 Drama Space" on the 5th floor of the Wangfujing Commercial Complex, near the Yongning Gate of the Xi'an City Wall, Shaanxi Province.
*   **Operator:** Shaanxi Experimental Opera Theatre, in partnership with the Xi'an Mass Art Centre.
*   **Launch:** The facility opened in December 2025.
*   **Program:** "Wednesday Drama Meeting" (剧会星期三), a regularized public welfare performance brand launched in 2026.
    *   **Schedule:** Every Wednesday at 19:45.
    *   **Cost:** Free admission (zero ticket price).
    *   **Format:** Includes classic plays, original experimental drama, film/TV drama adaptations, musical fragment sharing sessions, and cultural lectures. Actors sit with the audience before performances to share storylines, creation backgrounds, and character motivations.
*   **Capacity:** The space is 215 square meters with 80 movable seats.
*   **Performers:** Primarily young backbone actors from the Shaanxi Experimental Opera Theatre and literary volunteers, with an average age of approximately 20 years old.
*   **Repertoire Highlights:**
    *   Adaptation of Wang Wei's poem *Farewell to Yuan Er on His Mission to Anxi* titled *Sigh of Parting*.
    *   Opera excerpts from *The Long March* ("March Peach Blossoms in Heart") and *Yimeng Mountain* ("Waiting for a Lifetime").
    *   Play fragments from *Thunderstorm* and *Hidden Marriage Couples*.
    *   Original one-act plays *The Plague* and *Hey, Brother*.
*   **Impact Statistics (as of end of August 2026):**
    *   Served over 15,000 audience members.
    *   Commercial activity conversion rate exceeded 30%.
*   **Business Model:** Combines free Wednesday performances with regular commercial performances on weekends, linked to activities like "Drama Carnival" and "Youth Drama Competition Season."

**「Solution」** Drawing on pasta sauce market segmentation and Magic: The Gathering archetypes, the author proposes a new binary: &\#x27;spikish&\#x27; movements, which treat politics as a strategic tool to achieve a concrete goal, and &\#x27;jimmish&\#x27; movements, which use politics as a direct outlet for emotional impulses. **「Takeaway」** The central axis of political divergence is not the ultimate goal, but whether a movement’s actions are driven by a reasoned world-model or by a direct outlet for emotional impulses. Recognizing this difference allows observers to correctly predict why seemingly like-minded factions might oppose each other and understand the psychological origins of highly strategic yet counterproductive political stances.

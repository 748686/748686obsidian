### [China Advances Ecological Product Valuation and Credit Markets in Fujian](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/21/content_30182190.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Fujian Province&\#x27;s Jiangle County released a valuation report for 100,000 mu of carbon sink reserve forests and used the quantified results to sign a VEP \(Valuation of Ecosystem Products\) credit agreement with a bank. This move translates ecological assets into financial collateral, demonstrating the practical application of China&\#x27;s policy that ecosystems can be monetized. Last year, Fujian created a three-tier catalog of ecological products across six major ecosystems to standardize valuation. The province has also piloted other financial tools, such as VEP loans in Wuyishan and Yong&\#x27;an counties, to create a market for ecological services like water conservation and biodiversity protection.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 21, 00:26

**「Understanding Ecosystem Value Realization」** Ecosystem Product Value \(VEP\) refers to the monetizable worth of ecological services, such as water retention and carbon sequestration, treated as tradable assets with defined property rights. This framework allows natural resources to be priced and utilized in markets, enabling governments and financial institutions to support ecological protection through economic incentives rather than solely public expenditure.

**「Impact」** For local governments and banks in Fujian, these mechanisms provide a new financial channel to fund ecological conservation by converting natural resources into tradable assets. This model may encourage other regions to adopt similar valuation and credit frameworks, although its scalability depends on standardizing ecological product markets nationwide.

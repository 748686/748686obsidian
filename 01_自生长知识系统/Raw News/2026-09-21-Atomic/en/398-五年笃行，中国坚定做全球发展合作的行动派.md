### [China&\#x27;s August electricity use tops 1 trillion kWh for the second consecutive month](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/21/content_30182178.html) ⭐️ ?/10

China&\#x27;s total electricity consumption in August 2026 reached 1,033.2 terawatt-hours \(TWh\), a 1.7% year-over-year increase, marking the second consecutive month above the 1 trillion kWh threshold.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 21, 00:26

**「Background」** Electricity demand is a key indicator of industrial and economic activity; August&\#x27;s total load peaked at a record 1.56 billion kilowatts, exceeding last year&\#x27;s maximum by 4.97 million kilowatts and staying above that prior peak for seven days.

**「Impact」** High-tech manufacturing and new-energy vehicle production saw the fastest growth, with new-energy vehicle complete-vehicle electricity use rising 22.2% year-on-year, while charging and battery-swap services expanded by 51.2%.

### [PICC P&C reports 32.8 billion yuan H1 profit, up 32%](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/01/content_30178348.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 1, 2026, People&\#x27;s Daily reported that China PICC Property and Casualty Company released its 2026 interim results. The company recorded gross written premiums of 327.529 billion yuan, up 1.3% year-on-year, capturing 33.3% of the Chinese property insurance market. Underwriting profit reached 15.376 billion yuan, up 18.1%, while net profit climbed 32.0% to 32.284 billion yuan, supported by a 58.5% surge in total investment income to 27.359 billion yuan. The combined ratio improved to 94.0%, down 0.8 percentage points, and return on equity \(unannualized\) rose to 10.9%.

**「Background」** PICC P&C is a leading Chinese insurer and a key subsidiary of the People&\#x27;s Insurance Company \(Group\) of China. Property and casualty insurers generate revenue primarily through premiums and investment returns, with profitability measured by underwriting profit and the combined ratio, which is the sum of the loss ratio and expense ratio.

**「Impact」** Stronger investment returns and improved underwriting efficiency boosted PICC P&C&\#x27;s profitability, reinforcing its dominant 33.3% market share in China&\#x27;s property insurance sector. The results may support sustained dividend expectations and competitive positioning for institutional investors and industry stakeholders.

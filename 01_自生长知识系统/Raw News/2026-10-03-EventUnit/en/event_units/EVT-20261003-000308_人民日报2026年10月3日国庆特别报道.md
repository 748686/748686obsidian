## Event Overview

On October 3, 2026, during the National Day holiday, *People's Daily* (人民日报) published a special comprehensive report covering political, economic, cultural, and technological developments across China and the world. The event encompasses the newspaper's content across multiple sections (Editions 01–08), reflecting the 77th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China (celebrated on October 1, 2026), the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, and the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March.

## Core Facts

### Political Commemoration and Policy Direction
- On September 30, 2026, General Secretary Xi Jinping delivered a speech at the celebration banquet for the 77th anniversary of the Founding of the PRC, stating: "At this moment, we are incredibly proud of the remarkable achievements of New China and confident in the bright prospects of Chinese modernization."
- The speech emphasized the 77 years of struggle under the Party's leadership, the "15th Five-Year Plan" start, and the path toward the second centenary goal of building a modern socialist country in all respects.
- Officials and cadres across multiple regions expressed commitment to implementing Xi's instructions, focusing on high-quality development, technological self-reliance, and people's livelihood improvement.
- The article cited specific figures: Single Qingdong (单庆东), a large-scale grain grower in Boli County, Qitaihe City, Heilongjiang Province, planted 13,000 mu of corn and 7,000 mu of soybeans, achieving "ton-per-mu" corn yields.
- Shenzhen Nanshan District reported that high-tech manufacturing and advanced manufacturing accounted for 59.5% and 54.9% of above-scale industrial增加值 (added value) respectively in H1 2026.
- The Three Gorges Water Transport New Channel project completed its initial excavation blasting on September 30, 2026, entering a comprehensive construction phase. It is described as the first national major标志性工程 (landmark project) to begin construction during the "15th Five-Year Plan" period.

### Ecological Transformation Case: Gengche Town
- Gengche Town (耿车镇), Suqian City, Jiangsu Province, underwent a major ecological transformation after decades of低端废旧塑料回收加工 (low-end waste plastic recycling and processing) caused severe "water, land, and air" pollution.
- In December 2015, Suqian municipal and district governments launched a comprehensive rectification campaign, shutting down the industry within 66 days.
- By 2025, Gengche reported 302 air quality excellent days (up 119 days from before rectification), PM2.5 decreased by 55.2%, inferior Class V water bodies were eliminated, and soil reached high-quality standards.
- The town's 2025 GDP exceeded 4.8 billion yuan, and per capita disposable income reached 42,000 yuan. Over 6,000 new entrepreneurial entities were registered in 2025.
- Former practitioners transitioned to furniture e-commerce, floral and nursery stock, plastic deep processing, and logistics industries.

### Cultural Heritage and Tourism
- Shenyang Imperial Palace Museum celebrated its 100th anniversary (founded 1926 as the Northeastern Museum). In August 2026, the exhibition "Collecting the Mountains and Rivers: One Hundred Artifacts from the Shenyang Imperial Palace Museum's Centennial" displayed 100 artifacts, including previously dispersed collections returning to the palace.
- 2025 visitor numbers exceeded 5.85 million; H1 2026 saw over 2.7 million visitors (up 21.2% year-on-year). Night visits were added on Fridays and Saturdays during summer 2026, extending to 20:30.
- Copper雀 (Bronze Sparrow) Three Terraces site in Linzhang County, Hebei, features VR experiences and the indoor实景演出 (live-action performance) "Song of Yedu" (《邺都长歌》).
- Dunhuang Mogao Caves feature three representative decorative patterns: the "Three Rabbits Sharing Ears" pattern (隋代第407窟), pomegranate-grape patterns (初唐第209窟), and four-leaf flower patterns evolving from Northern Liang to Yuan dynasty. The "Three Rabbits Sharing Ears" was used as the cultural symbol for the 4th Dialogue on Civilizations in 2025.
- The "China Great Peace, Descendants Prosperously Flourish" (中国大宁 子孙益昌) Han dynasty gilded bronze mirror, excavated in Changsha in 1951–1952, is housed at the National Museum of China.

### Technological Innovation
- Shangyu District, Shaoxing City, Zhejiang Province, introduced 20 embodied intelligence industry projects with total investment exceeding 15 billion yuan. A four-legged robot was deployed at Runtu New Materials' liquid chlorine production workshop, reducing personnel exposure in high-risk areas by 90%.
- Hangzhou established a "Robot School" through Zhejiang University's Robotics Institute, training 30首批 "robot students" from industrial, service, and security domains. The system "Wuji Brain" (无际大脑) adds a logical reasoning layer to enable robots to handle unfamiliar scenarios.
- The 48th WorldSkills Competition recently concluded, with Chinese contestants achieving the best results in competition history.

### Intangible Cultural Heritage
- Zhang Tiantian (张甜甜), a post-90s Tujia nationality inheritor of Tujia brocade weaving技艺 in Zhangjiajie, Hunan, reported that her products are sold in over 30 countries and regions.

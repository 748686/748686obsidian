## Core Facts
*   **Legal Concept:** In Germany, spouses can enter into pre-nuptial agreements (*Eheverträge*) to modify the statutory default property regime.
*   **Statutory Default:** Without a pre-nuptial agreement, the statutory regime of "community of accrued gains" (*Zugewinngemeinschaft*) applies. This means assets acquired during the marriage are equalized upon divorce, but assets held before marriage generally remain separate unless commingled.
*   **Purpose:** A pre-nuptial agreement allows couples to opt for separation of property (*Gütertrennung*) or other arrangements to protect specific assets (e.g., business interests, inheritances, pre-marital wealth) from division in the event of divorce.
*   **Formal Requirements:** Under German law (§ 1412 BGB), pre-nuptial agreements must be notarized to be valid.

### [Qinzhou tailors acid-soil治理 per village to boost yields](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/31/content_30178103.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A People&\#x27;s Daily report published on August 31, 2026 describes how Qinnan District in Qinzhou, Guangxi, has moved from low-yield saline-acid coastal paddy fields to over 800 jin per mu this season, with high-yield plots exceeding 1,000 jin. The district was named in 2025 among China&\#x27;s second batch of key counties for acidified farmland governance and treated about 81,000 mu that year, raising project-area soil pH by an average of 0.25 units and increasing grain capacity by 3.52 percent compared with the previous year. The improvement relies on third-national-soil-census data, adjustable soil conditioners, organic amendments, freshwater leaching, straw return, green manures, and tailored advice rather than a single remedy. Local extension services have trained more than 2,000 farmers and distributed over 2,000 technical guides since implementing targeted measures by village and acidity level.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Aug 31, 00:26

**「Background」** Soil acidification and salinization commonly reduce crop yield and quality, especially in coastal reclaimed areas where seawater intrusion leaves salts and buried organic matter generates acidity during decomposition. China&\#x27;s national food-security policy has prioritized farmland quality improvement, with the 15th Five-Year Plan period explicitly calling for strengthened black-soil protection and治理 of acidified cultivated land.

**「Impact」** Growers in Qinnan District can now harvest substantially more rice from formerly degraded coastal fields, and the district&\#x27;s measured pH and capacity gains provide a local reference for similar coastal acid-saline environments. Results depend on continued soil testing, conditioners, and water management, so benefits may vary in untreated or more severely affected fields.

### [China-Aid Laos Zero-Carbon Library Unveiled in Luang Prabang](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/31/content_30178113.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On August 29, 2026, the first zero-carbon library in Laos was officially inaugurated at Luang Prabang Peace Middle School, constructed with Chinese aid. Led by State Grid Zhejiang Electric Power Co., Ltd., the facility employs an integrated photovoltaic-storage-charging microgrid system, where rooftop solar panels generate electricity stored in batteries for overnight use. The library is a prefabricated unit manufactured in Zhejiang and shipped to Laos, equipped with air conditioning, computers, television, bilingual books, and a digital database. The opening ceremony also launched an &\#x27;air classroom&\#x27; initiative connecting students from Luang Prabang Peace Middle School with those from Gaoqiao Junior High School in Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Aug 31, 00:26

**「Background」** Light, mid-range, and offshore wind power generation have become mature solutions for sustainable infrastructure in developing regions, while prefabricated construction methods allow for rapid deployment and reduced on-site environmental impact. China has increasingly partnered with ASEAN nations on green Belt and Road Initiative projects that combine renewable energy technology with educational and social infrastructure.

**「Impact」** The library provides Luang Prabang Peace Middle School students with a self-sustaining, climate-controlled study environment powered entirely by renewable energy, reducing reliance on conventional grid electricity. It establishes a replicable model for deploying off-grid educational facilities in remote areas across Laos and the broader region.

**「Impact」** The initiative strengthens diplomatic and economic ties among the six Lancang-Mekong nations through concrete infrastructure projects and ecological protection frameworks.

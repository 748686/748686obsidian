### [Yangtze River Delta deepens cross-regional collaborative innovation](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/31/content_30178116.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On August 31, 2026, the People&\#x27;s Daily reported that the Yangtze River Delta region continues to deepen coordinated technological innovation across Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Anhui. The region hosts over 30% of China&\#x27;s high-tech enterprises and recorded 46,800 technology contract transactions totaling 246.13 billion yuan in 2025, up 14.7% year-on-year. Key mechanisms include shared access to major scientific facilities like the Shanghai Synchrotron Radiation Facility, cross-regional conversion programs such as the Sci-Tech Innovation Coupon system, and integrated industrial chains exemplified by NIO&\#x27;s manufacturing hub in Hefei and satellite production in Taizhou.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Aug 31, 00:26

**「Background」** The Yangtze River Delta is one of China&\#x27;s most economically active and innovation-driven regions. The Chinese leadership has emphasized the need for cross-regional coordination of scientific and technological innovation to build a national innovation source. Major scientific infrastructure and policy frameworks like the G60 Science and Technology Innovation Corridor have been developed to facilitate resource sharing and industrial collaboration across provincial boundaries.

**「Impact」** Regional enterprises and research institutions benefit from reduced innovation costs and faster technology transfer through shared facilities, coordinated policies, and integrated supply chains. The model demonstrates how cross-provincial collaboration can accelerate industrial upgrading in strategic sectors including biopharma, new energy vehicles, advanced materials, and commercial satellites.

### [China–Laos Railway car shipments up nearly 6 times year-on-year](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/31/content_30178129.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Since the start of 2026, freight volumes of Chinese manufactured automobiles shipped via the China–Laos Railway have grown sharply as the line becomes a key logistics route for domestic new-energy and other vehicles entering Southeast Asia. Through August 28, the railway had dispatched 3,052 vehicles across 347 special JSQ trainloads, compared with 458 vehicles on 51 trainloads in the same period of 2025, representing an increase of nearly sixfold. Wangyaying West Station in Kunming recorded a single-day peak of 171 vehicles on July 16, and monthly shipments have risen from 53 vehicles at the beginning of the year to as many as 544, roughly a tenfold increase. The growth is supported by the routine use of JSQ凹底双层专用列车, expanded warehousing and a “pre-declaration plus smart inspection” customs model at the Mohan border.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Aug 31, 00:26

**「Background」** The China–Laos Railway connects Kunming in Yunnan Province with Vientiane in Laos and serves as a land corridor for Chinese automobile exports into Southeast Asia. JSQ-type well-car double-decker trainsets are specialized rail stock for transporting complete vehicles, offering higher capacity per train than ordinary container arrangements and better protection for finished cars during transit.

**「Impact」** Automakers and logistics operators using the route can now move significantly larger batches of domestic vehicles to Laos and onward markets with faster port clearance and higher per-train capacity. The scale-up also increases reliance on Wangyaping West Station’s car-logistics hub and cross-border coordination between China Railway Special Cargo and customs authorities.

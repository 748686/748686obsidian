After a deadly mudslide in Nepal caused major casualties and失联 at the Gyirong Port in Tibet&\#x27;s Shigatse on August 26, 2026, 18 Communist Party-member immigration management police formed a rescue assault team. On August 29, they advanced from Gyirong Town toward the disaster core as road and bridge infrastructure had been completely destroyed by the landslide. **「Background」** Jiulong \(Kyirong\) is a border crossing between Tibet, China, and Nepal located in a deep Himalayan valley where steep slopes and intense monsoon conditions make the area prone to debris flows. On August 26, 2026, a large debris flow originating on the Nepalese side struck the Jiulong port area, destroying roads, bridges, communications, and power, and causing significant casualties and missing persons among Chinese border police, foreign nationals, and local residents.

**「Impact」** The operation demonstrates the Chinese border management apparatus&\#x27;s capacity for rapid deployment into severely damaged terrain, though it highlights critical vulnerabilities when transport infrastructure fails in remote Himalayan border regions. Rescue effectiveness remains constrained by extreme topography, with helicopter access being the only viable means to reach the most isolated checkpoints.

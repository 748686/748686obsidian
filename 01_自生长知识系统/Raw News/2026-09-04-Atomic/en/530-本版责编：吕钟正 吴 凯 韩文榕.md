### [China reports five-year decline in student myopia rates](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30178991.html) ⭐️ ?/10

National disease control officials reported on September 3, 2026, that the overall myopia rate among Chinese students fell to 49.9% in 2025, marking a cumulative 3.7 percentage point drop from 53.6% in 2018 and continuing a five-year downward trend. The National Disease Control and Prevention Administration emphasized preventive guidelines for the back-to-school season, advising students to engage in at least 70 minutes of daily moderate-to-vigorous physical activity and limit recreational screen time to under one hour per day. Officials also highlighted rising risks for common school传染病 such as influenza, norovirus, and hand-foot-and-mouth disease, recommending seasonal flu vaccination ideally completed in September or October.

**「Background」** Myopia \(nearsightedness\) has been a persistent public health concern in China, with previous surveys indicating that over half of students were affected. The National Disease Control and Prevention Administration routinely issues seasonal health guidance around the start of the academic year to address disease outbreaks in densely populated school environments.

**「Impact」** Schools and families must implement stricter limits on screen time and ensure daily physical activity targets are met to sustain the declining myopia trend. Healthcare providers should prioritize flu vaccinations for children and elderly high-risk groups before the November peak season.

### [China&\#x27;s Healthcare Security Bureau Releases DRG/DIP 3.0 Grouping Scheme](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30178992.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 3, 2026, China&\#x27;s National Healthcare Security Administration released the 3.0 version of the grouping scheme for Diagnosis-Related Groups \(DRG\) and Big Data Diagnosis-Intervention Packet \(DIP\) payments. The new scheme requires regions implementing DRG to complete transition preparations by the end of 2026, with full application by the end of March 2027. The DRG component includes 492 core groups and 825 subgroups, while the DIP component reduces its core disease database from 9,520 items in version 2.0 to 5,125 diseases, increasing case coverage from 92% to 95%. The policy explicitly prohibits medical institutions from using payment standards as spending limits for department or staff performance assessments.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** DRG and DIP are payment reform mechanisms used by China&\#x27;s healthcare system to control medical costs and improve efficiency by grouping diagnoses and treatments into standardized categories with fixed payment values. These systems replaced fee-for-service models to incentivize hospitals to provide cost-effective care while maintaining quality standards.

**「Impact」** Medical institutions in China will need to restructure their coding and billing systems to comply with the 3.0 version requirements by the specified deadlines, affecting how they manage patient discharge records and claim reimbursements. Hospitals that previously used DRG/DIP payment standards as internal performance metrics will need to redesign their incentive structures to comply with the new prohibition on linking payment standards to departmental assessments.

**「Impact」** Anhui-based firms and research institutions will gain access to expanded commercial space and biomedical technology markets, with targeted industrial growth potentially creating thousands of high-skilled jobs in quantum, fusion, and aerospace sectors. ### [China Post integrates e-commerce, logistics, and finance to support rural revitalization](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179037.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Published on September 4, 2026, in People&\#x27;s Daily, this report details China Post&\#x27;s strategy to aggregate commercial, logistics, capital, and information flows to address rural revitalization challenges. During the 14th Five-Year Plan period, China Post’s agricultural-related loans grew by over 1 trillion yuan, while online sales reached 73.2 billion yuan and agricultural parcel deliveries exceeded 22.1 billion items. The company leverages its unique status as the only central enterprise with postal offices in every township and mail service in every village to operate the Youle.com e-commerce platform, which drove over 54.5 million packaging orders through "one product, one policy" operations in the first half of 2026. Additionally, Postal Savings Bank of China maintained an agricultural loan balance of 2.51 trillion yuan by end-2025, utilizing mobile "backpack banking" to serve rural clients lacking traditional collateral.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** China Post operates the most extensive rural distribution network in China, comprising approximately 48,000 village-level service stations and 85,000 rural delivery routes. It employs a "self-operated + agency" model that places roughly 70% of its financial outlets in county-level and rural areas, allowing it to bridge the gap between remote agricultural producers and national markets.

**「Impact」** The integration of these services creates a self-reinforcing cycle where successful agricultural sales generate parcel volume for logistics and customer inflow for financial services. This model provides a scalable framework for rural economic development, with goals to exceed 100 billion yuan in rural e-commerce transaction volume and 15 billion parcels annually by the end of the 15th Five-Year Plan.

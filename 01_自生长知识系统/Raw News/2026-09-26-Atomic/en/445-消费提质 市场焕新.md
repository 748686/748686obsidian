### [CPPCC holds wetlands science lecture at Beijing Forestry University](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/25/content_30183005.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 24, the Chinese People&\#x27;s Political Consultative Conference \(CPPCC\) held its "Committee Member Science Lecture" series at Beijing Forestry University, featuring an event titled "Understanding Wetlands and Practicing Ecological Civilization Thought." CPPCC member Cui Lijuan, deputy dean of the Chinese Academy of Forestry Sciences, delivered a report covering the scientific definition and classification of wetlands, their role as "Earth&\#x27;s kidneys," and the ecological challenges they face. The lecture also highlighted China&\#x27;s innovations in wetland restoration theory and technology, aligning with President Xi Jinping&\#x27;s directives on public science education.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 25, 00:26

**「Background」** The CPPCC&\#x27;s Committee Member Science Lecture series is part of the broader "serving the people" duty-fulfillment initiative, aiming to leverage the professional expertise of committee members to improve public scientific literacy. Wetlands are ecologically significant because they provide critical ecosystem services such as water purification and flood control, though they face global pressures from urbanization and pollution.

**「Impact」** The lecture reinforces official policy emphasis on ecological civilization by showcasing scientific advancements in wetland conservation. It aims to shape public opinion and support for national environmental priorities through accessible science communication.

### [Jiangsu strengthens online food safety governance](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/25/content_30183006.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Jiangsu province has launched a comprehensive crackdown on online catering food safety violations, designating it as one of three key projects for rectifying misconduct affecting public interests. The initiative, led by the Jiangsu Market Regulation Administration, has resulted in over 54,000 takeaway merchants completing rectification, with complaint volumes dropping 9.81% in August 2026. The province employs a "double task force" regulatory mechanism, leverages over 11,000 delivery riders as food safety supervisors through the "Food Safety随手拍" platform, and has integrated more than 200,000 enterprises into AI-powered online supervision systems.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 25, 00:26

**「Background」** Online food delivery has become a major channel for consumer dining, but regulatory challenges persist due to ghost kitchens and hygiene violations. Jiangsu&\#x27;s approach represents a multi-layered governance strategy combining traditional inspection, technology-enabled monitoring, and community participation to address systemic food safety issues in the digital economy.

**「Impact」** The campaign directly affects approximately 200,000+ food service businesses operating in Jiangsu province through mandatory compliance with digital monitoring requirements and potential suspension from platforms. Consumers benefit from improved food safety oversight, though the long-term effectiveness of these measures requires ongoing evaluation.

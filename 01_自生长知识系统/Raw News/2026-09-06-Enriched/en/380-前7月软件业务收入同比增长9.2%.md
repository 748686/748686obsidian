The Asia-Pacific Media High-Level Forum opened on September 5, 2026, in Shenzhen, Guangdong province. Li Shulei, member of the Politburo Standing Committee and head of the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee, delivered the keynote address, while Huang Kunming, party secretary of Guangdong province, gave opening remarks. The forum, themed "Building a Prosperous Path for the Asia-Pacific Community: Media Consensus and Action," was hosted by the Publicity Department, Xinhua News Agency, and the Guangdong provincial government, and attended by over 400 representatives from more than 40 countries and regions, as well as UN agencies and international organizations. Participants emphasized the role of media in supporting the APEC China Year and promoting regional cooperation, innovation, and cultural exchange.

**「Background」** China holds the APEC presidency for 2026, known as the "APEC China Year," with the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders&\#x27; Meeting scheduled to be hosted in China. This high-level forum brings together media professionals from across the Asia-Pacific region to discuss collaborative storytelling and policy communication around regional economic integration and technological innovation.

**「Impact」** The formation of the preparatory committee for the Asia-Pacific Media Partnership Initiative by 16 Chinese and foreign media outlets signals a structured effort to enhance cross-border media collaboration on APEC-related coverage. This could strengthen coordinated reporting on China&\#x27;s APEC priorities and improve regional narratives around open economy and tech cooperation.

### [China&\#x27;s Propaganda Dept holds meeting on improving editorial style in party media](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/06/content_30179358.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 5, 2026, the Publicity Department of the Chinese Communist Party Central Committee held an experience exchange conference in Hangzhou, Zhejiang, focused on improving editorial style in the theoretical sections of party newspapers and periodicals. The meeting emphasized that these sections serve as key platforms for promoting the Party&\#x27;s innovative theories and must align with President Xi Jinping&\#x27;s directives to make theoretical宣传 more accessible to the general public. Participants were urged to strengthen the practical application of theory, enhance content innovation through academic and historical depth, improve digital and international dissemination, and build a stronger team of editors and writers to support high-quality theoretical publishing.

**「Background」** In China&\#x27;s political system, party newspapers and periodicals such as People&\#x27;s Daily and Qiushi function as official channels for disseminating Marxist theory and Party policy. Theoretical sections in these publications are designed to interpret and popularize the Party&\#x27;s ideological innovations, and their editorial style directly affects how effectively these ideas reach grassroots audiences.

**「Impact」** The conference signals a centralized push to reform how party theoretical content is written and distributed, which will likely influence editorial standards across all official party media. Implementation will depend on provincial propaganda departments and publishing houses adopting the recommended practices for theory popularization.

本报深圳9月5日电  （记者陈尚文、程远州）亚太媒体高端论坛5日在广东深圳开幕。中共中央政治局委员、中宣部部长李书磊出席开幕式并发表主旨演讲。中共中央政治局委员、广东省委书记黄坤明出席并致辞。

与会嘉宾认为，习近平主席提出构建亚太共同体的重大主张，为共建开放型亚太经济、推动亚太共同繁荣提供了重要指引。今年是亚太经合组织（APEC）“中国年”，中国将举办亚太经合组织第三十三次领导人非正式会议，相信在各方支持下，会议将取得丰硕成果，推动亚太合作迈上新台阶。

与会嘉宾表示，亚太地区的发展合作，离不开亚太媒体的积极参与。要讲好亚太开放融通故事，全方位报道亚太各领域合作进展成效，营造亚太开放发展的良好舆论环境。要积极宣介APEC系列成果，阐释解读各方支持科技创新的政策举措，凝聚亚太创新发展的区域共识。要深入开展联合采访、技术创新、人才培养等务实合作，以媒体交流传承文明、传播文化、传递友好，促进亚太人民相知相亲。

论坛以“共筑亚太共同体繁荣之路：媒体的共识与行动”为主题，由中宣部、新华社、广东省人民政府主办，深圳市人民政府承办。来自40多个国家和地区以及联合国机构、国际组织的400余位代表出席。论坛上中外16家媒体发起成立亚太媒体合作伙伴计划筹委会。

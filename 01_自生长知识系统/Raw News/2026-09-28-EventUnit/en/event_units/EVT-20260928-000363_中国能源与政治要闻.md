## Event Overview

On September 28, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive set of reports spanning domestic energy milestones, institutional reform, and high-level diplomatic engagements. The central narrative thread connecting the key articles is China's strategic emphasis on high-quality development, green transition, and global cooperation. The flagship news item, reported on the front page, announced that photovoltaic (solar) power installation capacity had surpassed coal-fired power generation for the first time, marking a pivotal moment in China's energy mix. This milestone was contextualized through a retrospective analysis of the solar industry's evolution from its struggles in the early 2010s to its current global dominance. Concurrently, the edition covered National Vice Chairman Han Zheng's address at the 81st UN General Assembly, emphasizing multilateralism and the Global Development Initiative (GDI). Additionally, reports highlighted specific governance reforms, such as the reduction of reporting burdens at the grassroots level ("One Table Pass" policy), and detailed international exchanges, including US-China agricultural cooperation and cultural diplomacy.

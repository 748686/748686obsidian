# EVT-20260928-000371: 2026 World Manufacturing Conference and Anhui Intelligent Manufacturing Development

## Event Overview

Between September 20 and 23, 2026, the 2026 World Manufacturing Conference was held in Hefei, Anhui Province. The conference theme was "Intelligent Manufacturing for the World, Creating a Better Future." Following the event, on September 28, 2026, *People's Daily* published a dedicated special report (Page 15) titled "Jointly Embracing the Appointment of Intelligent Manufacturing, Sharing Development Opportunities," along with related articles on economic trends and technological innovation covering Pages 17 and 18. The coverage highlights Anhui’s transformation from a traditional agricultural province to an emerging manufacturing highland, emphasizing international cooperation, industrial clustering, energy security, and the role of patient capital in supporting technological innovation.

## Core Facts

**Conference Logistics and Economic Impact**
*   **Date:** September 20–23, 2026.
*   **Location:** Hefei, Anhui Province.
*   **Contracted Projects:** 790 projects were successfully signed at this edition, with a total investment of 363.41 billion RMB.
*   **Historical Context:** Since the conference's inception in 2018, 9 editions have cumulatively signed over 5,800 projects with a total investment of approximately 4 trillion RMB.
*   **Guest of Honor:** The United Kingdom served as the guest of honor for the second time, becoming the first country to "keep a second appointment" since the mechanism began. (Source: Article #451)

**Anhui Manufacturing Achievements ("The 14th Five-Year Plan" and Early 2026)**
*   **Industrial Revenue:** From the start of the "14th Five-Year Plan" period to 2025, Anhui’s above-average industrial revenue grew from 3.8 trillion RMB to 5.9 trillion RMB.
*   **Automotive Sector (H1 2026):** Anhui ranked first nationally in four indicators: total automobile production, new energy vehicle (NEV) production, automobile export volume, and automobile export value.
*   **Strategic Industries (Jan–Jul 2026):** High-tech manufacturing value-added grew by 48.3% year-on-year. Strategic emerging industry output value grew by 17.3%, accounting for 46.1% of above-average industrial output. (Source: Article #453)
*   **Energy Consumption:** From 2021 to 2025, Anhui’s total social electricity consumption grew at an annual average rate of 9.0%. In 2025, consumption exceeded 370 billion kWh, an increase of over 130 billion kWh compared to 2020. The peak electricity load reached 69.99 million kilowatts. (Source: Article #454)

**International Participation and Cooperation**
*   **UK Engagement:** UK Prime Minister Rishi Sunak’s administration is represented by Ambassador to China Leo Murray, who highlighted UK strengths in aerospace, automotive engineering, advanced materials, life sciences, AI, and clean energy.
*   **Global SME Alliance:** Carlos Magrinos, Global Chairman of the Global Alliance of SMEs, returned for the second time, noting that Chinese enterprises have evolved from parts suppliers to internationalized brand manufacturers.
*   **Côte d'Ivoire:** A presidential message was read by Solange Amitcha, representing the President’s cabinet minister, emphasizing industrialization as a core strategic task for Côte d'Ivoire and China’s importance as a partner. (Source: Article #451)
*   **Investment Data:** Over 5,000 foreign-invested enterprises have settled in Anhui, including more than 200 subsidiaries of Fortune Global 500 companies. In the previous year (2025), Anhui’s actual utilization of foreign capital reached 15.33 billion RMB, up 23.9% year-on-year. (Source: Article #452)
*   **Corporate Case Studies:**
    *   **Chery Automobile:** Sold over 233,000 vehicles across 24 European countries in the first 8 months of 2026 (up 193% YoY), with NEVs exceeding 114,000 units (up over 300% YoY).
    *   **Gotion High-tech:** Overseas business now accounts for over 40% of revenue, supported by strategic investment from Volkswagen and 10 overseas production bases.
    *   **Yingliu Group:** Self-developed over 20 types of superalloy blades for C919 and C929 large aircraft engines.
    *   **French Company yudoque:** Signed a cooperation agreement in January 2026 with Huainan High-tech Zone to build its first intelligent connected vehicle comprehensive service base in the Asia-Pacific region. (Sources: Articles #452, #453)

**Technological and Industrial Innovations**
*   **Digital-Intelligent Transformation:** The national integration of informatization and industrialization development level reached 67.1 in H1 2026. Anhui’s level rose to 68.2. AI application coverage in key manufacturing scenarios reached 34.2% (up 5.5 percentage points), with 10.6% of above-average manufacturing enterprises using AI large models. (Source: Article #451)
*   **New Products Released:** The conference released 10 leading new technologies and products, including quantum diamond chip detectors, memory engineering test equipment, crawling welding robots, wheeled-arm humanoid robots, and solid oxide fuel cells. (Source: Article #451)
*   **Quantum Technology:** Hefei is identified as a major innovation highland. The national quantum computing cloud platform provides computing services covering many countries and regions. (Source: Article #451)
*   **Recycled Plastics Technology:** Lv Huaixing, Vice Dean of the Jiangxi Green Cycle Polymer Material Circular Utilization Research Institute, detailed a breakthrough in removing impurities from recycled plastics. The team developed 5 cleaning processes and 8 sorting technologies, reducing impurity size and quantity by over 70%. They process approximately 250,000 tons of home appliance waste annually, reducing carbon emissions by over 400,000 tons. (Source: Article #455)

**Energy Infrastructure**
*   **Shaanxi-Anhui UHV Project:** The陕北—安徽 (Shaanbei–Anhui) ±800 kV UHV DC transmission project was officially commissioned on June 30, 2026. It is 1,055 km long and transmits over 36 billion kWh annually to Anhui, with more than half being green electricity.
*   **Energy Storage:** As of August 2026, Anhui had built 28 grid-side energy storage stations (total capacity 2.762 million kW) and 145 user-side energy storage stations (total capacity 1.377 million kW). (Source: Article #454)

**Financial and Market Context (Related Coverage)**
*   **Capital Markets:** In the first 8 months of 2026, 102 enterprises completed IPOs on the A-share market, over 90% of which belonged to strategic emerging industries.
*   **Listed Company Performance:** H1 2026 semi-annual reports showed listed companies achieved profits of 3.6 trillion RMB, up 19.5% YoY. The STAR Market (Science and Technology Innovation Board) saw net profit grow by 437.6% YoY.
*   **Patient Capital:** Articles emphasized the role of "patient capital" in supporting hard tech enterprises, citing examples such as Shanghai Guofang’s 630 million RMB lead investment in SynSense (燧原科技) and national fund support for ChangXin Memory Technologies and Origin Quantum. (Sources: Articles #457, #458)

## Event Conclusion

The 2026 World Manufacturing Conference, held in Hefei in September 2026, served as a pivotal moment showcasing Anhui’s evolution into a global manufacturing powerhouse. The event highlighted a successful convergence of traditional industrial upgrading (automotive, materials) and futuristic technology deployment (quantum computing, AI, fusion energy). International engagement was robust, with the UK returning as guest of honor and significant cross-border partnerships formalized. Concurrent media coverage emphasized that this industrial growth is underpinned by critical enablers: stable green energy infrastructure, the inflow of patient capital, and a regulatory framework aiming to balance AI innovation with safety. The conference reinforced Anhui’s narrative as a province where "intelligent manufacturing" is not just a slogan but a measurable economic reality, evidenced by record-breaking automotive exports and a rapidly expanding high-tech industrial base.

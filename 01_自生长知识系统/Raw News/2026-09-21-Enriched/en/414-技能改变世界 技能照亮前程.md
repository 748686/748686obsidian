On September 20, 2026, the fifth edition of the “湾区升明月” \(Bay Area Rises\) film and music gala was held in Macau, featuring over 100 performers from mainland China, Taiwan, the Hong Kong and Macau Special Administrative Regions, and overseas countries. The event was co-organized by the Movie Channel Program Center, the Macau SAR Cultural Bureau, the Ziying Cultural Group, and Phoenix TV. Performances included original songs like 《湾》 sung by Leoh-Pai Tseng, alongside cross-strait classics such as 《流星雨》 \(Meteor Shower\) and film narratives like 《回家之路》 \(The Way Home\). This marked the third consecutive year the gala was held in Macau, celebrating cultural integration and national cohesion during the opening year of the 15th Five-Year Plan.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 21, 00:26

**「About the event」** The "湾区升明月" \(Gulf Area Rising Moon\) event is a regular film and music gala focused on cultural exchange within the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, with its first edition held in 2021. The 2026 Gala is the fifth iteration of the event and the third consecutive edition to be held in Macau, organized by a coalition that includes China&\#x27;s Film Channel Program Center and Macau&\#x27;s government cultural bureau.

**「Cultural Ecosystem Impact」** The event&\#x27;s fifth iteration, held for the third consecutive year in Macao and broadcast on CCTV-6, actively promotes cultural integration across the Greater Bay Area and among Chinese diaspora communities by featuring cross-strait collaborative productions and uniting over a hundred performers from various regions. This annual gathering reinforces Macao&\#x27;s positioning as a key multicultural hub for the region while serving as a platform for patriotic and unified messaging during the new five-year plan launch year. \[tool-2-1\] \[tool-2-3\]

## Core Facts

### 1. 2026 WTT World Table Tennis Chinese Grand Smash
*   **Conclusion:** The 11-day tournament concluded on the evening of October 11, 2026, at Shougang Park in Beijing.
*   **Results:** The Chinese table tennis team won two championships:
    *   **Women's Singles:** Wang Manyu defeated Japan's Sato Tomoe 4-0 in the final.
    *   **Women's Doubles:** Wang Manyu and Kuai Man defeated the Japanese pair Miwa Harimoto and Hina Hayata.
*   **Context:** This was the third consecutive year the Chinese Grand Smash was hosted in Beijing. The venue (formerly a 2022 Beijing Winter Olympics training venue) and the adjacent Grand Smash Park (at the foot of the "Snow Flyer" ski jump) created an immersive scene integrating sports viewing, leisure, cultural tourism, and consumption.
*   **International Reaction:** ITTF President Morley expressed satisfaction with the post-Olympic utilization of the venue, suggesting the model could be promoted globally.

### 2. 2026 National Rural and Beautiful Village Basketball Tournament ("Village BA")
*   **Opening:** The Southern Zone competition opened on October 11, 2026, in Qiaorong Village, Dafeng Town, Shanglin County, Nanning City, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region.
*   **Participants:** 18 representative teams and over 210 farmer athletes from 9 provinces/regions: Shanghai, Zhejiang, Fujian, Jiangxi, Hunan, Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan, and Yunnan.
*   **Schedule:** The Southern Zone competition lasted for 8 days, concluding on October 18.
*   **Advancement:** The top five teams from the Southern Zone will advance to the National Finals, joining the top five from the Northern Zone and the top six from the Central Zone (including the host team).
*   **Finals Location:** The National Finals are scheduled for October 24-29, 2026, in Xianfeng County, Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture, Hubei Province.
*   **Local Impact:** Shanglin County introduced a harvest market featuring local products, intangible cultural heritage handicrafts, and tourism promotion to leverage the event's brand influence for rural economic development.

### 3. 12th Beijing Municipal Paralympic Games
*   **Conclusion:** The games concluded on October 11, 2026, after a 42-day duration.
*   **Scale:** Organized by the Beijing Municipal Disabled Persons' Federation, this edition was the largest in history, with nearly 2,000 participants.
*   **Events:** The competition included traditional sports (athletics, swimming, table tennis, chess), mass sports (dry-land curling), and Paralympic sports making their debut in this championship: equestrianism, supported racing, and rock climbing.
*   **Source Attribution:** Information attributed to Yang Ming, Deputy Director of the Beijing Disabled Persons' Cultural and Sports Service Center.

### 4. 78th Frankfurt Book Fair
*   **Conclusion:** The 5-day fair concluded on October 11, 2026, in Frankfurt, Germany.
*   **Scale:** Attracted over 4,000 publishing institutions from more than 100 countries and regions.
*   **Chinese Participation:** Organized by China National Publications Import & Export (Group) Corporation, featuring over 80 domestic publishing units under the "Reading China" banner.
*   **Output:** Exhibited over 3,000 types of精品 (boutique/quality) books (more than 4,600 volumes) and hosted 55 cultural exchange activities, including seminars, author dialogues, copyright signings, and book launches.
*   **Key Themes:** Promoting understanding of China's development path, governance, and theory; showcasing traditional culture (e.g., Long March themes, Sanxingdui civilization); and fostering European-Chinese academic and commercial publishing cooperation.

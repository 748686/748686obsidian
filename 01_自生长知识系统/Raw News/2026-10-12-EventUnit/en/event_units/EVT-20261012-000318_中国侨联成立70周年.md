# Event Name
70th Anniversary of the Founding of the All-China Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese (ACFROC)

## Event Overview
On October 11, 2026, the thematic exhibition "Family and Country United Towards Rejuvenation — Thematic Exhibition for the 70th Anniversary of the Founding of the All-China Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese" opened at the China Museum of Overseas Chinese History in Beijing. This event serves as a key commemorative activity for the 70th anniversary of the founding of the ACFROC, which was established on October 12, 1956. The exhibition and associated reporting highlight the organization's history under the leadership of the Communist Party of China, its role in uniting overseas Chinese and returned overseas Chinese, and its contribution to national rejuvenation.

## Core Facts
*   **Anniversary Date:** The All-China Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese (ACFROC) was officially approved by the CPC Central Committee and founded in Beijing on October 12, 1956. Consequently, October 2026 marks its 70th anniversary [Article #469].
*   **Exhibition Launch:** The thematic exhibition titled "Family and Country United Towards Rejuvenation" (家国同心向复兴) opened on October 11, 2026 [Article #466, #467, #469].
*   **Location:** The exhibition is held at the China Museum of Overseas Chinese History in Beijing [Article #466, #469].
*   **Organizers:** The exhibition is hosted by the All-China Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese and undertaken by the China Museum of Overseas Chinese History [Article #466].
*   **Historical Lineage:** The exhibition traces the organizational lineage back to the Yan'an Federation of Returned Overseas Chinese, identified as the "red source" of Party-led returned overseas Chinese groups [Article #466, #469].
*   **Key Historical Figure:** Chen Jiageng (first chairman of ACFROC) is highlighted as a symbolic figure. A photograph from May 31, 1940, records his arrival in Yan'an with the "South Seas Chinese Compatriots回国慰劳视察团" (Return Visit and Inspection Team), after which he firmly aligned with the Communist Party [Article #469].
*   **Current Leadership:** Wang Linggui serves as the Party Secretary and Chairman of the ACFROC [Article #469].
*   **Political Directive:** The commemoration follows a National Overseas Chinese Affairs Work Conference held on July 27–28, 2026, in Beijing, where directives from General Secretary Xi Jinping were conveyed. Xi emphasized "better凝聚侨心侨力" (cohesion of overseas Chinese hearts and strength) and guiding overseas Chinese to contribute to the Chinese Dream [Article #469].
*   **Organizational Scope:** There are currently 27,000 ACFROC organizations at all levels across China, and approximately 12,800 "Overseas Chinese Home" (侨胞之家) facilities cover urban and rural communities [Article #469].
*   **Service Achievements:**
    *   The ACFROC system assisted 58,000 people in escaping poverty during the targeted poverty alleviation campaign [Article #469].
    *   Overseas Chinese participated in donating to build the "Overseas Chinese Winter Olympics Ice and Snow Museum" in Chongli, Hebei [Article #469].
    *   During the COVID-19 pandemic, care health kits were sent to more than 50 countries and regions [Article #469].
    *   In response to the earthquakes in Venezuela (magnitude 7+) on June 24, 2026, the ACFROC coordinated with local embassies and overseas Chinese groups to ensure safety and deliver care [Article #469].
*   **Economic Engagement:**
    *   The 2026 "Belt and Road" Overseas Chinese and Chinese Businessmen Cooperation Development Conference featured an exchange event where over 10,000 Chinese products were promoted for export via the "Overseas Chinese Aid to a Thousand Enterprises and Ten Thousand Products Going Global" initiative [Article #469].
    *   In Yiwu, Zhejiang, approximately 110,000 overseas Chinese merchants and over 300,000 people involved in overseas Chinese affairs facilitate nearly 10 million visits annually [Article #469].

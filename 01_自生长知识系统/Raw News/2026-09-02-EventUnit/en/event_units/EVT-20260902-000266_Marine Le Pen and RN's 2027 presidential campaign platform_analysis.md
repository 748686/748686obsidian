### 📋 Analysis Subject
Marine Le Pen / National Rally (RN) – 2027 Presidential Bid

| | **Positive (Helpful)** | **Negative (Harmful)** |

### 🎯 Strategic Combinations (SO/WO/ST/WT)

*   **SO Strategy (Maxi-Maxi):** Leverage **brand recognition** and **inflation anxiety** to position the 2027 campaign as the only viable economic alternative to the status quo, ignoring opposition noise.
*   **WO Strategy (Mini-Maxi):** Use **party consolidation** and **organizational resources** to compensate for the **governance gap** by surrounding Le Pen with a technocratic "shadow cabinet" to project competence.
*   **ST Strategy (Maxi-Mini):** Utilize **proven resilience** against media attacks to frame any legal or political opposition as a "system attack" on democracy, rallying the base.
*   **WT Strategy (Mini-Mini):** Mitigate the risk of **Republican Front** coalitions by adopting a softer, more inclusive tone (further "dédiabolisation") to peel off moderate center-right voters before they consolidate.

## 3. Scenario Planning (2026–2027)

### 🔍 Key Drivers & Uncertainties

**Certainty Trends:**
*   Continued economic pressure on the middle class.
*   Deepening political polarization in French society.

**Key Uncertainties:**
1.  **Macron's Health/Status:** Will Macron run, and is he politically viable?
2.  **European Geopolitics:** Will the Ukraine conflict escalate further, impacting French security policy?
3.  **Legal Outcomes:** Will new judicial decisions impact the eligibility of key RN figures?

### 🎭 Scenario Construction

#### Scenario 1: 🌟 Ideal World (The RN Landslide)
*   **Core Assumption:** Macron's popularity collapses; the centrist coalition fragments.
*   **Description:** Le Pen campaigns on a clear "Change vs. Chaos" platform. With the traditional right unable to offer a compelling alternative, RN sweeps both the presidency and parliament. The international community adjusts quickly to a RN-led France.
*   **Impact:** Complete political realignment in France; significant shift in EU policy.

#### Scenario 2: 📊 Normality Continues (The Tight Race)
*   **Core Assumption:** Macron or a strong successor rallies the center; the RN remains popular but electorally capped.
*   **Description:** A polarized election where Le Pen performs strongly in the first round but faces a united republican front in the second. The outcome hinges on specific policy details and turnout.
*   **Impact:** Political gridlock or a "cohabitation" style pressure on the executive.

#### Scenario 3: ⚠️ Stress Test (The Legal/Political Crisis)
*   **Core Assumption:** A major legal scandal or external shock (e.g., sudden escalation in Eastern Europe) derails the standard campaign.
*   **Description:** Le Pen faces serious legal hurdles or the RN is forced to pivot abruptly to crisis management. Voter trust erodes rapidly.
*   **Impact:** Electoral collapse or a shift in RN leadership dynamics prior to the vote.

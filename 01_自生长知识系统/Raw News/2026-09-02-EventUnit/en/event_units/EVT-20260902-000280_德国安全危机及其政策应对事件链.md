# German Security Crisis and Policy Response Event Chain

## Event Overview
This EventUnit synthesizes a developing security crisis in Germany, characterized by drone attacks in Leipzig, infrastructure incidents (including incendiary devices linked to Russia), and political rhetoric from the Alternative for Germany (AfD). A key policy response to this crisis environment is the Hamburg government’s initiative to ban smart glasses in public baths and on trains, cited as a direct measure addressing safety and privacy concerns arising from the heightened security context.

## Core Facts
- **Leipzig Drone Attacks:** Multiple drone incidents occurred in Leipzig, causing alarm among residents. No injuries were reported, but the attacks prompted emergency responses including airport closures. Authorities are investigating whether these were isolated incidents or part of a broader campaign.
- **Hamburg Smart Glasses Ban:** The Hamburg senate passed a regulation banning smart glasses in public baths and on public transportation (HVV). This ban is characterized as a direct policy response to the ongoing security crisis, aiming to prevent unauthorized recording and ensure public safety in sensitive spaces.
- **Affiliated Incidents:** 
    - Incendiary devices were found in packages at logistics companies, with investigations pointing to Russian involvement.
    - Russia was also suspected of planning to shoot down passenger planes using drones over Germany.
    - A fire occurred at a transformer station, causing traffic disruptions at Berlin’s Hauptbahnhof; police treat it as an act of sabotage.
- **Political Discourse:** AfD leaders, including Tino Chrupalla, have publicly criticized Ukraine President Volodymyr Zelensky. Additionally, Defense Minister Boris Pistorius warned that Russia poses an immediate danger to Germany, necessitating increased defense spending and military modernization.
- **Public Sentiment:** Surveys indicate a majority of Germans believe their country is not currently at war and do not perceive an imminent threat of invasion, despite the series of security incidents.

# Event Name

Bank of Korea AI Sentiment Index Revamp

## Event Overview

On September 2, 2026, the Bank of Korea (BOK) announced it is overhauling its existing news sentiment index by integrating artificial intelligence. The upgrade aims to more accurately measure public opinion as reflected in media coverage, replacing a previous methodology that had faced criticism for inaccuracies and misclassifications. This initiative is part of the BOK's broader "Digital Transformation Strategy," which includes a 2 trillion won ($1.47 billion) investment over six years in AI infrastructure.

## Core Facts

*   **Date:** September 2, 2026.
*   **Actor:** Bank of Korea (BOK).
*   **Action:** Revamping the "Korea News Sentiment Index."
*   **Methodology Shift:** Moving from a previous manual or less sophisticated system to an AI-driven approach.
*   **Current Issue:** The existing index was reported to be inaccurate, with BOK officials stating it frequently missed the true "voice of the people" and misclassified negative news as positive.
*   **Strategic Context:** Part of the BOK’s Digital Transformation Strategy (announced December 2023), aiming to become a "smart central bank."
*   **Investment:** 2 trillion won ($1.47 billion) over six years (2024–2029) for AI infrastructure.
*   **Goal:** To enhance decision-making effectiveness and support data-driven policy by providing a more accurate assessment of public sentiment.

*   **Previous Inaccuracies:** Confirmed by Yonhap News, Kyungin Newspaper, and Maeil Business Newspaper, which cite the BOK's acknowledgment that the current/old method was inaccurate or failed to capture true sentiment.

**「Legal Framework Context」** The Computer Fraud and Abuse Act \(CFAA\) is the primary federal statute governing unauthorized computer access, requiring intentional or knowing conduct for liability. The economic loss rule in tort law prevents negligence claims for purely financial harm, limiting recovery to cases involving physical injury or property damage. Recent case law from multiple jurisdictions has consistently classified data breaches as economic losses rather than property damage.

**「Practical Implications」** AI companies developing autonomous agents may face limited legal exposure for cybersecurity incidents caused by agent behavior, even when negligence is involved. This creates a potential accountability gap where victims of AI-caused cyberattacks may have no legal remedy under existing frameworks.

### [2026 Silk Road Civilization Forum highlights pragmatic cooperation](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202611/11/content_30190520.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On November 4, 2026, the 2026 Silk Road Civilization Development Forum was held in Fuzhou, Fujian, as a sub-forum of the 2026 Belt and Road Media Cooperation Forum. The event gathered representatives from Chinese and foreign media, government departments, research institutions, and enterprises to discuss how cultural exchange and mutual learning lay the public opinion foundation for high-quality Belt and Road Initiative \(BRI\) cooperation. Speakers emphasized a three-tiered path of cooperation: hard connectivity through infrastructure projects like power grids and nuclear plants, soft connectivity via cultural and media exchanges, and heart connectivity to build mutual trust. Participants from countries including Hungary, Uruguay, Cuba, France, and Denmark affirmed the role of media in bridging peoples and advancing the vision of a maritime Silk Road open to the world.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Nov 11, 00:26

**「Background」** The Belt and Road Initiative, launched by China in 2013, promotes infrastructure, trade, and cultural connectivity across Asia, Europe, Africa, and beyond. The Silk Road Civilization Development Forum is part of the broader Belt and Road Media Cooperation Forum, which aims to strengthen people-to-people bonds through media and cultural exchange as a complement to large-scale infrastructure and economic projects.

**「Impact」** The forum reinforces China&\#x27;s diplomatic strategy of pairing infrastructure investment with cultural and media engagement to sustain long-term BRI support abroad. Its direct impact is limited to reinforcing narrative cohesion among participating media and government actors rather than producing new policy or funding commitments.

国之交在于民相亲，民相亲在于心相通。文明交流互鉴为高质量共建“一带一路”打下坚实的民意基础。

11月4日上午，2026“一带一路”媒体合作论坛的分论坛之一——2026丝路文明发展论坛在福建福州举行。来自中外媒体、政府部门、研究机构和企业界的代表齐聚一堂。

“一艘沉船就是一座博物馆。”武汉大学教授姜波以水下考古为线索展开讲述，“南海Ⅰ号”，南海西北陆坡一号、二号，市舶司遗址……一个个实证勾勒出海上丝绸之路的文明交流图景。

中亚地区是中国与欧亚国家文化交融、友好往来的重要枢纽。“2000余年的丝路互通、文明共生，积淀了平等互信、开放包容、互尊互利的合作基因，为‘一带一路’人文合作奠定了不可替代的历史根基。”国务院发展研究中心欧亚社会发展研究所中亚研究室主任许涛说。

历史的脉络延伸为今天的合作互信。“硬联通，筑牢物质之基；软联通，拓宽互鉴之径；心联通，熔铸民心之魂。”中国核工业集团宣传文化中心主任赖江南如此概括文明交流互鉴的现实路径。从东帝汶的国家电网到巴基斯坦的“华龙一号”，一项项工程成为增进了解、厚植互信的载体。

高质量共建“一带一路”，媒体是沟通民心的桥梁纽带。论坛上，来自匈牙利、乌拉圭、古巴、法国、丹麦等多国的媒体代表发言，表示愿发挥媒体作用，让丝路故事跨越山海、直抵人心。“最重要的桥梁并非由钢铁和混凝土构筑，而是由信任、尊重、友谊与人心来搭建。”丹麦《哥本哈根邮报》共同所有人耶斯珀·斯基尔说。

专题研讨以“从福建到世界：海上丝绸之路的文明交流互鉴”为议题展开。与会嘉宾表示，将立足各自领域深化交流合作，以务实合作增进相互理解、传递友好情谊，推动海丝文化通达世界。

## Core Facts

**1. New Energy Vehicle (NEV) Industry**
*   **Claim:** *People's Daily* commentary argues that China's NEV industry contributes to global growth rather than engaging in zero-sum competition ("making the cake bigger" rather than "snatching the cake").
*   **Data:** Citing the International Energy Agency (IEA), approximately 70% of pure electric vehicles sold in China in 2025 had a price lower than the average of comparable fuel vehicles.
*   **Evidence:** 16 Chinese automakers with 25 brands participated in the Paris Motor Show, accounting for roughly 40% of all participating auto brands. Foreign automakers such as Volkswagen (investing in XPeng), Stellantis (joint venture with Leapmotor), and Renault are deepening cooperation with Chinese partners.
*   **Source:** Article #361 (Page 5).

**2. Disease Prevention and Control (CDC)**
*   **Achievement:** Sichuan province has been officially confirmed to have eliminated schistosomiasis, a milestone for the most heavily affected mountainous region in China.
*   **Data:** Qinghai province reduced its echinococcosis prevalence rate from 0.63% in 2012 to 0.1% in 2025.
*   **Innovation:** Jiangsu Province developed an "AI Smart Snail Identification" WeChat mini-program to help identify *Oncomelania hupensis* snails, aiding schistosomiasis control. Zhejiang Yiwu utilizes approximately 2,000 monitoring哨 points (pharmacies, hotels, clinics) for infectious disease surveillance.
*   **Source:** Article #362 (Page 5).

**3. Domestic Policy and Discipline**
*   **Party Discipline:** Commentary emphasizes "ruling the Party according to regulations" and the principle of "no exceptions now" (*xian bu po li*), arguing that disciplinary rules must be applied consistently without tolerance for "first-time" violations.
*   **Legal/Administrative:** The Ministry of Civil Affairs announced new management measures for funeral homes and cemeteries, effective March 30, 2027. Local governments are designated as the investment and construction主体 (main bodies) for new cemeteries, which obtain state-owned land use rights through allocation.
*   **Source:** Article #361 (Page 5); Article #369 (Page 7).

**4. Infrastructure and Transport**
*   **Cruise Industry:** The second domestically produced large cruise ship, *Adora Huacheng*, commenced its maiden voyage from Guangzhou Nansha International Cruise Home Port. It is docked alongside the first domestic large cruise ship, *Adora Macheng*.
*   **Western Land-Sea New Corridor:** The Pinglu Canal officially opened to navigation in September 2026 after 1,481 days of construction by over 20,000 workers. The Baise Water Conservancy Hub navigational facilities are expected to be completed by 2027, shortening transport time for Yunnan goods by approximately 7 days.
*   **Source:** Article #363 (Page 5); Article #371 (Page 7).

**5. Science and Technology Awards**
*   **Recipient:** Song Rende, former director of the Yushu Prefecture Animal Disease Prevention and Control Center in Qinghai, was awarded the national title of "Most Beautiful Science and Technology Worker." He is the first scientist from Qinghai province to receive this honor.
*   **Achievement:** Song and his wife Li Guomei (a grassland scientist) and daughter Song Weishu (a resource environment science Ph.D.) have contributed to increasing calf birth weight by 30%, advancing the first calving age of female yaks by 2 years, and increasing herd throughput rates.
*   **Source:** Article #367 (Page 6).

*   **Key Themes:**

    *   **Economic Cooperation:** Discussion on extending the "cooperation list" based on market demand and industrial complementarity.

*   **Participants:** Speakers included Shen Xin (All-China People's Friendship Association), Liu Jin (Bank of China), Li Jianjun (China International Exchange Association), and various academics and business leaders.
*   **Source:** Articles #376–#379 (Page 10).

**7. Economic Development Initiatives**

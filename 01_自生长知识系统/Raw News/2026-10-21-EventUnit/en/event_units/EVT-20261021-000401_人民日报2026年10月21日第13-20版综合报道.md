## Event Overview

On October 21, 2026, the Chinese state newspaper *People's Daily* published its daily issue featuring a comprehensive array of reports across 13 distinct pages (Editions 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, and 20). This event unit synthesizes coverage related to Beijing’s digital social security infrastructure, the 44th Kaifeng Chrysanthemum Culture Festival, rural revitalization efforts documented by foreign photographers, economic analysis based on Q3 performance data, industrial development in Yudu County, cultural innovation in Shanxi, and commemorative literary reflections on the 90th anniversary of the Long March.

The coverage reflects a unified editorial stance emphasizing technological modernization, rural prosperity, cultural heritage preservation, and the historical continuity of the Communist Party’s revolutionary legacy.

## Core Facts

### 1. Beijing Social Security Digital Infrastructure
*   **Event:** The Beijing Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau launched a "Social Security Map" on the "Jingtong" (京通) APP.
*   **Launch Date:** Announced on October 20, 2026 (reported in the October 21 edition, Page 13).
*   **Scope:** The map aggregates 17 district-level social security agencies, 342 sub-district/township government service centers, and over 100 bank cooperation service outlets across Beijing.
*   **Features:** Four main便民 (convenience) functions were introduced: "Smart Recommendation," "One-Click Navigation," "One-Click Calling," and "Smart Q&A."
*   **Objective:** To allow citizens to inquire about services online, handle business nearby, and check details precisely, moving away from manual comparison of outlet capabilities.

### 2. Kaifeng Chrysanthemum Culture Festival
*   **Event:** The 44th China Kaifeng Chrysanthemum Culture Festival opened on October 18, 2026, and runs until November 17, 2026.
*   **Theme:** "Song Charm in Kaifeng, Chrysanthemum Fragrance Across China."
*   **Scale:** The city displays 3.3 million pots of chrysanthemums.
*   **Cultural Context:** Kaifeng is identified as a National Historical and Cultural City with a 4,100-year history of city construction and 168 years as a capital during the Northern Song Dynasty. The festival celebrates the city’s nickname, "China's Chrysanthemum Famous City."
*   **Economic Impact:** In 2025, Kaifeng received over 116 million tourists with cultural and tourism revenue exceeding 87 billion RMB. The city was included in the key tourism cities list of the "15th Five-Year Plan for Building a Tourism Powerhouse."
*   **Industry Integration:** The festival coincided with the 2026 Kaifeng Industry Investment Cooperation Exchange Conference and the 2026 China Automotive Supply Chain Conference.
*   **Product Development:** Kaifeng has over 2,300 chrysanthemum varieties and has developed products including tea, wine, cakes, honey, porcelain, and embroidery.

### 3. Rural Revitalization Through Foreign Lens
*   **Event:** Page 17 featured a series of photos and narratives by foreign friends documenting rural revitalization across China.
*   **Locations Highlighted:**
    *   **Shandong (Liangshan):** Black skin termite mushroom (Jizong) cultivation; 25 bases covering over 5 million square meters.
    *   **Anhui (Guichi District, Luocheng Village):** Integration of intangible cultural heritage (Guichi folk songs) with tourism and研学 (study tours).
    *   **Zhejiang (Jinhua):** A cross-strait agricultural tech park using AI and 4,000+ supplemental lights for dragon fruit cultivation.
    *   **Guangxi (Yangshuo):** Golden dragon parade on the Yulong River linking villages for tourism.
    *   **Hebei (Jizhou District):** Revival of Ming/Qing era street layouts for immersive nostalgia tourism.
    *   **Yunnan (Fugong County):** Old Mulden village transforming into a high-altitude tourism destination with 3,700+ mu of tea plantations.
    *   **Jiangxi (Xingan County):** "Lotus-fish symbiosis" ecological cycle farming.

### 4. Economic Analysis (Q3 2026)
*   **Source:** "Weekly Economic Review" (Page 19).
*   **Key Data:**
    *   New growth drivers (high-end manufacturing, digital economy, modern services) contributed over 40% to Q1-Q3 economic growth.
    *   Total goods imports and exports grew by 18.8% year-on-year in the first three quarters.
    *   Equipment manufacturing contributed nearly 90% of the export increase.
    *   Equipment manufacturing value-added grew by 10.2%; high-tech manufacturing grew by 14.3%.
    *   Capital investment in AI and humanoid robots grew by 184.1% in Q3.
*   **Major Projects Mentioned:**
    *   Completion of the Pinglu Canal (first canal connecting river and sea since the founding of the PRC).
    *  沙漠 (Desert) largest oil field exceeding 26 million tons of ultra-deep oil/gas production.
    *   Completion of the main body of China's first offshore carbon injection and gas enhancement platform.
*   **Policy:** Issuance of the "Opinions on Developing New Quality Productive Forces" in October 2026, outlining 19 key measures across 5 aspects.

### 5. Yudu County Textile Industry Transformation
*   **Location:** Yudu County, Ganzhou City, Jiangxi Province (the starting point of the Long March).
*   **Industry Scale:** Over 3,850 textile and garment enterprises, driving employment for approximately 300,000 people, with total industry revenue exceeding 100 billion RMB.
*   **Transformation:**
    *   **Product:** Shift from basic workwear to diverse fashion, including heated denim for Russian markets (800,000 pairs/year) and high-end women's wear.
    *   **Production:** Over 80% of above-scale enterprises have completed digital-intelligent transformation. Examples include "5G+AI Visual Digital Twin" smart spinning platforms and automated hanging systems.
    *   **Value Chain:** Movement from OEM (original equipment manufacturer) to brand building, with 42.2% of above-scale enterprises holding self-owned brands.

### 6. Cultural Innovation: Shanxi Museum "Micro-Museum"
*   **Product:** "Jieyou Museum" (解忧博物院)拼装 (assembly) figurines released in July 2026.
*   **Features:** Combines ancient architecture joinery (Mortise and Tenon), cultural relics like the Jin Hou Bird Zun, and IP characters like the Xiao Sou drumming in opera costume.
*   **Market:** Targets young consumers seeking emotional value and experiential consumption, following the surge in interest in Shanxi’s ancient architecture driven by the game *Black Myth: Wukong*.

### 7. Long March 90th Anniversary Commemoration
*   **Context:** 2026 marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March.
*   **Page 18 Feature ("Bridge" Seeing the Long March):**
    *   **Yudu:** Five modern bridges (Red Army, Long March, Assembly, Crossing River, Victory) now span the Yudu River, replacing the original plank浮桥 (floating bridges). The textile industry in Yudu has grown to 100 billion RMB revenue.
    *   **Luding:** Seven bridges now span the Dadu River, including the historic Luding Bridge and the modern Xingkang Special Bridge.
    *   **Wuwei (Gansu):** The Wei River Bridge connects red tourism resources.
    *   **Wuqiu (Guizhou):** The Wuqiu Expressway Bridge (main span 337m concrete arch) represents modern engineering overcoming natural barriers.
*   **Literary Features (Page 20):**
    *   **"In Front of Chen Shuxiang's Tomb":** Reflections on Chen Shuxiang (师长 of the Red 34th Division) who cut his own intestines to avoid capture after the Battle of Xiangjiang. His cemetery is in Dao County, Hunan.
    *   **"The Monument of the Quartermaster Chief":** A literary recounting of a quartermaster chief who froze to death on the Snowy Mountains after giving his cotton clothes to others.
    *   **"Take Half a Belt Out":** The story of Zhou Guangcai, a Red Fourth Front Army soldier who boiled and ate his leather belt during the Grasslands crossing, keeping half as a souvenir to "take to Yan'an to see Chairman Mao."
    *   **"Red Army Noodles in Shan Jia Ji":** A narrative from Xiying, Ningxia, about how locals learned to make potato noodles from the Red Army, renaming the dish "Red Army Noodles" to remember the军民 (army-people) bond.

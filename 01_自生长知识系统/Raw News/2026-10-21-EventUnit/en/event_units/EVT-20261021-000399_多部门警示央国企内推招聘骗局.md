# Event Name
Five Departments Issue Risk Warning Against Fake "Central and State-Owned Enterprise" Internal Referral Recruitment Scams

## Event Overview
On October 21, 2026, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS), the Cyberspace Administration of China, the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Public Security, and the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission of the State Council (SASAC) jointly issued a public risk warning. The advisory targets fraudulent recruitment schemes where bad actors impersonate central and state-owned enterprises (SOEs) to scam job seekers, particularly fresh graduates, out of money under the guise of "internal referrals" or "guaranteed hiring."

The warning was reported in *People's Daily* (Article #539), highlighting that the current period is peak recruitment season. Authorities are urging job seekers to increase their vigilance, understand正规 (regular) recruitment processes, and avoid falling for schemes that exploit information asymmetries.

## Core Facts
*   **Issuing Authorities**: MOHRSS, Cyberspace Administration of China, Ministry of Education, Ministry of Public Security, and SASAC.
*   **Date of Warning**: October 21, 2026.
*   **Target of Scams**: Primarily fresh college graduates and their parents, who lack experience with formal hiring processes and are susceptible to employment anxiety.
*   **Scam Modus Operandi**:
    1.  **Traffic Generation**: Fraudsters use social media, live-streaming, short-video platforms, forums, and apps to post exaggerated or false information (e.g., "Urgent hiring for tens of thousands," "No major restrictions," "Guaranteed formal tenure," "High annual salary") using SOE names as bait.
    2.  **Trust Building**: They fabricate "internal referral channels," claiming possession of limited internal quotas or connections with HR. They may forge chat logs, success stories, labor contracts, or employment agreements to appear legitimate.
    3.  **Monetization**: Scammers demand fees under various names such as "internal referral service fee," "interview facilitation fee," or "deposit."
    4.  **Execution**: After payment, scammers often delay using excuses like "policy changes" or "leadership adjustments," then go offline/disappear.
*   **Operational Model**: A "public domain traffic generation, private domain conversion" model is used. Scammers start with free value-adds (guides, mock interviews) on public platforms, then move victims to private chats/groups for paid conversions, evading public platform monitoring.
*   **Official Stance on SOE Hiring**:
    *   SOEs have standardized hiring procedures.
    *   Many SOEs have publicly declared they do not authorize other websites, social media, or individuals to publish recruitment information on their behalf.
    *   Legitimate SOE recruitment does **not** charge fees (no registration, handling, material, introduction, or service fees).
*   **Recommended Actions for Job Seekers**:
    *   Use official channels: SOE websites, public employment service agencies, legitimate campus recruitment events, or qualified vocational intermediaries.
    *   Verify information via: National Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security Government Service Platform, Electronic Social Security Card, "掌上12333" APP, "Employment Online," "National College Student Employment Service Platform," "State-owned Central Enterprise Recruitment Platform," and "China Public Recruitment Network."
    *   Reject any demand for high fees for "paid internal referrals."
    *   Preserve evidence (chat logs, agreements, transfer records).
    *   Report fraud to police immediately; report illegal HR services to MOHRSS labor inspection; report online false information to the Cyberspace Administration's reporting center.

## Event Conclusion
On October 21, 2026, five major Chinese government departments issued a unified risk warning alerting job seekers—particularly fresh graduates—to sophisticated recruitment scams. These scams involve impersonating central and state-owned enterprises to sell fake "internal referral" services. The authorities confirmed that legitimate SOE recruitment is free and does not involve paid internal channels. The advisory instructs the public to use specific national government platforms for job searches and to report suspected fraud to police, labor inspection, or cyberspace administration authorities.

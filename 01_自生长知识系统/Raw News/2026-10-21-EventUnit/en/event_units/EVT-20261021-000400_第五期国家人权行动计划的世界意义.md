# Event Name
Significance of the Fifth National Human Rights Action Plan

## Event Overview
On June 2026, the Chinese government released the "National Human Rights Action Plan (2026-2030)," marking the fifth such plan in China's history. This event attracted global attention, particularly during the 63rd session of the United Nations Human Rights Council in Geneva. Multiple Chinese and foreign experts gathered to discuss the Chinese experience with national human rights action plans and their implications for global human rights governance. The continuity and stability demonstrated across these five sequential plans provide a unique window for understanding how China translates human rights commitments into governance practices and efficacy.

## Core Facts
*   **Event Date:** June 2026 (Release of the plan).
*   **Publication Date of Analysis:** October 21, 2026.
*   **Key Document:** *National Human Rights Action Plan (2026–2030)*.
*   **Significance:** This is the fifth National Human Rights Action Plan formulated and implemented by the Chinese government.
*   **International Context:** During the 63rd session of the UN Human Rights Council in Geneva, Chinese and foreign experts discussed the Chinese experience of these plans and their guidance significance for global human rights governance.
*   **Core Philosophy:** The plans adhere to a people-centered concept of human rights, viewing "people's happy life as the greatest human right." Survival and development rights are treated as primary basic human rights.
*   **Implementation Features:** The plans are characterized by scientific planning, action orientation, and equitable development with inclusive sharing.
*   **Author of Source Article:** Li Juan (Researcher at the Human Rights Research Center, Central South University).

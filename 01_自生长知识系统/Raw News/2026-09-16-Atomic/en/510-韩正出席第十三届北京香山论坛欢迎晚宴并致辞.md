### [China Advances Advanced Manufacturing to Build Modern Industrial Framework](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/16/content_30181319.html) ⭐️ ?/10

People&\#x27;s Daily reports that China is accelerating the development of advanced manufacturing to strengthen the core structure of its modern industrial system, prioritizing innovation, intelligent transformation, and integration with services. Recent technological milestones include the successful one-rocket, ten-satellite launch of the Zhuque-2 improved rocket in September 2026, the mass production of 8.6-generation OLED glass substrates, and the delivery of the world&\#x27;s first 10,800-car LNG dual-fuel vehicle transport ship. The country has established 35,000 basic-level, 8,200 advanced-level, and over 500 elite-level smart factories, while green factory output value rose to 22% of total large-scale manufacturing during the &\#x27;14th Five-Year&\#x27; Plan. China currently holds 25 of the world&\#x27;s top 100 innovation clusters, ranking first for the fourth consecutive year, and leads globally in four-legged robot sales with a near-70% market share as of mid-2026.

**「Background」** Advanced manufacturing is defined as the manufacturing sector that maintains world-class technological standards through continuous innovation, encompassing both emerging technology-driven industries and traditional industries transformed by advanced production methods and management models. It serves as the backbone of a modern industrial system, integrating digital, intelligent, green, and service-oriented production capabilities to overcome critical &\#x27;bottleneck&\#x27; technologies that have previously hindered domestic industrial upgrading.

**「Impact」** For manufacturers and industrial organizations, the national &\#x27;Fifteenth Five-Year&\#x27; Plan explicitly mandates a shift toward &\#x27;smartification, greening, and integration&\#x27; of manufacturing, with specific targets to increase green factory output to 45% of total large-scale manufacturing value and achieve scale application of autonomous vehicles by 2030.

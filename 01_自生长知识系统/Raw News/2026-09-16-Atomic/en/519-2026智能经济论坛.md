### [China&\#x27;s Desertification Control Showcased at UN COP17](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/16/content_30181218.html) ⭐️ ?/10

During the 17th United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification Conference of the Parties \(COP17\) held in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, a dedicated "China Corner" exhibition highlighted the country&\#x27;s successful desertification control efforts, drawing attention from officials and scientists across Africa and Central Asia. China has achieved zero net land degradation globally, maintaining a continuous 40-year dual increase in both forest coverage and timber volume while steadily reducing its desertified and sandy land. These results contributed significantly to the United Nations&\#x27; 2030 target for zero land degradation. The country achieved these milestones by integrating ecological restoration into national development strategies over more than five decades, utilizing key ecological projects such as the Third-North Shelter Forest system, grain-for-green initiatives, and desertified land enclosure and restoration. Scientific innovations, including water-retaining agents capable of absorbing over a thousand times their weight in water, drone sowing, satellite remote sensing, and the breeding of drought-tolerant species, have helped overcome the high costs and poor retention rates that typically plague arid region management.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 16, 00:26

**「Global Desertification Context」** Desertification is a trans-regional and intergenerational global ecological challenge, with the United Nations reporting that approximately 40% of the world&\#x27;s land suffers from some degree of degradation. This degradation affects nearly half of the global population and results in over 870 billion US dollars in annual economic losses worldwide.

**「Economic and Ecological Synergy」** The demonstrated success of integrated models like Kubuqi Desert&\#x27;s "solar power above, crop planting below, and livestock rearing in between" shows affected communities and developing nations that ecological protection and economic livelihoods can coexist, breaking the assumption that desert control must sacrifice local development. This provides a replicable pathway for other arid and semi-arid regions balancing environmental goals with economic growth.

### [Kazakhstan to regulate e-commerce and target 18.5% retail share by 2029](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/16/content_30181219.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Kazakhstan&\#x27;s Ministry of Trade and Integration has released a catalog of prohibited and restricted goods for e-commerce platforms to strengthen regulatory compliance. E-commerce transaction volume in the country surged seven-fold from 4.76 trillion tenge in 2020 to 3.9 trillion tenge in 2025, representing 15% of total retail consumer spending. To support this growth, Kazakhstan has built 10 logistics centers and plans a $200 million, 291,000-square-meter order processing hub in Astana and Almaty. The government aims to expand e-commerce&\#x27;s share of retail to 18.5% by 2029 while deepening logistics and cross-border partnerships with Chinese firms like YTO Express and JD.com.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 16, 00:26

**「Context」** The rapid expansion of digital retail in Central Asia has prompted governments to establish formal compliance frameworks to manage online marketplaces. Kazakhstan recently formalized these rules through a banned and restricted goods catalog, aligning its e-commerce infrastructure with the nation&\#x27;s 92.9% internet penetration rate and growing digital payment ecosystem.

**「Impact」** E-commerce operators and logistics providers in Kazakhstan must adjust their product listings and supply chain operations to comply with the newly issued regulatory catalog. This policy framework is expected to shape regional cross-border trade routes as the country targets an 18.5% retail market share by 2029.

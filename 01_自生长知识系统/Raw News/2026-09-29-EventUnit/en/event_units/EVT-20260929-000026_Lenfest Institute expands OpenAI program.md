# Lenfest Institute Expands OpenAI Program

## Event Overview

On September 29, 2026, the Lenfest Institute, a Washington-based nonprofit dedicated to investigative journalism, announced an expansion of its partnership with OpenAI. This initiative provides newsroom journalists with access to advanced large language models, including GPT-4, to augment their investigative workflows. The program builds upon a previous three-year effort that has since been renewed and scaled up.

## Core Facts

- **Partnership Expansion**: The Lenfest Institute has secured expanded support from OpenAI to extend access to its AI tools to a broader range of journalists.
- **Tools Provided**: Participating journalists receive access to ChatGPT Plus, GitHub Copilot, and DALL-E 3.
- **Program History**: This expansion follows a pilot program launched in July 2023, which originally provided similar access to ten selected journalists.
- **Financial Details**: The partnership was initially funded with a $1 million grant. The new round of funding is reported to be an additional $1 million.
- **Leadership Statement**: Lenfest Institute president John Ydstie stated, "We are thrilled to be able to offer more journalists the opportunity to leverage the power of generative AI for their investigations."
- **Strategic Focus**: According to Ydstie, the goal is to use AI as a tool to make investigative journalism faster and more impactful.

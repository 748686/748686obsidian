### [Chinese universities issue AI tokens and practical-life courses to balance tech and labor education](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202611/26/content_30193241.html) ⭐️ ?/10

In late 2026, several Chinese universities began distributing new digital resources to students: Peking University, Tsinghua University, Tongji University, and Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications issued AI "red envelopes" such as tokens and computing-power vouchers, while Nankai University launched a new general-education elective in which cafeteria workers teach dough kneading and steam-bun making and maintenance staff teach pipe installation. The People&\#x27;s Daily commentary frames these moves as complementary responses to the same question about what abilities universities should cultivate, arguing that giving students token quotas supplies both trial-and-error room and better questioning practice, whereas hands-on life courses preserve the formative value of manual labor even as AI speeds up research workflows. To prevent overreliance on generative AI, institutions are pairing access with governance: Tsinghua has published AI education-application guidelines covering key teaching and research scenarios, and Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications requires students to disclose where and how extensively AI is used in assignments, lab reports, and theses. Proponents say the combined approach aims to develop a resilience in young people that comes from balancing fast, model-driven work with slow, embodied skill practice.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Nov 26, 00:26

**「Background」** Large language models and campus AI platforms have become widely available in Chinese higher education, shifting how students access information and complete routine tasks. At the same time, labor education has been promoted in China&\#x27;s university curriculum as a way to maintain practical life skills and character formation alongside technical training.

**「Impact」** The policies mainly affect undergraduate students at Peking University, Tsinghua University, Tongji University, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, and Nankai University by changing how they obtain AI compute and which life-skill courses are available. If other universities adopt similar token distribution and labor-education electives, related standards for AI-use disclosure and campus support could spread across the sector.

最近，北大、清华、同济、北邮等高校给学生账户发了新型“红包”：词元（Token）、算力券等，学生们晒出额度，直呼“解锁新体验”。南开大学开了新的通识选修课，食堂师傅教蒸馒头，维修师傅教装水管。课一开出，就有学生喊“抢不上”，网友留言“后劲真大”。

一边是算力，一边是面团；一边是大模型接口，一边是食堂后厨。反差之下，其实是同一个追问——大学该教给青年什么样的本事？

AI时代答案易得，却更显提问的珍贵。高校发Token，发的不只是额度，更是试错的空间和提问的“燃料”。大学生的创造力，往往在上手后才真正被激发。有人把重复性练习和调试交给AI，将重心转移到创造性工作中；有人调用校内大模型搭起文献跟踪工具，把更多时间留给不同研究方法的对比；还有人为自己量身定制工作台，提高工作效率……工具变强了、使用更方便了，进而考验的是人有没有好想法，能不能问得准、问得深。想要“妙意生花”，心里得先有一朵花的形状。

高校发Token，却并不止于发Token。面对技术升级，如何迅速适应并找到自身价值，是每名大学生面临的课题。当许多人还在争论AI是否会削弱学生思考的能力，不少高校已在尝试给出一个“堵疏相宜”的答案：发放免费资源的同时，也给出使用规则。清华制定人工智能教育应用指导原则，覆盖教学与学术研究重点场景；北邮要求学生在作业、实验报告、论文中主动标注AI的使用场景与范围……杜绝AI滥用、减少“认知外包”，高校在引导学生把工具真正用好。

网络上能搜索到的是知识，上手做成才是生活的本事。再看南开开设的这门课：除蒸馒头、装水管外，还有心肺复苏，连手工编织、饮品调制、园艺修剪也搬进了课堂。有些技能未必能经常用，却无法“一键获得”——揉面的手感、醒面的时长，拧水管接头的力道，都是试出来的。这门课教的是小事，托住的却是大事——技术浪潮奔涌向前，劳动的形态会变、场景会换，但劳动对人的塑造不会过时。

算力带给青年科技的速度，劳育带给青年生活的经验。青年成长的“韧劲”，或许就在这一快一慢、一张一弛的平衡之间。

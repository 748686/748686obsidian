### [China Issues First National Standard for Medical Escort Services](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/28/content_30177676.html) ⭐️ ?/10

China&\#x27;s State Administration for Market Regulation has approved the national standard GB/T 47801—2026, titled Basic Requirements for Socialized Medical Accompaniment Services. This is the country&\#x27;s first standardized framework for medical escort services, addressing issues of vague service boundaries and inconsistent quality. The standard clearly defines what escorts can and cannot do: they may assist with navigation, queuing, and medication pickup, but are prohibited from interpreting results or providing medical advice. Its implementation aims to regulate a growing industry driven by aging demographics and complex healthcare systems.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Aug 28, 00:26

**「Background」** China&\#x27;s rapidly aging population, combined with the needs of pregnant women, people with disabilities, and patients traveling from other regions, has led to a surge in demand for non-medical medical assistance services. Previously, these services lacked legal definition, often blurring the line between helpful companionship and illegal medical practice.

**「Impact」** Service providers must now align operations with strict protocols for personnel training, data privacy, and safety management. This creates a barrier to entry for unregulated operators while offering patients legal recourse and clearer expectations for future hires.

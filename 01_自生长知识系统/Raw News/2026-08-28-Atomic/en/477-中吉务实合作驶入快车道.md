### [China launches 9th Five-Year legal education plan](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/28/content_30177644.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On August 27, 2026, China&\#x27;s State Council Information Office announced the launch of the Ninth Five-Year Plan for Legal Publicity and Education \(2026–2030\), a directive recently issued by the Communist Party Central Committee and the State Council. The plan establishes a structural shift from policy-driven initiatives to implementation based on the Legal Publicity and Education Law, aiming to strengthen citizens&\#x27; legal literacy and social rule-of-law faith over the next five years. Key strategies include developing precise legal education models that integrate into digital platforms and daily life, emphasizing protections for youth against issues such as fraud and cyberbullying, and mandating that internet service providers fulfill public-interest legal education responsibilities.

**「Impact」** Internet service providers and media platforms must now actively produce accessible legal content tailored to high-frequency digital scenarios, such as e-commerce and online education.

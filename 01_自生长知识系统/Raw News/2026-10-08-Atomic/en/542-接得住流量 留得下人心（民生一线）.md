### [China issues first five-year national plan for disease control](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/08/content_30184497.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On October 8, 2026, the State Disease Control and Prevention Administration and six other departments issued the Disease Control &\#x27;15th Five-Year Plan,&\#x27; the first five-year national special plan in China&\#x27;s disease control sector. Published in People&\#x27;s Daily, the plan outlines a strategy to basically build a modern disease control system with Chinese characteristics by 2030. It aims to transform governance from professional prevention to systematic social co-governance, integrate multi-disease prevention strategies, strengthen early detection and rapid response capabilities for emerging infectious diseases, and shift the focus toward preventive care through expanded vaccination services and medical-prevention integration.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 8, 00:26

**「Context」** The &\#x27;15th Five-Year Plan&\#x27; period \(2026-2030\) builds upon the &\#x27;14th Five-Year Plan&\#x27; period, during which China underwent a historic systemic restructuring of its disease control system following the COVID-19 pandemic. Key achievements during this prior period included the completion of disease control institution reforms, expansion of infectious disease monitoring to over 84,000 medical institutions, construction of biosafety level-3 laboratories in 29 provinces, and the inclusion of HPV vaccines in the national immunization program.

**「Impact」** The plan establishes binding targets for China&\#x27;s public health infrastructure, including building a ready-plus-reserve epidemiological investigation workforce of approximately 77,000 personnel and developing a smart, multi-point trigger surveillance and early warning system. It requires all secondary and above healthcare institutions to provide vaccination services and pilots the authorization of public health physicians to issue vaccine prescriptions, directly affecting healthcare delivery standards nationwide.

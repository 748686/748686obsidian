## Core Facts

### 1. The Pinglu Canal Project
*   **Status:** As of October 18, 2026, the Pinglu Canal has been in operation for one month (passed on October 16, 2026).
*   **Significance:** It is the first new canal connecting rivers to the sea built since the founding of the People's Republic of China.
*   **Specifications:** The canal is 134.2 km long, with 6.5 km of newly excavated sections. The remaining sections utilize natural waterways that have been straightened, widened, and dredged.
*   **Engineering Challenges:**
    *   **Elevation Change:** The canal covers a total water level drop of 65 meters (equivalent to a 20+ story building).
    *   **Solution:** Three tiered hubs (Mada, Qishi, and Qingnian) are arranged from upstream to downstream. These function as "stairs," using valves to lift or lower ships vertically, allowing them to move to the next water level.
    *   **Slope Construction:** The highest vertical slope reaches 188 meters with complex rock layers. The construction team developed a "reverse method" (逆作法), described as "digging a slope like a tunnel."
    *   **Digital Twin:** The three major hubs use digital twin technology to simulate excavation processes virtually.

### 2. Ecological and Social Mitigation
*   **Ecological Protection:**
    *   **Oysters:** At the estuary, over 20,000 oyster parents were collected and relocated, maintaining an 85%+ survival rate in temporary cultivation.
    *   **Wildlife:** Bridges covered with soil and native vegetation were built in the watershed section to allow wildlife passage.
    *   **Fish:** A 480-meter fish passage was constructed near the Qingnian Hub; over 10 fish species have been monitored successfully migrating.
    *   **Trees:** 36 ancient trees along the canal were carefully transplanted and protected, with a 5-year tracking and maintenance plan.
*   **Social Impact:**
    *   Residents in the new canal city areas retain connections to ancestral lands (e.g., local resident Ling Changhe noted the emotional value of seeing old trees in new neighborhoods).

### 3. Economic and Strategic Impact
*   **Logistics Efficiency:**
    *   Previously, goods from Southwest China often routed via the Xijiang River to Guangzhou for sea export.
    *   The Pinglu Canal shortens the inland river voyage to the sea by approximately **560 kilometers**, reducing logistics costs.
    *   Guangxi is transitioning from a logistical "peripheral shop" to a key hub for imports/exports to ASEAN.
*   **Industry Growth:**
    *   Beihai Port New Materials Company utilized the canal to transport 500-ton stainless steel plates from Tieshangang to Hengzhou for shipbuilding.
    *   In Liangshan County, Shandong, the Neihe Port Liangshan Port has created jobs; residents like Wang Qinghua now earn over 6,000 RMB/month as port drivers, reducing the need for migrant work.
*   **National Vision:** By 2035, China aims to complete a roughly **25,000 km** "Four Verticals, Four Horizontals, Two Networks" national high-grade waterway system.

### 4. Historical and Cultural Context
*   **Historical Precedents:**
    *   **Lingqu Canal:** Connects the Xiang and Li rivers; uses a "plover's beak" (Huazui) structure to split flow "three parts to Li, seven parts to Xiang." Described by Guo Moruo as comparable to the Great Wall.
    *   **Dai Village Dam:** Located in Shandong, called the "Heart of the Canal," it splits Dawen River water to ensure flow for the Grand Canal.
    *   **Guo Shoujing:** Yuan Dynasty expert who solved Beijing's water shortage by leading water from Baifu Spring to Tonghui River.
*   **Global Connectivity:**
    *   The Grand Canal has historically facilitated exchange (e.g., Marco Polo, Choi Pu's *Diary of Drifting Sea*).
    *   The East Zhejiang Canal connects to Ningbo Port, a starting point of the Maritime Silk Road.

### 5. Technological Innovations (Pinglu Canal Specifics)
*   **Water Saving:** A首创 (first-of-its-kind) three-tier superimposed water-saving pool saves approximately **1 billion cubic meters** of water annually (60% water-saving rate).
*   **Hydraulic Technology:** A "labyrinth-type buffer sleeve" hydraulic opening/closing technology increases valve speed to 2–4 times that of conventional locks.
*   **Smart Management:** The "Digital Brain" connects over 20,000 sensing devices. The Guangxi Water Transport River-Sea Integration Smart Dispatch Platform (launched August 25, 2026) has registered 21,900 users and processed 37,000 vessel lock dispatches.

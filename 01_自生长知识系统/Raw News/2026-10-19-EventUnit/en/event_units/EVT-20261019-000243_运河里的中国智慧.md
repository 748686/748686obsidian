# Event Name
Water Management Wisdom and Industrial Transformation in China

## Event Overview
On October 18, 2026, *People's Daily* (page 04) published articles reflecting on contemporary Chinese development strategies. The primary event synthesis focuses on the construction of the Pinglu Canal, described as a showcase of "Chinese wisdom" in water management, overall planning, and precision engineering. Concurrently, the same edition featured an analysis of ecological and industrial transformation in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, where "desolate grasslands" have been converted into "smart computing hotspots." This synthesis highlights two distinct manifestations of China's push for high-quality development: large-scale infrastructure engineering and green industrial transition.

## Core Facts

### The Pinglu Canal Project
*   **Subject:** Construction of the Pinglu Canal.
*   **Thematic Focus:** The project is presented as a demonstration of three core principles:
    1.  **Wisdom of Water Management:** Understanding and utilizing hydrological systems.
    2.  **Method of Overall Planning:** Coordinated strategic layout.
    3.  **Art of Precision Engineering:** Technical excellence in construction.
*   **Source Attribution:** Explicitly cited in the *People's Daily* table of contents as the featured article for page 04, under the headline "Chinese Wisdom in the Canal."

### Ulanqab Ecological and Industrial Transformation
*   **Location:** Ulanqab City, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region.
*   **Leadership Context:** On October 16, 2026, General Secretary Xi Jinping inspected Ulanqab, visiting the Huangqi Lake Nature Reserve and the Chahar High-Tech Industrial Development Zone.
*   **Key Directives:**
    *   Coordinate high-quality development with high-level protection.
    *   Unswervingly follow the path of ecological priority and green development.
*   **Observed Outcomes:**
    *   **Ecological Recovery:** Huangqi Lake has restored its former appearance through governance.
    *   **Industry Growth:** Green computing power industry is growing rapidly; the region is branded as the "Grassland Cloud Valley" and the "Capital of Tokens."
    *   **Industrial Renewal:** Traditional iron alloy industries, formerly characterized as high-pollution and high-energy-consuming ("black"), have transitioned to green operations.
*   **Analytical Framework (People's Forum):**
    *   Green development is framed as a "must-answer question" rather than an elective one.
    *   The生态环境法典 (Ecological Environment Code), implemented on August 15, 2026, includes a specific section on green and low-carbon development to legally support this transition.
    *   The concept of "New Quality Productive Forces" is linked to green productivity ("new quality productive forces are inherently green productive forces").

### Regional Parallels in "Adapting to Local Conditions"
*   The article contrasts resource-endowed differences across China to illustrate the methodology of "developing according to local conditions" (因地制宜):
    *   **Ulanqab:** Leverages cool climate (4.3°C average annual temperature) for natural data center cooling and abundant wind/solar power.
    *   **Mohe, Heilongjiang:** Utilizes extreme cold for寒地测试 (cold-weather testing) industries.
    *   **Zhongwei, Ningxia:** Uses abundant green power and vast desert land to build a "green computing capital."
    *   **Shangrao, Jiangxi:** Uses good ecology to attract the "migratory bird economy."
    *   **Yibin, Sichuan:** Uses green electricity to transform from a "liquor capital" to a "lithium capital."
    *   **Lop Nur (Luobu), Xinjiang:** Implements "photovoltaic + agriculture" to achieve both greening and wealth creation.

# Event Name
Transformation of Ulanqab from a "Barren Grassland" to a "Smart Computing Hotspot"

## Event Overview
On October 16, 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping conducted field research in Ulanqab City, Inner Mongolia. The visit highlighted Ulanqab's successful economic transformation from a region with limited industrial options—historically characterized as a "barren grassland"—into a major global hub for artificial intelligence computing power. The central theme of the observations was the application of the principle of "seeking truth from facts" and adapting measures to local conditions (因地制宜) to develop new quality productive forces.

Xi Jinping emphasized that the region must follow contemporary trends, seize development opportunities, and leverage its comparative advantages. This transformation is framed not just as a local success story but as a model for integrated regional development and the national strategy of coordinating regional growth across China.

## Core Facts
*   **Event Date:** October 16, 2026.
*   **Location:** Ulanqab City, Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, China.
*   **Key Official Action:** President Xi Jinping visited the region to inspect its development progress.
*   **Current Status (as of August 2026):**
    *   Signed computing power projects: 109.
    *   Signed standard server racks: Over 7 million.
    *   Operational computing power scale: 190,000 PFLOPS (petaflops).
*   **Data Connectivity:** The latency between Ulanqab and Beijing is approximately 2.1 milliseconds via high-capacity optical fibers, covering a distance of over 240 kilometers.
*   **Global Assessment:** Goldman Sachs identified Ulanqab as one of the largest and fastest-growing AI computing hubs in Asia-Pacific.
*   **Strategic Positioning:** Ulanqab serves as a key node in China’s "Eastern Data, Western Computing" (东数西算) project, specifically targeting low-latency computing services to serve the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region.
*   **Policy Context:** The findings align with the recently issued "Opinions of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council on Developing New Quality Productive Forces," which advocates for a pragmatic approach of "establishing the new before breaking the old."

## Event Conclusion
Ulanqab's transformation from a "barren grassland" to a "smart computing hotspot" represents a concrete application of China's national strategies for regional coordination and the development of new quality productive forces. During his October 16, 2026 inspection, President Xi Jinping underscored that this success stems from leveraging local comparative advantages—specifically climate and proximity to Beijing—within the "East Data, West Computing" framework. The region now hosts significant AI computing infrastructure, recognized globally by firms like Goldman Sachs as a key Asia-Pacific hub. This case study is utilized by state media to advocate for a broader national policy: regions should identify their unique strengths (whether digital, agricultural, or ecological) to contribute to high-quality, integrated national development.

# EVT-20261019-000026: Investigation into Fire at Samsung Electronics Facility in Bucheon, South Korea

## Event Overview
On October 19, 2026, a fatal fire occurred at the Bucheon factory of Samsung Electronics Co. (also referred to as "Samsung Electro-Mechanics" in some contexts, though officially designated as "Samsung Electronics") in Gyeonggi-do, South Korea. The incident resulted in one death and required extensive rescue operations involving over 300 firefighters. Police and fire officials have launched a comprehensive investigation to determine the cause of the blaze.

## Core Facts
*   **Date:** October 19, 2026 (Fire extinguished by approximately 12:48 PM local time; explosion occurred around 1:30 PM local time).
*   **Location:** Samsung Electronics factory in Bucheon, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea. The facility is located on an industrial complex, approximately 40 kilometers southwest of Seoul.
*   **Casualties:** One person confirmed dead (a worker). Approximately 60 people were treated for symptoms such as dizziness and breathing difficulties, with four patients admitted to the hospital.
*   **Rescue Response:** Over 300 firefighters and more than 60 fire trucks were deployed. Two rescue helicopters and five drones were utilized to monitor the fire from the air.
*   **Explosion:** A secondary explosion occurred inside the factory around 1:30 PM local time.
*   **Cause:** Under investigation. Authorities are examining potential causes including electrical fires and hazardous substances. A gas cylinder explosion theory has been ruled out by officials.
*   **Impact:** No impact on Samsung’s OLED panel supply chain. Operations at the facility were suspended, and evacuations were conducted at nearby kindergartens and elementary schools.

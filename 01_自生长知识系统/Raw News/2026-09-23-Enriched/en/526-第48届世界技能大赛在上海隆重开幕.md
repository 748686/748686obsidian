### [August 2026 anti-corruption enforcement figures published](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/23/content_30182628.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 22, 2026, China&\#x27;s Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and National Supervisory Commission published August statistics on enforcement of the Central Eight-Point Regulation. Authorities investigated 29,171 violations nationwide in August, resulting in criticism and education or other handling of 35,901 people, including disciplinary and administrative sanctions against 24,494 individuals. Formalism and bureaucracy issues accounted for 15,431 cases, with dereliction affecting high-quality development being the largest category at 12,971 cases. Hedonism and extravagance violations totaled 13,740 cases, where illicit gift and specialty receipt was the most common issue at 8,603 cases. Enforcement involved 117 department-level officials, 1,568 county-level officials, and 27,486 township-level and below officials.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 23, 00:26

**「Background」** The Central Eight-Point Regulation, introduced in 2012, sets standards for official conduct to combat waste, privilege, and bureaucratic excess in the Chinese Communist Party and government institutions. The Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and National Supervisory Commission routinely publishes monthly violation statistics to demonstrate accountability enforcement and deter corruption.

**「Impact」** Government officials, party members, and state institution employees are subject to disciplinary review for compliance failures, with August data showing the majority of enforcement actions targeting grassroots cadres at the township level and below.

On September 22, the State Council Information Office held a press conference to introduce measures for the safe, efficient, and sustainable use of natural resources during the 15th Five-Year Plan period. The Ministry of Natural Resources announced that spatial planning will prioritize 2,800+ major projects through a unified planning map, while shifting focus from incremental land expansion to盘活 \(revitalizing\) existing stock land. Key targets include achieving a national forest coverage rate of 25.8% and forest stock volume of 22.4 billion cubic meters by 2030. Additionally, the耕地保护和质量提升法 \(Farmland Protection and Quality Improvement Law\), China&\#x27;s first specialized legislation in this field, will take effect on January 1, 2027.

**「Background」** The 15th Five-Year Plan \(2026-2030\) represents China&\#x27;s next major national development blueprint, with natural resource security serving as a foundational element for sustainable economic growth. China has been transitioning from extensive land-use models toward more efficient spatial governance, emphasizing integrated protection of mountains, rivers, forests, farmlands, deserts, and grasslands as part of its ecological civilization strategy.

**「Impact」** The new land-use prioritization and streamlined approval processes will accelerate implementation of major national infrastructure projects, while the shift toward存量土地盘活 \(stock land revitalization\) constrains urban sprawl and redirects development toward already-utilized areas. The forthcoming farmland protection law establishes legal consequences for land misuse, directly affecting agricultural and real estate developers operating in China.

本报上海9月22日电  （记者崔寅）9月22日下午，国务院总理李强在上海会见来华出席第48届世界技能大赛的世界技能组织主席乌汉和首席执行官霍伊。

李强表示，长期以来，中国同世界技能组织保持着密切良好合作关系。举办第48届世界技能大赛是习近平主席亲自关心、亲自推动的，体现了中方对技能人才的高度重视、对全球技能合作的坚定支持。在前期筹备过程中，中方同世界技能组织开展了精诚协作。我们有信心携手向世界奉献一届精彩的技能盛会。中方愿同世界技能组织进一步深化合作，共促全球技能事业发展，推动世界经济稳健增长，增进各国人民福祉。

李强指出，中国将继续加强国内技能工作，完善技能人才培养、使用、评价、激励等机制，同时继续为全球技能事业提供有益经验和公共产品，让技能发展红利惠及更多国家。中国愿一如既往支持世界技能组织转型发展，助力世界技能组织在赛事、教育培训、学术研究、技能减贫、南南合作等各领域展现更大作为。双方要用好本届世界技能大赛成果，运营好世界技能博物馆等平台。希望世界技能组织在人才培养、师资培训等方面同中方分享更多国际经验，支持中国在技能领域全球治理中发挥更大作用。

乌汉和霍伊高度赞赏中国技能事业蓬勃发展，并为其他国家提供帮助和借鉴。相信中方将向国际社会呈现一场前所未有的世界技能大赛，激励各国人民不断开拓创新、追求卓越。期待同中方共同推进世界技能组织2035愿景规划，合力加强高素质人才队伍建设，携手为世界和平稳定与繁荣发展贡献力量。

陈吉宁、吴政隆、谌贻琴参加会见。

### 11. China's Photovoltaic Industry Milestone
- As of July 2026, photovoltaic (PV) surpassed coal power to become China's largest installed power source.
- Renewables account for over 40% of power generation.
- Timeline: PV surpassed wind (Aug 2022), surpassed hydro (Mar 2023), combined PV+wind surpassed thermal (Mar 2025), PV surpassed coal (Jul 2026).
- "Gobi Desert" bases: over 150 GW installed by end-2025.
- 46 ultra-high voltage (UHV) channels in operation, "West-to-East Power Transmission" capacity exceeding 340 GW.
- Manufacturing: China holds >70% global market share in polysilicon, wafers, cells, and modules.
- Efficiency records: LONGi 26.81% (HJT, Nov 2022), 28.29% (back-contact, Sep 2026); Trina 32.22% (tandem, Aug 2026).
- LCOE now below coal (~0.15 RMB/kWh).

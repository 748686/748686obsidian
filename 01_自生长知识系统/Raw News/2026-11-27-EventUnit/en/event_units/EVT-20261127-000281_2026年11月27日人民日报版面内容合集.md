## Event Overview
On November 27, 2026, *People's Daily* (人民日报) published a 20-page edition covering topics across health, rural development, legal supervision, and cultural commentary. The edition includes a commentary on the boundaries between AI medical consultation and professional diagnosis, a report on Zhejiang's "smart disease control" (shuzhi difang) system for infectious disease monitoring, an observation on rural market revitalization in Tianjin, and a series of cultural reviews on library innovation and book publishing. Additionally, the edition reports on the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (NPC) reviewing the implementation of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau Ecological Protection Law and features a "People's Daily Comment" on leveraging sports consumption for urban economic growth.

## Core Facts
*   **Publication Date:** November 27, 2026.
*   **Health Sector (Page 17):** *People's Daily* published an editorial titled "Don't Treat 'Doubao' as a Doctor," noting that daily health consultations for the AI model "Doubao" exceed 20 million times. The article argues that while AI is a useful tool, it cannot replace the ultimate decision-making responsibility of qualified doctors.
*   **Public Health Innovation (Page 17):** A report detailed Zhejiang's "smart disease control" system, which uses AI to monitor infectious disease risks. The system achieved 100% deployment of pre-software in Zhejiang's 407 secondary-level or higher medical institutions and 1,391 primary-level medical institutions. Since its launch, the provincial platform has generated 3,456 precise warnings for infectious disease outbreaks in schools and elderly care facilities.
*   **Legal Supervision (Page 6):** On November 26, 2026, the NPC Standing Committee's enforcement inspection team for the *Qinghai-Tibet Plateau Ecological Protection Law* held its second plenary meeting to discuss the draft inspection report. Vice-Chairman Wang Dongming emphasized transforming the legal system's advantages into ecological protection efficacy.
*   **Rural Economy (Page 18):** An observation reported that the "Lutai Grand Fair" in Ninghe District, Tianjin, has evolved into a "comprehensive service platform." Since 2025, the fair has received over 5.5 million tourists, directly creating employment for over 1,000 residents and driving total comprehensive revenue exceeding 100 million RMB.
*   **Sports Economy (Page 5):** A commentary noted that the 2026 Chinese Super League (CSL) concluded with cumulative attendance exceeding 7 million spectators, a league record. The 2025 total national sports industry scale was 5.12 trillion RMB, with projections to exceed 7 trillion RMB by 2030.

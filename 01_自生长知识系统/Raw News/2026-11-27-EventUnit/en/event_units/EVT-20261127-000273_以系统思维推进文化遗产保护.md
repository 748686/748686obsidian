# 蔡赴朝 and Li Wei Expelled from the CCP for Serious Discipline Violations

## Event Overview

On November 26, 2026, the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI) and the National Supervisory Commission (NSC) of China completed their investigations into two former high-ranking officials: Cai Fuchao and Li Wei. Following approval by the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC), both officials were expelled from the party for serious violations of discipline and law. Cai Fuchao, a former Vice Minister of the Central Publicity Department and former Party Secretary/Director of the National Press and Publication Administration, was expelled from the party for bribery and corruption. Li Wei, a former Standing Member of the Jiangxi Provincial Standing Committee and former Director of the United Front Work Department, was expelled from both the party and the state civil service for similar offenses. The decisions were published in the *People's Daily* on November 27, 2026.

## Core Facts

*   **Cai Fuchao's Disposition:**
    *   **Former Roles:** Vice Minister of the Central Publicity Department; Party Secretary and Director of the National Press and Publication Administration (NPPA).
    *   **Decision:** Expelled from the Communist Party of China (CPC).
    *   **Violations:**
        *   Lost ideals and beliefs; opposed organizational review.
        *   Violated Central Eight-point Regulations (illegal receipt of gifts, bribes, and acceptance of banquets/tour arrangements).
        *   Abused power in employee recruitment and engaged in profit-making activities.
        *   **Corruption/Bribery:** Used his position to facilitate approvals and sales of film/TV works, acquisition of advertising operation rights, and tourism project development in exchange for huge sums of money.
        *   **Status:** Violations continued after the 18th CPC National Congress.
    *   **Outcome:** Illegal gains confiscated; criminal issues transferred to procuratorial organs for investigation and prosecution.

*   **Li Wei's Disposition:**
    *   **Former Roles:** Standing Member of the Jiangxi Provincial Standing Committee; Director of the United Front Work Department.
    *   **Decision:** Expelled from the CPC; dismissed from public office (by the National Supervisory Commission); his status as a delegate to the 20th CPC National Congress was terminated.
    *   **Violations:**
        *   Lost ideals and beliefs; opposed organizational review.
        *   Violated Central Eight-point Regulations (accepted banquets affecting official duties).
        *   Violated organizational principles (failed to report personal matters; abused power in employee recruitment).
        *   **Corruption/Bribery:** Used public power to benefit others in corporate mergers/acquisitions and project contracting in exchange for huge sums of money.
        *   **Status:** Violations continued after the 18th CPC National Congress.
    *   **Outcome:** Illegal gains confiscated; criminal issues transferred to procuratorial organs for investigation and prosecution.

*   **Administrative Context:**
    *   Both cases were investigated by the CCDI and NSC with approval from the CPC Central Committee.
    *   The specific date of the news report was November 26, 2026 (Beijing), published on November 27, 2026.

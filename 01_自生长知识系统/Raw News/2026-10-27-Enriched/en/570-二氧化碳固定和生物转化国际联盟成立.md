### [Terracotta Army makers likely included women and youths](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/27/content_30187838.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Researchers from the Museum of the Qin Terracotta Army and Mausoleum in Xi&\#x27;an and the School of Archaeology at Shandong University have published a study in The International Journal of Cultural Heritage indicating that women and adolescents may have participated in producing the terracotta warriors. The team extracted 79 fingerprint impressions from 23 pottery figures in Pit 1, square T23, and applied a recently proposed Bayesian mixed fingerprint model to estimate age and sex composition. Results show adult males accounted for about 45.0% of identifiable prints, adult females about 32.7%, and individuals under age 15 about 22.3%, providing quantitative biological evidence that production was male-dominated but not exclusively male.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 27, 00:26

**「Background」** 指纹识别技术通常用于法医学和生物识别，近年来被引入考古领域以分析古代工匠的生理特征。贝叶斯混合模型是一种统计方法，可用于从混合来源的生物样本中推断群体构成，此研究将其应用于指纹以估计不同年龄和性别的贡献比例。秦兵马俑是秦始皇陵的一部分，其建造者的身份和构成长期是学术研究的课题。

**「Impact」** 该研究为理解秦代手工业劳动力的组织结构提供了新的量化证据，可能影响对秦朝社会、劳动制度及工匠身份的学术讨论。

### [Huainan retains tourism流量 through refined services](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/27/content_30187839.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Huainan, Anhui province, is converting viral attention into sustained tourism growth by embedding practical details into its cultural experiences. After a People&\#x27;s Park stage designed as an unrolled sheet of Xuan paper attracted early visitors, the city expanded its offerings with light shows, intangible cultural heritage performances, and all-day activity routes in Shou County. The city also introduced ticket-receipt discounts, volunteer assistance, and six free bus lines connecting transit hubs to key sites. These efforts contributed to more than 4.3 million visitors during the recent National Day holiday, a year-on-year increase of over 25 percent, and tourism revenue exceeding 2.6 billion yuan, up more than 30 percent. The Huainan Culture and Tourism Bureau said the results reflect coordinated planning that links 17打卡 spots across six thematic sectors.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 27, 00:26

**「Background」** Chinese cities have increasingly relied on visually distinctive cultural projects and short-video exposure to attract domestic tourists. Huainan’s push builds on local assets such as Shou County’s historic walls, eight-family mountain ecology, Huai River old streets, and regional food and intangible cultural heritage traditions.

**「Impact」** Huainan’s model shows how targeted service details can extend the lifespan of viral tourism moments and boost local hospitality revenue. If comparable cities adopt similar coordination, visitor expectations for seamless, detail-driven experiences are likely to rise across China’s domestic tourism market.

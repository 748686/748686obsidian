# Event: Elderly Model Team in Nanmu Village, Chongqing

## Event Overview
In Nanmu Village (楠木村), Shuanglu Subdistrict, Fengdu County, Chongqing Municipality, an elderly fashion model team has been formed. The team, consisting of eight residents, participates in runway walks to promote locally handcrafted goods, providing the elderly with social engagement, a sense of purpose, and supplemental income. The initiative was driven by Tao Xuemei, the village's first secretary, in collaboration with graduate students from China University of Geosciences. The story was reported by the *People's Daily* on October 9, 2026.

## Core Facts
- **Location:** Nanmu Village, Shuanglu Subdistrict, Fengdu County, Chongqing Municipality, China.
- **Team Composition:** 8 elderly residents (1 male, 7 females).
- **Demographics:** Average age is nearly 80. The eldest member is Qin Datian (秦大田), aged 92. The youngest member is Jiang Fangzhen (江方珍), aged 61. Most members are widowed and live alone; young people in the village typically migrate for work and return only for holidays.
- **Key Figures:**
    - **Tao Xuemei (陶学梅):** First Secretary of Nanmu Village, who initiated the team.
    - **Tao Hongxu (陶泓旭):** A graduate student from China University of Geosciences and member of a social practice team that visited the village in 2025.
    - **Ran Guangsu (冉光素):** An 85-year-old team member.
    - **Luo Shiqiong (罗世琼):** A 75-year-old team member.
    - **Tan Xingmei (谭兴美):** A 75-year-old team member.
- **Activities:** The team rehearses in the village committee courtyard on a chalk-drawn "T-stage." They wear uniforms (white shirts, hats) and practice runway walks. They also receive makeup assistance.
- **Economic Model:** The team serves as "spokespersons" for handcrafted编织 bags and shoes designed by university students and woven by villagers. A "sponsorship fee" (代言费) of 5–10 RMB per sold item is recorded in the collective account and distributed equally among the elderly members at year-end.
- **Timeline:**
    - **July 2022:** Tao Xuemei arrived in Nanmu Village.
    - **2025:** China University of Geosciences graduate team conducted social practice in the village.
    - **August 2025:** The elderly team performed their first runway walk at a village成果展 (achievements exhibition).
    - **October 9, 2026:** *People's Daily* published the feature story.

# Event Name

**AI Interpretation of Chinese Traditional Thought and the Construction of Value-Driven General Artificial Intelligence**

## Event Overview

On October 9, 2026, Zhu Songchun, a professor at Peking University and Tsinghua University and President of the Beijing General Artificial Intelligence Institute, published an article titled "Giving Reason to Humanities, Exploring Civilizational Evolution in the Intelligent Age" in *People's Daily* (Page 17: International Supplement). In this piece, Zhu introduces his recent book series, *Giving Reason to Humanities* and *Setting Heart for Machines*, which proposes using AI mathematical models to construct rigorous logical systems for traditional Chinese thought. He outlines a U/V analytical framework to address the tension between Eastern and Western value systems and discusses the development of general AI agents and social simulators to explore civilizational coexistence in the intelligent age.

## Core Facts

*   **Author and Publication:** The content originates from an opinion/article by Zhu Songchun published in *People's Daily* on October 9, 2026.
*   **Core Thesis:** Zhu argues that while Chinese civilization boasts macro-confidence, it suffers from "micro-deprecation" because traditional concepts (Yin-Yang, Bagua, Mind-Nature studies) lack a strict mathematical system, often being dismissed as superstition in modern contexts. He posits that General Artificial Intelligence (AGI) research offers a "scientific language" to re-express these thoughts.
*   **The Book Series:**
    *   *Setting Heart for Machines*: Aims to endow AI with complete cognitive architectures and value systems, moving AI from "data-driven" to "value-driven."
    *   *Giving Reason to Humanities*: Uses AI mathematical models to interpret Chinese traditions, aiming to transform Chinese thought into productive forces for the intelligent age.
*   **U/V Framework:**
    *   **U System (Utility):** Represents "Li" (Reason/Principle), covering natural science models and social science ethical norms.
    *   **V System (Value):** Represents "Xin" (Heart/Mind), covering value judgments of individuals, others, collectives, states, and humanity.
    *   **Civilizational Analysis:** Zhu suggests that modern China adopted Western U systems (science/tech) but retained its own V system. The current tension between East and West is fundamentally a collision of V systems, not U systems. He cites Liang Shuming’s view that Western science addresses man-nature relations, while Chinese thought addresses man-man relations.
*   **Technological Implementations:**
    *   **Social Simulator:** Zhu’s team at the Wuhan Artificial Intelligence Research Institute (Peking University) built the world’s first large-scale social simulator to model multi-agent interactions and civilizational evolution.
    *   **"Tongtong" Agent:** Based on a "Cognition-Ability-Value" architecture, this is the world’s first general AI agent. After three iterations over two years, its mental level is equivalent to a preschooler. It has progressed through social environments ranging from a "small room" to a "small town," evolving from self-value drive to collective value recognition.
*   **Governance Implications:** Zhu advocates for embedding respect for digital sovereignty and local values into AI infrastructure. He opposes unilateral rule-making and "technological colonialism," promoting a "harmonious yet diverse" (*he er bu tong*) approach to global AI governance.

## Event Conclusion

This event marks a significant public articulation of a "computational humanities" approach by a leading figure in Chinese AI research. Zhu Songchun attempts to resolve the perceived deficit in Chinese traditional thought's modern expression by mapping it onto the architecture of General Artificial Intelligence. The core contribution is the U/V framework, which categorizes technological utility separately from civilizational values, arguing that while the West may lead in U, China possesses a distinct and vital V-system rooted in secular ethical evolution. The initiative highlights a strategic direction in China's AI development: prioritizing "value-driven" AGI that incorporates indigenous philosophical frameworks, distinct from the purely capability-focused approaches prevalent in other global AI narratives.

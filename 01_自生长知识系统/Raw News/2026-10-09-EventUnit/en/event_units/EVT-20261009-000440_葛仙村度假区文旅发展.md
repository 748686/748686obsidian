## Event Overview

This event concerns the development and economic impact of the Gexian Village Resort (葛仙村度假区) located in Yanshan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province. The resort, situated at the northern foot of the Wuyi Mountains, utilizes immersive night tourism projects—specifically the "Thousand Lights Splendid Realm" (千灯盛境)—and cultural performances to drive regional economic growth. The primary source reports on the resort's operational metrics, cultural content strategy, and its tangible benefit to surrounding rural communities, specifically highlighting increased incomes for local villagers through homestay and agricultural tourism businesses.

## Core Facts

*   **Location and Scale:** Gexian Village Resort is located in Yanshan County, Shangrao, Jiangxi, at the northern foot of the Wuyi Mountains. It opened in May 2020.
*   **Key Attractions:** The resort features the "Thousand Lights Splendid Realm" immersive night tour, large-scale real-scene performances titled *Encounter · Yanshan* (《遇见·铅山》), and intangible cultural heritage experiences.
*   **Tourism Metrics (Resort Level):**
    *   Overnight游客 (tourists staying overnight) account for over 20% of visitors.
    *   Average length of stay exceeds 1.5 days.
    *   The resort has been selected for the third batch of National Night Culture and Tourism Consumption Aggregation Zones.
*   **Infrastructure and Services:**
    *   Fleet of 38 sightseeing buses available on demand.
    *   Approximately 3,700 parking spaces for self-driving tourists.
    *   Free托管 (custody/daycare) services for families with children.
    *   "One shop, one product, one story" operational philosophy for merchant tenants.
*   **Cultural Content:**
    *   The performance *Encounter · Yanshan* uses a time-travel narrative following a youth named "A-Xun" to depict local history, including academic debates between Zhu Xi and Lu Jiuyuan, the patriotic sentiments of Xin Qiji, and the trade history of Hekou Ancient Town.
*   **Local Economic Impact (Village Level):**
    *   In Xiangyuan Village (Gexian Town), specifically the Jiayapai Group and Gexian Source New Village, approximately 120 homestays have been established.
    *   Over 90% of these homestays are operated autonomously by villagers.
    *   Annual output value of these homestays is approximately 24 million RMB.
    *   Direct employment generated: over 500 people.
    *   Case Study: Wang Xiwang (王细旺), a villager from Xiangyuan, returned from migrant work to operate a farmhouse restaurant (nongjiale). His family's net income exceeded 300,000 RMB in 2025.
*   **County-Level Impact (Yanshan County):**
    *   2025 Tourist Volume: 15.9 million人次.
    *   2025 Total Tourism Revenue: 11.9 billion RMB.
    *   Strategy: Promotion of a "Homestay + Characteristic Industry" model, integrating intangible cultural heritage research and folk experience products.

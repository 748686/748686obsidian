## Event Overview

Following the initial incident, legal proceedings began in Tokyo, and on October 8, 2026, the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) reiterated demands for strict punishment of the perpetrator, linking the handling of the case to Japan’s international credibility and compliance with international law.

*   **Location:** Chinese Embassy in Tokyo, Japan.
*   **Perpetrator:** Akira Murata (村田晃大), identified as a current/active-duty officer of the Japan Self-Defense Forces (SDF).

# Event Name
**USTC Talent Cultivation and Integrated Education-Science-Talent Reform** (Event Date: 2026-10-09)

## Event Overview
On October 9, 2026, *People's Daily* (Page 14, Education Section) published a coordinated series of reports highlighting the educational achievements, talent cultivation model, and reform initiatives of the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). The reporting focuses on USTC's history as a "red university" founded for the "Two Bombs, One Satellite" program, its recent high-level scientific achievements, and its strategic shift toward an integrated development model of education, science and technology, and talent. The coverage includes a main reportage piece, an editorial/op-ed by USTC President Chang Jin, and a personal reflection by 2025 National Highest Science and Technology Award recipient Academician Chen Liquan.

## Core Facts

**1. High-Level Talent Outcomes**
*   In July 2026, Chen Liquan, a 1959 alumnus of USTC’s Department of Technical Physics (Class 5902), was awarded the 2025 National Highest Science and Technology Award ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   Class 5902 has produced two recipients of the National Highest Science and Technology Award: Chen Liquan and Academician Zhao Zhongxian ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   A total of 12 alumni from USTC’s Class of 1959 have been elected as academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences or the Chinese Academy of Engineering ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   Chen Liquan is currently a researcher at the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and cited his USTC schooling as foundational to his research career ([ARTICLE #586](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184704.html)).

**2. Talent Cultivation Model ("Sci-Edu Integration 3.0")**
*   USTC is developing a "Sci-Edu Integration 3.0" system, linking the university with CAS institutes and large-scale scientific装置 (facilities) ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   The **Yan Jici Physics Science Talent Class** is highlighted as a model for cultivating top innovative talents through small-class teaching and open experimental platforms. Alumni include Deng Yuhao, a member of the "Jiuzhang" quantum computing team ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   USTC offers **100% major choice autonomy** for undergraduates, a policy maintained for over 20 years. Students select majors after their first year (college-wide) or second year (within college) ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   The curriculum emphasizes a strong foundation in mathematics and physics for the first three years, followed by immersive research internships at CAS institutes (specifically noted in Chen Liquan’s recollection of the Technical Physics Department) ([ARTICLE #586](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184704.html)).

**3. Scientific Achievements and National Strategy Alignment**
*   **Quantum Technology:** USTC is a pioneer in quantum communications and computing. Key achievements cited include the "Mozi" satellite, the "Jiuzhang" and "Zuchongzhi" quantum computers, and the Quantum Communication Beijing-Shanghai Trunk Line. Tang Shibiao, a 2025 "Annual Great Country Craftsman" award winner, contributed to these efforts while working at Guodun Quantum ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   **Deep Space & Nuclear:** USTC is involved in lunar and Martian exploration, the construction of a Deep Space Exploration Laboratory, and full-superconducting tokamak experimental device research ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   **Infrastructure:** The Hefei Advanced Light (HAL) project is being accelerated as a major scientific infrastructure ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).

**4. Institutional Reform and Policy Context**
*   In December 2025, the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) announced support for USTC to build a **Demonstration Zone for the Integrated Development of Education, Science, and Talent** ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   USTC President Chang Jin authored an op-ed outlining the strategy to build this demonstration zone, emphasizing the "Six-Have" student profile (idealistic, pursuing, responsible, capable,高品质, cultivated) and the integration of teaching and research ([ARTICLE #585](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184703.html)).
*   President Xi Jinping visited USTC in April 2016, encouraging the university to "dare to innovate, dare to surpass, and strive for first-class status" ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).
*   USTC School Party Secretary Shu Gequn and Dean of Academic Affairs Wu Xiaojun were interviewed regarding specific curriculum and reform details ([ARTICLE #584](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/09/content_30184702.html)).

# Event Name
Implementation of China's First National Standard for AI-Human Customer Service Coordination (GB/T 47746—2026)

## Event Overview
On September 1, 2026, China implemented its first national standard specifically focused on the coordination between human and intelligent customer service, titled *Customer Contact Service: Requirements for Coordination between Human and Intelligent Customer Service* (GB/T 47746—2026). The implementation of this standard is designed to address widespread consumer complaints regarding AI customer service systems, specifically issues such as failing to provide access to human agents, repetitive loops, and misaligned responses. The standard establishes clear regulatory frameworks for service division, data synchronization, and transition protocols between AI and human agents to enhance consumer protection and service quality.

## Core Facts
*   **Standard Identification**: The specific standard is **GB/T 47746—2026**, titled *Customer Contact Service: Requirements for Coordination between Human and Intelligent Customer Service*.
*   **Effective Date**: The standard came into force on **September 1, 2026**.
*   **Primary Objective**: To standardize the coordination processes between human and AI customer service, specifically addressing:
    *   Arbitrary task allocation.
    *   Disconnections during service switching.
    *   Poor data synchronization.
    *   Inconsistent service quality.
*   **Key Requirements**:
    *   Clear, accessible entry points for consumers to reach human agents when required.
    *   Scientific division of labor: Simple queries handled by AI; complex disputes escalated to humans.
    *   Seamless handover protocols preventing consumers from having to repeat information.
    *   Mandatory pre-deployment training and sandbox testing for AI systems to ensure adherence to business rules and prevention of unauthorized promises.
    *   Operators cannot disclaim liability for AI-generated content or claims by stating "AI responses do not represent company policy."
*   **Contextual Data**: According to the *2026 First Half National Consumer Association Organized Complaint Analysis* released by the China Consumer Association, approximately **50%** (1 in 2) of complaints received are related to services, with AI customer service identified as a major source of consumer frustration.

## Event Conclusion
The implementation of GB/T 47746—2026 on September 1, 2026, marks a significant regulatory milestone in China's approach to AI customer service. By mandating clear human-AI handover protocols, rigorous pre-deployment testing, and shared liability between operators and their AI agents, the standard directly addresses the systemic failures in service accessibility and quality that have fueled consumer complaints. The source indicates this move is part of a broader, layered governance framework in China that seeks to balance technological efficiency with consumer rights protection, ensuring that AI acts as a bridge for communication rather than a barrier.

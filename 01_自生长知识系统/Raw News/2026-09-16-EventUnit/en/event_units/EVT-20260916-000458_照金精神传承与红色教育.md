This event synthesis focuses on an article published in the *People's Daily* (supplement page 20) on September 16, 2026, titled "Zhaotong/Zhaojin Spirit, Zhaojin People" (照金精神照金人). Authored by Dang Yimin, the piece explores the historical legacy of the "Zhaojin Spirit" in Shaanxi Province and the contemporary development of red education in the Zhaojin region. The narrative weaves together personal family history—specifically the stories of the author's "Second Uncle" and his father's lineage—with historical accounts of the 1932 creation of the Northwest's first revolutionary base by the Red Army.     *   **1932:** The Red Army entered the Zhaojin region and established the first revolutionary base in the Northwest.

    *   **Education:**

        *   **Methodology:** Transitioned from "static display" to "dynamic inheritance," utilizing "immersive" red education models and active storytelling groups ("Malden Shining" Red Lecture Team and "Red" Children Lecture Team).

*   **Personal Narrative Elements:**
    *   **Dang Yimin's Background:** Born in the relevant region, studied at the National Defense University, and wrote the novel *The Base* (《根据地》), published in 2015. The novel was commissioned by Shaanxi Taibai Literary Press and recommended by Chen Zhongshi.
    *   **Family History:** The author's grandparents fled famine from Fuping County to Yijun County. The author's father and "Second Uncle" were born in Fuping but raised in Yijun. "Second Uncle" was adopted as an infant by a local man during the famine and later joined a passing Red Army unit, engaging in guerrilla warfare around Zhaojin. He returned to farming after the war and remained proud of his military history.

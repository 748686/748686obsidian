# Event Name
Betaine Group Leads Project Awarded Special Prize in Yunnan Provincial Science and Technology Progress Awards

## Event Overview
On September 15, 2026, Yunnan Betaine Biotechnology Group Co., Ltd. ("Betaine Group"), in collaboration with the Kunming Institute of Botany of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and other entities, received the Special Prize of the 2025 Yunnan Provincial Science and Technology Progress Awards. The award was granted for the project "R&D and Industrialization of Domestic Efficacy-Based Skincare Products," which was developed through a 12-year joint research effort between industry, academia, and research institutions. The project aims to address the scarcity of local skincare products in China that are specifically adapted to Chinese skin types and supported by complete medical clinical studies.

## Core Facts
- **Date of Award Ceremony:** September 15, 2026.
- **Awarding Body:** Yunnan Provincial Science and Technology Awards Conference.
- **Specific Award:** 2025 Yunnan Provincial Science and Technology Progress Awards - Special Prize.
- **Lead Entity:** Yunnan Betaine Biotechnology Group Co., Ltd.
- **Co-operating Entities:** Kunming Institute of Botany, Chinese Academy of Sciences; Yunnan Characteristic Plant Extraction Laboratory; and other medical institutions and research institutes.
- **Project Title:** "R&D and Industrialization of Domestic Efficacy-Based Skincare Products."
- **Development Duration:** 12 years of joint industry-academia-research (industry, university, and research institution) R&D.
- **Technical Breakthroughs:**
    - Elucidated pathogenic mechanisms of sensitive skin and chloasma.
    - Proposed a new theory for whitening.
    - Built a platform for mining Yunnan characteristic plant raw materials.
    - Developed key technologies for plant extraction, separation, purification, and formulation.
    - Established a large-sample clinical verification system and industry standards.
- **Resource Utilization:** The team screened over 2,000 plant resources and identified 266 active skincare ingredients, creating a leading domestic cosmetic raw material resource library.
- **Intellectual Property:** The project has accumulated 251 authorized patents and 83 SCI journal papers.
- **Standards:** Led the formulation of over 80 guidelines, national, and group standards related to efficacy-based skincare.
- **Economic Performance (2022-2025):** Cumulative sales of the company's products reached 21.63 billion CNY, with taxes and profits amounting to 5.875 billion CNY.
- **Market Reach:** Products cover offline pharmacies in all 31 provinces (autonomous regions, municipalities) and are present on all major e-commerce platforms. Cumulative users exceed 69.89 million.
- **Recognition:** The company's brand was selected into the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's first batch of "China Consumption Famous Products."
- **Green Supply Chain:** In 2025, 53.8% of raw material suppliers were localized.
- **Biodiversity Protection:** The company cultivates over 20 types of highland rare plants, including Yunnan peony and Dactyloriopsis, supporting the "Global Mountain Bioeconomy Framework."

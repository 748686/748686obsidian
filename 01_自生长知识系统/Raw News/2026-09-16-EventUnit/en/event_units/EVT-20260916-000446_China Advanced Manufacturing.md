## Event Overview

On September 16, 2026, the *People's Daily* (paper.people.com.cn) published a comprehensive report titled "China Advances Advanced Manufacturing to Build Modern Industrial Framework" (original title: “把现代化产业体系的‘骨架’挺起来”). The report, authored by Li Xinping, Wang Zheng, and Gu Yekai, details China's strategic progress in advanced manufacturing, framing it as the backbone of the country's modern industrial system.

The article outlines recent technological milestones, including successful commercial space launches, breakthroughs in display technologies, and the delivery of green shipping vessels. It further analyzes the national "Three Transformations" strategy—smartification, greening, and integration—as mandated by the "15th Five-Year" Plan. The report provides quantitative data on smart factory development, innovation cluster rankings, and specific goals for green manufacturing and autonomous vehicle adoption, positioning China's manufacturing sector in a period of accelerated transition toward high-value, service-integrated, and sustainable production models.

## Core Facts

The following facts are explicitly stated in the provided source material:

*   **Commercial Aerospace Milestone:** In September 2026, the Zhuque-2 improved rocket (Yao-7 mission) launched from the Dongfeng Commercial Space Innovation and Experimental Zone. This launch successfully placed the Qianfan polar-orbit Group 19, consisting of 10 satellites, into its预定 (pre-determined) orbit, achieving a "one-rocket, ten-satellite" deployment.
*   **Precision Manufacturing:** In March 2026, General Technology Shenyang Machine Tool released a vertical 5-axis machining center featuring integrated additive and subtractive manufacturing. This machine is capable of inscribing characters on a pinhead with a diameter of less than 0.2 millimeters.
*   **Green Shipping:** In April 2026, the world's first 10,800-carliquefied natural gas (LNG) dual-fuel vehicle transport ship was delivered. This vessel is capable of generating electricity while in transit.
*   **Smart Factory Statistics:** As of the report date, China has established:
    *   35,000 basic-level smart factories.
    *   8,200 advanced-level smart factories.
    *   Over 500 elite-level smart factories.
    *   15 enterprises are currently on the pilot list for "navigating-level" (leading-edge) smart factories.
*   **Artificial Intelligence Adoption:** The application rate of AI technology among large-scale manufacturing enterprises in China has exceeded 30%.
*   **Green Manufacturing Metrics:**
    *   During the "14th Five-Year Plan" period, the output value of national green factories increased from 9% to 22% of the total gross output value of large-scale manufacturing.
    *   The "15th Five-Year Plan" sets a target for green factory output to reach 45% of total large-scale manufacturing value by the end of the plan period.
    *   The plan aims to cultivate and construct 500 zero-carbon factories during the "15th Five-Year Plan" period.
*   **Innovation Rankings:** According to the World Intellectual Property Organization's *2026 Global Innovation Index*, China holds 25 of the world's top 100 innovation clusters, ranking first for the fourth consecutive year.
*   **Robotics Market Share:** As of the first half of 2026, China accounted for nearly 70% of global four-legged robot sales. The country also accounts for more than half of the global supply of humanoid robot complete products, with over 400 models available.
*   **IP Metrics:** As of the end of July 2026, China had 5.74 million valid domestic invention patents. PCT (Patent Cooperation Treaty) international patent applications have ranked first globally for seven consecutive years.
*   **Autonomous Vehicle Targets:** The *Intelligent Connected New Energy Vehicle Industry Development "15th Five-Year Plan"*, released in September 2026, sets a target for the scaled application of automobiles equipped with autonomous driving capabilities by 2030.
*   **Pilot Programs:** L3 conditional autonomous driving pilots have commenced in Beijing and Chongqing.

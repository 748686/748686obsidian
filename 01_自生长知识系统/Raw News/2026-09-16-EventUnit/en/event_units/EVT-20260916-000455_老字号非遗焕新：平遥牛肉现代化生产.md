# Event Name
Pingyao Beef Intangible Cultural Heritage Modernization: Transforming Century-Old Craft into "New Guochao"

## Event Overview
On September 16, 2026, *People's Daily* (Issue 19) published a report titled "Old Flavors Become 'New Guochao' (Time-Honored Brand Renewal Notes)" (Article #523), detailing the modernization of Pingyao beef, a state-level Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH). The report documents how companies like *Guanyun* and *Baojuyuan* are integrating traditional craftsmanship with industrial standardization, digital marketing, and cultural-tourism integration. This shift has transformed Pingyao beef from a local, short-shelf-life product into a nationally distributed, long-shelf-life snack, significantly boosting regional economic output.

## Core Facts
*   **Cultural Status:** Pingyao beef is one of the "Three Treasures of Pingyao," with over a millennium of processing history. The "Guanyun Pingyao Beef Traditional Processing Technique" was included in the second batch of the State Intangible Cultural Heritage List in 2008.
*   **Standardized Production:** 
    *   The *Guanyun* production line achieves an annual output of 20,000 tons, with a single pot capacity of 1,000 jin (500 kg) of beef.
    *   Fermentation conditions have been standardized to 3–4°C for 3–5 days, utilizing multi-needle brine injection instead of manual salting to ensure uniform flavor.
    *   The cooking process follows a fixed protocol: large fire for 2 hours, medium fire for 8 hours, and焖 (steaming) after heat extinction.
    *   Beef source: 3.5 to 4-year-old high-quality cattle.
*   **Shelf-Life Extension:** The adoption of a high-temperature sterilization process (120°C for 30 minutes) following vacuum packaging extended the product's shelf life from days to 12 months.
*   **Economic Performance (2025 Data):**
    *   *Guanyun* e-commerce sales exceeded 200 million yuan, with 90 million yuan generated from self-operated live-streaming.
    *   *Baojuyuan* recorded online production value of 120 million yuan and offline sales of approximately 200 million yuan.
    *   *Guanyun* annual production value exceeded 800 million yuan.
*   **Industrial Scale (H1 2026):**
    *   Pingyao County hosts 68 beef processing enterprises.
    *   Direct employment: >2,200 people; total industry-driven employment: >10,000 people.
    *   County-wide live cattle inventory: 22,600 head.
    *   Annual beef product output: 9,800 tons.
    *   Total industry output value: 2.35 billion yuan; sales: 2.12 billion yuan.
*   **Cultural-Tourism Integration:**
    *   *Guanyun* facility (a national 3A-grade tourist attraction) welcomes 300,000 visitors annually.
    *   *Baojuyuan* operates an 85-mu (approx. 5.6 hectares) industrial park with a "One Corridor, One Hall, One Museum, One Garden" layout, including a 240-mu farming base with over 3,000 head of cattle.
    *   *Baojuyuan* was founded in 1888 (14th year of Guangxu) and is over 130 years old.

## Event Conclusion
This event highlights a successful case of industrial modernization applied to Chinese Intangible Cultural Heritage (Pingyao Beef). By standardizing traditional processes (fermentation at 3–4°C, fixed cooking times) and integrating high-tech sterilization (120°C vacuum sealing), companies like *Guanyun* and *Baojuyuan* have bridged the gap between artisan heritage and mass-market viability. This transition has resulted in substantial economic growth for Pingyao County and a new "Cultural-Tourism" business model that attracts significant tourist traffic. The report from *People's Daily* frames this transformation as a model for "new Guochao" (new national trend), suggesting that protecting tradition does not require resisting industrialization, but rather using it to ensure stability, safety, and broader accessibility.

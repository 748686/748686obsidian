# Event Name
Integration of Financial Support for Agriculture and the Living Transformation of Xuzhou Han Culture into Urban Life

## Event Overview
This event unit captures two distinct thematic streams reported on September 17, 2026, in *People's Daily*. The first stream concerns the utilization of financial instruments, specifically loans, to support planting and breeding activities, thereby stabilizing the local "grain bin" and enlivening agricultural and livestock production. The second, more extensively detailed stream, describes the systematic integration of Chu-Han historical culture into the daily urban life of Xuzhou, Jiangsu Province. This cultural integration is manifested through themed public transportation, school curricula involving 3D printing, the development of the "Pengcheng Qili" (Seven Miles of Pengcheng) cultural axis, and the commercialization of intangible cultural heritage such as Hanfu making and clay modeling. The event reflects a broader trend of leveraging both financial policy and cultural heritage revitalization to stimulate local economic and social vitality.

## Core Facts
*   **Financial Support for Agriculture:** There is reported financial intervention ("loans") supporting planting and breeding. The stated goal is to stabilize food security ("steady grain bin") and activate the local agricultural and livestock economy.
*   **Xuzhou Cultural Integration:**
    *   **Public Transport:** Xuzhou operates multiple Han-culture-themed bus routes. One specific route stops at Pengcheng Plaza Station; the bus features historical figures on the exterior and chime-shaped handles inside. Attendants wear Hanfu and provide historical commentary during stops.
    *   **Resource Base:** Xuzhou possesses over 3,000 Han dynasty tombs and tens of thousands of Han dynasty cultural relics. The city is building a "Look at Two Han Cultures in Xuzhou" IP.
    *   **Education:** Yunlong Primary School has run a Han culture curriculum since 2023. Students used AI to generate images and 3D printed over 200 "Han Terracotta Warriors," which were exhibited in a museum.
    *   **Tourism & Commerce:** The "Pengcheng Qili" cultural axis contains over 200 historical cultural remains and more than 30 youth intangible cultural heritage shops (including wood joinery, lacquerware, rubbings, and shadow puppetry).
    *   **Footfall:** From January to July 2026, the "Pengcheng Qili" area recorded a total footfall of over 30 million people.
    *   **Museum Impact:** The Xuzhou Museum houses over 30,000 cultural relics, nearly half of which are Han dynasty remains. Its cultural creative store, which expanded from the museum to commercial districts, reported annual revenue exceeding 20 million yuan.
    *   **Digital Preservation:** Since 2019, a team from China University of Mining and Technology has collaborated with the Xuzhou Museum to digitally preserve Han pictorial reliefs using 3D scanning and cloud rubbing. Since 2024, they have used AI to animate these static images for immersive experiences.
    *   **Non-Heritage Practice:** Resident Li Ziyi, a post-00s generation, is the fifth-generation inheritor of Xuzhou clay modeling. Her shop in Hubu Mountain produces cartoon-style clay blind boxes that frequently sell out.

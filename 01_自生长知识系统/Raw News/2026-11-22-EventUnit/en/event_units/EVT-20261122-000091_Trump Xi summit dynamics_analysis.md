## 🎭 情景构建

### 情景一：🌟 理想世界
**情景名称：** "Co-Existence & Competence"

**核心假设：**
- Trump and Xi achieve a "Grand Bargain" limiting tech sanctions in exchange for Chinese restraint in South China Sea.
- Both nations face internal economic pressures that force pragmatism over ideology.

**情景描述：**
The post-summit period sees a stabilization of relations. While competition continues, explicit "rules of the road" are established for military and economic interaction. A joint US-China-G20 initiative on AI safety gains traction. Trade volumes remain stable, with some sectoral decoupling but overall interdependence preserved. The Global South benefits from reduced binary pressure to choose sides.

**对你的影响：**
- 机会： Market predictability returns; investment flows stabilize.
- 挑战： Competitive edge must be maintained through innovation, not just diplomacy.

**应对策略：**
1. **Accelerate Innovation:** Focus R&D spending on areas where the US holds a lead (semiconductors, biotech).
2. **Strengthen Alliances:** Deepen ties with EU and Indo-Pacific partners to ensure the "stable" equilibrium isn't just bilateral.

### 情景二：📊 常态延续
**情景名称：** "Managed Friction"

**核心假设：**
- No major breakthrough, but no major breakdown. Summit yields vague commitments and working groups.
- Status quo of "competitive coexistence" persists with periodic diplomatic spats.

**情景描述：**
Relations remain tense but predictable. Trade barriers rise incrementally. Military drills increase in frequency but remain controlled. Public discourse is hostile, but backchannel communications prevent escalation. The world adapts to a "new normal" of bifurcated tech standards (Chinese vs. Western). Economic growth slows globally due to inefficiencies from fragmentation.

**对你的影响：**
- 机会： Niche markets emerge for companies that can navigate dual-compliance.
- 挑战： Higher compliance costs; strategic uncertainty limits long-term planning.

**应对策略：**
1. **Build Resilience:** Diversify supply chains across multiple regions (Vietnam, Mexico, India) to hedge against policy shifts.
2. **Lobby for Clarity:** Engage actively in policy advocacy to ensure rules are transparent, even if restrictive.

### 情景三：⚠️ 压力测试
**情景名称：** "Decoupling & Detente Failure"

**核心假设：**
- Summit fails to produce tangible results; domestic politics in both countries radicalize positions.
- A crisis in the Taiwan Strait or South China Sea occurs, leading to severe sanctions and military posturing.

**情景描述：**
Economic ties fracture significantly. US imposes comprehensive tech embargoes; China retaliates with critical mineral bans. Travel and academic exchanges are nearly halted. The global economy enters a recession driven by trade war. Military risk is elevated, with increased chance of accidental engagement. Alliances harden into competing blocs.

**对你的影响：**
- 机会： Domestic producers in both nations may benefit from import substitution (though at high cost).
- 挑战 : Severe market volatility; supply chain collapse; reputational risk from association with either bloc.

**应对策略：**
1. **Radical Diversification:** Prepare for worst-case supply chain断链; stockpile critical resources.
2. **Legal & Compliance Fortress:** Strengthen legal teams to navigate conflicting sanctions regimes.

### 情景四：🔄 意外转折
**情景名称：** "Black Swan: Shared Crisis"

**触发条件：** Major global pandemic, nuclear test, or catastrophic climate event that forces unprecedented cooperation.

**情景描述：**
An external shock of existential magnitude overrides strategic rivalry. Trump and Xi announce a joint emergency response, leading to a temporary thaw and eventual structural cooperation on the specific issue. This creates a template for future collaboration but doesn't resolve underlying tensions.

**应对策略：**
1. **Crisis Preparedness:** Maintain contingency plans for rapid diplomatic engagement.
2. **Issue-Specific Partnerships:** Keep channels open for non-traditional security issues (climate, health) where cooperation is feasible even during rivalry.

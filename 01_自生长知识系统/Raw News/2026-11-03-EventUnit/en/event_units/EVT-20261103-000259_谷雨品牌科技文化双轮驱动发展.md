# Event Unit: 谷雨 (Guyu) Brand's Dual-Drive Development via Technology and Culture

## Event Overview

On November 3, 2026, the People's Daily published a feature article (12th Edition) highlighting the domestic skincare brand Guyu (谷雨). The article describes Guyu's strategic evolution over its first decade, characterized by a "dual-wheel drive" of technological innovation and cultural confidence. The narrative positions Guyu as a model for Chinese brands transitioning from simple product export to value and cultural export within the context of global economic trends and the Belt and Road Initiative.

The event focuses on Guyu's establishment of a full-stack self-research system, covering hardware infrastructure, software scientific research databases, core ingredient breakthroughs (specifically Glycyrrhiza glabra extract and Rare Ginsenoside CK), and the integration of traditional Chinese culture into brand storytelling and ESG initiatives.

## Core Facts

**1. Organizational Milestone**
*   **Entity:** Guyu Biotechnology Group Co., Ltd. (谷雨生物科技集团股份有限公司).
*   **Milestone:** The article was published during the brand's 10th anniversary year (established circa 2016).
*   **Mission Statement:** "Create health and beauty for humanity using Chinese technology and Chinese culture."

**2. Technological Infrastructure (Hardware & Software)**
*   **R&D Center:** The "Qingwen R&D Center" (青问研发中心) spans over 6,000 square meters with eight functional laboratories.
*   **Manufacturing:** A high-level cosmetics factory covering 45,000 square meters with 14 automated production lines.
*   **Supply Chain:** Four self-built raw material factories focusing on synthetic biology, chemical synthesis, food/health products, and plant extraction.
*   **Future Construction:** A global scientific research and smart manufacturing center (over 220,000 square meters) is under construction in Huangpu District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, projected to become the brand's "super brain."
*   **Agricultural Base:** A nearly 300-mu (approx. 20 hectares) Glycyrrhiza glabra scientific research and planting base in Kashgar, Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region.

**3. Scientific Research Collaborations & Databases**
*   **Database:** Collected over 50,000 dynamic samples and accumulated nearly 1.6 million skin type research indicators.
*   **Center Launch:** On May 2, 2026, the "Chinese People's Skin Type and Constitution Scientific Research Center" was officially unveiled. It establishes a special research fund to shift industry standards from "empirical judgment" to "evidence-based R&D."
*   **University Partnership:** In September 2025, Guyu co-established the "Xiamen University - Guyu Biomedical Joint Research Center" with Xiamen University. The director is a Professor of the School of Life Sciences at Xiamen University and an Academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.
*   **Research Focus:** Joint studies on the application activation effect of Rare Ginsenoside CK on the AMPK (AMP-activated protein kinase) pathway and its prospects in life health.

**4. Core Ingredient Breakthroughs**
*   **Glycyrrhiza Glabra Extract (Light Licorice/Guangcaoding):**
    *   Utilizes three proprietary technologies: "Fusion Crystallization Purification," "28nm Encapsulation," and "Targeted Delivery."
    *   Achieved purity of 99% for Glycyrrhiza glabra extract (Glabridin/Licochalcone A context implies high-purity active components), maintaining industry leadership.
*   **Rare Ginsenoside CK:**
    *   Produced via "Smart Enzyme Biomimetic Biosynthesis Technology."
    *   **Regulatory Milestone 1:** In May 2025, Guyu completed the industry's first cosmetic new raw material registration for Ginsenoside CK (Registration No.: Guo Zhuang Yuan Bei Zi 20250059).
    *   **Regulatory Milestone 2:** In April 2026, Guyu's Rare Ginsenoside CK obtained FDA DMF Class II filing (No. 043987).
*   **Cell Secretion HME (Exosome/SiRNA related):**
    *   Based on patented technologies including:
        *   "Preparation method and application of artificial synthetic exosomes with anti-aging effects" (Patent No.: ZL 2025 10999791.1).
        *   "siRNA with anti-skin cell aging and repair effects and application thereof" (Patent No.: ZL 2025 11596213.X).
        *   "Artificial synthetic bare exosomes with permeability promotion and repair effects and preparation method and application thereof" (Patent No.: ZL 2025 1 1010222.6).
    *   Launched series of new products in May 2026, marking the transition from laboratory to production line.

**5. International Exposure**
*   **Event:** In April 2026, Guyu exhibited at in-cosmetics Global in Paris, France.
*   **Role Shift:** Participated as a raw material supplier (not just a brand) presenting three core self-developed ingredients:极光甘草 (Aurora Licorice), Rare Ginsenoside CK, and Cell Secretion HME.
*   **Outcome:** Received multiple international cooperation intentions.

**6. Cultural and ESG Initiatives**
*   **Cultural Product Lines:**
    *   "Four Seasons Boundless" (四时无垠) themed gift box integrating the 24 Solar Terms.
    *   "Exquisite Moon Cup" (玲珑月盏) Mid-Autumn gift box inspired by intangible cultural heritage porcelain art.
    *   "Light Comes from the East" (光从东方来) city music concert incorporating shadow puppetry and Qinqiang opera.
*   **ESG Project:** "Rain and Wood Symbiosis" (雨木共生) public welfare project, running for 3 years.
    *   Recycles cosmetic empty bottles into school educational materials for rural areas.
    *   Locations: Shaoguan (Guangdong), Longde (Ningxia), and Kashgar (Xinjiang).
    *   Offers classes on scientific skincare, aesthetic education, and sports.
*   **Women's Health:** Partnered with China Women's News and Beijing Xingfu Public Welfare Foundation to launch a public welfare psychological counseling hotline for women.

# Event Name
Village Group Leader Misappropriation of Rural Water Installation Surplus Funds in Xinyu, Jiangxi

## Event Overview
In November 2026, the Disciplinary Inspection and Supervision Working Committee of the Xinyu High-tech Zone in Jiangxi Province disclosed a case of a village group leader misappropriating surplus funds collected from villagers for rural water supply installation. The incident was identified through a targeted supervision campaign focused on "small incisions" in the rural water supply sector. The official commentary, published in *People's Daily*, frames the case as an example of "micro-corruption" at the grassroots level, emphasizing the need to treat livelihood funds with strict legal and disciplinary adherence rather than as personal property.

## Core Facts
*   **Location:** A specific village group within the Xinyu High-tech Zone, Jiangxi Province.
*   **Perpetrator:** He Moumou (何某某), the leader of the village group.
*   **Fund Source:** Water installation fees collected on behalf of 91 households.
*   **Misappropriated Amount:** 42,300 RMB (approx. $5,800 USD).
*   **Context:** Following the completion of water installation in 2024, the surplus balance of 42,300 RMB should have been refunded to the villagers. Instead, He Moumou retained these funds for personal use over an extended period.
*   **Discovery Mechanism:** The issue came to light during a special supervision campaign in the rural water supply field initiated by the Xinyu High-tech Zone Disciplinary Inspection and Supervision Working Committee, triggered by villager complaints that water had been connected for nearly two years with no resolution regarding excess fees.
*   **Investigation Method:** The committee employed a "upgrade handling + regional collaboration" mechanism, utilizing data comparison, door-to-door verification, and fund tracing to cross-verify accounts and facts.
*   **Resolution:** Upon intervention by the disciplinary inspection department, He Moumou voluntarily refunded the misappropriated surplus.
*   **Disciplinary Outcome:** He Moumou received an administrative sanction (政务处分) for infringing upon the interests of the masses and violating work requirements.
*   **Official Commentary Key Points:**
    *   Livelihood funds are described as "heart-to-heart accounts" (民心账).
    *   The case highlights weak links in the supervision of grassroots small-power funds and insufficient transparency in power operation.
    *   Recommendations include standardizing small-power operations, clarifying boundaries, and promoting常态化 (normalized) public disclosure of village affairs and finances to curb grassroots "micro-corruption."

## Event Overview
On November 3, 2026, *People's Daily* published a report titled "Consolidating Its Own Advantages in Serving the Real Economy" (10th Edition, Comprehensive). The article details how Postal Savings Bank of China (PSBC) has focused on five key areas—technology finance, green finance, inclusive finance, pension finance, and digital finance—referred to collectively as the "Five Major Articles" (五篇大文章). The bank reports that these initiatives have translated into new growth drivers, helping it reshape its development momentum and advantages. The article includes specific case studies from Hunan, Chongqing, Yunnan, Hebei, and Jiangxi provinces, alongside financial performance data as of June 2026.

## Event Overview

This event unit synthesizes reporting from November 3, 2026, covering the aftermath of the Second Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC), held in late October 2026, and concurrent diplomatic activities involving visits by Omani Sultan Haitham bin Tariq. The central domestic political outcome was the adoption of a major decision on comprehensively and persistently advancing strict Party governance. Concurrently, high-level bilateral engagements were held with Oman, Russia, and Vietnam.

## Core Facts

**1. The 20th CPC Central Committee’s Second Plenary Session (2026)**
*   **Timing and Outcome:** The session was held in Beijing in late October 2026 (specifically convened around October 26–29). On the afternoon of October 29, 2026, it officially adopted the *Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persistently Advancing Strict Party Governance in an Unswerving Manner* (hereinafter "the Decision").
*   **Context:** The session took place at the critical juncture of the 105th anniversary of the founding of the CPC and the beginning of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period.
*   **Key Content of the Decision:**
    *   The Decision is described as a programmatic document for advancing the new great project of Party building in the new era.
    *   It summarizes the great achievements and valuable experience of strict Party governance since the 18th CPC National Congress.
    *   It analyzes new situations, tasks, and challenges in Party building.
    *   It proposes seven guiding principles: (1) Adhering to serving the mission and tasks; (2) Adhering to the overall leadership of Party political construction; (3) Adhering to comprehensive strictness; (4) Adhering to a problem-oriented approach; (5) Adhering to upholding fundamental principles while breaking new ground; (6) Adhering to leading by example from the top; (7) Adhering to governing the Party according to regulations.
    *   It identifies "three more arduous" tasks (safeguarding sovereignty/security/development, promoting reform/development/stability, maintaining Party先进性 and purity) and "four major challenges" (governing capability, leadership level, team quality, anti-corruption and integrity).
    *   It emphasizes finding the "two answers" to escaping the historical cycle of dynastic rise and fall: "letting the people supervise the government" and "self-revolution."
*   **Drafting Process:**
    *   The draft was led by General Secretary Xi Jinping.
    *   The process involved 23 key research topics commissioned to 16 units, resulting in 28 research reports.
    *   Solicitation of opinions occurred in multiple rounds (January, August), receiving feedback from Party members, retired leaders, democratic parties, and non-Party figures. A total of 1,173 modification opinions were submitted, with an absorption rate of 17.7%.
    *   The final version underwent 36 drafts during the process.
*   **Post-Session Implementation:**
    *   On November 2, 2026, a mobilization meeting for the Central Propaganda Team to study and implement the spirit of the Plenary Session was held in Beijing. Cai Qi attended and spoke.
    *   On November 2, 2026, Li Ganjie, on behalf of the CPC Central Committee, briefed non-Party人士 on the spirit of the Plenary Session.
    *   Across the country and among overseas Chinese communities, there was widespread response supporting the Decision and the push for strict Party governance.

**2. Diplomatic Activity: Visit of Omani Sultan Haitham bin Tariq to China**
*   **Dates:** November 1–2, 2026.
*   **Significance:** This was the first visit by an Omani Head of State to China since the establishment of diplomatic relations 48 years ago.
*   **Key Meetings:**
    *   **Xi Jinping – Sultan Haitham:** Held talks in Beijing on the morning of November 2. They witnessed the signing of cooperation documents in BeiDou satellite navigation, production/supply chains, green development, local currency swap, digital payment, and digital currency.
    *   **Li Qiang – Sultan Haitham:** Held a meeting in Beijing on the afternoon of November 2.
*   **Joint Output:** The *Joint Statement of the People's Republic of China and the Sultanate of Oman on Deepening the Strategic Partnership* was issued.
*   **Key Themes of Cooperation:**
    *   **Political:** Deepening strategic partnership, mutual support on core interests, Oman reaffirming the One-China principle and support for China’s unification.
    *   **Economic/Energy:** Deepening oil/gas cooperation, expanding renewable energy (solar, wind), connecting China’s Belt and Road Initiative with Oman’s "Vision 2040," promoting China-GCC Free Trade Agreement negotiations.
    *   **Technology:** Cooperation in artificial intelligence (Oman became a founding member of the World AI Cooperation Organization), aerospace, digital economy.
    *   **Regional/Global:** Coordination on Middle East peace and stability, support for Iran nuclear dialogue and Hormuz Strait navigation, support for Yemen political solution.
*   **Invitation:** Sultan Haitham invited President Xi Jinping to visit Oman.

**3. Other Diplomatic and Legislative Activities (November 2, 2026)**
*   **Russia:** Wang Huning met with Valentina Matviyenko, Chair of the Federation Council of Russia. Zhao Leji also held talks with Matviyenko. Topics included the 30th anniversary of China-Russia strategic coordination and 25th anniversary of the Treaty of Good-Neighborliness and Friendly Cooperation.
*   **Vietnam:** Wang Huning met with a delegation led by Nguyen Thanh Nghi of the Communist Party of Vietnam.
*   **Peru:** Xi Jinping exchanged congratulatory messages with Peruvian President Dina Boluarte (referred to as藤森庆子 in source text) on the 55th anniversary of diplomatic relations. The upgrade protocol to the China-Peru Free Trade Agreement entered into force on November 1, 2026.
*   **Legislature:** The 25th Session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People’s Congress convened on November 2, 2026, presided over by Zhao Leji. Key legislative items included reviews of the Procuratorial Public Interest Litigation Law, Water Law revision, Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law, and the approval of the Agreement on the Establishment of the World AI Cooperation Organization.

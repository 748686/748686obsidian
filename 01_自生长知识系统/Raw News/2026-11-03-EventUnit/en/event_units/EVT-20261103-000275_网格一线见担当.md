## Core Facts

**Case Study: Shenqiu County Land Dispute**
*   **Location:** Yangzhuang Village, Shenqiu County, Zhoukou City, Henan Province.
*   **Parties Involved:** Uncle Yang (resident), his son (migrant worker), and a neighbor.
*   **Incident:** The neighbor felled a large tree onto Uncle Yang’s cultivated land and repeatedly drove machinery over the field, damaging crops.
*   **Escalation Risk:** Uncle Yang called his son, who threatened to return immediately via high-speed rail to seek retribution, raising the risk of violent confrontation due to high return costs and anger.
*   **Intervention:** Chen Dan, a Party member grid worker patrolling the area, detected the disturbance and reported it to Village Party Secretary Yang Bing.
*   **Resolution:** Secretary Yang and Chen Dan visited the site the same day. They:
    *   Criticized and educated the neighbor.
    *   Mediated with Uncle Yang.
    *   Contacted the son to persuade him not to return.
    *   Assisted in coordinating a rototiller because the neighbor could not find one.
*   **Outcome:** The neighbor agreed to restore the land and apologize. The dispute was settled without the son needing to travel.

**Institutional Context (Zhoukou City)**
*   **Grid Structure:** Zhoukou has optimized the setup of 27,832 rural, community, and exclusive grids.
*   **Methodology ("3345"):** The city employs a "3345" grid worker work method:
    *   **3 Tasks:** Inspection, reporting, and disposal.
    *   **3 Time Slots:** Mandatory checks during morning, afternoon, and evening.
    *   **4 Key Items:** Mandatory inspection of four categories of important issues.
    *   **5 Bottom Lines:** Mandatory reporting of five types of底线 (bottom-line) problems.
*   **Workforce:**
    *   32,301 full-time and part-time grid workers are active across the city.
    *   968 Rural Revitalization Village Assistants have been deployed to villages for regular household visits and conflict排查 (investigation).
*   **Organizational Reforms:**
    *   "One village, one policy" rectification of weak and lax village Party organizations.
    *   Placement of capable Party members into village "two committees" (Party branch and village committee).
*   **Digital Infrastructure:** Construction of a "Digital Grid"依托 (relying on) high-efficiency governance platforms, enabling dynamic collection and real-time updates of six elements: people, land, matters, objects, emotions, and organizations.
*   **Performance Metrics:** Since the beginning of 2026, grid workers in Zhoukou have resolved 34,000 (3.4万) various types of conflicts.

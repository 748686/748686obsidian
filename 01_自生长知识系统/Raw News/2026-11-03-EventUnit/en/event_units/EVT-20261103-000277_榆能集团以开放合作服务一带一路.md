# EVT-20261103-000277: Shaanxi Yulin Energy Group Advances Belt and Road Cooperation Through Open Collaboration

## Event Overview

Between November 2024 and November 2026, Shaanxi Yulin Energy Group Co., Ltd. (hereinafter "Yumen Group") actively integrated into the Belt and Road Initiative, transforming from a traditional coal mining enterprise into an internationally oriented energy and chemical conglomerate. The initiative encompasses infrastructure connectivity (logistics networks), institutional platform building, direct investment in Central Asia, and green technology export. Yumen Group leveraged its position as a key state-owned enterprise in Yulin City, Shaanxi Province, to expand trade routes, establish overseas warehouses, and develop low-carbon technical solutions for international partners.

## Core Facts

**Entity Profile**
*   **Organization:** Shaanxi Yulin Energy Group Co., Ltd. (Yumen Group)
*   **Type:** Key municipal state-owned enterprise of Yulin City, Shaanxi Province
*   **Establishment:** 2012
*   **Status:** Ranked among "China's Top 500 Enterprises"
*   **Core Business:** Coal, electricity, chemicals, new materials ("4+N" industrial pattern)

**Infrastructure and Logistics Connectivity**
*   **Resource Base:** Relies on the Shenfu, Yushen, and Yuheng coal-rich areas.
*   **Railway Network:** Utilizes Shen-Shuo, Bao-Xi, and Jing-Shen railway special lines for large-scale container stations.
*   **Hunan-Huazhong Base:** Controlled construction and operation of the Hunan Huazhong Iron-Water Intermodal Energy Storage and Distribution Base. This base connects the Haoji Railway with the Yangtze River Golden Waterway, supporting north-south coal transport and opening a new channel for "Xinjiang coal to Hunan," while facilitating chemical product distribution.
*   **Port Access:** Leverages Qinhuangdao Port and Huanghua Port to ship Yulin thermal coal to domestic coastal markets and South Korea via railway and sea routes.
*   **Europe Route:** Utilizes the "Yulin-Western Europe" China-Europe Freight Train (via Xi'an Port/Chang'an Express) to export polyvinyl chloride, clean energy semi-coke, and metallic magnesium to Eurasia.

**Institutional and Platform Engagement**
*   **Silk Road International Expo:** Showcased the "4+N" industry structure at the 10th Silk Road International Expo.
*   **International Conferences:** Participated in the China International Mining Conference, Yulin International Coal and High-End Energy Chemical Expo, and the International Aluminium Industry Exhibition in Dusseldorf, Germany.
*   **Focus Areas:** Clean energy development, coal-based new materials trade, and low-carbon technology transfer.

**Central Asian Investment and Diplomacy (2026 Timeline)**
*   **April 2026:** Delegation led by the China Association for the Promotion of International Industry Overseas Development attended the First Turkmenistan-China Business Forum and Exhibition in Ashgabat. Yumen Group discussed frameworks for energy trade, green smart coal mining, chemical new materials, ecological governance, and new energy with Turkmenistan’s Ministry of Finance and Economy and Ministry of Environmental Protection. Topics included natural gas chemicals, natural gas extraction, and photovoltaics.
*   **May 2026:** A delegation from the Karakalpakstan Republic of Uzbekistan visited Yulin to study zero-carbon energy models and agreed to establish a normalized exchange platform.
*   **September 2026:** Yumen Group joined a Shaanxi delegation in Nukus, attending regional economic and trade activities and conducting in-depth docking with the Karakalpakstan Ministry of Investment, Industry, and Trade.
*   **October 2026:** Established a professional investment task force dedicated to Central Asia, transitioning negotiations into market research, investment calculations, and project ledgers.

**Metallic Magnesium Global Expansion**
*   **Overseas Warehousing:** Subsidiary Yumei Group established overseas warehouses in Rotterdam, Netherlands, linked with Tianjin export collection warehouses and domestic off-site warehouses. This creates a "domestic production – cross-border transport – overseas warehousing – nearby distribution" system.
*   **Historical Milestone:** In April 2021, Yulin’s first metallic magnesium export railway container train departed, transporting over 900 tons of raw magnesium to Tianjin for distribution to Canada and the Netherlands.
*   **Industrial Upgrade:** Moving from raw magnesium storage and trade toward alloy R&D and deep processing, shifting from single product exports to connecting with downstream applications and magnesium-based new materials.

**Green and Low-Carbon Technology Portfolio**
*   **Coal Mining:** Guojiatan Coal Mine employs "water-conserving coal mining" technology to minimize surface aquifer water loss.
*   **Power Generation:**
    *   Hengshan Coal-Power Integration Project: Features the world’s first commercially operated "three-tower-integrated" indirect air-cooled million-kilowatt unit, achieving ultra-low emissions and zero wastewater discharge.
    *   Yanghuopan Coal-Power Integration Power Plant: Implements the world’s first natural ventilation direct air-cooling system, selected as a national major first-set equipment project.
*   **Chemicals:** A >10 billion RMB epoxy resin integration project links "coal – methanol – propylene – epoxy resin." It utilizes chloropropene direct oxidation to produce chloropropanol, a domestic first industrial demonstration that solves high-chlorine and high-salt wastewater issues inherent in traditional processes.
*   **New Energy:** Developing wind-solar-power-storage integrated comprehensive energy bases to complement fossil fuel baseload with renewable energy reduction.

Jiaxing Leverages Wuzhen Summit Opportunity to Drive High-Quality AI Development (EVT-20261103-000257)

## Event Overview

From November 2 to 5, 2026, the 2026 World Internet Conference (WIC) Wuzhen Summit is being held in Wuzhen, Zhejiang Province, under the theme "Openness and Sharing — Working Together to Build a Community with a Shared Future in Cyberspace." In conjunction with the summit, Jiaxing City is launching two distinctive initiatives: the "Artificial Intelligence 'Jia' Carnival" and the "Digital Yangtze River Delta Development Conference." These activities aim to showcase the latest achievements in digital civilization construction within the Yangtze River Delta, promote comprehensive AI empowerment across industries, and accelerate the establishment of Jiaxing as a new highland for artificial intelligence development.

## Core Facts

**Event Timeline and Location**
*   **Dates:** November 2–5, 2026.
*   **Primary Venues:** Wuzhen Internet International Conference Center (Hall B) for the exhibition; locations across Wuzhen West Scenic Area for public interaction; venue for the Digital Yangtze River Delta Development Conference to be confirmed (scheduled for November 4).
*   **Source Attribution:** All core facts are derived from *People’s Daily* (Article #399), citing data from the Publicity Department of the Jiaxing Municipal Committee of the Communist Party of China.

**"Artificial Intelligence 'Jia' Carnival"**
*   **Structure:** A 1,500-square-meter super experience park themed "A Day in the Future," featuring five scenarios: smart home, smart office, future city, Jiaxing manufacturing, and digital leisure.
*   **Exhibitors:** 49 local Jiaxing enterprises showcased their capabilities, particularly in embodied intelligence.
*   **Key Demonstrations:**
    *   *Digital Leisure:* Ai Dachu (AI Chef) robot demonstrating culinary skills.
    *   *Smart Office:* Mainstream Chinese AI office agents, including Baidu Dazi and Tencent WorkBuddy.
    *   *Future City:* Zhiyuan Expedition A3Ultra humanoid robot (noted as a flagship exhibit from the World Artificial Intelligence Conference).
*   **Public Interaction:** Outside the exhibition center, commercial entities such as SenseTime’s "Shaomai Gou" fully autonomous robot shop and Unitree Robotics’ humanoid robots interacting with Hanfu NPCs were reported. CloudDeep’s robotic dogs were also noted for racing in the ancient town.

**"Digital Yangtze River Delta Development Conference"**
*   **Date:** November 4, 2026.
*   **Participants:** Over 300 guests from government, industry, academia, and research institutions.
*   **Key Outputs:**
    *   Release of the *Digital Yangtze River Delta Development Report (2026)*.
    *   Exhibition of 12 pioneer "AI+" high-value application scenarios from Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Anhui.
    *   Introduction of a novel "1 vs. 5 Pain Point Questioning" dialogue format involving five leading embodied intelligence enterprise representatives.
*   **Strategic Goal:** To facilitate scenario co-construction and sharing among partner cities in the "Wuzhen Dialogue" for digital civilization, promoting the model of "innovation in one location, reuse in multiple locations."

**Industrial and Infrastructure Data (Q3 2026)**
*   **Revenue:** The AI core industry generated 71 billion RMB in revenue in the first three quarters of 2026, representing a year-on-year growth of 26%.
*   **Computing Services:** Revenue from computing data services grew by 55% year-on-year.
*   **Computing Power Capacity:**
    *   Operational capacity: 118 EFLOPS (accounting for over 60% of Zhejiang Province’s total).
    *   Under construction: 197 EFLOPS.
    *   Projected status: National leading level upon completion.
*   **Key Facilities:**
    *   Jiashan County: Part of the National Integrated Computing Power Network Yangtze River Delta Hub Node.
    *   "Light of Wuzhen" Supercomputing Center: The 14th national supercomputing center and the only one in Zhejiang Province.
    *   Pinghu City: Home to the largest single high-performance computing center in the Yangtze River Delta region.
*   **Policy Alignment:** Jiaxing was selected for the 2026 City-level "Millisecond Computing" Special Action, with national computing power interconnection regional nodes accelerating implementation.

**Industrial Ecosystem Initiatives**
*   **"4+6" Terminal System:** Jiaxing is constructing an AI terminal industry system.
*   **Specific Projects:**
    *   *Nanhu District:* Established China’s first flexible electronic embodied intelligence training center.
    *   *Hangzhou-Tongxiang-Deqing Consortium:* Selected for the Intelligent Connected Vehicle "Vehicle-Road-Cloud Integration" application pilot.
    *   *Jiaxing Economic and Technological Development Zone:* Focusing on AIGC audio-visual tracks, utilizing Jiaxing Jiuzhou Culture to build an AIGC industrial park based on a "super individual" model.

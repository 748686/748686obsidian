# Event Analysis: Xifeng Liquor "Dual Heritage" Brand Narrative

Based on the analysis of the *People's Daily* advertisement published on November 3, 2026, the following marketing framework analysis deconstructs the brand narrative strategy employed by Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Factory Group Co., Ltd.

## 1. SCQA Analysis: Structural Logic of the Narrative

The advertisement employs the **SCQA model** (Situation-Complication-Question-Answer) to construct a logical chain that transitions from national policy context to specific brand action, establishing authority and relevance.

*   **S (Situation - Context & Consensus):**
    The narrative begins by establishing a broad, undeniable background aligned with national strategy. It references the **"15th Five-Year Plan for Cultural Heritage Protection and Inheritance,"** citing key principles such as "protection first" and "reasonable utilization." It further anchors the brand in history by stating Xifeng Liquor possesses an **unbroken 3,000-year lineage** of brewing culture. This creates a foundation of cultural confidence and political correctness that resonates with both domestic readers and the broader narrative of Chinese cultural export.

*   **C (Complication - Tension & Challenge):**
    The "complication" is implicitly defined by the challenge of *living transmission*. How does a 3,000-year-old craft survive in the modern era? How can traditional intangible cultural heritage (ICH) remain relevant and economically viable? The text highlights the tension between preserving history and meeting modern market demands. It subtly introduces the need for a strategy that is not just static preservation but "living transmission" (活态传承). The complexity lies in integrating industrial heritage (the factory site) with intangible heritage (the brewing technique) — the "Dual Heritage" concept.

*   **Q (Question - The Core Challenge):**
    While not explicitly phrased as a question in the ad, the narrative structure answers the implicit question: *"How can Xifeng Liquor fulfill its duty as a guardian of national heritage while expanding its influence globally?"* The article poses the challenge of transforming heritage status into tangible brand value and international reach.

*   **A (Answer - The Solution & Action):**
    The answer is the **"Dual Heritage" Strategy** executed through three pillars:
    1.  **Institutional Recognition:** Securing both National ICH status (2021) and National Industrial Heritage status (2021).
    2.  **Physical Infrastructure for "Living Transmission":** Opening the **Xifeng Liquor Culture Museum** (Sept 2025) and preserving the **Wine Sea Warehouse** (*Jiu Hai Ku*) using traditional Qinling blackberry branch weaving and paper-lining techniques.
    3.  **Global Expansion:** Moving from cultural preservation to commercial globalization, with trademarks in 20 countries and exports to 30+ regions across Southeast Asia, Europe, and Africa.

## 2. PAS Analysis: Emotional Persuasion and Value Proposition

The advertisement uses the **PAS framework** (Problem-Agitate-Solution) to evoke pride, urgency, and trust, positioning Xifeng Liquor not just as a product but as a cultural ambassador.

*   **P (Problem - Identifying the Pain Point):**
    The underlying "problem" addressed is the potential erosion of traditional craftsmanship and the lack of global visibility for Chinese liquor culture. The ad identifies the risk that ancient techniques might be lost or merely become museum artifacts rather than living traditions. It highlights the need for authentic, verifiable heritage in an era where "cultural confidence" is a national priority.

*   **A (Agitate - Amplifying the Stakes):**
    The advertisement agitates this problem by emphasizing the **scarcity and uniqueness** of Xifeng’s heritage. It details the specific, labor-intensive construction of the "Wine Seas" (hand-woven with over a hundred layers of paper), implying that such authentic methods are vanishing. It contrasts the fleeting nature of modern trends with the **3,000-year continuity** of Xifeng’s lineage. By linking the brand to the "15th Five-Year Plan," it elevates the stakes: supporting Xifeng is not just buying liquor; it is participating in the national mission of cultural protection. The agitation is subtle but effective—highlighting that without such "living transmission," this 3,000-year history could fade into obscurity.

*   **S (Solution - The Offer and Benefits):**
    The solution is presented as Xifeng’s comprehensive ecosystem:
    *   **For the Consumer:** Access to authentic, historically preserved liquor made via traditional methods (Wine Sea aging).
    *   **For the Industry/Nation:** A model of "Industrial Tourism" that combines culture, tourism, and production.
    *   **For the Global Market:** A premium Chinese liquor option with a proven track record in 30+ countries, serving as a "cultural ambassador" that tells the story of Chinese liquor well.
    The ad concludes by positioning Xifeng as the definitive answer to the challenge of modernizing heritage, offering both product quality and cultural significance.

## 3. AIDA Analysis: Consumer Journey and Engagement Path

The advertisement is structured to guide the reader through the **AIDA model** (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action), typical of high-impact brand narratives in official media.

*   **A (Attention - Grappling the Eye):**
    The headline **"Inheriting the 'Dual Heritage' Cultural Context to Start a New Chapter for Feng Flavor"** immediately captures attention by leveraging two powerful keywords: **"Dual Heritage"** (a unique differentiator) and **"New Chapter"** (suggesting innovation and future growth). Publication in *People's Daily*, Page 19, lends immediate credibility and ensures the message reaches a wide, influential audience. The claim of a **3,000-year lineage** serves as a striking hook.

*   **I (Interest - Building Relevance):**
    Once attention is grabbed, the ad builds interest by providing concrete, fascinating details:
    *   The specific history of the **Wine Sea Warehouse**.
    *   The technical details of the **Qinling blackberry branch** construction.
    *   The opening of the **Culture Museum** in September 2025.
    These specifics move the narrative from abstract heritage to tangible, interesting facts that invite further reading. The connection to the "15th Five-Year Plan" also appeals to readers interested in national policy and economic trends.

*   **D (Desire - Creating Want):**
    The ad cultivates desire by appealing to emotions of **pride, trust, and prestige**:
    *   **Trust:** The "Dual Heritage" status acts as a seal of quality and authenticity.
    *   **Prestige:** Associating with a brand that is recognized by the state and has a 3,000-year history enhances the consumer’s self-image when choosing or gifting the product.
    *   **Global Appeal:** The mention of presence in **Southeast Asia, Europe, and Africa** suggests that Xifeng is a world-class brand, increasing its desirability as a symbol of Chinese luxury and culture. The narrative of "living transmission" assures consumers that they are supporting a sustainable, ongoing legacy rather than a stagnant tradition.

*   **A (Action - Prompting Response):**
    While the ad is primarily brand-building rather than direct response sales, the implied action is clear:
    *   **Brand Loyalty:** Choose Xifeng Liquor as a representative of Chinese heritage.
    *   **Cultural Participation:** Engage with the brand’s narrative of cultural confidence.
    *   **Exploration:** Visit the Xifeng Liquor Culture Museum or the industrial tourism sites.
    *   **Global Support:** Support Chinese liquor in the international market.
    The closing emphasis on "Starting a New Chapter for Feng Flavor" implies an invitation to be part of this ongoing journey, encouraging consumption and advocacy.

## Summary

The advertisement for Xifeng Liquor in *People's Daily* is a sophisticated example of corporate branding that aligns commercial interests with national cultural policy. By leveraging the **SCQA** structure, it grounds its narrative in authoritative context. Through **PAS**, it elevates the emotional stakes of heritage preservation. Finally, using **AIDA**, it guides the reader from initial curiosity to a desire for association with a prestigious, globally recognized brand. The core message positions Xifeng not merely as a liquor producer, but as a vital custodian of China’s 3,000-year brewing civilization, actively contributing to national cultural confidence and global soft power.

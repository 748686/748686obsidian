## Core Facts

**1. Strategic Positioning and Financial Performance**
*   **Strategy:** Moutai follows a three-step strategy of "product export, brand出海, internationalization." It aims to shift from "channel-driven" to "consumer-driven" operations.
*   **Market Reach:** As of September 2026, Moutai products cover **67 countries and regions**.
*   **Revenue:** In 2025, the overseas market revenue of Kweichou Moutai Co., Ltd. was approximately **4.85 billion RMB**.
*   **Growth Metrics:** In 2025, sales in the Southeast Asian market grew by **over 4 times** year-on-year, and duty-free channel sales **doubled**.

**2. Quality and Production Base**
*   **Core Production Area:** Located by the Chishui River, covering **15.03 square kilometers**, recognized as the geographic indication protection scope.
*   **Craftsmanship:** The brewing technique was included in the first batch of National Intangible Cultural Heritage items in 2006. Production involves 30 processes, 165 operational links, and takes at least **5 years** before release.
*   **Quality Philosophy:** Adheres to "Quality is the Soul of Life," with the principle that production volume, cost, benefit, and speed all submit to quality.

**3. Scientific and Technological Innovation**
*   **Infrastructure:**
    *   2022: Establishment of the Moutai Science and Technology Research Institute.
    *   2025: Unveiling of Guizhou Moutai Biotechnology R&D Co., Ltd.
*   **Methodology:** Utilizes a "Space-Time Method" brewing system, focusing on the analysis of microbial communities (fungal system, enzyme system, material system).
*   **Digital Transformation:** Uses machine learning and big data to quantify artisan sensory experience into standardized, predictable data criteria.

**4. Awards and Governance (ESG)**
*   **National Quality Award:** Won in 2003, 2011, and 2023.
*   **EFQM Global Award:** In June 2024, Moutai received the 2023 EFQM Global Award Seven Diamonds and the "Inspiring Culture" Outstanding Achievement Award, becoming the first Chinese enterprise to achieve Seven Diamonds under the new evaluation standards.
*   **ESG Rating:** Maintains an **A-level** MSCI ESG rating with a three-tier ESG governance architecture.

**5. International Cultural Activities (2025–2026)**
*   **2025 World Expo:** Hosted "Moutai Night" and released the "Walk Into Series" (5 new products for 5 different countries), combining brand elements with local cultural features.
*   **USA Anniversary:** In 2025, celebrated the 110th anniversary of the Panama-Pacific International Exposition award and the 10th anniversary of San Francisco "Moutai Day," hosting a cultural special exhibition at the Palace of Fine Arts.
*   **South Korea Launch:** In October 2026, "Moutai Night" entered Seoul for the first time. The first promotional video for the overseas "Feitian Series," titled *"Warmth, Connecting the World,"* was released.
*   **Global Media:** The overseas "6+1" new media matrix achieved over **510 million** cumulative exposures on social media platforms from 2025 to the present.
*   **Factory Tours:** In 2025, the "Enter Moutai · Super Factory Tour" attracted over **400 guests** from **28 countries and regions**.

**6. Industry Status**
*   In 2026, the brewing industry was explicitly designated as a **"Historic Classic Industry"** in China.

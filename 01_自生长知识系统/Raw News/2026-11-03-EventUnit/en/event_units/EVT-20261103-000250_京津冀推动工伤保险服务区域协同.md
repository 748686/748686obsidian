# Event Name

Jing-Jin-Ji (Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei) Regional Collaboration on Work-Related Injury Insurance Services

## Event Overview

On November 2, 2026, a signing ceremony for the cooperation memorandum on the co-construction and sharing of the Xiong’an Labor Capacity Appraisal Base was held in Xiong’an New Area, Hebei Province. This event marks a strategic shift in the Jing-Jin-Ji region’s social security framework, aiming to transition work-related injury insurance services from purely territorial processing to regional协同 (synergy). The initiative seeks to establish a model of human resources and social security collaboration that is fair, just, convenient, and responsive to public needs.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Signing:** November 2, 2026.
*   **Location of Ceremony:** Xiong’an New Area, Hebei Province.
*   **Participating Entities:** Representatives from Beijing, Tianjin, and Hebei provinces/municipalities.
*   **Key Agreement:** Cooperation memorandum for the co-construction and sharing of the Xiong’an Labor Capacity Appraisal Base.
*   **Primary Objective:** To promote the transformation of work-related injury insurance services from local/territorial handling to regional collaboration.
*   **Guiding Principles:** Carrier co-construction, resource sharing, complementary advantages, efficiency and convenience, standardized processes, and clear rights and responsibilities.
*   **Operational Focus:** Integrated advancement of work-related injury recognition, labor capacity appraisal, and consultation on complex cases.
*   **Support Mechanism:** Beijing, Tianjin, and Hebei will support Xiong’an in hardware construction, informatization, and expert pools to create a standardized and digital collaborative demonstration base.
*   **Benefit to Citizens:** Enhanced delegation of appraisal authority allows applicants from all three regions to apply for labor capacity appraisal nearby, reducing the need for cross-regional travel and alleviating burdens on enterprises and employees.
*   **Concurrent Activity:** An expert business training and seminar on labor capacity appraisal was held simultaneously with the signing.
*   **Reported By:** Pan Junqiang (Pan Junqiang), People's Daily.

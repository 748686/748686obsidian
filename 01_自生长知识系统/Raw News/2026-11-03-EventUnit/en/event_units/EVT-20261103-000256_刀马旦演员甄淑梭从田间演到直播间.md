## Event Overview
On November 3, 2026, *People's Daily* published a feature article titled "Wherever There is Love in the Heart, Every Place is a Stage" (心中常怀热爱，何处不是舞台), detailing the life and career of Zhen Shusuo, a 55-year-old Dao Ma Dan (female warrior role) opera performer from Mayang Village, Quyang County, Hebei Province. The article highlights her journey from rigorous childhood training in opera to nearly a decade of supporting roles in rural troupes, her struggle and eventual success in Zhejiang province, and her recent transformation into a viral internet personality who performs traditional arts in agricultural settings.

## Core Facts
*   **Subject:** Zhen Shusuo (甄淑梭), 55 years old (as of Nov 2026).
*   **Role:** Dao Ma Dan opera performer, specializing in martial female roles.
*   **Origin:** Mayang Village, Quyang County, Baoding City, Hebei Province.
*   **Early Training:** Began opera training at age 13. Attended an opera school with sparse conditions (converted factory dormitories, straw beds). Trained daily despite harsh weather (frozen, bleeding feet in single pairs of sneakers).
*   **Professional Career Start:** Joined Tangxian Bangzi Troupe in Hebei. Spent approximately 10 years performing supporting roles (long-tao/jingong) in rural theaters, often without lines or visible face time, but valuing every stage opportunity.
*   **Move to Zhejiang:** In 1994, moved with her husband (also an opera actor) to Wenzhou, Zhejiang ("Hometown of Southern Opera").
*   **Early Struggle in Zhejiang:** Initial performances were marked by errors (dropped weapons, failed somersaults). She cried backstage and considered returning home. Encouraged by a fellow actor to persist, she practiced intensively for half a month.
*   **Success in Zhejiang:** Gained local fame for high-difficulty skills such as picking up spears with flag-adorned headdresses (kaoqi) and spinning swords. At her peak, performed over 60 shows per month.
*   **Return and Livestreaming:** Returned to Quyang in 2020. Started livestreaming practice sessions in her courtyard, fields, and near stoves upon her son’s suggestion.
*   **Viral Moment:** On the 7th day of livestreaming, gained over 10,000 concurrent viewers. A video of her sword play exceeded 10 million plays.
*   **Online Persona:** Operates the account "Zhen Ni Dao Ma Dan" (甄妮刀马旦).
*   **Current Status (2026):** Has accumulated over 3 million followers across platforms. Maintains a catalog of thousands of short videos showing practice in farming attire or after chores. Continues to perform公益 (public welfare) shows in remote villages and teaches children basic skills.
*   **Source:** People's Daily (paper.people.com.cn), Article ID 30189072, Page 11 (Culture Section).

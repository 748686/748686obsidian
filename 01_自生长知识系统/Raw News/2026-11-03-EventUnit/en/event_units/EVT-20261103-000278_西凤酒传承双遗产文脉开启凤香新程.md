On November 3, 2026, an advertisement titled "Inheriting the 'Dual Heritage' Cultural Context to Start a New Chapter for Feng Flavor" (*Chengchuan 'Shuang Yichan' Wenmai, Kaiqi Fengxiang Xin Cheng*) was published in the 19th edition (Advertising) of *People's Daily*. ## Core Facts
*   **Publication Date:** November 3, 2026.
*   **Source Medium:** *People's Daily*, Page 19 (Advertisement).
*   **Subject:** Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Factory Group Co., Ltd. (data source cited in article).
*   **Heritage Status:**
    *   In 2021, "Xifeng Liquor Brewing Technique" (*Xifeng Jiu Niangzao Jiyi*) was included in the 5th Batch of National Intangible Cultural Heritage Representative Project List.
    *   In 2021, "Xifeng Liquor Factory" was included in the 5th Batch of National Industrial Heritage List.
    *   The entity is described as maintaining an unbroken 3,000-year lineage of brewing culture and craftsmanship.
*   **Infrastructure & Preservation:**
    *   **Xifeng Liquor Culture Museum:** Opened in September 2025. Utilizes historical artifacts, scene复原 (restoration), and digital technology to display the 3,000-year brewing history.
    *   **Wine Sea Warehouse (*Jiu Hai Ku*):** Houses a 10,000-ton capacity warehouse featuring ancient "Wine Seas" (storage containers) hand-woven from Qinling荆条 (blackberry branches) with interiors lined with over a hundred layers of paper. These are still actively used for the natural aging of liquor.
    *   **Industrial Tourism:** Transitioned from simple factory visits to an experiential model combining cultural tourism and industry, based on the museum, brewing workshops, and wine sea warehouses.
*   **International Expansion ("Brand Going Global"):**
    *   Trademarks successfully registered in 20 countries and regions.
    *   Products exported to more than 30 countries and regions.
    *   Sales networks established in Southeast Asia, Europe, and Africa.
*   **Policy Context:** The article references the "15th Five-Year Plan for Cultural Heritage Protection and Inheritance" (*Wenhua Yichan Baohu Chuancheng "Shiwuwu" Guihua*), stated to be the first five-year plan specifically in the field of cultural heritage protection in China. Key principles cited include "protection first," "reasonable utilization," "minimal intervention," and the coordinated protection of material and intangible cultural heritage.

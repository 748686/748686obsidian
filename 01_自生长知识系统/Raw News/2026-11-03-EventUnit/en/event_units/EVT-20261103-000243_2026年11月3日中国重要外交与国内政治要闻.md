## Event Overview

On November 2-3, 2026, the People's Daily (Page 03) published a comprehensive collection of Chinese political and diplomatic news. The coverage spans high-level diplomatic engagements, regional multilateral conferences, bilateral state visits, and significant domestic political appointments and ideological mobilization. These events collectively reflect China’s simultaneous focus on Middle Eastern peace initiatives, regional economic cooperation (APEC, Mekong-Lancang), and internal party discipline following the 20th Central Committee's Fifth Plenary Session.

## Core Facts

**1. Diplomatic Engagements with Iran and Saudi Arabia (Nov 2)**
*   **Iran:** Wang Yi (Member of the Politburo Standing Committee, Director of the Office of the Central Foreign Affairs Commission) met with Bagheri, Deputy Secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council, in Beijing.
    *   *China's Position:* Emphasized that dialogue and negotiation are the only solutions to the protracted and escalating conflict in the Middle East. Supported Iran’s safeguarding of its sovereignty and security.
    *   *Iran's Position:* Expressed appreciation for President Xi Jinping’s initiatives and China’s consistent stance on multilateralism; called on China to play a more important role in international and regional peace.
*   **Saudi Arabia:** Wang Yi held talks with Aiban (State Minister, Cabinet Member, and National Security Advisor) in Beijing.
    *   *Key Development:* Aiban delivered a letter from Crown Prince and Premier Mohammed bin Salman to President Xi Jinping regarding bilateral relations and shared concerns.
    *   *China's Position:* Noted the steady development of China-Saudi relations under strategic guidance from top leaders. Stated the region is at a critical juncture between war and peace, urging dialogue. Reiterated commitment to President Xi’s initiatives and coordination with regional actors, including Saudi Arabia.

**2. State Visit by Gabon’s President (Nov 3-7)**
*   Brice Clotaire Oligui Nguema, President of the Gabonese Republic (aged 51, in office since May 2025), began a state visit to China on November 3.
*   This is President Nguema’s first state visit to China since assuming office.

**3. Multilateral Conference Leadership (Nov 3-4)**
*   **APEC:** Wang Yi and Wang Wentao (Minister of Commerce) co-chaired the 37th APEC Ministers Responsible for Trade and Diplomacy Video Conference on November 3 in Beijing.
*   **Mekong-Lancang Cooperation (MLC):** Wang Yi hosted the 11th MLC Foreign Ministers’ Meeting in Beijing from November 3-4.

**4. China-Oman Economic Cooperation (Nov 2)**
*   Mu Hong (Vice Chairperson of the National Committee of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference - CPPCC) attended the China-Oman Investment Cooperation Promotion Conference in Beijing.
*   Mu Hong held exchanges with Oman’s Sultan Haitham bin Tariq, who attended the promotion conference, regarding China-Oman cooperation.

**5. Party and State Leadership Engagements (Nov 2)**
*   **Russia:** Wang Huning (Member of the Politburo Standing Committee, Chairman of the CPPCC) met with Valentina Matviyenko, Speaker of the Federation Council of the Russian Federation.
    *   *China's Position:* Emphasized steady development of China-Russia comprehensive strategic partnership under President Xi and President Putin’s guidance. Committed to deepening pragmatic cooperation and strengthening CPPCC-russia interactions. Attendees included Qin Qinghua and Wang Dongfeng.
*   **Vietnam:** Wang Huning also met with a Vietnamese Communist Party delegation led by Nguyen Thanh Nghi (Member of the Politburo, Secretary of the Central Secretariat, Minister of the Central Policy and Strategy Commission).
    *   *China's Position:* Highlighted close communication between President Xi and General Secretary Su Lin. Committed to implementing high-level consensus, exchanging governance experience, and building a higher-level China-Vietnam community with a shared future. Attendee included Wang Yong.

**6. Domestic Political Appointment**
*   Liu Qifan was appointed Acting Governor of Yunnan Province.

**7. Ideological Mobilization**
*   **Event:** The mobilization meeting for the Central Propaganda Group for Studying and Implementing the Spirit of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee was held in Beijing on November 2.
*   **Key Speaker:** Cai Qi (Member of the Politburo Standing Committee, Secretary of the Secretariat) delivered a speech.
*   **Core Message:** Studying the Plenary Session spirit is a major political task. The session’s "Decision" is a programmatic document for promoting party governance and national rejuvenation in the new era.
*   **Objectives:** Guide cadres to align thoughts and actions with the Plenary Session spirit, strengthen the "Two Establishes," enhance "Four Consciousnesses," firm up "Four Confidences," and achieve "Two Upholds." Prepare for the 21st National Congress of the CPC through rigorous party governance.
*   **Attendees:** Li Shulei (Minister of Publicity) presided over the meeting; Chen Yiqin (State Councilor) attended. Members of the Central Leading Group for Propaganda, Ideology, and Culture, central propaganda group members, and heads of central propaganda and culture units were present.

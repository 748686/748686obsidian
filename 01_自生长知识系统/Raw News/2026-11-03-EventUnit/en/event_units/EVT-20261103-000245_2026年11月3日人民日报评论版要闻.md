# Event Unit: People's Daily Commentary Edition (November 3, 2026)

## Event Overview

On November 3, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive commentary and knowledge section (Page 05) covering strategic national development themes. The content synthesizes four distinct articles addressing: 1) Systematic approaches to cultivating "new quality productive forces" (新质生产力), 2) Rural infrastructure development from "peaceful waters" to "transportation arteries," 3) Ethical governance of artificial intelligence, and 4) Deep-sea exploration capabilities. The edition serves as policy commentary aligning with the upcoming "15th Five-Year Plan" period (2026-2030).

## Core Facts

### 1. Cultivation of New Quality Productive Forces
*(Source: Article #374 - "Building a Solid Development Foundation through Systematic Layout")*

*   **Strategic Directive:** The Central Committee and State Council’s "Opinions on Developing New Quality Productive Forces" state: "One must not blindly seek novelty or neglect other aspects" (不能盲目求新、顾此失彼).
*   **C919 Progress:** Domestic large aircraft C919 achieved three milestones in 2026:
    *   August: First international route successfully opened.
    *   September: First regular commercial flight executed at Qingdao Airport.
    *   October: Delivery of the 100th set of power distribution system components.
*   **Traditional Industry Role:** Traditional industries account for approximately 80% of all manufacturing indicators (output value, employment). They are described as the "basic plate" (基本盘) and should not be abandoned for new industries.
*   **Case Studies:**
    *   **Baosteel (Shanghai):** AI integration reduced annual blast furnace costs by approximately 15 million RMB.
    *   **Angang Steel (Anshan):** Silicon steel production lines operate on 100% green electricity.
    *   **Memory Chips:** Changxin Technology and Yangtze Memory Technologies (YMTC) submitted applications for STAR Market (科创板) listing. Hefei (Anhui) and Wuhan (Hubei) provided long-term policy support without frequent changes ("不翻烧饼").
    *   **Open Innovation:** DeepSeek (深度求索) gained global attention by fully open-sourcing its internal reasoning framework.
    *   **International Cooperation:** Airbus Tianjin assembly line expanded from one to two lines, engaging ~200 Chinese suppliers; Siemens Healthineers built a high-end medical equipment R&D base in Shenzhen.

### 2. Childcare and Social Support
*(Source: Article #375 - "Picture Report")*

*   **Current Statistics (as of end-2025):** China has 126,000 childcare service institutions and 6.66 million childcare slots.
*   **Policy Direction:** During the "15th Five-Year Plan" period, the government will promote the integration of nursery and kindergarten services (托幼一体化). Support will be given to communities and employers to establish affordable childcare services.
*   **Visual Evidence:** A photo shows children in a childcare class at a kindergarten in Lengshuitan District, Yongzhou City, Hunan Province (Photo by Lei Zhongxiang).

### 3. AI Ethics and Governance
*(Source: Article #376 - "'People Who Have Been There' vs. 'Face-Thieves'")*

*   **Contrasting Phenomena:**
    *   **Positive:** The song "Guolairen" (过来人) went viral due to AI-generated faces of ancient figures, evoking public empathy.
    *   **Negative:** Two criminals used AI-generated videos from stolen photos to bypass facial recognition on shopping platforms, stealing account information.
*   **Risks Identified:** Privacy leakage, over-reliance on AI for writing/exams, addiction to low-quality AI short dramas, and fraud via deepfake face-swapping and voice cloning.
*   **Governance Framework (Analogy to Taming the Heavenly Steed):**
    1.  **"Xiangma" (Appraising Horses):** Recognize AI’s purpose is to extend human senses and liberate people from repetitive labor, not to weaken critical thinking or social responsibility.
    2.  **"Tiaosheng" (Training/Vocalizing):** Ensure AI enhances well-being and efficiency rather than anxiety; embed safety, fairness, and向善 (goodness) into R&D.
    3.  **"Bianji" (Diagnosing Illness):** Establish legal red lines and ethical baselines to prevent造假 (counterfeiting) and infringement.

### 4. Deep-Sea Exploration
*(Source: Article #377 - "Towards the Deep Sea, Towards the Future")*

*   **Milestone Achievements:**
    *   **"Mengxiang" (Dream) Ship:** Completed trial drilling in the South China Sea with a maximum final drilling depth of 4,929.17 meters, breaking China’s record for autonomous deep-water drilling.
    *   **Manned Submersibles:** The trio "Jiaolong," "Shenhai Yongshi" (Deep Sea Warrior), and "Fendouzhe" (Striver) are expected to exceed 2,000 cumulative dives in 2026.
    *   **Research Facilities:** The "Deep and Distant Sea All-Weather Stationed Floating Research Facility" project entered a new engineering construction phase.
*   **Resource Statistics:**
    *   67% of over 100 globally discovered oil/gas blocks in the last 10 years are in deep sea, accounting for 68% of reserves.
    *   Polymetallic nodules containing manganese, copper, cobalt, and nickel are abundant and often higher grade than land deposits.
    *   Offshore wind energy in deep seas is a key source for future capacity expansion.
*   **Policy Context:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" outline proposes: "Implement deep-sea engineering and enhance capabilities in deep-sea access, exploration, development, and safety."
*   **Technological Innovations:**
    *   **"Shenhai No.1" Energy Station:** R&D team spent ~2 years on parameter analysis and model experiments to design a platform that meets gas field production needs while storing condensate oil.
    *   **Qingdao Pengpai Ocean Exploration Technology Co.:** Uses "simulation training + AI forecast large model + multi-layer protection" to improve deep-sea robot reliability.
*   **Institutional Reforms:**
    *   **Hainan:** Created a shared voyage platform for deep-sea technology equipment sea trials to reduce costs.
    *   **Guangdong & Shanghai:** Developing thousand-billion-yuan marine industry clusters covering materials, equipment, and resource development.
    *   **"Mengxiang" Ship:** Designed and built with collaboration from over 150 units, demonstrating the advantage of集中力量办大事 (concentrating resources to accomplish major tasks).

## Event Conclusion

The November 3, 2026, commentary edition of *People's Daily* presents a cohesive strategic narrative for China’s development during the pre-"15th Five-Year Plan" period. It advocates for a **systematic, balanced approach** to economic modernization: preserving traditional industrial strength while innovating in deep tech (C919, memory chips, deep-sea exploration). Simultaneously, it calls for **ethical governance of AI**, using the metaphor of taming the Heavenly Steed to argue for human-centric regulation. The edition underscores that China’s path to modernization relies on **self-reliance combined with openness**, and on institutional innovation to support long-cycle, high-risk technological endeavors.

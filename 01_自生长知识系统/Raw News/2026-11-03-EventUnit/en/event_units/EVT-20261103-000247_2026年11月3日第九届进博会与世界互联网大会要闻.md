## Event Overview

On November 2–3, 2026, concurrent major cultural, political, and technological events unfolded in China. The ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE) preparatory services were being finalized in Shanghai. The list of 2026 "National Top Ten Journalists" was announced in Beijing. In Jiaxing/Wuzhen, Zhejiang, the World Internet Conference's "Light of the Internet" Expo opened, followed by the announcement of the Leading Technology Awards. Additionally, the 9th Dunhuang Cultural Expo took place in Dunhuang, Gansu, focusing on cultural heritage and tourism integration. These events were covered comprehensively in the People's Daily on November 3, 2026, specifically on pages 07 and 08.

## Core Facts

### 1. Ninth China International Import Expo (Shanghai)
*   **Status:** City support preparations are complete; the expo is proceeding with established service guarantees. (Article 380)
*   **Scale:** 32 municipal support groups are focusing on 257 key urban support service tasks. (Article 380)
*   **Customs & Logistics:** Implementation of 18 customs facilitation measures and 123 special windows/channels. European jewelry and watch exhibits utilize the "CIIE Express" bonded warehouse policy for pre-exhibition viewing ("arrival at port enters warehouse, warehouse enters pre-exhibition"). (Article 380)
*   **Technology:** Venue deployed 1,400 Wi-Fi 7 units with 5G-A 10-gigabit network coverage, supporting 200,000 simultaneous online users for high-definition live streaming. (Article 380)
*   **Facilities:** Negotiation activity zones increased to 19, with an area three times that of the previous session. (Article 380)
*   **Services:** New AI-powered travel assistant "Xiao Jiao" launched for integrated parking, shuttle, and transit services. "Jinbo Shi" APP added an intelligent agent for navigation. Over 4,561 "Little Leaves" volunteers are stationed, with over 100 proficient in French, German, and Spanish. (Article 380)
*   **Economic Strategy:** Shanghai aims to transform CIIE popularity into 365-day "consumption traffic" and "development increment," positioning itself as a global "premiere site" for new products. (Article 380)

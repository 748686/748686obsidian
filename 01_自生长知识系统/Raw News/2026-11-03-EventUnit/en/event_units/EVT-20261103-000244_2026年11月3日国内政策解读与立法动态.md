### 2. Legislative Agenda: NPCSC 25th Session
*   **Event:** The 25th Session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress convened on November 2, 2026, reviewing multiple legal drafts (Reference: Article #370/371).
*   **Criminal Procedure Law Amendment Draft (55 Articles):**
    *   **Principles:** Refines the presumption of innocence and enhances protection for criminal victims.
    *   **Coercive Measures:** Introduces overall requirements for the application of coercive measures, mandating that arrest approvals involve questioning the suspect and listening to defense lawyers. It aims to prevent excessive detention.
    *   **Victim Rights:** Grants victims the right to legal aid if they cannot afford representation and allows victim lawyers to access case files. Courts may request police assistance if victims face difficulties collecting evidence for cases requiring private prosecution.
    *   **Defense Rights:** Expands legal aid notification for cases tried under ordinary procedures. Clarifies that duty lawyers have rights to了解案件情况, meet suspects, and review files.
    *   **Juvenile Proceedings:** Mandates legal aid for underage victims and allows suitable adults as guarantors for minors on bail. Requires one-time questioning of underage victims/witnesses to protect privacy.
*   **Renewable Energy Law Revision Draft (10 Chapters, 76 Articles):**
    *   **Scope Expansion:** Explicitly includes environmental heat and hydrogen energy.
    *   **Principles:** Establishes "priority development and utilization of renewable energy" and principles of government guidance/market dominance.
    *   **Grid & Market:** Mandates the construction of new power grids integrating main, distribution, and micro-grids. Establishes a unified national power market system.
    *   **Consumption Mechanism:** Introduces a mandatory and voluntary consumption mix, including renewable energy consumption responsibility weights and green electricity certificate systems.
*   **Tax Collection and Administration Law Revision Draft (6 Chapters, 100 Articles):**
    *   **Individual Tax Collection:** Allows tax authorities to enforce coercive measures against individuals with huge unpaid tax balances, subject to approval.
    *   **Platform Economy:** Mandates internet platform enterprises to handle tax declarations for operators and practitioners on their platforms.
    *   **Big Data:** Empowers tax authorities to use big data for risk analysis of taxpayer declarations.
    *   **Debt Recovery:** Allows direct recovery from shareholders who failed to fulfill capital contribution obligations or withdrew funds if the taxpayer cannot pay.
    *   **Law Enforcement Norms:** Prohibits entering personal residences for enforcement; requires retaining housing and necessities for the taxpayer and dependents.
    *   **Late Fees:** Caps late payment fees at no more than double the original tax amount.

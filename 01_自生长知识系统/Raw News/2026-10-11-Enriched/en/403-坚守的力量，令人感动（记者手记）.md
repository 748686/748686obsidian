### [China plans 11,356 teachers for rural support program](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content//content_30184826.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Finance jointly issued a notice on October 9 to implement the 2026 "Three Zones" Talent Support Program Teacher Special Plan. For the 2026-2027 academic year, the program targets formerly impoverished areas, with priority given to national rural revitalization key assistance counties, ethnic counties, revolutionary base counties, and border counties, planning to dispatch 11,356 teachers. Selected personnel generally must hold intermediate or higher professional titles, serve full-time for one year, and can be encouraged to extend their service. To support this, central and local governments will share deployment costs at a per-capita standard of 20,000 yuan annually for compulsory education teachers in receiving counties.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 10, 01:56

**「Background」** The "Three Zones" \(sanqu\) refers to central and western regions, border areas, and poverty-stricken counties in China that require targeted educational support to bridge resource gaps with developed areas. This special teacher plan is part of broader national strategies to promote rural revitalization and equitable access to quality education through structured talent deployment.

**「Impact」** Schools in designated rural and border counties will receive over 11,000 additional qualified teachers for the 2026-2027 academic year, directly addressing staffing shortages in critical educational regions. The policy creates career advancement opportunities for mid-career educators willing to serve in underserved areas while establishing a funded mechanism for sustainable teacher deployment.

### [China establishes 45 national hazmat emergency rescue teams](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content//content_30184828.html) ⭐️ ?/10

As of October 2026, China has built 580 hazardous chemical emergency rescue teams totaling more than 41,000 personnel, including 45 national-level teams that now cover key chemical regions and major risk sites, according to an October 10 report by the Ministry of Emergency Management. The structure combines enterprise-formed forces, local government teams, and nationally planned teams, supported by updated regulations such as the Emergency Preparedness Guidelines for Hazardous Chemical Enterprises and central funding for over 1,000 sets of advanced equipment. Since the first National Hazardous Chemical Rescue Skills Competition, these national teams have dispatched more than 41,000 personnel to 4,137 incidents and rescued 674 trapped individuals.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 10, 01:56

**「Background」** Hazardous chemical emergency rescue systems are specialized public-safety networks trained to respond to industrial spills, fires, and toxic releases. In China, these systems are coordinated through the Ministry of Emergency Management and typically combine enterprise, municipal, provincial, and national tiers.

**「Impact」** Chemical enterprises and local governments in designated high-risk zones should expect more standardized national support and equipment access for incident response. Regulatory compliance efforts may increasingly align with the newly published emergency preparedness guidelines.

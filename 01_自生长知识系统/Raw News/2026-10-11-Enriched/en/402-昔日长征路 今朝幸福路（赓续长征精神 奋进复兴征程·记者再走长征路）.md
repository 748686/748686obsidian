Published on October 10, 2026, by People&\#x27;s Daily, this article highlights the 80th anniversary of Party-led Yellow River governance and profiles the "Amala" Women&\#x27;s Volunteer River Patrol Team in Ruoergai County, Sichuan. Led by executive captain Si Hongying, the group of 74 local Tibetan women has conducted over 7,600 patrol sessions, removing more than 270 tons of waste and planting over 14,600 drought-resistant saplings across 750 mu of land. **「Background」** The Yellow River originates from the Bayan Har Mountains and is considered China&\#x27;s cradle of civilization; Ruoergai County at its upper reaches is known for the river&\#x27;s dramatic first bend. In 2014, the Chinese government elevated the ecological protection and high-quality development of the Yellow River basin to a national strategy, emphasizing water conservation and systematic environmental governance.

**「Impact」** The team&\#x27;s sustained volunteerism provides a replicable grassroots model for protecting vulnerable upper-reach wetlands against plastic pollution and bank degradation.

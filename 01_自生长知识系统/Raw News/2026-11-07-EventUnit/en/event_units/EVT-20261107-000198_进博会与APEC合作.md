## Event Overview
On November 7, 2026, *People's Daily* published a special report section covering major trade exhibitions. The primary focus of the synthesis event is the cooperation between the 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE) and the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC), as framed by the editorial titled "Embracing Asia-Pacific, Connecting the World." The publication simultaneously reported on the successful closure of the 140th Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair), highlighting its role in China's opening-up and Guangzhou's economic vitality.

## Core Facts
*   **Event Timing:** The reporting date is November 7, 2026.
*   **CIIE and APEC:** The 9th China International Import Expo is characterized by its cooperation with APEC and its provision of "opening dividends" (Article #341).
*   **Canton Fair Closure:** The 140th Canton Fair closed on November 4, 2026 (Article #342).
*   **Canton Fair Statistics:**
    *   Held across 3 phases from October 15 to November 4, 2026.
    *   Attracted 352,000 overseas buyers from 219 countries and regions, an increase of 12.2% over the previous session, setting a new historical record for attendance (Article #342).
    *   Over 30,000 enterprises participated (Article #342).
*   **Canton Fair Anniversary:** The 140th edition marked the 70th anniversary of the Canton Fair's establishment (first held in Spring 1957) (Article #342).
*   **Guangzhou Metrics:**
    *   First nine months of 2026: Total retail sales of consumer goods increased by 2.5% year-on-year; catering revenue increased by 5.3% (leading among first-tier cities) (Article #342).
    *   Baiyun International Airport passenger throughput reached 67.0905 million in the first three quarters, ranking first nationally (Article #342).
    *   Guangzhou South Railway Station is noted as the first railway station in China to exceed one million daily passenger arrivals and departures on a single day (Article #342).
*   **Urban Development:** The Guangzhou International Commercial Port construction commenced one week prior to the 140th Canton Fair opening (Article #342).

# Event: Conclusion of the 25th Session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress and Related Diplomatic Developments

## Event Overview

On November 6, 2026, the 25th session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress (NPC) concluded in Beijing. The session resulted in the passage of multiple laws, an international agreement regarding AI cooperation, and significant personnel appointments and dismissals within the Chinese government and legislative body. The conclusions were reported by *People's Daily* on November 7, 2026.

## Core Facts

**Legislative Actions:**
*   The NPC Standing Committee passed decisions on fifteen laws, including:
    *   The Procuratorial Public Interest Litigation Law.
    *   The Antarctic Activities and Environmental Protection Law.
    *   The newly revised Water Law.
    *   The Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law.
    *   Amendments to the Emergency Response Law.
*   President Xi Jinping signed Presidential Decrees Nos. 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, and 91 promulgating these laws.
*   The NPC Standing Committee approved the decision to ratify the "Agreement on the Establishment of the World AI Cooperation Organization."

**Personnel Changes:**
*   **Dismissal:** Li Lecheng was dismissed from his position as Minister of Industry and Information Technology.
*   **Appointments:**
    *   Wang Junzheng and Wang Ning appointed as Vice Chairs of the Ethnic Affairs Committee.
    *   Wang Kai appointed as Vice Chair of the监察 and Judicial Committee.
    *   Gong Zheng and Mao Weiming appointed as Vice Chairs of the Financial and Economic Committee.
    *   Liang Yanshun and Wang Yubo appointed as Vice Chairs of the Education, Science, Culture and Public Health Committee.
    *   Liu Ning, Ren Zhenhe, and Wang Zhengpu appointed as Vice Chairs of the Agriculture and Rural Affairs Committee.
    *   Shen Xiaoming and Wang Qingxian appointed as Vice Chairs of the Social Development Committee.
    *   Chen Fuli's request to resign from his position as Secretary-General of the NPC Standing Committee and as a committee member was accepted.

**Session Details:**
*   The session was presided over by Zhao Leji, Chairman of the NPC Standing Committee.
*   154 members of the Standing Committee attended, constituting a quorum.
*   A lecture was delivered by Gao Xiang, President of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, on the theme of studying the spirit of the 20th Central Committee's Fifth Plenary Session and advancing strict Party governance.

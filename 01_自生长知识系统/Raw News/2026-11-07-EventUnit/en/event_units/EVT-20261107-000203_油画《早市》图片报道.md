    *   Leaves are opposite, ovate-cordate, three fingers wide.

    *   **Shaoxing Style:** Vinegar-glazed *Ji Cai* (醋熘蕺菜). Tender stems/leaves are washed, salted, squeezed dry, and mixed with fine salt, ginger, sesame oil, and rice vinegar.
    *   **Health Use:** The author's father consumed *Ji Cai* tea for lung heat and coughs.
    *   **Sha County (Fujian) Style:**
        *   *Fish Mint Rice Water Soup* (鱼腥草饭汁): Rice is half-boiled, strained; the remaining liquid (rice water) is used to boil chopped blanched *Ji Cai*.
        *   *Fish Mint Small Intestine Soup*: Pork small intestines are stewed in mountain spring water for one hour, then *Ji Cai* is added and steamed for ten+ minutes.
5.  **Cultural Commentary:** The author links the resilience of *Ji Cai* (growing back after cutting) to the spirit of Shaxian snacks (such as crystal siu mai and wontons) spreading nationally, suggesting a "noble character" (浩然之气) akin to the historical perseverance of Yue.

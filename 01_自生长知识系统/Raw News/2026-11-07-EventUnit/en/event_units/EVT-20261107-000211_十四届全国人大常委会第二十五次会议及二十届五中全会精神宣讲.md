## Event Overview
On November 6, 2026, the 25th Meeting of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress concluded in Beijing. During and immediately following the session, a series of high-level political, legislative, and diplomatic activities took place. These included the promulgation of Presidential Decrees regarding diplomatic appointments, the approval of international agreements, the passage of amendments to fifteen domestic laws, personnel appointments within the Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate, and a constitutional oath-taking ceremony. Concurrently, the Central Propaganda Team for the 5th Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee conducted propaganda activities across multiple regions and departments to implement the spirit of the Plenary Session, which focused on comprehensive and rigorous party governance.

## Core Facts

### 1. Legislative Achievements
*   **Law Amendments:** The Standing Committee passed a decision to amend fifteen laws, including the Emergency Response Law, Work Safety Law, Island Protection Law, Occupational Disease Prevention Law, and Wildlife Protection Law. A significant trend in these amendments was the replacement of references to the "Environmental Protection Law" with the "Ecological Environment Code" (or similar ecological codes), indicating a systematic legal codification process.
*   **New Legislation:** The meeting passed five new laws:
    1.  Procuratorial Public Interest Litigation Law.
    2.  Antarctic Activities and Environmental Protection Law.
    3.  Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law.
    4.  Revised Water Law.
*   **International Agreements:** The Standing Committee approved the "Agreement on the Establishment of the World AI Cooperation Organization," signed by Chinese representatives in Shanghai on July 16, 2026.

### 2. Personnel Appointments and Removals
*   **Diplomatic Appointments (Presidential Decree):** President Xi Jinping, based on the Standing Committee's decision, appointed ambassadors to Norway, Czech Republic, Italy/San Marino, Kiribati, Fiji, and New Zealand/Cook Islands/Niue. Notably, Hou Yue (female) was reassigned from Norway to Italy/San Marino.
*   **NPC Special Committee Appointments:** Wang Junzheng, Wang Ning, Wang Kai, Gong Zheng, Mao Weiming, Liang Yanshun, Wang Yubo, Liu Ning, Ren Zhenhe, Wang Zhengpu, Shen Xiaoming, and Wang Qingxian were appointed as Deputy Directors of various NPC special committees (Ethnic, Supervisory, Financial, Education/Science/Culture/Health, Agriculture, Social Construction).
*   **Judicial Personnel:**
    *   **Supreme People's Court:** Dang Guangsuo appointed as Chief Judge of the Fifth Circuit Court; Yang Yongqing appointed as Chief Judge of the Civil Trial Tribunal; Lan Xiangdong appointed as Judge of the Trial Supervision Tribunal.
    *   **Supreme People's Procuratorate:** Chen Yifang (female) appointed as Member of the Procuratorial Committee and Procurator. Twenty-one new procurators were appointed.
*   **Resignations:** Chen Fuli accepted as resignation from Standing Committee member and Secretary-General duties. Multiple NPC deputies (Ye Jianchun, He Xiong, Ji Tao, Huang Yi, Xu Renbiao, Zhang Gong, Guo Jianjun, Wu Yuhong, Meng Qingbin) had their qualifications terminated due to removal or resignation.

### 3. Constitutional Oath
*   On the morning of November 6, a constitutional oath-taking ceremony was held at the Great Hall of the People. Li Hongzhong, Vice Chairman of the NPC Standing Committee, presided over and witnessed the oath for the newly appointed deputy directors of NPC special committees.

### 4. Closing Remarks by Zhao Leji
*   NPC Standing Committee Chairman Zhao Leji presided over the closing session. He emphasized:
    *   The significance of the new laws (Antarctic governance, anti-corruption, water security).
    *   The importance of the World AI Cooperation Organization agreement for global AI governance.
    *   The need to study and implement the spirit of the 5th Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee and Xi Jinping's thought on Party building.

### 5. Central Propaganda Team Activities
*   A Central Propaganda Team for the 5th Plenary Session conducted宣讲 (propaganda/lecture) activities on November 6 in:
    *   **Beijing:** Delivered by He Lifeng (Vice Premier) in分管领域 (his assigned sectors).
    *   **Tianjin:** Delivered by Chen Min'er (Municipal Party Secretary).
    *   **Chongqing:** Delivered by Yuan Jiajun (Municipal Party Secretary).
    *   **Jiangsu (Nanjing):** Delivered by Guo Wenqi (Central and State Organs Working Committee).
    *   **Henan (Zhengzhou):** Delivered by Zang Anmin (Former Secretary-General of the Central Organization Department).
*   **Key Theme:** The propaganda focused on "persevering in comprehensive and rigorous Party governance," implementing Xi Jinping's thought on Party building, and answering the "Yenan Dialogue" question on跳出历史周期率 (escaping the historical cycle of rise and fall).

### 6. Diplomatic and Executive Meetings
*   **State Council:** Premier Li Qiang presided over a State Council Executive Meeting on November 6 to discuss the economic situation and social assistance.
*   **NPC Standing Committee Leadership:** Zhao Leji met with Gabon President Etienne Nguel and Austrian Chancellor Starhemberg.
*   **Party Diplomacy:** Liu Baixing (Minister of United Front Work Department) met with Kim Sung-nam of the Workers' Party of Korea.

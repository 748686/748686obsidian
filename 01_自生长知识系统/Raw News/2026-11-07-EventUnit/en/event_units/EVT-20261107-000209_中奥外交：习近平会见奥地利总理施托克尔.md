# Event: Diplomatic Engagement Between China and Austria (Xi Jinping Meets Austrian Chancellor Stöcker)

## Event Overview
On November 6, 2026, Chinese State Council Premier Li Qiang held talks with Austrian Chancellor Stöcker, who was on an official visit to China. The meeting took place at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing. This engagement is part of a broader series of high-level diplomatic activities involving Austria, including separate meetings with other Chinese leaders. The event coincides with the 55th anniversary of diplomatic relations between China and Austria.

## Core Facts
*   **Date:** November 6, 2026 (reported in *People's Daily* on November 7, 2026).
*   **Location:** Great Hall of the People, Beijing, China.
*   **Key Participants:**
    *   Li Qiang, Premier of the State Council of the People's Republic of China.
    *   Stöcker, Chancellor of Austria.
    *   Wu Zhenglong (present at the meeting).
*   **Context:** Commemoration of the 55th anniversary of China-Austria diplomatic relations.
*   **Agreed Actions:** Both sides agreed to sign multiple cooperation documents covering trade, economy, social security, artificial intelligence, investment, tourism, and culture.
*   **Political Context:** The visit is framed within a context of global economic difficulty due to prolonged geopolitical conflicts, rising unilateralism, and protectionism. Both nations are characterized as supporters of multilateralism and free trade.

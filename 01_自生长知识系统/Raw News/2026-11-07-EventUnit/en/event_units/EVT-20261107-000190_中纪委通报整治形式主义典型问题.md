# Event Name
Joint Notification of Three Typical Issues on Rectifying Formalism and Reducing Burdens on the Grassroots Level

## Event Overview
On November 6, 2026, the Special Working Mechanism Office for Rectifying Formalism and Reducing Burdens on the Grassroots Level at the Central Level, jointly with the General Office of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI), issued a public notification regarding three typical issues concerning the rectification of formalism and the reduction of burdens on the grassroots level. The notification was published in *People's Daily* on November 7, 2026. The event highlights systemic failures in data integrity, project fund management, and state-owned enterprise performance evaluation, attributing these issues to a lack of implementation of the "Several Provisions on Rectifying Formalism to Reduce Burdens on the Grassroots Level" and inadequate execution of the "Six Rectifications" (六个纠治).

## Core Facts

**Issuing Authorities**
*   The Special Working Mechanism Office for Rectifying Formalism and Reducing Burdens on the Grassroots Level at the Central Level.
*   The General Office of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection.

**Notification Content: Three Typical Cases**

1.  **Statistical Fraud and Weak Oversight in Cangzhou, Hebei Province**
    *   **Context:** Between 2025 and August 2026, Cangzhou set excessively high growth targets for investment attraction and e-commerce development, frequently issuing notifications and conducting scheduling meetings.
    *   **Sunning County (肃宁县):** To meet investment tasks, local enterprises were required to rush the introduction of out-of-province shareholders with minimal capital contributions before assessment nodes. In some cases, shareholders contributed only 1,000 RMB in registered capital with a 0.02% equity stake. From 2025 to August 2026, the county falsely attracted 17 out-of-province projects via this method.
    *   **Qing County (青县):** Directed and authorized e-commerce enterprises to misreport statistical data and fabricated financial流水 (cash flow) vouchers. Among 5 sampled above-limit e-commerce enterprises, 4 existed misreporting behaviors. From January to August 2026, network sales figures were distorted by more than 3 times.
    *   **Municipal Failure:** Municipal departments engaged in "only assessing, not verifying" (只考不核) of indicator completion status.

2.  **False Packaging of Projects for Special Treasury Bonds in Bundulun District, Baotou, Inner Mongolia**
    *   **The Violation:** In May 2024, knowing that gas pipeline networks in 15 residential communities had already been completed and accepted, local authorities acquiesced to enterprises forging feasibility studies, preliminary designs, and safety risk assessments. These completed projects were falsely packaged as new construction applications for the 2024 Urban Underground Pipeline Network Construction and Transformation Ultra-Long Special Treasury Bond Project.
    *   **Regulatory Failure:** Both district and city-level departments failed to rigorously review application materials, allowing the false project to be reported layer by layer.
    *   **Financial Impact:** The project secured 39.08 million RMB in ultra-long special treasury bond funds. During investment review, no on-site verification was conducted. In September 2025, to meet fund payment progress requirements, 22 million RMB was disbursed in violation of regulations without auditing forged engineering supervision materials from the construction unit.

3.  **Unrealistic Performance Assessment and Systemic Revenue Inflation at China Post Heilongjiang Branch**
    *   **Context:** China Post Group Co., Ltd. Heilongjiang Provincial Branch prioritized scale over效益 (benefits/profitability) in strategic performance assessments, setting excessively high growth targets and increasing pressure layer by layer. Indicators were ranked and notified frequently (monthly or even daily).
    *   **Violation 1 (Courier Business):** From 2024 to August 2026, to meet courier business income targets, branches provided disguised postal fee discounts under the guise of "agency fees" but recorded revenue at full original price ("low receipt, high recording"). This inflated income by 87.72 million RMB.
    *   **Violation 2 (Express Service):** From 2024 to 2025, branches illegally reclassified commodity sales revenue into express service revenue to meet assessment targets, inflating express service revenue by over 19 million RMB.

**Official Critique and Directives**
*   The notification identifies three root causes:
    1.  Inadequate implementation of the Party Central Committee's requirements to prevent and punish statistical fraud; equating "grasping development" with "grasping data," leading to grassroots falsification.
    2.  Fake project packaging to defraud funds with layered failures in approval and supervision, creating risks for fiscal funds.
    3.  State-owned enterprises formulating unrealistic assessment indicators and decomposing tasks layer by layer, leading to systemic revenue inflation.
*   **Directives:**
    *   Party committees (leadership groups) at all levels must study the 5th Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee spirit, assume main responsibility for rectifying formalism, and conduct self-examination.
    *   Central and state organs must act as a "weather vane" for work style construction, adhering to "turning the blade inward" to identify problems in their respective systems.
    *   Special working mechanism offices at all levels must focus on key areas, deepen the "Six Rectifications," and strengthen problem rectification.

## Event Conclusion
On November 6, 2026, the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and the Central Special Working Mechanism Office publicly sanctioned three典型案例 (typical cases) of formalism: statistical fraud in Hebei's Canghou, fraudulent capture of special treasury bonds in Inner Mongolia's Baotou, and systemic revenue inflation in China Post's Heilongjiang branch. The event underscores the central leadership's emphasis on curbing "data-driven" formalism and strengthening oversight of fiscal funds and state-owned enterprise performance metrics. The notification calls for broader implementation of the "Six Rectifications" and requires party committees at all levels to actively identify and correct similar issues within their systems.

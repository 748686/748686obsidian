## Event Overview

On October 27, 2026, *People’s Daily* (*Renmin Ribao*) published a series of high-priority political news items on its front pages. These reports collectively highlight the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) consolidation of institutional control, reinforcement of ideological narratives, and focus on agricultural modernization. The coverage focuses on three primary themes: the commencement of the eighth round of inspections by the 20th Central Committee’s巡视 (inspection) teams; the promulgation of new regulations governing public institutions (*shiyedanwei*); and commemorative commentary on the 90th anniversary of the Long March victory. A secondary feature highlights advancements in rice seed breeding as part of national food security strategy.

## Core Facts

**1. Twenty-First Central Committee Eighth Round of Inspections Commenced**
*   On October 26, 2026, central inspection teams completed their deployment.
*   The inspections target the **Ministry of Education**, signaling an extension of intra-party oversight into the educational sector.

**2. Promulgation of the "Regulations on the Registration and Management of Public Institutions"**
*   **Authority:** State Council Premier Li Qiang signed a State Council decree promulgating the *Regulations on the Registration and Management of Public Institutions* (《事业单位登记管理条例》).
*   **Effective Date:** December 1, 2026.
*   **Structure:** The regulations consist of six chapters and 34 articles.
*   **Key Objectives:**
    *   Strengthen the Party’s leadership over public institutions.
    *   Reinforce the public welfare responsibilities of these institutions.
    *   Standardize registration management and safeguard legitimate rights.
*   **Specific Mandates:**
    *   **Political Loyalty:** Public institutions must adhere to the leadership of the CCP, implement Party lines and policies, and utilize Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era. Party organizations must be established according to the Party Constitution.
    *   **Public Welfare Orientation:** Institutions must prioritize social benefits. They are prohibited from deviating from their public service mandates, engaging in unauthorized business activities,违规举办企业 (violating regulations to establish enterprises), or conducting false advertising.
    *   **Supervision:** Competent authorities and organizers must supervise activities. Industry regulators must monitor professional ethics (teacher ethics, medical ethics, research integrity). Registration management agencies are tasked with policy guidance and inspection of public welfare fulfillment.
    *   **Registration Services:** Entities must register to obtain legal person status. Simplified procedures for cancellation are available for merged or abolished entities. Information must be disclosed via the National Public Institution Registration Management Information Platform.

**3. Ideological Commentary: Long March Spirit and Yan'an Apple Industry**
*   **Commemoration:** Commentary marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March.
*   **Historical Narrative:** The article highlights the Shaan-Gan (Shaanxi-Gansu) revolutionary base, centered on Zhaojin, as a critical link in the revolution. It credits Liu Bocheng, Xie Zichang, and Xi Zhongxun with establishing the first mountain revolutionary base in Northwest China.
*   **Symbolism:** The "Red Dogwood Forest" (Hongshaolin) and red apples of Yan’an are used as metaphors for revolutionary resilience and poverty alleviation successes.
*   **Current Policy Links:**
    *   Xi Jinping’s visits to Liangjiahe (2015) and Gaoqiao Town (post-20th Party Congress first out-of-beijing trip) are cited to illustrate continuity in rural development policy.
    *   Current economic achievements in Shaanxi and Gansu (millet aerospace breeding, Qingyang computing power hub, Yinchuan wine industry) are presented as evidence of "new quality productive forces" and correct performance views.
    *   The narrative emphasizes the "mass line" and connects historical revolutionaries to contemporary governance practices such as the "Fengqiao Experience" and social work department reforms.

**4. Agricultural Seed Industry Innovation**
*   **Focus:** Four specific rice varieties developed through breeding innovation are highlighted.
*   **"Songjing 60":** Developed by the Heilongjiang Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Features resistance to salinity, alkalinity, lodging, and disease. Currently promoted on over 10,000 mu.
*   **"Zhong’an Zao 35":** Jointly bred by Jinjian Seed Industry and the China National Rice Research Institute. Designed for Changde (Hunan) rice noodles. Features low cadmium accumulation and high amylose content (>25.5%). Promoted on over 1.2 million mu nationally.
*   **"Jifeng You 866":** Developed by the Guangdong Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Suitable for Cantonese rice rolls (cheung fun). Resistant to typhoons and high humidity. Approved nationally in 2024.
*   **"Chuannuo You 2803":** Developed by the Sichuan Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Low amylose content (1.9%), suitable for glutinous rice汤圆 (tangyuan). Yield is ~4% higher than conventional glutinous rice.

# Event Unit: Tibet Baqen County Earthquake Disaster and Rescue Response

## Event Overview

On October 26, 2026, a 7.0 magnitude earthquake struck Baqen County in the Nagqu area of the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. In response, the "Tibet Autonomous Region Baqen 7.0 Earthquake Disaster Emergency Command Headquarters" was activated to coordinate relief efforts. As of the evening of October 25, 2026, significant progress had been made in civilian evacuation, medical treatment, and resource deployment, with national-level ministries and organizations providing immediate support for disaster prevention and victim assistance.

## Core Facts

*   **Event Location:** Baqen County, Nagqu, Tibet Autonomous Region, China.
*   **Event Type:** 7.0 magnitude seismic event.
*   **Civilian Evacuation:** As of 18:00 on October 25, 2026, a total of 12,964 affected civilians had been transferred and resettled.
*   **Medical Response:**
    *   Total casualties treated: 76 individuals.
    *   Inpatients remaining as of the reporting cutoff: 18 individuals.
    *   Local hospitals (e.g., Suo County People's Hospital) initiated emergency response protocols immediately, including triage, classified treatment, and transfer logistics.
*   **Shelter and Supplies Deployment:**
    *   Temporary housing boards (panel rooms): 2,505 sets delivered; 526 sets assembled.
    *   Tents: 4,336 received; 3,076 assembled.
    *   Heating stoves allocated: 3,182 units.
*   **Damage Assessment:**
    *   Completed assessment for 2,840 households/buildings.
    *   Breakdown: 2,201 agricultural houses and 639 public buildings.
    *   Full coverage of agricultural house damage assessment was achieved in Maru (玛如) and Gangqie (岗切) townships.
*   **Geological Hazard Prevention:**
    *   The Ministry of Natural Resources conducted secondary geological disaster investigations.
    *   Hazard points within a 50-kilometer radius of the epicenter have been fully screened.
*   **Financial Aid:**
    *   The All-China Federation of Trade Unions disbursed 2 million Yuan to the Tibet Autonomous Region Federation of Trade Unions for aid to affected workers and consolation for frontline rescuers.

## Event Conclusion

The 7.0 magnitude earthquake in Baqen County, Tibet, on October 26, 2026, triggered an immediate and structured emergency response. By October 25/26, authorities had successfully evacuated and sheltered nearly 13,000 residents, treated 76 injured individuals, and secured supply chains for winterization. The response highlighted the effectiveness of China's centralized disaster management system, particularly the integration of local emergency commands with national ministries for geological screening and financial aid. Qualitative assessments indicate that the efficiency of the rescue operations was directly attributable to pre-existing infrastructure maintenance, regular disaster drills, and standardized administrative protocols established prior to the event.

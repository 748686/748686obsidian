# Event: Synthesis of Anti-Corruption and Governance Reforms in Chinese Local Administrations

## Event Overview

On October 27, 2026, the People's Daily (Article #557) published a report titled "Cadre Work Style Transformation Is Tangible to the Masses," part of a series observing cadre status and extending comprehensive strict party governance to the grassroots level. The article highlights specific governance reforms in three Chinese provinces—Liaoning, Jiangxi, and Shandong—that aim to improve administrative efficiency, prevent corruption, and enhance public service delivery through institutional innovation.

The event focuses on the operationalization of anti-corruption measures and bureaucratic efficiency reforms, rather than the historical urban governance theories referenced in the conflicting global merge reason. The synthesis identifies three distinct case studies: the integration of government services in Tieling, the digital supervision of official dining in Jiangxi, and the "role-reversal" inspection method in Qingdao.

## Core Facts

**1. Liaoning Tieling: Integrated Government Service Windows**
*   **Reform:** Implementation of a "One-Window General Handling" (一窗通办) model at the Tieling Administrative Approval Service Center.
*   **Scope:** The model breaks barriers between police departments, allowing a single window to handle 126 types of business across nine categories (household registration, traffic management, public security, cyber security, etc.) at both municipal and county levels.
*   **Digital Integration:** Establishment of 9 network-based service centers by the Tieling Public Security Bureau.
*   **Performance Metrics (Year-to-Date):**
    *   101,514 online-handled cases.
    *   1,532 problem clues received in the business environment sector.
    *   760 cases filed (li'an).
    *   802 work style and corruption issues investigated and handled.
    *   2,396 items classified as "no need to visit" (一次不用跑).
    *   2,182 items available for "immediate collection" (立等可取).
    *   81 livelihood items integrated into the "Liaoshitong" (辽事通) APP.

**2. Jiangxi: Digital Supervision of Official Dining**
*   **Reform:** Launch of the "Gan Can Ma" (赣餐码 - Jiangxi Meal Code) platform in December 2024 to regulate official dining across provincial, municipal, county, and township levels.
*   **Mechanism:** A QR-code-based system requiring officials to scan, input the meal cost, and generate an electronic voucher. This creates a closed loop of "scan payment – data traceability – intelligent supervision."
*   **Coverage:** Over 9,000 canteens across administrative institutions, state-owned enterprises, and universities.
*   **Cumulative Usage:** 948,800 official meal transactions processed.
*   **Supervision Features:** Automatic recording of time, location, personnel, and amount; automated alerts for anomalies such as high-frequency dining, over-standard spending, or large transactions to prevent risks like "one invitation for multiple meals" or false reimbursement.

**3. Shandong Qingdao: Frontline Role-Reversal Inspection**
*   **Initiative:** "Cadre Work Style Construction Year" activity launched in 2026.
*   **Method:** "I Go to the Frontline · Role-Reversal Experience" (我到一线·换位体验), where cadres perform the duties of service recipients or frontline workers to identify bureaucratic bottlenecks.
*   **Scale:** Over 5,000 cadre officials participated in activities such as "acting as a hotline operator for a day" or "accompanying the public through processes."
*   **Case Study:** A road maintenance project (National Highway 204) experienced payment delays due to complex internal workflows. After a cadre inspected the process, the payment timeline was compressed from 30 days to 10 days by separating metrology approval from fund disbursement.
*   **Output:** Issuance of a notice to accelerate payment for maintenance projects, mandating clear deadlines and responsibility assignment for each node.

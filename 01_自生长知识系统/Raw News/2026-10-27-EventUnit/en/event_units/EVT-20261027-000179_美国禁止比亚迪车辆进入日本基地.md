EVT-20261027-000179: United States Ban on BYD Vehicles at Japanese Bases

## Event Overview

On October 27, 2026, the United States issued a directive prohibiting BYD-brand vehicles from being used at Japanese military bases under the U.S.-Japan Status of Forces Agreement. The ban stems from heightened concerns regarding vehicle connectivity software and national security risks. While the restriction was formally ordered by U.S. authorities, Japan has indicated it will respect the decision.

## Core Facts

*   **Issuer:** United States (Pentagon/U.S. Forces).
*   **Target:** BYD-branded vehicles used at Japanese bases under the U.S.-Japan Status of Forces Agreement.
*   **Action:** Prohibition of BYD vehicles at these bases.
*   **Stated Reason:** The Pentagon cites "connectivity software" as a threat to national security.
*   **Japan's Response:** According to multiple Japanese media sources, Japanese officials have stated that Japan will respect the U.S. decision.
*   **Media Coverage:**

**2. Archaeological Study on Qin Terracotta Warriors**
*   **Institutions Involved**: The Museum of the Qin Terracotta Army and Mausoleum (Xi'an) and the School of Archaeology at Shandong University.
*   **Publication**: The study was published in *The International Journal of Cultural Heritage* (referred to in Chinese source as 《文化遗产杂志》).
*   **Methodology**: Researchers extracted 79 fingerprint impressions from 23 pottery figures in Pit 1, square T23. They applied a Bayesian mixed fingerprint model to estimate age and sex composition.
*   **Findings**:
    *   Adult males: ~45.0%
    *   Adult females: ~32.7%
    *   Individuals under age 15: ~22.3%
*   **Conclusion**: Production was male-dominated but not exclusively male; women and adolescents likely participated in making, transporting, or assembling the figures. This is described as the first quantitative biological evidence of this demographic mix in Qin俑 production.

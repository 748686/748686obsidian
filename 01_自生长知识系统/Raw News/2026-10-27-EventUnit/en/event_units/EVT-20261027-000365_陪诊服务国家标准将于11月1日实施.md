# Event Name

## Event Overview

On October 27, 2026, *People's Daily* published reports covering two distinct national initiatives aimed at improving public services and industrial standards in China.

First, the national standard "Basic Requirements for Socialized Medical Accompaniment Services" (*Shehuihua Peitong Jiuyi Fuwu Jiben Yaoqiu*) is set to take effect on November 1, 2026. This standard defines the responsibilities and boundaries of medical accompanists ("peizhen shi") to ensure the service is both guaranteed and within proper limits.

Second, the National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) officially released the first batch of 136 landmark major application scenario projects. Among these, the "Digital Home Full Industry Chain Cultivation and Open Scenario," led by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD), was selected as a comprehensive social governance service application scenario. Six cities were designated as the first batch of pilot sites for this digital home construction initiative.

## Core Facts

### National Standard for Medical Accompaniment Services
*   **Standard Name:** "Basic Requirements for Socialized Medical Accompaniment Services" (reported as part of the *Peizhen Fuwu Guojia Biaozhun*).
*   **Effective Date:** November 1, 2026.
*   **Objective:** To regulate the "socialized medical accompaniment service" industry, clarifying the duties and boundary restrictions of "peizhen shi" (medical accompanists).
*   **Guiding Principle:** The service should be "guaranteed but not exceed its boundaries" (*you baozhang bu yuejie*), implying a distinction between professional medical/escort services and illegal medical practice or overreach.

### Digital Home Construction Pilot
*   **Launching Body:** National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC).
*   **Date of Announcement:** October 26, 2026 (reported on October 27).
*   **Project List:** First batch of 136 landmark major application scenario projects.
*   **Specific Scenario:** "Digital Home Full Industry Chain Cultivation and Open Scenario" (shuju jiating quan chanyelian peiyu he kaifang changjing).
*   **Lead Ministry:** Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD).
*   **Classification:** Social governance service comprehensive application scenario.
*   **Three Main Lines:**
    1.  Improving the standard system.
    2.  Tackling core technologies.
    3.  Enriching application scenarios.
*   **Target Cities (First Batch):** Shenzhen, Qingdao, Ningbo, Hefei, Dongguan, and Jinhua.
*   **Economic Projections:**
    *   Incremental market cultivated during the "15th Five-Year Plan" period: 1.6 trillion RMB.
    *   Consumption potential released across the full industry chain: approximately 10 trillion RMB.
*   **Implementation Scope:** Commercial housing, affordable housing, new residential communities, and old residential communities.
*   **Strategic Goal:** Deep integration of digital home products and buildings; building "good houses" (haofangzi) that are smart, safe, comfortable, and green; supporting the deep integration of the digital economy and livelihood services.

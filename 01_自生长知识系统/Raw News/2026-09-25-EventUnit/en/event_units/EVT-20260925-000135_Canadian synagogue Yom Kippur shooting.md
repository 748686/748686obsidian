## Event Overview

On the evening of Sunday, September 14, 2025, a hate-motivated shooting occurred outside a synagogue in Montreal, Canada, during Yom Kippur celebrations. The incident is part of a broader wave of antisemitic attacks reported globally that same year. While two individuals were wounded in Montreal, another severe attack involving an axe and knife occurred simultaneously in Toronto. Security concerns were heightened across Quebec and nationally, with increased police presence and a publicized security alert for the upcoming Sukkot holiday.

*   **Date:** September 14, 2025 (Sunday evening).
*   **Location:** Outside a synagogue on Sherbrooke Street in Montreal, Quebec, Canada.

# Event: Xi Jinping’s State Visit to the United States

## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived in Washington, D.C., for a state visit at the invitation of U.S. President Donald Trump. The visit followed a previous meeting in May 2026 during which the two leaders agreed to build a "China-U.S. constructive strategic stability relationship." The event included a state welcome ceremony at the White House on September 24 and high-level diplomatic engagements involving multiple Chinese officials.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Arrival:** Local time, afternoon of September 23, 2026.
*   **Location:** Andrews Air Force Base, Washington, D.C.
*   **Primary Actors:**
    *   **China:** President Xi Jinping, First Lady Peng Liyuan, Cai Qi, Wang Yi, He Lifeng (Vice Premier), Xie Feng (Ambassador to the U.S.).
    *   **United States:** President Donald Trump, First Lady Melania Trump.
*   **Ceremonial Details:**
    *   Xi and Peng were greeted by Trump and Melania at the airport.
    *   Welcome protocol included military honor guards, playing of national anthems, a 21-gun salute, and fighter jet flyovers.
    *   American children presented flowers to the Chinese leaders.
    *   Overseas Chinese and student representatives lined the route to the hotel, waving flags of both nations.
*   **White House Welcome Ceremony (September 24, 2026):**
    *   President Xi delivered a speech titled *"Leading the Ship of China-U.S. Relations Steadily Forward."*
    *   In the speech, Xi congratulated the U.S. on its 250th anniversary of independence.
    *   **Key Announcements:**
        1.  **Youth Exchange:** China will invite 100,000 American youths to China for study and exchange over the next five years.
        2.  **Panda Diplomacy:** Giant pandas "Pingping" (平平) and "Fushuang" (福双) will be sent to the Atlanta Zoo shortly after the visit.
*   **Diplomatic Framework:**
    *   Both sides reaffirmed the agreement from May 2026 to construct a "China-U.S. constructive strategic stability relationship."
    *   Xi outlined three pillars for the relationship: strengthening communication, sincere cooperation, and peaceful coexistence.
    *   Xi stated that the "Thucydides Trap" is surmountable and that competition should be "benign and bounded."

### 3. Analysis via FAB Technique (Features-Advantages-Benefits)

**Feature (特征)**
- DoorDash offers a **15% discount on the first order** for new users.
- The deal is labeled as **"limited-time"**.

**Advantage (优势)**
- Compared to standard pricing, new users pay significantly less on their initial purchase.
- The "limited-time" label creates exclusivity and urgency, distinguishing this offer from permanent discounts.
- DoorDash’s extensive restaurant network is accessible at a reduced entry cost.

**Benefit (收益)**
- **Financial Savings**: Users save money immediately, reducing the perceived risk of trying a new service.
- **Convenience**: Access to thousands of restaurants delivered to their door, without the full price tag.
- **Trial Opportunity**: Users can test DoorDash’s service quality, app experience, and delivery speed with lower financial commitment.
- **Psychological Comfort**: The discount removes the "what if I regret this?" hesitation, encouraging first-time adoption.

### 4. Analysis via AIDA Model (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)

**A: Attention (注意)**
- **Hook**: "Save 15% on your first order with this limited-time DoorDash deal."
- **Why it works**: The percentage (15%) and the word "free/discount" grab attention. "Limited-time" adds urgency.

**I: Interest (兴趣)**
- **Build Connection**: DoorDash is a well-known platform with a vast selection of restaurants.
- **Engage**: The discount applies to the *first* order, which is the most critical friction point for new users.
- **Curiosity**: What restaurants are included? How easy is the sign-up process?

**D: Desire (欲望)**
- **Emphasize Benefits**: Saving money on a desired service (food delivery) appeals to both rational (cost-saving) and emotional (convenience, pleasure of food) needs.
- **Social Proof**: (Implied) DoorDash’s large user base suggests reliability and variety.
- **Contrast**: Paying full price vs. paying 15% less—most users will prefer the latter.

**A: Action (行动)**
- **Clear Instruction**: Sign up for DoorDash and apply the discount code at checkout.
- **Urgency**: "Limited-time" implies the offer may expire soon.
- **Risk Reduction**: First-order discounts lower the barrier to entry, making the action feel low-risk.
- **Call to Action**: "Start your first order now and save 15%."

## Core Facts

### 1. Promulgation of Revised Audit Law Implementation Regulations
*   **Action:** State Council Premier Li Qiang signed a State Council Decree on September 24, 2026, promulgating the revised *Implementation Regulations of the Audit Law of the People's Republic of China* (《中华人民共和国审计法实施条例》).
*   **Effective Date:** December 1, 2026.
*   **Structure:** The regulations consist of 7 chapters and 64 articles.
*   **Key Revisions:**
    *   **Party Leadership:** Explicitly stipulates that audit work must adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China.
    *   **Audit Scope:** Expanded to include detailed audits of fiscal final draft accounts and state-owned resource/asset management. "Other major public engineering projects involving national and public interests" are now defined. State-owned resource management is included in special audit investigations.
    *   **Procedures:** Introduction of a registration and reporting system for individuals attempting to probe or interfere with audit matters. Refinement of procedures for querying deposits in financial institutions related to audit matters.
    *   **Rectification Responsibility:** Audit subjects bear primary responsibility for rectification. Audit institutions must track rectification and certify completion. Government departments must coordinate to urge problem resolution.
    *   **Source:** Article #457 (Original Title Mismatch), derived from Xinhua report dated Sept 24.

### 4. "15th Five-Year Plan" Cultural and Tourism Policy Release
*   **Event:** State Council Information Office press conference held on September 24, 2026.
*   **Policy Direction:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" period will focus on deep integration of culture and tourism, coordinating the high-quality development of cultural undertakings, cultural industries, and tourism.
*   **Five Key Areas (Wang Jianhua, Deputy Minister of Culture and Tourism):**
    1.  Art creation: New era art creation guidance plan, national fine art collection plan.
    2.  Cultural heritage: Systematic protection, unified regulation, cultural routes, intangible heritage experiences.
    3.  Public services: Optimizing cultural/tourism facility networks (centers, toilets, parking, signage).
    4.  Industry vitality: "Culture/Tourism + " integration with sports, commercial districts, and parks.
    5.  Exchange: Inbound tourism promotion计划, improving便利化 (convenience) and国际化 (internationalization).
*   **Specific Initiatives:**
    *   **Drama:** Implementation of a three-year action plan for drama revitalization starting this year; support for rare opera genres; special support mechanism for small/medium-sized plays (Chen Binbin, Director of General Office).
    *   **Industry:** Support for original content in performing arts, animation, and creative design; "Hundred Cities, Hundred Districts" and "Hundred Cities, Thousand Stations" consumption actions; rural cultural tourism formats; promotion of "Guochao" (national trend) creative products overseas (Miao Muyang, Director of Industrial Development Dept).
    *   **Tourism Service:** Focus on livelihood benefits (employment platforms), refined services (addressing congestion, parking, sanitation, elderly-friendly modifications), and safety (market order rectification) (Man Hongwei, Director of Resource Development Dept).
*   **Source:** Article #460 (Original Title Mismatch), reported by Wang Ke (People's Daily).

*   **Case Study:** Hongjun Village, Gaoyan Town, Chengkou County, Chongqing.

## Event Overview

On October 23, 2026, China marked the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army. In observance of this milestone, a series of cultural and commemorative activities were launched or highlighted, focusing on the inheritance of the "Long March Spirit" through various art forms, including documentary film, opera, folk music, ballet, and drama. Major state media, particularly *People's Daily*, published special reports and creator reflections on October 24, 2026, documenting these efforts and the broader significance of the Long March in the context of the new era.

## Core Facts

*   **Commemorative Timing:** The core anniversary date is October 23, 2026, marking 90 years since the conclusion of the Long March.
*   **Cultural Productions:** Several major artistic works dedicated to the Long March were highlighted or premiered around this date:
    *   **Documentary:** A large-scale series documentary titled *Wan Shui Qian Shan* (Ten Thousand Rivers and Mountains) was released after over a year of production. It features historic footage and modern reenactments.
    *   **Opera:** The National Centre for the Performing Arts staged the premiere of a new version of its Chinese史诗歌剧 (historical epic opera) *The Long March* (*Chang Zheng*), which had originally been created for the 80th anniversary in 2016.
    *   **Music:** The China Broadcasting Art Troupe created and premiered a large-scale ethnic symphonic dramatic concert titled *Shan He Ru Yuan* (Mountains and Rivers as Desired), which began touring on October 9, 2026.
    *   **Ballet:** The Central Ballet's modern ballet *Long March, Long March* (*Chang Zheng Chang Zheng*) was performed as part of the *Spark · Eternal Symphony* program.
    *   **Drama:** The Gansu Provincial Opera House's play *The Lights of Hada Pu* (*Hada Pu De Dengguang*) is currently on tour along the Long March route.
*   **Historical Reflections:** Creators and commentators emphasized that the Long March represents not just historical suffering but also a "great expedition to test truth," citing key events such as the Zunyi Conference, the Four Crossings of Chishui, and the convergence of Red Army forces.
*   **Contemporary Significance:** The activities underscore the narrative that "every generation has its own Long March," linking the historical spirit to current goals of national rejuvenation, rural revitalization, and modern development along the Long March route.

The 90th anniversary of the Long March victory in October 2026 was commemorated through a coordinated series of high-profile cultural productions across China.

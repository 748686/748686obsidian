### 2. Xizang Earthquake and Rescue Operations
- On October 22, 2026, at approximately 22:33, a magnitude 7.0 earthquake struck Baxyain County (Baqing County), Ngari/Nagqu City, Xizang (Tibet).
- The earthquake caused "major casualties."
- Xi Jinping, General Secretary of the CPC Central Committee, President of the PRC, and Chairman of the CMC, issued important instructions emphasizing:
  - Full-scale search and rescue operations.
  - Medical treatment for the injured to minimize casualties.
  - Scientific rescue due to high-altitude, cold-weather conditions.
  - Prevention of secondary disasters.
  - Provision of relief supplies and proper resettlement of affected populations.
  - Repair of damaged infrastructure.
- Li Qiang, Premier of the State Council, issued instructions urging rapid verification of disaster extent, rescue coordination, and enhanced seismic monitoring by the Ministry of Emergency Management and the Earthquake Administration.
- The Ministry of Emergency Management dispatched a working group to the site.
- Rescue operations were ongoing as of October 23–24, 2026.
- **Sources:** Articles #444, #445, #450.

### 3. Xi Jinping’s Letter to the 2026 Global Governance Forum (Xiong'an)

- The forum opened on the same day in Xiong'an New Area, Hebei Province.
- Theme: "Rebuilding Confidence and Cooperation: Centennial Changes and Reforming Global Governance."
- Organizer: Chinese People's Association for Friendship with Foreign Countries.
- Xi’s letter highlighted:

  - Growing deficits in peace, development, security, and governance.
  - Promotion of the Global Governance Initiative.
  - Commitment to common consultation, contribution, and shared benefits.
  - Advancing a community with a shared future for mankind.
- **Source:** Article #446.

### 4. Commemoration of the 90th Anniversary of the Long March
- The article references the "Commemoration Conference on the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March."
- Xi Jinping stated that the Long March victory was inevitable and revealed the "way to victory" for the Party and the army.
- Four key factors identified:
  1. Correct Party leadership.
  2. Firm revolutionary will.
  3. Independence and seeking truth from facts.
  4. Support from the broad masses of the people.
- The commentary emphasizes applying these lessons to the current "new Long March" of Chinese modernization.
- **Source:** Article #447.

### 5. State Council Executive Meeting on Population and Services
- Premier Li Qiang presided over a State Council executive meeting on October 23, 2026.
- Two main agenda items:
  - **Population and economic/social development:** Emphasized integrating population policy with economic development, resource environment, and public services. Key measures include improving lifelong learning, skills training, employment promotion, regional population guidance, public service equalization, and building a fertility-friendly society.
  - **Service industry development:** Focused on expanding service sector capacity and quality, removing market access barriers, supporting private investment, reforming public utility pricing, and fostering service consumption.
- **Source:** Article #448.

### 6. Xiong'an New Area Development
- An editorial/report highlighted Xiong'an's integrated approach to high-quality construction and efficient governance.
- Key features:
  - Underground utility tunnels (>100 km) and digital twin infrastructure ("Xiong'an Eye").
  - Ecological restoration of Baiyangdian Lake (water quality stable at Class III for 5 years; bird species increased to 296, up 90 since establishment).
  - Community governance models ("civil affairs ledgers," one-file-per-household).
  - Integration of public services (e.g., Beijing No. 4 High School Xiong'an Campus).
- **Source:** Article #449.

### 7. Autumn Grain Purchase

- Procurement activities were proceeding in an orderly manner.
- Featured image: Grain transport vehicles at a reserve granary in Shuyang County, Suqian City, Jiangsu Province.
- **Source:** Article #450.

# EVT-20261024-000333: 7.0-Magnitude Earthquake in Baqen County, Naqu City, Tibet Autonomous Region, and Emergency Disaster Relief

## Event Overview
On October 2026, a 7.0-magnitude earthquake struck Baqen County, Naqu City, Tibet Autonomous Region, China. The seismic event was characterized by a high magnitude and shallow focal depth, causing significant damage in the epicentral areas of玛如乡 (Maru Township) and 岗切乡 (Gangqie Township). In response, Chinese central and regional authorities activated emergency response mechanisms, deployed thousands of rescue personnel, and mobilized substantial material and financial resources to conduct search-and-rescue operations, provide temporary shelter, and support affected populations under freezing conditions.

## Core Facts
*   **Seismic Event:** A 7.0-magnitude earthquake occurred in Baqen County, Naqu City, Tibet. The China Earthquake Networks Center described the event as a "tension-type rupture" (拉张型破裂).
*   **Impact Zone:** The core areas most severely affected included Maru Township and Gangqie Township in Baqen County.
*   **Casualties and Damage (as of October 23, 13:00):** According to the Naqu Municipal People's Government Press Office, the earthquake resulted in 16 deaths, 76 injuries, and 339 households with collapsed or damaged housing.
*   **Aftershocks:** Frequent aftershocks continued through the night of October 22 into the early hours of October 23.
*   **Rescue Operations:** A total of over 5,000 rescue personnel from autonomous region, city, and county levels were deployed. Teams were organized for search and rescue, medical care, settlement, supply guarantee, and hazard elimination. By the afternoon of October 23, three rounds of village-by-village searches had been completed.
*   **Population Evacuation:** 12,489 people were evacuated. As of the reporting date, 18 settlement points, including Maru Township Second Village, had completed tent construction, with additional sites under construction.
*   **Emergency Response Levels:**
    *   The Emergency Management Department elevated the National Earthquake Disaster Emergency Response from Level IV to Level III.
    *   The National Flood Control and Drought Relief Headquarters elevated the National Natural Disaster Relief Emergency Response to Level III.
    *   The Ministry of Transport activated a Level III emergency response for highway traffic incidents.
    *   The China Earthquake Administration activated a Level II earthquake emergency response.
*   **Resource Mobilization:**
    *   **Financial:** The Ministry of Finance and the Emergency Management Department allocated 50 million yuan in central natural disaster relief funds. The National Development and Reform Commission arranged 30 million yuan in central budget investment for infrastructure recovery. The Central Organization Department allocated 25 million yuan from centrally managed party funds. The China Charity Federation allocated 3 million yuan specifically for the Naqu disaster area.
    *   **Material Supplies:** Central and local authorities dispatched over 10,000 items of relief supplies, including 1,000 sets of prefabricated houses, 3,239 tents, 2,821 folding beds, and 4,280 quilts. An additional 43,000 items (mobile toilets, heating stoves, etc.) were mobilized through government-society collaborative mechanisms.
    *   **Personnel:** The national comprehensive fire and rescue force dispatched 350 personnel and 83 vehicles. As of October 23, 16:51, 307 personnel, 67 vehicles, and 15 dogs had arrived. The Tibet Fire and Rescue Corps dispatched 40 personnel and 15 vehicles. National work teams and expert groups were sent to the area.
*   **Logistics and Access:** Highway traffic control measures were implemented; rescue vehicles were permitted passage, while social vehicles were temporarily prohibited.
*   **Housing Transition Plan:** Authorities planned to transition residents from temporary tents to prefabricated板房 (banfang) as they arrived, ensuring safe accommodation, warm clothing and bedding, hot meals, and clean water.

## Event Conclusion
The 7.0-magnitude earthquake in Baqen County, Tibet, triggered a rapid and multi-level national emergency response. As of October 23, 2026, the operation has successfully evacuated over 12,000 residents and secured basic living supplies for those affected. The focus has shifted from immediate search-and-rescue to emergency sheltering, with a transition plan from tents to prefabricated housing underway. Authorities emphasize the protection of vulnerable groups (the elderly and children) and warn of ongoing seismic risks. The event highlights the centralized mobilization capacity of the Chinese state in high-altitude disaster scenarios.

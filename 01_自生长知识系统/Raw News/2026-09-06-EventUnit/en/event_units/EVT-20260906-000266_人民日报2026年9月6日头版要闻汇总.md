### 3. Educational Policy: School Uniform Procurement
*   **Guidance:** The Ministry of Education issued clarifications on school uniform procurement to address parental concerns about quality and mandatory purchasing.
*   **Key Principles:**
    *   **Voluntary Purchase:** Purchasing is voluntary; schools cannot mandate uniforms or use them as a threshold for campus activities/appearance.
    *   **Frequency:** Uniforms are generally procured in three-year cycles (e.g., grades 1-3, 4-6, middle school, high school). No procurement is organized for graduating grades.
    *   **Stability:** Styles should remain stable to reduce repeated costs for families.
    *   **Assistance:** Free uniforms are provided for students from low-income families, orphans, and disabled children.
*   **Quality Control:**
    *   Mandatory national standards (GB) for safety (formaldehyde, pH, azo dyes) must be specified in contracts.
    *   Labels must indicate fiber content and safety categories (per regulations effective July 1).
    *   "Double submission" inspection mechanism: Suppliers must inspect, and purchasers are encouraged to re-inspect.
*   **Oversight:** Parents can participate in uniform selection committees. Feedback channels include local procurement hotlines and petition systems. Examples of transparency mechanisms were cited from Quanzhou (Fujian) and Lixin County (Anhui).
*   **Source:** Article #377/#378 (Page 01, interviewed by Wu Dan).

### 4. Social Security: Long-Term Care Insurance (LTCI) Regulations
*   **New Guidelines:** The National Healthcare Security Administration issued "Guiding Opinions on Improving the Payment Management of Long-Term Care Insurance."
*   **Payment Scope:** Covers disability assessment fees, long-term care service fees at designated institutions, and third-party administrative fees.
    *   Basic medical insurance and LTCI payments must not overlap for the same service.
*   **Payment Methods:**
    *   Assessment services: Paid per person.
    *   Nursing services: Primarily paid by time unit (hourly for home care, bed-day for institutional care).
*   **Payment Standards:**
    *   Annual maximum payment limit per insured person shall not exceed **50%** of the previous year's per capita disposable income of urban and rural residents in the pooling area.
    *   Standards are adjusted based on local economic levels and wage rates.
*   **Fund Supervision:** Strict performance evaluations for designated institutions and social force agencies. Fraudulent claims or non-compliance result in deduction or denial of payment. Fund payments cannot be managed/dispensed by third-party social forces.
*   **Transition:** Pilot areas have approximately 3 years to gradually transition to the new standards.
*   **Source:** Article #381/#382 (Page 02, interviewed by Sun Xiuyan).

### 5. Media and International Cooperation: Asia-Pacific Media High-Level Forum
*   **Event:** The Asia-Pacific Media High-Level Forum opened in Shenzhen, Guangdong, on September 5, 2026.
*   **Key Figures:** Li Shulei (Member of the Politburo Standing Committee, Head of the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee) delivered the keynote address. Huang Kunming (Party Secretary of Guangdong Province) gave opening remarks.
*   **Theme:** "Building a Prosperous Path for the Asia-Pacific Community: Media Consensus and Action."
*   **Context:** Aligns with China’s APEC Presidency for 2026 ("APEC China Year") and the upcoming 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting.
*   **Participation:** Over 400 representatives from more than 40 countries and regions, plus UN agencies and international organizations.
*   **Outcome:** 16 Chinese and foreign media outlets initiated the establishment of a preparatory committee for the "Asia-Pacific Media Partnership Initiative."
*   **Source:** Article #380 (Page 02, interviewed by Chen Shangwen and Cheng Yuanzhou).

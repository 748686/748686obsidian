### [Xiamen aid worker reflects on five years in Minning](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/27/content_30187815.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A People&\#x27;s Daily profile published on October 27, 2026, looks back at Li Huiqin&\#x27;s five years in Minning Town, Ningxia, where he served in roles including head of the publicity group for Fujian&\#x27;s 14th aid contingent and technical expert group leader in Xiamen. The article highlights his efforts to modernize local agriculture through live-streamed e-commerce and short videos that documented daily life, helping farmers move from skepticism to independently selling products online. Fujian Province has played a leading role in supporting Ningxia since the mid-1990s, with Minning serving as a widely cited model for cross-regional cooperation.

**「Impact」** The account provides a public-sector case study in using digital sales channels and community engagement to sustain rural income growth in western China. It may influence how local governments frame technical-assistance programs that prioritize behavioral change alongside infrastructure investment.

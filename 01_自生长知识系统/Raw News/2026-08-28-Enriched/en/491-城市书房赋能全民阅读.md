### [Guizhou Village Super League expands to 105 teams with national industrial alliance](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202608/28/content_30177680.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The second Guizhou "Cunchao" \(Village Super League\) national finals concluded on August 22, 2026, with Guangdong Zhaoqing Gaoyao Jinli Chengjun FC defending their title by defeating Guangdong Wuhua Pumi FC in a penalty shootout. The tournament expanded from 51 teams in its first year to 105 teams from 102 county-level zones across China, shifting selection from provincial to county-based organization to broaden grassroots participation. The event introduced a dual-format combining 48 competitive cup matches with 57 exhibition games focused on cultural exchange and regional tourism promotion. A new County Industrial Development Alliance was established to connect rural products like Yunnan coffee and Xinjiang walnuts with national markets, while榕江县 provided operational guidance including volunteer recruitment and security standards to host counties.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Aug 28, 00:26

**「Background」** Guizhou&\#x27;s Village Super League originated as a local football tournament in Rongjiang County and evolved into a nationwide grassroots sports phenomenon by 2026, emphasizing community participation over professional athletics. The event has become a model for rural revitalization in China, integrating sports with cultural heritage display and agricultural product marketing to stimulate local economies beyond the province.

**「Impact」** County governments across China can now access proven operational frameworks from Rongjiang to organize similar sports-tourism events, with some areas reporting visitor increases exceeding 210万人次 and consumption growth over 35%. The structural shift to county-based qualification zones has democratized participation for amateur players while creating new distribution channels for rural agricultural products through event-related trade fairs.

# Event Name

Hunan Economic Development Research Report: "Transforming Methods and Adjusting Structures to Gather New Development Momentum" (Part of the "Vital China Research Series")

## Event Overview

On September 21, 2026, *People's Daily* published a special research report titled **"Transforming Methods and Adjusting Structures: Hunan Gathers New Development Momentum"** (活力中国调研行). The report, authored by journalists Yu Chunhui, Zheng Yanbing, and Yang Xun, details the economic transformation and high-quality development strategies of Hunan Province. It outlines how Hunan is implementing directives from General Secretary Xi Jinping to leverage scientific and technological innovation, boost domestic consumption, and enhance inland opening-up.

The event synthesizes observations from multiple industrial and cultural sites in Hunan, including Changsha, Loudi, Yiyang, Changde, Liuyang, Zhuzhou, and Huaihua, to illustrate the province's shift toward new quality productive forces.

## Core Facts

### 1. Strategic Directives
*   Since the 18th National Congress, General Secretary Xi Jinping has conducted multiple inspection tours in Hunan.
*   Key directives include: "Make greater efforts to lead industrial innovation with scientific and technological innovation" and "Strive to build a highland for national important advanced manufacturing, scientific and technological innovation with core competitiveness, and inland reform and opening up."

### 2. Consumption and Domestic Demand
*   **Policy Framework:** Hunan has issued the *Hunan Province Action Plan to Boost Consumption* and the *Hunan Province Implementation Plan for Cultivating New Growth Points in Service Consumption (2026–2028)*.
*   **Case Study - Changde He Street:**
    *   In March 2024, Xi Jinping inspected Changde He Street, emphasizing the inheritance and development of local cultural forms such as silk strings, high-pitch singing, and boatmen's chants.
    *   The city has restored the old river street appearance and gathered 87 intangible cultural heritage projects.
    *   **2025 Data:** Received 18.2467 million tourists (up 75% year-on-year); total annual turnover reached 1.239 billion yuan (up 58.85% year-on-year).
*   **Other Brands:** Wanlou in Xiangtan and Tianwen Island in Huaihua have become well-known cultural tourism consumption brands.
*   **Economic Data:**
    *   **2025 Trade-in Program:** Cumulative sales driven by trade-in policies approached 100 billion yuan.
    *   **2026 H1 Performance:** With 10 new categories of subsidized products, retail sales of restricted-scale enterprises grew by:
        *   Communication equipment: +11.1%
        *   New energy vehicles: +11.0%
        *   Wearable devices: +44.9%
    *   **Service Consumption H1 Growth:**
        *   Sports enterprises: +16.4%
        *   Literary and artistic creation/performance enterprises: +11.3%
        *   Cultural activity service enterprises: +15.1%
*   **Official Statement:** Shen Yumou, Party Secretary and Director of the Hunan Provincial Department of Commerce, stated that Hunan combines benefiting people's livelihood with promoting consumption, linking investment in objects with investment in people.

### 3. Technological Innovation and Industrial Upgrade
*   **Policy Framework:** Hunan has issued the *Hunan Province Modern Industrial System Construction Implementation Plan* and the *Implementation Plan for Deepening the "Smart Empowerment of Ten Thousand Enterprises" Action to Accelerate Manufacturing Digital Transformation*.
*   **Case Study - Lens Technology (Liuyang):**
    *   Transitioned from consumer electronics glass covers to semiconductor advanced packaging and embodied intelligent robots.
*   **Case Study - Zoomlion (Changsha):**
    *   In the Smart Industry City, 300+ smart manufacturing production lines and 2,000+ adaptive industrial robots work collaboratively.
    *   Output: One excavator下线 (goes offline/completes production) every 6 minutes on average.
    *   Capability: Mixed-flow production of over 100 excavator models.
    *   Embodied AI: Launched 8 models of humanoid robots and released the "Yungu Embodied Intelligence Large Model" and supporting toolchains, covering the full lifecycle from data collection to deployment.
*   **Case Study - Sunward Star Aviation (Zhuzhou):**
    *   A high-tech joint-stock private enterprise and参股 (shareholding) company of Sunward Intelligent Equipment Co., Ltd.
    *   Xi Jinping inspected the parent company (Sunward Intelligent) in September 2020, stating, "Innovation is the most important quality of enterprise management and what we must do to climb over hurdles."
    *   Focus: Low-altitude economy core components localization.
    *   Product: "Aerola" light sports aircraft.
    *   Status: Cumulative delivery of nearly 500 units; domestic market share exceeds 80%; exported overseas.
    *   Quote: Founder He Qinghua stated, "Our vision is to create Chinese value in the aviation field."
*   **Industrial Statistics (as of June 2026):**
    *   Hunan has 146 A-share listed companies, covering semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, non-ferrous metals, and precision tool manufacturing.
    *   **H1 2026 Production Growth (Scale以上 Industrial Enterprises):**
        *   Sensors: +22.1%
        *   Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs): +17.5%
        *   Optoelectronic Devices: +17.3%

### 4. Inland Opening-Up
*   **Case Study - Huaihua International Land Port:**
    *   Transformed from an inland backwater to a key portal of the New International Land-Sea Trade Corridor.
    *   Routes: Connected to Southeast Asia via China-Laos, China-Vietnam, and China-Myanmar channels.
    *   **Data (as of Sept 13, 2026):** Cumulative shipments of 49,156 TEUs (twenty-foot equivalent units), equivalent to 983 trains, up 14% year-on-year.
*   **Policy Framework:**
    *   *Implementation Plan for Deepening the Free Trade Zone Improvement Strategy and High-Standard Construction of China (Hunan) Pilot Free Trade Zone.*
    *   *Hunan Province Three-Year Action Plan for Non-African Economic and Trade Cooperation (2026–2028).*
*   **Non-Africa Cooperation:**
    *   Focuses on six advantageous industries: automobiles and parts, construction machinery, etc.
    *   Five directions: Industrial overseas expansion, entity cultivation, logistics assurance, financial empowerment, and model innovation.
    *   Utilizes zero-tariff policy for African products entering China.
*   **Logistics System:**
    *   China-Europe Railway Express (Changsha).
    *   Huaihua International Land Port.
    *   Xiang-Yue-Fei (Hunan-Guangdong-Africa) Sea-Rail Intermodal Transport.
    *   Yueyang Chenglingji Port River-Sea Intermodal Transport.
    *   Changsha Huanghua International Airport Aviation Port.
*   **Expert Analysis:** Li Yi, Deputy Researcher at the Chinese Academy of International Trade and Economic Cooperation, stated that these measures effectively resolve Hunan's inherent constraints as an inland region, transforming it from a hinterland to a frontier of opening-up.

# Event Name

Review of the 5th Anniversary of the Global Development Initiative (GDI) and its Implementation Outcomes

## Event Overview

On September 21, 2026, *People’s Daily* published two major editorial pieces on its front page (Page 02) marking the fifth anniversary of the proposal of the Global Development Initiative (GDI). Both articles were released in the early morning hours of September 21, 2026. The articles retrospectively analyze the initiative's progress since its proposal by Xi Jinping on September 21, 2021, at the 76th Session of the United Nations General Assembly. The coverage highlights the transition of the GDI from a Chinese proposition to an international consensus, detailing its institutional mechanisms, financial mobilization, practical cooperation projects, and strategic alignment with the UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

## Core Facts

**Origin and Timeline**
*   **Proposal Date:** September 21, 2021.
*   **Proposer:** Xi Jinping, during the general debate of the 76th session of the UN General Assembly.
*   **Anniversary Context:** The reports published on September 21, 2026, mark exactly five years since the proposal.
*   **Strategic Period:** 2026 is identified as the opening year of China’s "15th Five-Year Plan" (FYP).

**Core Principles ("Six Adherences")**
The articles cite the GDI’s systematic solution based on six principles:
1.  Development first.
2.  People-centered.
3. 普惠包容 (Inclusive and benefiting all).
4.  Innovation-driven.
5.  Harmony between humanity and nature.
6.  Action-oriented.

**Statistical Data (Cited in Reports)**
*   **Support Base:** Over 130 countries and international organizations actively support and participate in the initiative; 88 countries have formally joined the "Friends of the Global Development Initiative" group.
*   **Funding:** Over $23 billion in funds have been mobilized through mechanisms such as the China-UN Peace and Development Fund and the Global Development and South-South Cooperation Fund.
*   **Projects:** More than 2,000 cooperative projects have been carried out.
*   **Training:** Over 200,000 individuals from various fields have been trained.
*   **Beneficiaries:** Projects have benefited the general public in over 120 countries.
*   **China’s Economic Contribution:** China has maintained an average annual contribution rate of approximately 30% to global economic growth over the past five years.

**Key Mechanisms and Platforms**
*   **Conferences:** China has hosted a High-Level Meeting on the GDI during the UN General Assembly sessions for four consecutive years.
*   **Forums:** The establishment of the Global Shared Development Action Forum.
*   **Centers:** Leadership in establishing the Global Development Promotions Centre; collaboration with over 80 countries/regions/organizations to build a network of such centers.
*   **UN Engagement:** The UN system initiative promotion working group has attracted participation from over 20 development agencies.
*   **Special Funds:**
    *   Global Development and South-South Cooperation Fund upgraded to $4 billion.
    *   140 billion USD in special funds established in partnership with domestic and foreign financial institutions.
    *   Establishment of a Global Development Fund Pool.

**Specific Project Examples Cited**
*   **Nigeria:**菌草 (Juncao) technology demonstration farm for food security.
*   **Grenada:** Agricultural expertise transfer for crop yield improvement.
*   **Laos:** Mengsong Wind Power Project (creating over 1,000 jobs, reducing carbon emissions by 1.56 million tons annually).
*   **Ethiopia:** Luban Workshop (揭牌于 2021, dedicated to skilled talent training for industrialization).
*   **Lao PDR:** UNICEF合作 nutrition project training 1,900 frontline workers.
*   **Pakistan/Ethiopia/Solomon Islands/etc.:** "Mazu" weather intelligent early warning system applied in 7 countries.

**Global Development Context**
*   **UN Report:** The *2026 Sustainable Development Goals Report* indicates that among 139 comparable SDG indicators, only 36% are progressing well, nearly half are slow, and 15% are regressing.
*   **"15th FYP" Goals:** Emphasizes deepening solidarity with developing countries, supporting the joint self-strengthening of the Global South, and promoting global poverty reduction cooperation.

## Known Current Impact

*   **Policy Alignment:** The GDI is being integrated into China’s "15th Five-Year Plan," signaling continued state-level commitment beyond the initial five-year mark.
*   **Institutionalization:** The creation of the "Global Development Promotions Centre" network and the "Global Development Academy" indicates a shift from ad-hoc projects to structured institutional cooperation.
*   **Digital/AI Governance:** The September 13, 2026, BRICS AI initiatives represent a new frontier for the GDI, addressing the "digital divide" and "intelligent divide" as critical global development challenges.
*   **Multilateral Reinforcement:** The GDI is actively used to reinforce China’s diplomatic ties with the SCO, Latin America, BRICS, and African nations, positioning it as a unifying framework against单边主义 (unilateralism).

## Event Conclusion

The synthesis of Articles #406 and #407 confirms that as of September 21, 2026, the Global Development Initiative (GDI) is being officially reviewed and promoted by the Chinese government as a mature, impactful, and institutionalized framework for international cooperation. The event marks the 5th anniversary of its proposal, highlighting a transition from concept to concrete action involving over 130 participating nations and significant financial mobilization. The coverage underscores China’s strategic intent to position the GDI as the primary vehicle for addressing the stagnation of the UN 2030 Sustainable Development Goals, particularly through new focuses on digital cooperation, AI governance, and South-South collaboration.

# Event: Fourth-Eighty-Eighth WorldSkills Competition in Shanghai (2026)

## Event Overview

The 48th WorldSkills Competition is a major international vocational skills competition scheduled to be held in Shanghai, China, from September 22 to September 27, 2026. The event is themed "Skills Change the World, Skills Illuminate Futures." It follows the "One Competition, Two Conferences" model, which simultaneously hosts the WorldSkills Competition, the WorldSkills Conference, and the WorldSkills Expo. This event marks the first time Shanghai has hosted the competition.

According to a preview article published in *People's Daily* on September 21, 2026, the competition aims to showcase global vocational skills, promote policy dialogue, and offer public experience activities. China, a dominant force in recent WorldSkills history, has assembled a full delegation to participate in all 64 competition items.

## Core Facts

*   **Event Name:** 48th WorldSkills Competition (第四十八届世界技能大赛).
*   **Location:** Shanghai, China. Primary venue is the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai).
*   **Dates:** September 22–27, 2026.
*   **Theme:** "Skills Change the World, Skills Illuminate Futures."
*   **Organizer:** WorldSkills International (founded 1950).
*   **Scale:**
    *   80 countries and regions registered.
    *   68 countries and regions sending 1,385 competitors.
    *   Total of 64 competition items (a record high).
*   **China's Participation:**
    *   Delegation size: 220 people.
    *   Composition: 71 competitors, 64 experts, 64 translators, and staff.
    *   Competitors represent 21 provinces.
    *   Average age of competitors: 21 years.
    *   Over 60% of competitors and staff are from technical schools.
    *   The team will compete in all 64 items.
    *   China's historical record (since joining in 2010): 93 gold, 41 silver, 28 bronze medals, and 71 honorable mentions. China has ranked first in both the gold medal count and overall team score for the last four consecutive competitions.
*   **New Competition Items (7 added this session):**

    2.  Drone Systems (无人机系统)

    4.  Software Testing (软件测试)
    5.  Digital Interactive Media Design (数字交互媒体设计)
    6.  Oral Restorative Technology (口腔修复工艺技术)
    7.  Retail (零售)
*   **Accompanying Events:**
    *   **WorldSkills Conference:** A policy dialogue platform expected to host ~4,000 attendees, including government officials, vocational education representatives, industry leaders, and youth delegates.
    *   **WorldSkills Expo:** Features pavilions for WorldSkills member countries, sponsor enterprises, and a "One Province, One Skill" exhibition highlighting Chinese high-skilled talent. The China Pavilion includes displays of national heavy equipment (e.g., carrier rockets, deep-sea exploration vessels) and traditional crafts (e.g., rubbing, ancient book restoration).
*   **Logistics and Accessibility:**
    *   Venue area: Approximately 70,000 square meters across 8 venues at the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai).
    *   Public access: All competition viewing and experience activities are **free** but require advance registration.

    *   Volunteers: Over 3,800 volunteers recruited for multi-language services.

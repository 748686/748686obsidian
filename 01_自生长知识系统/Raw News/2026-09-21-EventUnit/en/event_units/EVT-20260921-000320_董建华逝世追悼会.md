# Event Overview
On September 20, 2026, a public memorial ceremony (Gongji) for Tung Chee-hwa, former Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR), was held at the Hong Kong Funeral Home. The ceremony was presided over by Leung Chun-ying, Vice Chairman of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC). A detailed biography and obituary were subsequently published in the *People's Daily* on September 21, 2026. Tung died on September 8, 2026, at the age of 89.

## Core Facts

**Death**
*   **Name:** Tung Chee-hwa (董建华).
*   **Date of Death:** September 8, 2026, at 21:57.
*   **Location:** Hong Kong.
*   **Cause:** Illness.
*   **Age:** 89.
*   **Origin:** Born in Shanghai (May 1937); ancestral home Zhoushan, Zhejiang. Moved to Hong Kong in 1947.

**Biography and Career**
*   **Education:** Graduated from the University of Liverpool in 1960 with a Bachelor of Science in Marine Engineering.
*   **Business:** Chairman and CEO of Orient Overseas (International) Limited; Chairman of King Ship Company. Served as Chairman of the Hong Kong Shipowners Association (1976–1977).
*   **Political Roles:**
    *   Chief Executive of HKSAR, Term 1 and 2 (July 1997 – July 2005).*
    *   Vice Chairman of the CPPCC (10th, 11th, 12th, and 13th sessions).*
*   **Other Positions:** Founding Chairman of the US-China Exchange Foundation; Founding Chairman of the Building a Better Hong Kong Foundation; Co-convenor of the Hong Kong Rebound Alliance.
*   **Honors:** "Outstanding Contributor to 'One Country, Two Systems'" National Honor Title (awarded Sept 29, 2019); Grand Bauhinia Medal.

**The Memorial Ceremony**
*   **Date:** September 20, 2026.
*   **Location:** Hong Kong Funeral Home.
*   **Time:** Commenced at 09:00.
*   **Presided by:** Leung Chun-ying (Vice Chairman, CPPCC; Director of the Central Government's Liaison Office to the HKSAR).
*   **Eulogy Delivered by:** Shi Taifeng (Member of the Politburo; Vice Chairman, CPPCC; Director, Hong Kong and Macao Work Office of the CPC Central Committee).
*   **Attending Officials:** Wang Dongfeng (Vice Chairman, CPPCC); Xia Baolong (Director, HK/MOAAC); John Lee (Chief Executive of HKSAR); Central government representatives; HKSAR principal officials; NPC deputies and CPPCC members from HK; social representatives.
*   **Protocol:** Coffin draped with the national flag of the PRC. Attendees bowed three times before the portrait and paid their last respects.

**Central Leadership Response**
*   President Xi Jinping and other central leaders (Li Qiang, Zhao Leji, Wang Huning, Cai Qi, Ding Xuexiang, Li Xi, Han Zheng, Hu Jintao*) extended condolences and expressed deep sympathy to the family through various channels.

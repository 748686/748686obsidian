# National Fall Job Fair (2026)

## Event Overview

On September 20, 2026, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security of China launched the "Job-Guiding the Future: 2026 National Urban Joint Recruitment Fall Special." This initiative is designed to support employment for the 2027 cohort of university graduates and previously unemployed graduates. The event operates as part of the broader "National Urban Joint Recruitment" system, a government-coordinated mechanism where cities pool resources to organize large-scale recruitment efforts.

The fair focuses on bridging the gap between young job seekers and employers, particularly in strategic industries aligned with national development goals, including the digital economy, green economy, advanced manufacturing, and modern services. The launch was reported by *People’s Daily* on September 21, 2026.

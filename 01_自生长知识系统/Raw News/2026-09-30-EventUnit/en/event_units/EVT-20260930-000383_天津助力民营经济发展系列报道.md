# Event Name
Tianjin Series of Reports on Supporting the Development of the Private Economy (2026-09-30)

## Event Overview
On September 30, 2026, *People's Daily* (People.cn) published a special series on Page 13 titled "Encouraging Private Enterprises to Dare to Break Through and Act." The series focuses on Tianjin's efforts to support private economic development through financial services, business environment optimization, and cadre training. The reports highlight specific initiatives in Beichen District, Hexi District, and Xiqing District, coinciding with the context of the 2026 China Private Enterprise Investment and Financing Discussion Meeting.

## Core Facts
*   **Financial Support (Beichen District & General):** Agricultural Bank of China (ABC) Tianjin Branch and Postal Savings Bank of China (PSBC) Tianjin Branch are innovating financial products to address financing difficulties for private enterprises in transformation, construction, and growth phases.
    *   ABC Tianjin Beichen Branch assisted Tianjiang Data Technology Co., Ltd. (a Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei IDC service provider formerly a heavy machinery factory) with six syndicated loans during its industrial transformation.
    *   ABC Tianjin Branch provided an 8 million yuan "Tech e-Loan" to Tianjin Deshengyuan Animal Husbandry Co., Ltd., a municipal-level agricultural industrialization leading enterprise, to support its green circular agriculture model.
    *   PSBC Tianjin Branch launched a "Small Enterprise Construction Loan" for Tianjin Grrui New Metal Materials Co., Ltd., addressing cash flow mismatches during the construction phase of new factories.
    *   PSBC Tianjin Dagang Branch provided a 15 million yuan loan to Hanyang Heavy Industry based on intellectual property credit enhancement, activating patent assets for a petrochemical enterprise.
*   **Business Environment Optimization (Beichen District):** Shuangkou Town in Beichen District implemented a full-process agency assistance mechanism to streamline administrative approvals.
    *   Tianjin Wantailida Plastic Products Co., Ltd. received assistance for its 150 million yuan new factory project in the Beichen District Medical Devices Industrial Park, saving approximately 3 months of construction time compared to self-handling procedures.
    *   The town attracted the Jim International Adventure World project to a 450-mu idle land site, completing approvals in 2 months and achieving over 40 million yuan in revenue within two years of opening.
    *   Beichen District Development and Reform Commission reported that leading cadres visited and served enterprises over 3,000 times and solved more than 500 problems in the first half of 2026.
*   **Industrial Policy and Cluster Development (Hexi District):** Hexi District issued the "Three-Year Action Plan for Promoting High-Quality Development of the Private Economy (2026-2028)" in July 2026, featuring 18 measures across 5 aspects.
    *   In the first half of 2026, Hexi District added 2,642 private enterprises (97.4% of new enterprises), with private economy value-added reaching 19.999 billion yuan, a year-on-year increase of 13.2%.
    *   Pascaline (PaciNI), a embodied intelligence enterprise, settled in the 764 Embodied Intelligence Industry Theme Park in 2025; its valuation grew from approximately 1 billion yuan to over 10 billion yuan, attracting supply chain companies like Shiyun Technology and Shutu Technology.
    *   The district attracted 46 high-quality private enterprises in 2026 through platforms such as the 2026 China International Intelligent Robotics Exhibition.
    *   Chen Tang Management Committee provided customized financial and scenario docking solutions for commercial aerospace enterprises like Huoyan Position and Sutai Kailing, attracting Galaxy Space, Guoyu Xingkong, and Qishang宇航.
    *   Hexi District visited over 50,000 market entities in two years and facilitated credit repair for enterprises through a "two documents delivered simultaneously" mechanism (administrative penalty decision + credit repair guidance), issuing over 200 credit repair notices.
    *   First-half 2026 private capital investment in Hexi District exceeded 2.5 billion yuan, supported by the revitalization of 306,800 square meters of existing stock area.
*   **Cadre Training and Service (Xiqing District):** Xiqing District implemented the "Qing" Feng Outstanding Training Camp" economic cadre cultivation plan to train officials as "long-term partners" for enterprises.
    *   72 cadres were selected for a 6-month program combining general training (14 topics including economics, finance, algorithms) and practical drills (project attraction, policy advice).
    *   Cadres visited 45 enterprises, including Tianjin Daqiao Wire & Cable Co., Ltd., collecting 120 demands.
    *   A specific case involved Song Fukang, Director of the Economic Development Office of Dasi Town, assisting Tianjin Daqiao Wire & Cable in applying for ultra-long special treasury bonds to resolve capital turnover issues, enabling the launch of a new智能化 production line.
    *   As of the end of June 2026, Xiqing District had 125,100 private enterprises (98.35% of operating entities), up from 118,000 in 2024.
    *   First-half 2026 private economy value-added in Xiqing District was 24.091 billion yuan, with private above-scale industrial output value reaching 37.505 billion yuan.

# Event: People's Daily Comprehensive Report on September 30, 2026

## Event Overview

On September 30, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive daily edition covering major political, policy, diplomatic, industrial, and social developments in China. The reporting centered on the commencement of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period (2026–2030), featuring high-level directives from the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) leadership, government policy announcements, foreign diplomatic activities, and national development milestones.

The event encompasses several distinct sub-events:
1.  **Domestic Governance:** Xi Jinping's instructions on building a higher-level "Safe China" and the commemoration of Zou Jiahua's 100th birthday.
2.  **Policy & Planning:** Issuance of the "15th Five-Year Plan for Cultural Heritage Protection and Inheritance" and agricultural modernization targets.
3.  **Economic Policy:** Adjustments to vehicle and vessel tax policies for new energy vehicles (NEVs) and monetary tool adjustments by the People's Bank of China.
4.  **Diplomacy:** Activities related to the China-BRICS Digital Trade Expo, relations with Japan, Papua New Guinea, and Arab nations, and the launch of the Lao-China Railway intercity service.
5.  **Science & Technology:** Record-breaking drilling by the "Mengxiang" (Dream) vessel and advancements in agricultural machinery.

## Core Facts

### Political Leadership and Governance
*   **Safe China Initiative:** General Secretary Xi Jinping issued important instructions on September 29, 2026, affirming achievements since the 18th National Congress and calling for a higher level of "Safe China" construction to create a new境界 of "Chinese Governance" (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 515).
*   **Zou Jiahua Commemoration:** A symposium was held in Beijing on September 29 to commemorate the 100th birth anniversary of Zou Jiahua, a former Vice Premier and Vice Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress. Zhao Leji attended, and Zhang Guoqing delivered a eulogy praising Zou's political character and reform spirit (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 516).
*   **Party Building Theory:** The Chinese Academy of Social Sciences hosted a theoretical symposium on Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building on September 29, emphasizing its role in forging the world's strongest Marxist ruling party (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 539).

### "15th Five-Year Plan" Period Initiation
*   **Agriculture:** At a September 29 State Council Information Office press conference, Agriculture Minister Zhang Zhu stated that China aims to reach a grain comprehensive production capacity of approximately 1.45 trillion jin (725 million metric tons) by 2030. Summer grain产量 exceeded 300 billion jin for the first time, and autumn grain harvest is underway with corn plantings increasing (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 523).
*   **Science and Technology:** Minister of Science and Technology Yin Hejun stated that the "15th Five-Year" period is a critical攻坚期 for building a科技强国. Key focuses include basic research, key core technology breakthroughs, and the integration of science, industry, and finance. The 3 international sci-tech innovation centers (Beijing, Shanghai, Greater Bay Area) account for 58.8% of national R&D expenditure and 81.1% of PCT international patent applications (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 524).
*   **Cultural Heritage:** The General Offices of the CCP Central Committee and the State Council issued the *Cultural Heritage Protection and Inheritance "15th Five-Year" Plan*. Key targets include completing the second national survey of moveable cultural relics and intangible cultural heritage by 2030, and establishing a national cultural heritage resource database (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 518).

### Economic and Fiscal Policy Adjustments
*   **Vehicle and Vessel Tax:** Starting in 2027, China will cancel the halved vehicle and vessel tax for energy-saving cars and the exemption for pure electric commercial vehicles, plug-in hybrids, and fuel cell commercial vehicles. The policy shift reflects the maturation of the NEV industry, where July 2026 monthly sales penetration exceeded 60% and cumulative penetration exceeded 50%. Pure electric and fuel cell *passenger* vehicles remain exempt as they do not fall under the engine displacement tax category (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 519).
*   **Monetary Policy:** On September 29, the People's Bank of China announced adjustments:
    *   PSL (Pledged Supplemental Lending) rate cut by 25 basis points to 1.5%.
    *   Expansion of PSL eligibility to include "six networks": water networks, new power grids, computing power networks, next-generation communication networks, urban underground pipe networks, and logistics networks.
    *   Increase of the technology innovation and technological transformation re-lending quota by 200 billion yuan to 1.4 trillion yuan, with the support ratio raised to 100%.
    *   Increase of agricultural and small-micro enterprise re-lending quota by 500 billion yuan to 4.85 trillion yuan, including a 300 billion yuan increase for private enterprises (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 525).
*   **Housing Loan Subsidy:** Starting October 1, 2026, eligible families purchasing their first home (area ≤120 sqm, price ≤1.5 million yuan) with a new commercial housing loan can receive a 1 percentage point annual interest subsidy from the finance department, capped at 1 million yuan loan amount and 5 years duration. The policy implementation period is tentatively set for one year (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 527).

### Diplomatic and International Engagement
*   **丁薛祥 at CIEC 2026:** Vice Premier Ding Xueliang attended the 2026 annual meeting of the China International Environmental Cooperation Commission for Sustainable Development. He proposed three suggestions: promoting green and low-carbon development, cultivating green productivity through tech innovation, and improving global environmental governance through open cooperation. The 8th CIEC was officially approved for establishment (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 517).
*   **Digital Trade Expo:** The 5th Global Digital Trade Expo was held in Hangzhou with participants from 163 countries. The *Global Digital Trade Development Report 2026* reported that 2025 global digital trade exports reached $7.75 trillion, growing 7.1% year-on-year. WTO Deputy Director-General Zhang Xiangchen stated AI could increase global trade by 34–37% by 2040 if widely adopted (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 531).
*   **Japan Relations:** Vice Premier He Lifeng met with the Japan Association for the Promotion of International Trade delegation. Separately, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs issued a strong statement on the 54th anniversary of the Sino-Japanese Joint Statement (Sept 29), urging Japan to return to the original intent of the four political documents, citing concerns over Japanese leaders' remarks on Taiwan and "re-militarization" (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 532, 536).
*   **Other Diplomatic Events:**
    *   Celebration of the 50th anniversary of diplomatic relations with Papua New Guinea (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 533).
    *   "World Dialogue on China Studies: Arab Countries Session" held in Cairo, focusing on Sino-Arab cultural exchange and global governance (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 534).
    *   Chinese Embassy in North Korea held sacrificial ceremonies for Chinese People's Volunteers martyrs in Hwacheon and Pyeongchang counties (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 535).
    *   Launch of the Moutai-Vientiane intercity train service on the Lao-China Railway, enabling same-day commutes (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 538).

### Scientific and Industrial Milestones
*   **"Mengxiang" Drilling Ship:** Completed deep-sea drilling in the South China Sea at a water depth of 4,100 meters, setting a new record for China's self-operated deep-water drilling with a maximum depth of 4,929.17 meters (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 526).
*   **Agricultural Mechanization:** In Henan Province, advanced machinery such as 200-horsepower harvesters can process one *mu* of land in 10 minutes. The province is piloting subsidy distributions based on machinery operation volume rather than purchase count, supporting the integration of AI, big data, and new energy in farming equipment (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 528).
*   **Xinjiang Infrastructure:** The Dashi Gorge Water Conservancy Project in Xinjiang had its first two units grid-connected on September 29, expected to generate 1.893 billion kWh annually upon full completion (*Renmin Ribao*, Art. 521).

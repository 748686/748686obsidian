# Event Unit: People's Daily International Edition Foreigner Stories Column (2026-09-30)

## Event Overview

On September 30, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Renmin Ribao) published a special column on **Page 17 (International)**, titled "Stories of Me and China." This edition featured three narrative articles profiling foreigners deeply integrated into Chinese society across different regions and disciplines, accompanied by editorial credits. The section is curated by editors Bai Yang and Li Anqi.

The three primary stories cover:
1.  **Hamza Boukili (Bern Han Zheng):** A Moroccan-French mathematical PhD and AI entrepreneur based in Shanghai.
2.  **Hansen Nick René (Xie Wanke):** A Luxembourgish volunteer who supported poverty alleviation in Guangxi.
3.  **Muhammad Alib (Mu Ruo Bai):** A Pakistani international student learning Qin Opera in Xi'an.

### Subject 1: Hamza Boukili (Bern Han Zheng)
*   **Nationality:** Dual Moroccan-French citizen.
*   **Location:** Shanghai.
*   **Profession:** Mathematics teacher at the Sino-French Paris Engineer College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Founder of Shanghai Yunsuan Technology Co., Ltd.
*   **Background:** Born in 1991. First came to China in 2016 for a exchange program at Tongji University. Earned a PhD in Applied Mathematics in 2020 and joined SJTU.
*   **Business:** Founded Yunsuan Technology in 2024. The company develops AI-driven "smart brain" systems for buildings/factories to optimize HVAC operations, save costs, and reduce carbon emissions.
*   **Key Event:** In April 2025, he was the **only foreigner present** when General Secretary Xi Jinping visited the "Model Speed Space" (Moshu Kongjian) large model innovation ecosystem community in Xuhui District, Shanghai. Boukili introduced his entrepreneurial story to Xi using fluent Chinese.
*   **Status:** Received his Foreigner Permanent Residence ID card on his birthday in 2024. He describes himself as a "New Shanghainese."
*   **Quote:** "Learning Chinese is a lifelong endeavor." / "Those who truly value things generally cannot obtain them easily; effort itself is the definition of value."

### Subject 2: Hansen Nick René (Xie Wanke)
*   **Nationality:** Luxembourgish.
*   **Location:** Zhadong Village, Hechi City, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region.
*   **Role:** Retired Luxembourg police officer turned volunteer; referred to as the "foreign assistant" to Xie Wanju, the former First Secretary of Zhadong Village.
*   **Timeline:** Arrived in March 2018 as a backpacker. Volunteered for over 8 years.
*   **Activities:** Assisted in building fruit sheds, connecting electricity/water, planting passion fruit and watermelons, surveying mountain roads, and fundraising.
*   **Observation on Poverty Alleviation:** Attributes success to the "persistence of not letting anyone fall behind" (citing the construction of a road for only three households) and the "unity spirit" toward common goals involving government, grassroots cadres, enterprises, and villagers.
*   **Current Focus:** Village development has shifted from basic infrastructure to sustainable industry, youth return, and rural tourism.
*   **Name Change:** Received the Chinese name "Xie Wanke" from Xie Wanju. "Xie" shares the surname (family bond); "Wan" is from Xie's generational name; "Ke" is a homophone for Nick and means "lovable/humorous."
*   **Recognition:** Received a pennant reading "Heart tied to Zhadong, selfless dedication" upon the village's poverty alleviation success in 2020.

### Subject 3: Muhammad Alib (Mu Ruo Bai)
*   **Nationality:** Pakistani.
*   **Location:** Xi'an, Shaanxi Province.
*   **Affiliation:** International student at the School of Materials Science and Engineering, Northwestern Polytechnical University.
*   **Timeline:** Arrived in Xi'an in 2025.
*   **Activity:** Enrolled in an elective course "Qin Opera Stage Art Presentation" in the spring of 2025.
*   **Classmates:** Included students from Nigeria, Cameroon, and other countries.
*   **Instructor:** Hui Minli, President of the Xi'an Yisu Society (a renowned Qin Opera troupe).
*   **Performance:** Performed as a "Wusheng" (martial male role) in the classic piece *Three Drops of Blood* (San Di Xue). Learned to use props like swords and cloud whisks.
*   **Cultural Insight:** Described Qin Opera as touching Chinese emotions regarding family, responsibility, and loyalty. Encourages deeper cultural exchange.

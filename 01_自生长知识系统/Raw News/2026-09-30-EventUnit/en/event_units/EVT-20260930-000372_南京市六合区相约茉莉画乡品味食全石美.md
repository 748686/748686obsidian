# Event: Cultural and Tourism Development in Liuhe District, Nanjing

## Event Overview
In late September 2026, the People's Daily reported on the integrated cultural and tourism development strategies of the Liuhe District in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province. The district is leveraging its local intangible cultural heritage—specifically the folk song "Jasmine Flower"—and its natural geological and ecological resources to build a "urban pastoral leisure vacation destination." The initiative aims to convert ecological products into economic value through the fusion of culture, tourism, agriculture, and sports, promoting the regional brand "Jasmine Painted Township, Wholesome Food and Stone Beauty" (Jīnmò Huàxiāng, Shíquán Shíměi).

## Core Facts

### Cultural Inheritance and Innovation
*   **Folk Song Heritage:** Liuhe District is identified as the origin of the classic folk song "Jasmine Flower."
*   **New Creations:** The newly composed work "Jasmine Blooms for Happiness," created by the Liuhe District Jasmine Flower Art Troupe, was performed at the 2026 Jiangsu Folk Song Conference and the national "Village Songs Ring Loud" showcase in the summer of 2026.
*   **Diverse Platforms:** The district has established a "Jasmine Flower Folk Song Culture Month" and created themed public cultural spaces within the district museum, cultural center, and library.
*   **Media and Arts:** An original stage play titled "The Masses' League – The People's 'Jasmine Flower'" was selected for the China Federation of Literary and Art Circles 2025 Youth Literary and Artistic Creation Support Plan. Short video series named "Folk Songs of Liuhe" have been released to modernize the presentation of traditional resources.
*   **Tourism Integration:** Events such as the Jinxiu Lake Jasmine Flower Music Festival, firework carnivals, and summer cultural tourism seasons connect musical performances with lakeside tourism and consumer activities.

### Ecological Value and Geological Resources
*   **Ecosystem Components:** The district possesses wetlands, rivers, hills, forests, and unique geological relics. Key sites include Chishan Lake, Jinxiu Lake, Daquan Lake, and Pingshan Forest Park.
*   **Seasonal Events (Summer/Autumn 2026):**
    *   The 1st Jiangsu Province Water Lily Variety Exhibition.
    *   The 2026 Chishan Lake Wetland Lotus Exhibition.
    *   Parent-child theme research at Jinxiu Lake Wildlife Kingdom.
    *   "Jianshan Creek Valley" stream trekking at Pingshan Forest Park.
*   **Geological Attractions:**
    *   **Jiangsu Liuhe National Geopark:** Features the geological wonder of Guizi Mountain Stone Column Forest.
    *   **Nanjing Yuhua Stone Geopark:** Hosts Yuhua Stone exhibitions, gravel layer profiles of the Yuhuatai Formation, and outdoor trading markets for geological science popularization.

### Sports and Tourism Integration
*   **Events Held in 2026:**
    *   Colorful Zhuzhen Bicycle Race around the Nanjing Metropolitan Area (China Grand Canal Series, Liuhe Station).
    *   2026 World Orienteering Masters Jiangsu Division, Liuhe·Pingshan Station (10th Anniversary).
    *   2026 China Grand Canal Sailing Series (Nanjing Jinxiu Lake Station) and Nanjing Sailing Open.
    *   2026 "Big Rivers" National Mass Open Water Swimming Series (Nanjing Jinxiu Lake Station).

### Agricultural and Cultural Fusion
*   **Longpao Street:** Utilizes the provincial intangible cultural heritage skill of "Longpao Crab Roe Soup Dumpling" making. It combines specialty foods and agricultural product exhibitions with Yangtze River wetland sightseeing and seasonal rural leisure, supported by national "Four Seasons Village Evening" platforms.
*   **Jinxiu Lake Tourism Resort:** Integrates Jasmine culture, grape picking, winter fishing customs, and lakeside water sports. It leverages the "Ecological Island" experimental zone for coordinated ecological governance and leisure resort quality improvement.
*   **Zhuzhen Town (Daquan Lake):** Features contiguous tea gardens and rural homestays. Strategies include combining red educational research with agricultural experiences (tea picking/processing). The district facilitated the landing of Jiangsu Province's first水土保持 (soil and water conservation) green credit facility to support sustainable development.

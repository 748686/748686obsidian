## Core Facts

**1. Flexible Electronics Research and Development**
*   **Lead Institution:** The National Key Laboratory of Flexible Electronics (NKLFLEX), Tsinghua University, and the Zhejiang Tsinghua Flexible Electronics Research Institute.
*   **Key Innovations:**
    *   Development of flexible organic semiconductors and integration of rigid inorganic materials into flexible substrates via structural design.
    *   A manufacturing platform in Jiaxing, Zhejiang, achieving over 95% production yield for flexible integrated devices and circuits.
    *   Creation of a flexible chip microsystem for space applications, which underwent 3 years of ground testing and irradiation tests before launching on the Tianzhou-6 spacecraft. It completed a stable 15-month orbital experiment outside the cabin.
    *   Development of "electronic skin" for embodied intelligence robots capable of perceiving pressure, temperature, and texture, enabling fine motor control.
    *   Medical applications include wearable systems for blood glucose, blood pressure, and blood oxygen monitoring, as well as wireless physiological monitoring systems for premature infants.
*   **Contributing Authors:** Feng Xue, Professor at the School of Aerospace Engineering, Tsinghua University.
*   **International Partners/Collaborators:** Researchers from Northwestern University (USA), City University of Hong Kong, UC San Diego (USA), Tokyo University (Japan), and Stanford University (USA) are cited for specific technological contributions in flexible bioelectronics and sensing.

**2. Typhoon Forecasting Breakthrough**
*   **Publication:** A study was published in *Communications Earth & Environment* (a Nature journal).
*   **Core Achievement:** Creation of the world's first third-generation typhoon forecast model that fully couples the atmosphere, ocean, and ocean waves.
*   **Performance:** Five-year forecast verification shows that the error in predicting maximum typhoon intensity 3 days in advance was reduced by **82%** compared to the average of multiple international operational forecast systems. The error decreased from **18.8 m/s to 3.3 m/s**.
*   **Scientific Basis:**
    *   Nobel laureate Klaus Hasselmann's 1991 hypothesis that waves play a critical role in ocean-atmosphere exchange was central.
    *   Chinese scholars identified three original theoretical breakthroughs regarding wave effects:
        1.  Wave-induced turbulence mixes cold sub-surface water to the surface, reducing heat exchange.
        2.  Wave spray/smooths airflow, reducing friction and potentially strengthening typhoons.
        3.  Spray carries seawater temperature into the atmosphere, increasing heat transfer and strengthening typhoons.
*   **Validation:** The model was tested against 37 rapidly intensifying typhoons in the Northwest Pacific between 2020 and 2024.
*   **Collaboration:** An international team comprising researchers from China, the United States, and Europe.
*   **Contributing Authors:** Qiao Fangli, Researcher at the First Institute of Oceanography, Ministry of Natural Resources.

**3. Bio-inspired Drone Navigation**
*   **Publication:** Research published in *Nature*.
*   **Institution:** Delft University of Technology (Netherlands) and other institutions.
*   **Methodology:** A navigation strategy mimicking honeybee behavior, utilizing "path integration" and "visual memory."
    *   Drones perform a short learning flight near the start point ("home"), using optical flow sensors, altitude sensors, and gyroscopes to estimate trajectory while capturing panoramic images.
    *   A small neural network correlates these images with a "home vector," creating a lightweight visual memory for return navigation.
*   **Performance:**
    *   Simulation experiments indicate high efficiency with minimal learning area required.
    *   Real-world flight tests achieved a **100% return success rate** in environments ranging from 30 to 110 meters.
    *   In outdoor environments with wind interference (200–600 meters), success rates decreased but demonstrated strong adaptability.
*   **Advantages:** Eliminates the need for expensive 3D mapping, large computing power, or external navigation infrastructure.
*   **Commentary:** Guido de Croon (Delft University of Technology) and Duan Haibin (Beihang University) are cited.

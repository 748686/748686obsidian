## Event Overview

On September 30, 2026, Dai Bin, President of the China Tourism Academy, published an article titled *"Promoting the Deep Integration of Culture and Tourism"* (推动文旅深度融合发展) in the "Theory" section (Page 09) of *People's Daily* (人民日报). The article analyzes the internal logic and practical paths of using culture to empower social and economic development, specifically focusing on the synergy between cultural and tourism sectors. It references the "15th Five-Year Plan" for High-Quality Development and the "15th Five-Year Plan for Building a Leading Tourism Country" as key policy frameworks guiding this integration.

## Core Facts

*   **Policy Context**: The "15th Five-Year Plan" (十五五规划) proposes deepening the integration of culture and tourism ("以文塑旅、以旅彰文") to advance the construction of a leading tourism country.
*   **Strategic Vision**: President Xi Jinping is cited stating that the prospects for cultural and tourism integration are broad, and the industry should be developed into a pillar industry, a livelihood industry, and a happiness industry.
*   **Current Status**: The tourism industry has entered a new cycle of prosperity and a new stage of high-quality development. However, challenges remain, including homogenization of tourism projects, insufficient public service guarantees, lack of cultural connotation, and the need to enhance international influence.
*   **Core Principle**: Deep integration is not a simple superposition of culture and tourism but a deep交融 (intermingling) of two resources. Culture is the soul of tourism, and tourism is the carrier of culture.
*   **Four Key Pathways Identified**:
    1.  **Cultivating Cultural Connotations**: Integrating socialist advanced culture, revolutionary culture, and outstanding traditional Chinese culture into resource development, destination construction, and marketing. Incorporating folk customs and intangible cultural heritage (ICH) into tourism projects.
    2.  **Demonstration-Driven Approach**: Creating demonstration zones, boutique routes, and benchmark scenic spots. Building cultural hub cities and international rural tourism destinations. Utilizing national cultural parks (Great Wall, Grand Canal, Long March, Yellow River, Yangtze River) and cultural corridors (Silk Road, Great Tea Road) to create new national tourism routes.
    3.  **Linking Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary Industries**: Exploring "Culture + Tourism +" models.
        *   *Agriculture*: "Agri-cultural tourism" (农文旅) integration, sightseeing agriculture, rural B&Bs, agricultural festivals.
        *   *Manufacturing*: Industrial tourism, smart manufacturing observation, aviation/navigation science experience. Examples include converting old granaries into cultural consumption landmarks.
        *   *Services*: Integration with wellness, sports, education, and exhibitions. Outdoor sports, forest wellness, and study tours are highlighted as emerging consumption hotspots.
    4.  **Strengthening Talent Cultivation**: Building a multi-level, full-chain talent cultivation system through industry-education integration. Focusing on cross-disciplinary training for heritage protection, planning/operation, and digital tourism. Leveraging local talents such as ICH inheritors and rural cultural leaders.

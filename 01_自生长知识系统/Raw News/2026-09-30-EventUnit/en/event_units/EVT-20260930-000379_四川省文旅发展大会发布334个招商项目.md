## Event Overview

On September 28, 2026, the 2026 Sichuan Provincial Cultural and Tourism Development Conference and the 12th China (Sichuan) International Tourism Investment Conference was held in Chengdu. The event focused on promoting the "Beautiful Tianfu, Comfortable Sichuan" cultural tourism brand and releasing investment guidelines for key cultural tourism projects in Sichuan Province. The conference serves as a significant platform for expanding opening-up and deepening cooperation within Sichuan's cultural tourism industry.

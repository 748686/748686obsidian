# Event Name

2026 World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit Preparations and the 7th China Internet Infrastructure Resources Conference

## Event Overview

On September 29, 2026, two significant events regarding China’s internet ecosystem were announced in Beijing: the release of preparations for the 2026 World Internet Conference (WIC) Wuzhen Summit and the holding of the 7th China Internet Infrastructure Resources Conference. These events are thematically linked under the banner of "Open Source and Openness, Promoting Co-construction and Sharing," reflecting a coordinated push toward AI-driven infrastructure and open development ahead of the November summit.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Announcement/Holding:** September 29, 2026.
*   **Location of Press Conference:** Beijing (hosted by the World Internet Conference International Organization).
*   **Location of Infrastructure Conference:** Beijing.
*   **2026 WIC Wuzhen Summit Details:**
    *   **Dates:** November 2–5, 2026.
    *   **Location:** Wuzhen, Zhejiang Province (Permanent host city).
    *   **Theme:** "Open Source and Openness, Promoting Co-construction and Sharing — Joining Hands to Build a Community with a Shared Future in Cyberspace."
    *   **Key Figures:** Ren Xianliang (Secretary-General, World Internet Conference); Li Yanyi (Member of the Standing Committee of the Zhejiang Provincial Party Committee and Head of the Provincial Publicity Department); Wang Jian (Party Secretary of Tongxiang City).
    *   **Highlights:**
        *   Focus on AI open-source development concepts and practices.
        *   New activity "AI Wuzhen Dialogue" in the main forum.
        *   Number of sub-forums reaches a recent high.
        *   Launch of "Wuzhen Grand Lecture Hall" and "Wuzhen Talk · Open Source Youth Talk" for the public.
        *   Establishment of the national’s first open-source themed ecological experience hall.
        *   Addition of two new tracks to the "Connect to Wuzhen" Global Internet Competition: Open Source and OPC (One-Person Company).
*   **7th China Internet Infrastructure Resources Conference Details:**
    *   **Organizers:** China Internet Network Information Center (CNNIC), China Internet Association, and Computer Network Information Center of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.
    *   **Theme:** "Gathering Wisdom Towards Innovation, Building the Network for the Future."
    *   **Structure:** 1 main forum and 7 sub-forums covering domain applications, internet routing security, domain infrastructure intelligence/security, full identifier fusion, and agent identifiers/security.
    *   **Key Releases (3 Major科研成果/Research Achievements):**
        1.  **Resource Public Key Infrastructure (RPKI) Service Platform:** Showcasing China’s practice in RPKI system construction and its international influence in internet routing security.
        2.  **"Agent Identifier and Security Assurance Technology Beijing Key Laboratory":** Formally unveiled; focuses on agent identity identification, trusted certification, and full-chain innovation from basic theory to industrial application.
        3.  **Agent Trusted Infrastructure (ATI) Platform:** Officially went online, providing registration management, compliance certification, transparent logging, and trusted discovery capabilities based on mature network infrastructure.

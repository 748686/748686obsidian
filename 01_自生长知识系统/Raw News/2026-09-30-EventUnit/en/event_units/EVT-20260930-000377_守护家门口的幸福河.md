# Event Name
Environmental Remediation and Cultural-Tourism Development in Gongshu District, Hangzhou (2025–2026)

## Event Overview
This event documents the ongoing comprehensive整治 (remediation and improvement) of the Beijing-Hangzhou Grand Canal in Gongshu District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province. Reported by *People's Daily* on September 30, 2026, the coverage highlights the district's dual focus on ecological restoration and industrial heritage transformation into cultural and creative industries. The report positions these efforts as part of a broader strategy to build a "People's Canal" and "Tourist's Canal," leveraging the waterway for both public welfare and economic growth.

## Core Facts

**Ecological Indicators**
*   As of 2025, all 20 cross-sections of the main stream of the Grand Canal (Hangzhou section) stabilized at Water Quality Class III standards.
*   Local biodiversity indicators include visible fish schools and frequent sightings of egrets in riparian grasslands.

**Industrial Heritage and Creative Economy**
*   The Hangzhou Silk Printing and Dyeing United Factory site has been preserved with its original steel beam frameworks, red brick walls, old-style reeling machines, and giant exhaust fans.
*   This site has been converted into the "Silk Union 166 Cultural and Creative Park" (*Silian 166 Wenchuang Yuan*).
*   **Tenants:** As of 2025, over 80 enterprises have settled in the park, primarily in advertising, photography, and fashion design.
*   **Regional Metrics:** Gongshu District has cultivated 23 cultural and creative parks (streets). It aggregates 281 licensed cultural enterprises above designated size and 42 licensed cultural "New Three Items" enterprises.
*   **Policy Support:** The local government provides tangible support including rent subsidies and incubation platforms for cultural enterprises.

**Public Service and Social Welfare**
*   To address urban service imbalances and shortages in elderly and child care, the district established the "15-Minute Public Service Circle."
*   **Infrastructure:** 46 "Grand Canal Happy Homes" (*Dayunhe Xingfu Jiayuan*) have been built.
*   **Utilization:** These centers integrate elderly care, childcare, domestic services, and cultural leisure, serving an average of over 11,000 people daily.

**Cultural Tourism Performance**
*   **Resources:** The district integrates 151 intangible cultural heritages, 23 industrial heritage sites, and 30 museums of various levels.
*   **Product Development:** 12 cultural and tourism routes have been created, fostering one-stop consumption scenarios ("eating, accommodation, travel, shopping, and entertainment").
*   **Activities:** Weekend events at locations such as the Grand Canal Hanggang Park Amusement Garden feature non-heritage iron-flower throwing (*datiehua*), art exhibitions, and music performances.
*   **Visitor Statistics:** In 2025 (referred to as "last year" in the Sept 2026 report), total tourism visits reached 45.75 million, representing a year-on-year increase of 8.2%.

**Official Positioning**
*   Ao Yuxin, Secretary of the Gongshu District Party Committee, stated the district aims to polish the "Come to Hangzhou, Walk the Grand Canal" brand and build the "bright southern gateway" of the Grand Canal Scenic Area.

## Event Overview

On September 30, 2026, *People's Daily* published a special report (Page 11) detailing the financial services provided by China Construction Bank Hunan Branch (CCB Hunan) to support Hunan Province's strategic development goals. The article, authored under the editorial supervision of Luo Yan, Shi Zhe, and Cao Jiwei, describes how CCB Hunan anchors itself to the province's "Three Highs and Four News" blueprint. The bank is focusing on serving the real economy by constructing a financial service pattern with credit投放 as the main channel, government-bank docking as the focal point, and product innovation as the booster. This effort aims to precisely direct financial resources into advanced manufacturing, technological innovation, and opening up, thereby serving Hunan's goal of building three specific "high grounds": a national important advanced manufacturing high ground, a scientific and technological innovation high ground with core competitiveness, and an inland regional reform and opening-up high ground.

## Core Facts

*   **Strategic Alignment:** CCB Hunan is aligning its operations with Hunan Province's "Three Highs and Four News" vision to serve the "Three High Grounds" construction.
*   **Advanced Manufacturing Support:**
    *   As of June 2026, manufacturing loans totaled 162.02 billion yuan, an increase of 24.46 billion yuan from the beginning of the year, with a year-on-year growth of 13.9%.
    *   The bank acts as a member of the Hunan Provincial Key Industrial Multiplication Plan Working Group, implementing a "one chain, one policy, one chain, one special class" mechanism.
    *   Financial products deployed for supply chain transactions include "e-Xintong," "e-Piaotong," and "Tuoheling Dai."
    *   **Case Study (Sany Heavy Industry):** CCB Hunan provided equipment update loans, cost consulting services, and support for photovoltaic green projects. The bank facilitated the repatriation of funds from Sany's Hong Kong stock listing and provided financial solutions for overseas business expansion.
*   **Technological Innovation Financing:**
    *   As of June 2026, the bank has served 23,000 technology-based enterprises in the province and has won the "Hunan Province Technology Financial Service Outstanding Unit" award for four consecutive years.
    *   The bank leverages its full license synergy ("equity, loans, bonds, insurance, leasing") within the CCB group.
    *   Cumulative debt-to-equity swap asset placement reached 9.267 billion yuan. In 2025, a 300 million yuan "Green + Sci-Tech" debt-to-equity swap project was completed for Liangang Electromagnetic Materials.
    *   Over the past three years, the bank has underwritten and subscribed to sci-tech innovation bonds totaling 3.335 billion yuan.
    *   The bank launched the first nationally structured asset-guaranteed sci-tech innovation note of its kind (renewable type) and underwrote the first national 10-year "Green + Sci-Tech" dual-labeled bond.
    *   Digital evaluation tools such as "Technology Flow" and "Starlight STAR" have been introduced to convert intangible assets (R&D, patents) into credit lines. A specific case involved Beiyun Technology receiving a 60 million yuan credit line based on this evaluation.
    *   A risk compensation platform for knowledge value credit loans was co-built with the Hunan Provincial Department of Science and Technology, achieving full coverage across 14 cities and prefectures.
    *   The "Xiang Chuang Dai" (Hunan Innovation Loan) product supports university student entrepreneurship, having connected with over 200 projects and serviced 145, achieving full coverage of investment enterprises from the Hunan University Student Entrepreneurship Investment Fund.
    *   A three-tier professional network has been established: Provincial Branch Innovation Center – City Direct Sales Center – Park Characteristic Sub-branch, with tech finance direct sales in six key cities including Changsha.
*   **Reform and Opening Up Support:**
    *   The bank constructed a "1+3+8" long-term mechanism for serving free trade zones, centered on the provincial branch with落地 entities in Changsha, Yueyang, and Chenzhou, featuring 8 characteristic ecological smart outlets. Approximately 8,000 enterprises in the zone are serviced.
    *   Cross-border linkages facilitated the IPO fund repatriation for three Hunan enterprises listed in Hong Kong: Sany Heavy Industry, Mingming Hen很忙 (MingMing Hen Mang), and Red Star Cold Chain.
    *   CCB Hunan acted as the lead sponsor and underwriter for Red Star Cold Chain’s Hong Kong IPO, working with CCB International.
    *   The bank provides structural financing and cross-border supply chain export commercial invoice financing (e.g., for China National United Group members) to help enterprises hedge exchange rate risks and reduce financing costs.

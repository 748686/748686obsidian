# Event: Business Environment Optimization and High-Quality Development in Lanzhou New Area

## Event Overview
On September 30, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a report detailing the reform initiatives and achievements of the Lanzhou New Area in Gansu Province, China, regarding the optimization of its business environment. The report highlights how institutional innovations in administrative approval, legal regulation, and customs clearance have accelerated project implementation and supported high-quality economic development. The event centers on the claim that these reforms have directly contributed to the growth of local enterprises, such as Gansu Hailiang New Energy Materials Co., Ltd., and an increase in market entities within the region.

## Core Facts
- **Location**: Lanzhou New Area, Gansu Province, China.
- **Source Publication**: *People's Daily*, Page 07 (News), dated September 30, 2026.
- **Author**: Song Jiajun (Reporter).
- **Key Administrative Reform**: The time required for projects to obtain certificates after land delivery has been reduced to one day. This is achieved through reforms including "integration of inspection and registration," "delivery of certificate upon land handover," "delivery of certificate upon completion," and "delivery of certificate upon house handover."
- **First-Mover Initiatives**:
    - Pioneered the province-wide implementation of "sensory-free renewal" (automated renewal without application) for 43 categories of high-frequency licenses and certificates.
    - Implemented a "three notices delivered simultaneously" mechanism for credit repair.
    - Established dedicated service lines for livelihood services and business environment on the 12345 hotline.
- **Corporate Case Study**: Gansu Hailiang New Energy Materials Co., Ltd., described as Gansu’s first "hidden unicorn," has built one of the world’s largest single lithium-electric copper foil production bases with an annual output of 90,000 tons of high-end copper foil.
- **Business Registration Efficiency**:
    - Enterprise establishment follows a "one-network handling" model, completed within 30 minutes at zero cost.
    - Individual business registration utilizes a "second-pass" (instant approval) system.
    - "One license for one industry" reform covers 8 fields, compressing approval time by over 50%.
- **Legal and Regulatory Framework**:
    - Adheres to principles of "law-based regulation, inclusive regulation, service when needed, and non-disturbance when not."
    - Implemented "two light and one exemption" flexible law enforcement: 657 exempted penalty items, 83 first-time minor violations subject to no penalty, and "comprehensive one-time inspection" in 41 fields.
    - Established a linkage mechanism of "supervision + legal aid + arbitration" and a dispute resolution system involving universities, industry associations, law firms, and grassroots organizations.
    - More than 40% of administrative reconsideration cases are resolved through mediation, with a prior mediation success rate of 94.9%.
- **Trade and Opening Up**:
    - As a key node in the Belt and Road Initiative, the area has simplified customs procedures using measures like "one票 multiple vehicles" and "advance declaration."
    - Promoted multimodal transport and the "bonded + port" model.
    - In 2025, the Lanzhou New Area contributed 16% to the province's foreign trade incremental growth.
- **Economic Outcomes**:
    - Market entities have grown by over 20% annually on average.
    - Total market entities have exceeded 50,000.
    - 16 enterprises are listed among the top 100 private enterprises in Gansu.
- **Future Direction**: The area plans to deepen the creation of a first-class business environment, maintaining the "Xin Shi Xin Ban" (New Matters Handled with Heart) brand.

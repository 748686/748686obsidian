# Event Unit: Preservation of Flagship Species Builds Bridges of Friendship

## Event Overview

On September 27, 2026, two giant pandas, "Pingping" (平平) and "Fushuang" (福双), from the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding arrived at the Atlanta Zoo in the United States. They are scheduled to begin a ten-year cooperative residency. This event marks a continuation of the long-standing Sino-US giant panda conservation cooperation, following the return of the previous pair, "Lunlun" and "Yangyang," to China in 2024 upon the expiration of their agreement. The cooperation involves joint efforts in feeding, breeding, disease prevention, scientific research, public education, and habitat protection.

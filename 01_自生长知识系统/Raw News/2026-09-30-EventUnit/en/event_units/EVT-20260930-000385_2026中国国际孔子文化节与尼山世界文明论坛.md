# Event Name
2026 China International Confucius Culture Festival and 2026 Nishan Global Civilization Forum

## Event Overview
The 2026 China International Confucius Culture Festival and the 2026 Nishan Global Civilization Forum were held consecutively in Qufu, Shandong Province, on September 28–29, 2026. The events convened international guests to celebrate Confucian culture, facilitate cross-civilizational dialogue, and address contemporary global governance challenges through the lens of traditional Chinese philosophy. The Culture Festival ran from September 28 to October 7, 2026.

The Culture Festival theme was "Benevolence and Ritual for the World, Harmony and Great Unity" (Ren Li Tianxia, Hehe Datong). The Nishan Forum theme was "Civilization, Order, and Universal Justice" (Wenming · Zhixu · Gongli).

## Core Facts

### Logistics and Participation
*   **Dates:** September 28–29, 2026 (Culture Festival continued until October 7, 2026).
*   **Location:** Qufu, Shandong Province, China.
*   **Confucius Culture Festival Attendees:** Approximately 300 guests from 42 countries and regions.
*   **Nishan Forum Attendees:** Approximately 340 guests from 44 countries and regions.

### Organizers
*   **Confucius Culture Festival:** Hosted by the Shandong Provincial People’s Government, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism of China, and the China National Commission for UNESCO.
*   **Nishan Global Civilization Forum:** Hosted by the Shandong Provincial People’s Government, the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, the Office of Overseas Chinese Affairs of the State Council, the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences (CASS), and the International Confederation of Confucian Institutes.

### Key Ceremonies and Activities
*   **Opening Ceremonies:** Held on the afternoon of September 28 for both events.
*   **Confucius Education Award:** The 21st UNESCO Confucius Prize for Literacy was awarded during the Culture Festival opening. Projects from Lebanon, Mexico, and Thailand received the honor. The award, established in 2005, promotes Confucius’s concept of "education for all without discrimination."
*   **Sacrificial Ceremony:** The Bingwu Year Public Sacrifice to Confucius was held at the Dacheng Hall of the Confucius Temple to commemorate the 2,577th anniversary of Confucius’s birth. The ceremony featured four chapters: "Receiving Guests from the World," "Opening the Sacred Door," "Honoring the Supreme Sage," and "Operating the Great Harmony."
*   **Global Cloud Sacrifice:** A simultaneous online sacrifice was held, connecting海内外 (domestic and overseas) Confucian temples, academies, and cultural institutions. New AI-driven features included AI Confucian dialogue, certificate generation, and knowledge quizzes.
*   **Cultural Market:** The "Sifen Hui Wan Jia" (Benevolence Benefits Ten Thousand Homes) Confucius Culture Temple Fair opened on Wumaci Street in the ancient city of Qufu, featuring 46 stalls across intangible cultural heritage, food, and local products sectors.
*   **Parallel Forums:** Three parallel forums were held alongside the main Nishan Forum:
    1.  Cultural China · Nishan Global Overseas Chinese Forum (with representatives from 21 countries/regions).
    2.  Sports Culture Forum.
    3.  Ruler-Scholar (Rushang) Culture Forum.

### Academic Output
*   **Papers Received:** Over 600 academic papers from domestic and international scholars.
*   **Consensus Document:** The "2026 Nishan Global Civilization Forum Consensus" was adopted at the closing session on September 29, 2026. It advocates for civilizational exchange over隔阂 (estrangement), mutual learning over conflict, and tolerance over superiority. It calls for placing universal justice above power and interest.

### Key Speakers and Contributions
*   **Thomas Bach (IOC Honorary President):** Delivered a video message highlighting the convergence between Confucian ideals of holistic development and Olympic values ("a sound mind in a sound body").
*   **Jin Minqing (CASS):** Argued that "Li" (ritual/propriety) is the normative basis of order. He asserted that civilization order should serve as a solid guarantee for world peace amidst global deficits in peace, development, security, and governance.
*   **Chen Lai (Tsinghua University):** Distinguished between narrow and broad definitions of civilizational dialogue, proposing that dialogue should promote civilizational co-prosperity.
*   **Dipesh Chakrabarty (Jawaharlal Nehru University):** Described translation as a process of co-creation and a sustained knowledge production工程 (project), citing historical Buddhist translation between India and China.
*   **Edmond Mulet (UNESCO Representative to Ghana):** Proposed shifting global governance from "global管控" (control) to "global concerto" (collaborative harmony), citing Chinese "Ren, Yi, He" (Benevolence, Righteousness, Harmony) as guiding principles.
*   **Mu Fengling (Saint Petersburg State University of Economics and Finance):** Described civilization, order, and universal justice as the deep foundation of human coexistence and Confucianism as a spiritual resource for addressing international crises.
*   **Rudolph Wagner (University of Hawaii at Manoa):** Suggested that contemporary Western ethics is unconsciously aligning with Chinese philosophy, which can restore ethical strength to Western discourse.

## Event Overview
On September 30, 2026, *People's Daily* published a theoretical article titled "Empowering Economic and Social Development Through Culture: Internal Logic and Practical Pathways" (Issue 09, Theory section). The article, edited by Guan Xuanyue, Lu Tao, and Zhang Weihao, analyzes the proposition "empowering economic and social development through culture" within the context of thoroughly studying Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era and the first five volumes of *The Governance of China*.

The article posits that this proposition was formally advanced by General Secretary Xi Jinping in 2025 during his participation in the deliberations of the Jiangsu delegation to the Third Session of the 14th National People's Congress. It frames the proposition as a strategic deployment based on the laws of human social development, socialist construction, and cultural development. The content synthesizes theoretical origins, practical exploration, and strategic deployment, arguing that culture serves as a spiritual force, a source of innovation, and an industrial driver for high-quality development.

## Core Facts

**1. Historical Evolution and Theoretical Origins**
*   **Local Practice Foundation:** The concept is rooted in Xi Jinping’s practices and theoretical reflections during his local work tenure.
    *   **Zhengding:** Proposed the "Culture Revitalizes the County" (Wenhua Xingxian) governance strategy.
    *   **Fujian:** Emphasized coordinated development of culture and economy/society to meet people's growing cultural needs.
    *   **Zhejiang:** Proposed "further promoting the prosperity of cultural undertakings and further expanding cultural industries," systematically planning and advancing eight projects for cultural construction to promote deep integration.
    *   **Shanghai:** Stated that "cultural soft power is the foundation of strength," "the economy is the flesh and blood, culture is the soul," and that cultural power should be integrated into economic development.
*   **Strategic Positioning:** Since the 18th National Congress, the Party Central Committee has elevated cultural construction to a strategic height concerning national destiny and people’s well-being. *The Governance of China* Volume 4 (Chapter: "Placing Cultural Construction in a More Prominent Position") asserts that without the flourishing of socialist culture, there is no socialist modernization.
*   **Formal Proposal:** In 2025, at the Third Session of the 14th NPC (Jiangsu delegation deliberation), Xi Jinping explicitly proposed "deepening urban and rural spiritual civilization construction, optimizing the supply of cultural products and services, and empowering economic and social development through culture."

**2. Internal Logic: Culture as a Driving Force**
The article identifies three main mechanisms through which culture empowers development:
*   **Value Orientation:** Cultivating common values based on Marxism and Socialist Core Values. It argues that economic development under socialism aims at human comprehensive development and social progress, drawing on traditional wisdom like "people as the foundation of the state" and "balancing righteousness and profit."
*   **Development Philosophy:** Nourishing the New Development Philosophy (innovation, coordination, green, openness, sharing). Cultural practices and traditional Chinese culture provide humanistic nourishment for these concepts, shifting cognition from single growth to comprehensive development.
*   **Social Governance:** Enhancing social civilization and governance efficiency. By fostering honesty, dedication, and mutual assistance, transaction costs are reduced, and cooperation efficiency increased. The article cites the innovative development of the "New Fengqiao Experience," which integrates grassroots cultural resources to resolve conflicts, combining autonomy, rule of law, and rule of virtue.

**3. Innovation and Creativity Source**
*   **Subject Cultivation:** Culture cultivates an awareness of innovation among social subjects, turning innovation from an external requirement into internalized voluntary action. It references traditional concepts like "if you can renew yourself in a day, do so every day" and revolutionary spirit.
*   **Resource Activation:** Cultural resources are activated through creative transformation and innovative development, driving synergy across technology, design, and communication. Examples include smart cultural tourism, immersive theaters, digital museums, and AI-created cultural products.
*   **Ecosystem Construction:** Creating an environment that encourages exploration and tolerates failure. This involves respecting labor, knowledge, talent, and creation.

**4. Practical Pathways: Industrial and Systemic Development**
*   **Cultural Industries:** The article highlights the role of cultural industries in optimizing national economic structure. It cites National Bureau of Statistics data for 2025:
    *   Operating revenue of above-scale cultural and related industry enterprises reached **152,135 billion yuan**, a year-on-year increase of **7.4%**.
    *   Operating revenue in the core cultural field reached **103,181 billion yuan**, a year-on-year increase of **11.5%**.
*   **Deep Integration:** Promoting the fusion of culture with economy and technology to drive traditional industry transformation.
    *   **Tourism:** Moving from "culture as a stage, economy as the play" to deep integration via immersive and interactive scenarios.
    *   **Manufacturing:** Integrating industrial design, brand narratives, and cultural IPs to shift from "contract manufacturing" to "intelligent creation."
    *   **Agriculture:** Developing leisure agriculture, cultural creative agricultural products, and rural aesthetic economies.
*   **Digital Empowerment:** Utilizing AI, big data, and blockchain to reconstruct the creative, production, and consumption chains of cultural industries. The article mentions deploying major cultural industry projects and developing new formats such as digital animation, immersive performances, online broadcasts, short videos, and micro-dramas as outlined in the "15th Five-Year Plan" outline.
*   **System Reform:** Deepening cultural system reform to establish a mechanism where social benefits are prioritized while integrating social and economic benefits. This includes reforming state-owned cultural enterprises, supporting private cultural enterprises, and innovating cultural financial service mechanisms.

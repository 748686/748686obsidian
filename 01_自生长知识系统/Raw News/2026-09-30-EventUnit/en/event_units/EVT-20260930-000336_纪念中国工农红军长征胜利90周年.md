# Event Name

Commemoration of the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March: Dual Theme Exhibitions Opening and Regional Historical Commemoration

## Event Overview

On September 29, 2026, two major Long March theme exhibitions officially opened to the public at the Party History Exhibition Hall of the Communist Party of China in Beijing. These events are part of a series of activities commemorating the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March. Concurrently, media coverage highlighted historical narratives from Ya'an, Sichuan, detailing the Red Army's march through the region, specifically focusing on the treacherous Paotonggang route and the crossing of Jiajin Mountain.

## Core Facts

**Exhibition Opening (September 29, 2026)**
*   **Location:** Party History Exhibition Hall of the Communist Party of China, Beijing.
*   **Exhibition 1:** "The Great Expedition – Archives Exhibition of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March" (伟大远征).
    *   **Organizers:** Central Archives and the Party History Exhibition Hall of the CPC.
    *   **Content:** 230+ precious Long March archival documents.
    *   **Highlights:** The manuscript draft of the 1937 *Record of the Red Army's Long March* (《红军长征记》) appeared before the public for the first time.
    *   **Structure:** Divided into 5 units, including special sections on "25,000 Li," "How the World Knew About the Long March," and "The Long March in Editions."
*   **Exhibition 2:** "Snow Mountains and Grasslands – Special Exhibition of Cultural Relics Commemorating the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March" (雪山草地).
    *   **Organizers:** Sichuan Provincial Cultural Heritage Administration and the Party History Exhibition Hall of the CPC.
    *   **Content:** 273 items/sets of exhibits, including red army nail-studded cloth shoes, half a leather belt, a pocket party flag, and wheat-cutting certificates.
    *   **Highlights:** First public display of the Jiajin Mountain relics and Bazuo Campaign site investigation specimens; the certificate proving the status of female Red Army soldier Wang Quanying (who was left behind); and other newly researched artifacts.
*   **Official Recognition:** Both exhibitions were included in the promotion list for theme陈列 (exhibition/display) activities for the 90th anniversary, published by the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee, the Cyberspace Administration of China, the Ministry of Veterans Affairs, and the National Cultural Heritage Administration.

**Historical Context: Red Army in Ya'an**
*   **Timeline:** From May 23, 1935, to June 18, 1935, the main force of the Central Red Army fought/marched through Ya'an for 27 days.
*   **Paotonggang (泡桐岗):** Described as the most difficult road of the Long March by Zhang Aiping. It is an area in Yingjing County, Ya'an, characterized by primitive forest with no established path. Historical accounts cite extreme conditions: climbing vines, deep mud, and cliffs.
    *   **Notable Accounts:** Chen Bojun’s diary (June 5, 1935) described cliffs and deep mud; Wu Yunfu’s diary (June 7, 1935) stated "the road has never been so difficult"; Chen Yun recorded in *Records of the Western Expedition with the Army* that Zhou Enlai stood in mud trenches all night at the summit.
    *   **Nomenclature:** The location is referred to variously as "Paotonggang," "Pao Tonggang," or "Ganzhushan" in different memoirs, indicating its remoteness.
*   **Jiajin Mountain (夹金山):** The first major snow mountain crossed by the Central Red Army. Located between Baoxing County (Ya'an) and Xiaojin County (Ngawa Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture).
    *   **Crossing Date:** June 12–18, 1935.
    *   **Conditions:** Soldiers from the south lacked winter clothing; some wore only single clothes. Local masses in Tianquan and Lushan provided logistical support (100,000 kg and 200,000+ kg of grain, respectively, plus pork, salt, ginger, and liquor).
    *   **Reunion:** On June 18, 1935, the Central Red Army crossed Jiajin Mountain and met the Two Armies in Maogong (now Xiaojin County).
*   **Modern Parallel:** The Jiajin Mountain Tunnel, connecting Qiaqi Tibetan Township (Baoxing) and Davi Town (Xiaojin), began construction on December 26, 2021, and began trial operation on October 1, 2025.

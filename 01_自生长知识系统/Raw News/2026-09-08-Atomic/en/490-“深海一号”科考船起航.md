The proposal keeps the existing legal framework but introduces specific adjustments, such as upgrading the approval authority for toll fees to provincial governments and clarifying that expired highways may continue charging fees for maintenance if they are not incorporated into municipal systems or fully covered by fiscal budgets. Terminology updates replace "poor areas" with "less developed areas" and "loans or fundraising" with "incurring debt in accordance with the law."

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 8, 00:26

**「Background」** The current Highway Law has been in effect since January 1, 1998, and originally helped break funding constraints by allowing toll collection. However, it lacks explicit provisions for how management and maintenance funds should be collected after the toll periods for early highways expire.

**「Impact」** The amendment directly affects highway operators and provincial authorities by establishing a clear legal pathway for continuing toll collection on expired highways strictly for maintenance funding. It also impacts drivers, as tolls may persist on certain routes that are not taken over by municipal governments or fully subsidized by public finance.

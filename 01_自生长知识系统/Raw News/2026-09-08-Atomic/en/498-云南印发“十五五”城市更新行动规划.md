### [Gansu Province implements direct provincial investigation for major natural resource violations](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/08/content_30179781.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 7, 2026, the Gansu Provincial Department of Natural Resources announced new measures for direct provincial investigation and supervision of major illegal natural resource cases. The measures apply to cases across municipal boundaries, those involving illegal occupation of permanent basic farmland or ecological protection red lines, and those causing significant losses through illegal mining of strategic resources. Authorities are mandated to issue administrative penalty decisions within 90 days of filing such cases. The initiative aims to strengthen law enforcement supervision, improve efficiency, and maintain natural resource management order in the province.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 8, 00:26

**「Background」** Natural resource governance in China involves complex jurisdictional arrangements between provincial and local authorities. Permanent basic farmland and ecological protection red lines are key regulatory zones where strict land-use controls are enforced to safeguard food security and environmental protection.

**「Impact」** Natural resource developers and operators in Gansu must now comply with stricter provincial oversight for major violations, with faster penalty timelines of 90 days applying to cross-jurisdictional cases and those affecting protected areas.

### [Police forces embedded in community grids in Anyang](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/08/content_30179782.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Anying City in Henan province has integrated police personnel into community governance grids to improve grassroots dispute resolution and crime prevention. All 115 police station directors now hold concurrent deputy administrative positions at the township or street level, while 3,305 officers join village and community committees. Over 500 standardized police offices share premises with party-mass service centers, and 771 community police officers are paired with more than 11,000 grid workers to conduct joint patrols, information collection, and mediation. Early results reported this year show a 25.66% drop in criminal and public security cases, a 10.56% decline in security incidents, and a 42.4% decrease in telecom fraud compared with the previous year.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 8, 00:26

**「Background」** The reform addresses a previous split between public security duties focused on crime control and community work centered on livelihood visits and hazard screening, which often led to duplicated data collection, poor coordination, and slower responses to residents&\#x27; urgent needs. By placing police within existing administrative grids, the model aims to catch conflicts before they escalate from civil disputes to criminal cases.

**「Impact」** Residents and local merchants in Anyang now have faster, face-to-face access to officers who follow up on disputes rather than only intervening after报警. The changes may also reduce administrative burden by allowing joint information collection for addresses, population, housing, and unit data in a single visit.

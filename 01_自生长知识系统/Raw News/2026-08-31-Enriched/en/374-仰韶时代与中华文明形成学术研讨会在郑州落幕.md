On August 30, 2026, Gansu Province upgraded its "12316" agricultural hotline into an AI service system accessible via a new "Gansu Agricultural Services" mini-program. This upgrade significantly reduces post-call administrative work from 20-30 minutes to 1-2 minutes while routing complex issues to human experts for final review.

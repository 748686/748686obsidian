# Event: National Health and Health Conference & Health China Strategic Deployment (October 2026)

## Event Overview

On October 16, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a commentarial article titled "Ensure Decisive Progress in Healthy China Construction," addressing the speech delivered by General Secretary Xi Jinping at the National Health and Health Conference. The event centers on the strategic deployment for the "15th Five-Year Plan" period (2026–2030), aiming to achieve the goal of building a "Healthy China" by 2035, as established by the 20th National Congress of the Communist Party of China. The conference emphasized shifting focus from treating existing diseases to preventing them, strengthening grassroots medical services, and addressing imbalances in healthcare resource distribution.

## Core Facts

*   **Event:** National Health and Health Conference.
*   **Key Speaker:** Xi Jinping (General Secretary of the CPC Central Committee).
*   **Strategic Goal:** To ensure decisive progress in Healthy China construction during the "15th Five-Year Plan" period, with a target completion date of 2035.
*   **Core Directive:** "Highlight advantages, consolidate foundations, address shortcomings, and strengthen weak areas" to promote high-quality development of the health sector.
*   **Timeframe:** The article notes that there are nine years remaining until the 2035 goal, identifying the "15th Five-Year Plan" as a critical period.
*   **Key Policy Shifts:**
    1.  **Prevention First:** Emphasizing foundational and source-level work to reduce illness incidence ("treating potential disease" rather than just "treating existing disease").
    2.  **Medical Reform:** Promoting coordinated development and governance of healthcare, medical insurance, and medicine.
    3.  **Service Accessibility:** Improving the inclusiveness, accessibility, and quality of medical services, particularly at the grassroots level.
*   **Current Statistics (as reported):**
    *   Over 1.1 million medical and health institutions exist across urban and rural areas.
    *   More than 16 million medical and health personnel are employed.
    *   Over 90% of residents can reach the nearest medical service point within 15 minutes.
*   **Identified Weaknesses:**
    *   Imbalanced distribution of total medical resources, structure, and geography.
    *   Grassroots medical services remain a短板 (shortboard/weak point).
    *   Deficiencies in specialized fields: rehabilitation, nursing, pediatrics, geriatric medicine, mental health, and psychology.

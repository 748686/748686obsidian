# EVT-20261016-000362: Joint Communique on Building a Community with a Shared Future Between China and Togo in the New Era

## Event Overview

On October 15, 2026, in Beijing, the Government of the People's Republic of China and the Government of the Togolese Republic issued a joint communique announcing the elevation of their bilateral relations to a "Community with a Shared Future Between China and Togo in the New Era" (新时代中多命运共同体).

The announcement followed the official state visit of Foire Essozimna Nazihombé (Foire), Chairman of the Togolese Council of Ministers, to China from October 9–16, 2026. The visit was made at the invitation of Chinese Premier Li Qiang. During the visit, Foire met with Chinese President Xi Jinping, Premier Li Qiang, and Zhao Leji, Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.

The event marks a significant diplomatic milestone, positioning the China-Togo relationship as an "outstanding example of China-Africa friendship and South-South cooperation."

## Core Facts

**Diplomatic Elevation**
*   Both sides agreed to upgrade bilateral relations to a **Community with a Shared Future in the New Era** between China and Togo.
*   The decision aims to deepen Sino-Togolese friendship and promote human progress.

**Political and Sovereignty Principles**
*   **China’s Stance:** China reaffirmed firm support for Togo’s independence, unity, territorial integrity, sovereignty, security, and development interests.
*   **Togo’s Stance:** Togo firmly adheres to the **One-China Principle**, recognizing that there is only one China, Taiwan is an inseparable part of Chinese territory, and the PRC government is the sole legitimate government representing all China.
*   Togo reaffirmed support for China’s efforts toward national reunification and坚决反对 (resolutely opposes) any form of "Taiwan independence."
*   Togo reaffirmed the authority of UN General Assembly Resolution 2758.
*   Togo expressed firm support for China’s positions on Hong Kong, Xinjiang, Tibet, human rights, and the South China Sea.
*   Both sides opposed the politicization of human rights and interference in internal affairs under the guise of human rights.

**Development and Governance**
*   Togo congratulated China on the successful completion of its **14th Five-Year Plan** and expressed confidence in the 15th Five-Year Plan.
*   China congratulated Togo on completing constitutional reforms on **May 24, 2024**, entering its Fifth Republic, and implementing its 2020–2025 government roadmap.
*   China supports Togo’s independent exploration of a modernization path guided by the principles of protection, unity, and transformation.

**Economic and Strategic Cooperation**
*   Both sides praised the high-quality construction of the **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)**. Togo aims to integrate BRI cooperation as a strategic pillar of its **"Vision 2040."**
*   Togo joined the **"Group of Friends of the Global Development Initiative."**
*   A **Protocol on Phytosanitary Requirements for the Importation of Togolese Soybeans into China** was signed.
*   Both parties agreed to deepen customs and quarantine cooperation through an AEO (Authorized Economic Operator) memorandum.
*   China intends to use zero-tariff measures to support Togo’s industrialization and agricultural modernization, aiming to develop Lomé into a trade hub for West Africa.
*   Both sides welcomed the signing of a framework agreement for a **Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)** and encouraged further module negotiations.

**Security and Global Governance**
*   Togo expressed support for the **Global Security Initiative**.
*   China welcomed Togo’s initiative to establish an African Political Alliance and its role in promoting Pan-Africanism.
*   China supported Togo’s role as an AU mediator in the Great Lakes region.
*   Togo welcomed China’s advocacy for the establishment of the **International Court of Justice (Mediation)** and signed the relevant convention.
*   Togo welcomed the Secretariat of the **Agreement on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction (BBNJ)** being established in Xiamen.
*   Togo joined the **World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization** and its "Friends of AI Capacity Building International Cooperation" group.

**Cultural and People-to-People Exchanges**
*   Both sides highlighted the "China-Africa People’s Exchange Year" activities, including youth exchanges and Chinese cultural troupes at Togo’s National Day parade.
*   The "China-Africa United for Warm Childhood" charity activity for African orphans was noted.
*   Cooperation will continue through the Confucius Institute at the University of Lomé.
*   China will continue to offer government scholarships and training quotas to Togolese youth.

**2. Property Management Service Upgrades (Article 524)**
*   **National Context:** As of 2025, China has over 375,000 property service enterprises, 8.833 million practitioners, and covers 40.57 billion square meters. The sector is shifting from incremental expansion to stock quality improvement.
*   **Chengdu Model ("Trust System"):**
    *   **Mechanism:** Property fees and public收益 (public proceeds) are deposited in independent小区 accounts. The property company acts as a管家 (butler), extracting only an agreed profit margin, while all收支 (income/expenses) are public.
    *   **Case Study:** Yihe Yaju Community (Wuhou District). Public revenue distribution of 557,000 RMB to 745 households. Property fee collection rate rose from <50% to 97%. Complaints dropped from dozens/month to <5/year.
    *   **Scale:** Pilot programs expanded from 7 communities to over 1,300 across Chengdu.
*   **Shenzhen Model ("Menu-Based" Services):**
    *   **Mechanism:** Services are broken into standardized items with clear prices (e.g., sweeping, window cleaning). Owners select services akin to ordering from a menu.
    *   **Case Study:** Ludan Mingyuan Estate (Luohu District). Property fees set at 4.5 RMB/sqm (relocated housing) and 5 RMB/sqm (commercial housing), reflecting differential service levels.
    *   **Standards:** The Shenzhen Property Management Industry Association defined 270 "minimum work units." The local standard *Residential Property Service Specification* categorizes services into Basic, Good, and High Quality levels.
    *   **Performance:** Owner satisfaction remains above 95%; fee collection rate is 99.5%. City-wide coverage rate is 97.6%.
*   **Changsha Model (Connected Area Packaging):**
    *   **Challenge:** Open old residential communities (372 in Yuhua District alone) lack stable management due to small scale and unclear property boundaries.
    *   **Solution:** "Party Building + District Property/Big Property" model. Multiple small communities are bundled into manageable zones.
    *   **Cost Efficiency:** Mobile maintenance staff serve multiple communities (e.g., 5 staff for 10 communities).
    *   **Revenue Generation:** Unified parking management generated >200,000 RMB annually for the Fengshushan project.
    *   **Subsidies:** Government provided "reward instead of subsidy" during transition, phasing out over time.
    *   **Outcome:** Over 80% of Yuhua District's open old communities now have stable management. City-wide coverage for such communities is approaching 70%.

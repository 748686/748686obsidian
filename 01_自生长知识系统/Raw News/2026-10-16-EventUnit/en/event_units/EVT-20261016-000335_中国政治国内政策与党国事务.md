# Event: Chinese Domestic Policy and Party-State Affairs (October 16, 2026)

## Event Overview
On October 16, 2026, the first edition of the *People's Daily* reported on several key domestic policy initiatives and diplomatic events of the Chinese government. The primary domestic focuses included the promotion of "comprehensive and strict governance of the Party," the advancement of the "Healthy China" initiative, and the expansion of high-level opening-up through the Canton Fair. These were reported alongside diplomatic engagements involving leaders from Togo and Botswana, as well as upcoming state visits by Premier Li Qiang.

## Core Facts

**1. Health and Social Development ("Healthy China")**
*   **Event:** The National Health and Health Conference was held in Beijing on October 12–13, 2026.
*   **Leadership:** General Secretary Xi Jinping attended and delivered a speech emphasizing the promotion of high-quality development in the health sector to ensure decisive progress in building a Healthy China (Source: Article #472).
*   **Goal:** The target is to build a Healthy China by 2035.
*   **Key Themes:**
    *   Expansion of high-quality medical resources to the grassroots level and the improvement of tiered diagnosis and treatment systems.
    *   Emphasis on medical talent cultivation, including "Yi-Fang-Guan" (medical, prevention, management) composite talents.
    *   Specific initiatives mentioned include children and adolescents' "five healths" screening and science popularization, and consolidating reforms to reduce burdens on patients seeking medical care.
*   **Stakeholder Responses:** Various health officials and practitioners expressed commitment to these goals:
    *   **Xing Shaoyun** (Hainan Fifth People's Hospital): Focus on leprosy prevention and community-based nursing.
    *   **Yuan Jin** (Beijing Tongren Hospital): Focus on improving diagnosis of critical illnesses and resource dispersion.
    *   **Jiang Hui** (Peking University First Hospital): Focus on regional medical centers and remote collaboration.
    *   **Hu Xiaozhong** (Shiye Town Health Center, Zhenjiang): Focus on patient satisfaction and primary care.
    *   **Wuhase Sulaiman** (Yumin County Pastoral Hospital): Focus on border health defense.
    *   **Shi Qiaorong** (Guangdong Provincial Hospital of Chinese Medicine): Focus on inheriting and innovating Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM).
    *   **Zhu Hongming** (National Health Commission Center for Health Development): Focus on medical talent队伍建设 (team building).
    *   **Zheng Zhongwei** (National Health Commission Science and Education Department): Focus on medical education reform and ethics.
    *   **Zhou Xingtao** (Fudan University Affiliated Eye, Ear, Nose and Throat Hospital): Focus on deepening talent development mechanism reforms.
    *   **Hou Hong** (Henan Provincial Health Commission): Focus on民生 (people's livelihood)实事 (practical things) and grassroots service capacity. (Source: Article #472)

**2. Economic Opening-Up (Canton Fair)**
*   **Event:** Xi Jinping sent a congratulatory letter to the 140th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair) on October 14, 2026.
*   **Context:** The Canton Fair celebrated its 70th anniversary (140 sessions).
*   **Xi's Stance:** The letter affirmed the fair's historical role in promoting Sino-foreign trade and reiterated China's determination to expand high-level opening-up and uphold the multilateral trading system.
*   **Stakeholder Responses:**
    *   **Zhang Jingsong** (Director, Guangdong Provincial Department of Commerce): Committed to local保障 (support/guarantee) responsibilities.
    *   **Wei Min** (Director, Guangzhou Municipal Bureau of Commerce): Aiming to upgrade visitors from trade partners to innovation/investment partners.
    *   **Liang Zhaoxian** (Galanz Group): Highlighted the fair's role in building initial foreign trade competitiveness.
    *   **Zhou Yunjie** (Haier Group): Noted the company's growth from a 5-square-meter booth to presence in 200+ countries.
    *   **Chen Ming** (ICBC Guangdong Branch): Committed to providing cross-border settlement and trade financing solutions.
    *   **Duan Yu** (CVTE): Highlighted the fair as a platform for AI technology and brand export; noted 2025 own-brand overseas income growth of 54.83%.
    *   **Wang Kexue** (Skyworth): Emphasized the link between tech innovation and high-quality foreign trade.
    *   **Xie Sheng** (Guangdong Daye Motorcycle): Viewed the fair as a platform for brand building and supply chain strengthening.
    *   **Ren Xiaoyu** (Dongyi Technology): Focused on embodied intelligence and supply chain collaboration.
    *   **Ira Tokareva** (Russian Purchaser): First-time participant, seeking reliable suppliers.
    *   **Shen Minghao** (Guangdong University of Foreign Studies): Suggested converting exhibition traffic into institutional opening-up increments for the Greater Bay Area. (Source: Article #468)

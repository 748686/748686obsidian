# Event Name
China's Diplomatic Activities and Multilateral Engagement (October 2026)

## Event Overview
Between October 14 and October 16, 2026, China conducted a series of high-level diplomatic engagements, multilateral conference participations, and international economic meetings. These activities took place in both Baku, Azerbaijan (multilateral), and Beijing, China (bilateral). The events highlight China's focus on Asian security frameworks (CICA), regional cooperation (SCO), economic dialogue with the United States, and bilateral party-to-party relations with Turkey. The timeframe coincides with China's official commencement of its "15th Five-Year Plan" period.

## Core Facts

### 1. Participation in the 7th CICA Summit (Baku, Azerbaijan)
*   **Date:** October 15, 2026.
*   **Representative:** Wang Yi, Member of the Politburo and Minister of Foreign Affairs, attended as a special representative of President Xi Jinping.
*   **Key Speech:** Wang Yi articulated four initiatives for Asian nations:
    1.  Jointly maintain peace and stability.
    2.  Jointly promote prosperity and revitalization.
    3.  Jointly facilitate exchanges and mutual learning among civilizations.
    4.  Jointly improve global governance.
*   **Strategic Context:** Wang Yi emphasized that 2026 is the opening year of China's "15th Five-Year Plan" and stated China would contribute to international stability through high-quality domestic development. He expressed support for Azerbaijan's chairmanship in building a "Stronger Asia."
*   **Outcome:** The summit adopted and issued the *Declaration of the 7th CICA Summit*.
*   **Bilateral Meetings:** During the summit, Wang Yi held separate meetings with:
    *   Isaac Herzog, President of Israel.
    *   Sergey Lavrov, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Russia.
    *   Abdullatif bin Rashid Al Zayani, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Bahrain.
    *   Hakan Fidan, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Turkey.
    *   Riad Al-Fayyad, Minister of Foreign Affairs of Palestine.

### 2. Adoption of the CICA Declaration
*   **Location:** Baku, Azerbaijan.
*   **Date:** October 15, 2026.
*   **Key Provisions:**
    *   Reaffirmed commitment to the UN Charter and international law; recognized the core role of the UN.
    *   Addressed global security challenges, expressing concern over the erosion of trust and multilateralism.
    *   Condemned the use of force leading to tension and humanitarian disasters (civilian casualties, infrastructure damage).
    *   Advocated for civilization dialogue and referenced UN resolutions on the "International Day for Cultural Dialogue" and the "International Decade for Culture-based Sustainable Development" (2027–2036).
    *   Emphasized digital connectivity, AI, and information communications as both opportunities and risks requiring responsible governance.
    *   Supported an open, fair, and non-discriminatory multilateral trading system; urged against unilateral economic restrictions violating international law.
    *   Highlighted energy diversification, cross-border infrastructure protection, and the right to peaceful nuclear energy.
    *   Noted the importance of SMEs, tourism, women's empowerment (referencing the Global Women's Summit held in Beijing, Oct 13–14, 2025), and youth participation.
    *   **Membership Update:** Welcomed the Lao PDR as a new CICA member.
    *   **Chairmanship:** Confirmed Azerbaijan's tenure as Chair for 2026–2028.

### 3. Economic Diplomacy: Meeting with Ray Dalio (Beijing)
*   **Date:** October 14, 2026.
*   **Host:** He Lifeng, Member of the Politburo and Director of the Central Financial and Economic Affairs Commission.
*   **Guest:** Ray Dalio, Founder of Bridgewater Associates (USA).
*   **Key Discussion Points:**
    *   He Lifeng highlighted China's positive economic trajectory in the opening year of the "15th Five-Year Plan."
    *   Referenced the successful visit of President Xi Jinping to the United States and its significance for bilateral relations.
    *   Stated that Sino-US economic and trade consultations have yielded "mutually beneficial, balanced, and positive results" under presidential strategic consensus.
    *   Dalio expressed optimism about China's economic prospects and willingness to promote Sino-US economic cooperation.

### 4. Upcoming Regional Engagements (SCO and Bilateral)
*   **Li Qiang's Scheduled Visit:**
    *   **Destinations:** Uzbekistan and Tajikistan.
    *   **Events:**
        1.  Attend the 25th Meeting of the Council of Heads of Government of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Member States.
        2.  Participate in the 31st Regular China-Russia Prime Ministers' Meeting in Russia.
*   **Other Related Activities (Same Period):**
    *   Li Qiang delivered a video address at the opening ceremony of the China-North Korea New Yalu River Bridge.
    *   Li Qiang met with Botswana President Mokgweetsi Masisi (Note: Source text lists "Boko" but context implies President of Botswana; *See Conflicts*).
    *   China-North Korea infrastructure cooperation (Yalu River Bridge).

### 5. Institutional and Party Diplomacy
*   **Turkey:** On October 15, 2026, Liu Haixing, Minister of the International Department of the CPC Central Committee, met with a high-level delegation from the Justice and Development Party (AK Party) of Turkey, led by Vice Chairman Ilei. Discussions focused on implementing heads-of-state consensus and strengthening party-to-party exchanges.
*   **Sports Diplomacy:** State Councilor Chen Yiqin inspected preparations for the Changchun Winter Universiade (Oct 13–15). She emphasized the event as China's first major international sporting event of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period and called for high-quality organization. She also inspected football youth training centers in Jilin Province.

### 6. Regional Connectivity Milestone
*   **China-Laos Railway:** As of October 15, 2026, cumulative cross-border passenger traffic exceeded 1 million. Over 4,000 train services have been operated. Foreign nationals accounted for over 151,000 passengers. Top nationalities were Lao, Thai, and Malaysian. Over 55% of foreign entrants utilized visa-free policies.

## Unique Information by Source

### From Article #551 (Yu Ganshi, Editorial)
*   **Editorial Philosophy:** Argues that if news is the "skeleton" of a newspaper, the supplement is the "qi and blood" (vitality) that nourishes its spirit.
*   **Wang Meng’s Examples:** Cites Wang Meng’s writing on swimming (elevating personal memory to reflections on Chinese modernization) and party membership (focusing on the detail of singing while walking home near Shicha Hai rather than the oath ceremony) as examples of "dimensional elevation" and "detail landing."
*   **Classical Reference:** Invokes Confucius’s sigh in the *Analects* ("I agree with Dian") to illustrate how profound political ideals can be expressed through relaxed, everyday language.
*   **"Three Qi" (三气) Framework:** Summarizes colleague feedback as requiring supplements to be:
    1.  *Jie Di Qi* (Grounded/Earthly): Rooted in reality and贴近时代 (close to the times).
    2.  *You Wen Qi* (Literary/Cultured): Refining text, rejecting sloppy production.
    3.  *Dai Rui Qi* (Sharp/Pioneering): Facing realistic problems, showing individuality, and adhering to media responsibility.
*   **Warning on Tone:** Identifies two detrimental tones: "Material Tone" (工整 but empty) and "Literary Tone" (flowery but emotionally empty).

### From Article #552 (Su Tao, Book Review)
*   **Scientific Context:** Discusses the reconstruction of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau’s past using plant fossils.
*   **Historical Expeditions:**
    *   1992–1993: Zhou Zhekun participated in the Medog overwintering plant expedition; ~30,000 specimens collected despite lack of roads.
    *   1960s: Liu Dongsheng and Shi Yafeng collected *Quercus* (oak) leaf fossils near 6,000 meters on Xixingbama Peak, pioneering Chinese study of plateau uplift.
*   **Specific Fossil Discoveries:**
    *   *Lagokarpos* (Rabbit-ear fruit) fossils in Bangong County: Indicates floristic exchange between Qinghai-Tibet, North America, and Europe.
    *   Palm and catfish fossils in Lunpoa Basin: Evidence that the plateau was once a tropical haven.
    *   Large palm leaf fossils in Lunpoa Basin: Prove the existence of a "Central Valley" 39 million years ago.
    *   Jianglang plant group in Bangong County: Recreates the subtropical forest appearance of central Qinghai-Tibet 47 million years ago.
*   **Author’s Background:** Su Tao studied paleobotany under Zhou Zhekun from 2005 to 2011.

### From Article #553 (Wang Yanping & Zhang Chao, Book Review)

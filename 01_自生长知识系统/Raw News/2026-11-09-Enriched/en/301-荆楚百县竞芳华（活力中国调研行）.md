### [China&\#x27;s rural per capita income grew 5.6% in first three quarters](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202611/09/content_30190179.html) ⭐️ ?/10

China&\#x27;s rural residents earned a per capita disposable income of 18,809 yuan during the first three quarters of 2026, reflecting a real growth rate of 5.6%, which outpaced urban income growth by 2 percentage points, according to the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs. Agricultural trade remained strong, with total imports and exports reaching $252.36 billion, up 9.7% year-on-year, driven by a $171.46 billion import increase of 10.2% and an $80.9 billion export increase of 8.7%. On the policy front, pilot programs for extending rural land contracts for another 30 years have resulted in over 30 million contracts signed nationwide, while approximately 6 million family farms and cooperatives have been cultivated, with 1.08 million operating entities providing socialized services.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Nov 9, 00:26

**「Background」** Rural land contract extensions are a central policy tool in China aimed at securing long-term land rights for farmers to encourage agricultural investment and stability. The cultivation of family farms and cooperatives represents a shift toward modernized, scaled agriculture to boost rural productivity and income levels.

**「Impact」** The data indicates that rural economic vitality is strengthening, with agricultural reforms and trade performance supporting continued income growth in China&\#x27;s countryside.

Xinhua News Agency reported on November 16 that Wang Xiaohong, member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and State Councilor, surveyed Sichuan Province on November 15–16 to assess progress toward annual economic and social development targets. Emphasizing the spirit of the 20th CPC Central Committee&\#x27;s Fifth Plenary Session, Wang urged local authorities to strengthen technological innovation, optimize foreign trade structures toward high-value exports, and accelerate modern water infrastructure. The survey included visits to enterprises and grassroots units in Mianyang and Chengdu, with a focus on integrating innovation platforms with regional industries and safeguarding national security to underpin high-quality development.

### [China&\#x27;s economic new growth drivers accelerate in October](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202611/17/content_30191539.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A People&\#x27;s Daily commentary on November 17, 2026, highlights that China&\#x27;s high-tech manufacturing output grew by 17.6% year-on-year in October, significantly outpacing the 12.8% growth of all industrial enterprises above designated size. Early indicators for the first ten months show a 9.2% increase in investment related to intellectual property products and a 4.9% rise in service retail sales, pointing to the strengthening contribution of new economic momentum. The article identifies the conversion between old and new growth drivers as the key focus for upcoming economic policy, emphasizing innovation, industrial upgrades, and green transition as essential tasks for sustaining recovery through the end of the year.

**「Background」** China has prioritized &\#x27;new quality productive forces&\#x27; as a strategic policy goal since 2023, aiming to shift the economy from reliance on traditional heavy industry and real estate toward high-value technology, green energy, and digital services. Key policy frameworks include constructing industrial networks and promoting comprehensive green transitions, as outlined in recent central economic work directives.

**「Impact」** For policymakers and economic analysts, these figures provide the latest evidence that structural reform is successfully accelerating faster than the broader economy, though full-year impact data remains incomplete. Market participants should monitor whether the 17.6% high-tech growth rate sustains through Q4 as the government implements its year-end stimulus and transformation targets.

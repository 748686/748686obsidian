## Core Facts

*   **Publication:** *People's Daily*, November 26, 2026, Page 18 (Rule of Law).
*   **Journalist:** Ni Yi (倪弋).
*   **Subject Organization:** Dazhou City People's Procuratorate, Sichuan Province.
*   **Core Narrative:** The Dazhou procuratorate uses public interest litigation to protect "Bashan Red" (revolutionary heritage) and "Ecological Green" (environmental protection).
*   **Case 1: Willow Pass (Yuliu Guan) Memorial Site Repair**
    *   **Location:** Boundary between Xuanhan County, Sichuan, and Kaizhou District, Chongqing.
    *   **History:** Site of the 1933 Battle of Willow Pass (Red 33rd Army and Red 4th Army, 10th Division). Over 1,000 anonymous red soldiers were buried there.
    *   **Trigger:** November 2019, a municipal People's Congress deputy reported the need for repair of the anonymous soldier cemetery.
    *   **Action:** Joint investigation by Xuanhan County and Kaizhou District Procuratorates.
    *   **Outcome:** Pre-litigation procuratorial recommendations led to the completion of the memorial park in Xuanhan by the end of 2021. Trench sites in Kaizhou were repaired and marked.
*   **Case 2: Qu River Bank Ecological Restoration**
    *   **Location:** Qu County, Dazhou City.
    *   **Issue:** Illegal cultivation on riverbanks (approx. 23,276 square meters), threatening water quality and flood control.
    *   **Action:** Procuratorate issued recommendations in March/April (year referenced as "last year" relative to 2026, implying 2025). When partial cleanup persisted, an administrative public interest lawsuit was filed against the Water Affairs Bureau.
    *   **Outcome:** Complete cleanup within 20 days of the legal pressure. Establishment of a "Shared Vegetable Garden" to balance public interest and residents' agricultural needs.
*   **Case 3: Ren River Water Source Protection**
    *   **Location:** Anyuan City, Dazhou City (Ren River section).
    *   **Context:** The Ren River is a major tributary of the Han River and supplies the Middle Route of the South-to-North Water Diversion Project. It provides over 2 billion cubic meters annually to the Danjiangkou Reservoir (5.96% of total diversion).
    *   **Trigger:** March 2024, Deputy Tian Yan observed sand/stone dumping sites discharging waste water.
    *   **Action:** Procuratorate identified 4 illegal sites occupying 19,506.08 square meters of riverbank. Litigation was filed in August 2024 after administrative inaction.
    *   **Outcome:** By October 2024, sites were demolished, water quality reached Class II standards.
    *   **Long-term Impact (2025-2026):** Shift from single-point to regional governance. Allocation of 65.85 million yuan for treatment. Cross-provincial cooperation established with Chongqing (Chengkou) and Shaanxi (Ziyang).

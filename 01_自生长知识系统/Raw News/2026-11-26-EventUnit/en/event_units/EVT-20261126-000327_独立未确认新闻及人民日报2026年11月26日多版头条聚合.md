# Event Name

**Shenzhou-24 Crewed Spacecraft Launch: First International Astronaut Mission to China's Tiangong Station**

## Event Overview

On November 25, 2026, China successfully launched the Shenzhou-24 crewed spacecraft, marking the first time a foreign astronaut from Pakistan participated in a mission to the China Space Station (Tiangong). The spacecraft launched from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center at 08:18 Beijing time and docked with the station at 11:56 the same day. This mission is identified as the 41st launch of China's manned space program and the 8th crewed flight since the station entered the application and development phase. In conjunction with the mission, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Pakistani President Asif Ali Zardari exchanged congratulatory cables on November 25, 2026, which were published in People's Daily on November 26, 2026. The event is characterized by three key dimensions: the launch and docking operation, the scientific experiments (including four new China-Pakistan international cooperation projects), and the high-level diplomatic exchange celebrating the 75th anniversary of diplomatic ties between the two nations.

## Core Facts

*   **Launch Date and Time:** November 25, 2026, at 08:18 Beijing time.
*   **Launch Vehicle:** Long March 2F Y24 (长征二号F遥二十四) rocket.
*   **Launch Site:** Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center.
*   **Manned Mission:** Shenzhou-24 (神舟二十四号).
*   **Docking Details:**
    *   **Time:** 11:56 Beijing time on November 25, 2026.
    *   **Target:** Forward port of the Tianhe core module.
    *   **Duration:** Approximately 3.5 hours for autonomous rapid rendezvous and docking.
    *   **Mode:** 3.5-hour rapid rendezvous (the 4th time this mode has been used).
*   **Crew Composition:** Three astronauts arrived on Shenzhou-24 to join the Shenzhou-23 crew. The arrival included the first foreign (Pakistani) astronaut to the station.
*   **Significance:**
    *   41st launch of the China Manned Space Program.
    *   8th crewed mission since the station entered the application and development phase.
    *   9th "space rendezvous" (meeting of two crews in orbit).
    *   First time Tiangong has welcomed a foreign astronaut.
*   **Diplomatic Exchange:** On November 25, 2026, President Xi Jinping and President Zardari exchanged congratulatory cables.
*   **Scientific Payloads:** The mission plans to carry out 90 experiments (tests and projects).
    *   **4 International Cooperation Projects:** Specific projects involving Pakistan include:
        1.  Algal space cultivation (blue algae) to support life sustainability.
        2.  Space radiation and microgravity exposure experiments on 32 quinoa seeds.
        3.  Out-of-cabin exposure experiment on graphene-based hybrid composite shielding materials.
        4.  Research on repair efficiency of self-healing composite materials in the space environment.

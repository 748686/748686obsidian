3.  **Maritime Dispute Resolution (Tianjin):** The Tianjin Water "One-Stop" Dispute Resolution Center (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Port Economic Cooperation Center Station) has officially commenced operations. 4.  **AI Implementation (Wuhan):** 22,000 AI agents have been deployed across 5,700 scenarios in Wuhan. 1,489 replicable agents were selected for the municipal key product library. In the first three quarters of 2026, Wuhan's AI industry scale reached 136 billion RMB, a year-on-year increase of 44%. 18 scenarios were selected as national typical cases, and 467 as provincial benchmarks.
5.  **Agricultural Talent (Baoji, Shaanxi):** Baoji City has cumulatively cultivated 75,000 "new farmers." Of these, over 6,000 have established enterprises or cooperatives, driving local employment for more than 50,000 people.
6.  **Rural Place Names (Liupanshui, Guizhou):** As of October 2026, 3,472 rural place names in Liupanshui have been standardized and registered in the national place name information database. This is part of the "Rural Noted Action" to address the issue of "land without names" for roads, industrial parks, scenic areas, squares, and logistics sites.

### Scientific and Technological Advancements (Page 06)

*   **Cell-Free Synthetic Biology:** Zhang Yiheng, Director of the State Key Laboratory of Low-Carbon Synthetic Engineering Biology and Director of the Cell-Free Synthetic Biology Center at the Tianjin Institute of Industrial Biotechnology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), outlined how extracellular synthetic biology provides new options for achieving "dual carbon" goals. This technology involves extracting enzymes from cells to assemble multi-enzyme molecular machines that operate outside the cell.
    *   **Industrial Application:** In Sichuan, a world-first 10,000-ton annual inositol factory uses multi-enzyme molecular machines to convert starch into inositol. Compared to traditional corn extraction, this reduces energy consumption by 90%, phosphorus pollution by 95%, and production costs by 50%–75%. (Source: Article #322)
    *   **Efficiency Metrics:** For trehalose-6-phosphate synthesis, production speed is over 100 times faster than microbial fermentation; continuous production using immobilized multi-enzyme molecular machines has a manufacturing strength over 1,000 times that of microbial fermentation. (Source: Article #322)
    *   **Energy Storage:** The research team proposed starch-based hydrogen storage with a density of 14.8%, which is 2.5 times that of high-pressure hydrogen storage and over 10 times that of lithium batteries. (Source: Article #322)
*   **Space Cold Atom Physics:** A rubidium atom cloud on the Chinese Space Station achieved an equivalent temperature of 9.7 picokelvins, creating a human "cold extreme" record.
    *   **Performance:** The atoms moved only about 500 micrometers in 7 seconds. Nobel laureate Wolfgang Ketterle noted that the 7-second free evolution time is more significant than the temperature record itself. (Source: Article #323)
    *   **Mechanism:** Two-stage crossed optical trap cooling was used, involving evaporative cooling and non-adiabatic diffusive cooling. The spatial positioning error for key laser intersections had to be within 5 micrometers. (Source: Article #323)
    *   **Future Goals:** Researchers aim to use "momentum filtering" to reach the hundreds-of-femtokelvin range. Potential applications include high-precision atomic interferometers for measuring Earth's gravity field changes and probing dark matter. (Source: Article #323)
*   **Plant Pest Defense Mechanism:** Academician Chen Xiaoya and Dr. Wan Li's teams at the CAS Center for Excellence in Molecular Plant Sciences discovered a new mechanism by which plants sense insect damage.
    *   **Mechanism:** Insect saliva enzymes break down into small molecules that act as "warning signals." These are captured by the nucleic acid-binding protein NIB1 in rice. NIB1 then forms condensates in the nucleus, recruiting the immune hub protein EDS1 to启动 (initiate) anti-insect defense.
    *   **Publication:** Findings were published in the international journal *Nature*. (Source: Article #324)
*   **Other 6th Page Headlines:** The page also featured reports on quantum computers acquiring "high-speed memory," global cryosphere degradation acceleration, and the establishment of a Marine Biological Manufacturing Innovation Center. (Sources: Articles #322, #323, #324)

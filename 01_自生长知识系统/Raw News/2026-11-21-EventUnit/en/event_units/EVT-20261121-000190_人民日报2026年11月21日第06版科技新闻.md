## Core Facts

**1. Quantum Computing: Global First QRAM Realization**
*   **Institution:** Zhejiang University (School of Physics, School of Computer Science, School of Software, Ningbo International Sino-Swedish Biomedical Research Center).
*   **Hardware:** "Tianmu-2" superconducting quantum chip, prepared at the Zhejiang University Micro-Nano Processing Center.
*   **Achievement:** Global first true-machine implementation of QRAM (Quantum Random Access Memory).
*   **Publication:** *Nature Physics* (*Nature Physics*).
*   **Key Personnel:**
    *   Co-first authors: Ph.D. students Shen Fanhao and Xiang Debin (Future Computing Innovation Center).
    *   Researcher: Lu Liqiang (School of Software/Ningbo Center).
    *   Researcher: Song Chao (School of Physics).
    *   Corresponding author: Yin Jianwei (Dean, School of Software).
*   **Technical Details:** The team reconstructed the routing architecture to run a tree-structured QRAM on a 2D grid chip. They utilized quantum teleportation to create "logical tunnels" between distant qubits and employed qubits as "monitors" to detect level transitions/logical errors.
*   **Performance Metrics:** Successfully ran 4-bit and 8-bit data access prototypes. Accuracy rates were 80.9% (4-bit) and 60.4% (8-bit). Circuit depth reduced by >30% compared to unoptimized schemes.
*   **Significance:** Demonstrates local noise-resistance capabilities, allowing practical QRAM construction on imperfect hardware. Key technologies from chip design to measurement are domestically controlled.

**2. Global Cryosphere Degradation Study**
*   **Source Agency:** Xinhua News Agency (Reported from Stockholm via Helsinki).
*   **Journal:** *Science*.
*   **Participating Institutions:** Stockholm University (Sweden), Tsinghua University (China), Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (Germany), and other international organizations.
*   **Methodology:** Utilized the "Planetary Boundaries" framework. Used the Holocene epoch (starting ~11,700 years ago) as a reference for stable climate conditions to define the "safe operating space" for the cryosphere.
*   **Findings:** Global cryosphere degradation is accelerating; some changes are irreversible. Primary drivers are human-induced climate change, black carbon emissions, industrial activities, and infrastructure expansion.
*   **Recommendations:** Calls for more active emission reduction targets and reduced human disturbance in cryosphere-sensitive regions.

**3. Marine Bio-Manufacturing Innovation Center Establishment**
*   **Location:** Yazhou Bay Science and Technology City, Sanya, Hainan.
*   **Event:** Establishment and launch of the Marine Bio-Manufacturing Innovation Center during the Bioeconomy 50 Forum (Sanya).
*   **Key Partners:** BGI Research Institute of Sanya Hainan and Hainan Tropical Ocean University signed an agreement to co-establish the Marine Bio-Manufacturing College.
*   **Mission:** Focus on marine microbial resource development. Build an open innovation platform integrating R&D, pilot services,成果转化 (achievement transformation), and industry incubation.
*   **Educational Component:** The new college aims to integrate research institute platforms with university discipline/talent advantages, creating a "teaching-training-employment" chain to supply localized professional talent.

**4. Plant Pest Sensing Mechanism**
*   **Finding:** A Chinese scientific team revealed a new mechanism by which plants sense pest attacks.
*   **Source Constraint:** The provided content for this article (Article #325 text body) is dominated by the QRAM report; the specific biological details of the plant sensing mechanism are listed in the table of contents but the full body text was not fully distinct in the provided payload (see Unique Information section).

**5. Space Station Cold Atom Experiment**
*   **Context:** Report titled "Creating the 'Cold Pole' in the Space Station."
*   **Source Constraint:** Listed in the edition's table of contents, but full article content was not provided in the source material.

**6. Cultural Heritage: Lin Tan "Che Sheng" (Rope Pulling)**
*   **Location:** Lintan County, Gannan (Gansu Province).
*   **Activity:** "Che Sheng" (Rope Pulling), a folk activity originating from the Ming Dynasty (Hongwu era, >600 years ago).
*   **Characteristics:** Involves tens of thousands of participants. The rope is >1 kilometer long, weighs ~10 tons, contains steel wires, and is divided into dragon head, snake body, and swallow tail sections.
*   **Timing:** Held during the Lantern Festival period.
*   **Status:** Intangible Cultural Heritage.
*   **Author:** Lao Teng (Old Vine), published in the Supplement (Page 08).

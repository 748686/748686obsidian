# Event Name
French Police Sentenced for Beating of Music Producer Michel Zecler (BBC / People's Daily)

## Event Overview
On November 20, 2026, a French court delivered verdicts in the trial of three police officers who beat black music producer Michel Zecler during an arrest at his Paris recording studio on May 25, 2020. The court convicted the officers of assault and falsifying police reports, and upheld the aggravating circumstance of racism based on racial slurs used during the attack. The case became a focal point for critics alleging systematic racism within French law enforcement.

The event is documented via a BBC News article (Nov 20, 2026) and cross-referenced in the People's Daily (Nov 21, 2026) as part of its international news section.

## Core Facts
- **Incident Date**: May 25, 2020, during COVID-19 lockdown in France.
- **Location**: Michel Zecler's recording studio in Paris.
- **Victim**: Michel Zecler, a 47-year-old black music producer.
- **Defendants**: Three French police officers — Aurélien Lapersonne (29), Philippe Tondeux (50), and Pierre Plantier (37).
- **Charges**: Beating of a civilian; signing a falsified police report claiming violent resistance.
- **Court Finding**: The beating was an aggravated circumstance of racism. The officers used racial slurs during the assault, as testified by Zecler and corroborated by CCTV footage.
- **Verdict**: All three officers convicted. Sentences range from 5 years imprisonment with varying suspended portions:
  - Lapersonne: 3 years suspended.
  - Tondeux: 3.5 years suspended.
  - Plantier: 4 years suspended; serves 1 year with an ankle monitor.
- **Professional Consequence**: All three officers are banned from serving in the police force again.
- **Key Evidence**: CCTV footage showed Zecler suffering approximately 30 punches, kicks, and baton strikes while offering little resistance. No evidence of drug possession was found. The officers claimed Zecler lacked a mask and behaved suspiciously, but the court found these justifications unsupported.
- **Trial Dynamics**: The officers admitted to the beating and the falsified report but denied racial motivation. The court accepted Zecler's testimony regarding racial slurs due to his overall verifiable credibility.
- **Final Statements**: All three officers apologized before sentencing. Tondeux expressed regret for the image of the police he had given.

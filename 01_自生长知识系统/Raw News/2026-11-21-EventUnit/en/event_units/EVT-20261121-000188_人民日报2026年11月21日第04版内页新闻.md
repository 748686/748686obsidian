## Event Overview
On November 21, 2026, *People’s Daily* (pages 04 and 05) published a suite of reports covering high-level diplomacy, macroeconomic policy, technological innovation in artificial intelligence, regional legal cooperation, and local industrial development. The primary events synthesized include the 11th annual China-Australia Prime Ministers' Meeting, the issuance of sovereign bonds in US dollars, the growth of China's open-source AI ecosystem, the 8th China-ASEAN Rule of Law Forum, and the industrial development of blueberries in Guizhou province.

## Core Facts

### 1. China-Australia Diplomatic Engagement
On the afternoon of November 20, 2026, Chinese Premier Li Qiang met with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese at the Diaoyutai State Guesthouse in Beijing for the 11th annual China-Australia Prime Ministers' Meeting. This followed a meeting between President Xi Jinping and Prime Minister Albanese in Shenzhen on the morning of November 20 regarding the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC)Leaders’ Meeting.
*   **Chinese Position:** Premier Li highlighted the positive momentum in bilateral relations, mutual complementarity in industry and technology, and the need to manage differences while focusing on common interests. He proposed expanding trade scale and optimizing structure, particularly in green/low-carbon sectors, new energy vehicles, and AI. He emphasized the importance of the upcoming "China-Australia Tourism Year" in 2027 and joint support for multilateralism.
*   **Australian Position:** Prime Minister Albanese reiterated that Australia’s One-China policy remains unchanged. He expressed that despite global volatility, both nations should look past differences to promote cooperation for mutual benefit and global welfare, particularly in trade, agriculture, energy, and emerging technologies.
*   **Outcome:** Both sides jointly issued the *Joint Statement of Outcomes from the 11th Annual China-Australia Prime Ministers’ Meeting*. Vice Premier Wu Zhenglong attended the meeting.

### 2. Chinese Open-Source AI Ecosystem
Reports indicate that China’s open-source large model ecosystem has achieved global leadership in download volume and application depth.
*   **Market Data:** As of mid-November 2026, China’s daily token invocation volume exceeded 500 trillion. On the OpenRouter platform (Nov 9–15), Chinese models accounted for 119.1 trillion tokens, ranking first globally for 29 consecutive weeks. Cumulative global downloads of Chinese AI open-source models surpassed 10 billion.
*   **Technological Innovation:** Key developments include the release of Kimi K3.1 Pro by Moonshot AI (noted as the largest parameter-scale open-source model) and Hy4 by Tencent Hunyuan. DeepSeek’s earlier innovations in Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture have influenced global trends. Monthshot AI’s Kimi K3 introduced a proprietary mixed linear attention mechanism.
*   **Domestic Adaptation:** Meituan announced in November 2026 the launch of LongCat-2.5, a trillion-parameter model trained and inferred on domestic computing power, moving from "running" to "running fast and efficiently."
*   **Applications:** Use cases range from industrial quality control (BOE’s Blue Whale Display Model) to low-code developer platforms (MeiDa) and international projects (Singapore’s "Sea Lion V4" model based on Alibaba’s Qwen).

### 3. Eighth China-ASEAN Rule of Law Forum
Held in Chongqing on November 19, 2026, under the theme "Deepening Rule of Law Exchange and Cooperation to Promote Regional Prosperity and Stability."
*   **Context:** The China-ASEAN Free Trade Area 3.0 Upgrade Agreement is scheduled to take effect on December 1, 2026.
*   **Key Discussions:** Participants focused on aligning laws, rules, and standards; combating transnational crime (specifically telecommunications fraud); and fostering talent.
*   **Specific Initiatives:** The China-ASEAN Law Education and Research Alliance, created in 2025, now includes universities from 8 countries and 20 institutions. Guangxi Minzu University highlighted its "six ASEAN resources" integration strategy for legal education.

### 4. Macroeconomic Policy Actions

*   **Training:** The General Office of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress conducted performance training for newly appointed county-level directors of standing committees.

### 5. Local Industrial Development: Guizhou Blueberry Industry
Guizhou’s Qiandongnan Miao and Dong Autonomous Prefecture has developed its blueberry industry into a major economic driver.
*   **Scale:** Planting area covers approximately 190,000 mu with a total output value of 1.649 billion yuan.
*   **Cluster Layout:** Maixian County serves as the core, radiating to Kaili City (21,700 mu), Danzhai County (over 30,000 mu), and other counties.
*   **Value Chain:** The sector follows a "70% processing, 30% fresh sales" model, producing frozen fruit, NFC juice, anthocyanin extracts, and wine.
*   **Standardization:** On September 1, 2025, the national standard GB/T 45691—2025 for "Geographical Indication Product Quality Requirements - Majiang Blueberry" came into effect, the first such national standard for blueberries in China.
*   **Tourism Integration:** The Mazhang Blueberry Ecological Tourism Area (Blue Dream Valley) generated 242.4 million yuan in tourism revenue in 2025, benefiting over 36,000 residents.

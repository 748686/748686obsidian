## Event Overview

From November 20 to 21, 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping participated in the 2026 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Leadership Week held in Shenzhen, China. During this period, he engaged in a密集 schedule of bilateral meetings with leaders from Indonesia, Australia, Singapore, Thailand, and Malaysia, as well as a historic trilateral meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump. Concurrently, Chinese Premier Li Qiang and NPC Chairman Zhao Leji conducted separate diplomatic engagements with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese in Beijing. The event was characterized by high-level strategic dialogues, the signing of joint statements, and the hosting of the first-ever meeting of the three most powerful nations' leaders.

## Core Facts

### 1. Trilateral Historic Summit (Russia-China-US)
*   **Date/Location:** November 19, 2026; Shenzhen.
*   **Participants:** Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin, Donald Trump.
*   **Outcome:** The three leaders held a meeting described as a "Peace Summit." They issued the **"Joint Declaration on Peace by the Leaders of China, Russia, and the United States"** (《中俄美元首和平宣言》).
*   **Key Themes:** The declaration focused on maintaining strategic stability and international security. Xi Jinping proposed four principles for great-power relations: mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and dialogue and consultation.
*   **Significance:** Marked the first time the leaders of these three nations met face-to-face.

### 2. Bilateral Meetings by President Xi Jinping (Shenzhen)
*   **Indonesia (Nov 20 Morning):** Met with President Prabowo. Both leaders announced the **launch of negotiations for a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)**. Discussions covered the Jakarta-Bandung High-Speed Railway, "Two Countries, Two Parks" projects, and cooperation in AI, digital economy, and clean energy. Prabowo affirmed Indonesia's one-China policy.
*   **Thailand (Nov 20 Morning):** Met with Prime Minister Anutin. Topics included strengthening the China-Thailand community with shared destiny, combating cyber fraud and online gambling, and fostering cooperation in agriculture, connectivity, AI, and new energy vehicles. China expressed willingness to help mediate Cambodia-Thailand relations.
*   **Malaysia (Nov 20 Noon):** Met with Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim. They reviewed progress since the April 2025 consensus to enter a new "Golden 50 Years" of relations. Key areas included infrastructure projects, the Confucian-Islamic Civilization Dialogue, and AI cooperation. Anwar congratulated China on the APEC summit and the trilateral summit.
*   **Australia (Nov 20 Morning):** Met with Prime Minister Anthony Albanese. This provided strategic guidance for further deepening China-Australia relations ahead of the 55th anniversary of diplomatic ties in 2027.
*   **Singapore (Contextual):** While Article #302 headline mentions a meeting with Singapore PM Lawrence Wong, the full text provided in the source article predominantly details the meeting with Thailand. However, the sidebar navigation of the newspaper edition lists "Xi Jinping Meets Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong" alongside the other meetings, confirming the event took place within this diplomatic window.

### 3. Diplomatic Activities in Beijing (Nov 20 Afternoon/Evening)
*   **Premier Li Qiang & Albanese:** Held the **11th China-Australia Prime Minister Annual Consultation** at the Diaoyutai State Guesthouse. They issued a **Joint Outcome Statement** outlining cooperation in trade, AI dialogue mechanisms, climate change, green steel, and tourism (designating 2027 as the "China-Australia Tourism Year"). They also attended the 9th CEO Roundtable.
*   **NPC Chairman Zhao Leji & Albanese:** Met to discuss parliamentary exchanges and implementing consensus from the Xi-Albanese meeting.
*   **Ding Xuexiang (Upcoming):** Announced that State Council Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang would visit Singapore on Nov 22-23 to co-chair the 22nd China-Singapore Joint Committee on Bilateral Cooperation and other joint coordination councils.

### 4. APEC Multilateral Outcomes
*   **Xi’s Speech:** Delivered a keynote speech titled "Building an Asia-Pacific Community, Writing a New Chapter of the 'Asia-Pacific Miracle'" at the Leaders' Session on Nov 19.
*   **Institutional Proposal:** Xi announced China’s support for establishing the **World Bay Area Economic Cooperation Alliance** to deepen exchanges among major bays globally.
*   **Declaração de Shenzhen:** The APEC Leaders' Declaration adopted the "Shenzhen Outlook," providing a roadmap for building an Asia-Pacific Community with four pillars: trade, connectivity, innovation, and development.

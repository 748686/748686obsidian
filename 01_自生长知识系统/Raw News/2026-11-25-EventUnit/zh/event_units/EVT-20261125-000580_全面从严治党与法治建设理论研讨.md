### 4. Style Construction: The "Constant" and "Long-term" (Source: Art #388, #389*)
*\*Note: Art #389 content in the prompt is actually the Kuliang Forum. Art #388 is the Style Construction article. I will synthesize from Art #388.*

*   **Core Argument:** Style construction is an eternal topic. It requires grasping both "Chang" (Constant/Frequent) and "Chang" (Long-term).
*   **"Chang" (Constant/Frequent):** Refers to regular supervision and education. Preventing "Four Malfeasances" from resurfacing requires daily vigilance, not just集中整治 (centralized campaigns). Example given: Jiao Yulu's "Ten Prohibitions for Cadres" in Lankao.
*   **"Chang" (Long-term):** Refers to sustainable institutional and ideological foundations. Relying on institutional constraints (solidifying results into rigid norms) and ideological tempering (ideal and belief education).
*   **Dialectical Unity:** "Chang" (frequency) is the prerequisite for "Chang" (longevity); "Chang" (longevity) is the purpose of "Chang" (frequency).

# Event Unit Synthesis: People's Daily November 25, 2026 (Social & Economic Sections)

## Event Overview

On **November 25, 2026**, *People's Daily* (Renmin Ribao) published content across its 11th (Society), 12th (Advertising), and 13th (Economy) editions. The primary reporting focuses on three distinct socio-economic developments in rural and border regions of China:
1.  **Healthcare Modernization:** The deployment of Jiangsu province's self-developed 5G intelligent dental diagnostic vehicles in Kizilsu Kyrgyz Autonomous Prefecture, Xinjiang, marking the 10th anniversary of medical aid efforts.
2.  **Agricultural Talent & Industry:** A case study in Dulan County, Qinghai, illustrating how targeted talent introduction ("industry sets the question, talent provides the answer") transformed the goji berry and livestock sectors through technology and branding.
3.  **Industrial Upgrading:** An investigative report on Donghai County, Jiangsu, detailing the transformation of the local crystal industry from raw material exports to high-value cultural crafts,跨境 e-commerce, and high-tech silicon materials.

## Core Facts

### 1. Jiangsu Medical Aid in Xinjiang (Kizilsu Prefecture)
*   **Event:** Nanjing Medical University Affiliated Stomatological Hospital (Jiangsu Provincial Stomatological Hospital) deployed a self-developed 5G oral intelligent diagnostic vehicle to the Pamir Plateau in Kizilsu Kyrgyz Autonomous Prefecture (Kekesuer, Xinjiang).
*   **Context:** This coincides with the 10th anniversary of Jiangsu's "group-style" medical aid to Kizilsu and the "Runxin Plan."
*   **Activity Name:** "Su-Ke同心·Smart Travel across the Pamir" (Su-Ke Tongxin · Zhi Xing Pamir) health campaign.
*   **Coverage:** The vehicle, affectionately called the "Love Tooth Car," traveled from Artux City to border哨 posts, special schools, teaching campuses, and nursing homes.
*   **Output:** Provided science popularization and diagnostic services to over **1,100 people**.
*   **Technology:** The vehicle integrates 5G remote consultation and AI-assisted oral screening. It performs one-stop imaging/signs collection; AI makes preliminary judgments; data is transmitted in real-time to experts in Jiangsu via 5G, achieving an "on-board clinic" effect.
*   **Specific Actions:**
    *   Established "border guard oral health records" for soldiers.
    *   Connected students at Xiayite Primary School in Halajun Township with Jiangsu experts remotely ("register in Kizilsu, consult in Jiangsu").

### 2. Dulan County Agricultural Transformation (Qinghai)
*   **Location:** Dulan County, Haixi Mongol and Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, Qinghai Province.
*   **Model:** "Industry sets the question, talent provides the answer" (Chan Yi Chu Ti, Ren Cai Jie Ti) targeted talent introduction mechanism.
*   **Talent Sourcing:** Flexible introduction of over **70 high-level agricultural experts** via platforms like the "Wisdom of the Three Rivers Source" talent fair, Beijing-Qinghai expert assistance, and scientific commissioners.
*   **Key Technologies:**
    *   Vacuum pulse lock-fresh technology for goji berries, solving preservation issues.
    *   Standardized planting techniques for salt-alkali land adaptation.
*   **Economic Impact:**
    *   Goji berry purchase price increased by **2 RMB per *jin* (500g)** due to added value.
    *   Local veterinarian Guo Zhengpu (Re Shui Township) achieved a **97% lamb survival rate** and enabled 4-month fattening cycles (5kg weight gain per lamb) via standardized breeding.
    *   Cultivated **1,300+ local rural technical talents** and **23 senior/junior rural talents**.
    *   Training covered **50+ sessions** for 1,000+ people, leading to stable employment for 600+ people.
    *   Goji berry harvesting labor absorbs **34,500 workers annually**, generating **179 million RMB** in income.
*   **Industrial Structure:**
    *   Main agricultural/livestock product processing conversion rate remains stable above **60%**.
    *   Qorghay (coarse cereal) processing conversion rate rose to **75%**.
    *   Branding: "Magical Qaidam" and "Dulan Youpin" regional public brands; Yaks and Tibetan sheep selected as national famous/special/excellent/new agricultural products.
    *   Export: Goji berries account for **35% of the province's export volume**, reaching **18 countries and regions**.
    *   Overall chain value added increased by an average of **50%**.

### 3. Donghai County Crystal Industry Upgrade (Jiangsu)
*   **Location:** Donghai County, Jiangsu Province (approx. 300,000 tons of crystal reserves).
*   **Historical Shift:** Over 40 years, shifted from selling raw stones/fused products to manufacturing fashionable consumer goods and high-end industrial materials.
*   **Segment A: Cultural/Crafts**
    *   ~50,000 "stone淘ing" (raw stone hunting) personnel import over 100,000 tons of overseas raw stone annually.
    *   Crystal carving listed as a national intangible cultural heritage project in 2021.
    *   County has over **12,000 crystal carvers**; annual output of carved crafts is **5 million pieces**.
    *   Master-level carvings sell for tens of thousands of RMB vs. hundreds for common goods.
*   **Segment B: Wearable Nails (Cross-border E-commerce)**
    *   2025 Sales: **15 billion RMB** (accounting for >70% of national sales).
    *   2025 Export: **6 billion RMB**, shipped to **30+ countries/regions**.
    *   Innovation: Upcycling crystal waste/bits into high-value decorative nails (e.g., designs featuring Sun Wukong, Dunhuang motifs).
    *   Policy Support: In Sept 2025, 7 provincial departments issued measures supporting the industry. Original design proportion rose from <5% (2022) to ~30%.
    *   2025 Total Crystal Trading Volume: **60.6 billion RMB** (E-commerce portion: 36.7 billion RMB).
*   **Segment C: High-Tech Silicon Materials**
    *   Utilization of impure quartz/crystal waste for high-purity silicon micro powder.
    *   **Aviation:** Jiangsu Zhijiang Aviation Tech Co. produces ceramic cores for aero-engine blades (value: tens of RMB per gram vs. <100 RMB/ton for raw ore).
    *   **AI/Semiconductors:** Jiangsu Hongyang Quartz Products Co. focuses on electronic-grade drawing quartz rods. Jiangsu Lianrui New Materials produces spherical silicon micro powder for AI server copper-clad laminates (price >200,000 RMB/ton).
    *   2026 Projection: Silicon industry above-scale output value expected to exceed **20 billion RMB**.

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives

1.  **Jiangsu-Xinjiang Collaboration (Source 394):** Highlights the "pairing assistance" (duikou yuanyuan) model, where a prosperous coastal province (Jiangsu) transfers advanced medical technology (5G/AI diagnostic vehicles) to a border region (Xinjiang) to improve healthcare accessibility for ethnic minorities and border guards.
2.  **Qinghai-Tibet Plateau Development (Source 395):** Focuses on the integration of talent strategy with traditional agriculture in a high-altitude, ecologically fragile region (Qaidam Basin). It emphasizes moving from "blood transfusion" (external aid) to "hematopoiesis" (local capacity building) to solve issues like low yield and poor preservation.
3.  **Eastern County Industrial Upgrading (Source 399):** Illustrates a mature industrial cluster (Donghai) transitioning from resource-dependent extraction to value-added manufacturing, cultural IP integration, and high-tech material science, demonstrating industrial resilience and globalization (cross-border e-commerce).

## Known Current Impact

*   **Healthcare Access:** Medical services previously confined to fixed hospitals in Kizilsu are now accessible in schools, border哨 posts, and nursing homes via 5G telemedicine, reducing the need for patients to travel to Urumqi or Nanjing for initial screenings.
*   **Income Growth:** Dulan County goji farmers have seen a direct price increase of 2 RMB/jin, addressing the "abundant harvest but low income" dilemma. Local employment in agriculture and related logistics has been stabilized (179 million RMB in wages).
*   **Industrial Value Chain:** Donghai County has successfully diversified its economy. The upcycling of crystal waste into aviation and AI hardware materials has created a new growth engine (projected 20 billion RMB silicon industry), reducing reliance on raw stone sales.
*   **Cultural Export:** Donghai's crystal穿戴甲 and carved crafts are now exported to 30+ countries, turning local intangible cultural heritage into global consumer goods.

## What Cannot Currently Be Determined

*   **Long-term Sustainability of Dulan Model:** It is unclear if the "industry sets question, talent answers" model can be replicated in other parts of Qinghai without the specific conditions found in Dulan.
*   **Scale of 5G Diagnostic Vehicle Deployment:** While the current campaign served 1,100+ people, the long-term operational schedule and frequency of the vehicle's visits to remote border areas are not specified.
*   **Market Saturation of Wearable Nails:** It is unknown if the rapid growth of Donghai's wearable nail industry (150 billion RMB in 2025) faces imminent market saturation or trade barrier challenges in target export markets.
*   **Causal Link in Goji Prices:** While the article attributes the 2 RMB/jin increase to processing technology and branding, the exact proportion of price increase attributable to each factor (processing vs. brand premium vs. supply/demand) cannot be disaggregated from the text.

## Event Conclusion

On November 25, 2026, *People's Daily* reported on coordinated developmental strategies across western and eastern China. In Xinjiang, Jiangsu's medical aid utilized 5G and AI to extend healthcare to remote border populations. In Qinghai, Dulan County leveraged targeted talent introduction to modernize agriculture, increasing farmer income and export capacity. In Jiangsu, Donghai County exemplified industrial upgrading, transforming a traditional raw material sector into a high-value hub for cultural crafts and advanced silicon materials. These events collectively illustrate the Chinese state's focus on technological empowerment, rural revitalization, and industrial chain modernization as key drivers of regional economic disparity reduction.

## Event Overview
On November 25, 2026, the theoretical section (Page 09) of *People's Daily* published two major articles intended to guide ideological study and academic development in China. The first article, authored by Tang Fangyu, summarizes the "great achievements of comprehensive strict governance of the Party in the New Era" in light of the decisions from the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee. The second article, authored by Zhu Feng of Nanjing University, discusses how Xi Jinping's diplomatic thought guides the construction of an independent knowledge system for China's international relations studies. Both articles were published on the same date and page, indicating a coordinated editorial effort to reinforce theoretical frameworks for governance and international relations.

## Core Facts

### Article 1: Great Achievements of Comprehensive Strict Party Governance
*   **Author:** Tang Fangyu.
*   **Publication:** *People's Daily*, Page 09, November 25, 2026.
*   **Context:** Interpreting the "Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persistently Advancing the Comprehensive Strict Governance of the Party" adopted by the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee.
*   **Key Content:** The article outlines eight aspects of achievement since the 18th National Congress:
    1.  Strengthening overall Party leadership and central unified leadership.
    2.  New leap in the Sinicization and modernization of Marxism.
    3.  Effective implementation of the Party's organizational line in the New Era.
    4.  Deepening the rectification of "Four Malfeasances" (formalism, bureaucracy, hedonism, and extravagance) via the Central Eight-Point Regulation.
    5.  Solid progress in Party discipline construction.
    6.  Unprecedented anti-corruption struggle.
    7.  Improvement of the intra-Party regulatory system (citing 2,674 regulations enacted or revised by June 2026).
    8.  Enhanced awareness and responsibility implementation among Party organizations.
*   **Theoretical Conclusions:** The article identifies three key takeaways: the achievements are historic and all-around; the fundamental reason is the leadership of the Party Central Committee with Comrade Xi Jinping at the core and the guidance of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era; and these achievements provide confidence for future challenges rather than grounds for complacency.

### Article 2: Xi Jinping Diplomatic Thought and International Relations Knowledge System
*   **Author:** Zhu Feng (Professor, School of International Relations, Nanjing University).
*   **Publication:** *People's Daily*, Page 09, November 25, 2026.
*   **Key Content:**
    *   Argues that Xi Jinping's diplomatic thought provides the theoretical direction and spiritual动力 for constructing an independent knowledge system for Chinese international relations studies.
    *   Highlights the concept of a "Community with a Shared Future for Mankind" as surpassing Western International Relations theories that assume anarchy and inevitable conflict.
    *   Details the "four beams and eight pillars" of the community concept and the "six musts" for diplomatic work.

## Core Facts
**1. Huawei HarmonyOS Ecosystem Milestone**
*   **Date of Announcement:** November 24, 2026.
*   **Key Metric:** Huawei announced that the number of HarmonyOS terminal devices has exceeded 100 million.
*   **Timeline:** First released in 2019; officially launched on smartphones in 2021.
*   **Ecosystem Stats:** The system comprises 460,000 applications and services, over 12 million registered developers, and more than 120 industry-specific editions.
*   **OpenHarmony Contribution:** The open-source HarmonyOS project has gathered over 14,000 contributors.
*   **Strategic Context:** The growth is attributed to overcoming long-term restrictions in the operating system sector (described historically as "lacking chips and souls," where the "soul" refers to the OS).

**2. Harbin Pingfang District (Harbin Economic and Technological Development Zone) Industrial Development**
*   **Status:** An old industrial base in Harbin, Heilongjiang Province, covering 85% of the province's industrial categories. It was selected as a pilot unit for the National Quality Strong County (District/Town) cultivation construction innovation task in 2025.
*   **2026 Project Progress:** 133 industrial projects under the "Four Lists Integration" (四单合一) initiative are being advanced.
    *   74 newly started projects.
    *   59 continuing projects.
    *   **Completion Status:** All projects have started or resumed work.
    *   **Investment:** 12.11 billion RMB completed, representing a completion rate of 141.7%.
*   **Key Industries:** Aviation, Digital Economy, Automobile, and Bio-economy.
    *   67 major industry projects (50.4% of all projects).
    *   **Aviation:** 24 projects including Hafei C919 and high-end equipment projects.
    *   **Digital Economy:** 18 projects including ultra-large computing power facilities.
    *   **Automobile:** 15 projects including Yunsong Automobile expansion and Dong'an Power production lines.
    *   **Bio-economy:** 10 projects including Xier Bio blood product生产基地 and Dazhong Pharmaceutical API workshops.
*   **Future Goals:** Building a national advanced manufacturing base, a national sci-tech innovation city demonstration zone, a national leading area for opening up to the North, and a world-class ice/snow tourism destination.

**3. 4th Summer Youth Olympics in Dakar, Senegal**
*   **Significance:** The first Olympic series event held on the African continent.
*   **Educational Impact:** Over 1 million students participated in Olympic education (exceeding the initial target of 900,000).
*   **Talent Development:** The Dakar Youth Olympic Games Learning Academy trained over 300 event operation professionals across 21 African countries and regions.
*   **Volunteerism:** 23,000 applications received; 6,000 volunteers selected (average age 28.6, >90% local African talent). By November 12, 5,647 volunteers were actively serving.
*   **Ticket Distribution:** Out of ~960,000 tickets, 300,000 "Solidarity Tickets" were allocated to children, students, and local sports associations.
*   **Infrastructure Legacy:**
    *   Diop Stadium and the Olympic Pool were renovated.
    *   The Youth Olympic Village utilizes facilities from Amadou Mahtar Mbow University and will remain as public resources for students and the community post-games.
    *   IOC partners equipped 5 community centers with sports equipment and fitness courses.
*   **Environmental Initiatives:** A "Plastic-Free Youth Olympics" project was introduced to reduce single-use plastics and strengthen waste management, extending环保 behaviors from the event to the community.

**4. Sino-Senegalese Cooperation in Sports and Infrastructure**
*   **Infrastructure Construction:**
    *   **China Civil Engineering Construction Corporation (CCECC):** Participated in the reconstruction of Diop Stadium in Dakar. New facilities include an 8,000-seat football field with standard athletics tracks and a 3,000-seat indoor handball gym. Peak construction employed over 400 workers, 350+ of whom were Senegalese. CCECC is transferring operation and maintenance technologies.
    *   **China State Construction Engineering Corporation (CSCEC):** Completed and handed over the renovation of 4 stadiums in Senegal (Senghor Stadium in Dakar, plus stadiums in Ziguinchor, Kolda, and Tambacounda) in April 2025. This created over 1,000 jobs.
    *   **China Road and Bridge Corporation (CRBC):** Donated fitness parks in Senegal.
*   **Digital & Educational Support:**
    *   **Alibaba:** Built proprietary cloud infrastructure for the games, which was handed over to Senegal for continued local digital construction. Donated sports equipment packages to 850 primary schools, benefiting over 350,000 children.
*   **Athlete Training:**
    *   Senegal sent nearly 100 athletes and coaches annually to China for two consecutive years for training in track and field, swimming, judo, taekwondo, and wushu.
    *   66 athletes who underwent double training won 2 gold medals and 4 bronze medals for Senegal at the Games.
    *   Wushu coach Zhou Yang from Henan Polytechnic University trained local athletes in Dakar in August.

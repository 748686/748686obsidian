## Event Overview

On November 25, 2026, the *People's Daily* (*Renmin Ribao*) published content across its theoretical and special report sections. The first-layer synthesis focused on Article #388 (Theory Section, Page 09), which addresses the theoretical principles of the Communist Party of China’s (CPC) work style construction. However, the source data for this merge also includes Articles #389 and #390 (Special Report Section, Page 10), which cover the 2026 Guling Forum and Sino-American youth exchanges.

## Core Facts

### 1. Theoretical Analysis of Party Work Style (From Article #388)
*   **Publication Date:** November 25, 2026.
*   **Section:** Page 09 (Theory).
*   **Author:** Chi Jiarui (迟嘉瑞).
*   **Primary Theme:** The dialectical relationship between "Chang" (constancy/frequency) and "Chang" (long-term/endurance) in the construction of the Party's work style.
*   **Policy Context:** The article cites the "Decision" of the CPC Central Committee's Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee, describing "relentlessly correcting conduct and enforcing discipline" as a strategic task for comprehensive strict governance of the Party.
*   **Key Quotes from Xi Jinping:**
    *   "Treat both the symptoms and the root cause; frequent inspection to see normalcy, in-depth inspection to see effectiveness, and persistent inspection to see long-term effect."
    *   "Conduct construction is always on the way."
*   **Historical Reference:** The article cites Jiao Yulu’s establishment of the "Ten Prohibitions for Cadres" in Lankao as a model for integrating rules into daily details (e.g., ticket prices, meals).
*   **Argument:**
    *   **"Chang" (Frequency):** Answering "how often to grasp" by integrating strict requirements into daily education and supervision.
    *   **"Chang" (Long-term):** Answering "how long to grasp" by using institutional constraints and ideal/belief education to build a solid foundation.
    *   **Conclusion:** The two are dialectically unified; frequent supervision is the premise, and long-term effectiveness is the goal.

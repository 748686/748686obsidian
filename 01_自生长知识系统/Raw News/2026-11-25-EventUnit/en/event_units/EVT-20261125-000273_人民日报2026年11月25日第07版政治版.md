## Core Facts

### 1. Xuyi County "City Reading" Initiative (Jiangsu)
- **Date:** November 25, 2026 (Publication date).
- **Location:** Xuyi County (Xuyi), Huai'an City, Jiangsu Province.
- **Action:** In 2026, Xuyi launched eight "City Reading" (读城) actions under the theme "Lixiang Duliang Deep Read Xuyi" (理响都梁深读盱眙).
- **The Eight Actions:**
  1. "Lixiang Duliang Deep Read Xuyi" (理响都梁深读盱眙)
  2. "Walking Duliang Study Read Xuyi" (行走都梁研读盱眙)
  3. "Accompanying Silver Hair Happy Read Xuyi" (陪伴银发悦读盱眙)
  4. "City Trace Pursuit Taste Read Xuyi" (城迹追寻品读盱眙)
  5. "E-See Beautiful Web Read Xuyi" (E见美好网读盱眙)
  6. "Cultivating People with Culture Fine Read Xuyi" (以文化人精读盱眙)
  7. "Light and Shadow Canal Slow Read Xuyi" (光影运河慢读盱眙)
  8. "I Love My Home Farming Read Xuyi" (我爱我家耕读盱眙)
- **Industrial Highlights:**
  - Yute Optoelectronics successfully listed.
  - TBEA (特变电工) established supply chain supporting enterprises after landing in Xuyi.
  - Huacheng Industrial Cloth (rooted for 16 years) expanded production.
  - Wurancai Green Circular Polyester New Material project put into operation.
  - Huachen Optoelectronics Phase II power issue resolved within two days.
  - Feiyin Precision project signed and reached trial production within one month.
  - "Xuyi No. 1" (盱眙1号) crayfish variety developed over 12 years through six generations of selection.
  - Xuyi Crayfish Super Factory covers 200,000 square meters.
  - Local culinary heritage includes Huanghuatang century-old steamed bun shop (4th generation) and Taoyuan Restaurant's old brine (decades).
- **Urban Living Improvements:**
  - Renovation recorded for 27 residential communities and 3,217 households.
  - 176 public appeals/suggestions collected and addressed via "one community, one plan; one household, one policy."
  - Three state-owned property management enterprises took over 113 residential communities, serving over 30,000 households.
  - Service channels include community councils, 24-hour hotlines, and online photo-submission platforms.
  - 7.4-kilometer Huai River trail connects landscape and historical sites.
- **Cultural/Historical Activities:**
  - Locations visited: First Mountain National Forest Park (摩崖石刻), Dayunshan Han King Tomb Museum, Mingzu陵 (Ming祖陵), Huanghuatang New Fourth Army Headquarters Memorial.
  - 50 national reading series events held; thousands of families participated in parent-child city reading video submissions.
  - Nearly 50 writers and photographers participated in "Light and Shadow Canal Slow Read Xuyi."
- **Data Source:** Propaganda Department of the Xuyi County Committee of the CPC.

### 5. Meizhou Jinyou (Golden Pomelo) Rural Revitalization (Guangdong)
- **Location:** Nanfu Village, Yanyang Town, Meixian District, Meizhou City, Guangdong Province.
- **Political Context:** Follow-up to General Secretary Xi Jinping's visit on November 7, 2025.
- **Visit Details:** Xi Jinping visited the Nanfu Golden Pomelo Planting Base, listened to reports on support for revolutionary old areas and rural revitalization, inspected pomelo products and cultural/creative goods, and spoke with farmers and agricultural technicians.
- **Key Directives from Visit:** Develop distinctive rural industries as the foundation for comprehensive rural revitalization; strengthen scientific and technological application; promote integration of agriculture, culture, and tourism; extend industrial chains; increase added value; and drive farmer income growth.
- **One-Year Progress (as of Nov 2026):**
  - **Technology:** Adoption of smart agriculture digital platforms and water-fertilizer integration systems; use of drones and mountain track vehicles for transport; standardization of pest control and soil testing.
  - **Quality Improvement:** Excellent fruit rate increased from 70% to 91%.
  - **Industrial Integration:** Development of pomelo essential oil, pomelo flower tea, and naringin (柚皮苷) products.
  - **Tourism:** Transformation of orchards into open pomelo parks; establishment of pomelo cafes and agricultural markets; linkage with Ye Jianying Memorial Park and Yanshan Lake International Garden Resort.
  - **Economic Impact:**
    - Returnee youth livestream sales: approx. 8 million jin (4,000 metric tons) sold online annually.
    - "Party Branch + Cooperative + Base + Farmer" model utilized.
    - 430+ households in Nanfu Village grow pomelos; planting area exceeds 5,000 mu (~333 hectares).
    - Expected annual output: 30 million jin (15,000 metric tons).
    - Per capita annual income increase: approx. 30,000 RMB, accounting for >80% of total income.
  - **City-Wide Statistics (Meizhou):**
    - Total planting area: approx. 660,000 mu (~44,000 hectares).
    - Full industry chain output value: approx. 9 billion RMB.
    - Beneficiaries: Over 100,000 farming households.
- **Media Team:** Photographers/Reporters Wu Qiqiang, Li Ge, Cheng Yuanzhou, Zhang Wujun.
- **Publication:** *People's Daily*, Page 08 (Visual), November 25, 2026.

It emphasizes the integration of industrial observation (crayfish, optoelectronics), livelihood improvement (old community renovation, property management), and historical preservation. The perspective is that of a developed coastal province leveraging cultural capital and industrial upgrading to enrich city identity.
2.  **Guangdong (Rural/Revolutionary Old Area Revitalization):** Focuses on Meizhou's transformation of a singular agricultural product (golden pomelo) into a multi-billion RMB industry. It highlights the role of state-directed policy (follow-up to central leadership visits), technological modernization in agriculture, and the integration of primary, secondary, and tertiary industries (agriculture, processing, tourism). The perspective emphasizes the success of the "Hundred, Thousand, Ten Thousand High-Quality Development Project" in former revolutionary base areas.

Both narratives align with the central theme of "high-quality development" and "rural/urban revitalization" but target different geographic and economic contexts (industrial county vs. agricultural old revolutionary area).

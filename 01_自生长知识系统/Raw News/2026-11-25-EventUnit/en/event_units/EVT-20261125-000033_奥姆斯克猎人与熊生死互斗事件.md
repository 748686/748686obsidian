The encounter resulted in the death of the hunter and fatal injuries to the bear, which was subsequently located. Multiple news outlets have reported on the event, confirming the mutual fatalities but offering varying levels of detail regarding the circumstances.

## Core Facts

Based on the synthesis of available reporting:

*   **Date:** November 24, 2026.
*   **Location:** Omsk region, Russia (specifically near the village of Kurchum in the Omsk district).
*   **Subjects:** A hunter (identified as a 57-year-old local resident) and a bear.
*   **Outcome:** Both the hunter and the bear died. The hunter suffered fatal trauma, while the bear was found with severe injuries.
*   **Identification:** The hunter's identity has been confirmed by regional investigative authorities.

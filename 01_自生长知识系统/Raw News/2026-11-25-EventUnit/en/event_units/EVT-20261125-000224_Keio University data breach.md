# Keio University Data Breach

## Event Overview

Between September 2026 and November 2026, Keio University disclosed a significant data breach that exposed the personal information of over 11 million individuals. The incident was attributed to a cyberattack in which an unauthorized third party gained access to the university's external computer systems.

## Core Facts

*   **Scope of Impact:** The breach affects approximately 11.34 million people, comprising students, staff, alumni, and other associated individuals.
*   **Timeline:**
    *   **September 2026:** The university discovered the external intrusion and identified that personal information had been copied by the unauthorized party.
    *   **November 12, 2026:** Keio University released an official statement disclosing the scale of the breach to the public.
    *   **November 18, 2026:** University President Toshiyuki Nishikawa issued an apology letter. The letter was made available in Japanese via the university website and in English via its social media channels.
*   **Nature of Intrusion:** The leak resulted from a cyberattack where a third party accessed external systems without authorization.
*   **Data Categories Compromised:** The leaked information includes names, contact details, academic records, dates of birth, addresses, ID numbers, and in certain cases, financial account details.

## Cross-Source Verification

*   **Confirmation of Cyberattack and Scale:** Multiple sources corroborate that the breach was caused by unauthorized external access (cyberattack) and involves over 11 million (specifically ~11.34 million) records.
*   **Timeline Consistency:** Reports from early November confirm the discovery occurred in September, with the official disclosure following in mid-November.
*   **Apology Issued:** Sources confirm President Toshiyuki Nishikawa issued an apology in late November.

*   **Institutional Response:** Keio University has initiated support measures, including free credit monitoring services for victims.
*   **Public Accountability:** The university leadership has issued public apologies, though specific technical details regarding the breach's entry point remain undisclosed.

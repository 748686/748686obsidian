## Core Facts

### Tourism Infrastructure: "County-Bound Travel" Boom
- Shanxi Province's Yuncheng City has seen rising popularity in "county-bound travel" (奔县游) driven by the 2024 completion of the Yellow River No. 1 Tourism Highway.
- The Yuncheng section spans approximately 500 kilometers, connecting 93 natural and cultural landscapes across the region.
- Three counties were surveyed during the holiday:
  - **Yuanqu County**: Focused on supporting service capacity, including highway rest stations with nursing rooms and reading rooms.
  - **Ruicheng County**: Leveraged the Yuan Dynasty Yongle Palace murals and Tang Dynasty Guangrenwang Temple; reported a 50% year-on-year increase in visitors on the first day, exceeding 26,000 daily visitors.
  - **Yongji City**: Shared parking facilities (opening party/government compounds) and nighttime tourism activities including lantern displays and music events at the Stork Tower scenic area.
- Shanxi has 102 national key cultural relic protection units, ranking among the highest for prefecture-level cities nationwide. (Source: Articles #385, #386)

### Red Heritage and Military-Civilian Relations
- A small wooden sparrow carving, preserved for over 90 years by Chen Shiguo (68) in Yingtai Zhai Village, Taohexi Township, Yingshan County, Hubei, commemorates Red Army soldiers' care during the 1934 Long March.
- The Red 25th Army stationed in Taohexi fought a three-day, two-night battle, suffering over 280 casualties; local civilians hid and treated over 100 wounded soldiers.
- A PLA squad leader carved the sparrow with a bayonet before departing, leaving it as a token to reunite after revolution's success.
- The Long March National Cultural Park (Yingshan园区) opened in November 2023, has been rated a National 4A Tourist Attraction, and has received nearly one million visitors since opening. (Source: Articles #386, #387)

### University Library Reform
- Zhejiang University transformed its library into a育人场 (educational space), where students independently curate exhibitions and produce immersive theater productions such as *Night at Guantong Hall*, with tickets selling out in 16 seconds.
- Hangzhou Normal University integrated history exhibitions (Ma Xulun and Shen Junru) and a 8,000-volume collection of 60+ scholars' works into its library, enabling students to interact with historical figures through digital projects. (Source: Articles #387, #388)

### National Day Cultural Phenomena
- Museum attendance surged during the holiday; exhibitions included the National Museum's "River East" exhibition (200+ artifacts on Yuncheng and early Chinese civilization), the恭王府景泰蓝 exhibition, and the良渚博物院 jade cong exhibition.
- Technology-enhanced exhibits featured immersive甲骨文 recognition apps at the Yinxu Museum and digital金陵图 experiences at Nanjing Deji Plaza.
- Patriotic activities included flag-raising ceremonies at Tiananmen Square (over 100,000 attendees on October 1), Erenhot border gate, and Hongqilafu checkpoint.
- The Communist Party's 105th anniversary and the 90th anniversary of the Long March were observed through exhibitions at the Chinese People's Revolution Military Museum (300+ new artifacts) and the red stage play *The First Crossing of the Long March* in Jiangxi. (Source: Articles #388)

### Laborer Dedication
- Workers in construction, security, and livelihood services remained on duty during the holiday, photographed across multiple provinces including Beijing, Jiangsu, Hebei, Gansu, Guangxi, Sichuan, Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Fujian, and Shandong. (Source: Articles #389, #390, #391)

### National Archaeological Sites
- The "15th Five-Year Plan" for cultural relic事业发展 proposes increasing national archaeological site parks from 65 to 70 by 2030.
- Three site parks were highlighted:
  - **Henan Yangshao Village National Archaeological Site Park**: Opened in 2021 for the 100th anniversary of Yangshao culture discovery; annual visitors exceed 300,000; village collective income increased by over 15%.
  - **Hubei Panlongcheng Site**: A 3,500-year-old Shang Dynasty city;采用了3D printing and非遗泥塑 techniques for palace restoration; features "invisible" drainage systems and "combination repair method" for artifacts.
  - **Guangdong Bijiashan Chaozhou Kiln Site**: A Song Dynasty kiln complex spanning 2 kilometers; conducted excavations from 2016-2024 revealing 13 dragon kilns and architectural remains; offers public archaeology programs. (Source: Articles #392, #393)

### Museum Innovation and Management
- Museums faced "ticket sold out in seconds" phenomena; solutions include dynamic ticket release (e.g., Inner Mongolia Museum releasing cancelled slots in real-time),联票联动 schemes, and anti-scalper enforcement.
- Digital innovations include cloud exhibitions, AI-guided tours, and virtual reality experiences. (Source: Articles #393, #394)

### Scientific Research and Public Engagement
- Yunnan transformed科研 institutions into科普 sites:
  - Xishuangbanna Tropical Floral Garden introduced night tours, mushroom foraging, and rubber tapping experiences; quarterly revenue exceeded previous annual totals.
  - Kunming Institute of Botany adopted预约制 for lab visits and community outreach.
- Key原则: spatial zoning (non-open areas for core research), content review by scientists, and scalable activities (small batches offline, broad reach online). (Source: Articles #395, #396)

### Cultural Relic IP and Museums
- **Hubei Provincial Museum**: The Zeng Hou Yi Bells (discovered 1978) serve as the museum emblem; 2025 saw 1,330 performances and 470,000 attendees; digital audio collaboration with Tencent Games preserved bell sounds for gaming.
- **Guangxi Museum**: The Han Dynasty羽纹铜凤灯 (feather-patterned bronze phoenix lamp) serves as the museum emblem since 2006; features smoke filtration through water-filled cavity; offers文创 products andAR exhibits. (Source: Articles #396, #397, #398)

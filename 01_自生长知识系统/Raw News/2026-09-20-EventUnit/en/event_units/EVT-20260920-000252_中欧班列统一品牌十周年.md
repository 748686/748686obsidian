# Event: Ten Years of the Unified Brand for China-Europe Railway Express (CR Express)

## Event Overview

On September 20, 2026, *People's Daily* published a two-part in-depth report marking the tenth anniversary of the unified branding of the China-Europe Railway Express (CR Express). The report highlights the transition from fragmented local operations to a standardized international logistics brand. The article notes that CR Express cumulative shipments have exceeded 130,000 trains, with cargo value surpassing $520 billion, establishing it as a globally recognized international logistics brand.

## Core Facts

### Historical Trajectory and Growth
*   **Initial Launch:** The first CR Express route, "Yu Xin Ou," departed from Chongqing in 2011. Subsequent routes included "Han Xin Ou," "Xiang Ou Express," and "Zheng Xin Ou."
*   **Pre-Unity Volume:** In 2013, annual train volume was 80. By 2015, this rose to 815 trains.
*   **Unified Brand Initiation:** On June 8, 2016, the unified brand for CR Express was officially launched.
*   **Brand Identity:** The logo features a galloping train and flowing silk, incorporating elements of the China Railway emblem and English abbreviation, using Chinese red and black as primary colors.
*   **Growth Metrics:** Annual train volume increased from 1,702 in 2016 to 20,022 in 2025, representing a 10.8-fold increase with an average annual growth rate of 31.5%.
*   **Cumulative Totals:** As of 2026, cumulative trains exceed 130,000, with total cargo value exceeding $520 billion.

### Operational Network and Standards
*   **Network Expansion:** There are now 129 cities in China operating CR Express routes, connecting to 236 cities across 26 European countries. This represents an increase of 113 domestic and 216 foreign cities compared to 2016.
*   **Infrastructure:** The network comprises three main corridors (West, Central, East) within China, featuring 96 fixed train lines with a speed limit of 120 km/h.
*   **Speed and Capacity:**
    *   Domestic section speed: Uniformly 1,600 km/day.
    *   International section speed: 1,000–1,300 km/day (approx. 1/3 faster than traditional international rail transport).
    *   Maximum consists: 55 cars; Traction mass: 3,000 tons.
*   **Customs Efficiency:** Utilizing the "Railway Express Pass" mode and "Digital Port" systems, customs clearance time at ports has been compressed to as little as 30 minutes. The inter-station transparent system at Khorgos (Xinjiang) improved operational efficiency by 11% and saves 10 minutes per train.

### Economic and Environmental Impact
*   **Cost Reduction:** Transport prices (domestic and international) have decreased by over 40% compared to the initial period of operations.
*   **Cargo Diversity:** Goods span 53 categories and over 50,000 types. High value-added exports include auto parts, machinery, and electronics. Imports include European timber, pulp, agricultural products, and consumer goods.
*   **Case Study:** A shipment of 18,600 tons of chemical raw materials (310 containers) from China to a European enterprise was completed in 17 days (Zhengzhou to European hinterland), compared to 45–60 days by sea. Total logistics costs were reduced by 29%.
*   **Green Initiatives:** On June 24, 2026, the first "Zero Carbon" CR Express from the Yangtze River Delta arrived at Yiwu West Railway Station. It utilized 100% green electricity on electrified sections and Gold Standard certified carbon offsets on non-electrified sections.

*   **Service Branding:** CR Express is positioned as a premier "China Service" brand, demonstrating the quality of China's logistics and service sectors.
*   **Logistics Alternative:** It provides a viable alternative to sea freight (faster) and air freight (cheaper), particularly for high-value manufactured goods.

## Event Conclusion

The China-Europe Railway Express has successfully matured over its first decade, transforming from a collection of uncoordinated local routes into a unified, standardized, and globally competitive logistics brand. The implementation of a unified brand in 2016 was the critical catalyst, driving a 10.8-fold increase in annual volume and establishing a network covering 129 Chinese cities and 236 European cities. The service now offers significant advantages in speed, cost, and reliability compared to traditional freight modes, while increasingly integrating green logistics standards. The data confirms its status as a major international public product and a key component of China's service export branding strategy.

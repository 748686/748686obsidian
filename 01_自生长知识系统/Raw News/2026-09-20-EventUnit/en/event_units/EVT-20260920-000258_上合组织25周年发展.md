## Event Overview

On September 20, 2026, the *People's Daily* published an international forum article titled "The Shanghai Cooperation Organization Continues to Radiate New Vitality," commemorating the 25th anniversary of the founding of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO). The article, authored by Serik Kozhumbarov, Editor-in-Chief of the Kazakh newspaper *Kaspiy Oil and Gas Business*, analyzes the SCO’s development trajectory from its inception to its current status as a major regional cooperation organization. It highlights the "Shanghai Spirit" as the core guiding principle and outlines recent strategic initiatives announced during the SCO Bishkek Summit, focusing on the shift from traditional sectors to emerging technologies and digital economies.

## Core Facts

**Historical Context and Status**
*   **Anniversary:** The year 2026 marks the 25th anniversary of the establishment of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization.
*   **Scope:** Over 25 years, the SCO has grown into the world’s largest regional cooperation organization by territory and population, with significant development potential.
*   **Diversity:** Member states possess diverse political systems, development paths, and cultural traditions, yet have maintained consensus and mutual trust.

**Strategic Framework ("The Three 'Mosts')"**
*   During the SCO Bishkek Summit, General Secretary Xi Jinping summarized the organization's 25-year history using three key descriptors:
    1.  **"The most significant development achievement"** – reflecting the organization's growth.
    2.  **"The most valuable spiritual wealth"** – referring to the "Shanghai Spirit."
    3.  **"The most important cooperation experience"** – outlining the practical path of development.
*   **"Shanghai Spirit":** Defined as mutual trust, mutual benefit, equality, consultation, respect for diverse civilizations, and common development. This is cited as the fundamental adherence for the organization's growth.
*   **Coexistence Principle:** The SCO follows a path of non-alignment, non-confrontation, and non-targeting of third parties, distinguishing it from traditional military alliances.

**Development Directions**
*   Xi Jinping proposed four guidelines for high-quality development:
    1.  Adhere to development priority.
    2.  Adhere to security as a key component.
    3.  Adhere to people-centered approaches.
    4.  Adhere to dialogue and consultation.
*   **Security-Development Nexus:** The article asserts that stable security is a prerequisite for economic development, while industrial and infrastructure development provides the foundation for long-term security.

**Expansion of Cooperation Areas**
*   **Traditional Sectors:** Economic and trade investment, infrastructure connectivity.
*   **Emerging Sectors:** Artificial intelligence (AI), digital economy, clean energy, industrial digitization, modern agriculture, cross-border e-commerce, and modern logistics.

**Kazakhstan-China Specific Cooperation**
*   Kazakhstan is identified as a founding member of the SCO and a country located in the heart of the Eurasian continent.
*   Key projects include the promotion of the Trans-Caspian International Transport Route and port infrastructure construction.
*   Benefits cited include increased freight convenience, growth in warehousing, logistics, manufacturing, and e-commerce, and the creation of new jobs and investment opportunities.

**Institutional and Project Support Announced at Bishkek Summit**
*   **New Centers:** China announced the establishment of the **China-SCO Port Economy Cooperation Center** and the **Basic Education Cooperation Center**.
*   **Science and Technology Initiative:** China committed to jointly implementing **100 science and technology cooperation projects** with SCO member states over the next three years.

**Global Governance Role**
*   At the "SCO+" meeting, Xi Jinping elaborated on the core connotations of an equal and orderly multi polar world from four dimensions: fundamental principles, necessary requirements, key pathways, and development directions.
*   The SCO is urged to use "SCO strength" to drive prosperity in Eurasia, "SCO wisdom" to promote the development of the Global South, and "SCO action" to lead global governance reform.

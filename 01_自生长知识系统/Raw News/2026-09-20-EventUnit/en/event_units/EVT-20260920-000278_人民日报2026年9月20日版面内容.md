## Core Facts

**1. Greater Bay Area (GBA) Integration and "15th Five-Year Plan"**
*   **Context:** 2026 is the opening year of China's national "15th Five-Year Plan." Hong Kong has released its first-ever five-year plan, and Macao has released its "15th Five-Year" plan.
*   **Event:** A joint interview activity titled "Connecting with the '15th Five-Year Plan' and Leveraging New Advantages," hosted by the Central Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office and the All-China Journalists Association, took place in Guangdong (Stage 2).
*   **Key Projects and Entities:**
    *   **Jiaodu Technology (Guangzhou):** Upgraded traffic systems using large model technology; assisted in the Deep Hong Kong One Card device renovation (covering 327 cities). Established an overseas R&D center in Hong Kong Science Park in April 2026 to serve as a "conversion hub" for Chinese solutions.
    *   **Macao International Airport Hengqin Pre-position Cargo Station:** Completed its first flight ("first order, first flight") for air board loading business. The station's logistics capacity is designed to reach 300,000 tons annually, supporting Macao's current ~100,000-ton capacity.
    *   **Guangzhou Pharmaceutical Holdings:** Planning a 1 billion RMB investment in Hengqin for an internationalized traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) manufacturing center, utilizing a model of "Macao registration + Hengqin production + overseas sales."
    *   **Youth Entrepreneurship:** Guangzhou Tianhe District has incubated over 600 Macao/Hong Kong startups, with 16 valued over 100 million RMB, supported by an investment alliance investing nearly 120 million RMB.
    *   **Higher Education Collaboration:**
        *   Hong Kong City University (Dongguan) graduated its first master's cohort in 2026.
        *   On September 8, 2026, Macao University, Macao Polytechnic University, and University of Macau Tourism held a joint flag-raising and opening ceremony at the Hengqin Macao-Hengqin International Education (University) City Phase I campus, with over 1,450 graduate students enrolling in AI, microelectronics, and other fields. Cross-school credit recognition is being implemented.
    *   **Policy Support:** A special meeting on promoting the Hengqin Guangdong-Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone was held on September 10, 2026, to accelerate the implementation of policy measures.

**2. 2026 World Manufacturing Congress (Hefei, Anhui)**
*   **Dates:** September 20–23, 2026.
*   **Theme:** "Smart Manufacturing the World, Creating a Better Future."
*   **International Participation:** UK designated as the guest of honor. Over 40 enterprises from the US, France, Russia, and other countries participating. Over 1,000 global guests, including more than 500 foreign representatives.
*   **Exhibition Scale:** Total area of 70,000 square meters (20,000 sqm for manufacturing achievements, 50,000 sqm for market exhibition).
*   **Key Technologies Showcased:**
    *   180-qubit superconducting quantum processor.
    *   BEST compact fusion energy experimental device.
    *   "Lingjing Zao Wu" platform for scientific intelligent matter creation.
    *   Solid-state transformers and planetary environment ground simulation facilities.
    *   16 types of robots in a near-2,000 sqm embodied intelligence exhibition area.
*   **Anhui Manufacturing Achievements (Data as of 2025/early 2026):**
    *   **New Energy Vehicles:** Anhui ranked first nationally in H1 2026 for vehicle output, new energy vehicle output, and automotive exports.
    *   **Integrated Circuits:** Anhui's DRAM dynamic random access memory capacity ranked first nationally in 2025, led by ChangXin Memory Technologies.
    *   **Industrial Robots:** Anhui ranked fifth nationally in robot production in 2025.
    *   **New Displays:** Accounted for nearly 1/10 of global display panel production during the 14th Five-Year Plan period.
    *   **Advanced Optoelectronics/Energy Storage:** Produced 1/3 of the nation's photovoltaic inverters during the 14th Five-Year Plan period, led by Sungrow.
    *   **Quantum Information & Fusion:** Home to the CAS Key Laboratory of Quantum Information; 13 major scientific devices under construction/planning with fusion energy as a core direction.

# Event Name
Nanjing High-Standard Development of a First-Class Business Environment (September 2026)

## Event Overview
On September 20, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a report detailing how the city of Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, is implementing high-standard measures to create a first-class business environment. Framed within the context of the "15th Five-Year Plan" (十五五), the report outlines Nanjing's strategic alignment with central government directives to optimize policy, market, administrative, legal, and cultural environments for enterprises. The article highlights specific reforms in administrative efficiency, government-enterprise service models, and regulatory oversight, alongside statistical outcomes regarding the growth of the private economy.

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Report:** September 20, 2026.
*   **Location:** Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, specifically the Jiangning Development Zone and Lishui Economic Development Zone.
*   **Policy Framework:** Nanjing has incorporated the goal of "building a first-class business environment to a higher standard" into its "15th Five-Year Plan" outline.
*   **Administrative Reform ("Efficiently Handling One Thing"):**
    *   Since the launch of the "Efficiently Handling One Thing" (高效办成一件事) reform, the average processing time for key items has been reduced by 66%, and material requirements have been reduced by 70%.
    *   The reform has benefited 9.06 million enterprise and citizen transactions to date.
    *   "Five certificates issued simultaneously" (五证齐发) has become a standard practice; for example, the "Central Reserve Guoneng 100MW Advanced Compressed Air Energy Storage System Equipment Manufacturing Base" project completed all five certificates in just 5 working days after signing the land transfer contract.
    *   New scenarios include cross-border e-commerce filing and enterprise "going global" services.
*   **Service Model Shift ("Opportunity Supply" to "Capability Supply"):**
    *   Nanjing released its first batch of government AI demand scenarios for enterprise application.
    *   A closed-loop mechanism of "department issues questions—enterprise verifies—government pays" was established.
    *   Enterprises receive free computing power, models, and data support.
    *   The *Action Plan for Promoting Large-Scale Application of New Scenarios and High-Level Construction of a City Open to Scenarios (2026–2028)* was issued in January 2026, targeting AI, smart grids, and embodied intelligence, with plans for 10 city-level and 100 industry-level comprehensive scenarios.
*   **Enterprise Matching ("Government Serves Dishes" to "Enterprise Orders Dishes"):**
    *   A specific case was reported where "Service Commissioner" Shi Jin facilitated cooperation between Nanjing Chuangfeng Precision Sheet Metal Manufacturing Co., Ltd. and Nanjing Yika Smart Vehicle Technology Co., Ltd.
    *   The process involved: direct contact within one week, on-site inspection by the buyer within half a month, and the first order landed within three months.
    *   Nanjing has assigned "service commissioners" to over 20,000 key enterprises.
*   **Regulatory Optimization ("Subtraction" and "Addition"):**
    *   On June 15, 2026, the *Nanjing Municipal Measures for Regulating Enterprise-Related Administrative Inspections* took effect.
    *   The measures define "five prohibitions" and "eight don'ts" as rigid red lines to prevent arbitrary or baseless inspections.
    *   An integrated law enforcement supervision platform manages enterprise appeals in a closed loop.
    *   The "Ninggong Product Promotion" activity helped 246 enterprises establish stable contact channels with target customers.
*   **Economic Outcome:** The share of private economy added value in Nanjing's total economic volume rose to 47.6%.

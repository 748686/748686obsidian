## Event Overview

On September 20, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Overseas Edition), in its "Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao and Overseas Chinese" section, published a commentary titled "Cultural Identity Flows Naturally" (文化认同自然流露). The article highlights a recent surge in two-way cultural exchange between mainland China and Taiwan through film and television works. It characterizes this trend as moving beyond simple content broadcasting to deeper talent interchange and co-creation, ultimately framing it as a natural expression of shared cultural identity and emotional connection that cannot be severed.

## Core Facts

*   **Recent Content Trends:**
    *   The mainland urban workplace drama *Early Spring Clear* (《早春晴朗》) recently sparked a viewing craze in Taiwan, with audiences actively requesting updates online.
    *   The Taiwanese family drama *Fairy Tales Part Two* (《童话故事下集》) has attracted attention after being broadcast on mainland television stations.
*   **Historical Context:**
    *   Past popular works include Qiongyao dramas and *Justice Pao* (《包青天》) in mainland China.
    *   Recent hits in Taiwan include *Empress in the Palace* (《甄嬛传》) and *Blossoms Shanghai* (《繁花》).
*   **Co-Production and Talent Exchange:**
    *   Taiwanese filmmakers are deeply involved in mainland剧集 production.
    *   Specific examples of co-productions/taiwan-directed mainland productions popular in Taiwan include *Hard to Entangle* (《难哄》) and *Wild Dog Bone* (《野狗骨头》).
    *   Taiwanese director Huang Tian-ren (黄天仁) described the relationship not as one side bringing something to the other, but as "mutual integration."
*   **Policy Support:**
    *   In April 2026, the Central Taiwan Affairs Office issued ten policy measures to promote cross-strait exchanges and cooperation.
    *   These measures explicitly state that Taiwanese practitioners may participate in mainland micro-drama (微短剧) creation in various ways.
*   **Case Study - Micro-Drama:**
    *   The micro-drama *Jiaqing Emperor's Tour of Taiwan* (《嘉庆君游台湾》) was cited as a recent practical example.
    *   Features: Taiwanese actors participated, Minnan dialect dubbing was used, Minnan culture was integrated, and puppet theatre (布袋木偶戏) served as a key plot element.
    *   Significance: While "micro" in format, it symbolically presents the shared cultural roots of both sides.
*   **Nature of Exchange:**
    *   The exchange is described as a "two-way rush" (双向奔赴) involving both audience emotional resonance (screen) and collaborative production refinement (behind the scenes).

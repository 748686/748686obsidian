# Event Name

Perth Shark Attack Fatality

## Event Overview

On September 19, 2026, a 42-year-old man died after being attacked by a shark at the Coogee swimming enclosure in Perth, Australia. In response to the incident, the Western Australia Parks and Wildlife Service announced an emergency cull of sharks, authorizing the kill of up to 100 sharks in an effort to prevent further attacks on beachgoers.

## Core Facts

- **Incident Date:** September 19, 2026
- **Location:** Coogee swimming enclosure, Perth, Australia.
- **Victim:** A 42-year-old man.
- **Outcome:** The victim died from injuries sustained during the shark attack.
- **Official Response:** The WA Parks and Wildlife Service declared an emergency cull, resulting in approximately 100 sharks being killed off the coast of Perth.
- **Environmental Context:** The event occurred during a period where Western Australia was already experiencing unusually high shark activity.

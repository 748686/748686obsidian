# Event Name

## Event Overview
The 5th BeiDou Large-Scale Application International Summit, themed "Together with the World, Sharing BeiDou — Empowering the Globe" (同世界·共北斗——赋能全球), was recently held in Zhuzhou, Hunan Province. The summit highlights the continued growth of the BeiDou Navigation Satellite System (BDS) in market reach, industrial support, and international influence since the inaugural summit in 2021.

## Core Facts
*   **Event Location:** Zhuzhou, Hunan Province, China.
*   **Event Timing:** "Recently" relative to September 20, 2026.
*   **System Upgrade:** In July 2026, the BeiDou system completed the on-orbit iterative upgrade of all satellites.
*   **Performance Capabilities:** Current capabilities include nationwide real-time centimeter-level positioning, global high-precision positioning with fast convergence, and support for 1 billion-level terminal access with stable online presence for hundreds of millions of mass-market users and millions of industry users.
*   **Terminal Adoption:** Over 70 mobile phone terminal models globally support BeiDou short-message functionality, with a user scale exceeding 450 million.
*   **Industrial Growth:** During the 14th Five-Year Plan period (ending 2025), the BeiDou industry scale increased by over 53%.
*   **Commercial Application:** By the end of 2025, the BeiDou navigation industry had expanded into key sectors including transportation, energy, urban construction, and emergency disaster reduction, with extensions into low-altitude economy, marine economy, and sports/events.
*   **User Volume:** On the sixth day of the first lunar month in 2026, Gaode (AutoNavi) called upon BeiDou for positioning 1 trillion times daily, supporting a total navigation distance of 9 billion kilometers.
*   **Cost Reduction:** A new system for spatial short messages, led by China Spacetime Information Group, reduced chip costs by 70% and drove shipments of 50 million new-system chips.
*   **International Reach:** BeiDou products have been exported to over 140 countries and regions. BeiDou is recognized as one of the four global satellite navigation systems by the United Nations.
*   **Host City Industry:** Zhuzhou has gathered 188 enterprises forming a complete "satellite-launch-network-terminal-data" industry chain. In 2025, the Zhuzhou BeiDou Spacetime Information Industry Cluster reached a scale of 22.96 billion RMB, with an average annual growth rate of over 30% in the past three years.

## Event Conclusion
The 5th BeiDou Large-Scale Application International Summit, held in Zhuzhou, Hunan, in September 2026, underscores the maturation and global expansion of China's BeiDou Navigation Satellite System. Following a comprehensive in-orbit upgrade in July 2026, BeiDou now offers real-time centimeter-level positioning and supports hundreds of millions of users via smartphone short-message capabilities. The system has transitioned from specialized industrial use to mass-market adoption, evidenced by trillion-level daily positioning calls in consumer navigation apps. Internationally, BeiDou products have reached over 140 countries, with notable applications in Cambodian digital infrastructure and Saudi Arabian oil exploration. The summit highlights China's strategic push toward integrating BeiDou with emerging technologies like 5G/6G and AI for the upcoming "15th Five-Year Plan," aiming for全覆盖 spatiotemporal services from indoors to deep space. The host city, Zhuzhou, serves as a model for industrial clustering, demonstrating significant economic growth in the北斗 sector.

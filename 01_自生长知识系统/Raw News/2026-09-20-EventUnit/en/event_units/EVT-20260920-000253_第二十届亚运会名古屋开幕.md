## Event Overview

On the evening of September 19, 2026 (local time), the 20th Asian Games opened in Nagoya, Aichi Prefecture, Japan. The event features approximately 11,000 athletes from 45 Asian countries and regions. This marks the 14th time the Chinese Sports Delegation has participated in the Asian Games. The Games are scheduled to run from September 19 to October 4, 2026.

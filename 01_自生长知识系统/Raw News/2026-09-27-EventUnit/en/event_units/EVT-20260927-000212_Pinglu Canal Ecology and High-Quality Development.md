## Core Facts

### From Article #307: Yunnan Green Development and Red Culture
1.  **Historical Context:** In May 1935, during the Long March, tens of thousands of Red Army soldiers crossed the Jinsha River at Jiaoping Ferry with the help of 37 boatmen, escaping enemy blockade.  
2.  **Policy Directive:** In January 2020, General Secretary Xi Jinping visited Yunnan and emphasized using Yunnan’s revolutionary traditions as educational material for “不忘初心、牢记使命” (Stay True to the Original Aspiration and Keep the Mission Firmly in Mind) education.  
3.  **Red Heritage Protection:**  
    -   **Xundian County (Kunming):** The Red Army Long March Kedu Memorial Hall uses digital tools (e.g., 3D models) to exhibit artifacts like an 1935 music box incident demonstrating strict military discipline.  
    -   **Weixin County (Zhaotong):** Invested approximately **32 million RMB** to renovate **29 major revolutionary sites** and built a Red Army Long March Slogan Museum.  
    -   **Xiangyun County (Dali):** Named and protected **44 revolutionary sites**, establishing “one site, one card” archives.  
    -   **Qujing Economic and Technological Development Zone:** Used physical objects, historical images, and dynamic sandboxes around the San Yuan Gong Conference Site.  
    -   **Provincial Goal:** Accelerating the construction of the Long March National Cultural Park.  
4.  **Red + Tourism Integration:**  
    -   **Luding County (Wudongde Town):** A restaurant operated by Pei Xinhuai (granddaughter of boatman Zhang Chaoman) serves “boatman cuisine” (peanut and bean curd residue soup) and uses a wooden boat for storytelling.  
    -   **Weixin County:** Created **11 study tour bases** and **5 premium routes** around the Zhaxi Meeting sites, attracting **45,000+ visits**.  
    -   **Dayao County (Chuxiong):** Qijie Community attracted over **20,000 tourists** in 2025.  
    -   **Regional Statistics (2025):** The 7 prefectures/cities along the Long March route received **811 million tourist visits**, generating **837.44 billion RMB** in tourism revenue.  
5.  **Green Energy and Industry (Jinsha River Basin):**  
    -   **Hydroelectricity:** World-class hydropower stations including Wudongde, Baihetan, and Xiangjiaba are now located on the former Jinsha River natural barrier.  
    -   **Baihetan Hydropower Station:** The world’s second-largest installed capacity hydro project. Average annual generation: **62.443 billion kWh**. Maximum daily generation can meet the needs of **148 million people** for one day.  
    -   **Agriculture:** Qiaojia County (Zhaotong) promoted standardized planting of green peppercorns, benefiting **46,000+ households** across **17 towns/streets**.  
    -   **New Energy Battery Industry:** Yunnan Shuifu Economic and Technological Development Zone formed a cluster covering positive/negative electrode materials and cell manufacturing. 2025 industrial output value: **3.16 billion RMB**.  
    -   **Clean Energy Stats:** Clean energy accounts for **over 90%** of Yunnan’s total installed power capacity. Jan–Aug 2026 green power trading volume: **1.177 billion kWh**, a **124% year-on-year increase**.  

### From Article #308: Tiananmen Square Floral Display

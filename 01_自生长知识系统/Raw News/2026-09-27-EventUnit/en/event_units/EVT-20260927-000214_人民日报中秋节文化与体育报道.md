## Core Facts

### 1. Mid-Autumn Festival Customs and Community Activities
- **Location**: Suichang County, Lishui City, Zhejiang Province; Yongdeng County, Gansu Province
- **Zhejiang Mooncake Activity**: On the eve of Mid-Autumn Festival, staff from Miaogao Subdistrict in Suichang County collaborated with local food entrepreneur Huang Mengjun (负责人 of Lishui Suixiangji Food Co.) to hand-make mooncakes in a subdistrict kitchen. The activity was framed as an alternative to purchasing mooncakes, emphasizing anti-waste principles and clean government-citizen relations.独居老人巫善云 (age 71) received homemade mooncakes and commented: "这样的月饼，准香！" (These mooncakes will surely be delicious!) [Article #316, #317]
- **Gansu Traditional Mooncakes**: In Yongdeng County, Gansu Province, the traditional "thousand-layer mooncake" (千层月饼) is steamed rather than baked. These mooncakes can be up to 50 centimeters in diameter. Ingredients include bitter water rose jam (苦水玫瑰), sesame oil, turmeric, red yeast, and bitter beans. The craft is linked to Silk Road culinary traditions. Over 30 workshops operate in Hongcheng Town. [Article #322, #323]

### 2. Ecological Conservation at Pinglu Canal
- **Fact**: The Pinglu Canal (平陆运河) officially commenced navigation. It is 134.2 kilometers long and is described as the first river-to-sea canal built since the founding of the PRC. [Article #319, #320]
- **Fish Passage**: A 480.2-meter vertical-slot fishway at the Qingnian Hub began operation in April 2025. It has recorded 119,000 descending fish and 78,000 ascending fish, with over 10 species resuming migration. [Article #318]
- **Wildlife Bridge**: A 240-meter exclusive wildlife crossing bridge spans the canal at the Fenshuiling section. The bridge is planted with native soil (0.6–1.2 meters deep), fruit-bearing trees (longan, small-leaf banyan, false maple), and flowering plants to create a food chain for animals including spotted lentil fish (斑林狸), leopard cats (豹猫), and red-bellied squirrels (赤腹松鼠). [Article #318]
- **Mangrove Protection**: At the canal's estuary in Qinzhou, approximately 50 mu of mangrove forest was preserved through route adjustments. An additional 275,000 mangrove plants were cultivated across 32.2968 hectares using self-developed techniques. Bird species in the area increased from 28 to 34. [Article #318]
- **Ancient Tree Relocation**: 36 ancient trees (including camphor, lychee, and longan) were transplanted. One 200+year-old camphor tree weighing over 50 tons was moved to a new location in Liangwu Village, Shaping Town. [Article #318]
- **Wastewater Management**: A "store on board, deliver ashore" system was implemented. All discharge pipes above the freeboard deck are lead-sealed. Two water service areas (Xinfu and Qinzhou) handle wastewater collection. [Article #318]
- **Soil Remediation**: Approximately 315 million cubic meters of excavation soil have been repurposed. A demonstration base near the Madao Hub grows vegetables and melons using remediated canal soil. As of July 2026, 26,420 mu of land has been reclaimed for planting. [Article #318]

### 3. Moon Mountain Regional Features
- **Hubei Huangshi Moon Mountain**: A 400+ meter elevation site near Cihun Lake and the Yangtze River. Previously degraded by limestone mining since the 1970s–80s, it was restored starting in the early 2000s. Huang Xiulian and her husband returned in 2008 to operate Meilin Villa, a agritainment establishment. The site features Feiyun Cave with historical connections to Tang dynasty poet Yuan Jie. [Article #321, #322]
- **Guizhou Rongjiang Moon Mountain**: Part of the Miaoling mountain range, with a main peak at 1,490 meters. Home to the Baibei Miao village and the unique "hundred-bird robe" (百鸟衣) cultural tradition. The name derives from the proximity of the moon appearing to touch the summit. Mid-Autumn customs include "stealing melons and vegetables" (摸秋) and making glutinous rice cakes (糍粑). [Article #321, #322]
- **Ningxia Xiji Moon Mountain**: Located at the border of Xiji and Haiyuan counties, part of the Liupan Mountains foothills, at an elevation of 2,633 meters. Previously degraded by overgrazing and unauthorized reclamation in the 1980s, ecological restoration began in 1998. By 2023, 237,600 mu of water conservation forest had been established with 18.2478 million seedlings. Current grassland coverage reaches 91%. [Article #321, #322]

### 4. Sichuan University 130th Anniversary
- **Milestone**: Sichuan University celebrated its 130th founding anniversary in September 2026, tracing its origins to the 1896 Sichuan Central-Western Academy. [Article #317]
- **Red Heritage**: The university is associated with revolutionary figure Jiang Zhuyun (Jiang Jie). A memorial hall opened in 2019. "Jiang Jie Classes" have been established across multiple colleges. [Article #317]
- **Research Achievements**:
  - Academician Xie Heping's team published seawater direct hydrogen production research in *Nature* (November 2022), now being validated for commercial application by Orient Electric.
  - The National Key Laboratory of Biomedical Therapy has obtained clinical approval for 19 new drugs since 2023.
  - In 2025, the university's "Advanced Material Energy Conversion Regeneration System" orbital experiment device launched aboard Tianzhou-9.
  - In 2026, the university received 200 milligrams of Chang'e-5 and Chang'e-6 lunar soil samples. [Article #317]
- **Talent Distribution**: Over 60% of undergraduate and 70% of graduate students are from western regions; over 60% of graduates work in western China. Since 1999, 405 teaching volunteers have been sent to the Daliang Mountains, helping 52 students enter "Double First-Class" universities in the past decade. [Article #317]

- **Men's Football**: China's men's football team advanced to the semi-finals (four strongest) of the Asian Games. [Article #316, #317]

### 6. Art and Cultural Commentary (Page 08)
- **Painting and Moon Imagery**: Professor Gu Ping (南京艺术学院) analyzed the evolution of moon depictions in Chinese painting from Han dynasty stone carvings through Song dynasty literati works (Ma Yuan's *Toast to the Moon*), Ming dynasty family reunion themes (Shen Zhou), modern patriotic expressions (Feng Zikai, Zhao Zongzao's coastal defense prints), and contemporary space exploration themes (Wang Fa's *Lunar Landing*, Lin Peisen's *Lunar Backside Milestone*). [Article #326, #327]
- **Calligraphy**: Professor Yu Xiaoyong (北京师范大学) discussed the *Zhongqiu Tie* (中秋帖), attributed to Wang Xianzhi (Eastern Jin dynasty). The piece was identified by Qi Gong as a copy by Mi Fu. It was recovered by the Zhou Enlai government from overseas in difficult financial circumstances and is now in the Palace Museum. [Article #327, #328]
- **Mooncake Patterns**: Researcher Wang Laihua (天津社会科学院) documented mooncake motif aesthetics, including Tang dynasty lotus patterns from Turpan archaeological finds, palace-themed designs, huìwén (return pattern) borders symbolizing "endless prosperity," and modern abstract variations. [Article #328, #329]
- **Rabbit deity (兔儿爷)**: Associate Researcher Wang Lianhai (清华大学美术学院) traced the Beijing folk art tradition to Han dynasty moon rabbit mythology and Ming dynasty official moon worship rituals. Modern variants include cartoon and doll styles using new materials. [Article #329, #330]
- **Osmanthus in Painting**: Professor Ding Chaohong (浙江科技大学) analyzed Chen Shizeng's *Osmanthus with Wine* (1921), painted for friend Yao Hua's restaurant opening, with inscription referencing Liu Guo's poem about autumn reunion. [Article #330, #331]

### 7. Guizhou Cultural-Tourism Development
- **Event**: The 19th China Original Ecology International Photography Exhibition and the first "Colorful Guizhou Earth Art Season" opened on September 28, 2026, in Hongjiang Village, Libo County. [Article #324, #325]
- **Hongjiang Village Transformation**: Since 2017, the village has attracted over 100 artists, revitalized 48 old houses, and created 23 art studios. Village collective income rose from under 20,000 RMB to over 500,000 RMB annually.近20,000 study tour and photography visitors arrive annually. [Article #324, #325]
- **Provincial Data**: As of end-2025, Guizhou has approximately 13,000 homestay operators, 638 graded tourism homestays. 82% of rural homestays revitalize idle assets, employing approximately 100,000 people and benefiting nearly 160,000 farming households. 2.38 million people stayed in Guizhou for 15+ days in 2025. [Article #324, #325]
- **"Village" IP Phenomena**: "Village BA" basketball (Tapancun), "Village Super League" football (Rongjiang), "Village Marathon" (Sandu Water Nationality), "Village T" fashion (Kaili), "Village Dance" (Xingyi), and "Earth Fingerprint" concert ( Dafang County) are cited as examples of rural cultural IP generating tourism revenue. "Village Super League" generated nearly 30 billion RMB in tourism revenue for Rongjiang County by end-2025. [Article #324, #325]

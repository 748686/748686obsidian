## Event Overview
On October 30–31, 2026, the Chinese leadership conducted a series of high-level diplomatic meetings and held the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee. The plenary session focused on the topic of "comprehensively and rigorously governing the Party in the new era." Key diplomatic engagements included talks with German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier and Swiss Federal Council President Élisabeth Baume-Schneider (referred to as Pam Lan in the source text). Concurrently, senior Chinese officials presided over meetings of the State Council Party Leadership Group and the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection.

## Core Facts
*   **CPC 20th Central Committee Fifth Plenary Session:** Held from October 26 to 29, 2026, in Beijing. The session's focus was on comprehensively and rigorously governing the Party. It reviewed and approved the "Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Some Major Issues Concerning Comprehensively and Rigorously Governing the Party in an Unremitting Manner." It was also announced that the 21st National Congress of the CPC will be held in the second half of 2027. 
*   **Xi Jinping's Diplomatic Activities (October 30):**
    *   **Germany:** Held talks with German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing. Topics included bilateral relations (noting the upcoming 55th anniversary of diplomatic ties), global governance, and cooperation in AI, green low-carbon, and digital economies.
    *   **Switzerland:** Met with Swiss Federal Council President Élisabeth Baume-Schneider (referred to as Pam Lan) in Beijing. They witnessed the signing of the protocol on upgrading the China-Switzerland Free Trade Agreement and several other cooperation documents.
*   **Other Leadership Meetings:**
    *   **Li Qiang** (Premier) presided over a meeting of the Party Leadership Group of the State Council on October 30 to study the spirit of the Fifth Plenary Session.
    *   **Zhao Leji** (Chairman of the NPC Standing Committee) met with German President Steinmeier on October 30.
    *   **Li Xi** presided over a meeting of the Standing Committee of the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection.
    *   A symposium with non-Party personages was held by the CPC Central Committee.
*   **Personnel Changes:**
    *   **Hunan:** Xie Weijiang was appointed Acting Governor of Hunan Province.
    *   **Qinghai:** Zhuge Yujie was appointed Acting Governor of Qinghai Province.
    *   **State Council Appointments/Removals:** Wang Shouli was appointed Deputy Director of the National Ethnic Affairs Commission; Zhang Li was appointed China's Deputy Chief Trade Negotiator (ministerial level); Feng Pengzhi was appointed Dean of the National Academy of Governance. Li Zhong, Qin Haixiang, Zhang Li, and Chen Ronghui were removed from their respective ministerial/vice-ministerial posts.
*   **Upcoming Diplomacy:**
    *   Husayn, Sultan of Oman, will visit China for a state visit on November 1–2, 2026.
    *   The Austrian Chancellor is scheduled to visit China.
    *   Gao Zhidan will attend the Dakar 4th Youth Olympic Games Opening Ceremony as Xi Jinping's special envoy.

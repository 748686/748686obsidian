## Core Facts

### Technological Principles
*   **Mechanism:** Friction nanogeneration is derived from the combination of "frictional electrification" (triboelectric effect) and "electrostatic induction." When two different materials contact and separate, surface charges form; repeated motion creates voltage changes that drive electron flow.
*   **Theoretical Foundation:** Unlike traditional generators based on conduction current theory, TENGs are grounded in the **displacement current** within Maxwell’s equations.
*   **New Discipline:** The research has led to the creation of a new interdisciplinary field called **"Dynamic Interface Electronics,"** which studies electrical processes generated and controlled at dynamic interfaces during contact, rather than solely within material interiors.

### Operational Characteristics
*   **Weak Energy Capture:** Unlike traditional methods that concentrate large-scale energy (coal, dams, wind farms), TENGs perform "precision capture of weak energy," akin to micro-water wheels in small streams.
*   **High Sensitivity:** The technology does not require magnets or coils. It can generate electricity from a gentle breath or slight hand movement, unlike traditional wind turbines that typically require Force Grade 3+ winds.
*   **Versatility:** It functions as a "distributed micro-power station," harvesting energy from human motion, ocean waves, and mechanical vibrations without being site-specific.

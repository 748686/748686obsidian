## Event Overview
On October 31, 2026, the People's Daily (Science & Technology section) reported on two distinct scientific and technological developments featured in the 26th China International Industry Expo. The primary headline event is the first successful generation of light ("first light") by China's hard X-ray free-electron laser facility. Concurrently, the report details the debut of "Danbao," a robot developed by Fudan University that utilizes a pure vision-based edge brain for autonomous navigation without pre-built maps or remote control.

## Core Facts

### Hard X-ray Free-Electron Laser
*   **Event:** The hard X-ray free-electron laser device achieved its first light emission.
*   **Significance:** This marks a milestone in fundamental research infrastructure, often linked to broader discussions on the value of frontier research in daily life.

### "Danbao" Robot Deployment
*   **Developer:** Jointly developed by the Future Information Innovation College of Fudan University and the Jimu Tianshu (极目天舒) team.
*   **Leader:** Research team leader Zhang Tianzi.
*   **Location:** Unveiled at the 26th China International Industry Expo in Shanghai.
*   **Technology:**
    *   Equipped with a "pure vision edge-side brain."
    *   Utilizes a "visual-spatial intelligent navigation" physical skill.
    *   Unifies various robot movements under a single "navigation" model: locomotion is defined as navigating the body to a target, while manipulation (grasping) is defined as navigating the fingertip to an object.
*   **Performance Metrics:**
    *   Model parameter size: Approximately 120 million (significantly smaller than the billions-of-parameters typical of current embodied intelligence navigation large models).
    *   Power consumption: Under 20 watts.
    *   Input capacity: Supports real-time edge-side operation with up to 8 visual inputs.
*   **Capabilities:**
    *   Autonomous pathfinding in complex, dynamic environments (e.g., crowded expos).
    *   Active avoidance of pedestrians.
    *   No reliance on pre-set maps, joysticks, or pre-programming.
    *   Overcomes data inconsistency and high costs associated with using separate systems for grasping and walking.
*   **Current Status:** The technology is being adapted for various robot types including delivery, inspection, agriculture, and cleaning.
*   **Future Trajectory:** Development aims to evolve from autonomous walking to autonomous task completion and ultimately intent understanding.

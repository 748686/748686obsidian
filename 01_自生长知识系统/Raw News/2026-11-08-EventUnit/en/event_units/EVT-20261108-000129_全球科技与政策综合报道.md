# Event Name

Implementation of the "If the Matter Cannot Be Accomplished, Find Me" Government Service Mechanism in Qiuxian, China

## Event Overview

On November 8, 2026, *People's Daily* published a report detailing the operational success of a government service initiative in Qiuxian County, Handan City, Hebei Province. The report highlights how local authorities are implementing directives from the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee to address public concerns ("urgent, difficult, and worrying issues"). The central focus is a dedicated service window designed to handle complex or unresolved civic and business requests that standard administrative channels have failed to resolve.

## Core Facts

*   **Location:** Qiuxian County Government Service Center, Handan City, Hebei Province, China.
*   **Initiative Name:** "If the Matter Cannot Be Accomplished, Find Me" (Shi Ban Bu Cheng Zhao Wo) window.
*   **Administrative Context:** The initiative is framed as an on-the-ground implementation of the *Decision* from the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee, which calls for party members and cadres to strengthen their sense of purpose and servitude to the people.
*   **Oversight Body:** The Qiuxian Commission for Discipline Inspection and Supervision (Qiuxian Jiwei Jianwei) is responsible for coordinating and supervising the mechanism.
*   **Operational Structure:**
    *   **Tiered Handling:** General convenience matters are handled at the township/sub-district window level. Cross-sector or high-coordination-difficulty appeals are escalated to county-level coordination.
    *   **Service Timelines:**
        *   Matters that can be processed on the same day are completed immediately.
        *   Multi-department联动 (linkage/cooperation) matters are completed within 3 days.
        *   Complex issues that have persisted for a long time receive a response within 7 days.
*   **Reported Statistics:** The window has cumulatively concluded over 3,200 livelihood and enterprise-related appeals, with a completion rate of 99.8%.
*   **Case Study 1 (Small Business Support):** Yuan Bin, a shop owner in Xinmatou Town, sought help for difficulties obtaining a business license and approving store renovation. Town staff provided material checklists and hands-on guidance for online applications. Law enforcement officials conducted a pre-visit inspection, identifying issues such as unclear separation of raw and cooked food areas. Instead of requiring a full demolition and rebuild, officials advised adjusting layouts and installing facilities, allowing the shop to pass review and obtain its license quickly.
*   **Case Study 2 (Infrastructure Coordination):** Guo Xiaojia, a merchant at Jiaxin Supermarket in Wanquan Hui Mall (located in a basement with weak network signals), faced persistent payment processing issues. After local township coordination failed to compel the telecom operators to act, the merchant utilized the "If the Matter Cannot Be Accomplished, Find Me" window. The Qiuxian Commission for Discipline Inspection and Supervision triggered a county-level supervision mechanism, coordinating China Tower Corporation and telecom operators to repair lines and replace antennas, resolving the signal instability.
*   **Supervision Mechanism:** The discipline inspection commission integrates supervision across the entire lifecycle of a case: appeal collection, tiered routing, assignment and handling,回访 (return visit/evaluation), and accountability.

## Core Facts

### 1. International Events and Diplomacy
- The **7th World Media Summit** opened in Beijing on November 7, 2026, running through November 9. It was co-hosted by Xinhua News Agency and the Beijing Municipal People's Government, with the theme "Media Mission and Cooperative Actions in the Era of Intelligent Transformation." Nearly 500 representatives from nearly 330 mainstream media, think tanks, government departments, and international organizations across 112 countries and regions attended. Li Shulei, Member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and Minister of Publicity of the CPC Central Committee, delivered the keynote speech; Yin Li, Member of the Political Bureau and Secretary of the Beijing Municipal Party Committee, gave remarks.
- **China-Pakistan Space Cooperation Milestone**: The Shenzhou-24 manned spaceflight mission will include one Pakistani astronaut participating as a payload specialist, marking the first joint flight of Chinese and foreign astronauts in China's manned space program history. The Chinese Manned Space Agency announced this on November 7, 2026. The astronaut will complete a short-duration orbital flight and return aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft. The cooperation was initiated by a bilateral agreement signed in February 2025 in Islamabad, following a rigorous three-stage selection process that produced two backup and flight-ready Pakistani astronaut candidates.
- **Youth Diplomatic Engagement**: President Xi Jinping recently replied to a letter from 110 Australian youth students, encouraging them to be inheritors of China-Australia friendship and cooperation. The editorial noted the 55th anniversary of the Sino-US "Ping-Pong Diplomacy" and referenced the China-Africa Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition as examples of youth-driven international friendship.

### 2. International Trade and Exhibition (CIIE)
- The **9th China International Import Expo** opened on November 5, 2026. The event featured over **480 representative exhibits** that were global debuts, Asian premieres, or China premieres—the highest number in CIIE history. More than **300 Fortune 500 companies and industry leaders** participated for the first time, with nearly 200 enterprises and institutions having attended all nine sessions.
- **Trade Agreements**: Sinopec signed a $41.3 billion procurement deal with 37 partners from 18 countries and regions on the eve of the opening, covering green/low-carbon technologies, AI, high-end equipment, and digital supply chains. On the opening day, Jinfo Technology signed a $120 million order with South Korea's E1 Company for low-temperature propane.
- **Corporate Participation Highlights**:
  - **Honeywell** displayed its Forge Intelligent Interconnection Platform, launched in China in July 2026, and showcased ecosystem co-creation成果 with over 200 local partners.
  - **Samsung**, a "full-attendance" exhibitor for nine years, presented cutting-edge semiconductor and AI technologies including the industry's first LPDDR memory with in-memory computing technology, next-generation UFS 5.0 storage, and the Galaxy S26 Ultra smartphone with a privacy screen. The company also debuted its R20 flagship ultrasound diagnostic system, which is being localized in production in Suzhou.
  - **ABB** showcased "AI + Power" microgrid intelligent body solutions, with a pilot implementation at its Xiamen Industrial Center achieving nearly 90% green power local consumption rate and 1.34万吨 annual carbon reduction.
  - **Siemens** presented China premieres including synchronous condenser technology for new power systems.
  - **Medtronic** introduced 7 debut products including surgical robots, AI imaging, and brain-computer interface solutions.
  - **Danone** globally premiered the "Nuenstar Youpei加水奶" formula developed specifically for Chinese consumers and manufactured in Wuxi, with exports to Poland beginning in September 2026.
  - **Yili Group** displayed global innovation products including New Zealand imported milk and showcased its 15 global innovation centers across Asia, Europe, Oceania, and the Americas.
- **CIIE Cumulative Impact (First 8 Sessions)**: Over 3,400 new products, technologies, and services were showcased; annual intent transaction volume grew from $57.83 billion at the 1st session to $83.49 billion at the 8th session.

### 3. Energy Transition and Industrial Transformation
- **Ordos, Inner Mongolia Green Transition**: The city is exploring a new path for resource-based city green transition, integrating traditional and new energy. Key data:
  - Annual coal production: nearly 900 million tons; coal electricity supplied to 28 provinces.
  - Thermal power installed capacity: 38.34 million kilowatts; annual generation exceeding 180 billion kWh, accounting for over 30% of the autonomous region's thermal power.
  - Smart coal mines: 164 constructed, representing approximately 90% of productive coal mine capacity.
  - Modern coal chemical production capacity: 24.51 million tons, ranking among the nation's forefront.
  - "ShaGeHuang" (desert/gobi/barren land) new energy base: approved projects totaling nearly 110 million kilowatts; connected renewable capacity approaching 28 million kilowatts.
  - Green power trading (first 9 months): 8.62 billion kWh traded; 8.6 million green certificates issued; 9 market-based projects totaling 3.22 million kilowatts.
  - "Wind-Solar-Hydrogen-Storage-Vehicle" industrial cluster: over 100 chain enterprises introduced including LONGi, Envision, CRRC, and CATL; wind equipment 15 GW, solar cells/modules 85 GW, hydrogen equipment over 20,000 sets, energy storage 67 GWh, new energy vehicles 350,000 units.
- **Xinjiang Multi-Energy Complementary Project**: The Luneng Fukang 100-megawatt molten salt tower solar thermal power storage project connected to the grid in Fukang City, Changji Hui Autonomous Prefecture. Annual power generation from solar thermal: approximately 120 million kWh; annual standard coal savings: approximately 40,000 tons.

### 4. Technological Innovation
- **Agricultural Machinery Innovation**: Worthington Agricultural Machinery Co., Ltd. (Jiangsu) developed the "Lodging King" harvester capable of harvesting completely lodged rice, and the 4LZ-8.0EP self-propelled crawler grain combine harvester for multi-crop operation. Farmers reported that modern combines with autopilot systems cost several thousand yuan extra, and autopilot performance remains limited on small or irregularly shaped fields.
- **Advanced Manufacturing**: In the first three quarters of 2026, equipment manufacturing value-added grew 10.2% year-on-year; high-tech manufacturing grew 14.3%, outpacing overall industrial growth by 4.9 and 9.0 percentage points respectively. Equipment manufacturing contributed nearly 90% of export increments.
- **Newspace Development**: The Chinese military celebrated the 77th anniversary of the Air Force with the Bayi Aerobatic Team performing low-altitude valley flights over the Huangyaguan Great Wall in Tianjin on November 6, 2026.

### 5. Social Livelihood and Governance
- **Urban Residential Community Renovation**: In the first three quarters of 2026, China completed renovation of 16,000 urban old residential communities with 13,000 more under implementation, benefiting 5.02 million households. Upgraded aging pipelines exceeded 7,200 kilometers; 2,800 communities received elderly-friendly and barrier-free modifications; 86,000 parking spaces added; 9.54 million square meters of building energy efficiency retrofits completed; over 10,000 EV and e-bike charging stations installed.
- **Elevator Renewal**: The 2026 ultra-long special treasury bond allocated 30.9 billion yuan to support replacement of 197,000 old residential elevators. Since 2024, cumulative support has covered 364,000 elevators benefiting 14.57 million households; 192,000 elevators have been completed.
- **Complete Community Construction**: In the first three quarters, 976 communities implemented complete community construction, adding 2,336 public service facilities, 1,867 convenient commercial service facilities, and 3.983 million square meters of public activity spaces and green areas, benefiting 3.632 million households.
- **Vocational Skills Training**: Over 8 million person-times received subsidized skills training in the first three quarters of 2026. 379 industry-education evaluation skill ecology chains were cultivated involving 3,190 enterprises, driving 280,000 person-times of training.
- **Tax Administration Law Revision**: The draft revision of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection was first reviewed by the 25th Session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress. Key reforms include: mandatory confidentiality of taxpayer information; establishment of a correction declaration system allowing taxpayers to fix non-intentional errors; capping tax late fees at no more than twice the principal tax amount; eliminating the requirement to pay taxes or provide guarantees before applying for administrative reconsideration of tax disputes.

### 6. Cultural and Social Trends
- **Youth "Ancient Site Visiting" (Fanggu) Phenomenon**: A growing trend among young Chinese people visiting historical sites, tombs of historical figures, and ancient architectural villages. On Xiaohongshu, the topic "My Fanggu Tour" accumulated over 79 million views and 500,000 interactions. Young visitors engage in personalized, equal-dialogue-style remembrance (e.g., leaving ibuprofen at Cao Cao's tomb, offering spicy strips at Huo Qubing's tomb). Scholars note this reflects growing cultural confidence and a desire for spiritual anchoring in rapid-changing society. However, concerns were raised about algorithm-amplified distorted historical narratives and extreme interpretive camps.
- **"Drinking Big Water" Trend**: A youth lifestyle shift toward drinking plain water instead of sugary beverages, reflecting efforts to regain control over personal health and life rhythm. This mirrors broader trends of young people adopting practices like goji berry tea, intelligent sleep tracking, and Baduanjin exercises.
- **Newspaper Supplement Research Annual Conference**: Held in Jiujiang, Jiangxi on November 7, 2026, with 180+ supplement editors from over 100 media outlets. Theme: "Working Hard to Run Supplements Well." Emphasis on supplements' political, journalistic, and literary attributes; advocating for "supplement +" concepts and multimedia integration.

### 7. Agricultural and Rural Development
- **Rural Revitalization Cases**:
  - **Heilongjiang**: Heilan Agricultural Ecology Experimental Station pioneered the "Longjiang Model" for black soil conservation (incorporated into national program in 2021); Li Yanhua's team promoted soybean varieties across over 120 million mu in Northeast China, increasing farmer income by over 10 billion yuan.
  - **Henan**: China YTO's intelligent tractor production line has annual capacity exceeding 45,000 units; the national manufacturing innovation center for agricultural machinery has produced China's first 5G+hydrogen fuel electric tractor.
  - **Hubei**: Ezhou's Wuchang fish industry achieved over 20 billion yuan chain output in 2025, benefiting 15,000 households; Zigui County's脐橙 (nectarine) industry covers 400,000 mu with over 1 million tons annual output and 25 billion yuan chain output, supported by 220+ drone socialized service teams.
  - **Yanhe Village (Hubei)**: Transformed from poor village to 4A scenic area; 2025 tourism revenue exceeded 100 million yuan with per capita net income of 40,000 yuan, radiating to 7 surrounding villages.
  - **Xinping Village (Gansu)**: Post-relocation village achieving per capita income growth from under 2,000 yuan (2015) to over 20,000 yuan, with 2024 agricultural market sales exceeding 260 million yuan.

### 8. Legal and Judicial Governance
- **Legal Service Stations**: Nationwide, 55,000+ grassroots legal service stations have been established; 2,844 stations are embedded in county-level comprehensive social governance centers (99.8% coverage). Over 28,000 legal experts are registered in the China Legal Consulting Center database. Experts have participated in resolving 6,600+ disputes and provided legal consultation services to 240,000+ person-times.

# Event Unit: Green Computing Power Industry Development & APEC 2026 Cooperation Dynamics

## Event Overview
This synthesis documents a dual-stream event captured in the *People's Daily* on November 20, 2026. The primary focus is the strategic promotion of the **Green Computing Power (Suanli) Industry** as a key component of new quality productive forces and digital ecological civilization. The secondary stream covers the **APEC Economic Leaders' Week** held in Shenzhen, highlighting Guangdong’s role in fostering Asia-Pacific innovation collaboration and the broader vision of an Asia-Pacific Community.

While these appear as distinct articles (one theoretical analysis, one regional reportage, one commentary), they share a thematic convergence: the modernization of infrastructure through green/technological innovation and international open cooperation.

## Core Facts

### 1. Green Computing Power Strategy
*   **Strategic Directive:** President Xi Jinping, during an inspection in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, emphasized that the computing power network is one of the state-planned "Six Networks." Key requirements include efficiency, green standards, and safety, specifically noting "computing-power and electricity synergy" (Suan-Dian Xietong) and "computing-power and water synergy" (Suan-Shui Xietong).
*   **Policy Basis:** The State Council’s *15th Five-Year Plan Carbon Peak Action Plan* mandates the green and low-carbon transformation of computing facilities and their collaborative layout with renewable energy.
*   **Global Context:** The global computing competition has shifted from pure scale to **green computing comprehensive supply capacity**, driven by rising energy consumption and carbon emissions from AI iteration.
*   **Domestic Context:** Green computing is defined as essential for building a "green and wise digital ecological civilization" and cultivating new quality productive forces.

### 2. Operational Framework for Green Computing
*   **Spatial Layout:** Implementation of the "Eastern Data, Western Computing" (Dong Shu Xi Suan) project. High-load, high-energy AI training demands are to be transferred to western green power-rich areas.
    *   *Example:* Beijing’s implementation plan promotes the integrated development of computing power in Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, and Inner Mongolia.
*   **Industrial Chain Construction:**
    *   Focus on AI chips, large model architectures, and algorithm frameworks.
    *   Promotion of 100% green energy zero-carbon intelligent computing centers.
    *   Development of cloud computing, industrial internet, and intelligent terminals.
    *   Application scenarios in industry, tourism, transportation, healthcare, education, and urban governance.
*   **Computing-Electricity Synergy:**
    *   Breaking down data barriers between computing power scheduling and power grid scheduling.
    *   Establishing standards for energy efficiency evaluation and green power traceability.

### 3. APEC 2026 and Regional Innovation (Shenzhen/Guangdong)
*   **Event:** APEC Economic Leaders' Week (33rd Leaders' Informal Meeting) held in Shenzhen on November 19, 2026.
*   **Innovation Index:** The "Shenzhen-Hong Kong-Guangzhou" innovation cluster ranked **first globally** in the World Intellectual Property Organization’s *2026 Global Innovation Index* report for the second consecutive year.
*   **Key Collaborative Projects:**
    *   **Synthetic Biology:** Shenzhen Advanced Institute of Technology (part of CAS) leads a consortium of 100+ labs across 6 Asian countries to create the first 10-year technology roadmap for synthetic cells in Asia. Results published in *Nature Biotechnology* (May 26, 2026).
    *   **Agri-Tech:** Sino-Singapore Guangzhou Knowledge City hosts a smart plant factory for heat-resistant strawberries ("Yan Tian," "Yan Yu"), developed via molecular breeding in Singapore and scaled in the Greater Bay Area (GBA) for export back to tropical Asian markets.
    *   **Robotics:** Variable Robot (Shenzhen) is exploring partnerships with Malaysia Post for logistics sorting using embodied AI.
*   **APEC Vision:** President Xi proposed building the "four beams and eight pillars" of an Asia-Pacific Community, emphasizing:
    1.  Trade and economic pillars (FTAAP).
    2.  Connectivity pillars (hard, soft, and people-to-people).
    3.  Innovation pillars (digital economy and AI as engines).
    4.  Development pillars (capacity building and talent vitality).

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives

*   **China (National/State Level):** Positions green computing not just as an environmental measure but as a strategic component of national security and economic competitiveness ("winning the initiative in future digital competition"). It frames the "Eastern Data, Western Computing" project as a domestic redistribution of resources to balance regional development (West vs. East).
*   **China (Regional Level - Guangdong/Shenzhen):** Portrays itself as a hub for *open* international innovation. The narrative shifts from internal resource allocation to cross-border collaboration with Singapore, Japan, Korea, Southeast Asia, and Europe (Valetio). It highlights Shenzhen as a destination for foreign investment and a launchpad for Chinese tech going global (e.g., robotics to Malaysia).
*   **International Partners (Implied/Referenced):**
    *   **Singapore:** Partner in synthetic biology (leadership in R&D) and agriculture (heat-resistant crops for tropical markets).
    *   **Malaysia:** Partner in logistics robotics (Malaysia Post).
    *   **EU (France/Valetio):** Deep integration into GBA supply chains, evolving from manufacturing to global R&D.

## Information Differences and Conflicts

**No direct factual conflicts** were identified between the sources regarding the events described. However, there is a **thematic disjunction**:
*   **Article 395** focuses on *internal* infrastructure, energy policy, and carbon reduction within China.
*   **Articles 396/397** focus on *external* diplomatic and economic cooperation within the Asia-Pacific region.

The synthesis treats these as complementary aspects of China's 2026 strategy: **domestic green industrial upgrading** supports the capacity needed for **international technological leadership**.

## Known Current Impact

1.  **Industrial Shift:** Accelerated migration of AI training workloads to Western China (Inner Mongolia, etc.) due to green energy availability.
2.  **Standard Setting:** China is actively working to establish standards for "computing-economy synergy" and green power traceability, aiming to make these global benchmarks.
3.  **Innovation Ecosystem:** The ranking of the Shenzhen-HK-Guangzhou cluster as #1 globally reinforces the region's status as a primary node for Asian tech innovation.
4.  **Diplomatic Platform:** APEC 2026 in Shenzhen served as a platform to solidify the "Asia-Pacific Community" concept, with China positioning itself as the host driving both trade (FTAAP) and innovation (Digital/AI) agendas.

## What Cannot Currently Be Determined

1.  **Implementation Metrics:** Article 395 outlines *goals* (e.g., "100% green electricity" centers) but does not provide current statistical data on how much of China's computing power is currently green or the specific CO2 reduction tonnage achieved to date.
2.  **Economic Valuation:** While the synthetic biology facility cost is known (720M RMB), the economic return on investment for the "Eastern Data, Western Computing" project is not quantified in these sources.
3.  **APEC Outcomes:** Article 397 mentions "new initiatives" announced by President Xi but does not detail the specific financial commitments or treaty text agreed upon during the November 19 meeting.

## Event Conclusion

The November 20, 2026 issue of *People's Daily* presents a cohesive strategic narrative linking **internal industrial transformation** with **external diplomatic engagement**.

1.  **Green Computing as a National Priority:** The development of green computing power is elevated to a strategic necessity for national security and new quality productive forces. The strategy relies on spatial optimization ("Eastern Data, Western Computing") and technological synergy (grid-computing integration).
2.  **Guangdong as an Innovation Hub:** Concurrently, the APEC summit in Shenzhen highlights the Pearl River Delta's role as a global innovation node, specifically through cross-border collaborations in biotechnology, agriculture, and robotics.
3.  **Unified Theme:** Both narratives emphasize **sustainability** (green energy for computing) and **connectivity** (data grids and ASEAN cooperation networks) as the foundations for future economic growth in the post-AI-boom era.

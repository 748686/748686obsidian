## Event Overview

On November 20, 2026, *People's Daily* published a coordinated news and commentary segment under the theme "China's Consumption Moving Toward the New: Expanding Automobile Consumption." This event highlights a strategic policy shift toward comprehensively reforming the automobile consumer chain—from production and sales to modification and recycling. The initiative focuses on two parallel objectives: revitalizing stock (by improving end-of-life vehicle recycling and parts reuse) and expanding increments (by simplifying new vehicle purchase processes and developing high-value specialized vehicles).

The content is synthesized from a main report detailing specific reform pilots across different Chinese regions and an editorial note providing analytical context on the "full-chain" strategy.

## Core Facts

*   **Policy Direction:** The Chinese government is implementing reforms to strengthen the entire chain of automobile consumption, linking production, circulation, consumption, and recycling.
*   **Strategic Dual Focus:**
    1.  **Revitalizing Stock:** Improving channels for scrapping old vehicles, recycling scrap steel and aluminum, and refining the repair/reuse of high-value parts to ensure "old cars retire smoothly."
    2.  **Expanding Increments:** Simplifying new car purchase procedures and developing high-value-added specialized vehicles to create new growth points in niche markets.
*   **Regional Pilots Identified:**
    *   **Hefei, Anhui:** Implemented "one-stop" processing at vehicle delivery sites, allowing consumers to complete procedures on-site.
    *   **Haikou, Hainan:** Utilized Free Trade Port policies to assist automotive enterprises in conducting dedicated vehicle modifications.
    *   **Wuzhou, Guangxi:** Achieved refined再利用 (reuse/recycling) of scrapped automobiles through classified dismantling.
*   **Operational Shifts:**
    *   **Production-to-Sales Integration:** Inspection and registration steps are being moved forward to the factory level to streamline the purchase process.
    *   **Specialized Vehicle Development:** Focus on high附加值 (value-added) vehicles such as ambulances and fire trucks.

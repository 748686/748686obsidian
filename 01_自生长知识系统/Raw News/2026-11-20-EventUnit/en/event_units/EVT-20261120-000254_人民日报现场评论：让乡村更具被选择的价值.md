## Event Overview

On November 20, 2026, *People’s Daily* (Vol. 5 and Vol. 6) published content regarding social governance, rural revitalization, and migrant worker welfare in Guizhou Province. The primary report details the policies and outcomes of Bijie City in supporting its 1.93 million migrant workers. Concurrently, a separate editorial commentary emphasized the strategic importance of making rural areas more attractive to returnees.

This synthesis focuses on the concrete policy measures and statistical data provided in the main report (Article #377), while acknowledging the thematic editorial published in the same issue.

## Core Facts

**Source Context:**
The core facts are derived from the full-text deep-read article "Warming the Path of Outward Employment for 1.93 Million People" (Vol. 06), authored by reporters Ma Yuefeng and Su Bin.

**1. Migration Demographics and Economic Impact**
*   **Scale:** Guizhou Province is a major labor export province with a常年 (annual) outward migration scale of approximately 6 million people.
*   **Bijie Specifics:** Bijie City is the largest exporter of labor人口 in Guizhou, with 1.93 million people working outside their home region. Of this total, 1.57 million work in other provinces.
*   **Income Dependency:** Migrant labor income constitutes:
    *   70% of post-poverty-alleviation household income in Bijie.
    *   90% of income for relocated households.

**2. Policy Framework**
*   **Provincial Guidance:** In July 2026 (reporting date: Nov 20, 2026), Guizhou Province issued the *"Guiding Opinions on Establishing and Improving Support and Guarantee Mechanisms to Solve the Worries of Outward Migrant Workers."*
*   **Key Mechanism:** The document proposes "promoting the Bijie pilot experience" and exploring the establishment of a public service liaison mechanism for the families of migrant workers.
*   **Focus Areas:** The guidelines cover five aspects, including care for the elderly and young ("一老一小"), comprehensive grassroots social governance, and promotion of public service levels.

**3. Care for "The Elderly and Young" ("Yi Lao Yi Xiao")**
*   **Objective:** To reduce the need for migrant workers to frequently return home due to family care issues.
*   **Infrastructure:**
    *   112 operational elderly care institutions built.
    *   62 urban elderly day-care centers supported.
    *   89 rural mutual aid happiness homes established.
*   **Childcare Services:** Reliance on Children's Homes and Party-Mass Service Centers to provide psychological counseling and holiday custody services.
*   **Reach:** Since August 2024, these child-focused services have benefited 680,000 child-times (人次).
*   **Local Example:** Daxinqiao Subdistrict opened an elderly cafeteria and conducted regular free health check-ups for the elderly.

**4. Grassroots Social Governance and Community Organization**
*   **Organic Mobilization:** Establishment of群众自治组织 (mass self-governance organizations). Bijie has formed over 2,800 such organizations.
*   **Service Lists:** 12 categories of mutual aid service lists have been defined, including medical accompaniment, household chores, and agricultural assistance.
*   **Funding:** Bijie has secured nearly 6 billion RMB in various funds to build a four-level linkage system (City, County, Township, Village) for resolving worries.
*   **Case Study:** In Zhongping Village (Yumo Town, Jinsha County), a "Niangniang Bang" (Aunties' Help) service team was formed by留守妇女 (left-behind women). Initially 6 members, it has expanded to over 40 members, engaging in conflict mediation, agricultural互助 (mutual aid), and cultural activities.

**5. Legal Rights Protection and Employment Services**
*   **Dispute Resolution Case:** A 32-year-old worker (Li Jun) from Qianxi City was injured at a factory in Wenzhou, Zhejiang. The Qianxi City Labor Cooperation Station in Wenzhou mediated between local and Zhejiang authorities and the employer. A mediation agreement was signed and compensation paid in 3 days.
*   **Statistics (Rights Protection):**
    *   25 provincial-outside labor cooperation stations established.
    *   34,400 migrant workers assisted.
    *   563 million RMB in unpaid wages recovered.
*   **Employment Support:**
    *   Training: 78,600 person-times trained since 2025.
    *   Job Postings: 479,000 positions collected/published.
    *   Flexible Workers: 238,700 person-times served.

## Event Conclusion

On November 20, 2026, *People's Daily* highlighted Bijie City, Guizhou, as a leading model for supporting migrant workers. The city addresses the "worries" of its 1.93 million outward migrants through a combination of rural infrastructure investment (elderly/child care), grassroots self-governance (e.g., the "Niangniang Bang" team), and cross-regional legal support. The provincial government has since codified the Bijie pilot experience into the July 2026 *Guiding Opinions on Supporting Migrant Workers*. This practical report is accompanied by a theoretical editorial in the same issue calling for the enhancement of rural value, signaling a dual policy approach: supporting those who leave and making rural life viable for those who stay or return.

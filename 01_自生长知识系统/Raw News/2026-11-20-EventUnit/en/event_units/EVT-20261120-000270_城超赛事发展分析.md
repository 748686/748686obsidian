Development and Sustainability Analysis of "Chengchao" (City Super League) Grassroots Football Tournaments

## Event Overview
On November 20, 2026, *People's Daily* published a series of reports (Articles 406, 407, and 408) analyzing the development trajectory of "Chengchao" (city-level grassroots football leagues). The coverage examines how these tournaments transitioned from viral popularity to sustainable growth. The central thesis argues that while "Chengchao" has successfully expanded the space for mass sports participation and generated cultural tourism spillover effects, its long-term success depends on avoiding homogeneous "copycat" models. Instead, regions must tailor event positioning, operational mechanisms, and cultural expression to their specific local resources, economic conditions, and football foundations.

## Core Facts
*   **Phenomenon Recognition:** "Chengchao" has achieved significant public engagement, characterized by broad mass participation, high spectator attendance, and positive spillover effects on local cultural tourism consumption. It is recognized as a鲜活实践样本 (vivid practical sample) for developing mass sports (Article 406).
*   **Challenge of Homogenization:** There is a risk of "copycat" path dependency where regions blindly benchmark against successful viral examples ("爆款") without considering local disparities in economy, human tradition, and sports foundations (Article 406).
*   **Prerequisite for Sustainability:** Football cultural heritage cannot be fabricated through short-term hype; it requires long-term support in funding, operational capability, and citizen participation willingness (Article 406).
*   **Differentiated Operational Strategies:**
    *   **Strong Foundation Areas:** Should leverage existing momentum to host high-quality events (Article 406).
    *   **Cultivating Areas:** Should prioritize the popularization of mass football and youth football to gradually cultivate a healthy mass sports ecosystem (Article 406).
    *   **Economically Developed Areas:** Can explore enriching event supporting facilities and amenities (Article 406).
    *   **Cultural Tourism Rich Areas:** Should deepen linkages between events and scenic spots/commercial districts to drive tourism through sports ("以赛聚人、以赛带游") (Article 406).
    *   **Small/Medium Cities:** Should adopt lightweight event hosting models, focusing on high-participation, low-barrier formats and sustainable, low-cost operational modes (Article 406).
*   **Cultural Integration:** To avoid a "thousand games, one face" (homogenized) aesthetic, events must integrate local folk customs, historical culture, and regional characteristics into venue布置, event segments, and supporting activities to build emotional connections and distinct IP (Article 406).

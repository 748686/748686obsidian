## Unique Information by Source

**Article #421 (Primary Content Source):**
*   Contains the full narrative of Zhou Shuigen (周水根), a 59-year-old resident of the foot of Wugong Mountain (武功山), Pingxiang City, Jiangxi Province.
*   **Biographical Details:** Zhou grew up in the area; at age 17, he was crushed by a falling log and saved by two neighbors, which instilled in him the philosophy that "saving a person is saving a family."
*   **Rescue Timeline:**
    *   Began guiding/helping tourists in **1998** when Wugong Mountain tourism was in its infancy with few signs or marked paths.
    *   First recorded rescue call in Spring 1998: Rescued 5 lost tourists at 1,600 meters altitude after locating them by sound.
    *   **2015 Incident:** Rescued a male tourist who fell 100+ meters from Tieji Peak (铁蹄峰). Zhou rappelled down, created a makeshift stretcher, and participated in a 9-hour relay with fire fighters to extract the victim. Zhou suffered abrasions to his shoulders and knees.
    *   **National Day Incident:** Rescued a young man with suspected hypoglycemia on "Despair Slope" (绝望坡) at 1,700 meters altitude. Zhou carried the ~150-jin (approx. 75kg) man on his back while weighing less than 90 jin (approx. 45kg).
*   **Team Formation:** In **August 2021**, Zhou co-founded the **Wugong Mountain Wilderness Rescue Team** (武功山山野救援队) with 10 other people (scenic area guides, inn owners). It is a purely public welfare team.
*   **Current Status:** The team has grown to **17 members**. All hold rescue certificates.
*   **Impact Statistics:** Over **28 years**, the team has conducted **200+ rescues** and assisted **520+ people**.
*   **Interviewer:** Yao Xueqing (姚雪青), People's Daily reporter.

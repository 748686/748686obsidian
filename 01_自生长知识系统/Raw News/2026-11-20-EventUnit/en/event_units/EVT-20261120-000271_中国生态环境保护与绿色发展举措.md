# Event Name
China’s Ecological Conservation and Green Development Initiatives (2026-11-20)

## Event Overview
On November 20, 2026, the ecological affairs section of *People’s Daily* (Page 14) published a series of reports detailing China’s multi-faceted approach to environmental protection and sustainable development. The coverage encompasses national policy frameworks for state-owned forest farms, cross-regional ecological governance mechanisms, river restoration projects, biodiversity conservation, cultural heritage protection, and the integration of ecological resources with local economies. A supplementary report on Page 15 highlights a resource-based region’s industrial transformation strategy. These articles collectively illustrate China’s shift from extensive resource extraction toward high-quality, ecologically integrated development.

## Core Facts

### National Policy: State-Owned Forest Farms
*   **Policy Framework:** During the "15th Five-Year Plan" period, China aims to accelerate the construction of modern state-owned forest farms, focusing on four systems: ecological protection, characteristic industries, technology/equipment, and management institutions.
*   **Current Statistics:** As of the report date, there are 4,297 state-owned forest farms across 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities. They are managed by approximately 255,000 staff and cover a total operating area of 1.184 billion mu (approx. 78.9 million hectares).
*   **Forest Metrics:** The forested area is 840 million mu, with a timber volume of 3.81 billion cubic meters.
*   **2030 Targets:** The goal is to increase forest stock per unit area by approximately 5% by 2030, improve management systems, ensure effective ecological protection and restoration, and establish mechanisms for sustainable resource use.

### Regional Governance: Sichuan-Chongqing Collaboration
*   **Initiative:** Suining (Sichuan) and Tongnan (Chongqing) have established a "Suizhong Integration" Forestry Chief System Demonstration Zone covering approximately 200,000 mu.
*   **Personnel:** 193 joint forestry chiefs (jointly appointed officials) and associated technical personnel/patrol guards are deployed to address the challenges of 24 interlocking townships across administrative boundaries.
*   **Outcomes:** In the current year (2026), the two regions have conducted over 1,100 joint forest patrols, identifying and resolving 37 issues.
*   **Economic Integration:** Since 2025, three joint forestry industry demonstration bases have been established, focusing on under-forest farming and flower/seedling industries. Cross-regional nature study activities are常态化 (normalized).

### Ecological Restoration: Shandong’s Mother Rivers
*   **Action:** Since launching the "Mother River Revival" action in 2022, Shandong has progressively restored 14 major rivers.
*   **Scope:** 4 provincial-level mother rivers (Da Wen River, Si River, Da Gu River, Xiao Qing River) and 10 municipal-level rivers.
*   **Results:** Cumulative restored river length reaches 448.8 kilometers. Ecological flow at 13 important control cross-sections has stabilized to meet standards.
*   **Methodology:** A "One River, One Policy" approach using a model of "tiered interception and storage + unified dispatch + emergency guarantee + cultural empowerment." The Da Wen River has achieved continuous full-line stable flow for five consecutive years.

### Biodiversity and Heritage Conservation
*   **Yunnan (Asian Elephants):** Reports focus on efforts in Lincang to expand the Asian elephant population ("adding members and expanding the herd"), part of broader美丽中国 (Beautiful China) initiatives.
*   **Hainan (Ancient Trees):** Hainan has established dynamic archives for 17,935 ancient and famous trees, achieving 100% coverage with "one tree, one file."
    *   **Breakdown:** 152 first-class protected ancient trees, 903 second-class, 16,721 third-class, and 153 famous trees (with 6 trees classified as both ancient and famous).
    *   **Measures:** Implementation of smart management systems, standardized appraisal/care protocols, and inclusion of tree patrol duties for ecological forest rangers ("one tree, one person").

### Economic Transformation and Integration
*   **Jiangxi Xinyu:** Promotes the deep integration of commerce, agriculture, culture, tourism, sports, and health.
    *   **Catalyst:** The 2026 Xinyu Fairy Lake Marathon ("Xianma") served as a key driver.
    *   **Economic Data:**
        *   2026 "May Day" period: 1.1309 million visitors across 28 key scenic spots and 2 consumption districts.
        *   Karting endurance race (first stop of EuroSpeed 500 laps): Driven over 30% growth in surrounding consumption.
        *   Fairy Lake visitor numbers (H1 2026): Increased by 50.3% year-on-year.
    *   **Agricultural Brand:** Xinyu honey tangerines utilize 220,000 mu of orchards, generating a total output value exceeding 2.1 billion RMB and a brand value of 5 billion RMB, benefiting over 80,000 farmers (average annual income increase >10,000 RMB).
    *   **Cultural Element:** Leverages the legacy of Song Yingxing’s *Tiangong Kaiwu* ("The Exploitation of the Works of Nature"), with a dance drama having performed over 150 times globally.
*   **Inner Mongolia Zhungeer Banner:** Implemented the "518" industrial development strategy (since early 2025) to transition from a single coal dependency ("one coal dominates") to a diversified "five bases" system (Coal-based, Ash-based, Soil-based, Silicon-based, Green-based).
    *   **Coal Industry:** Modern coal chemical industry output exceeds 100 billion RMB. 108 above-scale coal mines have >60%智能化 (intelligent mining) coverage.
    *   **Green/Silicon Base:** World’s first 10,000-ton new energy hydrogen production project operates in Nasongsong Town. A 20,000-ton silicon-carbon anode material project (using CVD technology) has entered phase one production.
    *   **Innovation:** Collaboration with Tsinghua University and the Ordos Laboratory on "sand thermal batteries" (megawatt-hour level pilot) and carbon capture/utilization technologies.

## Event Overview

On November 20, 2026, *People's Daily* (*Renmin Ribao*) published a field commentary (现场评论) titled **"Let the Countryside Have More Value as a Choice"** (让乡村更具被选择的价值). The article, authored by Li Yanan, a reporter for the newspaper's Xinjiang branch, analyzes the shift in rural talent dynamics from brain drain to回流 (return flow). It argues that rural revitalization depends not on emotional appeals ("nostalgia cards") but on creating tangible economic opportunities and professional stages for young people.

The article was part of **Page 5 (Comment Section)** of the daily edition, coordinated by editors Li Bin, Zou Xiang, and Yu Xuan. This page also featured other commentaries and a separate feature titled "The Deep Logic of Selling Lamps at a Loss" (贴钱卖灯的深层逻辑), though the latter appears to be a navigation list item rather than part of the synthesized event core.

## Core Facts

- **Publication Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Publisher:** People's Daily (人民日报)
- **Section:** Page 05 (Comment/Review Section)
- **Article Title:** Let the Countryside Have More Value as a Choice (现场评论)
- **Author:** Li Yanan (Rural Reporter, Xinjiang Branch)
- **Core Argument:** Rural revitalization requires shifting from "soliciting return" to "creating value." Talent retention is sustainable only when it is a two-way, mutually beneficial relationship where talent finds specific career stages and growth paths, rather than relying on sentiment.
- **Case Study (Xinjiang):** Yaozhanzi Village, Banjie gou Town, Qitai County.
    - **Subject:** Han Le, who returned from Urumqi in 2018.
    - **Progression:** Started as Village Committee Director Assistant; now Deputy General Manager of a village-run tourism company.
    - **Metrics:** Over 70 non-local households have settled; 28 college graduates have returned for entrepreneurship/employment. Last year, per capita annual net income of farmers and herdsmen reached **33,500 RMB**.
- **Comparative Examples:**
    - **Zhejiang Anji Yucun:** Offers 8 track types (study tours, cultural creativity, etc.), 100,000 sq meters of startup space, 20,000 sq meters of factory space, and 60,000 mu of forest/farmland, attracting over 1,000 residents.
    - **Guizhou Rongjiang:** Leveraged the "Cun Chao" (Village Super League) sports IP to aggregate industrial development potential, turning return migration from a "fallback option" into a "path forward."

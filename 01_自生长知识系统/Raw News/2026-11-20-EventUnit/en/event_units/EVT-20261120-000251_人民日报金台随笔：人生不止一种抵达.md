## Event Overview

On November 20, 2026, the fifth edition of *People's Daily* published an opinion commentary titled "The Deep Logic Behind Selling Lights at a Loss" (贴钱卖灯的深层逻辑), part of the "Zongheng" series. Written by Lu Haitao and adapted from *Zhejiang Daily*, the article examines the business practices of Yiwu merchant Huang Haicui. Despite producing a loss, Huang continues to supply obsolete dry-battery emergency lamps to long-term clients in Africa and other regions, subsidizing the shipping costs herself. The article uses this case to illustrate three core characteristics of Chinese foreign trade and manufacturing: long-termism, respect for diverse consumer markets, and resilience of business entities.

## Core Facts

*   **Publication Date:** November 20, 2026.
*   **Source:** *People's Daily*, 5th Edition (Commentary), Column "Zongheng" (纵横).
*   **Author:** Lu Haitao.
*   **Original Adaptation:** Adapted from an article in *Zhejiang Daily* titled "Why Does the Yiwu Boss Lady Sell Old Lamps at a Loss?"
*   **Key Subject:** Huang Haicui, a lighting merchant at the Yiwu International Trade City, Zhejiang Province.
*   **Business Practice:** Huang continues to export an obsolete model of dry-battery emergency lamp—originally designed 10–20 years ago—which major factories have abandoned producing. She subsidizes shipping costs of over 10,000 RMB per month for these items.
*   **Market Demographic:** The products are sold to dozens of long-term customers (clients of nearly 20 years) in Africa and similar regions, where cost-effective older models remain in demand.
*   **Financial Impact on Merchant:** This product line accounts for approximately 5% of Huang's total export volume. The subsidies are supported by reasonable profit margins from other product categories.
*   **Economic Context:** In 2025, over 13,000 foreign trade enterprises in Yiwu had actual import/export records, with trade covering more than 230 countries and regions worldwide.
*   **Analytical Framework:** The article posits that this practice reflects three deeper logics:
    1.  **Long-termism:** Prioritizing accumulated trust and stable client relationships over single-order profit margins.
    2.  **Respect for Diverse Markets:** Providing suitable goods for different development levels globally, not just high-tech items.
    3.  **Resilience:** Business entities maintaining stability and not severing long-term ties during short-term fluctuations.

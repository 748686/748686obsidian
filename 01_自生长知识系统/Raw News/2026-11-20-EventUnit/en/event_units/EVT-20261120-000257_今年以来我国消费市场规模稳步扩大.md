# Event Name

Water Resources Ministry Advances Construction of Safe, Ecological, and Intelligent Dams in China (November 19, 2026)

## Event Overview

On November 19, 2026, the Ministry of Water Resources (MWR) of China held a press conference in Beijing to outline progress and future directives regarding the construction of "safe dams, ecological dams, and intelligent dams" (often referred to as the "Three Dams" initiative). The event marked the introduction of systematic guidelines and implementation plans aimed at modernizing the operation and management of reservoir dams across China. The conference highlighted significant achievements during the 14th Five-Year Plan period (2021–2025), particularly in reinforcing vulnerable reservoirs and upgrading monitoring infrastructure.

## Core Facts

- **Event Date:** November 19, 2026.
- **Event Location:** Beijing, China.
- **Key Authority:** Ministry of Water Resources (MWR).
- **Policy Release:** The MWR recently issued the *Guiding Opinions on Promoting the Construction of Safe Dams, Ecological Dams, and Intelligent Dams* along with supporting implementation plans.
- **Strategic Framework:**
    - **Safety:** Described as the lifeline of dam development; involves implementing reservoir dam safety responsibility systems.
    - **Ecology:** Described as the foundational color of dam development.
    - **Intelligence:** Described as the future of dam development; focuses on digital, networked, and intelligent management levels.
- **Infrastructure Achievements ("Since the 14th Five-Year Plan"):**
    - Approximately 19,000 disease-afflicted or risky reservoirs have undergone remediation and reinforcement.
    - 62,000+ reservoir rainwater and condition monitoring/reporting facilities have been completed.
    - 55,000+ reservoir dam safety monitoring facilities have been completed.
    - 48,000 scattered small reservoirs have achieved professional maintenance and management.
- **Pilot Programs:**
    - A modern reservoir operation and management matrix pilot has been completed in 329 reservoirs across 65 regions.
    - This pilot has driven the near-completion of operation management matrices in over 12,000 reservoirs.
    - Over 18,000 additional reservoirs have initiated the establishment of these matrices.
- **Funding:** The MWR is coordinating central government funds to support the maintenance and upkeep of medium and small-sized reservoirs.

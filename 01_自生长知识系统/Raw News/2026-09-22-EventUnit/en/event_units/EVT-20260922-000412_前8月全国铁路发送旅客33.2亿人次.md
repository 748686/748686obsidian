# Event Name

EVT-20260922-000412: National Railway Transport Performance in China (January–August 2026)

## Event Overview

In September 2026, data released by China State Railway Group Co., Ltd. indicated that China’s national railway system achieved record-high passenger and freight volumes for the first eight months of the year. The event is characterized by a significant milestone in passenger travel, reaching 3.32 billion trips—a new historical high for this period—alongside robust growth in cross-border rail connections and international traveler arrivals. Freight operations remained steady at high levels, with critical commodity transport (coal, grain, chemicals) and international logistics (China-Europe Freight Train service) showing strong performance.

## Core Facts

The following facts are derived from the official report issued on September 20, 2026, and published in *People's Daily* on September 21, 2026.

**Passenger Transport Statistics (Jan–Aug 2026)**
*   **Total Passengers:** 3.32 billion trips.
*   **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth:** +3.9%.
*   **Status:** Record high for the same period in history.
*   **Daily Operations:** Average of 11,508 passenger trains dispatched daily, representing a 5.0% increase compared to the previous year.

**Cross-Border and International Passenger Trends**
*   **Guangzhou-Shenzhen-Hong Kong High-Speed Railway:** Transported 23.408 million cross-border passengers, up 10.7% YoY.
*   **China-Laos Railway:** Transported 245,000 cross-border passengers, up 39.1% YoY.
*   **Foreign Nationals:** Total foreign passengers sent nationwide reached 16.683 million, a substantial increase of 33.3% YoY.

**Freight Transport Statistics (Jan–Aug 2026)**
*   **Total Freight Volume:** 2.69 billion tons.
*   **Daily Loading:** Average of 187,400 freight cars loaded per day.
*   **Trend:** Slight increase YoY, maintaining high-level operations.
*   **Coal:** 1389 million tons total; including 928 million tons of power-generation coal. Direct supply plants maintained high coal stock levels.
*   **Grain:** 9.9% YoY growth.
*   **Chemicals:** 5.5% YoY growth.
*   **Textiles:** 21.7% YoY growth.

**International Logistics**
*   **China-Europe Freight Trains:** 14,842 trains dispatched, up 15.9% YoY.

**Source Attribution**
*   **Reporter:** Li Xinping (People's Daily).
*   **Issuing Authority:** China State Railway Group Co., Ltd.
*   **Publication Date:** September 21, 2026.

## Core Facts

**1. 26th National History Academic Annual Conference**
*   **Date:** September 19–20, 2026.
*   **Location:** Hefei, Anhui Province, China.
*   **Theme:** "The Theory and Practice of Establishing and Practicing a Correct View of Political Achievements" (树立和践行正确政绩观的理论与实践).
*   **Participants:** Over 80 experts and scholars from relevant fields.
*   **Organizers:** The Society of National History of the People's Republic of China, the Institute of Contemporary China Studies, the Anhui Provincial Academy of Social Sciences, and Anhui University.
*   **Key Consensus:** Scholars indicated that a correct view of political achievements possesses both value and institutional attributes, capable of transforming ideological guidance into governance effectiveness. The research emphasizes integrating this view with the study of New China's history, focusing on performance assessment, practical paths, and institutional design to advance the compilation and research of national history and build an independent knowledge system for national history research in China.

**2. 2025 National Science Popularization Statistics**
*   **Release Date:** Reported on September 20, 2026 (data covers the year 2025).
*   **Source Agency:** Ministry of Science and Technology (科技部).
*   **Reporter:** Zhao Yongxin (赵永新).
*   **Overall Trend:** Sustained growth in science popularization teams, funding, and other indicators; continuous strengthening of science popularization capacity.

**Detailed Statistics (2025 vs. 2024):**

*   **Funding:**

# Event Name
August 2026 National Total Electricity Consumption Surpasses 1 Trillion kWh

## Event Overview
In August 2026, China's total social electricity consumption exceeded 100 billion kWh for the month, reaching a new record high in terms of peak electricity load. According to data released on September 20, 2026, by People's Daily citing official sources, the country maintained steady economic activity with notable growth in high-tech manufacturing and digital services sectors, despite a relatively modest overall year-on-year growth rate.

## Core Facts

### Overall Consumption and Load
*   **Total Consumption:** August 2026 total social electricity consumption reached **103.32 billion kWh** (10,332亿千瓦时).
*   **Year-on-Year Growth:** The total consumption increased by **1.7%** compared to August 2025.
*   **Peak Load Record:** The electricity load hit a historical high of **1.56 billion kW** (15.6亿千瓦).
*   **Load Comparison:** This peak load exceeded the previous year's maximum load by **49.69 million kW**.
*   **Duration of High Load:** There were **7 days** in August where the daily peak load exceeded the previous year's maximum single-day load.

### Sectoral Breakdown
1.  **Primary Industry (Agriculture, etc.):**
    *   Consumption: **16.9 billion kWh**.
    *   Growth: **+2.7%** year-on-year.

2.  **Secondary Industry (Industry and Construction):**
    *   Total Consumption: **612.4 billion kWh**.
    *   Growth: **+2.4%** year-on-year.
    *   **Industrial Consumption:** **606.5 billion kWh** (+2.7% YoY).
    *   **High-Tech and Equipment Manufacturing:** Total consumption of **122.2 billion kWh** (+7.2% YoY), showing robust growth momentum.
        *   Computer, communications, and other electronic equipment manufacturing: **+7.5%**.
        *   Automotive manufacturing: **+7.6%** (with New Energy Vehicle (NEV)整车 manufacturing surging at **+22.2%**).
        *   General equipment manufacturing: **+7.6%**.
        *   Special equipment manufacturing: **+8.2%**.
        *   Electrical machinery manufacturing: **+12.3%**.

3.  **Tertiary Industry (Services):**
    *   Total Consumption: **215.5 billion kWh**.
    *   Growth: **+5.1%** year-on-year.
    *   **Charging/ swapping services:** Consumption of **17.2 billion kWh**, with a significant growth rate of **+51.2%**.
    *   **Internet Data Services:** Consumption of **10.2 billion kWh**, with growth of **+37.9%**.

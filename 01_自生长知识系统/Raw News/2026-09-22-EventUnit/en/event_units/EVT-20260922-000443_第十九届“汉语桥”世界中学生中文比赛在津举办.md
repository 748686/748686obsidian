## Event Overview

From September 20–21, 2026, the 19th "Chinese Bridge" World Middle School Chinese Competition was held in Tianjin. The event served as a focal point for discussing new trends in international Chinese language education, emphasizing low-age learner engagement, immersive digital learning, and the integration of city resources into language education.

Parallel reporting on the same date addressed the second anniversary of the inscription of "Beijing Central Axis" onto the UNESCO World Heritage List, highlighting systemic protection and revitalization efforts in Beijing.

## Core Facts

**The 19th "Chinese Bridge" Competition (Tianjin)**
*   **Location:** Tianjin, China.
*   **Date:** Held around September 20–21, 2026.
*   **Key Participants & Outcomes:**
    *   **You Zijun (UK):** Competed in the middle school division. Previously won the global championship at the 4th "Chinese Bridge" World Primary School Chinese Show in 2024. His brother, You Ziyuan, won the "Best Style Award" at the 5th edition in 2025.
    *   **Ma Lele (Spain):** Participant in the primary school division (5th edition, 2025).
    *   **Zhang Yuchen (Kyrgyzstan):** From Bishkek; participates in competitions supported by the Confucius Institute at Kyrgyz National University.
    *   **Bai Aili (Montenegro):** Participated via the Confucius Institute at the University of Montenegro.
*   **Educational Model:**
    *   **City as Classroom:** Learning extended beyond textbooks to locations such as Sancha River, Drama Museum, Xiaozhan Rice Planting Area, Drone Arena, and New Energy Vehicle Science Popularization Hall.
    *   **Digital Integration:** Use of AI virtual hosts and digital tools to create immersive experiences.
    *   **Content:** Emphasis on "Chinese + Education" fusion, connecting language learning with contemporary Chinese reality, history, and global governance.
*   **Institutional Statements:**
    *   **Yu Yunfeng** (Director, Center for Language Education and Cooperation, Ministry of Education): Described the event as a platform for youth to build friendship and mutual trust through language.
    *   **Zhang Tao** (Director, Tianjin Municipal Education Commission): Highlighted the shift from textbook-based learning to experiencing China in real urban contexts.
    *   **Zhong Yinghua** (President, World Chinese Language Education Association; Director, Tianjin International Chinese Education Development Center): Characterized the evolution as an "upgrade of the educational ecosystem" rather than mere element addition.
*   **Contextual Data:** Over 90 countries have incorporated Chinese into their national education systems. The competition marks the 25th anniversary of the "Chinese Bridge" series.

**Beijing Central Axis Heritage (Contextual Reporting)**
*   **Status:** "Beijing Central Axis – The Royal Architecture of China’s Ideal Capital Order" was inscribed on the World Heritage List on July 27, 2024.
*   **Current Focus (2026):** The second anniversary of the inscription is being marked by reporting on systematic protection and revitalization ("the second half of the article").
*   **Protection Measures:**
    *   **Digital Twin:** A monitoring platform using digital twin technology tracks over 200 cultural heritage sites, 30 historical blocks, and thousands of ancient trees.
    *   **Specific Projects:** Systematic reinforcement of the Bell Tower frame (first since 1747); AI and meteorological monitoring at the Temple of Heaven (specifically the Circular Mound Altar); relocation of the Dizhi Altar stone niches back to their original site at Xiannongtan.
    *   **Legal Framework:** Implementation of the "Beijing Central Axis World Cultural Heritage Protection Regulation" and the "Protection and Management Plan (2022–2035)."
*   **Revitalization:** Opening of the Zhengyangmen Arrow Tower to the public; activation of the Temple of Heaven Museum; promotion of "Night at the Bell Tower" cultural activities.

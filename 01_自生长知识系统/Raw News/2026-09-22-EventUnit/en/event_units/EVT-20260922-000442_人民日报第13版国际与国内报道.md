## Event Overview

On **September 21, 2026**, *People's Daily* published a cluster of reports across Pages 13 and 14. The editorial team for Page 13 consisted of Zou Zhipeng, Zhang Huizhong, and Chu Jun, while Page 14 was managed by Su Xianlong, Zhao Xiaoxi, and Xu Leipeng.

The coverage synthesized three distinct geopolitical and domestic policy narratives: the China-Latin America human rights dialogue focused on "Global South" governance; a critical analysis of Japan's accelerating military expansion under the Kishi administration; and a developmental report on Chongqing's Jiulongpo District. Concurrently, Page 14 featured a thematic report on the 25th anniversary of the "Chinese Bridge" (Han Yu Qiao) competition, highlighting Tianjin's role as the host city.

## Core Facts

### 1. The Third China-Latin America Human Rights Roundtable (Lima, Peru)
*   **Event:** The 3rd China-Latin America Human Rights Roundtable was held in Lima on the eve of September 21, 2026.
*   **Theme:** "Multilateralism and Human Rights."
*   **Participants:** Nearly 200 attendees from China and 23 Latin American and Caribbean countries.
*   **Key Outcomes:**
    *   Launch of the *"China-Latin America Human Rights Academic Research 2026–2030 Action Plan."*
    *   Establishment of the *"China-Latin America Human Rights Research Cooperation Network Expert Committee."*
    *   Release of the 2026–2027 joint research topics.
*   **Policy Context:** The article references the 2025 launch of the "Five Major Projects" (Unity, Development, Civilization, Peace, and People's Hearts) at the China-Latin America Forum Ministerial Conference, and the December 2025 publication of China's third policy document on Latin America and the Caribbean.

### 2. Japan’s 2027 Fiscal Defense Budget
*   **Date of Report:** September 21, 2026.
*   **Budget Figures:** The 2027 fiscal year defense budget application is approximately **890 billion yen** (noted at an exchange rate of ~23.4 yen per 1 RMB). Predictions suggest the final total may exceed **1 trillion yen**.
*   **Historical Trend:** This marks the **15th consecutive year** of increase. Compared to the 2022 fiscal year (540 billion yen), the application represents a **60% increase**.
*   **GDP Thresholds:**
    *   The 2% of GDP threshold was achieved early in the 2025 fiscal year under Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi.
    *   Some factions within the ruling party are advocating for an increase to **3.5%** of GDP.
*   **Funding Mechanisms:**
    *   Since 2023, construction bonds have been used for defense (breaking a post-war restriction).
    *   Introduction of a new "Special Defense Corporation Tax."
    *   Planned new "Special Income Tax for Defense" starting January 2027.
*   **Strategic Shift:** Movement toward "offensive strike capabilities," including remote missiles, hypersonic missiles, and AI-integrated unmanned systems, aiming to move from "attacking bases" to achieving "global first" status in unmanned warfare.

### 3. Chongqing Jiulongpo District Development
*   **Economic Milestone:** Jiulongpo has become a "200-billion-yuan" economic district.
*   **Income Data:** Per capita disposable income of all residents exceeds **57,000 RMB**.
*   **Urban Planning:** The district is pursuing three main lines: "Urban-Rural Integration," "East-West Integration," and "全域 (Whole-Area) Urbanization."
*   **Key Infrastructure:** The Taojia Tunnel, which opened on **May 31, 2026**, reduced commute time between the east and west sides of Zhongliang Mountain to under 30 minutes.
*   **Agriculture:** Urban agriculture output value exceeded **12 billion RMB** in 2025.

### 4. "Chinese Bridge" 25th Anniversary
*   **Event:** The 19th "Chinese Bridge" World Middle School Chinese Speech Competition and the 6th "Chinese Bridge" World Primary School Chinese Show暨Summer Camp were held in Tianjin from September 8–22, 2026.
*   **Participants:** 407 participants from 105 countries.
*   **Legacy:** 1.8 million+ overseas youth have participated since the brand's inception 25 years ago.
*   **Host Significance:** Tianjin has hosted the primary/secondary school events for 3 consecutive years.

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives

### Latin America & Global South (Article #577)
The perspective presented is one of **"Southern Solidarity" and Multilateralism**.
*   **Argentina:** Advocates for a multipolar world order where human rights discourse is diversified, reducing single-standard dominance.
*   **Mercosur:** Emphasizes that human rights governance must improve people's lives through public policy, rejecting the instrumentalization of human rights for political/ideological tools.
*   **Colombia:** Views multilateralism as essential for addressing global challenges (climate, poverty) that no single nation can solve alone.
*   **Peru:** Highlights pragmatic cooperation (Chancay Port) as the foundation for human rights realization, linking trade efficiency to tangible livelihood improvements.
*   **Brazil:** Argues that cooperation goes beyond trade volume to enhance productive capacity and food security, providing conditions for the "right to development."
*   **General Consensus:** Human rights cannot be limited to legal texts; development, poverty alleviation, and livelihood improvement are the prerequisites for rights.

### Japan / East Asia Security (Article #578)
The article presents a **critical domestic and regional perspective** on Japan's military trajectory.
*   **Domestic Critics:** Japanese experts criticize the expansion for severely impacting the economy.
*   **Historical Warning:** The author warns that the current "external security crisis" narrative mirrors the tactics of the 1930s, which led to devastation for Asia and Japan itself.
*   **Strategic Implication:** The move toward "offensive" capabilities and nuclear-adjacent economic integration (civilian tech to military) is framed as a breach of the "Peace Constitution" constraints.

### China (Domestic Policy) (Articles #579, #581)
*   **Jiulongpo (Article #579):** Focuses on **Urban-Rural Integration** and **Common Prosperity**. The narrative emphasizes that high-quality development must be rooted in people's livelihoods ("Chinese modernization, people's livelihood is paramount").
*   **Tianjin/Chinese Bridge (Article #581):** Focuses on **Cultural Soft Power** and **Youth Diplomacy**. The "bridge" metaphor signifies not just language learning, but the transformation of participants into cultural ambassadors who connect China with the world.

## Event Conclusion

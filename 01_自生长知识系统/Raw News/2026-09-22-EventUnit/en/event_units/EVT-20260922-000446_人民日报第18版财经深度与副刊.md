## Core Facts

**1. Rural Governance and Insurance in Qixian, Henan**
*   **Mechanism:** The "Comprehensive Social Security Family Property Insurance" (综治家财险) is a government-led welfare insurance project directed by the Henan Provincial Political and Legal Affairs Commission, featuring fiscal subsidies and marketized insurance operations.
*   **Coverage:** Households pay 100 RMB to cover 39 types of incidents, including theft, natural disasters, and personal accidents.
*   **Data (Jan 2025 – Jun 2026):** Insurers PICC P&C, Zhongyuan Agricultural Insurance, and China Life paid out 6,640 claims across more than 32,600 households in Qixian.
*   **Incident Profile:** Personal accident claims account for approximately 88.4% of total claims. The primary risks are falls and animal bites among the elderly (60+) and children (under 14), who constitute roughly 65% of injured parties.
*   **Financial Status:** In 2025, total premiums collected were 1.36 million RMB with payouts of 1.38 million RMB, resulting in a slight operational loss absorbed for social benefit.
*   **Trends (H1 2026):** Claim volumes dropped 22.23% and payout amounts dropped 27.4% compared to H1 2025, attributed to proactive hazard elimination based on insurance data feedback to public security and fire departments.
*   **Gap Analysis:** Fire-related claims represent only 2.21% of volume but 11.49% of costs (average payout 2,418 RMB). Large claims (>2,000 RMB) comprise 8% of cases but 33% of costs, indicating insufficient coverage for extreme poverty-alleviation risks.

**2. Manufacturing Policy: "Investing in People"**
*   **Argument:** Transitioning from manufacturing to "smart manufacturing" requires prioritizing human capital ("investing in people") to ensure workers can驾驭 (master/control) advanced systems.
*   **Statistics:** By end of 2025, China had built over 35,000 basic-level, 8,200 advanced-level, and 500 excellent-level smart factories. Key process digitalization in industrial enterprises rose from 52.1% (2020) to 68.6% (2025). First-half 2026 saw the number of basic-level smart factories exceed 56,000.
*   **Objective:** To align technological progress with human development, moving labor from repetitive operations to system control and innovation.

**3. Scientific Achievements and Public Campaigns**
*   **Lunar Laser Communication:** A team from the Chinese Academy of Sciences achieved two-way high-speed laser communication between Earth and the Moon. The downlink rate reached 100 Mbps, allowing an uncompressed 8K panoramic photo to transfer in 12 seconds (versus 4-5 minutes via microwave). This makes China the second country globally to master this technology.
*   **Mesoporous Carbon Research:** Academician Zhao Dongyuan and his team were awarded the 2026 Future Science Prize for Physical Science for developing a stepwise synthesis method for mesoporous carbons, applied to battery silicon hosts and heavy oil upgrading catalysts.
*   **Public Visibility Campaign:** Starting September 2026, commercial screens in Heze, Zibo, Hulunbuir, Hefei, and Hangzhou began displaying portraits of scientists such as Qian Xuesen, Deng Jiaxian, Yuan Longping, and Yu Min, reflecting a shift by mall operators to prioritize public value over commercial advertising.

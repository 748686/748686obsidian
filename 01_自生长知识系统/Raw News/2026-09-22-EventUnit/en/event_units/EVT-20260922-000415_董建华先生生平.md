## Core Facts

**Death Details:**
*   **Date of Death:** September 8, 2026, at 21:57.
*   **Location:** Hong Kong.
*   **Cause:** Illness.
*   **Age at Death:** 89 years old.

**Biographical Data:**
*   **Birth:** May 1937, Shanghai.
*   **Ancestral Home:** Zhoushan, Zhejiang Province.
*   **Migration:** Moved to Hong Kong with his family in 1947.
*   **Education:** Studied in the UK starting in 1954; graduated from the University of Liverpool in 1960 with a Bachelor of Science degree in Maritime Engineering.
*   **Early Career:** Worked in the United States after graduation; returned to Hong Kong in 1969 to participate in the family shipping business.

**Political and Public Service Career:**
*   **Chief Executive:** Served as the 1st Chief Executive (July 1997 – July 2002) and the 2nd Chief Executive (July 2002 – July 2005).
*   **CPPCC Roles:** Member of the 8th National Committee; Vice Chairperson of the 10th, 11th, 12th, and 13th National Committees of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC).
*   **Key Appointments:**
    *   1985: Member of the Basic Law Drafting Consultative Committee.
    *   1992: Appointed as one of the first batch of Hong Kong Affairs Advisors by the State Council Affairs Office for Hong Kong and Macao and Xinhua News Agency Hong Kong Branch.
    *   1995: Deputy Director of the Preparatory Committee for the Hong Kong SAR.

**Business Career:**
*   1976–1977: Chairman of the Hong Kong Shipowners Association.
*   1979: Chairman and CEO of Orient Overseas (International) Limited; Chairman and CEO of King's Landing Shipping Company.
*   Revived the family shipping business from debt to profitability during the 1980s.
*   Chairman of the Hong Kong Management Association.

**Civic and Organizational Leadership:**
*   Founding Chairman of the China-US Exchange Foundation.
*   Founding Chairman of the United Hong Kong Fund.
*   Co-General Convener of the Hong Kong Rejuvenation Coalition (established 2020).

**Honors:**
*   Recipient of the "Distinguished Contributor to 'One Country, Two Systems'" National Honor Title (awarded by Xi Jinping on September 29, 2019).
*   Grand Bauhinia Medal awarded by the Hong Kong SAR Government.

# Event Name

2026 "Writing the Chapter of Youth on the Motherland's Land" Ideological and Political Education Event and Youth Science-Tech Patriotism Narrative

## Event Overview

On the evening of September 6, 2026, at the Chinese University of Science and Technology (USTC) Gymnasium in Hefei, Anhui Province, the 2026 "Writing the Chapter of Youth on the Motherland's Land" online thematic publicity and interactive guidance activity for ideological and political education was held. The event brought together academicians, experts, "Role Models of the Times," and young technological innovation pioneers to engage youth in an online and offline ideological and political education session centered on patriotic struggle and youth development.

The event was co-organized by multiple national institutions including the Cyberspace Administration of China, the Ministry of Education, People's Daily, the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), and the Communist Youth League Central Committee. It featured four main segments: "Science and Education for the Nation, Brave Climbing of Peaks," "Embracing the Era, Living Up to Youth," "Practical Struggle, Passing the Torch," and "National Heavy Equipment." The event highlighted stories of scientific achievement, technological self-reliance, and youth entrepreneurial innovation, with particular emphasis on the intersection of personal ambition and national needs.

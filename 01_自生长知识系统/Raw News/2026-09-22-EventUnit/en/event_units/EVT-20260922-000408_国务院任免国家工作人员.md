## Event Overview
On September 20, 2026, the State Council of the People's Republic of China announced a series of appointments and dismissals of key national officials. The announcement, issued from Beijing via Xinhua News Agency, was published in the *People's Daily* on September 21, 2026, on page 03 under the section "Key News" (要闻). This constitutes a routine administrative personnel change within the Chinese government structure.

## Core Facts
The following appointments and dismissals were confirmed by the official State Council announcement reported by Xinhua:

**Appointments:**
1. **Yu Xiaohui** (余晓晖) appointed as Vice Minister of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.
2. **Pan Wenbo** (潘文博) appointed as Vice Minister of the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs.
3. **Li Chenggang** (李成钢) appointed as Director of the Position of China International Trade Negotiator (rank of full Minister / 正部长级).
4. **Ling Ji** (凌激) appointed as Deputy Director of the Position of China International Trade Negotiator (concurrently serving in this capacity).
5. **Cai Dong** (蔡东) appointed as Vice Minister of the National Financial Regulatory Administration.
6. **Zhang Yongjun** (张拥军) appointed as Deputy Director of the Cyberspace Administration of China.
7. **Zhang Xiaotang** (张孝堂) appointed as Deputy Director of the State Tobacco Monopoly Administration.
8. **Liu Zhen** (刘震) appointed as Deputy Director of the National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine.

**Dismissals:**
1. **Xu Qingsen** (徐青森) removed from the post of Vice Minister of Education.
2. **Hu Weilie** (胡卫列) removed from the post of Vice Minister of Justice.
3. **Niu Yibing** (牛一兵) removed from the post of Deputy Director of the Cyberspace Administration of China.
4. **Liao Zhiwei** (廖志伟) removed from the post of Director of the Yangtze River Water Resources Commission.

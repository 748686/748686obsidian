## Event Overview
On September 20, 2026, the People's Daily reported that four ministries and commissions jointly issued the "Implementation Plan on Accelerating the High-Quality Development of Agricultural Insurance" (《关于加快农业保险高质量发展的实施方案》). The document was issued by the Ministry of Finance in coordination with the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, the National Financial Regulatory Administration, and the National Forestry and Grassland Administration. The policy sets a target for the year 2030 to establish a multi-tier agricultural insurance system aligned with modern agricultural development and农户 risk protection needs. A key innovation introduced in the plan is the first-time policy-level proposal to implement a "one household, one policy" (一户一单) model to ensure the authenticity of underwriting information.

## Core Facts
*   **Issuing Bodies**: Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, National Financial Regulatory Administration, National Forestry and Grassland Administration.
*   **Document Title**: Implementation Plan on Accelerating the High-Quality Development of Agricultural Insurance.
*   **Publication Date**: Reported on September 21, 2026 (dated September 20, 2026).
*   **Source Report**: People's Daily (paper.people.com.cn), Article by Wang Guan (王观).
*   **2030 Goal**: To basically build a multi-tier agricultural insurance system that is consistent with farmers' risk protection needs and adaptable to modern agricultural development. The system will feature clear responsibilities between the central and local governments.
*   **Target Standards**: The overall system aims to reach international advanced levels.
*   **Strategic Outcomes**: Achieve a multi-win situation characterized by:
    *   High efficiency in subsidies.
    *   Security for industries.
    *   Tangible benefits for farmers.
    *   Sustainability for institutions.
*   **"One Household, One Policy" (一户一单)**:
    *   This is the first time this specific policy measure has been proposed at the national policy level.
    *   It mandates issuing insurance policies based on individual farming households as the primary subject.
    *   It aims to achieve "policies to households, vouchers to households, and coverage to households."
    *   It requires interoperability and sharing of data between agricultural insurance underwriting and agricultural-related production/operation data.
    *   Cross-verification of this data is intended to solidify the authenticity of underwriting information from the source.

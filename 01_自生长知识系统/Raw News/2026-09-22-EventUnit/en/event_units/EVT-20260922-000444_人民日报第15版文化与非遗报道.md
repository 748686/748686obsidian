# Event Name
People's Daily Cultural Heritage and Cultural Arts Coverage (September 21, 2026)

## Event Overview
On September 21, 2026, *People's Daily* published dedicated coverage of cultural heritage preservation, traditional arts, and literary expression. The reporting focuses on the post-UNESCO inscription period for the Beijing Central Axis, the socioeconomic revitalization of Tulu embroidered art in Qinghai province through industrial innovation, and reflective perspectives on the function of poetry in contemporary life. Additionally, promotional coverage highlights a media partnership aimed at sustaining intangible cultural heritage through commercial and documentary platforms.

## Core Facts

**1. Beijing Central Axis Cultural Observation**
*   **Source:** Article #587, titled "一条轴线，何以生生不息" (Why Does the Axis Live On Indefinitely?).
*   **Context:** This article serves as the first installment in a series titled "Post-Inscription Observations" (#587). It examines the preservation and continuity of the Beijing Central Axis following its UNESCO World Heritage inscription.
*   **Publication Details:** Published in *People's Daily*, September 21, 2026.

**2. Tulu Embroidery Industrialization in Qinghai**
*   **Source:** Article #588, titled "绣千年 绣未来" (Embroidering Millenniums, Embroidering the Future).
*   **Subject:** The development of the Tulu embroidery industry in Huzhu Tu Autonomous County, Qinghai Province.
*   **Economic Impact:** The industry has achieved an annual output value exceeding 27 million RMB.
*   **Workforce Scale:** There are 75 recognized inheritors and over 8,300 embroiderers (5,300 full-time and over 3,000 seasonal).
*   **Income Outcomes:** High-performing embroiderers report annual incomes between 40,000 and 50,000 RMB.
*   **Cultural Innovation:** Collaboration with universities and design studios has produced cross-category products (blind boxes, fashion wear) with annual cultural product sales exceeding 3 million RMB.
*   **International/High-Profile Exposure:**
    *   October 2025: Exhibition in Paris, France.
    *   China International Fashion Week: Display of traditional wedding attire.
    *   Domestic retail: Sales operations at Qianmen Street, Beijing; partnerships with livestreaming influencers.

**3. Literary Reflection**
*   **Source:** Article #588, titled "诗歌或篝火" (Poetry or Bonfire).
*   **Author:** Zhang Ergun (张二棍), identified as a poet and recipient of the 9th Lu Xun Literature Prize.
*   **Theme:** The author reflects on literature as a metaphorical "bonfire" that provides warmth, community, and dignity. The piece emphasizes the writer's duty to document the realities of the common people ("marketplace") and preserve the vitality of the Chinese language.

**4. Intangible Cultural Heritage Media Partnership**
*   **Source:** Article #590, titled "胡姬花携手《非遗之城·守护季》..." (Hu Jihua Partners with 'City of Intangible Cultural Heritage: Guardians Season'...).
*   **Event Date:** September 14, 2026.
*   **Event:** A scheduling launch conference for the documentary series *City of Intangible Cultural Heritage: Guardians Season* (《非遗之城·守护季》), produced by Youku and exclusively sponsored by Hu Jihua (Yihai Kerry).
*   **Purpose:** To promote the transition of intangible cultural heritage from "protection" to "utility" (from being preserved to being needed in daily life).
*   **Hu Jihua Heritage Status:**
    *   "Hu Jihua Ancient Method Peanut Oil Pressing Technique" listed as a Qingdao municipal intangible cultural heritage in 2015.
    *   "Ancient Method Peanut Oil Pressing Technique" listed as a Shandong provincial intangible cultural heritage in 2021.
*   **Awards:** The brand's ancient method small-press peanut oil received the 2024 Global Food Industry Award for Best Product Flavor.

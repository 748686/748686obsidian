## Event Overview

On September 21, 2026, *People's Daily* published the 17th edition of a special series focused on the five-year anniversary of the Global Initiative on Development (GID). The section, titled "The Guiding Effect of Ideas Continues to Be Released, and Practical Cooperation Achievements Are Landing One After Another (Authoritative Forum)," features expert commentary from four domestic and international specialists. They analyze the practical foundations, era value, and cooperation achievements of the GID, highlighting China's commitment to multilateralism and global governance improvement.

The initiative was proposed by President Xi Jinping on September 21, 2021, during the general debate of the 76th session of the UN General Assembly. Over the subsequent five years, it has evolved from a Chinese proposition into an international consensus, with over 2,000 cooperative projects launched and more than 200,000 individuals trained, benefiting people in over 120 countries.

## Core Facts

**Event Specifics:**
*   **Date:** September 21, 2026.
*   **Editorial Context:** Published in *People's Daily*, Vol. 17 (International), with editors Wang Yuan, Song Yiran, and Bai Ziwei.
*   **Topic:** Five-year retrospective and expert forum on the Global Initiative on Development.

**Initiative Status (as reported by *People's Daily*):**
*   Launched on September 21, 2021.
*   **Projects:** Over 2,000 cooperative projects carried out jointly with various parties.
*   **Training:** More than 200,000 talents trained across various categories.
*   **Beneficiaries:** People in over 120 countries.
*   **Support:** More than 130 countries and international organizations support the initiative; nearly 100 countries, international, and regional organizations have signed cooperation documents with China.
*   **Mechanisms:** The "Friends Group on the Global Development Initiative" has expanded to 88 countries; the UN initiative promotion working group attracts participation from more than 20 development agencies.

**Key Expert Perspectives:**
*   **Zhu Xufeng** (Dean, School of Public Administration and Policy, Tsinghua University): Emphasized that the GID is rooted in the CPC's long-term practice, translating the philosophy of "development for the people" to the international level.
*   **Li Sifan** (UNDP Resident Representative in China): Highlighted the synergy between national development and global development, citing the China-UNDP partnership (over 35 funded projects benefiting over 18 million people).
*   **Tecla Entehab** (Former State Minister, Ministry of Trade and Industry, Ethiopia): Stressed that Chinese experience proves countries can explore modernization paths suited to their own national conditions.
*   **Evandro Carvalho** (Professor of International Law, Federal University of Fluminense, Brazil): Noted that China’s practice demonstrates modernization does not equal Westernization, offering valuable reference for the Global South.

**Illustrative Projects Cited:**
*   **Malaysia:** The Lubu Bridge in Sarawak State, completed by Chinese enterprises, ended long-term reliance on ferries.
*   **Zimbabwe:**援菌草 (Aid to Zimbabwe Mushroom Grass) technology project launched in February 2025; as of June 2026, 19 training sessions were held.
*   **Tanzania:** Expert health education for the Zanzibar Schistosomiasis Control and Treatment Technology Project assisted by China.
*   **Nigeria:** Abuja Large Water Supply Project completed in June 2025, resolving water issues for approximately 3 million residents.
*   **Bangladesh:** "Urban Poverty Community Livelihood Support Project" implemented with UNDP, covering 387,000 people in 6 cities as of June 2026.

Together, they present a unified editorial stance: China’s development model (both domestically and internationally via the GID) is inherently convergent and inclusive, unlike the divergent models observed in some Western economies.

## Known Current Impact

**Domestic Impact (Economic Narrative):**
According to Dong Yu (Article #593), the clarification of economic misconceptions supports the narrative of high-quality development. The government is actively countering "pessimistic scenarios" of permanent class solidification by highlighting the spill-over effects of new kinetic energy to SMEs and下沉 markets (lower-tier markets).

**International Impact (Cooperation Outcomes):**
*   **Project Diversification:** The GID has moved beyond simple aid to include industrial parks, manufacturing clusters, and digital infrastructure (e.g., the Malaysia bridge, Nigeria water project).
*   **Capacity Building:** Focus has shifted to training (200,000+ people) and technology sharing (mushroom grass technology in Zimbabwe) to ensure sustainability after project completion.
*   **Institutional Integration:** The GID is increasingly integrated with regional agendas, such as the AU’s Agenda 2063, facilitating dialogue between China and African nations on industrialization and vocational training.

## What Cannot Currently Be Determined

**Long-term Project Sustainability:**
While the Bangladesh project claims to enhance local execution capacity, the articles do not provide data on the long-term survival rate of the micro-enterprises supported after the project concluded.

**Quantifiable Impact on SDGs:**
The articles state that the GID injects "strong momentum" into the 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda, but they do not provide a specific quantified metric (e.g., percentage reduction in poverty attributable solely to GID projects) beyond the beneficiary counts.

**Specifics of "K-Shaped" Analysis:**
While Dong Yu refutes the application of the "K-shaped" model to China, the article does not provide a detailed breakdown of which specific industries or regions are experiencing the most significant transitional "temperature differences" (温差) mentioned in the text.

## Event Conclusion

The September 21, 2026, *People's Daily* forum marks the fifth anniversary of the Global Initiative on Development by synthesizing expert opinion to validate the initiative's transition from concept to concrete practice. The event confirms that the GID has established a robust framework for cooperation involving over 130 countries and numerous international organizations. Concurrently, the publication of Dong Yu’s economic commentary on the same day reinforces the ideological underpinning of the GID: that China’s development model offers a path of inclusive, convergent growth that rejects the polarized "K-shaped" trajectories associated with Western economic crises. The collective evidence suggests the GID is currently serving as a primary vehicle for China to export development capacity, infrastructure expertise, and governance philosophy to the Global South.

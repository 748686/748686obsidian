# Event Name

Beijing Issues Action Plan for the Accelerated Development of "Token" Economy (2026–2028)

## Event Overview

On September 20, 2026, Beijing officially released the *"Beijing Action Plan for the Accelerated Development of Token Economy (2026–2028)"* (《北京市加快词元经济发展的行动方案（2026—2028年）》). The action plan aims to promote AI-empowered new industrialization by establishing "token factories,"健全ing market-based pricing and circulation mechanisms for tokens, and expanding application scenarios.

The term "token" (词元) is defined in the release as the smallest unit of information processed by artificial intelligence large models.

## Core Facts

*   **Document Title:** *Beijing Action Plan for the Accelerated Development of Token Economy (2026–2028)*
*   **Release Date:** September 20, 2026 (Announced in media on September 21, 2026)
*   **Issuing Authority:** Beijing Municipal Government (Reported by People's Daily)
*   **Key Objective:** Promote AI-empowered new industrialization (推动人工智能赋能新型工业化)
*   **Strategic Initiatives:**
    1.  Build world-class "token factories" (打造世界级词元工厂)
    2.  Improve market-based pricing and circulation mechanisms for tokens (健全词元市场化定价流通机制)
    3.  Expand token application scenarios (拓展词元应用场景)
*   **Definition Provided:** Tokens are the smallest units of information processed by AI large models.

## Event Conclusion

Beijing has formally launched its strategic initiative for a "Token Economy" via a three-year action plan (2026–2028). The policy explicitly links the concept of AI "tokens" (defined as the smallest units of information processing) to broader industrial modernization goals. Key pillars include constructing large-scale token production facilities ("factories"), establishing market-driven pricing for these tokens, and widening their application scope. This represents a significant step in treating AI data units as formal economic assets within China's largest tech hub.

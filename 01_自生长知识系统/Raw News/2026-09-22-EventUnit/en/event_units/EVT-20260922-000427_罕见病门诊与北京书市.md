## EventOverview
On September 20, 2026, two distinct policy and cultural events were reported in the People's Daily (Vol. 06). The first involves a national healthcare directive requiring the establishment of rare disease outpatient clinics across China by the end of 2026. The second marks the opening of the 2026 "I and the Earth Park" Beijing Book Fair at the Ditan Park in Beijing, highlighting cultural exchange within the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region.

## CoreFacts

### Rare Disease Prevention and Control Directive
*   **Issuing Bodies:** General Office of the National Health Commission and Comprehensive Department of the National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine.
*   **Document:** *Notice on Further Strengthening the Prevention and Control of Rare Diseases*.
*   **Key Target (Provincial Level):** By the end of 2026, every province, autonomous region, municipality directly under the central government, and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps must establish at least one rare disease outpatient clinic依托 (relying on) a provincial-level or above hospital.
*   **Key Target (Prefecture/City Level):** In prefecture-level cities with a population exceeding 3 million, at least one hospital must establish a rare disease outpatient clinic with dedicated specialist physicians providing standardized diagnostic and treatment services.
*   **Prevention Measures:**
    *   Implementation of eugenics and obstetric health services basic public health projects.
    *   Pre-pregnancy health checks for couples with genetic disease family histories or adverse pregnancy histories.
    *   Exploration of pre-pregnancy screening for single-gene genetic diseases and high-risk populations for rare diseases in areas with favorable conditions.
    *   Enhancement of prenatal screening management to improve the identification of birth defects and rare diseases.
    *   Guidance for high-risk groups to undergo timely prenatal diagnosis and receive standardized genetic counseling.
*   **Treatment Capacity:** Encouragement for medical institutions with conditions to advance rare disease ward construction, utilizing multi-disciplinary diagnosis and treatment (MDT) and integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine approaches.

### 2026 Beijing Book Fair
*   **Event Name:** 2026 "I and the Earth Park" Beijing Book Fair.
*   **Location:** Ditan Park, Beijing.
*   **Organizer:** Beijing Municipal Committee of the Publicity Department of the CPC.
*   **Duration:** 11 days, opening in late September 2026 and continuing until September 27, 2026.
*   **Scale:**
    *   Exhibition area exceeds 20,000 square meters.
    *   Nine major exhibition zones.
    *   Over 400 publishing houses, publishing institutions, and characteristic physical bookstores participating.
    *   More than 500 exhibition booths.
    *   Both the exhibition area and the number of booths are the highest in history.
*   **Zones:** Thematic publishing zone, famous publisher boutique zone, old books and new knowledge zone, characteristic bookstore zone, etc.
*   **Content Ratio:** Book exhibition booths account for 80% of the area.
*   **Activities:** Over 150 cultural activities planned during the fair.
*   **Regional Expansion:** The book fair will extend to Baoding (Hebei Province) and Tianjin to promote cultural integration within the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region.

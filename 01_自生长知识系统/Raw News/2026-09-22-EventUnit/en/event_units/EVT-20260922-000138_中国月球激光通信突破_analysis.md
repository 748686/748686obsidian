### Scenario Construction

#### Scenario 1: 🌟 Ideal World — "Lunar Internet Enabled"
**Core Assumptions:**
- The breakthrough is real and technically superior to existing RF systems.
- China successfully deploys a operational lunar laser communication network by 2027.
- International cooperation remains intact despite competition.

**Scenario Description:**
China’s lunar laser communication system enables real-time 4K/8K video streaming from the Moon, revolutionizing scientific exploration and enabling new commercial services (e.g., lunar data hubs, tele-presence). The technology becomes a standard for next-gen space missions, with international partners seeking access. The "Lunar Internet" concept gains traction, with China positioning itself as a key infrastructure provider.

**Impact:**
- **Opportunities:** First-mover advantage in lunar data services; enhanced diplomatic leverage through tech sharing.
- **Challenges:** Pressure to open standards; risk of being seen as monopolizing critical space infrastructure.

**Response Strategies:**
1. Diversify domestic capabilities in optical space communications to reduce dependency.
2. Engage in multilateral forums to shape governance of lunar communication standards.

---

#### Scenario 2: 📊 Status Quo — "Incremental Progress"
**Core Assumptions:**
- The breakthrough is incremental, not transformative.
- Technical details remain vague or are partially exaggerated.
- Global space comms evolve gradually without disruptive change.

**Scenario Description:**
China continues steady progress in lunar communications, but the "breakthrough" is absorbed into normal R&D timelines. No immediate strategic shift occurs. International competitors respond with incremental improvements rather than panic. The lunar economy develops slowly, with RF and early optical systems coexisting.

**Impact:**
- **Opportunities:** Time to strengthen existing RF-based systems; no urgent need for paradigm shifts.
- **Challenges:** Missed window for first-mover advantage in optical comms if the technology proves viable later.

**Response Strategies:**
1. Maintain balanced investment in both RF and laser communication R&D.
2. Monitor Chinese technical publications for concrete evidence of advancement.

#### Scenario 3: ⚠️ Stress Test — "Strategic Panic & Escalation"
**Core Assumptions:**
- The breakthrough is real and creates a significant capability gap.
- Perception of threat leads to accelerated military-civilian fusion in space.
- International response becomes zero-sum, with export controls and espionage allegations.

**Scenario Description:**
The verified breakthrough triggers a new phase of the space race. Competing nations impose strict technology bans, accuse each other of espionage, and accelerate weaponization of space communication satellites. Lunar missions become security-sensitive, limiting scientific collaboration. The global lunar economy fragments into competing blocs.

**Impact:**
- **Opportunities:** Increased government funding for domestic space tech; potential for domestic industry growth.
- **Challenges:** Isolation from international space networks; higher costs and delays for commercial lunar ventures.

**Response Strategies:**
1. Accelerate domestic optical communication satellite programs.
2. Build resilient, independent space communication networks to reduce vulnerability to external disruptions.

#### Scenario 4: 🔄 Black Swan — "False Signal & Credibility Loss"
**Core Assumptions:**
- The breakthrough claim is debunked as misinformation or misattribution.
- Sources admit error, leading to embarrassment for Chinese space agencies.
- International community dismisses future Chinese space announcements with heightened skepticism.

**Scenario Description:**
Investigative journalism or independent verification reveals that the "breakthrough" was based on misinterpreted data or an incorrect source (as seen in this EventUnit’s source mismatch). China’s credibility in space tech reporting faces temporary damage. However, underlying technological progress continues quietly without fanfare.

**Impact:**
- **Opportunities:** Reduced immediate pressure; time to catch up without public scrutiny.
- **Challenges:** Need to rebuild trust in future announcements; potential for reduced foreign partnership interest.

**Response Strategies:**
1. Strengthen transparency and verification mechanisms for future tech announcements.
2. Continue R&D efforts without relying on public perception for strategic planning.

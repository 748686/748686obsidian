**「Solution」** Using Claude Code with Opus 5, the author tasked the agent with scanning Professor Emily Clader’s LaTeX papers for open problems involving checkable counterexamples. **「Takeaway」** The episode illustrates how AI agents can now perform the full arc of mathematical research—search, computation, and proof construction—at speeds that render human effort obsolete, raising philosophical questions about the value of intellectual struggle when the &\#x27;sacred flame&\#x27; of knowledge can be sustained without it.

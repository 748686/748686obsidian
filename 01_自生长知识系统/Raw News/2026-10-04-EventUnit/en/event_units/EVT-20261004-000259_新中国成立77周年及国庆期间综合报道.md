## Event Overview
On October 3, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive series of reports covering the 77th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China (celebrated on October 1). The reporting synthesizes reactions to General Secretary Xi Jinping's speech at the reception held on September 30, 2026, alongside specific reports on cultural tourism, industrial policy (embodied AI, private economy), international diplomacy, sports achievements at the Asian Games, and heritage preservation. The event window encompasses the early days of the National Day holiday period.

## Core Facts

**1. Political Commemoration and Leadership Speech**
*   On the evening of September 30, 2026, Xi Jinping delivered a speech at the reception celebrating the 77th anniversary of the PRC.
*   Key themes included pride in the nation's achievements, confidence in Chinese modernization, and the importance of starting the "15th Five-Year Plan" period well.
*   The year 2026 marks the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China and the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Red Army's Long March.

**2. Economic and Industrial Development ("15th Five-Year Plan" Outlook)**
*   **Major Infrastructure:** The Three Gorges Water Transport New Channel project completed its preliminary excavation blasting operations on September 30, 2026, marking the start of full-scale construction. It is identified as the first major national标志性工程 (landmark project) started during the 15th Five-Year Plan period.
*   **Private Economy:** In the first eight months of 2026, private enterprises accounted for 56.9% of China's import and export volume, totaling 19.79 trillion yuan, with a growth of 17.6%. Private enterprises account for over 92% of national high-tech enterprises (up from 62.4% in 2012).
*   **Embodied AI:** Shangyu District in Shaoxing, Zhejiang, has introduced 20 embodied intelligence projects with total investment exceeding 15 billion yuan. A four-legged robot was deployed at Zhejiang Runtu New Material Co., Ltd., reducing personnel exposure time in high-risk areas by 90%.
*   **Regional Industry:** Shandong province's sports industry exceeded 600 billion yuan in total size in 2025. Cao County in Heze became a hub for Hanfu (traditional clothing) sales, exceeding 13 billion yuan in 2025.

**3. International Diplomacy and Global South Engagement**
*   **France:** A French edition promotion meeting and seminar on "Chinese Modernization" for *The Governance of China* (Volume V) was held in Paris, hosted by the Chinese Embassy. Participants included French political figures, business leaders, and academics.
*   **United Nations:** Jessen Odhiambo, UN Development System Coordinator in China, stated that China's Five-Year Plans demonstrate strong planning capabilities and resilience. He cited Xi Jinping’s remarks at the 2026 World AI Conference regarding global AI governance and helping Global South nations bridge the digital divide.
*   **Tanzania:** Francis Michael, Principal of the Party School of the Chama Cha Mapinduzi (Tanzania), wrote that Chinese modernization provides valuable insights for Global South countries, emphasizing independent development paths and long-term strategic planning.

**4. Cultural and Social Activities**
*   **Hong Kong Youth:** A sharing session for the "Salute to the National Flag" Hong Kong and Macao Youth Flag Raising Team representatives was held on October 2, 2026. Nearly 1,000 Hong Kong teachers and students participated in the delegation visit to Beijing from September 18–22.
*   **Heritage:** Shenyang Imperial Palace Museum celebrated its 100th anniversary (founded as Dongsan Province Museum in 1926). It welcomed over 2.7 million visitors in the first half of 2026.
*   **Green Transition:** Gengche Town in Suqian, Jiangsu, is cited as a model for implementing the "Two Mountains" theory, having transformed from a polluted plastic recycling hub to a green logistics and e-commerce center.

**5. Sports Achievements (Nagoya Asian Games)**
*   As of October 2, 2026, the Chinese delegation had won 162 gold, 81 silver, and 73 bronze medals.
*   **Tennis:** Wang Xiyu defeated teammate Zhang Shuai 6-2, 6-1 in the women's singles final, securing the sixth consecutive Asian Games gold for China and a quota spot for the Los Angeles Olympics.
*   **Boxing:** Wu Yu (51kg) defended her Olympic title, winning gold.
*   **Other Medals:** Golds were also won in Judo (Niu Xinran), Taekwondo (Ma Jingyue), Archery (mixed team), Canoeing (slalom), and Cycling (sprint).

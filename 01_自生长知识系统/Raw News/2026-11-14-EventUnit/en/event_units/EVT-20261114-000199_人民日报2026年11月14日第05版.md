## Event Overview

On November 13, 2026, four significant events occurred in China regarding regulatory enforcement, technological infrastructure, industrial innovation, and ideological education. These events were published together in the Comprehensive Section (Page 05) of *People's Daily* on November 14, 2026. The articles cover the State Council Work Safety Committee's约谈 (interview/admonishment) of eleven municipal governments, the Ministry of Transport's release of the first batch of AI+Transportation scenarios, the opening of the 2026 Enterprise Scientific and Technological Innovation Development Forum in Tianjin, and a symposium on the exemplary deeds of Wang Ji.

## Core Facts

**1. Safety Production Regulatory Enforcement**
*   **Action:** The Office of the State Council Work Safety Committee conducted safety production interviews (约谈) with the heads of 11 municipal-level governments.
*   **Date of Action:** "Recently" (reported on Nov 13, 2026).
*   **Targeted Regions:**
    *   Cities where major accidents occurred this year: Baotou (Inner Mongolia), Nanyang (Henan), and Xiangyang (Hubei).
    *   Cities where 3 or more relatively large accidents occurred within 6 months: Lvliang, Shuozhou, and Xinzhou (Shanxi); Mudanjiang (Heilongjiang); Yantai (Shandong); Zhengzhou (Henan); and Honghe Hani and Yi Autonomous Prefecture and Qujing (Yunnan).
*   **Key Directives:** Officials were urged to establish correct views on political achievements, implement responsibility measures, and adhere to the "Three Manages and Three Musts" (三管三必须) principle. Specific focus areas include fireworks/explosives enforcement, mine safety "eight hard measures," hazardous chemicals risk prevention, and public safety in crowded places.

**2. Artificial Intelligence in Transportation**
*   **Action:** The Ministry of Transport released the first batch of 354 typical application scenario projects for "Artificial Intelligence + Transportation."
*   **Purpose:** To provide real-world scenario spaces for the scaled innovative application of AI in transportation.
*   **Coverage:** Smart driving, smart highways, smart shipping, smart construction/maintenance, smart travel services, smart freight, and intelligent safety supervision.
*   **Task Categories:** Breakthrough research, innovation demonstration, and application promotion.
*   **Reporting Date:** November 13, 2026 (Beijing).

**3. Enterprise Technological Innovation Forum**
*   **Event:** The 2026 Enterprise Scientific and Technological Innovation Development Forum opened on November 13, 2026.
*   **Location:** Tianjin.
*   **Organizers:** Science and Technology Daily and the Management Committee of Tianjin Binhai High-tech Industrial Development Zone.
*   **Theme:** "Open Innovation · Fusion and Symbiosis."
*   **Key Releases:**
    *   *Disruptive Technology Industrialization Index Report (2026):* Indicates global disruptive technology industrialization has entered a critical window period; China is in a "total volume rise, speed shift"攻坚 (tackling hard issues) period.
    *   *National Scenario Supply-Demand Matching Platform:* Currently holds over 3,000 scenario construction requirements and over 500 technology supply information entries. It functions as a hub for centralized release of demands, display of supplies, and intelligent matchmaking.

**4. Learning from Model Wang Ji**
*   **Event:** A symposium was held in Beijing on the afternoon of November 13, 2026, for military and local heroes and outstanding youth representatives to study the advanced deeds of Comrade Wang Ji.
*   **Participants:** 10 representatives from different regions and fields.
*   **Key Themes:** Loyalty, perseverance, contribution to national defense/technology, and integrating personal growth into national development.
*   **Notable Attendees/Quotes:**
    *   Zhang Qijun (Platoon Leader, "Liu Laozhuang Company," 82nd Group Army): Emphasized Wang Ji as a benchmark for young soldiers.
    *   Hou Changling (Sergeant Major, PLA Rocket Force): Highlighted Wang Ji's loyalty and dedication.
    *   Wang Chen (Sergeant Major, PLA Air Force): Stated初心 (original aspiration) is long-term persistence, not momentary passion.
    *   Gao Chao (Deputy Secretary of Party Committee, Marxism School, Beijing Normal University): Advocated planting seeds of truth, goodness, and beauty in students.
    *   Liu Xinxin (Chief Skill Master, Xiangtan Electric Manufacturing Group Co., Ltd.): Expressed determination to work hard despite Wang Ji's physical disabilities (referencing him being in a wheelchair).

## Core Facts

### 1. Solid Waste Management and "Zero-Waste City" Initiative
*   **Policy Background:** In July 2026, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, along with the National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, issued the *Solid Waste Pollution Prevention "15th Five-Year Plan"*, aiming to build a number of "Zero-Waste Cities" by 2030 (Article #302).
*   **Shanghai Case Study:** In 2024, Shanghai enacted China's first local regulation on "Zero-Waste City" construction, the *Shanghai Zero-Waste City Construction Regulations* (Article #302).
*   **Industrial Circulation:**
    *   Shanghai Jicheng Environmental Protection Technology Co., Ltd. processes waste activated carbon. From 1 ton of waste carbon, 600-700 kg of regenerated carbon is produced. Their current capacity is 10,000 tons per year (Article #302).
    *   Shanghai Weizu Amino Acid Co., Ltd. produces 250 tons of waste activated carbon annually. Previously, disposal costs were approximately one million yuan (Article #302).
    *   Shanghai has established 196 national-level green factories and 8 national-level green parks. In 2025, the comprehensive utilization rate of general industrial solid waste reached 98.2%, with a total generation of 21,39.8 tons (Article #302).
*   **Agricultural Circulation:**
    *   Shanghai Songlin Agricultural Development Co., Ltd. (Jinshan District) uses an automatic system where pig manure is anaerobically fermented to produce biogas, upgraded to bio-natural gas for the city grid, and converted into green methanol for ship fuel (Article #302).
    *   Guangming牧业 (Guangming Dairy Farm) in Langxia Town produces 1.835 million cubic meters of bio-natural gas and 31,000 tons of digestate annually (Article #302).
    *   Shanghai's large-scale livestock and poultry farms have a 100% match rate for manure treatment facilities, and crop straw comprehensive utilization is stable at around 98% (Article #302).
*   **Community Engagement:**
    *   Xinhua Road Subdistrict in Changning District launched the "One Square Meter" initiative in 2021, supporting community projects like "Toy Exchange Houses" and "Zero-Waste Gardens" (Article #302).
    *   Shanghai has built 15,000 "two-network integration" recycling service points and 198 transfer stations, with a domestic waste recycling and utilization rate of 45.3% (Article #302).

### 2. Sun Yat-sen Commemoration (160th Anniversary)
*   **Beijing Ceremony:** A grand conference commemorating the 160th anniversary of Sun Yat-sen's birth was held at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing on November 12, 2026. General Secretary Xi Jinping delivered a speech honoring Sun's contributions and calling for national reunification and modernization (Article #307).
*   **Guangdong Ceremony:** On the morning of November 13, 2026, the Guangdong Provincial Commemoration Conference was held at the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall in Guangzhou. Attendees included provincial leaders, relatives of Sun Yat-sen, representatives from Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, overseas Chinese, and university students. A reverent ceremony followed, involving three bows before Sun Yat-sen's copper statue (Articles #304, #307).
*   **Public Reaction:** The speech sparked strong resonance domestically and internationally. Respondents included staff from the Xinhai Revolution Museum in Wuhan, community workers in Beijing, Russian sinologist Yuri Tavorovsky, and leaders from the China Democratic National Construction Association (Article #307).

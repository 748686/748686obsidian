## Core Facts

### 1. APEC "China Year" and Shenzhen Summit Preparations
*   **Host Nation Status:** China holds the APEC chairmanship for the year 2026, marking its return as host after a 12-year interval (having last hosted in 2014).
*   **Key Event:** The APEC 33rd Leaders' Week will take place in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province.
*   **Schedule:**
    *   **November 17–18:** APEC Business Advisory Council (ABAC) Summit in Shenzhen. Theme: *"Promoting Prosperity Together, Achieving Win-Win Outcomes in the Future."* President Xi Jinping will attend the opening and deliver the keynote speech.
    *   **November 18–19:** APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen. Theme: *"Building an Asia-Pacific Community, Promoting Common Prosperity."* President Xi Jinping will chair the meeting and deliver a speech.
*   **Diplomatic Activities:** During the meeting, President Xi will host welcome banquets and conduct bilateral meetings with foreign leaders.
*   **Preparatory Statistics:** Over the past year, China has hosted more than 300 APEC events with over 10,000 participants. These included high-level officials' meetings, 10 ministerial meetings (covering women, trade, tourism, forestry, food security, SMEs, energy, human resources, transportation, and finance), and high-level digital week activities.
*   **Logistics & Atmosphere:** Shenzhen is currently undergoing extensive preparations, including the deployment of APEC branding at transit hubs, bus stops, and ports (specifically the Shenzhen Bay Port).

### 2. China's Foreign Policy Engagements
*   **Premier Li Qiang’s Visit to Thailand:** On November 13, 2026, Premier Li Qiang held talks with Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul in Bangkok.
    *   **Context:** This was a reciprocal visit; Anutin visited China in July 2026 for a meeting with President Xi Jinping. The mutual visits within four months highlight the high level of bilateral relations.
    *   **Agreed Cooperation Areas:** China-Thailand Railway construction, new five-year economic and trade cooperation plans, geographical indication product mutual protection, finance, new energy, semiconductors, and AI.
    *   **Security:** Both sides agreed to strengthen law enforcement cooperation, specifically targeting online gambling and telecommunications fraud, to ensure the safety of Chinese tourists.
    *   **Documents Signed:** Cooperation agreements in the automotive industry, vocational education, agriculture, customs, and security were signed.
*   **International Reaction:** International figures from Australia, New Zealand, South Korea, Mexico, Thailand, Malaysia, and Russia expressed expectations that the Shenzhen summit would signal continued support for multilateralism, open regionalism, and the construction of an Asia-Pacific Community.

### 3. World Chinese Language Conference
*   **Event:** 2026 World Chinese Language Conference (WCLC) opened in Beijing on November 13, 2026.
*   **VIP Presence:** Han Zheng, Vice President of the PRC, attended the opening ceremony and delivered a speech.
*   **Theme:** *"Chinese: More Than Just a Language."*
*   **Key Statistics Cited:**
    *   There are approximately 210 million Chinese language learners/users worldwide.
    *   In 2025, over 82 million foreigners entered or exited China (up 26.4% year-on-year).
    *   In the first half of 2026, foreign entries/exits grew by another 20.6% year-on-year.
*   **Four Proposals by Han Zheng:**
    1.  Strengthen capacity building for international Chinese language education.
    2.  Cultivate people-to-people bonds through localized education and teacher training.
    3.  Promote mutual learning among civilizations using Chinese as a bridge.
    4.  Leverage Chinese to empower international cooperation in trade, education, and technology.
*   **Attendance:** Approximately 2,000 attendees, including government officials, scholars, university heads, and international organization representatives.

### 4. China International Agri-Food Expo (CIAFE)
*   **Event:** The 23rd China International Agri-Food Expo opened in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, on November 13, 2026.
*   **Theme:** *"Building Agriculture into a Modernized Large-Scale Industry."*
*   **Scale:** Exhibition area exceeding 120,000 square meters; nearly 5,000 enterprises participating with over 75,000 products. Domestic exhibitors increased by 26.1% compared to the previous session. Expected attendance of over 60,000 buyers and professionals.
*   **Special Features:**
    *   **"AI+" Agriculture Zone:** Showcasing smart agricultural equipment, agricultural large models, and agents.
    *   **Cross-Strait Integration:** Co-located with the Cross-Strait Modern Agriculture Expo and Cross-Strait Flower Expo, featuring over 350 enterprises.
    *   **Zhejiang Agricultural Expo:** Featuring 15,000+ products and distribution of over 3.5 million RMB in consumption vouchers.
    *   **Guest of Honor:** Mexico.
    *   **APEC Connection:** A new "China-APEC Agricultural Cooperation Pavilion" was established, focusing on food security and sustainable agriculture within the APEC framework.

### 5. Commemoration of Sun Yat-sen’s 160th Birth Anniversary
*   **Event:** A commemorative conference was held at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing on November 12, 2026.
*   **Keynote:** President Xi Jinping delivered a speech emphasizing the inheritance of Sun Yat-sen’s spirit for national rejuvenation, unity, and the peaceful development of cross-strait relations.

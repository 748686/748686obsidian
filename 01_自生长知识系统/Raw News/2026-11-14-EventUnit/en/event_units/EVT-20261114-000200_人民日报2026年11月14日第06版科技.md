## Core Facts

**1. Lithium Battery Breakthrough (Lithium-Fluorine Coordination)**
*   **Innovation:** Researchers from Nankai University and the Shanghai Space Power Supply Institute developed a new electrolyte technology based on "lithium-fluorine coordination," shifting from the traditional "lithium-oxygen coordination."
*   **Mechanism:** The team utilized fluorinated hydrocarbon solvents to create a "super sports car" for lithium ions, improving desolvation energy efficiency, oxidation stability, and low-temperature performance.
*   **Performance Claims:** Experimental results suggest energy density could more than double compared to top-tier traditional lithium batteries under equivalent volume and weight. Range exceeding 1,000 km is considered possible.
*   **Publication:** The findings were published in the international journal *Nature*.
*   **Timeline to Market:** Engineering and industrialization challenges (synthesis, purification, manufacturing standards) may require an additional 1–2 years or more.
*   **Source Attribution:** Academic piece by Chen Jun (Academician, Nankai University); extended reading by Yan Zhenhua (Nankai University).

**2. Abolition of the Leap Second**
*   **Policy Change:** The 28th General Conference on Weights and Measures (CGPM), held in Versailles, France, voted to discontinue the use of leap seconds.
*   **Implementation Date:** A "continuous Coordinated Universal Time" (UTC) will commence on May 20, 2027.
*   **New System ("Leap Hour"):** Instead of adding/subtracting single seconds, the new system allows atomic time and world time to drift apart until the difference accumulates to approximately one hour, at which point a single adjustment will be made.
*   **Historical Context:** Since 1972, 27 positive leap seconds have been inserted. The last occurred on January 1, 2017 (Beijing time).
*   **Rationale:** Digital systems (aviation, GPS, telecom) struggle with the discontinuity of leap seconds. Recent signs of Earth's rotational acceleration also raise the probability of negative leap seconds, which pose unknown risks to software systems.
*   **Source Attribution:** Dou Zhong (Party Secretary, National Time Service Center, Chinese Academy of Sciences).

**3. China's 43rd Antarctic Expedition**
*   **Status:** The expedition team departed for Antarctica.
*   **Logistics:** Supported by the icebreakers *Xue Long*, *Xue Long 2*, and *Dayang*. Approximately 600 team members from over 100 domestic institutions are participating.
*   **Duration:** Expected to return in April 2027.
*   **Key Tasks:**
    *   Completion of the Qinling Station construction and acceptance.
    *   Surveying the Mary Bird Land station site.
    *   Upgrading the Great Wall and Zhongshan Stations.
    *   "Qilin" subglacial lake drilling mission.
    *   Testing domestic equipment, including the AR500 large unmanned helicopter.

**4. Barley Genetics Decoded**
*   **Discovery:** A team led by Yang Ping (Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences) revealed the genetic basis for the协同 (synergy) between early maturity and stable yield in autumn-sown barley in two-crop-per-year regions.
*   **Genetic Mechanism:** Identified a gene module consisting of the phytochrome gene *PhyC* (acting as a "gas pedal" for flowering) linked with the vernalization gene *VRN-H1* (acting as a "brake").
*   **Publication:** Results published in *Nature*.
*   **Breeding History:** The study mapped 80+ years of Chinese barley breeding, noting the fusion of introduced European high-yield barley with local varieties since the mid-20th century.

**5. Popcorn-Flavored Tomato**
*   **Innovation:** Researchers at Xianghu Laboratory (Hangzhou) and the University of Adelaide developed a tomato variety with a popcorn-like aroma.
*   **Mechanism:** The flavor comes from 2-AP (1-acetyl-2-pyrroline), typically found in fragrant rice. Scientists disabled two BADH family genes (*SlBADH1* and *SlBADH2*) in the tomato genome, which normally act as "suppressors" of 2-AP accumulation.
*   **Quality:** The modification did not affect flowering time, plant height, fruit weight, sweetness, or acidity compared to ordinary tomatoes.

**6. Space Station Extravehicular Laser Repair**
*   **Achievement:** A "extravehicular laser maintenance device," jointly developed by Huazhong University of Science and Technology (Prof. Huang Yu's team) and the Shanghai Space Power Supply Institute, successfully completed laser cutting maintenance trials on the Chinese Space Station.
*   **Significance:** This is reported as the world's first remote-controlled precision laser cutting test in the extreme space environment hundreds of kilometers above Earth.
*   **Technology:** Utilized an "8+2" axis multi-degree-of-freedom fine-tuning mechanism and a three-degree-of-freedom capture mechanism for micron-level precision.

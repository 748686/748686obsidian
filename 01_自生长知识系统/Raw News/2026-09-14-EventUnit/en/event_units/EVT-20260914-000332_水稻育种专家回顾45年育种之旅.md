# 为水稻打造"中国芯"（我身边的最强大脑）

## Event Overview
In 2026-09-14, People's Daily published a feature article titled "为水稻打造'中国芯'（我身边的最强大脑）" (Building a 'Chinese Chip' for Rice: The Most Powerful Brains Around Me). The article is an interview with Yang Yuanzhu (杨远柱), identified as the Chief Scientist of Rice at Yuan Longping Agricultural Science and Technology Co., Ltd. The text details his 45-year career in rice breeding, specifically highlighting the discovery and development of the temperature-sensitive male-sterile line "Zhu 1S" (株1S) and the establishment of a commercialized, segmented breeding system that significantly improved hybrid rice efficiency and food security in China.

## Core Facts
*   **Author/Subject:** Yang Yuanzhu, Chief Scientist of Rice at Yuan Longping Agricultural Science and Technology Co., Ltd.
*   **Duration of Career:** The subject states he has been engaged in rice breeding for 45 years.
*   **Key Discovery (1989):** While working at the Huaihua City Academy of Agricultural Sciences in Hunan, he discovered a spontaneously induced temperature-sensitive male-sterile rice material in a batch of indica-japonica hybrid descendants.
*   **Validation Process:** He preserved 10-12 seeds using desiccant. In 1993, after relocating to the Zhuzhou City Academy of Agricultural Sciences, he sowed the seeds. Only 3 germinated; 2 were fertile, and 1 was sterile. This single sterile plant was confirmed to switch from sterile to fertile by October.
*   **Resulting Material ("Zhu 1S"):** The team developed the "Zhu 1S" line, characterized by an infertile onset temperature as low as 22.6°C and insensitivity to continuous 23°C low temperatures for six days.
*   **Technical Innovation:** To solve the "dizziness" problem (where seed production fails if temperatures drop below 24°C for 2-3 consecutive days), the team used a 50,000 yuan grant to build a cold-water irrigation platform and created a dual-pressure stress selection system (natural and artificial low-temperature stress).
*   **Endorsement:** Yuan Longping is quoted as praising "Zhu 1S" as one of the safest and most widely used core seed sources for early indica temperature-sensitive sterile lines.
*   **Systemic Change (1999):** The team established a "commercialized breeding system" and a "factory-style, segmented" breeding model, dividing the process into 6 specialized stages.
*   **Metrics of Success:**
    *   Breeding efficiency increased by over 50%.
    *   Breeding cycle shortened by 3-4 years.
    *   611 new hybrid rice varieties were developed over the subsequent 27 years.
    *   Cumulative promotion area reached nearly 800 million mu.

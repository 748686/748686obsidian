## Event Overview

On September 14, 2026, the third edition of the "New Era Youth 'Retro-Walk' the Long March Route" event was held in Guizhou province, China. Organized to mark the 90th anniversary of the Red Army's victory in the Long March, the initiative brought together over 300 young representatives from across China. The event combined physical hiking through historic sites with on-site ideological education, utilizing digital technologies such as holograms and interactive installations to make historical context tangible for the youth. The activity aimed to internalize the "Long March Spirit" as an actionable source of motivation for young people, encouraging them to apply this resilience in fields such as technological innovation and rural revitalization.

*   **Date:** September 14, 2026.
*   **Location:** Guizhou Province, specifically sites in northern Guizhou (Qianbei), including the Zunyi Conference site, Lou Shan Pass, Four Crossings of Chishui River Memorial Hall, Qiangang Slope Martyrs' Monument, Gouba Conference site, and Huamao Village in Fengxiang Town, Bozhou District, Zunyi.
*   **Participation:** Over 300 young representatives from across China. The participants included university students, grassroots officials, engineers working on "bottleneck" technologies, rural entrepreneurs, red site lecturers, and young internet journalists.
*   **Anniversary:** The event coincided with the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Red Army's Long March.
*   **Key Activities:**
    *   A 7-kilometer rural hike connecting the Gouba Conference site to Huamao Village via the "Truth Path."
    *   Visits to historic sites: Zunyi Conference site, Lou Shan Pass battlefield ruins, Four Crossings of Chishui River Memorial Hall, and Qiangang Slope Martyrs' Monument.
    *   On-site ideological education (political-legal education or "sizheng" classes) held at old meeting sites, battle ruins, and memorial halls.
    *   Technology-enabled experiences: Viewing the live performance art *Grand Turning Point* (《伟大转折》), visiting the Guizhou Long March Culture Digital Art Gallery (featuring holographic images and interactive installations), and listening to red story lectures using scenario reproduction and historical data analysis.
*   **Thematic Focus:** "Innovating Red Education, Inheriting the Red Gene." The event emphasized that the Long March was not just a geographical route but a spiritual context connecting history with reality. It highlighted the demographic parallel that during the 1934-1935 Long March, the average age of commanders was under 25 and fighters was under 20, mirroring the youthful demographic of the modern participants.

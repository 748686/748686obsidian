### [China reports 21.44 billion cross-regional trips during National Day holiday](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/08/content_30184434.html) ⭐️ ?/10

According to a People&\#x27;s Daily report dated October 8, 2026, the Ministry of Transport estimates that total cross-regional personnel flows in China reached 2.144 billion person-times during the 2026 National Day holiday from October 1 to 7, averaging 306 million daily. Road travel remained the dominant mode, accounting for an estimated 196.42 billion person-times at an average of 28.06 million daily, while commercial road passenger volume grew 6.8% year-on-year to 286.76 million. Rail客运 volume surged 13.1% to 152.36 million, water transport rose 7.8% to 10.99 million, and civil aviation remained essentially flat year-on-year at 16.95 million person-times.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 8, 00:26

**「Background」** The National Day holiday, also known as Golden Week, is China&\#x27;s longest annual travel period, typically generating massive cross-regional mobility as workers and students return home or tour domestically. The Ministry of Transport publishes pre- and post-holiday flow estimates to help plan capacity and inform the public.

**「Impact」** Transport operators and infrastructure managers should expect sustained high rail and road demand during peak holiday windows, with rail growth outpacing other modes. No immediate policy changes are indicated, but the data may influence future capacity investments in high-speed rail and highway corridors.

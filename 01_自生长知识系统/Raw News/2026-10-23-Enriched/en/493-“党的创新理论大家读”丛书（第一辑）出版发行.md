### [Leung Chun-ying attends Greater Bay Area chamber conference in Macau](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/23/content_30187348.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The 4th Greater Bay Area Development Industry and Commerce Conference opened on October 22, 2026, in Macau, with National Committee of the Chinese People&\#x27;s Political Consultative Conference Vice Chair Leung Chun-ying delivering remarks at the opening ceremony. Sponsored by the CCPIT and the governments of Guangdong Province and the Hong Kong and Macau SARs, the event drew over 1,000 government and business representatives and focused on the theme "Integrating the Bay Area, Linking the World." Leung framed the seven-year implementation of the Greater Bay Area Development Planning Outline as a major national strategy that is turning its blueprint into reality and strengthening the practice of "One Country, Two Systems."

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 23, 00:26

**「Background」** The Greater Bay Area strategy links Guangdong Province with the Hong Kong and Macau SARs to deepen economic integration across one of China&\#x27;s most active economic regions. The national plan guiding the initiative was released seven years ago in February 2019, and subsequent updates have been used to track regional development progress.

**「Impact」** Business and government attendees at the Macau conference are being encouraged to engage with Greater Bay Area opportunities, which could shape near-term corporate networking, cross-border investment discussions, and policy coordination across the region.

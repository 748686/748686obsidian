### [Shanghai Police Launch AI-Powered National Anti-Fraud App](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180452.html) ⭐️ ?/10

In September 2026, the "National Anti-Fraud AI" \(国家反诈AI\) APP, guided by the Criminal Investigation Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security and independently developed by the Shanghai Municipal Public Security Bureau, officially launched to boost the informatization and intelligentization of telecom-fraud prevention. The app offers three core functions: an AI large-model intelligent Q&A engine that assesses fraud risk, identifies fraud types, and links video case studies in real time; an anti-fraud news feed embedding content from the National Anti-Fraud Center and Shanghai&\#x27;s "803" anti-fraud media matrix, which periodically publishes fraud-trend alerts and case breakdowns in text-plus-short-video format; and a searchable anti-fraud "dictionary" housing electronic copies of the national anti-fraud publicity handbook and guide, accessible via category browsing, keyword search, and voice reading. The article illustrates its use with a police officer in Shanghai&\#x27;s Fengxian district who queried a suspicious app called "诚远致信" through the tool, which flagged it as likely a scam and pushed a related fraud-case video, convincing a hesitant resident to cancel a transfer. The app is available on major mobile app stores and as WeChat and Alipay mini-programs, making it accessible without requiring a separate installation.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Background」** Telecom and network fraud is a persistent public-security priority in China; the existing "National Anti-Fraud Center" \(国家反诈中心\) app has long served as the primary government-distributed tool for fraud warnings and device registration. Shanghai&\#x27;s "803" is a dedicated anti-fraud hotline and media matrix operated under the Shanghai Municipal Public Security Bureau, whose content is now integrated into the new AI app&\#x27;s news feed.

**「Impact」** For the large population of elderly and less tech-savvy residents who are frequent targets of telecom fraud, the app provides an immediate, free AI verification tool that can flag suspicious applications or transaction requests in real time, shortening the window between a fraud attempt and victim awareness without requiring a phone call to a hotline.

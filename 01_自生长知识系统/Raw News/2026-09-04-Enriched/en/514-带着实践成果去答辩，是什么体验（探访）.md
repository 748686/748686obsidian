### [Expert commentary on recognizing practice outcomes in graduate degree evaluation](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179088.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A 4 September 2026 People&\#x27;s Daily expert commentary argues that evaluating graduate students through practice outcomes is a constructive reform that breaks reliance on the single standard of academic theses. The article distinguishes research-oriented disciplines, where thesis writing remains essential, from applied and professional fields such as medicine, engineering machinery, and publishing, where practice-aligned outputs better match disciplinary attributes. It frames this shift not as lowering graduation standards, but as requiring higher实践能力 benchmarks tied to real industry needs, while noting that traditional theses still offer irreplaceable theoretical value. The piece calls for continued refinement of practice-outcome recognition criteria and quality oversight to cultivate more application-oriented talents who serve industrial development.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** Chinese higher-education reform has increasingly emphasized the separation of academic and professional degree tracks, seeking evaluation methods that align with distinct career-oriented learning outcomes. Practice-outcome assessment typically involves industry projects, design portfolios, clinical cases, or technical reports rather than conventional literature-based dissertations.

**「Impact」** The commentary signals editorial support for universities expanding practice-based graduation options in applied master&\#x27;s and professional programs, which may accelerate policy adoption in fields such as medicine and engineering. Whether institutions can implement consistent recognition standards and oversight remains contingent on further regulatory guidance.

### [伽师县新梅产业带动二十余万人就业](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179089.html) ⭐️ ?/10

In Jiashi County, Xinjiang, the prunus \(new plum/ximei\) industry has become a major economic driver, with planted area stable at 570,000 mu accounting for 50% of China&\#x27;s total and 2026 output expected to reach over 410,000 tonnes, about 70% of national production. To overcome the fruit&\#x27;s short shelf life of three to five days, the county built a 500,000-tonne cold-chain storage system, extended the sales window from 10–20 days to two to six months, and paired it with rural e-commerce stations and express delivery coverage that now reaches 100% of villages. Technical teams provide standardized orchard management, while digital supervision and full-chain traceability have earned the product green-food and climate-optimized certifications. The integrated planting, packaging, processing, and sales chain now employs more than 200,000 people, pushes per-capita farmer income to 17,000 yuan, and has generated over ten billion yuan in industry output.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** 新梅，又称西梅，是新疆伽师县的特产品种。伽师县地处戈壁滩区域，过去主要种植玉米和棉花，农业效益较低。近年来，通过引入标准化种植技术、冷链物流体系和电商销售网络，当地成功将新梅产业打造为乡村振兴的重要支柱。

**「Impact」** Jiashi County farmers growing prunes now earn over 5,000 yuan per mu compared with less than 1,000 yuan previously, and the county&\#x27;s cold-chain and e-commerce infrastructure enables nationwide fresh delivery within 48–72 hours. The model demonstrates how targeted agri-tech extension and logistics investment can transform a highly perishable regional crop into a scalable national commodity.

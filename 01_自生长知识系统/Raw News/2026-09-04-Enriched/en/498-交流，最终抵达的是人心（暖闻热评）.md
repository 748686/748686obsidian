### [China&\#x27;s high-tech manufacturing profits surge amid industrial upgrade](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179070.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Data released by China&\#x27;s National Bureau of Statistics shows that from January to July 2026, profit in above-average-sized high-tech manufacturing enterprises grew by 50.1% year-on-year, pulling total industrial profit growth by 9.6 percentage points. The figures reflect continued advancement up the manufacturing value chain and rapid expansion of new growth drivers during the first seven months of the year. National bureau data described overall industrial production as steadily expanding with a positive profit trend for firms above the designated size.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** China has been steering industrial policy toward high-tech and advanced manufacturing sectors to reduce reliance on low-margin export assembly and bolster technological self-reliance. Profit growth in the high-tech manufacturing segment is commonly used as a gauge of whether that structural upgrade is accelerating across the broader industrial base.

**「Impact」** The reported high-tech profit surge signals stronger earnings momentum for firms in advanced manufacturing and suggests policy support for industrial upgrading is contributing to improved corporate performance in those sectors. Uncertainty remains about whether the gain is concentrated in specific subindustries or reflects broad-based improvement across regions and ownership types.

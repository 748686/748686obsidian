### [Foshan &\#x27;West League&\#x27; football tournament becomes Greater Bay Area cultural and economic engine](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179005.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The 20th edition of Foshan&\#x27;s amateur "West League" \(西甲\) football tournament concluded on August 23, 2026, at Yunxiushan Stadium with Tanwan Gaming defeating Baishui Huaxing 4-2. The 45-day competition featured 48 teams from 11 cities across the Greater Bay Area, including 125 international players from over 10 countries. This grassroots tournament, rooted in the region&\#x27;s manufacturing workforce since 2002, generated approximately 2.5 billion yuan in tourism and consumption through its ticket-driven economy, engaging 2 million visitors.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** The Foshan "West League" originated as a football tournament for industrial workers in Sanshui District in 2002, inspired by the Spanish La Liga. It has evolved from casual matches on vacant land into a major regional sporting event with professional-grade facilities, leveraging Foshan&\#x27;s position as China&\#x27;s largest manufacturing hub with over 3 million industrial workers.

**「Impact」** The tournament has transformed local sports infrastructure, increasing Sanshui District&\#x27;s football facilities to 159 venues by 2025 \(1.8 per 10,000 residents\), making it a winter training base for 70 professional clubs. The event created a sustainable model for grassroots sports driving regional economic development through tourism and local business engagement.

### [Chinese climbing team targets medals at Aichi-Nagoya 2026 Asian Games](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179006.html) ⭐️ ?/10

China&\#x27;s national rock climbing team is intensifying preparations at the National Mountaineering Training Base in Huairou, Beijing, for the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. The squad, which won two golds at the Hangzhou Games and secured China&\#x27;s first Olympic climbing medals with silver finishes by Deng Lijuan and Wu Peng in Paris, enters the 2026 season with strong momentum, having broken world records in speed climbing at multiple World Cup stops earlier this year. Team leadership has set explicit targets of winning one gold in speed events and one medal in lead/bouldering, relying on data-driven technical analysis and individualized training plans to address both performance and logistical challenges such as humidity and evening finals.

**「Background」** Rock climbing made its Asian Games debut at Hangzhou 2022, with speed, lead, and bouldering contested as combined or separate disciplines. By 2026, the Aichi-Nagoya program expanded to six individual golds: men&\#x27;s and women&\#x27;s lead, men&\#x27;s and women&\#x27;s bouldering, and two mixed team speed relays, reflecting the sport&\#x27;s growing specialization. China&\#x27;s rapid rise stems from focused investment in speed infrastructure and motion-capture analytics after early Olympic breakthroughs highlighted the event&\#x27;s global competitiveness.

**「Impact」** The team&\#x27;s detailed roster selections and medal targets will shape China&\#x27;s expected presence in six climbing events and reinforce the nation&\#x27;s status as a leading speed climbing power in Asia.

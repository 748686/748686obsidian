### [Yan&\#x27;an Road Bridge in Bengbu Achieves Main Span Closure](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/04/content_30179091.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 3, 2026, the main bridge of the Yan&\#x27;an Road Bridge in Bengbu, Anhui province, was successfully closed after the precise hoisting and placement of its closure section steel box girder. As the largest span cable-stayed bridge crossing the Huai River basin, the project marks a critical milestone toward full opening to traffic. Upon completion, the bridge will significantly alleviate local transportation pressure and eliminate the need for residents to take detours to cross the river.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 4, 00:26

**「Background」** Bridge closure refers to the final stage of construction where the last segment of a bridge span is installed to connect both sides, creating a continuous structural system. Cable-stayed bridges like Yan&\#x27;an Road Bridge use tall towers and direct cables to support the deck, making them suitable for crossing wide waterways such as the Huai River.

**「Impact」** The closure enables continued construction toward full通车, which will directly benefit Bengbu commuters by reducing travel time and improving regional connectivity across the Huai River.

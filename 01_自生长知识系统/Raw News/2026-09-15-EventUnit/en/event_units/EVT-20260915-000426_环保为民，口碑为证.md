## Event Overview
On September 15, 2026, *People’s Daily* published a commentary by Yu Jinxing titled “Literature Gains Power by Rooting Itself in the Masses” (《文学，扎根群众才更有力量》), analyzing the implications of the recently concluded “2026 China Literary Festival · Lu Xun Literary Week” in Shanghai. The event marked a strategic shift in the Lu Xun Literature Prize, expanding its scope from a single-day ceremony to a week-long series of activities embedded in community spaces such as bookstores, parks, and museums. The commentary argues that this integration into daily urban life is essential for maintaining the relevance of literature in an era dominated by digital technology, online literature, and micro-drama adaptations, aligning with the goals of the “15th Five-Year Plan” for enhanced cultural quality of life.

## Event Conclusion
The "Return Me Ji Jie" phenomenon is not merely a complaint about acting or writing in the reboot of *Major Case Squad*, but a sociological marker of the evolving power dynamics in China's cultural industry. It illustrates that legal "canon rights" held by production companies are no longer sufficient to guarantee audience acceptance. The event demonstrates that for classic IPs, the "soul" of the character, as defined by the audience's collective memory and emotional investment, acts as an invisible constraint on creative adaptation. The conflict between commercial IP exploitation and audience guardianship of cultural memory is becoming a defining feature of contemporary media consumption.

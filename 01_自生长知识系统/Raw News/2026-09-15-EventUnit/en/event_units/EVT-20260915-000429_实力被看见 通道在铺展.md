## Event Overview

On September 15, 2026, the 2026 China International Fair for Trade in Services (CIFTIS) was held at the Beijing Shougang Park. The event highlighted the expansion of Chinese services overseas, characterized by two key trends: the enhancement of corporate technological strength ("strength being seen") and the development of infrastructure connectivity and service support systems ("channels being opened"). The event featured specialized exhibits on sports technology and logistics, along with a newly established "Overseas Expansion Service Promotion Roadshow Zone" designed to provide one-stop support for Chinese enterprises venturing abroad.

## Core Facts

*   **Event Location and Context:** The 2026 CIFTIS took place at Beijing Shougang Park. The theme for this year's exhibition regarding overseas expansion is "Going Global" (Chuhai).
*   **Sports Technology Display:**
    *   Beijing Ruigai Technology Co., Ltd. showcased intelligent refereeing and match replay systems for table tennis, volleyball, football, tennis, and baseball/softball.
    *   The systems utilize self-developed 3D trajectory tracking algorithms and high-speed visual collection, achieving millimeter-level accuracy.
    *   Innovations include full 3D display for table tennis and an automatic line-judging function for volleyball, replacing previous hand-drawn 3D visuals.
    *   The company has expanded its business to over 20 countries and regions.
*   **Logistics and Infrastructure:**
    *   Beijing International Land Port was highlighted as a key platform for operating regularized, scheduled China-Europe and China-Central Asia international trains.
    *   Its logistics network covers over 20 countries and regions in Asia and Europe, serving as a channel for "Beijing Manufacturing" to reach global markets.
*   **Service Support Matrix:**
    *   A new "Overseas Expansion Service Display Area" was established, gathering dozens of professional institutions in finance, law, accounting, advertising, and human resources.
    *   This area offers a "one-stop" service matrix for risk control, HR support, marketing/logistics, and cultural/financial synergy.
*   **Roadshow Initiative:**
    *   For the first time, CIFTIS established an "Overseas Expansion Service Promotion Roadshow Zone."
    *   There were 40 roadshows during the event period, focusing on HR and logistics services.
    *   Examples of services presented included HR system building by Air China and intelligent warehousing solutions by Thundersoft (Zhongke Chuangda).
    *   Doumi Group, a HR service provider covering over 180 countries, participated for the first time.

## Event Conclusion

The 2026 CIFTIS demonstrated a maturing phase in China's service trade and corporate global expansion. The event moved beyond simple product display to include a robust ecosystem of supporting services, infrastructure, and technology. The "Strength Being Seen" aspect was exemplified by high-tech solutions like Ruigai’s sports AI, while "Channels Being Opened" was reflected in the consolidation of logistics networks and the new one-stop service zones for legal, financial, and HR support. This synthesis suggests that Chinese overseas expansion is becoming less reliant on ad-hoc efforts and more structured, supported by specialized service providers and stable infrastructure corridors.

“十里钢城”变身“会展小镇”: Transformation of Shougang Park into an International Convention Town

## Event Overview

In September 2026, the Beijing Shougang Park (首钢园), historically known as the "Ten-Mile Steel City," successfully hosted the 2026 China International Fair for Trade in Services (CIFTIS/服贸会). This event marked the first year of operation for the newly completed permanent venue at Shougang Park, signifying a pivotal shift from an industrial legacy site to a modern international convention hub. The transformation is part of a broader urban renewal strategy that integrates industrial heritage with contemporary exhibition functions, aiming to establish Shougang Park as a model for expanding service industry openness in China.

## Core Facts

The following facts are derived directly from the source article (ARTICLE #521):

*   **Event Timeline:** The 2026 China International Fair for Trade in Services (CIFTIS) was held at Beijing Shougang Park. It is described as the first exhibition in the "15th Five-Year Plan" opening year.
*   **Milestones:** This marked the sixth year of cooperation between CIFTIS and Shougang Park, and the first year of use for the permanent exhibition venue.
*   **Venue Specifications:**
    *   Total exhibition area: Over 100,000 square meters.
    *   Design Elements: Use of "steel gray," "rust red," and "metal silver" color palettes to blend with industrial aesthetics.
    *   Structural Layout: Halls 1–4 are connected by outdoor corridors to create an experiential visitor flow.
    *   "Fifth Facade" Enhancement: Roofs and terraces of Halls 2, 3, and 4 were converted into green public spaces ("sky gardens") for leisure and observation.
*   **Activity Volume:** In the first half of 2026, Shougang Park hosted 221 events.
*   **Industrial Agglomeration:** As of the end of August 2026, over 1,300 enterprises had moved into the park. These companies are concentrated in digital industries such as gaming, esports, virtual reality (VR), and robotics.
*   **Operational Model:** The park utilizes a "Convention +" model, creating a three-dimensional matrix of commercial facilities, ticket-based economy ("ticket root economy"), and special activities. A smart convention management platform was established for real-time tracking of visitor flow, parking, and traffic.

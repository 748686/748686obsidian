## Event Overview

On September 15, 2026, *People's Daily* published a viewer commentary titled "Refining Realistic Texture Without Neglecting Aspects (Spectator's Voice)" on its Page 20 (Supplement/Arts & Culture). The article critically analyzes the Chinese workplace drama *Early Spring Clear and Bright* (早春晴朗), arguing that while the drama succeeds in depicting the daily life of Beijing white-collar workers (such as shared housing in Tiantongyuan and crowded subways), it fails to maintain realistic continuity in its workplace narratives. The piece suggests that using the workplace merely as a backdrop for romance, with unrealistic details like empty executive offices and rapid promotions, undermines the drama's credibility.

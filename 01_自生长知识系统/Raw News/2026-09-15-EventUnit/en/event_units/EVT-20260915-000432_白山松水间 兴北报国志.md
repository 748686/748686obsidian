## Event Overview
On September 15, 2026, *People's Daily* published a special feature titled "Between White Mountains and Pine Waters: The Will to Serve the Nation from the North" on its 15th page. The article documents a Jilin University educational achievement exhibition held at the Dingxin Library on the eve of the university's 80th anniversary, which is scheduled for September 16, 2026. The exhibition highlights the university's achievements in natural sciences, philosophy and social sciences, and talent cultivation. *   **Event Date:** The exhibition opened "recently" prior to the September 15, 2026, publication date. The university's 80th anniversary is cited as September 16, 2026.
*   **Location:** Jilin University Dingxin Library, Changchun.
*   **Exhibition Scale:** Approximately 1,100 square meters.
*   **Exhibition Structure:** Three main sections: Natural Science Research, Philosophy and Social Science Research, and Talent Cultivation.
*   **Key Scientific Achievements:**
    *   **"Crust No. 1":** A 10,000-meter drilling machine that enabled China to become the third country in the world to master scientific ultra-deep well drilling capabilities at 10,000 meters underground.

    *   **Other Breakthroughs:** Production of 2-inch single-crystal diamonds, digital tire design, precision manufacturing of special optoelectronic devices, high-level chimerism of human hematopoietic immune cells in pigs, robotic hand function rehabilitation training, and wearable multimodal brain function imaging analyzers.

*   **Research Rankings (Reported Data):**

    *   16 disciplines received Grade A ratings in the Ministry of Education's fifth round of discipline evaluation.
*   **Humanities and Social Sciences:**
    *   6 key humanities and social sciences research bases.
    *   2 Ministry of Education philosophy and social sciences laboratories.
    *   Ongoing project: "Oral History of New China's Northeast Industrial Population," involving interviews with elderly workers from the Daqing Oilfield Campaign.
*   **Talent Cultivation Metrics:**
    *   88 national-level first-class undergraduate major construction points.
    *   9 National Basic Discipline Top Student Training Program 2.0 bases.
    *   115 national-level first-class undergraduate courses.
    *   11 achievements selected for the 2022 National Undergraduate Teaching Achievement Award.
    *   5 teacher teams selected for National High-Performance Huang Danian-style Teacher Teams.
*   **Key Figures:**
    *   **Tian Hui:** Secretary of the Jilin University Party Committee.
    *   **Zhang Xi:** President of Jilin University.
    *   **Tian Yipeng:** Professor and Director of the Jilin Provincial Urban Governance Research Base.
    *   **Wang Zhiqiu, Men Jiewei:** Reporters from *People's Daily*.
*   **Strategic Direction:** For the "15th Five-Year Plan" (2026-2030), Jilin University aims to build a world-class university with Chinese characteristics, integrate education, science, technology, and talent, and serve the comprehensive revitalization of Northeast China.

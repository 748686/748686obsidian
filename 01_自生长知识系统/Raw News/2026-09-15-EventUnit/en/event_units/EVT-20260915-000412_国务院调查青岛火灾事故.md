## Event Overview
On September 15, 2026, the State Council of China announced the establishment of a dedicated investigative group for a major fire accident that occurred at China Shipbuilding Group Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding Co., Ltd. The incident took place on September 10, 2026, involving a foreign-owned cargo ship during repair operations. The accident resulted in 25 deaths and 5 injuries. The investigation is being led by the Ministry of Emergency Management and involves multiple central government departments and local authorities, reflecting an "upgraded" level of scrutiny due to the severity of the incident.

## Core Facts
*   **Incident Date and Time:** September 10, 2026, at approximately 11:15 AM.
*   **Location:** China Shipbuilding Group Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding Co., Ltd., Qingdao, Shandong Province, China.
*   **Context:** A foreign-owned cargo ship was docked for maintenance/repair when the fire occurred.
*   **Casualties:** 25 people died; 5 people were injured.
*   **Investigation Body:** The State Council established an "Investigation Group for the '9·10' Major Fire Accident at Shandong Qingdao China Shipbuilding Group Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding Co., Ltd."
*   **Leadership:** The investigation is led by the Ministry of Emergency Management.
    *   **Group Leader:** Song Yuanming, Vice Minister of Emergency Management and Vice Chair of the State Council Work Safety Committee Office.
*   **Participants:** The investigation group includes representatives from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Transport, State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission (SASAC), All-China Federation of Trade Unions, National Fire and Rescue Administration, and the Shandong Provincial Government.
*   **Key Meeting:** The first plenary meeting of the State Council accident investigation group was held on the afternoon of September 14, 2026.
*   **Principles:** The investigation will follow the principles of "scientific rigor, legality and rule of law, seeking truth from facts, and effectiveness" as well as the "Four No-Let-Gos" (四不放过) principle.

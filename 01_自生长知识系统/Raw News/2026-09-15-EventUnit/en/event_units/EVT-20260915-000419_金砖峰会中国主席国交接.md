BRICS Summit: China's Chairmanship Transition in New Delhi

## Event Overview
On September 12–13, 2026, President Xi Jinping attended the 18th BRICS Leaders’ Summit in New Delhi, India, at the invitation of Prime Minister Narendra Modi. During the summit, China formally announced it would assume the BRICS presidency for the following year, marking the fourth time China holds this role. The event centered on the theme "Inclusive Global Governance and Strengthening Multilateralism," with a focus on empowering the Global South and advancing "Grand BRICS Cooperation." Key outcomes included China’s proposals for practical cooperation in artificial intelligence (AI) open-source initiatives, digital industry platforms, and engineer training, as well as high-level discussions on China-India relations and broader global governance reforms.

## Core Facts
*   **Date and Location:** The 18th BRICS Leaders’ Summit was held in New Delhi, India, on September 12–13, 2026.
*   **Key Participants:** Chinese President Xi Jinping and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired/attended the summit. Other leaders present included Egyptian President Sisi and South African President Ramaphosa, among others.
*   **Presidency Transition:** China announced it will host the 19th BRICS Summit and serve as the BRICS President in 2027. This is the fourth time China has held the chairmanship, following 2011 (Sanya), 2017 (Xiamen), and 2022 (Virtual summit).
*   **Strategic Directions:** President Xi outlined four strategic directions ("Four Pioneers") for BRICS: promoting innovation and development, maintaining peace and stability, fostering cultural exchange, and reforming/improving global governance.
*   **Practical Initiatives:** China proposed five specific initiatives:
    1.  BRICS AI Open-Source Pledge/Zone.
    2.  BRICS Special Economic Zone Partnership.
    3.  BRICS Digital Industry Cloud Platform.
    4.  BRICS Engineer Training Alliance.
    5.  BRICS Youth Science and Innovation Exchange Program.
*   **China-India Relations:** President Xi and Prime Minister Modi held a bilateral meeting. Both emphasized viewing their relationship as "partners, not rivals" and "mutual opportunities, not threats." They reaffirmed a commitment to strategic and long-term cooperation based on mutual respect and political wisdom.
*   **Summit Theme:** "Building a Resilient, Innovative, Co-operative and Sustainable BRICS" (whose initial letters spell "BRICS").
*   **Global South Focus:** The summit emphasized the collective rise of the Global South as a key driver of world multipolarity, with China advocating for the "Global South" to strengthen its voice in international affairs.

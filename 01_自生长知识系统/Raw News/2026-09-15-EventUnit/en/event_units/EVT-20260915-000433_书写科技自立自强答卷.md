**Research and Technical Achievements**
*   **Crystal Structure Calculation:** Academician Ma Yanming’s team developed a calculation method named "Calypso" (卡利普索), including software with 14 functional modules and 10 software copyrights, enabling accurate calculation of crystal structures based on chemical components.
*   **Molecular Sieve Materials:** Academician Yu Jihong’s team developed a "theoretical simulation + data mining + high-throughput experiment" route. They created 10 molecular sieve topological structures named "J" (for Jilin University), which were certified and included by the International Molecular Sieves Association.
*   **OLED Materials:** The State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structures and Materials at Jilin University developed green light materials applied in OLED display mass production in 2025. In April 2026, founder Academician Shen Jiacong received the 5th Chinese Chemical Society Lifetime Achievement Award.
*   **Automotive Technology:** Academician Guo Konghui’s team achieved the full domestication of air suspension systems, from key components to the complete system.
*   **Robotics:** Professor Ren Lei developed a bionics-inspired tensegrity robot theory and a dexterous hand with 38 degrees of freedom. This technology was commercialized at Yuequan Bionic Technology, surpassing traditional rigid hands in force, speed, precision, flexibility, and compliance.
*   **Deep Earth Exploration:**
    *   Academician Sun Youhong’s team developed the "Crust No. 1" (地壳一号) 10,000-meter scientific drilling machine, solving issues with low rotational speed and low automation in traditional equipment.
    *   Academician Lin Jun’s team developed an air-land integrated ground exploration technology system, including aviation and ground-air electromagnetic detection and intelligent unmanned platforms.
*   **Remote Sensing:** Academician Lin Jun and Professor Zhang Liu’s team developed "high timeliness, high geometry, high spectral" optical remote sensing technology supporting the "Jilin University No. 1" satellite. The technology was incubated into Suzhou Jitian Xingzhou Space Technology Co., Ltd.
*   **Ultralight Laser Processing:** Jilin University’s ultra-fast laser processing team discovered the optical far-field induced near-field breakdown effect, enabling super-invisible cutting, 3D photonic chips, and femtosecond laser blackening.

**Economic and Industrial Impact**
*   **Automotive Technology Transfer:** The automatic wheel smart chassis technology achieved a technology transfer value of 10.70 million RMB, landing at Changchun Medig Smart Auto Technology Co., Ltd. This resulted in a cumulative new output value of over 50 million RMB and new financing of 53 million RMB, with mass production cooperation established with top domestic car manufacturers.

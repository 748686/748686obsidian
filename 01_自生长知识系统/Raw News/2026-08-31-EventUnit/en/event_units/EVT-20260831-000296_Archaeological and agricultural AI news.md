## Core Facts

1.  **Majiayao Culture Seminar (Zhengzhou, Henan):**
    *   **Date:** August 30, 2026.
    *   **Occasion:** Marking the 105th anniversary of the discovery of Majiayao culture.
    *   **Location:** Zhengzhou, Henan Province, including field trips to Dahecun National Archaeological Site Park and Shuanghuashu Site in Gongyi.
    *   **Participants:** Nearly 100 experts and scholars from archaeological research institutions across China.
    *   **Key Topics:** New archaeological discoveries of the Majiayao period, regional cultural interaction, early civilizational processes, cultural genealogy, cultural exchange, settlement patterns, resources, technology, and jade artifacts.
    *   **Organizers:** Chinese Archaeological Society Neolithic Archaeology Professional Committee, Henan Provincial Cultural Relics Archaeology Society, Zhengzhou Institute of the Source of Chinese Civilization and Songshan Civilization, Henan Provincial Academy of Cultural Relics and Archaeology, and Henan Museum.
    *   **Reporting:** Reported by Zhang Wenhao (Reporter, *People's Daily*).

2.  **Gansu Smart Agricultural AI Service System Launch:**
    *   **Date:** Upgraded/launched around August 30, 2026.
    *   **Location:** Gansu Province.
    *   **Platform:** "12316" Agricultural Hotline upgraded to an AI service system, accessible via a new "Gansu Agricultural Services" (甘肃农服) mini-program.
    *   **Features:**
        *   Dual-channel system: Call center (voice) + mobile mini-program.
        *   Real-time voice transcription, instant knowledge matching, and script recommendations.
        *   Dialect-optimized speech recognition for local accuracy.
        *   "AI initial screening, expert precision diagnosis" workflow: Complex issues are routed to human experts.
    *   **Impact:** Reduced post-call administrative work time from 20-30 minutes to 1-2 minutes.
    *   **Reporting:** Reported by Song Chaojun (Reporter, *People's Daily*).

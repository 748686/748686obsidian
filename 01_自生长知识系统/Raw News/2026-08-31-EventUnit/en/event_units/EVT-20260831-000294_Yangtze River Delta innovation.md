# Event Name
Yangtze River Delta Innovation and Regional Collaborative Synergy

## Event Overview
On August 31, 2026, *People's Daily* published a report titled "Yangtze River Delta Continues to Deepen Collaborative Innovation" as part of its "Vital China Research Row" series. The article details how the Yangtze River Delta (YRD) region—comprising Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Anhui—is implementing cross-regional coordination in science and technology innovation. Driven by directives from General Secretary Xi Jinping to integrate innovation forces and build a technology innovation source, the region is transitioning from physical connectivity to institutional alignment. The report highlights structural shifts in how raw innovation,成果转化 (achievement transformation), and industrial chains are managed across provincial borders.

## Core Facts
*   **Regional Status**: The YRD is identified as one of China's most active economic zones with the highest openness and strongest innovation capacity.
*   **Statistical Data (2025)**:
    *   High-tech enterprises in the YRD account for over 30% of the national total.
    *   Technology contract transactions reached 46,800 items.
    *   Technology transaction volume amounted to 246.13 billion RMB, representing a year-on-year growth of 14.7%.
*   **Infrastructure Sharing**:
    *   The Shanghai Light Source (Shanghai Synchrotron Radiation Facility), a key facility in the YRD large-scale scientific instrument cluster, served nearly 1,000 user units and supported approximately 30,000 experimental projects since opening.
    *   From January to June 2026, operating hours for enterprise users increased by 44.2%, and hours open to the broader YRD region increased by 22.3% compared to the previous year.
    *   The region currently has 16 major scientific and technological infrastructure facilities under construction or completed, with 11 more under construction.
*   **Policy Framework**:
    *   Implementation of the "YRD Science and Technology Innovation Community Joint Attack Cooperation Mechanism."
    *   Enactment of the first collaborative legislation among the three provinces and one municipality: "Decision on Promoting Collaborative Development of Science and Technology Innovation in the Yangtze River Delta."
*   **Industrial Collaboration Models**:
    *   **Biopharma**: Foxcon Bio (信达生物) has R&D extending from Suzhou to Shanghai; it holds 20% of the national monoclonal antibody production capacity. Over 60% of innovation outcomes from Shanghai's Zhangjiang Pharmaceutical Valley are transformed within the YRD.
    *   **New Energy Vehicles (NEV)**: NIO’s second electric drive manufacturing plant in Hefei utilizes smart production lines with over 70% automation. The regional supply chain involves Shanghai (smart driving chips/OS), Jiangsu/Zhejiang (key components), and Anhui (vehicle manufacturing).
    *   **Commercial Satellites**: Starfly Satellite Factory in Taizhou, Zhejiang, produces satellites in 28 days using assembly-line methods derived from automotive manufacturing, reducing costs by over 45% compared to traditional methods. Annual capacity is 500 satellites.
    *   **Shipbuilding**: Yangzijiang Shipbuilding in Jingjiang, Jiangsu, achieved a global completion share of 8.5% in 2025. The model relies on R&D in Shanghai, component support from Jiangsu/Zhejiang/Anhui, and final assembly in Jingjiang.
*   **Innovation Corridors**:
    *   **G60 Science and Technology Innovation Corridor**: Spans nine cities across Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Anhui. High-tech enterprises in these nine cities grew from 16,000 to 59,000 over ten years, accounting for nearly 1/7 of the national total.
    *   **Along Shanghai-Nanjing Industrial Innovation Belt**: Developing in parallel with G60 to form a north-south coordinated innovation pattern.

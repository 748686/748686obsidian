## Event Overview
On November 15, 2026, reports emerged regarding a criminal scam operating in Russia in which fraudsters target the biometric data pages of foreign passports. The scheme involves identity theft or unauthorized copying of passport information. In a significant escalation of this scam, criminals are now reportedly using the stolen data to file baseless lawsuits against their victims. These fraudulent legal claims are intended to suppress evidence, block access to bank accounts, and interfere with the victims' ability to seek justice or protect their assets.

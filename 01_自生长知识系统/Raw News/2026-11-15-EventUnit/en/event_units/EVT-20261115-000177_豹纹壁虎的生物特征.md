*   **Consumer Behavior:** Cost-of-living pressures and interest rate rises have entrenched "deal-hunting behaviour," reshaping how consumers engage with department stores.
*   **Store Counts:** Myer has reduced its store count to 56 (from 60 pre-pandemic). David Jones is closing 19 of its 38 stores.

**2. Article 1: "Passing the Warmth of 'Half a Quilt' with a Paintbrush" (征程如画)**
*   **Author:** Huang Huas san (黄华三), Professor at the School of Art, Renmin University of China.
*   **Subject:** Contemporary artistic responses to the "Half a Quilt" story from the Long March (1934).
*   **Key Works Analyzed:**
    *   **Gao Yi (高毅):** Chinese painting 《Half a Quilt》. Focuses on emotional expression and historical accuracy; used her daughter as a model for the embrace.
    *   **Zhang Guoping (张国平):** Serial watercolor painting 《Half a Quilt》 (16 panels). Uses cold tones for the setting and warm tones for human connection; employs dramatic仰视 (looking up) perspective in the 7th panel.
    *   **Liu Shaoning (刘少宁) & Zhao Shengchen (赵胜chen):** Contrasting styles (complex/heavy color vs. simple/light color) depicting the farewell moment.
*   **Thematic Core:** The story of three female Red Army soldiers sharing and cutting a quilt with villager Xu Jiexiu in Shazhou Village, Rucheng County, Hunan, symbolizing the Party's bond with the people.

**3. Article 2: "Engraving Mountains and Rivers, Sowing the Seeds of Art" (镌刻山河岁月 播撒艺术火种)**
*   **Author:** Ge Yujun (葛玉君), Professor at the Central Academy of Fine Arts.
*   **Subject:** A review of the "Sower" (播种者) exhibition at the Central Academy of Fine Arts Museum, focusing on 100-year-old artist Wu Bi duan (伍必端).
*   **Key Biographical Details:**
    *   Influenced by Tao Xingzhi’s "Life is Education" philosophy.
    *   Studied under woodcut pioneers Chen Yanqiao (陈烟桥) and Wang Qi (王琦).
    *   First batch of warzone art reporters to the Korean War; created famous sketch 《Gun Here》 (枪在这儿).
    *   Created the classic print 《Portrait of Premier Zhou》 (周总理画像) for the cover of 《Selected Works of Zhou Enlai》.
    *   Collaborated with Jin Shangyi (靳尚谊) on 《Chairman Mao with the People of Asia, Africa, and Latin America》.
    *   Founded the first screen printing and illustration studios in China.
    *   Created the symbolic print 《The Sower》 (播种者).

**4. Article 3: "Carving Myriad Realms from a Single Falling Leaf" (一片落叶雕万象)**
*   **Author:** Chen Liming (陈 Liming), Representative Inheritor of Henan Provincial Intangible Cultural Heritage (Chen Family Leaf Carving). Interviewed by reporter Zhang Wenhao (张文hao).
*   **Subject:** The art of Leaf Carving (Ye Diao / Jian Ye).
*   **Historical Context:** Traces origins to the Zhou Dynasty and the典故 (allusion) of "Cutting the桐 Leaf to enfeoff Shu Yu" (剪桐封弟) recorded in 《Lüshi Chunqiu》.
*   **Technique:** Selection of autumn leaves, multi-step processing (soaking, boiling, drying), and carving. Chen developed "gradient shallow carving wet carving technique" (梯度浅雕湿雕技法) to create translucent shading without镂空 (cutting out), blending Chinese painting lines with sketch shading.
*   **Philosophy:** "Tian Ren He Yi" (Harmony of Heaven and Humanity); respecting the natural veins and flaws of the leaf.

**5. Article 4: "Hidden Vitality in Desolate Cold" (荒寒深处藏生意)**
*   **Author:** Shi Xiaoqin (施晓琴), Associate Editor at 《Fine Arts》 Magazine.
*   **Subject:** Analysis of traditional Chinese winter paintings featuring animals.
*   **Works Analyzed:**
    *   **Tang Dynasty:** Diao Guangyin (刁光胤)'s 《Wild Flower and Bamboo Album: Sleeping Rabbit by the Rock Spring》. Features a rabbit huddled in snow, eyes dotting in vermilion.
    *   **Song Dynasty:** 《Snow Bamboo and Cold Birds》. Depicts three sparrows huddling for warmth on frost-laden bamboo.
    *   **Yuan Dynasty:** Attributed to Ge Shuying (葛 Shuying), 《Squirrel图》 (Chestnut Squirrel). Two squirrels with chestnuts, symbolizing autumn harvest and winter storage.
*   **Philosophical Theme:** "Winter Storage" (冬藏) as accumulation and preparation, not silence or death.

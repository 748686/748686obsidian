## Event Overview
In mid-November 2026, preparations for the APEC Leaders' Week in Shenzhen were confirmed as complete. Concurrently, high-level diplomatic activities focused on stabilizing China-US relations and promoting regional cooperation, alongside a major domestic commemoration of Sun Yat-sen's 160th birth anniversary emphasizing national unity. The period was characterized by Xi Jinping's state visit to the US (the first in 11 years) and subsequent diplomatic follow-ups aimed at defining a "constructive strategic stability" relationship.

## Core Facts

**APEC 2026 Shenzhen Hosting**
*   **Event:** The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Week will be held in Shenzhen, China.
*   **Status:** As of November 14, 2026, all preparatory work was reported as complete [ARTICLE #258].
*   **Theme:** The China-hosted APEC year focuses on "Building an Asia-Pacific Community, Promoting Common Prosperity," with priority areas of Openness, Innovation, and Cooperation [ARTICLE #246].
*   **Venue & Logistics:** Shenzhen has upgraded existing facilities with a focus on green empowerment, cultural integration, and technological assistance (AI navigation, translation, smart clearance) [ARTICLE #258].
*   **Infrastructure:** Over 100,000 standardized foreign language signs have been installed across the city. AI translation devices are deployed at ports, airports, and rail transit. Taxis feature bilingual announcements [ARTICLE #258]. Dedicated channels for representatives are set up at ports of entry [ARTICLE #258].
*   **Tourism/Delegation Routes:** Guangdong province offers 218 visit sites and 43 routes; Shenzhen specifically highlights the Qianhai and Nanshan routes [ARTICLE #258].

**China-US Diplomatic Relations**
*   **Leadership Engagement:** President Xi Jinping conducted a state visit to the US in 2026, his first in 11 years. The two leaders met within six months, creating a historical precedent for high-frequency engagement [ARTICLE #246].
*   **Strategic Framework:** Both nations agreed to build a "constructive strategic stability relationship based on respect, fairness, and reciprocity" [ARTICLE #246].
*   **Economic Engagement:** Trade and economic consultations have yielded steady results. US business registration for the upcoming APEC Business Advisory Council (ABAC) summit was the highest among all nations, indicating strong corporate interest in openness [ARTICLE #246].
*   **Technology/AI Cooperation:** The two nations established an AI dialogue and a communication channel for AI incidents. China invited the US to join the World AI Cooperation Organization [ARTICLE #246].
*   **People-to-People Exchange:** Xi announced a new initiative to invite 100,000 US youth to China for exchanges over the next five years, building on the previously achieved goal of the "5 years, 50,000" initiative [ARTICLE #246].
*   **Visa Policy:** China has implemented unilateral visa-free entry for 50 countries and a 240-hour transit visa-free policy for 57 countries, including the US. In the first half of 2026, 22.914 million foreign nationals entered China [ARTICLE #246].

**Commemoration of Sun Yat-sen (Nov 12, 2026)**
*   **Event:** A ceremony marking the 160th anniversary of Sun Yat-sen's birth was held. Xi Jinping delivered a speech emphasizing that resolving the Taiwan issue and achieving complete national reunification is an unshakable historical task of the Communist Party of China and a common aspiration of all Chinese people [ARTICLE #259].
*   **Key Themes:** The speech called for inheriting Sun's spirit, opposing "Taiwan independence" separatist activities, and deepening cross-strait integration. It highlighted that "reunification is the only correct path" and warned against external interference [ARTICLE #259].

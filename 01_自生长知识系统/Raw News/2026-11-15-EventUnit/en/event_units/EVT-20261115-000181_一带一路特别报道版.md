## Event Conclusion
The November 15, 2026, special report in *People's Daily* highlights the 2026 Belt and Road Media Cooperation Forum as a platform for enhancing global south connectivity through media, culture, and enterprise. The event underscores a strategic shift toward "soft connectivity," emphasizing that while infrastructure and trade are vital, mutual understanding requires localized cultural content (music, media, products), transparent use of AI, and direct human engagement. Fujian Province is positioned as a key hub for this exchange, leveraging its historical Maritime Silk Road legacy and upcoming international cultural conferences to promote "Chinese modernization" narratives globally.

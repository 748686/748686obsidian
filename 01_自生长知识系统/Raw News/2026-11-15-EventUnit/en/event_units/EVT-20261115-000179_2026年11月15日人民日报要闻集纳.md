## Event Overview
On November 15, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive collection of key news items (Yao Wen) covering major political, diplomatic, economic, and social developments in China. The event encompasses the preparations for the APEC China Year meetings in Shenzhen, commemorative activities for the 160th anniversary of Sun Yat-sen's birth, progress in the Greater Bay Area and Hong Kong/Macau integration, domestic policy implementations regarding state-owned enterprises and judicial fairness, and livelihood improvements in Tibet and Hainan.

## Core Facts
*   **APEC China Year (Shenzhen):** The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting is scheduled for November 18–19 in Shenzhen. The three priority areas for APEC's "China Year" are "Openness, Innovation, and Cooperation." Vice Premier He Lifeng attended the opening of the fourth APEC Business Advisory Council meeting in Shenzhen. Premier Li Qiang met with Thai Prime Minister Anutin and held a symposium with Chinese and Thai entrepreneurs to discuss bilateral economic cooperation and trade relations upgrading (Source: Articles #248, #252).
*   **Sun Yat-sen Commemoration (160th Anniversary):**
    *   A memorial gathering attended by overseas relatives, friends' descendants, and representatives from Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan paid respects at the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum in Nanjing on November 14. He Baoxiang (Vice Chairman of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference) and Sun Weiren (Sun Yat-sen's great-great-grandson) were present (Source: Articles #249, #250).
    *   The International Academic Symposium on Commemorating the 160th Anniversary of Sun Yat-sen's Birth was held in Zhongshan, Guangdong, on November 14. Nearly 150 experts from over 60 domestic and international research institutions participated. The theme was "Sun Yat-sen and Modern China from a Global Perspective" (Source: Article #253).
*   **Greater Bay Area & Hong Kong/Macau Integration:**
    *   **Hong Kong:** The "First Five-Year Plan for Economic and Social Development of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (2026–2030)" was announced in September 2026. Key projects include the Northern Metropolis development, with traffic infrastructure like the North Metropolis Highway (Xintian section) aiming to shorten commute times. The Hongshuigang University Town is proceeding with applications for institutions to arrive by 2027–2028. The Sha Ling data park aims to provide advanced computing power equivalent to 36 times the current Hong Kong capacity by 2032. Hong Kong also hosted the 33rd APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting on October 20–21 (Source: Article #266).
    *   **Macau:** The University of Macau celebrated its 45th anniversary, highlighting its transition from a "campus without grounds" to an international research university following the authorization of its Hengqin campus. The "Macau Research, Hengqin Conversion" chain is being strengthened through the Hengqin Guangdong-Macau In-Depth Cooperation Zone. Three Macau universities (University of Macau, Macau University of Science and Technology, Macao Polytechnic University) have begun extending their campuses to the cooperation zone, with over 1,450 graduate students enrolling in the first phase (Source: Article #267).
    *   **Cross-Strait Cooperation:** Taiwan fish products (approximately 350 tons of grouper and threadfin bream) arrived in Xiamen, Fujian, for the first time under new facilitation policies announced in April 2026. Xiamen has established a green channel for Taiwanese agricultural and fishery products, reducing clearance time to under 2 hours (Source: Article #270).
*   **Tibet Livelihood Improvements:** Tibet has implemented heating and oxygen supply projects in counties such as Longzi and Cuoqin.民生 expenditure accounts for over 80% of the general public budget. The region has achieved 100% road connectivity for townships and administrative villages. Over 30 employment promotion policies have been issued in the past five years, with 16.291 billion yuan in employment subsidy funds allocated (Source: Article #254).
*   **Hainan Tourism Development:** Hainan is promoting high-quality tourism development through industry integration (culture, sports, tourism, health). In the first three quarters of 2026, Hainan received 1.4279 million inbound tourists, a 43.1% increase year-on-year. The Boao Lecheng International Medical Tourism Pilot Zone received 863,500 medical tourists in the first three quarters, an 84.59% increase (Source: Article #255).
*   **State-Owned Enterprise (SOE) Party Building:** General Secretary Xi Jinping issued important instructions emphasizing that high-quality Party building should lead the high-quality development of SOEs. Key directives include integrating Party leadership into corporate governance, strengthening leadership team construction, and strictly enforcing discipline and anti-corruption measures. Representatives from major SOEs (China Huadian, China Railway, China Mobile, etc.) expressed their commitment to implementing these instructions (Source: Article #251).
*   **Judicial Fairness:** The article highlights the Chinese judiciary's efforts to ensure fairness, including cracking down on telecom fraud (41,000 cases tried in 2025), addressing drunk driving (sentences dropped from 320,000 in 2023 to 231,000 in 2025), and protecting intellectual property rights (a record 640 million RMB compensation awarded in one case). New judicial interpretations on AI-related disputes were also noted (Source: Article #256).
*   **Educational Initiatives:**
    *   **Senior Education:** China has over 320 million people aged 60 and above (as of end-2025).老年大学 (Senior Universities) are promoted as a key response to aging, providing learning opportunities that enhance social integration and personal well-being (Source: Article #262).
    *   **Innovative Talent Cultivation:** Dalian University of Technology is collaborating with high schools to establish "Sci-Tech Innovation Laboratories" under the "Top-notch Plan" outlined in the "Education Powerhouse Construction Planning Outline (2024–2035)." The model involves a central hub at the university and N nodes in high schools, aiming to identify and cultivate innovative talents early (Source: Article #264).

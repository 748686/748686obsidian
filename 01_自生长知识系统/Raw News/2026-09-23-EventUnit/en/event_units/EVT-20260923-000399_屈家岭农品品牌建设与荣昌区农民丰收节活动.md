## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, the main venue for the 2026 Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival in Chongqing Municipality, along with the third Farmers' Farming Sports Meet, was held at Hezhong Island in Qingjiang Town, Rongchang District. The event served as a platform to showcase regional agricultural branding and technological advancement in rural revitalization. A central feature of the reporting surrounding this event was the promotion and strengthening of the "Qu Ling Yuan" (屈岭源) brand, which aims to introduce high-quality agricultural products from the Qujialing area.

The festival activities included an opening ceremony, a brand gala, industrial matchmaking, farming experiences, cultural and tourism integration, and consumer sales exhibitions. The event highlighted Rongchang District's agricultural foundation, which is built upon a 30-billion-yuan industry chain and supported by significant technological investment in livestock, crops, and digital agriculture.

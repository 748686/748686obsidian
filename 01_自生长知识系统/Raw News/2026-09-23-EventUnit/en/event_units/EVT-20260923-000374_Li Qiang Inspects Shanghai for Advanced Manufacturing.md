# Event: Li Qiang Inspects Shanghai for Advanced Manufacturing

## Event Overview

On September 22, 2026, Li Qiang, Member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and Premier of the State Council, conducted an inspection tour in Shanghai. The inspection focused on the development of emerging industries and advanced manufacturing. During the visit, Li Qiang emphasized the need to deeply implement the important instructions of General Secretary Xi Jinping on developing advanced manufacturing and the spirit of the National Advanced Manufacturing Conference. He called for persisting in the direction of intelligence, green development, and integration, promoting the deep fusion of scientific and technological innovation and industrial innovation, and continuously strengthening and expanding advanced manufacturing.

## Core Facts

*   **Date:** September 22, 2026
*   **Location:** Shanghai, China
*   **Key Figure:** Li Qiang (Premier of the State Council)
*   **Participants:** Chen Jining and Wu Zhenglong accompanied the inspection.
*   **Primary Objective:** To inspect the development of emerging industries and advanced manufacturing, with a focus on intelligent manufacturing and future industries.
*   **Key Locations Visited:**
    1.  **Shanghai Electric National AI Application Pilot Base:** Li Qiang observed special robots and industrial robots, listened to reports on "Artificial Intelligence+" integrated service providers and the construction of AI-native factories, and visited the intelligent robot assembly and debugging pilot workshop.
    2.  **Shanghai Future Qi Dian Community:** A sci-tech innovation incubation platform focused on future industries. Li Qiang reviewed the innovation ecosystem, the operation of the Shanghai Future Industry Fund, and observed technology products in fields such as brain-computer interfaces and quantum computing. He also communicated with sci-tech innovation personnel and community staff.
*   **Policy Directives Issued:**
    *   **Intelligent Manufacturing:** Promote "AI + Manufacturing," deepen the integration of manufacturing and digital-intelligent technologies, activate data element potential, and drive comprehensive digital-intelligent transformation across industries. Build more manufacturing production service platforms focusing on industrial design, pilot verification, and intelligent operation and maintenance to help enterprises, especially SMEs, carry out intelligent upgrades efficiently and at low cost.
    *   **Future Industries:** View future industries as a "potential blue ocean" for advanced manufacturing. Strengthen tracking and analysis of future technology development, reinforce key core technology breakthroughs, and lay a solid foundation for basic research. Encourage bold exploration and tolerance for errors. Build a first-class innovation ecosystem, promote deep integration of industry, academia, research, and application, and actively cultivate and open up application scenarios. Develop venture capital, strengthen patient capital, and establish mechanisms for growth in future industry investment and risk sharing.
    *   **Talent:** Emphasize that talent is the primary resource. Strengthen the talent aggregation and training functions of research institutions, innovation platforms, enterprises, and sci-tech programs. Explore new models for cultivating top innovative talents.

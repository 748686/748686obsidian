## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, the People's Daily published coverage and commentary marking the occasion of the 9th Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival. The coverage, spanning news reports, opinion pieces, and visual photography, emphasized the principles of comprehensive rural revitalization, food security, and the construction of harmonious countryside. The event served as a platform to highlight achievements in agricultural production, rural cultural enrichment, and infrastructure development during the opening year of the "15th Five-Year Plan."

## Core Facts

*   **Event Occasion:** The 9th Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival, observed annually on September 23.
*   **Leadership Address:** General Secretary Xi Jinping issued festival greetings and instructions to farmers nationwide and those working in agriculture and rural affairs. He emphasized implementing party central decisions, promoting comprehensive rural revitalization, improving policy effectiveness for agriculture, enhancing agricultural comprehensive production capacity, and building livable and business-friendly harmonious countryside.
*   **Agricultural Production Status ("15th Five-Year Plan" Opening Year):**
    *   **Summer Grain:** Output exceeded 300 billion jin for the first time.
    *   **Early Rice:** Output remained stable at over 56 billion jin for six consecutive years.
    *   **Autumn Crops:** Planting area is expected to increase, with overall growth conditions normal, providing a good foundation for the annual grain harvest.
    *   **Infrastructure Baseline (End of 2025):** High-standard farmland exceeded 1 billion mu; rural sanitation toilet普及率 reached approximately 77%; rural tap water普及率 reached 96%.
*   **Strategic Goals:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" outlines a goal to reach a national grain comprehensive production capacity of approximately 1.45 trillion jin during the plan period.
*   **Cultural & Social Indicators (End of 2025):** There were 14,900 intangible cultural heritage workshops nationwide, driving employment and income for over 1.3 million people.

## Event Overview
On September 21–22, 2026, Ding Zhongli, Vice Chairperson of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (NPC) of China, led a delegation to South Korea at the invitation of the National Assembly of the Republic of Korea. The primary objective of the visit was to attend the 14th Session of the China-South Korea Parliamentary Exchange Mechanism. During the visit, Ding met with South Korean National Assembly Speaker Cho Jung-seok and held discussions with Deputy Speaker Nam In-sun. The event was reported by *People's Daily* on September 23, 2026.

## Event Overview
The 48th WorldSkills Competition was held in Shanghai, China, from September 22 to 27, 2026. The event was hosted by the WorldSkills Organization and co-organized by China's Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the Shanghai Municipal People's Government. The competition's theme was "Skills Change the World, Skills Illuminate the Future" (Chinese: 技能改变世界 技能照亮前程).

The event served as a focal point for high-level diplomatic engagement and domestic policy promotion. Key activities included the presentation of congratulatory messages by Chinese national leadership, pre-competition diplomatic meetings between Chinese government officials and WorldSkills Organization executives, and the attendance of foreign heads of government.

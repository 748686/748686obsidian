## Core Facts

### 1. Xinjiang Yumin Safflower Industry (Source: Article #546)
- **Location:** Jiland Village, Jiangges Township, Yumin County, Xinjiang.
- **Key Figure:** Zhu Changjun (large-scale safflower grower, 400 mu in 2026).
- **Industry Scale:** Full industry chain annual output value exceeds 300 million RMB; stable employment/income for over 5,700 farmers and herdsmen.
- **Acreage:** Stable at 150,000 mu in 2026.
- **Natural Advantages:** Over 3,000 hours of annual sunlight, >15°C diurnal temperature variation, irrigation from Balruke Mountain snowmelt. High accumulation of crocin and linoleic acid.
- **Key Innovation:** "Yumin Thornless Safflower" accounts for 99% of planting area. Developed over decades to solve the "thorny/hard to harvest" bottleneck.
- **Seed Model:** "Free seed supply, seed payment via harvest" (以籽抵种) model initiated in 2024.
- **Infrastructure:** China Thornless Safflower Trading Center built in 2025 with 70 million RMB project funds (91 mu total; includes 3,200 sqm R&D center and 15,000 sqm warehouse).
- **Research:** Shihezi University Safflower Industry Research Institute Yumin Branch (joined with Wenzhou Medical University Academician team). Collected ~60 germplasm resources. Breed "Yuhong No. 1" increased yield by 20-30%.
- **Processing:** Xinjiang Tianding Safflower Oil Co., Ltd. invested 80 million RMB for a 30,000-ton/year automated line (EU organic certified).
- **Market:** Export to Central Asia/Europe via China-Europe freight trains. Signed contracts worth 179 million RMB over three domestic conferences; ~1.55 billion RMB意向 (intent) at Eurasia Expo.
- **Tourism:** 100,000 mu safflower viewing belt; "Yumin Mountain Flower Festival".

### 2. Hubei Xingshan Mine Ecological Restoration (Source: Article #547)
- **Location:** Maocaoping Village, Shuiyuesi Town, Xingshan County, Yichang, Hubei.
- **Key Figure:** Zhao Jiahai (First Secretary, 3rd term, dispatched by Hubei Xingfa Chemical Group).
- **History:** 70+ mines opened in the 1980s; severe ecological damage (body landslides, tailings, polluted Kongzi River).
- **Intervention:** 2007 resource consolidation by Hubei Xingfa Chemical Group.
- **Investment:** ~100 million RMB for goaf treatment; 30 million RMB for historical legacy issues; 40 million RMB for 2.3 sq km geological disaster restoration.
- **Technique:** "Full-stratum mining, separate mining and transport"; "waste rock stays in mine, tailings not piled outside".
- **Outcome:** Shukongping Phosphate Mine入选 (listed) in the Ministry of Natural Resources "National Green Mine List".
- **Livelihood Changes:**
    - Relocation: Mass relocation from 2015 into new concrete housing communities.
    - Employment: 150+ villagers employed locally (e.g., Xu Jinlan, former Guangzhou worker, now shift supervisor; husband drives transport). Combined income ~200,000 RMB/year.
    - Agriculture: Walnuts introduced in 2009, upgraded to amber walnuts. 110 of 216 households planting; ~80,000 jin annual output.
- **Water:** Kongzi River transformed from "discharging mine water" to "nourishing village water".

### 3. Beijing Zhongguancun Academy AI Education (Source: Article #549)
- **Location:** Beijing Haidian District (Zhongguancun Northwest旺 Science Park).
- **Establishment:** Founded 2 years prior to Sept 2026 (approx. Sept 2024) by Ministry of Education and Beijing Municipality.
- **Structure:** No physical wall; connected to entrepreneurial community via skybridge.
- **Students:** 300+ joint-training PhD students from 31 universities.
- **Pedagogy:** "Project first, learn later." Students choose projects freely (not assigned).
- **Key Projects Mentioned:**
    - Zhang Boya (Nankai/UoG joint): Breast tumor intelligent minimally invasive surgery system.
    - Lv Dingyang: MatOS (autonomous agent OS for material R&D), speeding up processes by 4x.
    - Bai Fengshuo: Student entrepreneur rejecting "big factory" job to start own company in self-evolving AI infrastructure.
- **K-12 Integration:** "Beijing Youth Artificial Intelligence Academy (Haidian)" established in 2025.
- **Notable Event:** Nobel Laureate Michael Levitt visited; interacted with a 9-year-old.
- **Stats:** 120+ organized research projects; 40 incubated startups (half are student projects).

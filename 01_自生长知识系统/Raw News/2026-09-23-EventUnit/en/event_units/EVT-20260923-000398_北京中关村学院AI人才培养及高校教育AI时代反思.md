## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, *People's Daily* (Renmin Ribao) published a dedicated education-themed section (Page 14), titled "Education" (教育). This collection of articles addresses the pedagogical and societal challenges posed by the integration of Artificial Intelligence into higher education, the value of vocational training, and the role of university social practice in ideological and moral education. A subsequent advertisement on Page 15 highlights agricultural brand development in Hubei province, which is thematically distinct from the educational discourse on Page 14 but included in the source dataset.

The central theme of the editorial content is the transition of contemporary university students into an "AI-native generation" and the corresponding need to re-evaluate educational goals, emphasizing critical thinking, independent judgment, and practical craftsmanship over rote knowledge acquisition and standard-answer dependency.

## Core Facts

**1. AI Integration in Higher Education (Fudan University Survey)**
*   **Source:** Article #550 ("Don't Be Trapped by 'Asking AI When Unsure'")
*   **Fact:** Fudan University conducted an AI usage survey among its entire incoming class of 2026.
*   **Statistic:** Nearly all surveyed students had used AI tools, and approximately 80% (nearly eight in ten) had formed a usage habit.
*   **Usage Patterns:** Students use AI for text polishing, creating group assignment presentations, translating foreign materials, generating literature reviews, and using AI as a search engine or chat companion.

**2. University Leadership Perspectives on AI**
*   **Source:** Article #550
*   **Peking University President Gao Song:** Stated that while AI can integrate existing information, it cannot replace intrinsic curiosity; while it provides standardized conclusions, it cannot replace independent judgment formed through repeated trial and error.
*   **Westlake University President Shi Yigong:** Cited the classical concept "Junzi bu qi" (a gentleman is not a vessel/tool), arguing that humans must not become tools and that learning is not merely about storing knowledge but building personal logical systems, cognitive frameworks, and value judgments.
*   **Fudan University President Jin Li:** Shared a student's perspective that in the AI era, students should stop obsessing over standard answers and focus on three skills: asking good questions (clearly defining vague needs), practicing judgment (identifying AI errors and selecting appropriate answers), and daring to take responsibility (making decisions under uncertainty).

**3. Academic Warnings on AI in Research**
*   **Source:** Article #550
*   **Fact:** 25 Fields Medalists, including Terence Tao (Tao Zhexuan) and Deng Yu (Deng Yu), issued a joint statement warning that the rapid application of AI to solve mathematical problems may cause a "serious misalignment" between AI development goals and mathematical research goals.
*   **Concern:** The mass generation of mathematical results by AI could compress time allocated for result verification, methodological summary, academic exchange, and citing previous achievements.

**4. Vocational Education and Craftsmanship**
*   **Source:** Article #551 ("Where Welding Sparks Fly, There Is Starlight")
*   **Statistic:** Over the past five years, vocational education has provided more than 70% of the new high-quality, high-skilled talents for modern industries.
*   **Case Study:** Zhao Kongbiao, a welding training teacher at Jiangsu Shipping Polytechnic, emphasized that welding requires repeated practice and overcoming hardship. He noted that a single weld seam relates to the safety of all crew members.
*   **Achievement:** Under his guidance, students won first place in the National Seafarer Skills Competition. His studio has produced over 20 provincial and municipal technical experts and tackled "chokepoint" technical problems such as low-temperature high-strength steel welding.

**5. Social Practice and Ideological Education (Capital Normal University)**
*   **Source:** Article #552 ("Tempering the Original Intention of Teachers in Practice")
*   **Scale:** During the summer vacation of 2026, Capital Normal University organized over 400 practice teams and more than 4,000 teachers and students across the country.
*   **Context:** The activities coincided with the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army's Long March.
*   **Activities:**
    *   **"Yi Qi Qian Xing" Team:** 36 teachers and students from the School of Music, School of Fine Arts, and Institute of Chinese Calligraphy and Culture went to Qixingguan District, Bijie City, Guizhou Province, establishing 13 special clubs in four primary and secondary schools to provide systematic aesthetic education.
    *   **"Qianbei Xunji" Team:** Members from the School of Marxism visited the Zunyi Conference Memorial Hall, Gouba Conference Site, and Loushan Pass to explore Long March stories and conducted theory宣讲 (preaching/explanation) exchanges with Zunyi Medical University.
    *   **Other Initiatives:** Teams in ShiLou County, Shanxi Province, developed long-term rural planning (3-5 years); AI College teams in FangShan District, Beijing, used AI for rural cultural design; collaborations in Kashgar, Xinjiang, promoted standardized teaching and national common language.
*   **Leadership View:** Cao Wenjun, Secretary of the Party Committee of Capital Normal University, defined the "greatness" of a teacher as aligning personal ideals with national needs and the "goodness" as possessing excellent knowledge, virtue, and educational skills.

**6. Agricultural Brand Development (Supplementary Context)**
*   **Source:** Article #554 (Page 15 Advertisement)
*   **Fact:** The Qujialing Management Area in Hubei Province has built the regional public brand "Quling Yuan" (屈岭源), leveraging 5,000-year-old Neolithic farming civilization heritage (specifically carbonized rice grains dated to 5,800 years ago).
*   **Impact:** The brand system has driven the development of 22 provincial and municipal-level agricultural leading enterprises and resulted in sub-brands like "Wusan Deer" and "Wusan Yellow Peach" being included in the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs' 2024 Second Batch of Ancient Farming and Farming Product Memory Index List.

## Cross-Source Verification

*   **Holistic Education Approach:** There is a consistent thread across Articles #550, #551, and #552 regarding the definition of "quality talent."
    *   Article #550 argues for "questioning, judging, and taking responsibility" over standard answers.
    *   Article #551 argues for "craftsmanship and resilience" over theoretical knowledge alone.
    *   Article #552 argues for "social practice and ideological alignment" over classroom-only learning.
    *   *Synthesis:* All three sources collectively support the position that the education system must prioritize human-centric traits (curiosity, ethics, practical skill, social responsibility) that AI or rote learning cannot replicate.
*   **Institutional Sources:** The actors mentioned (Peking University, Westlake University, Fudan University, Capital Normal University, Jiangsu Shipping Polytechnic) are distinct and independently cited, providing no conflicting institutional claims.

    2.  **Strategic Talent Needs:** Linking vocational education directly to "modern industry" and "maritime power" strategies (Article #551), and university practice to "serving the capital" and "national rejuvenation" (Article #552).

## Known Current Impact

*   **Pedagogical Shift:** Universities are actively reframing curricula to emphasize critical thinking and problem-posing over answer-receiving, citing the risk of students falling into "information cocoons" (Article #550).
*   **Academic Caution:** The joint statement by Fields Medalists indicates that top-tier scientific communities are beginning to scrutinize the integrity and pace of AI-assisted research, suggesting potential future regulatory or ethical guidelines for academic publishing.
*   **Vocational Valorization:** There is a sustained effort to elevate the social status of vocational education by highlighting its critical role in industrial supply chains and successful competition outcomes.
*   **Rural Revitalization:** University social practice is being utilized as a mechanism for rural development, providing aesthetic education in remote areas (Guizhou) and technical solutions (AI for rural branding in Beijing).

## What Cannot Currently Be Determined

## Event Conclusion

The September 23, 2026, edition of *People's Daily* (Page 14) presents a cohesive editorial stance on the transformation of Chinese higher and vocational education in the AI era. The core conclusion is that while AI tools are ubiquitous among university students (the "AI-native generation"), the educational system must resist the temptation of standardization and passive consumption. Instead, it must cultivate "human" capabilities that AI cannot replicate: intrinsic curiosity, independent judgment, ethical responsibility, and practical craftsmanship.

This is supported by three distinct narratives:
1.  **Academic Critique:** Top universities (Peking, Fudan, Westlake) and global scientific elites (Fields Medalists) warn against over-reliance on AI for knowledge generation, advocating for "questioning" and "verification" as primary educational goals.
2.  **Vocational Resilience:** The story of vocational educator Zhao Kongbiao illustrates that high-stakes, physical, and iterative skills (welding) remain vital to national industry and require human perseverance.
3.  **Social Praxis:** Capital Normal University's extensive social practice programs demonstrate the state's preference for grounding education in real-world socialist modernization efforts, rural revitalization, and ideological continuity (Long March spirit).

The inclusion of the agricultural brand advertisement (Article #554) further reinforces the theme of integrating traditional cultural heritage and local resources with modern economic and technological standards, mirroring the educational call to blend tradition with innovation.

# Event Name

**Publication of Four Theoretical Articles in *People’s Daily* (September 23, 2026): Agriculture, Party Building, Rule of Law, and AI for Public Welfare**

## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, the theoretical section (Page 09) of *People’s Daily* published four distinct theoretical articles. While originating from the same issue and editorial team, these articles address four separate policy and ideological themes: agricultural technological innovation, Party building theory, the rule of law, and the integration of artificial intelligence with public welfare.

The articles were authored by academics and experts associated with leading Chinese research institutions (China Academy of Agricultural Sciences, Tsinghua University) and party理论研究 (theoretical research) circles. The publication coincided with the Ninth "Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival" (Qiu Fen) and aligned with the strategic directives outlined in the "15th Five-Year Plan" outline regarding agricultural modernization and the "Artificial Intelligence+" action.

## Core Facts

**1. Publication Context**
*   **Date:** September 23, 2026.
*   **Outlet:** *People’s Daily* (Renmin Ribao), Page 09 (Theory Section).
*   **Editors:** Kang Yan, Song Yu, Wang Bo.
*   **Occasion:** The timing coincides with the Ninth Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival (Sept 23), upon which General Secretary Xi Jinping sent congratulatory messages emphasizing "solidly advancing comprehensive rural revitalization."

**2. Article Themes and Key Arguments**

*   **Article A: Agricultural Technological Innovation**
    *   *Title:* "Supporting the Construction of a Strong Agricultural Nation with Agricultural Scientific and Technological Innovation"
    *   *Author/Source:* China Academy of Agricultural Sciences (CAAS).
    *   *Key Argument:* Agricultural science and technology is the key tool for building a strong agricultural nation and ensuring food security.
    *   *Statistics Cited:* China’s arable land accounts for 9% of the world total; per capita arable land is less than 40% of the world average; per capita freshwater resources are 35% of the world average. Smallholder households account for over 98% of agricultural business entities, with an average scale of 7.8 mu.
    *   *Policy Reference:* The "15th Five-Year Plan" outline includes a dedicated section on accelerating agricultural and rural modernization.
    *   *Strategic Goal:* Achieve high-level scientific and technological self-reliance in agriculture. Move from "single breakthroughs" to "systematic operations" in innovation.

*   **Article B: Party Building Thought**
    *   *Title:* "Understanding the Essence and Key Points of Xi Jinping’s Party Building Thought from an Essential Perspective"
    *   *Author:* Li Hongfeng.
    *   *Key Argument:* Xi Jinping’s Party Building Thought answers the era’s major question: "What kind of long-ruling Marxist party to build, and how to build it."
    *   *Framework:* The article structures its analysis around three pillars:
        1.  *Theoretical Origins:* Integration of Marxist party-building theory with Chinese reality and excellent traditional Chinese culture ("Two Integrations").
        2.  *Practical Foundation:* The macro exploration of comprehensive strict governance of the Party in the new era.
        3.  *Ideological Connotation:* Centered on the "Fourteen Persists," integrating party building with national rejuvenation, upholding integrity while innovating, and unifying epistemology with methodology.

*   **Article C: Rule of Law Confidence**
    *   *Title:* "Theoretical Innovation of the Important Proposition of 'Firmly Establishing Confidence in the Rule of Law'"
    *   *Source Material:* Excerpt from *Guangming Daily* (August 7, 2026), Author: Zhou Youyong.
    *   *Key Argument:* "Rule of law confidence" is a key component of China’s new-era rule of law discourse. It represents a leap from institutional construction to cultural identity and spiritual belief.
    *   *Significance:* It reflects the independent and autonomous nature of socialist rule of law with Chinese characteristics, moving from passive alignment with international norms to autonomous construction. It serves as a spiritual force for internal consensus and external global governance participation.

*   **Article D: AI and Public Welfare**
    *   *Title:* "Strengthening the Deep Integration of Artificial Intelligence with Ensuring and Improving People’s Livelihood"
    *   *Author:* Liu Zhiyuan (Tenured Professor, Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University).
    *   *Key Argument:* AI development must be people-centric and benevolent, serving the goal of creating a better life.
    *   *Applications:*
        *   *Productivity:* AI empowers new quality productive forces. Example: As of Q1 2026, 197卓越级 (excellent-level) smart factories were built in the equipment industry, with defective rates down 48.4% and efficiency up 22.3%.
        *   *Public Services:* AI assists in education (e.g., Tsinghua’s MAIC multi-agent education platform for personalized learning) and healthcare (intelligent assisted diagnosis for grassroots doctors).
    *   *Governance:* Emphasizes safety, reliability, and controllability. Warns against algorithmic bias, hallucinations, and privacy leaks. Calls for scientific evaluation of AI applications based on actual livelihood benefits rather than sheer scale or compute power.

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives

*   **Domestic Focus:** The entire set of articles is produced within the Chinese state media apparatus (*People’s Daily*, *Guangming Daily*). The perspective is inherently internal, focusing on national strategy, self-reliance, and the governance capabilities of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP).
*   **International Comparison:** Article #537 explicitly compares China’s agricultural conditions with Canada and the Netherlands to justify why China cannot simply copy foreign models. It also references US and EU agricultural tech strategies as benchmarks for global competition.
*   **Global Governance:** Article #539 (Rule of Law) and #540 (AI) frame Chinese domestic policies as contributing to global governance (e.g., "Belt and Road," "Community with a Shared Future for Mankind," "Global AI Governance"), presenting China as an active participant and standard-setter.

## Information Differences and Conflicts

*   **No Conflicts Detected:** The sources do not contradict each other. They cover separate domains.
*   **Dependency Note:** Article #539 is not an original *People’s Daily* article but an excerpt/republication from *Guangming Daily*. This is a common practice in Chinese state media for cross-promotion of theoretical debates. The source material for the "Rule of Law" content is definitively Zhou Youyong’s *Guangming Daily* piece, not an original *People’s Daily* author.

## Known Current Impact

*   **Policy Guidance:** The articles serve to reinforce and disseminate the theoretical framework for the upcoming "15th Five-Year Plan" implementation, specifically in agriculture and AI.
*   **Academic Mobilization:** By publishing in top-tier theoretical journals, the state signals priority areas for academic and institutional research (e.g., CAAS’s "1234" work system, Tsinghua’s AI education initiatives).
*   **Public Narrative:** The timing around the Harvest Festival links agricultural technology directly to national pride and festive celebration, aiming to boost public support for rural revitalization and tech-driven farming.

## Event Conclusion

On September 23, 2026, *People’s Daily* released a coordinated set of four theoretical articles aimed at reinforcing key strategic pillars of Chinese governance: agricultural self-reliance, party consolidation, legal confidence, and benevolent AI. These articles collectively argue that technological and institutional innovation must be subservient to national security and public welfare. The event highlights the Chinese state’s strategy of using theoretical discourse to legitimize and guide the "15th Five-Year Plan" priorities, emphasizing indigenous innovation (in agriculture and AI) and ideological consistency (in party building and rule of law).

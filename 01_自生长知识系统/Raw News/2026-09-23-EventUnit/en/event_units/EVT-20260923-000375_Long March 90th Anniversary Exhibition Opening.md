## Event Overview

On the morning of September 22, 2026, the opening ceremony for the thematic exhibition titled "The Great Expedition—Commemorating the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March" was held in Beijing. The event took place at the Chinese People's Revolution Military Museum. Cai Qi, a member of the Politburo Standing Committee of the CPC Central Committee and Secretary-General of the Central Secretariat, delivered a speech and officially declared the exhibition open.

The exhibition serves as a platform for ideal and belief education, patriotism education, and revolutionary tradition education, aiming to guide cadres and the masses to remember the exploits of the Party and the Red Army and to carry forward the great Long March spirit on the new journey toward Chinese modernization.

# EVT-20260923-000373: US-China Friendship and Public Support for Cooperation

## Event Overview
In the context of the upcoming state visit by Chinese President Xi Jinping to the United States, two distinct but thematically linked developments were reported on September 23, 2026. First, Chinese state media published feature stories from Maquating, Iowa, highlighting the long-term personal friendships between President Xi and American local residents, as well as the impact of the "5 Years, 50,000" student exchange initiative. Second, a survey released by the Chicago Council on Global Affairs indicated that 57% of the US public supports friendly cooperation and engagement with China, marking a continued trend of shifting public opinion since 2024.

## Core Facts

**1. Iowa Residents' Perspectives on Xi Jinping's Upcoming Visit**
*   **Context:** Reporters from *People’s Daily* visited Maquating, Iowa, ahead of President Xi’s state visit, interviewing Americans who have known him for over 40 years.
*   **Sarah Lantian (88 years old):**
    *   Met President Xi in 1985 when he was County Party Secretary of Zhengding, Hebei Province.
    *   Reunited with him in 2012 (when he was Vice President) and November 2023 (in San Francisco).
    *   Credited the "5 Years, 50,000" initiative (announced in Nov 2023) with transforming opportunities for local youth.
    *   Facilitated the participation of over 20 Maquating High School students in the first batch of exchanges to China (Jan 24–30, 2024), visiting Beijing, Hebei, and Shanghai.
    *   Views: Heads of state face-to-face communication is irreplaceable for stabilizing relations; advocates for US-China cooperation on AI governance rules; hopes youth exchanges will consolidate the public opinion foundation of bilateral relations.
*   **Luca Belong (Member, Iowa China Friendship Committee Board):**
    *   Accompanied President Xi’s 1985 delegation on farm visits.
    *   Notes that President Xi remembers details from 41 years ago, reflecting deep value placed on friendship.
    *   Views: Expects meaningful consultations, increased direct flights, and expanded personnel exchanges; believes cooperation benefits both sides and future generations.
    *   Noted that parents of participating students spontaneously contacted him to express gratitude to President Xi and the Chinese people.
    *   Received a reply and New Year’s greeting card from President Xi in February 2026 after sending blessings on behalf of Iowa friends.
*   **Dworczak (Homeowner, 2911 Bonnie Street, Maquating):**
    *   Hosted President Xi in 1985; their home is now known locally as the "US-China Friendship House."
    *   Views: Sincere friendship can withstand political differences; head-to-head communication reduces miscalculation risks and provides a stable framework for cooperation; calls for normalized youth exchanges and continued economic/humanitarian ties.

**2. US Public Opinion Survey Data**
*   **Source:** Chicago Council on Global Affairs report released September 21, 2026.
*   **Finding:** 57% of Americans believe the United States should pursue friendly cooperation and engagement with China.
*   **Trend:** This figure continues a gradual shift toward engagement observed since 2024.
*   **Publication:** Reported by Xinhua and featured in *People’s Daily* on September 23, 2026.

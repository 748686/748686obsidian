## Core Facts

### 1. Guizhou Moutai Quality Management
*   **Context:** During the national "Quality Month" in September 2026, Guizhou Moutai publicized its commitment to quality, framing it as the "soul of life."
*   **Philosophy:** The enterprise adheres to the principle of "revering the basics, abiding by the way, preserving aged brew, and not selling new wine." Production follows traditional timing (dragon head water for making qu in early summer, sand falling for brewing in double ninth festival).
*   **Process:** A single bottle requires at least five years from raw material input to finished product. The system involves five major processes, 30 procedures, and 165 process links.
*   **Management Model:** Moutai employs a matrix quality management model featuring a Chief Quality Officer system. This includes vertical multi-level safety responsibility systems and horizontal full-process quality control from "good seeds" to "products."
*   **Modernization:** The company utilizes machine learning to construct four quality evaluation models for brewing engineering and applies microbial genomics, flavor chemistry, and metabolic analysis to optimize standards.
*   **Strategic Shift:** In 2026, Moutai launched a "Full Towards C" (consumer-centric) strategy, extending quality management from production to the entire supply chain, including design, procurement, and after-sales service.
*   **Awards:** Moutai received the National Quality Award in 2003, 2011, and 2023. In June 2024, it won the 2023 EFQM Global Award (Seven Diamonds) and the "Inspiring Culture" outstanding achievement award.

### 2. Tajik Wedding on the Pamir Plateau
*   **Location:** Wangxidi Village, Tashkurgan Tajik Autonomous County, Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, China.
*   **Event:** A wedding featuring Xiao Gongni Longjik, a fourth-generation border guardian and deputy director of the Paiyek Border Police Station stationed in the Wakhan Corridor.
*   **Family Legacy:** The family has a four-generation tradition of border service:
    *   **Great-grandfather:** Abuduke Limu al-Bik, who served as a guide for the PLA and built watchposts (First Generation).
    *   **Grandfather:** Kadre Abuduke Limu, the prototype for the soldier Amir in the film *Ice Mountain Combat*. He served in the PLA starting in 1951, was wounded but completed his mission, received a First Class Merit, and later carved family instructions ("Follow the Party," "Guard the Border," "Love the Country") on stones at an altitude of 4,700 meters in the Wakhan Corridor.
    *   **Father:** Longjik Kadre, served in the PLA for over 30 years and as an obligatory border guard for 18 years after retirement. Awarded "Most Beautiful Veteran" nationally in 2021.
    *   **Current Generation:** Xiao Gongni Longjik and his brother Abdul Jami serve as the fourth generation of border guards.
*   **Cultural Customs:**
    *   The wedding featured the traditional Tajik eagle dance, characterized by shoulder shaking and imitating eagle movements.
    *   **Betrothal Gifts:** The custom requires 12 sets of bridal outfits as part of the betrothal gifts instead of monetary gifts.
    *   **Attire:** Brides wear red bridal outfits for a full year after marriage.
*   **Author:** Tian Wenxian.

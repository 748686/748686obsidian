## Core Facts

*   **Subject Craft:** Lan County Dough Figurine (岚县面塑), a folk art with over a century of history, involving kneading, fermenting, steaming, and painting dough using natural vegetable colors (pumpkin for yellow, dragon fruit/beet for red, spinach for green).
*   **Key Figures:**
    *   **Yuan Jianhua (袁建花):** 62 years old, National Representative Inheritor of Lan County Dough Figurine, 7th generation craftsman. She has been based in the Dough Figurine Street for 6 years. She focuses on ancient methods and traditional民俗 (folk custom) themes.
    *   **Liu Lili (刘丽丽):** Daughter of Yuan Jianhua, Provincial Representative Inheritor. She focuses on innovative designs that align with contemporary aesthetics and cultural tourism market demands.
*   **Geographic Location:** Yijuyuan North Street, Lan County, Lvliang City, Shanxi Province.
*   **Industrial Development:**
    *   In 2020, Lan County established a non-heritage industrial cluster comprising a Dough Figurine Street, theme park, and cultural exchange center.
    *   In 2023, Lan County Dough Figurine was included in the Shanxi Province Public Whole-Skill Enhancement Project and awarded the title of municipal labor brand.
*   **Economic Impact:**
    *   The street features 14 workshops.
    *   Cumulative impact: Driven income increase for 656 households; over 2,400 training sessions delivered.
    *   Annual training: 600 free skill training participants and 150 practical skills trainees.
    *   Revenue: Top-tier workshops generate 300,000–400,000 RMB annually; ordinary workshops generate approximately 100,000 RMB annually.
    *   Total county industry output value: Nearly 10 million RMB.
*   **Product Categories:** Four mature categories have formed: Folk Customs, Sacrificial/Ritual, Wedding/Birthday Celebrations, and Creative Cultural/Tourism Scenes.
*   **Historical Milestone:** Lan County Dough Figurine was selected into the National Intangible Cultural Heritage Representative Project名录 in 2014.
*   **Cultural Significance:** The craft is traditionally used throughout the lifecycle of Lan County residents, from birth (full moon/100 days) and marriage to death.

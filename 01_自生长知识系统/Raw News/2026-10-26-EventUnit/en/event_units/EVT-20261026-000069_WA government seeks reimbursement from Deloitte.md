# Event Name

Western Australian Government Seeks Reimbursement from Deloitte

## Event Overview

On October 26, 2026, the government of Western Australia initiated legal proceedings against the professional services firm Deloitte. The lawsuit seeks financial reimbursement for costs incurred by the state. This action follows the revelation that Deloitte failed to disclose a prior security breach at the same organization it had been contracted to audit.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Event:** October 26, 2026.
*   **Jurisdiction:** Perth, Western Australia.
*   **Plaintiff:** The State of Western Australia (Government).
*   **Defendant:** Deloitte (professional services firm).
*   **Legal Venue:** Supreme Court of Western Australia.
*   **Objective of Litigation:** Recovery of "tens of millions" of dollars incurred in investigating and remediating data security issues.
*   **Underlying Cause:** Deloitte admitted to a data breach affecting the WA government but did not report this breach to the government at the time it occurred.
*   **Discovery Timeline:** The WA government became aware of the unreported breach in January 2024 while conducting its own investigation into security concerns.

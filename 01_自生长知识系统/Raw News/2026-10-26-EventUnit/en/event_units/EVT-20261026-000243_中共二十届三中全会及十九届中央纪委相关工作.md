## Event Overview

On October 26, 2026, *People's Daily* published a series of high-profile articles and commentaries centered on the Communist Party of China's (CPC) internal governance, ideological consolidation, and administrative expansion. The events span two primary clusters: (1) the conclusion of the seventh round of inspections by the 20th Central Committee and the commencement of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee to study comprehensive strict party governance; and (2) the establishment of the National Data Group, a new state-owned enterprise focused on data element utilization. These events are framed within the context of the 105th anniversary of the CPC's founding, the 90th anniversary of the Long March victory, and the opening year of the "15th Five-Year Plan."

## Core Facts

**1. Party Governance and Inspection Conclusion**
*   **Inspection Completion:** The seventh round of inspections by the 20th Central Committee has completed its feedback phase. This is described as part of the ongoing effort to create a new situation in party management and governance in the new era.
*   **Fifth Plenary Session:** The Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee is scheduled to convene from October 26 to 29, 2026, in Beijing.
*   **Session Agenda:** The session will study major issues regarding "perseveringly advancing comprehensive strict party governance" (持之以恒推进全面从严治党).
*   **Ideological Framework:** The discourse emphasizes Xi Jinping's thought on party building, highlighting the "two establishments" (两个确立) and "two safeguards" (两个维护) as fundamental to the party's political leadership, ideological guidance, mass organization, and social appeal.
*   **Historical Context:** The commentary draws parallels between the current era and the Zunyi Conference (1935), citing it as a "life-and-death turning point" that established Mao Zedong's core leadership. It also marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March and the 105th anniversary of the CPC.

**2. Establishment of the National Data Group**
*   **Entity:** The National Data Group (国家数据集团) was established on October 20, 2026, in Xiong'an New Area, Hebei Province.
*   **Nature:** It is a newly created central state-owned enterprise (SOE) with the primary responsibility of data resource development and utilization.
*   **Ownership:** It is a wholly state-owned company jointly funded by the State Council, the Xiong'an New Area Management Committee, China Telecom, China Unicom, China Mobile, State Grid, and China Southern Power Grid.
*   **Strategic Role:** The group aims to connect, govern, and revitalize high-quality data resources (described as "rich mines") from shareholders, such as operator communication data and grid operational data. It seeks to build a national integrated data infrastructure, lower data circulation costs, and support the development of artificial intelligence and the digital economy.
*   **Location Rationale:** Xiong'an is selected due to its status as an "intelligent native city" of the AI era, with a digital foundation ("Xiong'an Eye" computing center) containing over 40 billion data entries and over a million全域感知 terminals.

**3. Economic and Administrative Indicators**
*   **Shipbuilding:** In the first three quarters of 2026, the three major shipbuilding indicators (output value, completions, and new orders) showed comprehensive growth.
*   **Trade:** In the first three quarters, China's goods trade imports and exports grew by 18.8% year-on-year, the fastest growth rate in five years, with consecutive months of double-digit growth.
*   **Leadership Change:** Zhao Chenxin (赵辰昕) was appointed Acting Governor of Hebei Province.
*   **Foreign Relations:** President Xi Jinping exchanged congratulatory messages with King Philippe of Belgium on the 55th anniversary of diplomatic relations.

## Event Conclusion

The events of October 26, 2026, represent a convergence of political consolidation and economic strategic deployment by the CPC. The conclusion of the central inspection round and the preparatory phase for the Fifth Plenary Session underscore the party's priority on internal governance and disciplinary compliance. Simultaneously, the launch of the National Data Group signals a significant step in leveraging data as a critical factor of production to drive economic growth and technological advancement, particularly in AI. These actions are framed within a broader narrative of historical continuity, drawing on the legacy of the Long March and the Zunyi Conference to legitimize current policies and mobilize support for the "15th Five-Year Plan" objectives.

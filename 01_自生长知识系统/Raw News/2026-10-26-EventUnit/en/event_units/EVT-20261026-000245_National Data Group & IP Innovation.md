# National Data Group & IP Innovation

## Event Overview
On October 26, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Issue No. 2) published a coordinated series of articles covering two primary themes: the strategic role of new central state-owned enterprises (央企) in releasing data element value, and the critical role of intellectual property (IP) in driving pharmaceutical innovation. The event coincides with the opening of the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (October 26–29, 2026), which focuses on comprehensively advancing strict Party governance.

## Core Facts

### 1. Intellectual Property as an Innovation Driver
*   **Macro Data:** In the first three quarters of 2026, investment in IP products in China grew by **9.3%** year-on-year, accounting for a **2.4 percentage point** increase in the share of total investment compared to the same period last year.
*   **Corporate Case Study (Haichuang Pharmaceutical):**
    *   **Location:** Chengdu, Sichuan.
    *   **Strategy:** The company concentrates resources on core pipelines, specifically supporting clinical advancements for new indications of innovative drugs and follow-up development of main products.
    *   **IP Investment:** As of the report date, Haichuang has filed over **300** Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) and Chinese invention patents, with **130+** authorized.
    *   **Patent Term Extension:** An innovative drug launched the previous year has compound patent validity until 2032, with a potential extension of up to **5 years** under relevant regulations. This "time dividend" allows the company to target earlier-stage patient populations (e.g., early high-risk biochemically recurrent prostate cancer).
    *   **Team Structure:** The R&D team consists of over 100 people. It includes a specialized IP team (patent agents and legal counsel) embedded in the pipeline to conduct freedom-to-operate searches, prevent leakage of clinical data, and monitor competitor patents.
    *   **Incentives:** Compensation includes project bonuses and equity incentives tied to clinical progress and patent outputs.

### 2. Pharmaceutical IP Conversion Challenges
*   **Expert Analysis (Shao Zhenhua, Deputy Director of the National Key Laboratory for Biotherapy, West China Hospital, Sichuan University):**
    *   **Current Gap:** While patent numbers are growing rapidly, high-quality conversion remains a短板 (shortcoming).
    *   **Issues Identified:** Late patent layout, unsystematic claim protection, disconnect between patents and product development, and capital market divergence that ignores long-term investment in clinical validation and pilot industrialization.
    *   **Recommendations:** Investment should focus on "early, small, long-term, and hard tech." Universities and research institutions must establish conversion awareness earlier, considering patent quality and industrial paths during the R&D stage.

### 3. Political Context: 20th CPC Central Committee Fifth Plenary Session
*   **Event:** The Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th Central Committee was held in Beijing from **October 26 to 29, 2026**.
*   **Focus:** Studying major issues regarding the persistent advancement of strict Party governance throughout the Party.
*   **Significance:** Marked as a profound plan for governing the Party and strengthening the Party in the new era, aiming to provide strong guarantees for the "15th Five-Year Plan" opening period and socialist modernization.

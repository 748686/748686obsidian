## Event Name

## Event Overview
On or before October 25, 2026, an earthquake occurred in Baqing County (巴青县), Nagqu City, Tibet Autonomous Region, China. In the aftermath, rescue and relief operations were underway, with a focus on emergency resettlement of affected residents into temporary housing structures.

## Core Facts
- **Location:** Baqing County (巴青县), Nagqu, Tibet Autonomous Region, China.
- **Date of Key Development:** October 25, 2026.
- **Primary Actor:** China Railway (中国中铁).
- **Action:** Construction of emergency resettlement prefabricated板房 (temporary housing units).
- **Capacity:** A total of 500 sets of emergency安置板房 were planned for construction in玛如乡 (Maru Township), Baqing County.
- **Progress (as of Oct 25):** Approximately 200 units had been completed by October 25.
- **Completion Timeline:** Full completion was expected by October 27, 2026, with centralized relocation of affected residents scheduled for October 28, 2026.
- **Housing Standards:** Each unit is 18 square meters, built to withstand winter conditions ("保暖、防风、可过冬"), and equipped with electricity, lighting, heating, and cooking facilities.
- **Utilities:** Power supply was restored to the玛如乡二村 resettlement site on October 25 at 20:00, attributed to efforts by State Grid Tibet Electric Power Company (国网西藏电力).
- **Status:** Partially affected residents had moved into the temporary housing by October 25; subsequent resettlement efforts were being advanced.

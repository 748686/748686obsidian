# Event Name
Commemoration of the 90th Anniversary of the Long March Victory: Letter Stories and Documentary Broadcast

## Event Overview
On October 15, 2026, media reports highlighted events surrounding the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army. The coverage, published in *People's Daily* (Page 4), focused on two main elements: the preservation and exhibition of ten historical letters at the Fourth Crossing of Chishui River Memorial Hall, illustrating the bond between the Red Army and local civilians; and the premiere of a new seven-episode television documentary titled *The Great Expedition* (*Weida Yuanzheng*), approved by the Central Military Commission.

## Core Facts

**1. The Ten Letters and the Story of Huang Wanxian**
*   **Artifact Origin:** The Fourth Crossing of Chishui River Memorial Hall (Sichuan/Guizhou region context) houses ten letters spanning 24 years, donated in 2019 by Zhang Degao (son of Zhang Zhixian).
*   **Key Historical Figure:** Huang Wanxian, an old Red Army soldier from Funian County, Fujian Province.
*   **Event Narrative:** During the Battle of Qinggangpo (January 28, 1935), Huang Wanxian was shot in the collarbone and collapsed near a haystack owned by local villager Zhang Xian'an. Zhang's son, Zhang Zhixian, and over ten other villagers rescued him, protected him from local militia ("white terror"), hid him in an empty grave, and transferred him to relative Yuan Cheng'an (who possessed medical knowledge) for treatment.
*   **Correspondence:** Huang Wanxian recovered by early 1936 and returned to Fujian. After initial attempts to write thank-you letters failed due to unclear addresses, he successfully contacted the Zhang family in 1965. The ten letters document this relationship from 1965 to 1989.
*   **Bequest:** In his final will, Huang Wanxian instructed his son, Huang Guanrong, to send money (90 yuan to the Zhang family, 60 yuan to the Yuan family) as a gesture of gratitude.
*   **Recent Activity:** In late 2026 (prior to Oct 15), the "Third New Era Youth 'Retracing the Long March' Activity" was held in Zunyi, Guizhou, where over 300 participants, including youth representatives and descendants of old Red Army soldiers, visited the memorial hall.

**2. Broadcast of Documentary *The Great Expedition***
*   **Production:** Approved by the Central Military Commission (CMC). Jointly produced by the Political Work Department of the CMC, the Party History and Literature Research Institute of the CPC Central Committee, the National Radio and Television Administration, and China Media Group (CMG).
*   **Schedule:** Premiered on October 15, 2026. Broadcast daily at 8:00 PM on the CCTV-1 (General Channel) prime time slot. Repeats on the National Defense and Military Channel. Simultaneous online distribution via major central news websites, portal sites, and the "Xuexi Qiangguo" platform.
*   **Structure:** The documentary consists of 7 episodes, each approximately 50 minutes long. The titles are:
    1.  *Wending* (Asking the Way/Seeking Direction)
    2.  *Dingxiang* (Orientation)
    3.  *Tieliu* (Iron Stream)
    4.  *Xinyang* (Faith)
    5.  *Panshi* (Bedrock)
    6.  *Genji* (Foundation)
    7.  *Fengbei* (Monument)
*   **Themes:** The documentary aims to highlight the spirit of the Long March, the loyalty of the People's Army to Party command, and connect these historical events to contemporary goals of national rejuvenation and military modernization (specifically the "centenary goal" of the military).

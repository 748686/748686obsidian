# Event Name
Implementation of "Contract Canteen" (He Yue Shi Tang) for Wedding and Funerary Banquets in Guizhou as a Vehicle for Changing Social Customs

## Event Overview
In Bailuoging Subdistrict (Bai Guo Jing Jie Dao), Kaili City, Qiandongnan Miao and Dong Autonomous Prefecture, Guizhou Province, local authorities have established a community-based venue termed the "Contract Canteen." This initiative aims to address the financial and social burdens associated with traditional rural weddings and funerals. By standardizing banquet costs and providing professional services, the program seeks to reduce the "face" (mianzi) pressure that traditionally drives excessive spending, thereby promoting social etiquette reform (yi feng yi su).

The initiative is highlighted in a report by the *People's Daily* on October 15, 2026, citing original reporting by a Guizhou Daily journalist.

## Core Facts

**1. Operational Model**
*   **Venue:** The community repurposed idle sites to create the "Contract Canteen."
*   **Services Provided:** The facility is described as spacious and fully equipped with cookware and tableware, staffed by professional chefs.
*   **Cost Structure:** The reported cost for wedding banquets is 300 RMB per table.
*   **Governance:** The specific scope of allowed banquets, size limits, and expenditure caps are discussed and established through community meetings ("mass meetings").

**2. Reported Outcomes**
*   **Case Study:** Resident Pan Mingying (Pan Ming Ying) hosted a wedding with 20 tables at a total cost of 6,000 RMB.
*   **Comparison:** Pan cited previous local customs requiring 4-5 days of preparation, elaborate food and drink standards, tent rental, and borrowed furniture, costing tens of thousands of yuan.
*   **Impact:** Since its launch in 2024, the canteen has reportedly saved residents significant expenses.

**3. Key Actors**
*   **Long Wenbin (Long Wen Bin):** Deputy Director of the Community Neighborhood Committee, who promoted the service to residents.
*   **Xu Yehai (Xu Ye Hai):** Secretary of the Party Working Committee of Bailuoging Subdistrict, who identified the core challenge as setting "standards" for banquets.
*   **Pan Mingying:** Resident beneficiary.

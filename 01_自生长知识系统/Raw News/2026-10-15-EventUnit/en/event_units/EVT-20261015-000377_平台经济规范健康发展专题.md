# Event Name

Platform Economy Regulation and Healthy Development: Moving Beyond Traffic-Centric Models to Prioritize People's Livelihoods

## Event Overview

On October 15, 2026, the theoretical section of *People's Daily* published a special thematic article titled "Let Platform Economy Add More 'Smoke and Fire' (Vitality) to the Lives of the People" (让平台经济更添百姓“烟火气”), authored by Huang Xiwei from the University of International Business and Economics. The article analyzes the developmental trajectory, challenges, and future governance directions of China's platform economy. It emphasizes the need to shift from a "traffic-thinking" mindset—characterized by aggressive scale expansion and price wars—to a model that prioritizes public welfare, equitable competition, and sustainable growth. This publication reflects the alignment of academic discourse with recent high-level policy directives from the Politburo, the Central Economic Work Conference, and the 15th Five-Year Plan.

## Core Facts

*   **Policy Context:**
    *   **July 30, 2026:** The Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee held a meeting emphasizing "promoting the standardized and healthy development of the platform economy" [Source: Article #507].
    *   **2025 Central Economic Work Conference:** Explicitly stated the goal of "promoting win-win development among platform enterprises, operators within platforms, and workers" [Source: Article #507].
    *   **15th Five-Year Plan Outline:** Proposes strengthening supervision over platform enterprise data, algorithms, traffic, and rules, while promoting win-win development for operators and workers [Source: Article #507].
    *   **Xi Jinping's Directive:** Emphasizes looking to the long term, addressing current issues, filling短板 (shortcomings), strengthening weak links, and creating an innovation environment to solve prominent contradictions [Source: Article #507].

*   **Current Status of Platform Economy:**
    *   It is a key component of the digital economy and a new economic form based on internet platforms where large, medium, small, and micro enterprises coexist [Source: Article #507].
    *   **Contributions:** Lowered transaction/search costs, broke time-space barriers in trade, released consumption potential, promoted innovation/entrepreneurship, facilitated industrial upgrading, and smoothed national economic circulation [Source: Article #507].
    *   **Specific Sectors:** Live-streaming e-commerce and instant retail have enriched market supply, facilitated daily life, and generated flexible employment opportunities [Source: Article #507].

*   **Identified Problems:**
    *   The industry has exhibited phenomena driven by "traffic thinking," where there is one-sided pursuit of operational scale and economic benefits [Source: Article #507].
    *   Current competition often relies on price wars and "involution-style" (内卷式) competition rather than quality or innovation [Source: Article #507].

*   **Prescribed Governance Measures:**
    1.  **Effective Supervision:** Use digital regulatory tools; establish full-chain supervision (prior guidance, in-process dynamic monitoring, post-event legal disposal); deepen anti-monopoly and anti-unfair competition enforcement; standardize commission standards, traffic sorting, and transaction rules; ensure fee transparency [Source: Article #507].
    2.  **Open Ecosystem:** Promote internal cooperation and cross-platform collaboration; improve compliance and quality management systems; establish negotiation mechanisms for major matters between platforms and operators; guide cost reduction and rule optimization to help SMEs; prohibit using data/traffic/technology/capital advantages to restrict other platforms from independent operation [Source: Article #507].
    3.  **Consumption Upgrade:** Shift from "price competition" to "quality and innovation competition"; encourage brand and quality consumption promotions; optimize public services in healthcare, social security, and employment to improve inclusiveness, convenience, and personalization [Source: Article #507].

*   **Author Affiliation:** Huang Xiwei, University of International Business and Economics [Source: Article #507].

## Event Overview

On October 14, 2026, the State Council Information Office (SCIO) of China held a press conference to introduce the preparation progress for the Changchun 2027 World University Winter Games. The event is scheduled to take place from January 15 to 25, 2027. It marks the return of the World University Winter Games to China after 18 years and follows previous major international winter sports events hosted in the country, specifically the Beijing Winter Olympics and the Harbin Asian Winter Games.

The Games aim to utilize existing ice and snow resources in Jilin Province, leveraging a "Dual-City Linkage" model involving both Changchun and Jilin cities. Officials emphasized that the event will not only serve as a sporting competition but also as a platform for cultural exchange and dialogue among global youth.

## Core Facts

*   **Event Dates:** January 15–25, 2027.
*   **Theme:** "Chasing Dreams on Ice and Snow, Youth Everlasting" (逐梦冰雪，长驻青春).
*   **Scope:** 12 major disciplines and 97 sub-events.
*   **Participation:** Over 3,700 athletes, coaches, and officials from 63 countries and regions have registered, which is the highest number in the history of the World University Winter Games.
*   **Venue Model:** A "Changchun Main Venue + Jilin Sub-Venue" dual-city linkage model. There are 13 venues in total, all based on existing facilities undergoing "light intervention and micro-renovation" with low-carbon upgrades.
*   **Construction Status:** 12 of the 13 venues have completed construction; the remaining venue is expected to undergo testing soon.
*   **China Team Plan:** The Chinese delegation plans to compete in 11 out of the 12 major disciplines. The preliminary team size is approximately 400 personnel, including about 240 athletes.
*   **Testing Schedule:** 15 test events/drills are scheduled to begin the following month (November 2026) to verify command scheduling, venue operations, equipment, logistics, and scoring systems.
*   **Volunteers:** 8,100 volunteers were selected from 120,000 applicants to provide services such as ticket scanning, traffic guidance, and luggage assistance at key hubs.
*   **Support Infrastructure:** 41 reception hotels, 27 designated hospitals, 29 food supply bases, and over 1,100 support vehicles have been arranged.
*   **Mascots and Symbols:**
    *   Mascots: "Ji Bing" (吉冰) and "Ji Xue" (吉雪), released in March 2026.
    *   Medals: "Ji Meng" (吉梦), released in October 2026.
    *   Torch: "Ji Xiang" (吉翔), released in October 2026.
    *   Logo: "Snow and Ice Dream Soaring" (冰雪梦翔).
*   **Cauldron/Fire:** The game fire was successfully collected in Turin, Italy, on October 8, 2026, and a welcome ceremony was held in Changchun on October 14, 2026.

## Event Conclusion

The Changchun 2027 World University Winter Games are in the advanced stages of preparation, with official confirmation from the State Council Information Office on October 14, 2026. The event is projected to be the most widely participated Winter Universiade in history, with over 3,700 registered participants from 63 countries. The organizing committee, led by representatives from the Ministry of Education, the General Administration of Sport, and the Jilin Provincial Government, is focusing on utilizing existing infrastructure through minimal renovation, ensuring strict anti-doping compliance, and leveraging the Games to promote year-round tourism and cultural exchange in the Jilin region. The upcoming months will see the execution of 15 test events to validate operational readiness before the Games commence in January 2027.

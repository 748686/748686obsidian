## Event Overview
On October 14, 2026, the 2026 "Light Up Reading Little Wishes" book drift activity was officially launched in Sichuan Province. The event took place at Yangbai Town Central Primary School in Tongjiang County, Bazhong City. Organized by the Printing and Distribution Bureau of the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee, the initiative focuses on rural youth literacy and reading promotion. It involves mobilizing publishing houses, cultural figures, and volunteers to support reading among rural children through book selection, wish collection, online publishing, charitable donations, and co-reading sessions.

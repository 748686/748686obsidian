## Event Overview

On the afternoon of October 14, 2026, Li Qiang, Premier of the State Council of the People's Republic of China, attended the opening ceremony of the 140th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair) in Guangzhou and delivered a keynote speech. The event coincided with the 70th anniversary of the establishment of the Canton Fair. Xi Jinping, President of China, sent a congratulatory letter specifically acknowledging the fair's achievements and reiterating China's determination to expand high-level opening-up.

The ceremony served as a platform for China to articulate its stance on international trade, emphasizing openness, innovation, and reliability. World Trade Organization (WTO) Director-General Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala delivered a video address. The event highlighted China's role as both a major global seller and buyer, framing its trade surplus as beneficial to partner nations rather than a unilateral advantage.

**Speech Themes:**
Li Qiang’s speech was structured around three main characteristics of contemporary China and three philosophical insights on global trade:

1.  **China’s Characteristics:**
    *   **Openness:** Bringing new opportunities. China is the main trade partner for over 160 countries and the top export destination for nearly 80. Foreign investment stock reaches $3.4 trillion.
    *   **Innovation:** Providing new momentum. Exhibits have evolved from agricultural products to embodied intelligence and smart healthcare. China is emerging as a leader in AI, green energy, and biomedicine.
    *   **Reliability:** Injecting certainty. The Canton Fair has operated continuously for 70 years, reflecting China’s commitment to contracts and policy stability.

2.  **Trade Insights:**
    *   **Market Connectivity:** Not a tool for博弈 (game/theft), but a method for creating incremental value. Protectionism cuts off market expansion.
    *   **Trade Balance:** Not merely账面 (book/accounting) figures. Imbalance is dynamic and natural. China argues against "downward balance" (closing markets) in favor of "upward balance" (expanding openness). China positions itself as both a "super seller" (creating "neighborly benefit" through affordable goods) and a "super buyer" (creating "rich neighbor effects" by importing goods that boost foreign revenues).
    *   **Win-Win Cooperation:** Not one-sided compromise, but mutual empowerment. Developed nations are urged to provide more support to developing nations. China committed to helping other developing countries modernize.

3.  **Future Actions:**
    *   Share more market opportunities (consumer retail sales exceeded 50 trillion RMB last year; expected to grow by ~10 trillion RMB over the next 5 years).
    *   Expand mutual cooperation (support Chinese enterprises to invest overseas, particularly in the Global South).
    *   Uphold international economic and trade order (support WTO reform and multilateralism).

**Event Statistics:**

*   **Canton Fair Historical Data:** Exhibition area grew from 9,600 sq m (first session) to 1.55 million sq m (current). Buyers grew from 1,200 to over 300,000. Cumulative transaction volume reached $1.6 trillion.

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives

*   **China (Official Stance):** The speech presents a proactive defense of China’s trade policies against accusations of protectionism and unfair trade practices. It reframes the narrative of China’s trade surplus as a "win-win" outcome where "surplus is in China, but benefits are shared." It positions China as a reliable partner to the "Global South" and a champion of multilateralism.
*   **World Trade Organization (International Institution):** WTO Director-General Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala’s video address (reported in Article #496) acknowledges China’s achievements and the importance of the Canton Fair as a bridge. She calls for collective action to reform the WTO and build an open, rules-based system amid rising uncertainty, aligning with China’s stated goal of upholding multilateralism.
*   **Implicit Global Context:** The speech critiques the trend of "politicizing, instrumentalizing, and weaponizing" economic and trade issues and increasing artificial market barriers. This reflects a direct response to global protectionist trends and trade tensions, particularly those involving export controls and decoupling efforts by other major economies.

## Event Conclusion

Premier Li Qiang’s keynote speech at the 140th Canton Fair marks a significant diplomatic and economic moment in China’s 70-year trade history. By articulating a vision of trade as a generator of incremental value rather than a zero-sum game, China seeks to counter rising global protectionism. The event, supported by the presence of WTO leadership and delegates from nearly 90 countries, reinforces Guangzhou’s status as a hub for international commerce. China’s pledge to expand market access, increase imports, and support the Global South suggests a strategic effort to stabilize trade relations and position itself as a predictable partner in a volatile geopolitical landscape.

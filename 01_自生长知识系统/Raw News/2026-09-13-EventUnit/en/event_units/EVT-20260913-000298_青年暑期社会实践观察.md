## Event Overview

This event centers on the 2026 summer season of "social practice" (social internship/community engagement) for university students in China. Reporting published on September 13, 2026, highlights how students transitioned from theoretical learning to practical application in rural areas, communities, and factories. The core theme is the cultivation of a sense of responsibility to the nation and the identification of practical societal needs through direct engagement. Key activities included teaching digital skills, creating AI-generated portraits for elderly villagers, and developing agricultural robotics solutions for local cooperatives. The reporting contrasts "tourism-style" superficial visits with deep, problem-solving engagements, aligning with the "15th Five-Year Plan" for Education which aims to institutionalize such practices.

## Core Facts

**Confirmed Facts:**
*   **Publication Date:** The relevant report was published in the *People's Daily* on September 13, 2026 (Page 05).
*   **General Trend:** Thousands of students participated in summer social practice activities across fields such as rural villages, community streets, and factory workshops.
*   **Specific Activity (Shanghai Jiao Tong University):** A team conducted an "AI Empower Rural Revitalization" project in Xinjie Town, Taixing City, Jiangsu Province.
*   **Specific Outcome (SJTU):** The team used AI to create "wish portraits" for local elderly residents, addressing the difficulty of gathering complete family photos for those with dispersed children. Examples cited include a retired veteran "reuniting" with Beijing, an elderly person with mobility issues, and a gold-anniversary couple.
*   **Specific Activity (Beijing Institute of Technology):** A team named "He Guang Zhineng" (禾光智能) operated in Chifeng, Inner Mongolia.
*   **Specific Outcome (BIT):** The team developed a weeding robot solution for a local agricultural cooperative.
*   **Quantified Impact (BIT):** The robot improved comprehensive weeding efficiency by **21.3%** compared to traditional methods (which required four people for a full day on one mu of land; the robot completes it in two hours).
*   **Institutional Context:** The *Education Development "15th Five-Year Plan"* proposes the establishment of modern practice education courses and dedicated university student social practice courses.
*   **Criticisms:** The report acknowledges that some social practices remain superficial ("checking in"), with results often shelved after the semester ends.

## Event Conclusion

The 2026 summer social practice season for Chinese university students is characterized by a shift towards "deep" engagement with local and national issues, particularly in rural areas. Key case studies demonstrate the application of emerging technologies (AI for emotional/social needs, robotics for agricultural efficiency) to solve "real problems" identified by grassroots communities. While the *People's Daily* highlights successful integrations of theory and practice—aligning with new educational planning goals—it simultaneously acknowledges that superficial "tourism-style" practices persist. The event serves as a policy and educational marker, where student activity is increasingly framed not just as internship, but as a mechanism for national development and personal ideological solidification. The specific quantifiable success of the weeding robot (21.3% efficiency gain) stands as a concrete example of this "knowledge-action" integration.

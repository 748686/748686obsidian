# Event Name

**18th BRICS Leaders' Summit (New Delhi)**

## Event Overview

On September 12, 2026, the 18th BRICS Summit of Heads of State was held in New Delhi, India. This summit marked the 20th anniversary of BRICS cooperation. President of the People's Republic of China, Xi Jinping, traveled to New Delhi to attend the first-stage meeting of the summit and deliver a keynote speech. During the summit, Xi also held bilateral talks with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The meeting focused on the future of BRICS cooperation, the role of the "Global South," and the response to global challenges such as regional conflicts, technological development, and the reform of global governance.

## Core Facts

*   **Event:** 18th BRICS Summit of Heads of State.
*   **Date:** September 12, 2026.
*   **Location:** New Delhi, India.
*   **Key Participants:**
    *   **China:** President Xi Jinping (accompanied by Cai Qi and Wang Yi).
    *   **India:** Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
*   **Key Milestones:**
    *   Marked the 20th anniversary of the BRICS cooperation mechanism.
    *   Noted BRICS's historic expansion and the entry of the "Big BRICS" high-quality development phase.
    *   China announced it will assume the BRICS chairmanship in 2027 and host the 19th summit.
*   **Key Agreements/Initiatives:**
    *   China-India Bilateral: Both leaders agreed to treat each other as partners, not rivals. They committed to balancing economic and trade concerns and maintaining border peace and tranquility.
    *   BRICS Multilateral: Xi proposed that BRICS nations act as pioneers in four areas: promoting innovative development, maintaining peace and stability, promoting mutual learning among civilizations, and reforming global governance.
    *   Specific Proposal: China proposed the "BRICS Initiative on Empowering New-Type Industrialization with Artificial Intelligence."

## Event Conclusion

The 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi, held on September 12, 2026, served as a pivotal milestone marking the 20th anniversary of the bloc's cooperation. The event reinforced BRICS as a leading platform for the "Global South," characterized by historic expansion and a shift toward "Big BRICS" high-quality development. Key outcomes included a strengthened diplomatic consensus between China and India to view each other as partners, and a renewed commitment by all members to pursue "four pioneers" strategies: innovation (including AI), peace maintenance, civilizational dialogue, and global governance reform. With China scheduled to assume the chairmanship in 2027, the summit laid the groundwork for the next phase of BRICS influence in countering unilateralism and promoting a multipolar world order.

# Event Name
Wuhan Advances Regional Sci-Tech Innovation Center Construction

## Event Overview
On September 13, 2026, the People's Daily reported that Wuhan is actively cultivating an open innovation ecosystem and intensifying efforts to build a regional sci-tech innovation center. As a major hub in central China, the city is leveraging its scientific, educational, and industrial advantages to serve as a key innovation pole for the Hubei province and the broader central region.

Key initiatives include:
*   **Spatial Layout:** Accelerating the construction of the “One City, Three Corridors, Multiple Belts” innovation spatial layout.
*   **Specific Plans:** Deeply implementing the “Koi Plan” to stimulate industrial innovation vitality.
*   **Integrated Approach:** Coordinating innovation in science, industry, finance, regional development, openness, and governance to release “multiplier effects” of innovation.

The strategy involves advancing into unexplored scientific frontiers, revitalizing existing urban spaces for R&D, strengthening the pilot-to-production (mid-stream testing) pipeline, and expanding regional cooperation through cross-border and inter-city collaborations.

## Core Facts
*   **Source Date:** 2026-09-13
*   **Primary Actor:** Wuhan, Hubei Province, China.
*   **Strategic Goal:** To build a high-level Wuhan Regional Sci-Tech Innovation Center to serve as a strong engine for Hubei’s “pivot” construction.
*   **Spatial Framework:** The “One City, Three Corridors, Multiple Belts” layout is being implemented to optimize 1.47 million square meters of low-efficiency sci-tech space.
*   **The “Koi Plan”:**
    *   The “Koi Project” (Jinli Project) in Guangbutun, Hongshan District, is the first-phase project.
    *   Over 80% of its space is dedicated to sci-tech R&D.
    *   It focuses on two major tracks: new materials and artificial intelligence.
    *   It has reserved over 100 sci-tech projects (including student startups and one-person companies).
    *   Phase 1 is scheduled to open in October 2026.
*   **Research Achievements:**
    *   Wuhan released “365” major sci-tech innovation achievements at its annual conference.
    *   15 basic research outcomes were highlighted for original innovation, including a high-precision DNA replication tool (Wuhan University) and a wireless ultrasound sensor for intracranial physiological signals (Huazhong University of Science and Technology).
    *   Wuhan University developed the world’s smallest chip atomic clock (smaller than a fingernail), with an error of only 1 second in 30,000 years, crucial for low-orbit satellite constellations.
*   **Innovation Infrastructure:**
    *   41 National Key Laboratories and 8 Hubei Provincial Laboratories are established or under construction.
    *   7 major sci-tech infrastructure projects are built or underway.
    *   12 Industrial Innovation Joint Laboratories aggregate over 100 industry-academia-research units and nearly 4,000 researchers.
*   **Pilot-to-Production (Mid-stream) Capabilities:**
    *   The Hubei Jiufengshan Laboratory, the only national-level manufacturing pilot platform in the integrated circuit field, has completed full-flow verification of an 8-inch high-throughput DNA sequencing chip.
    *   Citywide, there are 2 national-level, 40 provincial-level, and 210 municipal-level registered pilot platforms.
*   **Regional and Financial Innovation:**
    *   **Regional:** Wuhan East Lake High-Tech Zone (Optics Valley) signed an agreement with Ezhou on September 4, 2026, to launch the construction of the Optics Valley Pan-Semiconductor Industrial Park. The tech departments of the nine cities in the Wuhan Metropolitan Area signed a cooperation document to form a sci-tech innovation community.
    *   **Financial:** The Wuhan University-Center Innovation Development Belt Angel Investment Alliance was unveiled in Hongshan District, signing for the first phase of a 6 billion RMB landed fund.
    *   **Global Connectivity:** Seven off-shore sci-tech innovation bases are operational, enabling 231 innovation entities to conduct R&D in Wuhan and transform outputs elsewhere. A logistics chain allows chips to move from Gezhou to key Wuhan firms in 20 minutes, to Huahu International Airport cargo planes in 1 hour, and to RCEP member countries within 72 hours.

## Event Conclusion
Wuhan is systematically transforming its urban landscape and industrial infrastructure to establish itself as a dominant regional sci-tech innovation center. Through a strategy that combines frontier basic research (such as atomic clocks and DNA tools) with the practical revitalization of existing urban spaces and an expanded pilot-to-production pipeline, the city aims to bridge the gap between academic discovery and commercial viability. The expansion of regional cooperation (nine-city innovation community and the Optics Valley-Ezhou semiconductor link) and new financial mechanisms (6 billion RMB angel fund) are designed to amplify its competitiveness both within central China and on the global stage.

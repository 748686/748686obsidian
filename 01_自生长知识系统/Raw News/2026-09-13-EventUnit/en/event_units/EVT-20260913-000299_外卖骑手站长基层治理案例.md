## Event Overview

On September 13, 2026, *People's Daily* published a first-person account by Guo Fuqiang, the station manager of the Zhongyuan Road Station for Taobao Flash Purchase (淘宝闪购) in Changhai Road Subdistrict, Yangpu District, Shanghai. The article documents his transformation from a new food delivery worker in 2016 to a station manager overseeing nearly 150 riders. The core narrative highlights how delivery workers are transitioning from being viewed solely as urban logistics participants ("runners") to active participants in grassroots community governance. Guo describes how he and his team report public safety hazards, assist residents, and integrate with local subdistrict organizations, leading to their station becoming a "Yuodong Partner" (悦动合伙人) of a characteristic street area.

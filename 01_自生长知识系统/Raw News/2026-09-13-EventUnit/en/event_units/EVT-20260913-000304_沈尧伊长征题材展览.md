## Event Overview

On September 13, 2026, an exhibition titled "The Red Ribbon Journey — Shen Yaoyi Long March Sketch Works Exhibition" ("红飘带之行——沈尧伊长征路写生作品展") was reported to be currently underway at the National Centre for the Performing Arts Museum (国家大剧院艺术馆). The exhibition features approximately 200 works, including gouache, acrylic, and oil paintings, specifically highlighting original field sketches created by the artist Shen Yaoyi during his two expeditions to retrace the Long March route in 1975 and 1977. This event serves as a retrospective of Shen Yaoyi’s over-50-year dedication to Long March-themed art, emphasizing his methodology of "no sketch without on-site verification, no creation without historical fact."

*   **Venue:** National Centre for the Performing Arts Museum (国家大剧院艺术馆).
*   **Status as of 2026-09-13:** The exhibition is ongoing ("正在...举办").
*   **Artist:** Shen Yaoyi (沈尧伊).
*   **Exhibit Volume:** Approximately 200 works (甄选近200幅).
*   **Mediums:** Gouache (水粉), Acrylic (丙烯), Oil (油画).
*   **Key Feature:** First public release of original field sketches (写生原稿) completed during Shen’s 1975 and 1977 trips to retrace the Long March route.
*   **Artistic Philosophy:** Adhered to the principle established by mentor Li Hua: "Draw what you see with your eyes." Shen’s specific准则: "No sketch without on-site verification, no creation without historical fact."
*   **Historical Context of Sketched Works:**
    *   1975: Started from Southern Jiangxi, spent 3 months traveling north along the Red Army's route.
    *   1977: Second trip to fill in gaps and verify materials.
*   **Significant Works Mentioned in Context of Style:**
    *   Combat scenes: *Seizing Luding Bridge* (*飞夺泸定桥*), *Blood Battle at Xiang River* (*血战湘江*).
    *   Classic Oil Paintings: *Revolutionary Ideals Are Higher Than the Sky*, *Now Taking the First Step to Cross*, *Zhunyi Conference*, *Wayaobao Conference*.
    *   New Works (Romantic/Integration style): *Rapid Advance to Lazibao Mountain*, *Red Army Path in Zhangjiajie*, *Crossing the Snowy Mountains* (total 6 new oil paintings mentioned).
    *   Large-scale Comic: *Earth's Red Ribbon* (*地球的红飘带*).

# Event Name

Fuzhou Intangible Cultural Copyright Protection and Transformation

## Event Overview

As of September 2026, Fuzhou City in Jiangxi Province, China, is actively advancing a comprehensive strategy to protect and commercialize intangible cultural folklore through a pilot copyright protection and promotion program. Having been designated as one of the first such pilot cities nationwide, Fuzhou has implemented a "Four Major Projects" framework covering copyright leadership, creation, protection, and promotion. This initiative includes systematic resource surveys, enhanced registration processes, the establishment of judicial and administrative protection bodies, and the creation of digital platforms to market folk art products. The event highlights the transition of traditional folk crafts into a sustainable industrial resource, with reported annual revenues from specific copyright文创 (cultural creative) products exceeding 1 billion RMB and a broader related industry scale approaching 10 billion RMB.

## Core Facts

*   **Pilot Status**: Fuzhou is identified as one of the first cities in China to pilot intangible cultural copyright protection and promotion.
*   **Resource Survey**: In 2023, Fuzhou conducted its first intangible cultural resource survey, confirming 205 projects across 10 major categories, including wood carving, paper cutting, opera, and nuo dance.
*   **Copyright Registration**: The total number of intangible cultural copyright registrations in the city has reached 24,000.
*   **Key Institutions**:
    *   Fuzhou Intangible Cultural Copyright Protection and Promotion Center.
    *   Fuzhou Intangible Cultural Copyright Judicial Protection Station (led by the Intermediate People’s Court).
    *   Intellectual Property Circuit Courts established in Linchuan, Dongxiang, and Nancheng districts/counties.
    *   Fuzhou Intangible Cultural Copyright Museum in the Wenchangli Historic and Cultural District, which has exhibited over 1,600 high-quality works and received over 500,000 visitors.
*   **Talent Development**:
    *   Over 60 folk artists have been identified as "virtuous and accomplished" experts, with 33 establishing individual studios.
    *   In the last three years, 280 new members were added to the municipal-level Intangible Cultural Literature and Art Association, 30 to the provincial-level, and 17 to the national-level, totaling over 400 members.
    *   Over 10,000 skilled technicians were cultivated through a "institutional cultivation, master inheritance" model.
*   **Digital and Market Platforms**:
    *   The "Fuzhou Intangible Cultural Online Market" (via the "Fu Guan Tian Xia" app) lists over 100 goods, with cumulative sales exceeding 1.2 million RMB.
    *   Copyrights and products were showcased at the 22nd China (Shenzhen) International Culture Industry Expo in May 2026, generating on-site sales of over 300,000 RMB.
*   **International Exposure**: Results have been presented at the 2024 China-Latin America Civilizational Dialogue, the 2025 Osaka World Expo, the 2026 International Symposium on Intangible Cultural Copyright Protection and Promotion, and the 10th China International Copyright Expo.
*   **Economic Impact**:
    *   Annual revenue from copyright cultural creative products (e.g., nuo masks, craft ceramics) exceeds 1 billion RMB.
    *   The total scale of related special industries (ceramics, nuo masks, wood carving, oil painting, rush grass) is nearly 10 billion RMB.

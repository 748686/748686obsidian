## Event Overview

On September 13, 2026, the People's Daily published a commentary on the "Blooming — Third National College Student Fine Arts Exhibition (Beijing Exhibition)" [第三届全国大学生美术作品展览（进京展）], which is currently being held at the Central Academy of Fine Arts Museum and Today Art Museum in Beijing. The exhibition features over 400 selected works from eight separate exhibition zones, representing 21 fine arts disciplines. This edition of the exhibition serves as a platform to observe contemporary youth artistic creation and the development of higher fine arts education in China.

## Unique Information by Source

**ARTICLE #369 (People's Daily):**
*   **Critique of Trends**: Identifies two main issues in youth creation: (1) a tendency toward "production-ization" (over-reliance on image resources and technical proficiency leading to hollow expression), and (2) insufficient ability to convert artistic inheritance into innovation (failure to deeply study the essence of predecessors' work).
*   **Historical Examples**: Cites Wang Ximeng's "A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains" (Song Dynasty) and Zhan Junjun's "The Five Heroes of Langya Mountain" as examples of masterpieces created by artists in their youth, emphasizing that art is about emotional expression rather than technical competition.
*   **Educational Implication**: Notes that the issues identified are a subject for deep consideration in higher fine arts education.
*   **Specific Reference**: Mentions a specific artwork: Li Sisi's lacquer painting "Competing Sails" [李思思漆画《风帆竞航》].

## Event Conclusion

The Third National College Student Fine Arts Exhibition, held in Beijing in 2026, marks a significant expansion of the event established in 2017, growing from 4 to 21 artistic disciplines. The exhibition reflects a broader trend among Chinese youth artists who are integrating national cultural aesthetics with contemporary themes such as smart technology and social development. However, editorial analysis highlights a persistent challenge: a tendency toward technical "production-ization" that lacks deep emotional resonance and genuine innovation. The event serves not only as a showcase but as a critical benchmark for the quality of talent cultivation in China's higher fine arts education system.

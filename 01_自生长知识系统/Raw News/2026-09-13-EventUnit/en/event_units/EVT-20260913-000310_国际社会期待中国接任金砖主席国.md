# International Community Awaits China's Assumption of BRICS Chairmanship

## Event Overview
On September 12, 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping announced during the first stage of the 18th BRICS Leaders' Meeting in New Delhi that China will assume the BRICS Chairmanship in 2027 and host the 19th BRICS Leaders' Meeting. This announcement was reported by *People's Daily* on September 13, 2026. The article highlights that international experts, scholars, and officials from various BRICS and "Global South" countries expressed strong expectations for the next decade of BRICS cooperation, often referred to as the third "Golden Decade," emphasizing the role of China's leadership in advancing high-quality development in "Greater BRICS Cooperation."

## Core Facts
*   **Announcement Date and Location:** September 12, 2026, in New Delhi, India.
*   **Speaker:** Chinese President Xi Jinping.
*   **Context:** 18th BRICS Leaders' Meeting, first stage.
*   **Key Declaration:** China will assume the BRICS Chairmanship the following year (2027) and host the 19th BRICS Leaders' Meeting.
*   **Strategic Goal:** To initiate the third "Golden Decade" of BRICS cooperation and create a promising future of prosperity and progress.
*   **Historical Context:** This marks China's fourth term as BRICS Chair, following three previous terms. The 20th anniversary of BRICS cooperation falls in 2026.
*   **Source Date:** The reporting is dated September 13, 2026, by *People's Daily*.

## Event Conclusion
On September 12, 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping formally announced China's assumption of the BRICS Chairmanship for 2027 and its role as host for the 19th BRICS Leaders' Meeting during the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi. This event was widely reported on September 13, 2026, by *People's Daily*, which compiled extensive reactions from international experts and officials from Russia, Malaysia, Egypt, Thailand, Brazil, India, Ethiopia, South Africa, and the UAE. These stakeholders unanimously expressed optimism, citing China's previous successful chairmanships and its leadership in areas such as technology, green development, and institutional innovation. The announcement is framed as a pivotal moment to launch the third "Golden Decade" of BRICS cooperation, with expectations for enhanced "Greater BRICS" integration, deeper Global South solidarity, and practical results in addressing global challenges.

### [People&\#x27;s Daily publishes poem &\#x27;Four Seasons of the Long March&\#x27;](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/22/content_30187057.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On October 22, 2026, the People&\#x27;s Daily published a poem titled "Four Seasons of the Journey" on page 16 by author Ding Xiaowei. The piece chronicles the Chinese Red Army&\#x27;s Long March through seasonal imagery, referencing the autumn departure from the Yudu River, the winter turn at Zunyi and Gouba, the spring Four Crossings of the Chishui River, and the summer hardships of the Songpan grasslands. It highlights the 86,000 soldiers, the sharing of belt leather during food shortages, and the emphasis on unity as vital to survival. The poem frames the retreat not merely as a military maneuver but as a broader struggle for ideological conviction.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 22, 00:26

**「Background」** The Long March \(1934–1935\) was a strategic withdrawal by the Chinese Communist Party&\#x27;s Red Army from Nationalist forces, covering thousands of kilometers across diverse and hostile terrain. Key episodes include the Zunyi Conference, which established Mao Zedong&\#x27;s leadership, and the crossing of the Chishui River, noted for its tactical complexity. The event is a foundational narrative in modern Chinese history and is regularly commemorated in state media.

**「Impact」** The publication reinforces the Long March as an ongoing cultural and political symbol in contemporary China, particularly through state-affiliated outlets like the People&\#x27;s Daily. Its effect is primarily commemorative and educational rather than operational or technical.

### [Documentary team follows the lantern&\#x27;s light on the Long March route](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202610/22/content_30187058.html) ⭐️ ?/10

An October 22, 2026 article by Xiao Pu in People&\#x27;s Daily recounts how a documentary crew traveled the Long March route to explore what sustained the Red Army near extinction. The narrative centers on a rusty oil lamp displayed at the Central Red Army Long March Departure Memorial Hall in Yudu, used symbolically to represent leadership and conviction during critical historical moments such as Mao Zedong&\#x27;s late-night journey to Zhou Enlai&\#x27;s residence in Gouba. The team documented sites in Guizhou, Sichuan, Gansu, and Henan, deliberately exposing themselves to high-altitude cold and oxygen deprivation to reflect on the soldiers&\#x27; hardships. They contrast those struggles with the region&\#x27;s current economic development, noting that local families continue traditions of supporting collective causes decades later.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Oct 22, 00:26

**「Background」** The Long March refers to the Red Army&\#x27;s strategic relocation from 1934 to 1936 amid civil war and external threats. The documentary series mentioned is titled The Great Expedition, and the piece connects specific battle sites and meeting locations to broader themes of revolutionary history and contemporary remembrance.

**「Impact」** The article reinforces public commemoration of the Long March through televised and print media, linking historical memory to current regional development narratives in provinces along the route.

用生命替大家挡住无名的毒

在草地，最致命的毒是分裂和不团结

草地没有诗意，反而残酷相告——

这不仅是战略的长征，更是真理的远征

**2. Key Policy Themes (Page 09 - Theory)**
*   **Institutional Opening-Up:** The edition featured an article on scientifically grasping the strategic significance and practical requirements of promoting institutional opening-up, linked to the study of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era (Volumes 1–5 of *The Governance of China*).
*   **Economics Knowledge System:** An article titled "Focus on Building China's Autonomous Economics Knowledge System" was published as part of the series on accelerating the construction of an autonomous knowledge system for Chinese philosophy and social sciences.
*   **Sports and Health ("Health Self-Formation"):**
    *   **Context:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" outline proposes "accelerating the construction of a leading sports nation," integrated into the chapter on "accelerating the construction of a Healthy China."
    *   **Problem Statement:** While national fitness has achieved new benefits since the 18th National Congress, challenges remain: weak grassroots scientific fitness guidance, insufficient talent in exercise prescriptions, mismatch between service supply and precise health needs, and inadequate cross-departmental coordination.
    *   **Recommendations:**
        1.  **Improve Public Fitness Service System:** Expand "15-minute fitness circles," ensure equity/accessibility, and provide services for the elderly and disabled.
        2.  **Promote Sports-Medicine Integration:** Establish mechanisms between sports and health departments, set up sports health guidance clinics in medical institutions, train exercise prescription specialists, and build demonstration bases for chronic disease management and rehabilitation.
        3.  **Expand Sports Industry:** Integrate sports with elderly care, cultural tourism, and health/康养 industries. Develop smart fitness scenarios and digital platforms.
        4.  **Build Collaborative Governance:** Strengthen cross-departmental coordination, support grassroots sports social organizations, and widen channels for social capital participation.
        5.  **Cultivate Health Culture:** Promote traditional Chinese sports culture and the "Chinese Sports Spirit," turning sports participation into a social norm.

**3. International Cooperation Cases (Page 10 - Special Report)**
The special report titled "Sharing Opportunities and Achieving Win-Win Results in Open Development" highlights specific China-APEC cooperation projects:
*   **Australia-China Green Energy:** Australian智慧能源展 (Sydney) showcased Chinese companies (Jinko Power, LONGi) supplying photovoltaic components and energy storage systems. Projects in Queensland are reducing electricity costs for retailers.
*   **China-Brunai Petrochemical:** Hengyi Petrochemical's Greater Molala Island comprehensive refining project in Brunei. Phase I started in 2019 (8 million tons capacity); Phase II agreement signed Nov 2023 (total capacity 20 million tons). Local hiring reached 40% target by 2020.
*   **Canada-China Ice/Snow Economy:** Bombardier BRP partnered with Harbin during the first International Ice and Snow Economy Expo (Jan 2026), following the Jan 2026 trade cooperation roadmap between Prime Minister Carney and China.
*   **China-Chile Cherry Trade:** Chile’s cherry exports to China rose from <20,000 tons (2011-12 season) to 546,000 tons (2024-25 season), accounting for >90% of China’s imports. 2025 export value was ~$3.38 billion.
*   **China-Indonesia High-Speed Rail:** Jakarta-Bandung High-Speed Railway (opened Oct 2023) reduced travel time from 3+ hours to 46 minutes. Localized training of drivers and mechanics is ongoing.
*   **Japan-China Investment:** Panasonic opened new factories in Suzhou (June 2026) and Shanghai. 59 affiliated companies in China account for ~25% of global business. 59% of Japanese firms plan to maintain/increase investment (June 2026 White Paper).
*   **China-South Korea Logistics:** "Four-Port Linkage" (Weihai-Incheon) reduces logistics time by ~5 hours and cost by ~30%. Weihai became the top city for cross-border e-commerce retail exports to Korea in 2024.
*   **China-Malaysia Industrial Parks:** "Two Countries, Two Parks" model (Guangxi-Qinnan and Malaysia-China Kuantan Industrial Park). Direct container shipping since 2017; cold chain route launched 2025.
*   **Mexico-China Rail Transit:** CRRC Zhuzhou supplied light rail trains for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Local supplier base includes 100+ companies; 200+ technicians trained.
*   **China-New Zealand Science:** Joint deep-sea submersible research in the Puysegur Trench (March 2025) and Kermadec Trench (Nov 2022). Collaboration discussed at the 25th APEC Fisheries Working Group Meeting (Feb 2026).
*   **China-Papua New Guinea Infrastructure:** China Railway Construction Engineering Group completed the Oketedy Road Phase II (March 2026), totaling >850 km of roads built in PNG.
*   **China-Peru Port Logistics:** Chancay Port (opened Nov 2024) reduced shipping time to Shanghai to 23 days. Throughput near 600,000 TEUs with >$4.5 billion in import/export value.
*   **China-Philippines Agriculture:** Longping High-Tech’s R&D center in Philippines promotes hybrid rice ("Xiling No. 8"), covering ~6 million mu of planting area.
*   **China-Russia Energy:** East Route of the Russia-China Oil and Gas Pipeline (fully connected Dec 2024) is 8,000+ km long, reducing CO2 emissions by 164 million tons annually.

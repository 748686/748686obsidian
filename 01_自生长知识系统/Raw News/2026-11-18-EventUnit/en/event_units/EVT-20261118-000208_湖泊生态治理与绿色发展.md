# Event Name
Ecological Restoration and Green Transformation of the Yangtze River Nanjing Section: From "Industrial Belt" to "Scenic Belt"

## Event Overview
On November 18, 2026, *People's Daily* published an article titled " 'Industrial Belt' Transforms into 'Scenic Belt' (The Nearby Battle for Pure Soil)," featuring an interview with Ju Xiaoqiu, Deputy Director of the Nanjing Municipal Bureau of Ecology and Environment. The piece details the comprehensive ecological restoration of the Yangtze River shoreline in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province. It highlights the transition from heavy industrial usage to ecological conservation, driven by the national directive of "prioritizing big protection and eschewing large-scale development" established during the 2016 Symposium on Promoting the Yangtze River Economic Belt. The narrative underscores the integration of political leadership, systematic ecological engineering, and public engagement in achieving sustainable urban-green development.

## Core Facts
*   **Policy Foundation:** The transformation was catalyzed by the 2016 Symposium on Promoting the Yangtze River Economic Belt, which set the strategic direction of "co-grasping major protection and not pursuing large-scale development."
*   **Timeline of Intervention:**
    *   **2016:** Policy direction established.
    *   **2017 (Late):** Formation of specialized work teams in response to environmental inspections by central and provincial authorities.
    *   **November 2020:** Completion of the initial专项整治 (special rectification) campaign.
*   **Scale of Remediation (Pukou District, Qiaolin Subdistrict):**
    *   **Area:** 13.3 km of riverfront previously hosting nearly 50 enterprises.
    *   **Demolition:** Removal of over 300 sets of equipment and approximately 130,000 square meters of buildings.
    *   **Ground Restoration:** Clearance of 1.5 million square meters of hardened ground.
    *   **Investment:** Approximately 2.4 billion RMB.
*   **Ecological Outcomes:**
    *   Afforestation and greening completed: 3,585 mu along the Yangtze River.
    *   Ecological shoreline restored: Approximately 11 km.
    *   Industrial heritage preserved: Old shipyard sites converted into recreational spaces (e.g., Donglongshan Country Park), with cranes retained as historical markers.
*   **Water Quality Improvements:**
    *   **Branch Rivers:** All 28 rivers flowing into the Yangtze in Nanjing now consistently meet Class III water quality standards (achieved since the 14th Five-Year Plan period).
    *   **Main Stream:** Water quality in the Yangtze River Nanjing section remains stably at Class II.
*   **Biodiversity Gains:**
    *   **New Jimizhou National Wetland Park:** Bird species recorded increased from 103 in 2016 to 239 currently.
    *   **Yangtze Finless Porpoises:** Population in the Nanjing section has grown to 65 individuals.
*   **Current Status:**
    *   Total production shoreline withdrawn: 37.6 km.
    *   Proportion of ecological shoreline: Increased to 80.3%.
    *   Riverside greenway: 72 km已基本贯通 (basically connected).

## Event Conclusion
The ecological restoration of the Nanjing Yangtze River shoreline represents a successful implementation of China's "Green Development" strategy at the municipal level. By adhering to the "co-grasping protection, not large-scale development" principle, Nanjing reversed decades of industrial encroachment, converting 37.6 km of production shoreline into ecological space. The project demonstrates that systematic ecological repair—combining demolition, habitat reconstruction, tributary治理 (governance), and public engagement—can yield tangible economic, social, and biodiversity benefits. The preservation of industrial heritage (cranes) alongside new green spaces illustrates a model of sustainable urban memory and ecological renewal.

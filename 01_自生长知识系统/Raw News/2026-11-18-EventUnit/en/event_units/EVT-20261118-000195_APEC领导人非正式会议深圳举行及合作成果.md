# APEC Leaders' Week 2026 in Shenzhen and Cooperation Outcomes

## Event Overview

The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) 33rd Leaders' Meeting was held in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China, on November 18–19, 2026. China served as the APEC host for the second time, the first being in 2014. The meeting's theme was "Building an Asia-Pacific Community, Promoting Common Prosperity," with three priority areas: "Openness, Innovation, and Cooperation."

The event encompassed not only the Leaders' Meeting but also the APEC Business Advisory Council (ABAC) Summit, various ministerial meetings, and extensive bilateral diplomatic activities. The preceding weeks saw over 300 supporting events and activities held across China, including APEC Trade Ministers' Meetings in Suzhou (May) and Digital/AI Ministers' Meetings in Chengdu (July).

This synthesis reflects activities reported during the week of November 17–18, 2026, including President Xi Jinping's series of bilateral meetings with attending leaders and Russia's President Vladimir Putin's arrival in Shenzhen.

## Core Facts

### Event Logistics and Theme
- **Dates:** November 18–19, 2026 (Leaders' Meeting); supporting events held throughout 2026.
- **Location:** Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China.
- **Theme:** "Building an Asia-Pacific Community, Promoting Common Prosperity."
- **Host Significance:** China's second time hosting APEC (first was 2014 in Beijing); first time since 2014.
- **China's Role:** China is the largest developing economy within APEC and the第一大贸易伙伴 (largest trade partner) of 13 APEC economies.

### Bilateral Meetings by President Xi Jinping (November 17, 2026)
President Xi Jinping held bilateral meetings with multiple leaders attending the APEC event in Shenzhen on November 17:

1. **Vietnam:** Met with越共中央总书记、国家主席苏林 (General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam Central Committee and President Suong Lin). Discussed building a higher-level strategic Vietnamese-Chinese community with socialist orientation, party-building exchange, multi-dimensional connectivity, digital transformation, green development, artificial intelligence cooperation, maritime dispute management, and joint APEC hosting (Vietnam as next host in 2027).

   - Emphasized cooperation in traditional areas (trade, energy, mining, infrastructure, astronomy) and emerging areas (AI, polar research, deep-sea exploration).

   - Discussion of Belt and Road Initiative alignment with PNG's development plans.

### Russian President Vladimir Putin's Arrival
- Russian President Vladimir Putin arrived in Shenzhen on the early morning of November 18, 2026, by special flight to attend the APEC Leaders' Meeting.
- Cai Qi (蔡奇), Member of the Politburo Standing Committee and Secretary-General of the Central Secretariat, went to the airport to welcome him.

### APEC Economic Context
- Since 1989, APEC regional average tariffs have fallen from 16.9% to 5.1% (as of 2024).
- Asia-Pacific economies account for less than 40% of global population but generate over 60% of global GDP and nearly 50% of global trade.
- In the first three quarters of 2026, China's imports and exports with other APEC economies reached 24.07 trillion yuan, up 23.9% year-on-year.
- China contributed over 60% to Asia-Pacific economic growth in recent years.

### Business and Practical Cooperation Outcomes
- **AEO (Authorized Economic Operator) Program:** China has Mutual Recognition Agreements with 10 APEC economies (excluding Papua New Guinea). In the first seven months of 2026, China's AEO enterprises conducted 9.19 trillion yuan in trade with APEC economies, up 21.87% year-on-year.
- **Cross-border Payment Integration:** Alipay+ "外包内用" service enables 90+ e-wallets from 18 countries/regions to be used in China. In the first three quarters of 2026, transactions by APEC economy visitors via these services grew 56% year-on-year.
- **Shenzhen Qianhai Cooperation Zone:**
  - Imports and exports exceeded 1 trillion yuan for the first time (January–October 2026), reaching 1068.08 billion yuan, up 73.9%.
  - Cross-border data validation platform completed over 50,000 transactions, with 2026 alone seeing 17 times the volume of the previous two years combined.
  - FT Account (Free Trade Account) pilot banks increased to 9, with cross-border receipts and payments exceeding 190 billion yuan.
  - Foreign investment utilized in the first three quarters of 2026 reached 29.28 billion yuan, up 91.8%.
  - Over 14,000 Hong Kong residents live and work in Qianhai; over 11,000 Hong Kong-invested enterprises are registered there.

### Financial and Service Support for APEC
- **Bank of China:** Became the official partner of the APEC meeting on October 31, 2026. Launched the "Laihua Tong" (来华通) app supporting 8 languages for food, accommodation, travel, tourism, shopping, and medical services. Established service teams and volunteers at airports, ports, venues, hotels, and key commercial districts.
- **Agricultural Bank of China:** Served as a strategic partner for the 2026 APEC Meeting, offering cross-border financial services.

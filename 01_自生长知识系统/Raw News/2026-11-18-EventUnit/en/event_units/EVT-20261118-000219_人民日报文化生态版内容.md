## Core Facts

### 1. Science Education Integration in Huairou District, Beijing
*   **Program Name:** "Guohuai Plan" (国槐计划), a collaboration between the Huairou District Education Commission and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS).
*   **Implementation:** As of the report, the program has reached 35 primary and secondary schools in Huairou District, covering 992 classes.
*   **Staffing:** Each class is assigned a "science homeroom teacher" (科技班主任), recruited from current undergraduate and graduate students at UCAS.
*   **Historical Context:** The district implemented a "science vice-principal" system in 2019; the "science homeroom teacher" role was promoted in 2025.
*   **Supporting Structures:**
    *   The Huairou District Science and Technology Education Center was recently unveiled to coordinate resources, incubate courses, and manage the dispatch of scientific facilities and experts into schools.
    *   A three-party联动 (linkage) system is forming: the Science and Technology Education Center统筹s resources; science vice-principals integrate external resources into school planning; and science homeroom teachers deliver classroom instruction.
*   **Activities:** Over 500 immersive science and education activities have been conducted, utilizing sites such as the CAS Memorial Hall for "Two Bombs, One Satellite," UCAS history museums, research laboratories, and博士 farms (Ph.D. farms).
*   **Scientific Base:** Huairou Science City hosts 37 technology facility platforms, including 6 major scientific devices, with over 28,000 researchers and nearly 20,000 UCAS faculty and students.
*   **Specific Examples:**
    *   Zhongguancun First Primary School Huairou Branch: Teacher Hou Xiaocong demonstrates atmospheric pressure principles using plastic bottles; PhD student Ci Ruixi (UCAS AI program) guides students in robotics programming.

### 2. Qingdao Jiaozhou Bay Second Undersea Tunnel Construction
*   **Status:** The shield tunneling construction task for the main line was completed on October 2, 2026.
*   **Specifications:**
    *   Total main line length: 17.48 km.
    *   Tunnel length: 14.37 km.
    *   Configuration: Dual main tunnels plus an intermediate service tunnel.
    *   Depth: Buried 11–70+ meters below the seabed (surface level 115 meters below sea level at the deepest point). Water depth above ranges from 20 to 46 meters.
*   **Engineering Innovations (Green/Low-Impact):**
    *   **Ventilation:** Avoided constructing artificial islands or breakwaters in the bay. Instead, the intermediate service tunnel (used for maintenance/emergencies) functions as a "lying-down ventilation tower," using fans to push fresh air into the main tunnels.
    *   **Geological Adaptation:** Utilized a combined "Drilling and Blasting + Shield Method" approach. Shield machines were used near the main urban area (soft tuff/sand), while drilling and blasting was used near Huangdao (hard granite). The route crosses 22 fault zones.
    *   **Mud Recycling:** Shield machines used a mud-water circulation system. During the 3,255-meter shield drive, over 1.3 million cubic meters of waste slurry and 600,000+ cubic meters of slag were produced. Six pressure filter systems processed slurry daily (4,000 cubic meters/day), compressing it into泥 cakes (<30% water content) for use as roadbed backfill aggregate.
    *   **Prefabrication:** A 5th-generation green intelligent segment production line was used, factory-precasting over 15,000 concrete segments per single tunnel to reduce coastal pollution and carbon emissions.
    *   **Controlled Blasting:** Used high-precision jack rigs (drilling 260 holes in 160 sqm in 3 hours) and digital electronic detonators to minimize vibration. Nearly 4,000 controlled blasts were executed with negligible impact on surface vessels.
    *   **Digital Management:** A digital construction platform using 5G, Beidou positioning, and AI robots tracks personnel location and safety metrics in real-time.
*   **Equipment:** Three shield machines, totaling over 10,000 tons, were used (notably the "Guohao" machine).

### 3. Plant Bioacoustics Research
*   **Finding:** Plants emit ultrasonic sounds (40–80 kHz), which are inaudible to humans (limit ~20 kHz).
*   **Correlation:** When water-stressed, plants increase emission frequency from approximately once per hour to dozens of times per hour.
*   **Acoustic Influence on Growth:** Specific sound frequencies (e.g., 1,000 Hz, 90–95 dB) can upregulate gene expression and enzyme activity, promoting ethylene and anthocyanin synthesis. This leads to earlier ripening and improved appearance in grapes.
*   **Other Effects:** Light music stimulation was observed to increase the growth rate and protein content of duckweed.
*   **Application:** Potential for monitoring crop health and optimizing irrigation/fertilization in ecological agriculture.

### 4. Policy Update: Ecological Product Gross Value Accounting
*   **Document:** *Ecological Product Gross Value Accounting Standards (Trial)* was revised and officially issued in November 2026.
*   **Issuing Bodies:** National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC), Ministry of Natural Resources, National Bureau of Statistics, and other departments.
*   **Key Revisions:**
    *   Added chapters on accounting methods based on ecological product classification.
    *   Clarified statistical caliber and standardized data sources to resolve inconsistencies in regional parameters.
    *   Optimized the workflow by removing the "ecological product list compilation" step and simplifying parameter references, lowering the barrier for localized routine accounting.
    *   Aligned with national economic accounting systems and international standards to promote mutual recognition of results.

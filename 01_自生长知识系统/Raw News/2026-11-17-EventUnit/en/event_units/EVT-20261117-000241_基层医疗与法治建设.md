**2. Grassroots Dispute Resolution (Jiangsu)**
*   **Policy Framework:** Jiangsu Province adheres to and develops the new era "Fengqiao Experience," driven by directives from the Fifth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee regarding the integration of autonomy, rule of law, and virtue in grassroots governance.
*   **Statistical Achievement:** During the "14th Five-Year Plan" period, Jiangsu resolved a cumulative total of **7.569 million** conflicts and disputes. Over **95%** of these were resolved at the township and village levels.
*   **Mechanism:** The province promotes the sinking of governance forces and the integration of dispute resolution resources to listen to public appeals and settle neighborhood conflicts.

**3. Case Studies in Jiangsu Governance**
*   **Nanjing (Noise Dispute):** Residents in Wuyi Oasis, Jiangning District, complained about low-frequency resonance from Metro Line 5. A "Three Meetings" mechanism (Community Stability Assessment Meeting, Street Session Meeting, Resident Consultation Meeting) facilitated dialogue between residents, traffic/transport departments, ecology/environment bureaus, and the metro company. Investigation revealed specific train batches caused resonance at certain speeds. Resolution involved adjusting train operation times and optimizing vibration reduction technologies. Jiangning District resolved 18,942 disputes this year with a 98.37% resolution rate.
*   **Yancheng (Property Damage Dispute):** An elderly resident (Gao Huilin) in Dafeng District had a 3-year dispute with a construction company over property damage (security mesh, air conditioning) caused by elevator installation. The Dafeng District Comprehensive Governance Center led a special mediation task force. The mechanism included:
    *   Housing and Urban-Rural Development Bureau verifying contract performance and liability.
    *   Judges providing pre-litigation professional guidance on property damage and contract breach laws.
    *   Mediators distinguishing between statutory compensation and emotional compensation.
    *   Result: Voluntary agreement on payment amounts and timelines was reached within a legal framework.
*   **Wuxi (Corporate Equity Dispute):** A company in Xinwu District underwent equity changes, affecting 113 employees' compensation. The Xinwu District Social Contradiction Comprehensive Governance Information Platform issued a warning. A task force led by the District Political and Legal Affairs Commission (involving HR, Commerce, Public Security, and街道/sub-district) intervened within 15 working days. The platform integrated data from 9 lines (12345, 12315, Public Security, HR, Petition, etc.) to identify patterns like "one person, multiple lawsuits." All 113 employees reached an agreement.

## Event Overview

On November 17, 2026, multiple high-level diplomatic and economic events were reported in Chinese state media, centering on China’s foreign engagement in Southeast Asia and the Arab world, as well as domestic economic performance during the APEC China year.

The primary event synthesized from these sources is the concurrent observation of China’s diplomatic activities in two key regions: **Southeast Asia (ASEAN)** and the **Arab world**. These activities are part of China’s broader strategy as the host of the 2026 APEC year, emphasizing innovation, green transition, and digital cooperation across the Asia-Pacific region.

Key developments include:
- Premier Li Qiang’s attendance at the 29th China-ASEAN Leaders’ Meeting.
- Li Hongzhong’s meeting with foreign delegates from the 5th China-Arab States Party Dialogue Conference.
- China-Indonesia bilateral cooperation milestones, including the first Indonesian panda bond issuance in China and the opening of an Indonesian RMB clearing bank.
- Statistical releases indicating China’s October economic performance, highlighting steady growth in high-tech manufacturing and new economic drivers.

### Economic Performance (October 2026)

5. **Industrial Output:**
   - Industrial value-added above designated size grew **4.8% YoY** in October.
   - High-tech manufacturing value-added grew **17.6% YoY**, outpacing overall industrial growth by 12.8 percentage points.
   - Source: Article #367, Article #368.

6. **Trade:**
   - Total imports and exports grew **31.2% YoY** in October.
   - Imports grew **34.7% YoY**, outpacing exports by 6 percentage points.
   - High-tech products and “new three items” (EVs, lithium batteries, solar cells) exports grew over **50% YoY**.
   - Source: Article #367, Article #368.

7. **Consumption:**
   - Retail sales of consumer goods and services grew **2.5%** in the first 10 months.
   - Service retail sales grew **4.9%** in the first 10 months, outpacing goods retail sales by 4 percentage points.
   - Online service retail sales grew **5%** in the first 10 months.
   - Source: Article #367, Article #368.

8. **Employment and Prices:**
   - National urban survey unemployment rate was **5.1%** in October, down 0.1 percentage points from September.
   - Core CPI (excluding food and energy) rose **0.8% YoY** in October.
   - CPI rose **0.7% YoY** in October.
   - Source: Article #367, Article #368.

9. **Investment:**
   - Investment in intellectual property products grew **9.2%** in the first 10 months.
   - Investment in machinery and equipment tools grew **8.9%** in the first 10 months.
   - Overall fixed-asset investment declined in the first 10 months, attributed to economic stage transitions and industrial restructuring.
   - Source: Article #367, Article #368.

10. **New Energy and Green Transition:**
    - New energy vehicle production grew **17.1% YoY** in October.
    - Clean energy generation accounted for a higher share of total industrial generation.
    - Source: Article #361, Article #367, Article #368.

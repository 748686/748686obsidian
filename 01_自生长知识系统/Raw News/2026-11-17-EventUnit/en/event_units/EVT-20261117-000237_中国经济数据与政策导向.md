### 2. Ecological Restoration and Industrial Transition
*   **Case Study (Hefei, Anhui):** The Hefei Eastern New Center, formerly an industrial hub (steel, textiles, chemical) accounting for ~70% of municipal industrial output, faced soil pollution on approximately 5,200 mu of land.
*   **Remediation Action:**
    *   Integrated into the city’s "14th Five-Year Plan" ecological restoration goals.
    *   Utilized the Nanjing Institute of Environmental Sciences (Ministry of Ecology and Environment) for technical consulting.
    *   Implemented a "141+" management model: 1 technical consulting unit, 4 supervisory units (construction, engineering, environmental, assessment), 1 third-party inspection unit, plus a smart management system.
    *   Divided into 16 sub-projects for classified, zoned, and phased治理.
*   **Outcomes:**
    *   Completed remediation of over 4,200 mu; 3,900+ mu removed from the polluted land名录.
    *   Enabled construction of urban expressways, metro lines, and modern industrial parks.
    *   Established nearly 100 mu of urban green space.
    *   Formulated 4 provincial group standards, filling gaps in Anhui’s soil remediation technical norms.
    *   Recognized by the Ministry of Ecology and Environment in 2025 as a "New Era Ecological Civilization Construction Practice Case."
*   **Case Study (Qiandao Lake, Zhejiang):** In Chun’an County, Hangzhou, ecological floating islands were deployed in typical reservoir bays, river mouths, and wastewater treatment plant outfalls. On November 16, 2026, staff planted winter-growing water celery to purify water bodies and create habitats for birds and fish.

## Core Facts
- **Event:** Software bug outage at Chase bank.
- **Date:** November 17, 2026.
- **Impact:** Disrupted online banking services.
- **Duration:** Approximately five hours (from around 12:30 p.m. ET to approximately 5:45 p.m. ET).

- **Scope:** Affected customers across various states, including California, Illinois, Minnesota, and New York.

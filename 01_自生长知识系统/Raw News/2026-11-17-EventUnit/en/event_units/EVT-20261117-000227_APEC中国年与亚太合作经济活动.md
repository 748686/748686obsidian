## Event Overview

This event synthesizes reporting from November 17, 2026, regarding the diplomatic and economic preparations for the 2026 APEC China Year, specifically focusing on the upcoming APEC Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen. The primary source of substantive information is an interview with Roh Jae-heon (卢载宪), the Ambassador of the Republic of Korea to China, published in *People's Daily*. The reporting highlights the continuity of high-level China-Korea diplomatic engagement, the strategic importance of APEC as a platform for regional integration, and specific areas of bilateral and multilateral cooperation, including artificial intelligence (AI), digital economy, and supply chain resilience.

## Core Facts

**Date of Event/Reporting:** November 17, 2026
**Location Context:** Shenzhen, China (Host of upcoming APEC); Seoul/Gyeongju, South Korea (Host of previous APEC)
**Key Actor:** Roh Jae-heon (卢载宪), Ambassador of the Republic of Korea to China
**Source of Primary Information:** *People's Daily* (Interview conducted by reporter Chen Shangwen)

1.  **Continuity of APEC Hosting:** Roh Jae-heon emphasized the significance of China and South Korea consecutively hosting APEC Leaders' Meetings, noting the transition from Gyeongju (the previous year) to Shenzhen (the current year).
2.  **Economic Weight of APEC:** The APEC forum comprises 21 economies that account for over 60% of global GDP and nearly half of global trade volume.
3.  **Strategic Shift in APEC Focus:** Beyond traditional trade and investment liberalization, APEC cooperation has expanded to include:
    *   New growth drivers under AI and digital transformation.
    *   Enhancement of regional supply chain resilience.
4.  **China-Korea Relations:**
    *   President Lee Jae-myung (李在明) of South Korea paid an official state visit to China earlier in 2026.
    *   President Xi Jinping visited Gyeongju for the previous APEC summit and conducted an official state visit to South Korea 11 years after his prior visit.
    *   The two nations are advancing their strategic cooperative partnership to a higher level.
5.  **AI and Technological Cooperation:**
    *   Roh highlighted China’s "remarkable results" in AI development, driven by clear policy direction and synergy between government and enterprise in R&D and commercialization.
    *   China’s rise in embodied AI and AI application sectors is noted as receiving global attention.
    *   Future cooperation is expected to be led by civil society/people-to-people exchanges, particularly in using AI to cultivate new economic growth drivers and address shared challenges such as climate change, infectious diseases, and aging populations.
6.  **Korean Diplomatic Role:** South Korea, as the previous host, intends to build upon the *APEC AI Initiative* and the *APEC Framework for Action on Population Structure Changes* adopted in Gyeongju, contributing to the success of the Shenzhen meeting.

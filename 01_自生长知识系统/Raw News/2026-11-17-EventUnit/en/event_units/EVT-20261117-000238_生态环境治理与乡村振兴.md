## Event Overview
On November 17, 2026, *People's Daily* published a series of reports highlighting ecological civilization practices in China. These articles document the transition from resource extraction and environmental degradation to sustainable ecological tourism and reforestation. The coverage focuses on three distinct case studies: the conversion of an abandoned quarry village into a cultural tourism destination in Tianjin; large-scale afforestation efforts in Inner Mongolia; and the integration of ethnic unity with ecological and social development in Xinjiang. The underlying theme is the "Green Waters and Green Mountains" strategy, where ecological restoration drives economic revitalization and social cohesion.

## Core Facts

**1. Ecological Tourism in Tianjin (Xijingyu Village)**
*   **Location:** Xijingyu Village, Yuyang Town, Jizhou District, Tianjin.
*   **Historical Context:** In the 1980s and 1990s, the village’s economy relied on quarrying. By 2004, mines were fully closed, leaving exposed山体 (mountain faces) and dust issues.
*   **Transformation:** Post-closure ecological restoration revealed the village’s traditional stone architecture (shale walls, quartz roads, grey tile roofs), leading to its nickname "Stone Village."
*   **Economic Shift:** The village moved from selling stones to selling scenery and experiences.
    *   **Homestays:** As of the report date, there are 51 boutique homestays. Average annual visitor count exceeds 500,000. One-third of households are engaged in tourism.
    *   **Branding:** In 2015, a professional operation team was introduced to establish the "Youxuan Nongshe" (Preferred Farmhouse) cooperative and unify visual planning based on stone elements.
    *   **New Business Formats:** Includes a kiln-baked bread shop using stone ovens and walnut/chestnut wood (peak daily revenue ~10,000 RMB), coffee shops, and art studios.
*   **Key Figures:**
    *   Zhou Jian (Village Party Secretary): Noted the shift from "stone rice" to tourism.
    *   Zhou Zhihua (Intangible Cultural Heritage inheritor of stone masonry) and his son Zhou Yunlong: Upgraded a family farm to a boutique homestay with smart home features; peak season income approaches 400,000 RMB annually.
    *   Li Xiaohuan (Bread Shop Manager): Reports high demand from Beijing tourists.
    *   Liu Anqin (82-year-old "New Villager"): Moved from Heilongjiang to operate a stone painting art studio.

**2. Afforestation in Inner Mongolia (Baobu Township)**
*   **Location:** Baobu Township, Qingshuihe County, Hohhot, Inner Mongolia.
*   **Key Figure:** Lu Shuanhou (94 years old, former Deputy Secretary of the Township Party Committee).
*   **Problem:** The area previously had severe soil erosion, sandstorms, and low crop yields due to lack of vegetation.
*   **Methodology:** Lu developed a scientific planting method summarized as "wearing a hat, wearing boots, and tying a belt":
    *   "Wearing a hat": Planting drought-resistant trees/shrubs on mountain peaks to fix soil.
    *   "Wearing boots": Building check dams in gullies to intercept sediment.
    *   "Tying a belt": Building terraces on slopes for agriculture.
*   **Results:**
    *   Forest coverage in Qingshuihe County rose from <0.1% to 20.62%.
    *   Grassland comprehensive vegetation coverage stabilizes at 50%.
    *   Baobu Township now has a 20km x 10km ecological forest belt.
    *   Sandstorms and floods are effectively controlled; no major floods in nearly 30 years.
    *   Economic benefits: Development of apricot and sea buckthorn orchards and processing plants; some大户 (large households) increase income by over 30,000 RMB annually.
*   **Personal Sacrifice:** In the 1980s, Lu suffered a broken leg and three rib fractures while protecting an elderly villager from a rockslide.

**3. Ethnic Unity and Ecological/Social Governance in Xinjiang**
*   **Locations:** Ili Kazakh Autonomous Prefecture (Huocheng County), Aksu City, Tacheng City, Urumqi City.
*   **Framework:** "Tangible" carriers, "Perceptible" bonds, and "Effective" grasps to forge a strong sense of community for the Chinese nation.
*   **Cultural/Historical:**
    *   Ili General's Manor (Ili): A national key cultural relic site receiving over 600,000 youth visitors for patriotic education.
    *   Aksu Experience Hall: Uses digital technology and interactive exhibits (e.g., "Zhu Lao" cruise ship) to display 5,000+ years of shared Chinese history.
*   **Social Integration:**
    *   Haerdun Community (Tacheng): Hosts "Hundred Families Banquets" featuring foods from 14 different ethnic groups (Kazakh, Han, Uzbek, Russian, etc.). 466 out of 1,510 households are multi-ethnic families.
    *   Guyuanxiang Community (Urumqi): Offers interest classes and resolves resident issues through party-building liaison meetings.
*   **Role Models:**
    *   Shalekejiang Yiming: Organized 1,418 flag-raising ceremonies since Oct 2019, reaching over 450,000 listeners.
    *   Lü Peigui & Yacen Tusun: Lü has helped his Uyghur brother Yacen for over 20 years, helping him build a house and buy agricultural machinery (annual income ~200,000 RMB). Lü’s cooperative now serves 200+ ethnic masses.

## Event Overview
On November 16, 2026, OpenAI announced two significant initiatives aimed at reinforcing AI safety protocols and expanding access to its frontier models for the scientific community. The company launched its first phase of "embedded assessments" through partnerships with independent research organizations to evaluate model alignment and safeguards. These moves reflect a dual strategy of ensuring safety governance while broadening the utility of advanced AI in academic settings.

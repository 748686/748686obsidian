### [Second World Humanoid Robot Games Held in Beijing](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/01/content_30178419.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The Second World Humanoid Robot Games recently took place at the National Speed Skating Oval, known as "Ice Ribbon," in Beijing. The event attracted 666 teams from 16 countries and 2,056 robots, marking a 138% increase in team participation compared to the first edition and a fourfold growth in the number of robots. The competition expanded its scenario-based events into real-world environments, covering sectors such as industry, hospitality, domestic use, and logistics.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 1, 00:26

**「Background」** The World Humanoid Robot Games is an international competition designed to advance humanoid robotics through practical, real-world challenges rather than purely theoretical benchmarks. By testing robots in environments like factories, hotels, and homes, organizers aim to accelerate the transition of humanoid robots from laboratories to commercial and domestic applications.

**「Impact」** The significant increase in international participation signals growing global investment and competitiveness in humanoid robotics development. Expansion into real-world operational scenarios provides manufacturers with critical performance data needed to refine robots for industrial and consumer deployment.

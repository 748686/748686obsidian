### [China&\#x27;s Ministry of Justice Advances National University Student Rule-of-Law Promotion Program](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180433.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 10, 2026, China&\#x27;s Ministry of Justice convened a work-promotion meeting for the National University Student Rule-of-Law Promotion Group, as reported by People&\#x27;s Daily on September 11. The meeting stressed the need to implement the requirements of the Ninth Five-Year Legal Publicity Plan, summarize and disseminate the experience of the first batch of student rule-of-law promotion groups, and build the program as a recognized brand to motivate university students to engage in legal education and publicity. The ministry also outlined plans to form a second batch of national university student rule-of-law promotion groups, with a focus on studying and publicizing Xi Jinping&\#x27;s rule-of-law thought, and to organize touring performances and exhibitions of high-quality promotional outputs to create a demonstration effect. The stated goals include持续提升 \(continuously improving\) the rule-of-law literacy of young people and guiding youth to become devoted supporters, observers, and defenders of the socialist rule of law.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Legal Publicity Planning Framework」** China implements periodic legal publicity and education plans \(普法规划\), with the current "九五" plan explicitly designating youth as a priority group and calling for a structured youth legal-education framework. The Legal Publicity and Education Law \(法治宣传教育法\), which governs these plans, establishes the principle of "依法普法" \(rule-of-law-based legal education\) and sets out evaluation and supervision mechanisms administered by judicial administrative departments. The National College Student Rule of Law Lecture Team \(全国大学生法治宣讲团\) is a mechanism created under this framework to mobilize university students as peer educators who disseminate legal knowledge and Xi&\#x27;s rule-of-law thought among the broader public.

**「Impact on Youth Legal Education」** The proposed formation of a second batch of national university student rule-of-law propaganda teams will expand youth-led legal outreach across China through touring exhibitions of quality propaganda achievements, directly increasing the number of student participants serving as community legal educators. Because the second batch is still at the research and planning stage, its size, timeline, and specific institutional arrangements remain undetermined.

### [Gansu Releases Ten Civil Rural Culture Construction Work Cases](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180434.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 10, 2026, the Gansu Provincial Party Committee Propaganda Department, the Provincial Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, and the Provincial Department of Culture and Tourism held a provincial civil rural culture construction exchange activity in Qingyang, Gansu, and released 10 work cases. Among the highlighted cases, Huachi County in Qingyang leveraged its &\#x27;Nanliang Spark&\#x27; lecture brand to activate red cultural resources, Guanghe County in Linxia used village-level basketball tournaments \(&\#x27;Village BA&\#x27;\) to cultivate wholesome rural customs, and the ten-thousand-mu pear orchard in Gaolan, Lanzhou, together with Zhagana Village in Diebu, Gannan, explored creative transformation of agricultural cultural heritage and traditional farming culture. In Qingyang alone, 1,261 villages and 94 urban communities have completed revisions of their village covenants, and 4,825 volunteer service teams comprising 517,000 registered volunteers are active across the city. The initiative reflects a provincial-level effort to integrate cultural heritage, sports, agriculture, and volunteer service into a unified framework for rural civility construction.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「China&\#x27;s Civil Rural Culture Initiative」** Civil rural culture building \(文明乡风建设\) is one of the four pillars of China&\#x27;s national rural revitalization strategy, alongside prosperous industry, effective governance, and rural ecological beauty. It focuses on reshaping rural social norms through village covenants \(村规民约\), volunteer service networks, preservation of agricultural and intangible cultural heritage, and community sports and public-interest activities. Gansu Province, a western region with diverse ethnic and agricultural traditions, has incorporated such efforts into its broader 2026 rural development plan, which includes 220 key agricultural projects totaling 5.32 billion yuan in investment.

**「Impact」** The ten published cases provide county-level governments across Gansu and other provinces with concrete, replicable models for embedding cultural, sporting, and agricultural heritage into grassroots civil-culture programs, giving local officials and community organizers actionable templates for revising village covenants and mobilizing volunteer forces.

### [Nantong Tongzhou District Consolidates New-Resident Services in Textile Town Chuanjiang](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180435.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Chuanjiang Town in Tongzhou District, Nantong City, Jiangsu Province, has absorbed 85,000 new residents in recent years owing to its textile-industry base, prompting local authorities to consolidate public security, human resources, and health services into a single New Resident Party-Mass Service Center. The center offers a dedicated new-resident service window handling cross-province identity-card applications, children&\#x27;s school-enrollment paperwork, and other one-stop conveniences. The district has formalised a &\#x27;party committee takes primary responsibility, party branch takes primary grasp, party members take primary role&\#x27; mechanism, listing long-term, high-impact projects such as the new-resident service centre and the Airport Red Bay New Employment Group Home in its annual public-welfare work plan. According to the district&\#x27;s Organization Department head, officials aim to treat residents&\#x27; pressing concerns as top-priority matters so that small everyday issues become sources of household well-being. The article, published in People&\#x27;s Daily on 11 September 2026, frames the initiative as part of a broader effort to respond to residents&\#x27; most urgent difficulties and anxieties.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Background」** In Chinese urban policy, "new residents" \(新市民\) typically refers to migrants who have settled in a city but have not yet obtained local household registration \(hukou\), which can limit their access to public services such as education and healthcare. The governance phrase "急难愁盼" groups the four categories of public concern—urgent, difficult, worrisome, and anticipated issues—that local party committees are directed to prioritize. Chuanjiang Town in Tongzhou District is a long-standing textile manufacturing hub in Nantong, Jiangsu Province, whose industry drew roughly 85,000 migrant workers in recent years, creating a concentrated demand for cross-departmental public services.

**「Impact」** The 85,000 new residents of Chuanjiang Town can now complete multiple government-service transactions—previously spread across separate agencies—under one roof, reducing the administrative burden on migrants and their families settling in the area.

### [Yunnan Forms 7,025 Police Mentor-Apprentice Pairs to Strengthen Grassroots Conflict Resolution](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180441.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Yunnan Province&\#x27;s Public Security Department has launched a "mentor-apprentice pairing" \(师徒结对\) initiative across grassroots police stations, forming 7,025 pairs of veteran and junior officers to transmit on-the-ground community work skills. The program, exemplified by 58-year-old community officer Zhou Zhixian \(a former deputy chief of Nanhua County Public Security Bureau who stepped down in November 2024\) mentoring young officer Xie Lifang since September 2025, focuses on resolving everyday disputes and fraud schemes before they escalate. In one case the pair mediated a landlord-tenant wall-crack dispute by commissioning a building-department survey that attributed 80% of repair costs to the landlord and 20% to the tenant; in another, Zhou undercover-discovered a company illegally raising 500,000 yuan from 33 elderly residents under a fake agricultural-product investment scheme, prompting a multi-agency investigation that returned the full sum. Qian Xingzhong, deputy division chief of the Personnel Building Division at the provincial public security department, stated the goal is to embed the modern "Fengqiao Experience" into daily enforcement by pairing seasoned judgment with younger officers&\#x27; digital tools.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Fengqiao Experience Context」** The "Fengqiao Experience" \(枫桥经验\), originating from a 1960s grassroots mediation model in Zhejiang province, is a long-standing Chinese governance principle that disputes should be resolved at the village or community level before they require formal litigation or upward escalation. Community police officers \(社区民警\) in China are assigned to specific neighborhoods and are expected to maintain relationships with residents, mediate minor conflicts, and identify emerging risks such as fraud, rather than waiting for formal police reports.

**「Effect on Elderly Residents and Junior Officers」** Elderly community members in Yunnan&\#x27;s grass-roots districts gain a structured early-warning channel for investment-fraud and neighbor-dispute risks, while junior officers receive hands-on training in legal adjudication and public communication that shortens the learning curve previously requiring years of independent trial-and-error. The 7,025-pair scale suggests the province is institutionalizing this transfer of tacit knowledge rather than leaving it to informal, station-by-station practice.

### [People&\#x27;s Daily Calls for Unlocking Rural Tourism Consumption Potential](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180499.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A September 11, 2026 commentary by Ren Rong, associate professor at Shandong University&\#x27;s School of Management, published in People&\#x27;s Daily argues that China&\#x27;s rural tourism market is expanding as 2025 rural per-capita disposable income reached 24,456 yuan, a real 6 percent year-on-year gain, and that the 4.7 million kilometers of rural roads plus universal township bus coverage have made "spontaneous" trips feasible. The piece links this shift to the Tourism Power Construction 15th Five-Year Plan&\#x27;s goal of promoting two-way urban-rural tourist flows and complementary resource use, and urges operators to supply products matching rural preferences in niche segments such as self-driving, study, and wellness travel. It also flags persistent gaps—supply-demand product mismatch and information-skill barriers—and recommends township-level tourism consulting outposts, consumption subsidies, and off-peak travel incentives as long-term mechanisms. The broader argument is that releasing rural tourism spending both injects new demand into the domestic economy and strengthens the common-prosperity foundation.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Context: China&\#x27;s Tourism Planning and Urban-Rural Divide」** The &\#x27;Fifteenth Five-Year Plan&\#x27; \(十五五, 2026–2030\) for building China into a tourism powerhouse, referenced in the article, is the national economic and social planning framework that sets multi-year targets for sectors including tourism; its emphasis on two-way flow of tourists and resources between cities and countryside targets the long-standing urban–rural dual structure \(城乡二元结构\) that has historically channeled tourism demand and investment toward urban centers. The article also alludes to China&\#x27;s rural infrastructure catch-up, where the 4.7-million-kilometer rural road network and universal township bus access now underpin what was previously an urban-only leisure activity.

**「Impact on rural tourism stakeholders」** For rural tourism operators, transportation providers, and local governments, the shift of rural residents from tourism &\#x27;hosts&\#x27; to &\#x27;travelers&\#x27; creates a new, growing consumer segment that could sustainably expand revenue across transport, lodging, and food-service sectors, but only if product-supply mismatches and information-access barriers are resolved. The 18.0% year-on-year increase in domestic tourism trips \(4.998 billion in the first three quarters of 2025\) underscores the broader market momentum into which this rural traveler segment is entering.

### [Qiyunfang Advances Domestic EDA and CIM Software for China&\#x27;s Semiconductor Industry](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180467.html) ⭐️ ?/10

People&\#x27;s Daily reported on September 11, 2026 that Qiyunfang Technology \(启云方科技\) has built a fully domestic industrial software stack covering EDA \(electronic design automation\) and CIM \(computer integrated manufacturing\) for China&\#x27;s semiconductor and electronics sectors, with 100% self-controllability from underlying hardware and operating systems through middleware to business modules. The EDA platform supports 100 engineers collaborating on circuit schematic design and 20 on PCB layout, shortening product time-to-market by more than 40%, while its integrated AI features for auto-library building and intelligent routing reduce repetitive design tasks. The CIM solution comprises over 30 microservice-based subsystems with big-data and AI capabilities, has been deployed across more than 10 twelve-inch wafer fab production lines, and achieved a landmark delivery at an ultra-large wafer fab in 2025. The broader ecosystem now covers the full &\#x27;R&D–production–supply–marketing–service&\#x27; chain and serves over 20,000 engineers, addressing a long-standing dependence on foreign EDA and CIM vendors in China&\#x27;s chip industry.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「EDA and CIM in the semiconductor supply chain」** EDA \(Electronic Design Automation\) software and CIM \(Computer Integrated Manufacturing\) systems are the foundational digital tools of the semiconductor industry: EDA drives chip and PCB design from schematic to layout, while CIM orchestrates the day-to-day operations of a wafer fabrication plant. The global EDA market remains heavily concentrated among three firms—Synopsys, Cadence, and Siemens EDA—which together control nearly 70% of the industry, and in May 2025 the United States directed those three companies to halt exports to China, sharpening the urgency for domestic Chinese manufacturers to build indigenous, full-stack industrial software.

**「Domestic CIM option for 12-inch fab operators」** Chinese semiconductor fab operators expanding 12-inch production lines—amid global 12-inch wafer fab equipment investment that SEMI projected would exceed USD 100 billion in 2025—can now deploy a fully domestic, AI-integrated CIM system \(30+ subsystems, microservice-based\) already validated on more than 10 such production lines, materially reducing their manufacturing-software dependence on imported vendor stacks.

### [Xmi Wuhan Smart Appliance Factory Achieves 6.5-Second Air Conditioner Output](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180468.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Xiaomi&\#x27;s smart home appliance factory in Wuhan&\#x27;s Donghu High-Tech Zone now produces one air conditioner every 6.5 seconds, powered by the company&\#x27;s self-developed Pengpai Smart Manufacturing Platform. The main production lines integrate 65 sets of AI visual inspection systems and 120,000 data-collection nodes, enabling a first-pass yield rate above 99 % for key components. A 4.2 km aerial conveyor belt equipped with an AI fault-diagnosis system links six workshops and handles 80 % of in-plant material transport, while more than 100 autonomous mobile robots manage ground-level part delivery. Xiaomi&\#x27;s self-developed AI control algorithm has been embedded across the full quality-detection pipeline, and the company reported 19.6 million cumulatively connected air-conditioner units that support cloud-based, second-level fault screening for end users. With the Wuhan plant completed, Xiaomi has closed a full design–R&D–production–verification loop and stated plans to extend its smart-control algorithms to refrigerators, washing machines, and other major appliance categories.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Context」** The Xiaomi Pheonix Smart Manufacturing Platform \(小米澎湃智能制造平台\) is a self-developed, fully digitalized and informatized production system designed to integrate AI-driven quality inspection, real-time data collection, and autonomous logistics coordination across factory operations. The Wuhan smart home appliance factory, situated in the East Lake High-Tech Zone on a 751-mu site, is Xiaomi&\#x27;s first dedicated smart home appliance manufacturing facility and completes the company&\#x27;s full in-house loop spanning design, R&D, production, and verification, a capability it had previously lacked by outsourcing most white-goods manufacturing to contract partners. This factory formalizes Xiaomi&\#x27;s vertical integration into large-appliance production, extending the AI-control algorithms originally developed for smartphones into physical home-appliance manufacturing.

**「Industry Impact」** Xiaomi&\#x27;s stated plan to extend its proprietary AI smart-control algorithms from air conditioners to refrigerators, washing machines, and all major appliance categories will raise the manufacturing baseline for AI-integrated quality inspection \(first-pass yield above 99%\) and cloud-based second-level fault diagnostics across China&\#x27;s white-goods sector. Competing appliance makers may face increasing pressure to adopt comparable AI-driven logistics and post-sale service architectures to match the one-visit repair efficiency now enabled by Xiaomi&\#x27;s 19.6-million-unit connected fleet.

### [Fiberhome Unveils 800G Transoceanic Optical Transmission System](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180469.html) ⭐️ ?/10

Fiberhome Communications, a subsidiary of China Information and Communication Technology Group, unveiled an 800G transoceanic ultra-large-capacity optical transmission system at the 21st China Guanggu Optoelectronic Expo in May 2026, with a demonstrated reach of 6,000 kilometers that fills China&\#x27;s gap above 200G in cross-ocean transmission. The system achieves a single-fiber-pair capacity of 26.4 Tbps and a spectral efficiency of 5.5 bits per second per Hertz, setting a domestic record for similar systems. The announcement comes amid surging demand from AI model training, cloud computing, and cross-regional computing-power scheduling, all of which rely on submarine cables that already carry 99 percent of intercontinental data traffic. In the same year, Fiberhome&\#x27;s manufacturing arm also introduced a 300-millimeter-diameter, 3.5-meter fiber preform capable of producing roughly 20,000 kilometers of optical fiber per rod, a major productivity leap from the previous 80-millimeter standard that yielded only 400 kilometers. The company frames these advances as part of a broader push for technological self-reliance in high-speed, long-haul marine optical communications.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Submarine Cables and Optical Fiber Preforms」** Submarine optical cables carry roughly 99% of global intercontinental data traffic; transoceanic links have moved from earlier 200G-class systems to a current mainstream of 400G, making 800G the next capacity frontier. An optical fiber preform is a high-purity silica rod from which thin fiber strands are drawn in a manufacturing process—larger-diameter preforms let a single rod yield far more kilometers of fiber at lower unit cost. The rise of AI-era workloads such as large-model training, cloud services, and cross-regional compute scheduling has sharply increased demand for higher-capacity, longer-distance transoceanic transmission.

**「Impact on Submarine-Cable Operators and AI Infrastructure」** For cloud and AI operators expanding intercontinental connectivity, the system&\#x27;s 26.4 Tbps per-fiber-pair capacity at 6,000 km reach reduces the fiber-pair count needed for a given bandwidth, and the 300 mm preform—yielding roughly 20,000 km of fiber per rod versus ~400 km from legacy 80 mm preforms—lowers manufacturing cost and lead time for new submarine-cable builds such as the 2Africa project entering commercial service in 2026.

### [Ruoikes Laser Completes Domestic Fiber Laser Industry Upgrade in China](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/11/content_30180470.html) ⭐️ ?/10

People&\#x27;s Daily \(September 11, 2026\) profiled Ruoikes Laser&\#x27;s decade-plus journey from a 2007 founding into a fully domesticated industrial fiber laser platform. In 2013 the company produced China&\#x27;s first 10,000-watt continuous fiber laser, yet key raw materials and components remained under foreign patent control. Beginning in 2018, the R&D team developed a circular solid doped fiber architecture deliberately distinct from the prevailing foreign octagonal fiber route, then vertically integrated from special fibers and critical components through to complete machine systems. Ruoikes now markets six product categories spanning more than 200 varieties, with its single-mode 10,000-watt fiber laser described as reaching leading performance at the same power class. Applications cited include C919 aircraft &\#x27;welding-in-place-of-riveting&\#x27; custom lasers, high-speed rail track laser cladding, shipbuilding cut-and-weld operations, aerospace precision machining, new-energy battery micro-processing, high-voltage line intelligent clearing, and emerging fields such as embodied intelligence.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 11, 00:26

**「Background」** Industrial fiber lasers are core tooling for high-precision cutting, welding, and 3D printing in advanced manufacturing; before Chinese domestic entry, the market was dominated by foreign suppliers whose patents and key optical materials created a structural dependency. A single imported high-power laser unit previously carried a price tag of roughly 8 million RMB, with the underlying fiber-design and gain-media technology held by overseas firms.

**「Impact」** Chinese manufacturers in aviation, rail, shipbuilding, and new-energy sectors can now procure lower-cost, domestically controlled high-power laser systems, reducing both capital expenditure and exposure to foreign patent or export-control restrictions on critical optical components.

越来越多的农村居民有旅游意愿，根源在于“钱袋子”更鼓了。2025年，我国农村居民人均可支配收入24456元，比上年实际增长6%。农村居民出游热潮涌动，是城乡居民收入差距缩小、生活品质提升的生动写照。

从乡村旅游的“接待者”到走出家门的“出游者”，农村居民消费观念悄然转变，带动交通、住宿、餐饮等营收持续增长。无论景区游，还是自驾游，越来越多的农村居民将旅游融入日常，丰富了美好生活，也为促进消费、扩大内需注入新的活力。

农民出游意愿提升，离不开日益完善的交通基础设施作支撑。近年来，我国高铁网络不断延伸，农村公路总里程达470万公里，具备条件的乡镇和建制村全部实现通客车，农村地区汽车普及率不断提升，为“说走就走的旅行”创造了便利。

激活农民旅游消费新动能，需要纳入城乡融合发展的大局统筹推进。《旅游强国建设“十五五”规划》提出，“促进城乡客源双向流动、资源优势互补。”市民下乡、农民进城、城乡游客双向奔赴，正是以城乡要素双向流动破除二元壁垒的重要抓手。双向流动重塑格局，出游反哺乡村振兴。

自驾游、研学游、康养游等细分旅游市场，为农村居民出游提供了多元选择。但不容忽视的是，农民出游还面临产品供需错配、信息技能壁垒等痛点。未来，应鼓励经营主体开发更多适配农民的旅游产品，完善乡镇一级旅游咨询服务网点，探索建立农民出游消费补贴、错峰出游激励等长效机制。

持续完善适配农民的旅游产品供给与公共服务保障，既是加快释放农民旅游消费潜力的有效途径，也是夯实共同富裕基础的必然要求。

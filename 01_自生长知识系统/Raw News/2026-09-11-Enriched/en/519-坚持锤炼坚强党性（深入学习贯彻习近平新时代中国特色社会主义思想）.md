The app offers three core functions: an AI large-model intelligent Q&A engine that assesses fraud risk, identifies fraud types, and links video case studies in real time; an anti-fraud news feed embedding content from the National Anti-Fraud Center and Shanghai&\#x27;s "803" anti-fraud media matrix, which periodically publishes fraud-trend alerts and case breakdowns in text-plus-short-video format; and a searchable anti-fraud "dictionary" housing electronic copies of the national anti-fraud publicity handbook and guide, accessible via category browsing, keyword search, and voice reading. The app is available on major mobile app stores and as WeChat and Alipay mini-programs, making it accessible without requiring a separate installation.

**「Background」** Telecom and network fraud is a persistent public-security priority in China; the existing "National Anti-Fraud Center" \(国家反诈中心\) app has long served as the primary government-distributed tool for fraud warnings and device registration.

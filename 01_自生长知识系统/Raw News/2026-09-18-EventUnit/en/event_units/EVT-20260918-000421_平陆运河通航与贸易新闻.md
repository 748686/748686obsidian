# Pinglu Canal Opening to Navigation and Logistics Impact

## Event Overview
On September 16, 2026, the Pinglu Canal in Southwest China officially opened to navigation. This 134.2-kilometer waterway, which was constructed over four years, serves as a critical infrastructure component of China’s Western Land-Sea New Corridor. The opening is reported to significantly reduce inland shipping distances by over 560 kilometers for cargo moving from the southwest to coastal ports, thereby connecting regional industrial clusters directly with Southeast Asian markets and global supply chains.

## Core Facts
*   **Opening Date:** September 16, 2026.
*   **Location:** Pinglu Canal, Southwest China.
*   **Infrastructure Details:**
    *   **Length:** 134.2 kilometers.
    *   **Construction Duration:** Four years.
    *   **Vessel Capacity:** Designed to carry 5,000-ton vessels.
    *   **Distance Reduction:** Cuts over 560 kilometers off the inland journey for cargo moving to ports.
*   **Projected Cost Savings:** Overall logistics costs are expected to decrease by 18% to 30% compared to previous inland shipping routes.
*   **Strategic Significance:** The canal is a key part of China’s Western Land-Sea New Corridor.
*   **Related Context:** The event occurred concurrently with the opening of the 23rd China-ASEAN Expo and China-ASEAN Business & Investment Summit, where progress on the China-ASEAN Free Trade Area 3.0 was highlighted.

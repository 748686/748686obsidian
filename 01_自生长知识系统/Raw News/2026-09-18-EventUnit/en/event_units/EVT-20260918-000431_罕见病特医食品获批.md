# Event Name

Regulatory Approval of China's First Medical Formula Food Based on Amino Acid Components for Rare Disease

## Event Overview

On September 18, 2026, *People's Daily* reported that a new Special Medical Purpose Formula Food (SPF) was recently approved for registration in China. This product is the first of its kind in China to use a "nutrient component" formula (specifically amino acid components) designed for patients with phenylketonuria (PKU), a rare metabolic disease. The approval marks a shift from previously registered PKU-specific foods, which were "amino acid metabolic disorder" formulas that served as complete nutritional replacements. The new product allows patients to supplement most nutrients through regular low-protein food, potentially improving their quality of life by permitting more diverse diet choices.

## Core Facts

*   **Product Type:** Special Medical Purpose Formula Food (SPF) / 特殊医学用途配方食品 (特医食品).
*   **Classification:** Amino acid component formula (氨基酸组件类), distinct from the "amino acid metabolic disorder" formula used in previous PKU products.
*   **Target Population:** Phenylketonuria (PKU) patients aged 1 year and older.
*   **Significance:** Described as the first SPF in China applicable to rare disease patients that uses a nutrient component approach, filling a domestic gap ("填补了国内空白").
*   **Regulatory Status:** Approved for registration ("获批注册") in China.
*   **Precedent Context:** Prior to this approval, China had 8 registered PKU SPFs, all belonging to the "amino acid metabolic disorder" category, which required amino acids (with little/no phenylalanine) as the main ingredient supplemented with fats, carbs, vitamins, and minerals.
*   **Mechanism:** The new formula provides sufficient protein (amino acids); other essential nutrients (fats, carbs, vitamins, minerals) can be obtained from other low-protein regular foods.
*   **Patient Demographics (China):**
    *   Average incidence rate: Approximately 0.9 per 10,000 births (万分之0.9).
    *   Registered patient population: Estimated between 120,000 and 150,000 individuals.
*   **Clinical Context:** PKU patients require lifelong low-phenylalanine diet management under medical guidance.
*   **Efficacy Reference:** Cited research indicates that PKU patients using SPF saw disease improvement rates exceeding 94.8%.

## Event Conclusion

The approval of China's first rare disease-specific amino acid component medical formula food for phenylketonuria (PKU) represents a significant advancement in specialized nutrition care within China. By shifting from complete-replacement formulas to a component-based approach, this regulatory approval allows patients to integrate specialized protein sources into a broader diet, potentially improving quality of life and dietary adherence. This development fills a previously identified gap in China's medical food landscape for rare metabolic diseases.

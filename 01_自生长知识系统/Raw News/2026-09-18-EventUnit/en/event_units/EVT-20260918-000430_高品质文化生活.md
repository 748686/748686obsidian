## Event Overview

This EventUnit documents a theoretical article published in *People's Daily* (《人民日报》) on September 18, 2026. The article, titled "Letting the People Enjoy a Higher Quality of Cultural Life (Thematic Reflections)" (让人民享受更高品质的文化生活（专题深思）), was authored by Xiao Guiqing, a researcher at the Ministry of Education's Research Center for the Thought of Xi Jinping on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era.

The article outlines the conceptual framework for improving the quality of cultural life for the Chinese public. It defines "higher quality" through four key dimensions: accessibility of services, ideological depth of products, interactivity of participation, and agency of creation. The text advocates for a supply-side approach to cultural services, emphasizing the creation of people-centered works, the enhancement of public cultural service efficiency, support for grassroots cultural activities, and the systematic protection and transmission of cultural heritage. This aligns with the objectives of China's 15th Five-Year Plan regarding public cultural service systems.

*   **Core Definition:** "Higher quality cultural life" is defined as a qualitative breakthrough in four areas:
    1.  **Accessibility:** Making cultural services more "at hand" and effective, integrating culture with daily life.
    2.  **Ideological Depth:** Ensuring cultural products contain substantial content that fosters identification with socialist advanced culture, revolutionary culture, and outstanding traditional Chinese culture.
    3.  **Interactivity:** Enhancing the interactive and participatory nature of cultural experiences to foster emotional resonance and value identification.
    4.  **Agency:** Enabling the public to participate directly in cultural creation using digital media (short videos, online literature, digital music).
*   **Policy Reference:** The article references the "15th Five-Year Plan" (十五五规划纲要), which proposes to "improve the public cultural service system, inherit and promote the outstanding traditional Chinese culture, and let the people enjoy a higher quality of cultural life."
*   **Key Recommendations:**
    *   Adhere to a people-centered creation orientation.
    *   Improve the quality and efficiency of public cultural services (e.g., via urban libraries, community cultural stations).
    *   Leverage technology to bridge the urban-rural digital divide (smart museums, libraries, cloud services).
    *   Support grassroots cultural activities (citing Guizhou's "Village" brand activities and Yunnan's "Village Good Song Gathering" as examples of cultural IPs).
    *   Systematically protect and transmit cultural heritage, including intangible cultural heritage and national cultural parks.

# Event Name
Beijing Chest Hospital Launches Free Lung Cancer and Tuberculosis CT Screening Initiative in Yudu, Jiangxi

## Event Overview
On August 29, 2026, the Beijing Chest Hospital (Capital Medical University Affiliated Beijing Chest Hospital) initiated a mobile screening project in Yudu County, Jiangxi Province. The event, titled "Smoking Control and Lung Protection: Dual Screening Long March Project" (“控烟护肺·一扫双筛”肺癌、肺结核联合筛查“长征行”), involved providing free chest CT scans for lung cancer and tuberculosis to residents of former revolutionary base areas. The screening was conducted via mobile diagnostic vehicles at the Changzhengyuan Community and Siyuan Community in Yudu County. This activity is part of a broader "Three-Year Million Screening Action" launched by the hospital in April 2026, aiming to provide free screenings in 30 key counties across 15 provinces along the historical route of the Long March.

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Event:** August 29, 2026 (Reported on September 18, 2026).
*   **Location:** Changzhengyuan Community and Siyuan Community, Yudu County, Jiangxi Province (Historical departure point of the Red Army's Long March).
*   **Organizers:** Primarily led by Beijing Chest Hospital; co-organized by the China Tobacco Control and Health Association.
*   **Methodology:** Use of mobile diagnostic vehicles equipped with low-dose spiral CT scanners. Two vehicle types were used: a standard 12-meter mobile clinic and an 8-meter pure electric small diagnostic vehicle designed for narrow rural roads and weight limits.
*   **Screening Scope:** Simultaneous screening for lung cancer and tuberculosis. The scan can also identify other chest pathologies such as pulmonary nodules, pneumonia, and coronary artery calcification.
*   **Daily Statistics:** A cumulative total of 568 free chest CT screenings were completed at the two sites on the day of the launch.
*   **Diagnostic Process:** Imaging data is transmitted to the Beijing Chest Hospital for centralized review by doctors. Results are delivered to participants' mobile phones approximately one week after the scan.
*   **Capacity Building:** Experts from Beijing Chest Hospital conducted on-site film reading, case discussions, and hands-on training with five local doctors from Yudu County People's Hospital to improve local diagnostic capabilities.
*   **Historical Context of Program:** Since the program's inception in 2023, it has covered 19 provinces, 53 counties/cities, and 130 streets/townships, screening over 166,000 people and detecting thousands of cases of lung cancer and tuberculosis.

# Event Name

## Event Overview

On September 16, 2026, China's National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) officially released the Statistical Reform and Development "15th Five-Year" Plan. This event was reported on September 18, 2026, in *People's Daily*. The plan aims to advance the modernization of statistics to support the country's broader modernization goals, specifically focusing on improving the statistical institutional and methodological framework, refining the national economic accounting system, and establishing modern statistical measures for evolving industries.

## Core Facts

*   **Release Date:** September 16, 2026.
*   **Issuing Body:** National Bureau of Statistics of China.
*   **Document Title:** *Statistical Reform and Development "15th Five-Year" Plan*.
*   **Primary Objectives:**
    *   Improve the statistical institutional and methodological framework.
    *   Refine the national economic accounting system.
    *   Establish more modern statistical measures for evolving industries.
*   **Key Strategic Areas:**
    *   **Economic Accounting:** Consolidate the results of the unified accounting reform for regional Gross Domestic Product (GDP) to improve scientific rigor, standardization, and international comparability of macroeconomic data.
    *   **Industrial Monitoring:** Strengthen statistical monitoring of agriculture, manufacturing, services, and the integrated development of the three major industries to better reflect the construction of a modern industrial system.
    *   **New Quality Productive Forces:** Enrich statistical indicators to reflect the development of new economies, new fields, new quality productive forces, and improvements in social livelihood.
    *   **Data Integrity:** Implement strict investigations and punishments for data falsification, including false reporting, concealment, and refusal to report. This includes increasing the handling of major statistical violations, publicizing typical cases, and deepening warning education.
*   **Strategic Alignment:** The plan aligns with goals for building a strong science and technology nation, a strong domestic market, and a beautiful China, while also focusing on comprehensive green transformation and the construction of a new development pattern.

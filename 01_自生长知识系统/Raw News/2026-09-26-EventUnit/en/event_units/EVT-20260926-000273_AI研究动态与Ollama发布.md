## Event Overview
On September 25, 2026, a collection of AI-related technical developments and safety warnings emerged from various sources, including LessWrong, GitHub, Der Spiegel, and People's Daily. The events center on three main pillars: advances in AI alignment forecasting and interpretability research, the release of Ollama v0.40.0-rc0 with new Apple Silicon support, and high-profile warnings regarding AI existential risks. ## Core Facts
*   **AI Alignment Benchmark:** A new benchmark and method for predicting AI model misalignment from training data before fine-tuning has been introduced, potentially reducing post-training audit costs (Source: LessWrong).
*   **Interpretability Research:** A study introduced "spurious probes" as a black-box alternative to activation probing, demonstrating that unrelated category-mention questions can reliably reveal a model's internal state after specific experiences (Source: LessWrong).
*   **Ollama Update:** Ollama released version v0.40.0-rc0. This release introduces automatic MLX runtime support for Apple Silicon, replacing the default Metal backend for compatible models (Source: GitHub).
*   **VS Code Update:** Microsoft released VS Code version 1.139.1, a patch release likely containing bug fixes and minor improvements (Source: GitHub).
*   **AI Safety Warnings:** Bill Gates joined other tech figures in warning about the existential dangers of artificial intelligence, specifically citing fears of up to one billion deaths (Source: Der Spiegel).
*   **Diplomatic Context:** During Chinese President Xi Jinping's state visit to the US, general AI cooperation between the two nations was discussed alongside economic ties (Source: People's Daily).

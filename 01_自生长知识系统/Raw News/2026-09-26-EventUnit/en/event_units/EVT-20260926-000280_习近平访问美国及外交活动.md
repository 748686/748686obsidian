Xi Jinping's State Visit to the United States and Diplomatic Activities (September 2026)

## Event Overview

In September 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping conducted a state visit to the United States at the invitation of U.S. President Donald Trump. The visit included high-level diplomatic meetings, ceremonial welcome events, cultural exchanges involving the spouses of both leaders, and discussions on bilateral and international issues. According to the synthesis of the provided source materials, the visit took place between September 23 and September 24, 2026, with reports published on September 25, 2026.

## Core Facts

**Arrival and Welcome Ceremony**
*   **Arrival:** On the afternoon of September 23, 2026 (local time), President Xi Jinping arrived at Andrews Air Force Base in Washington, D.C., for the state visit. He was greeted by President Trump and First Lady Melania Trump. A 21-gun salute was fired, and military bands played the national anthems of both countries. Children presented flowers to the Xi and彭丽媛 couple. [Article #429, Article #436]
*   **Welcome Reception:** On the morning of September 24, 2026, President Trump held a grand welcome ceremony at the White House. President Xi and Madame Peng Liyuan attended. [Article #428, Article #434]
*   **Ceremonial Details:** During the ceremony, the two leaders signed the guest book, listened to national anthems, and delivered speeches. President Xi congratulated the United States on its 250th anniversary of independence. After the speeches, they reviewed the honor guard in the Rose Garden and watched a U.S. Marine Corps performance. Fighter jets flew over the White House in tribute. [Article #428]

**Bilateral Talks**
*   **Meeting:** President Xi and President Trump held talks in the Oval Office of the White House on the morning of September 24, 2026. Attendees included Cai Qi, Wang Yi, and He Lifeng. [Article #428, Article #433]
*   **Strategic Framework:** Both leaders reaffirmed the consensus reached during Trump's visit to China in May 2026 to build a "China-U.S. Constructive Strategic Stabil Relationship." Xi described the goal as moving toward a relationship characterized by "cooperation as the main theme, managed competition, controllable differences, and predictable peace." [Article #428, Article #433]
*   **AI Cooperation:** Both sides acknowledged their status as major AI powers. Xi emphasized the need to develop and manage AI responsibly, ensuring it remains under human control and benefits humanity. Trump stated that AI concerns the future of humanity and that both nations should maintain dialogue and strengthen cooperation. [Article #428, Article #433]
*   **Economic Trade:** The two leaders noted that their economic teams had held a new round of consultations and reached a new joint arrangement. Xi urged both sides to expand the cooperation list and compress the problem list, while Trump expressed optimism about reaching better agreements through continued dialogue. [Article #433]
*   **Taiwan Issue:** Xi stated clearly that China's position on safeguarding national unity and territorial integrity is clear and hoped the U.S. side would adhere to the correct stance of opposing "Taiwan independence" and handle the Taiwan issue prudently. [Article #433]
*   **International Issues:** The two heads of state exchanged views on major international and regional issues, including the Middle East situation, the Ukraine crisis, and the Korean Peninsula. They also confirmed mutual support for hosting the 2026 APEC Leaders' Meeting and the G20 Leaders' Summit. [Article #433]

**Spouses' Activities**
*   **Museum Visit:** On the noon of September 24, 2026, Peng Liyuan and Melania Trump visited the National Museum of Asian Art in Washington. They viewed exhibits in the Peacock Hall and other galleries. Peng Liyuan expressed appreciation for the recent return of Chinese cultural relics by the U.S. side and called for strengthened cultural exchange and heritage protection cooperation. They also watched American青少年 sing Chinese songs and interacted with them. [Article #431]

**Youth and People-to-People Exchange**
*   **New Initiative:** During the welcome ceremony, President Xi announced that China would invite 100,000 American adolescents to visit China for study and exchange over the next five years. This doubles a previous commitment of 50,000 students made during Xi's 2023 visit. [Article #428, Article #432]
*   **Panda Diplomacy:** Xi announced that giant pandas "Pingping" and "Fushuang" would soon be settled at the Atlanta Zoo to meet the American public. [Article #428, Article #430]

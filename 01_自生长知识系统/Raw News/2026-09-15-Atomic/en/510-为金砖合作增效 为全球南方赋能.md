President Xi Jinping attended the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi from September 12 to 13, 2026, at the invitation of Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi. During the summit, President Xi announced that China will assume the BRICS presidency and host the 19th summit next year, marking the fourth time China holds this role. He outlined four strategic directions for the bloc, including promoting innovation, maintaining peace, fostering cultural exchange, and reforming global governance. Additionally, China proposed practical initiatives in AI open-source cooperation, digital industry platforms, and engineer training to strengthen ties among the Global South and advance inclusive global development.

**「Background」** The BRICS grouping originated from the &\#x27;BRIC&\#x27; economic acronym and evolved into an expanded intergovernmental organization centered on the cooperation of major emerging economies. This 18th summit focused on &\#x27;Inclusive Global Governance and Strengthening Multilateralism&\#x27; as a response to the growing influence of the Global South in international affairs.

**「Impact」** The summit formally established China&\#x27;s upcoming presidency of the BRICS bloc, which will serve as the institutional framework for the group&\#x27;s next phase of multilateral economic and technological cooperation among developing nations.

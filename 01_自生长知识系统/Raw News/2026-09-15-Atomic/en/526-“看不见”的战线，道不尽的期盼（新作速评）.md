**「Background」** &\#x27;Major Case Squad&\#x27; is a popular Chinese police procedural drama series from the late 1990s and early 2000s, known for its depiction of the &\#x27;Major Case Squad&\#x27; and its charismatic female lead, Ji Jie, portrayed by actress Xu Jinglei. In recent years, the franchise has seen revivals and derivative works, which frequently trigger debates about faithfulness to the original material among nostalgic viewers. The term &\#x27;canon&\#x27; \(正典权\) in IP development refers to the creative authority and boundaries of a story&\#x27;s universe, traditionally controlled by copyright holders, but increasingly contested by audiences who expect adaptations to honor the core spirit of the original character and setting.

**「Impact」** The sustained online discourse signals to IP developers and media producers in China that audience emotional investment and perceived fidelity to a character&\#x27;s established core traits are becoming critical success factors for revivals, often outweighing strict legal ownership of canon in determining public reception. This shift places a premium on narrative continuity and respectful adaptation to mitigate audience backlash against commercially-driven reboots.

# Event Name

Murders of Australian surfers in Mexico

## Event Overview

On or around **9 September 2023**, two Australian surfers, **Kai Winters** (21) and **Benjamin Snyder** (25), were stabbed to death in Mexico. The incident occurred at the Playa Mavori hostel in **Santa Maria del Mar**, a surfing village on the Pacific coast, approximately 165 kilometers (about 100 miles) south of Acapulco in the state of **Guerrero**.

The attackers, described as gang members or cartel affiliates, targeted the victims after allegedly failing to receive payment for escort services they had previously provided. The bodies were discovered by other travelers on a beach near the hostel. The crime has drawn international attention due to its brutality and the targeting of foreign tourists in an area known for cartel activity.

Mexican authorities, including the Guerrero state Attorney General’s Office and the National Guard, have been involved in the investigation. Multiple suspects, including individuals allegedly linked to local gangs or the CJNG (Cartel de Jalisco Nueva Generación), have been arrested. One suspect, identified as **Luis Daniel S.**, was reportedly captured near the scene, while others, including **Karla Yuridia R.** and **Gustavo Adolfo C.**, were apprehended in separate operations. A manhunt was launched for additional suspects, including **David Alejandro V.** and **Erick G.**, who were believed to be fleeing toward the states of Michoacán or Puebla.

The case has intensified concerns about tourist safety in Guerrero and highlighted the dangers posed by organized crime in Mexico’s tourism corridors. It also sparked diplomatic discussions between Australia and Mexico regarding consular assistance and security for foreign visitors.

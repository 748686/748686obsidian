# Event EVD-20260908-000336: "China Smart Manufacturing" and AI Development Exhibition

## Event Overview
On September 8, 2026, *People’s Daily* published a photo report and commentary titled “China Smart Manufacturing,” portraying it as a bright name card for Chinese modernization. The coverage focused on open-source large AI models, intelligent connected vehicles, humanoid robots, and the people-centric philosophy guiding China’s AI development. The event highlights China’s strategic emphasis on transforming manufacturing through intelligent technology to enhance quality of life.

## Core Facts
- **Date:** September 8, 2026
- **Publisher:** *People’s Daily* (人民日报), specifically the Visual (08) and Theory (09) sections
- **Headline Theme:** “China Smart Manufacturing” as a bright name card for Chinese modernization (Chinese: “中国智造”，中国式现代化的亮丽名片）
- **Key Policy Reference:** Xi Jinping’s speech at the opening ceremony of the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) and the High-Level Meeting on AI Global Governance
- **Core Philosophy:** People-centric (以人为本), upward and benevolent (向上向善)
- **Technological Highlights:**
  - Next-generation open-source model **Kimi K3**
  - Intelligent connected vehicles entering households
  - Accelerating evolution of humanoid robots
- **Four Pillars of “Brightness”:**
  1. **People-oriented:** Open-source openness and cooperative sharing to provide affordable new technologies
  2. **Tech Innovation:**万物智联 (Internet of Everything), human-machine collaboration, cross-boundary integration
  3. **Industrial Development:** Industrial software innovation, smart manufacturing equipment, supply chain intelligent coordination
  4. **Scenario Applications:** AI robotic arms in workshops, intelligent vehicles in traffic, smart agricultural machinery in fields
- **Strategic Framing:** AI as a “key variable” (关键变量) becoming a “strong increment” (强劲增量) for high-quality development
- **Secondary Topic:** Same issue featured a theoretical article on “Upholding the Original Aspiration and Mission” by Professor Duan Yan from Northeast Normal University (Section 09)

## Event Overview
On September 4, 2026, the 2026 China Daheibao (International) Motorcycle Off-road Rally Race commenced at the Hami Sports Center camp in Xinjiang, China. The event features 35 teams and 126 riders competing over a total distance of 2,500 kilometers. After the initial qualifying and six special stages, the race is scheduled to conclude on September 11, 2026.

First held in 2024, the event was upgraded to a national-level competition in 2025. The course is characterized by high difficulty, featuring complex terrain such as fish-scale sand and dry riverbeds, with significant emphasis on navigation and vehicle maintenance skills. The rally has attracted a mix of international veterans and domestic teams, including the first-time participation of the Chinese domestic motorcycle brand "Zhang Xue Motorcycles."

# Event Unit: Cultural Empowerment of Rural Areas and Ecological Conservation Practices

## Event Overview

On September 8, 2026, *People's Daily* (People.cn) published a special report detailing China's multi-faceted approach to rural revitalization and ecological conservation. The event encompasses two distinct but thematically linked narratives presented on pages 11 and 13 of the cultural and ecological sections, respectively.

The primary narrative focuses on the "Cultural Empowerment of Rural Areas" initiative, highlighting how local cultural heritage along the "boundary river" (Jiejiang) is utilized to enrich rural communities. A secondary, but equally detailed, narrative covers the practical achievements in rare wildlife artificial breeding and wilderness rehabilitation, showcasing specific cases from Sichuan, Qinghai, and Shaanxi. These efforts are framed within the broader context of the ecological civilization strategy and the "15th Five-Year Plan" for ecological protection.

**2. Wildlife Rehabilitation and Breeding Practices**
*   **Sichuan Wolong (Giant Pandas):**
    *   Location: China Giant Panda Protection Research Center, Wolong Shenshuping Base.
    *   Subject: Sub-adult pandas (ages 1.5–5 years) undergoing "classroom" training.
    *   Curriculum: Dietary habits (transitioning from milk to bamboo with supplementary carrots/apples) and social skills (managing personality differences and resource competition).
    *   Methodology: Environmental enrichment using bamboo slides, flower racks, and caves. A shift toward maternal care, where mothers teach survival skills (climbing,躲避天敌/evasion of natural enemies), while keepers act as "teaching assistants."
    *   Keeper Quote: Liu Huaiting emphasizes providing the best growth environment and making toys from locally sourced bamboo.
*   **Qinghai Hoh Xil (Tibetan Antelopes):**
    *   Location: Suonannaje Protection Station Wildlife Rescue Center (Sanjiangyuan National Park, Yangtze River Source Zone).
    *   Target Population: Calves separated from mothers during migration to Zhuonai Lake (May–July).
    *   Training: "Physical education" involving walking/jogging with rangers to promote digestion and grass-eating habits.
    *   Socialization: Calves imprint on black uniforms (ranger attire) and become wary of strangers in other colors.
    *   Timeline: Rescue to release takes 2–3 years. Pre-release isolation period of 3–4 months without human contact to ensure wildness.
    *   Statistics: Cumulative rescue of over 800 wild animals since the center's operation.
*   **Shaanxi Hanzhong (Crested Ibis):**
    *   Location: Hanzhong Crested Ibis National Nature Reserve Artificial Breeding Center.
    *   Facility: A 5,000-square-meter semi-wilderness net cage simulating natural environments with flowing water and trees.
    *   Timeline: 45 days in small cages followed by entry into the net cage "kindergarten" (usually mid-to-late May). Release readiness achieved in approximately six months.
    *   Curriculum: Flying (circular flight patterns, collision avoidance) and foraging (hunting live loaches in ponds).
    *   Population Data: Global population grew from 7 individuals discovered in 1981 to over 12,000 by the end of 2025.

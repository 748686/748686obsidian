**「Background」** The terms "Loushanguan" and "Lazikou" refer to critical, nearly insurmountable battles during the Chinese Communist Party&\#x27;s historic Long March. In modern Chinese political rhetoric, they are used metaphorically to describe pivotal difficulties that must be overcome through perseverance and struggle. China&\#x27;s current ambitious goals include sending astronauts to the moon and retrieving samples from Mars, tasks that require mastering complex, unproven technologies in deep space.

**「Impact」** The editorial serves as a mobilization narrative for China&\#x27;s aerospace sector, emphasizing that future missions like lunar landing and Mars sample return rely on technological self-reliance and resilience.

### [Agricultural Scientist Recalls 45-Year Journey Breeding High-Yield Rice Varieties](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/14/content_30180813.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The article is a retrospective interview with a chief scientist of rice at Yuan Longping Agricultural Science and Technology Co., Ltd., who has spent 45 years advancing rice breeding. In 1989, he discovered a spontaneously induced temperature-sensitive male-sterile rice material that later formed the basis for developing &\#x27;Zhu 1S&\#x27;, an early indica male-sterile line capable of producing seeds safely at temperatures as low as 22.6°C. To solve the &\#x27;dizziness&\#x27; problem—where early two-line hybrid rice seed production failed if temperatures dropped below 24°C for two to three consecutive days—his team used a 50,000 yuan grant to build a cold-water irrigation platform and created a dual-pressure stress selection system that isolated plants insensitive to a six-day stretch at 23°C. Yuan Longping praised &\#x27;Zhu 1S&\#x27; as one of the safest and most widely used core seed sources for early indica temperature-sensitive sterile lines. Under a commercial breeding system and &\#x27;factory-style&\#x27; segment production model established in 1999, which split the process into six specialized stages, the team improved efficiency by more than 50%, shortened the breeding cycle by 3 to 4 years, and developed 611 new hybrid rice varieties covering nearly 800 million mu.

**「Background」** Two-line hybrid rice in China relies on temperature-sensitive male-sterile \(TSMS\) lines whose male sterility can be reversed by a temperature shift; if sterile at high temperatures but fertile at low temperatures, lines can undergo &\#x27;dizziness&\#x27; and fail to produce pure hybrid seed during vulnerable seed production windows. The three-line system was the earlier breakthrough that made large-scale hybrid rice production possible in China.

**「Impact」** For Chinese rice farmers, the &\#x27;Zhu 1S&\#x27; core seed source and the commercial, segmented breeding system significantly improved seed production safety, variety stability, and yield gains across diverse environments. The 611 resulting hybrid varieties—cumulatively planted over nearly 800 million mu—have strengthened the country&\#x27;s hybrid rice supply chain and food security.

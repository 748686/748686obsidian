### [Third Generation Young Walks the Long March Route in Guizhou](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/14/content_30180793.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 14, 2026, over 300 young representatives from across China participated in the third edition of the "Retro walking the Long March Route" event in Guizhou province, marking the 90th anniversary of the Red Army&\#x27;s Long March victory. Participants walked through historic sites including the site of the Zunyi Conference, the Lou Shan Pass, the Four Crossings of Chishui River Memorial Hall, and the Qiangang Slope Martyrs&\#x27; Monument to experience the historical context firsthand. The 7-kilometer rural hike connected the Gouba Conference site and the "Truth Path" to Huamao Village, a once-barren area transformed into a prosperous rural community. The event integrated physical walks with on-site ideological education, using digital holograms, interactive installations, and live performance art to make history tangible for the youth.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 14, 00:26

**「Historical Context」** The Long March was a 1934-1935 military strategic transfer by the Chinese Red Army, during which the average age of commanders was under 25. The event is rooted in the history of the Red Army&\#x27;s activities in Guizhou, which were the longest and most widespread during the march and included the historic Zunyi Conference, a pivotal turning point in the Chinese Communist Party&\#x27;s history.

**「Impact」** The initiative aims to internalize the "Long March Spirit" as an actionable source of motivation for young people from diverse backgrounds, including university students, grassroots officials, and engineers, encouraging them to apply this resilience in fields such as technological innovation and rural revitalization.

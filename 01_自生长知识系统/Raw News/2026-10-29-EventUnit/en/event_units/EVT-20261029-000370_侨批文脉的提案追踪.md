## Event Overview
On October 29, 2026, the Chinese political and civil society discourse focused on the protection of cultural heritage and rural revitalization. Specifically, the *People's Daily* (Democratic Politics Section, Page 18) highlighted the tracking of proposals regarding "Qiaopi Cultural Heritage" and featured an advisory article on "Village Career Managers." Additionally, a proposal summary from the Chinese Peasants and Workers Democratic Party (Min Ge Central) addressed the high-quality development of cold-arid characteristic agriculture in Northwest China. This event synthesizes the tracking of these civil affairs and cultural protection initiatives.

## Core Facts
*   **Qiaopi Cultural Heritage Protection:** There is an ongoing tracking of proposals aimed at gathering forces to protect the "Qiaopi Cultural Heritage" (Article #576 title context). While the detailed body text of Article #576 provided in the source material focuses on rural career managers, the header and index confirm the presence of this specific proposal tracking on the same page of the Democratic Politics section.
*   **Rural Career Managers:** Xie Ru (Member of the Standing Committee of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference [CPPCC] and Vice Chair of the Jiangxi Provincial Committee) authored an advisory piece titled "Fully Giving Play to the Role of Rural Career Managers" (Article #577 content/Article #576 header context).
    *   **Case Study:** Huichang County, Ganzhou City, Jiangxi Province, has implemented models such as "Village Collective + Local Grain Growing Experts," "Cooperative + Field Manager," and "Large-Scale Grain Grower Drive." These models increased average yield by 200 jin per mu and income by over 190 RMB per mu.
    *   **National Trend:** Following Zhejiang’s "Thousand Village CEOs Cultivation Plan," China is implementing a "Ten Thousand Rural Career Managers Cultivation Plan," and the rural collective economy manager has been listed as a new national occupation.
    *   **Challenges Identified:** Issues include difficulty in retaining talent due to salary sustainability ("remuneration hard to continue"), failure to integrate into local conditions ("acclimatization"), and inability to operate alone ("doing it single-handedly").
    *   **Recommendations:**
        1.  Combine specialized and professional training; build a tiered training system (e.g., China Agricultural University and Chengkou County, Chongqing model).
        2.  Improve policy guarantees, including "base salary + performance" incentive mechanisms and long-term evaluation systems.
        3.  Connect villages with markets, supporting integrated village operations and multi-village teams.
*   **Cold-Arid Agriculture Proposal:** The Chinese Peasants and Workers Democratic Party (Min Ge Central) submitted a proposal for the high-quality development of cold-arid characteristic agriculture in Northwest China (Article #577 title context/content).
    *   **Problems:** Lagging industrial development, weak infrastructure, and insufficient scientific and technological support.
    *   **Recommendations:**
        1.  Strengthen top-level design: Led by the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, create a national modern cold-arid characteristic agricultural map and set up special support funds.
        2.  Protect germplasm resources: Establish native germplasm resource protection zones and databases for characteristic crops and endangered Chinese materia medica.
        3.  Strengthen scientific support: Build innovation platforms and national high-tech zones for cold-arid agriculture.
        4.  Serve the Belt and Road Initiative: Establish market monitoring platforms and overseas warehousing/direct sales networks.

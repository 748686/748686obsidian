## Core Facts

### 1. Political Leadership and Remembrance

- **Death Announcement**: Liu Yandong (刘延东), former Vice Premier of the State Council and member of the 17th and 18th CPC Central Committee Political Bureau, passed away on October 28, 2026, at 14:53 in Beijing at the age of 81. - **Anti-corruption**: Editorial content emphasized the importance of "clearly and firmly advancing the struggle against corruption" as part of the Party Secretary's people-centered philosophy.

### 2. Economic and Industrial Performance

- **Consumer Trade-in Policy**: Since the beginning of 2026, the consumer goods trade-in policy has driven sales exceeding 2 trillion yuan, benefiting over 250 million person-times, according to Ministry of Commerce data announced on October 28.
- **Transportation Investment**: In the first three quarters of 2026, transportation fixed asset investment reached 2.4 trillion yuan, with highway investment at 1.6 trillion yuan and waterway investment at 157.1 billion yuan.
- **Marine Economy**: The marine gross domestic product for the first three quarters reached 8.5 trillion yuan, representing year-on-year growth of 4.9%.
- **Industrial Profits**:规模以上工业企业 (above-scale industrial enterprises) profits grew by 15.5% in the first nine months of 2026.
- **Green Finance**: China's green loan balance reached 49.22 trillion yuan.

### 3. Technological Innovation and Manufacturing

- **Glasses Industry Smart Transformation**: Danyang City in Zhenjiang, Jiangsu Province, produces approximately 45% of the world's resin lenses annually and hosts over 1,600 supply chain enterprises. The visual optics industry's overall self-sufficiency rate in supporting components exceeded 90%.

- **Semiconductor Technology Application**: Companies in Danyang have applied semiconductor lithography processes to lens production, achieving processing precision at the 0.1-micron level and material utilization rates above 95%.
- **Brain-Computer Interface**: China achieved a new breakthrough in international standards for the brain-computer interface field.

### 4. Diplomatic Activities

- **Sino-European Relations**: Cai Dafeng, Vice Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, met with Bernd Lange, Chairman of the European Parliament's International Trade Committee, on October 28 in Beijing, exchanging views on Sino-European relations and legislative body exchanges.

### 5. Cultural Heritage and Repatriation

- **Australian Cultural Relic Repatriation**: Australia returned two Chinese cultural relics to China on October 28 in Canberra. The items were the heads of a military official statue from Yongyu Mausoleum and a civil official statue from Yongchang Mausoleum, both from Northern Song Dynasty imperial tombs in Henan Province. The repatriation was conducted under the framework of the UNESCO 1970 Convention and a 2009 bilateral memorandum.
- **Archaeological Discovery**: The Yue Kingdom Capital and Han-Six Dynasties Kuaiji Commandery site in Shaoxing, Zhejiang Province, was selected as one of China's Top Ten Archaeological Discoveries. The site revealed 2,500-year-old Yue Kingdom palace architecture foundations and over 2,000 bamboo manuscripts from the Han Dynasty.
- **Mekong-Lancang Cultural Festival**: The 14th Mekong-Lancang River Basin Countries Cultural Arts Festival opened in Jinghong, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan Province, on October 28.

### 6. Disaster Response

- **Tibet Earthquake Response**: Following an earthquake in Baqing County, Naqu City, Tibet, over 530 police officers and auxiliary police conducted 24-hour patrols and rescue operations. Emergency shelters were established with tent cities and prefabricated housing units being installed.

### 7. Agricultural Development

- **Winter Rice Breeding**: Over 20 years of breeding work by researchers at the Jiamusi Branch of the Heilongjiang Academy of Agricultural Sciences produced the "Hejing 21" rice variety, which yields over 700 kg per mu and has application areas exceeding 500,000 mu with cumulative production of approximately 320 million kilograms.

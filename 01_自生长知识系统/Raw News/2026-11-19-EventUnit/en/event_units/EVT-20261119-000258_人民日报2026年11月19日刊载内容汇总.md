## Core Facts

### 1. Geographic Indications Policy ("15th Five-Year Plan")
*   **Source**: Article #343 (Page 06).
*   **Fact**: On November 17, 2026, the China National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA) issued the *"Geographic Indication Protection and Application '15th Five-Year' Plan"* (Source: State Council/Xinhua via People's Daily).
*   **Targets**: By 2030, direct output value of geographic indication products is expected to reach **1.1 trillion yuan**, with **160 national geographic indication protection demonstration zones** to be established.
*   **Goal**: Full empowerment of regional characteristic economic high-quality development; serving as a driver for rural revitalization and high-level opening up.

### 2. 2026 APEC Business Leaders Summit & Shenzhen Initiative
*   **Source**: Articles #346–#350 (Pages 07, 11, 14).
*   **Event**: The APEC Business Leaders Summit was held in **Shenzhen, Guangdong** on November 17–18, 2026.
*   **Theme**: "Promoting Prosperity Together, Winning the Future Together."
*   **Key Output**: The **"2026 APEC Business Leaders Summit Shenzhen Initiative"** was issued,凝聚ing business community consensus on four pillars:
    1.  **Openness and Inclusiveness**: Deepening open regional economy, advancing Free Trade Area of Asia-Pacific (FTAAP), reducing non-tariff barriers.
    2.  **Innovative Growth**: AI governance frameworks, "AI+" cooperation for SMEs, green data centers.
    3.  **Connectivity**: Upgrading the APEC Connectivity Blueprint (2015–2025), green trade rules, digital infrastructure.
    4.  **Win-Win Cooperation**: Energy transition, agricultural tech innovation, medical innovation, carbon market dialogue.
*   **Economic Data**:
    *   Shenzhen's imports/exports with APEC economies (Jan–Oct 2026): **3.65 trillion yuan** (+42.08% YoY), accounting for **69.55%** of Shenzhen's total foreign trade.
    *   Guangdong's total imports/exports: **9.8 trillion yuan**; trade with APEC economies: **6.47 trillion yuan** (~2/3 of provincial total).
    *   Shenzhen exported "New Three" (EVs, lithium batteries, solar cells) to APEC: **44.675 billion yuan** (+5.94%).
*   **Youth Opportunities**: Articles highlight AI application roles, green energy jobs, and cross-border e-commerce opportunities for youth.

### 3. High-Quality Development of Vocational Education
*   **Source**: Article #354 (Page 10).
*   **Fact**: The Ministry of Education launched the **second phase of the "Double High Construction Plan"** (High-Level Higher Vocational Schools and Professional Construction), selecting **220 vocational colleges** as national-level skill-oriented institutions.
*   **Context**: China topped the medal table at the **48th World Skills Competition** with **41 gold medals**.
*   **Impact**: Institutional reforms are improving professional title evaluation, further education channels, and talent recognition for skilled workers, aiming to make vocational success a "predictable and replicable path."

### 4. Foreign Investment Trends
*   **Source**: Article #362 (Page 11).
*   **Data (Jan–Sep 2026)**:
    *   High-tech industry actual use of foreign capital: **228.53 billion yuan** (+33.8% YoY), accounting for **41.6%** of total national use.
    *   Foreign profit reinvestment: **939.9 billion yuan** (+4.7% YoY).
*   **Analysis**: Investment is shifting from scale expansion to quality improvement, with growth concentrated in tech transformation services (+76.8%), R&D/design (+59.2%), and electronic communications equipment (+32.8%).
*   **Stock**: Foreign direct investment equity stock reached **26.8 trillion yuan**, ranking second globally, with 6 consecutive years of growth.

### 5. Judicial System Reform
*   **Source**: Article #365 (Page 14).
*   **Fact**: At the 23rd National Courts Work Conference (reported Nov 18, 2026), it was announced that from Jan 2023 to Sep 2026, Chinese courts handled **166 million cases** and closed **161 million cases**.
*   **Achievements**:
    *   First-instance criminal cases decreased from 1.243 million (2023) to 1.056 million (2025).
    *   Serious violent crime cases decreased from 52,000 to 46,000.
    *   **Basic-level courts have achieved full coverage** in stationed personnel at county-level comprehensive governance centers, connecting with 34,000 township/sub-district centers.

### 6. Safety Production Inspections
*   **Source**: Article #366 (Page 14).
*   **Action**: Starting November 18, 2026, **24 central safety production assessment and inspection teams** were dispatched to all 31 provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities, and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps.
*   **Focus**: Inspecting implementation of Central Committee directives, the "Three-Year Action Plan for Safety Production," and rectification of hidden dangers in key sectors (contracting, fire safety).

### 7. Academic and Scientific Achievements
*   **Source**: Article #369 (Page 14).
*   **Institution**: Jilin University, College of Instrumentation and Electrical Engineering.
*   **Achievement**: Developed a "air-ground-well" 3D integrated magnetic resonance wideband measurement system. Achieved **zero breakthrough** in direct water探测 (detection) in permafrost at **4,400 meters** altitude on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. Water parameter estimation accuracy exceeds **85%**.

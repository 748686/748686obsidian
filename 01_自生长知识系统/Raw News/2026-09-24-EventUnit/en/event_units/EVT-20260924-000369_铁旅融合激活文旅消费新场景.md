## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, *People's Daily* published a report titled "Traveling by Train (New Window)," detailing how the integration of railway transport and cultural tourism has transformed trains from mere transit tools into comprehensive consumption scenarios. The report highlights three specific thematic train services: the "Long March High-Speed Rail" connecting Jiangxi and Shaanxi for red tourism, the Sichuan-Qinghai special line featuring intangible cultural heritage and ethnic experiences, and the "Qilu No. 1" silver-hair (elderly) tourism train in Shandong. These initiatives are supported by national policy frameworks, including the "15th Five-Year Plan for Building a Tourism Powerhouse" and the "15th Five-Year Plan for Expanding Consumption."

## Core Facts
*   **Summer Transport Statistics:** Between July 1 and August 31, 2026, during the summer rush, railways operated a total of 699 tourism trains, comprising 415 tourism special trains and 284 tourism special lines.
*   **Policy Support:** Multiple special "15th Five-Year" plans, specifically the *Tourism Powerhouse Construction "15th Five-Year" Plan* and the *Expanding Consumption "15th Five-Year" Plan*, have deployed measures to promote the integration of culture and railways and optimize tourism special train supply.
*   **Long March High-Speed Rail (G3998):**
    *   Route:瑞金 (Ruijin) to Yan’an.
    *   Duration: 11 hours and 32 minutes.
    *   Significance: First direct high-speed train service connecting the two locations, replacing previous routes requiring two or three transfers.
    *   Context: Launched in observance of the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March.
*   **"Long March Source" Train (K571/2):**
    *   Route: Operates along the Beijing-Kowloon Railway in Jiangxi.
    *   Key Stop: Xingguo Station, where the Beijing-Kowloon Railway and Changsha-Ganzhou High-Speed Railway intersect (known as the "Xingguo Curve").
    *   Impact: Xingguo County has become a popular destination for study tours from the Guangdong-Fujian regions, with nearly half of the station's passenger flow originating from these provinces.
*   **Sichuan-Qinghai Tourism Special Line (C5762):**
    *   Route: Chengdu East Station to Huangshengguan Station.
    *   Launch: Officially commenced operations in August 2026.
    *   Features: Customized services including "one station, one broadcast," "one card, one view," and "one map to tour the route." On-board intangible cultural heritage performances (Qiang flute, folk songs) and ethnic-themed dining.
*   **"Qilu No. 1" (Shandong):**
    *   Operator: China Railway Jinan Group Co., Ltd., in collaboration with the Shandong Provincial Party Committee and Government.
    *   Launch Date: April 10, 2025.
    *   Scale: Connects 11 cities, 22 stations, and over 200 scenic spots in Shandong.
    *   Cumulative Passengers: 1.273 million since launch.
    *   Adaptations: Upgraded from a conventional green train with age-friendly modifications, including anti-slip mats, handrails, and availability of reading glasses and sewing kits.

## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, *People's Daily* (People's Republic of China) published a commentary in its 07th Edition (Commentary Section) titled "Integrating the Scientist Spirit into the Urban Fabric." The article argues that enhancing the visibility, influence, and shaping power of the scientist spirit requires transforming public urban spaces from commercial advertising hubs into platforms for scientific heroism and education. It posits that this integration is a systematic engineering project that must be rooted in local cultural contexts ("grounded") and supported by institutional guarantees, industrial backing, and tangible talent policies to foster innovation.

*   **Key Political Message:** General Secretary Xi Jinping conveyed holiday greetings to farmers and those working in the "agriculture, rural areas, and farmers" (San Nong) sectors. He emphasized:
    *   Solidly advancing comprehensive rural revitalization.
    *   Improving the efficiency of policies benefiting agriculture and farmers.
    *   Enhancing comprehensive agricultural production capacity and quality/efficiency.
    *   Broadening channels for farmers to increase income and prosperity.
    *   Building livable, business-friendly, and beautiful villages according to local conditions.

    *   Southern China: Double-cropped late rice is in the booting and heading stage, a critical period for yield formation.
    *   Northern China: Autumn crops are in the grain-filling and maturation stage, with large-scale harvesting expected in less than half a month.
    *   National Autumn Crops: Sown area is stable to increasing, with most crops showing normal growth.
*   **Policy Actions:**
    *   The Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs has organized experts and technicians to key areas to implement key measures such as fertilizer/water management and "one spray, multiple promotions" (一喷多促).
    *   For the first time this year, China launched 20 large-scale yield enhancement promotion cities on a whole-county basis to promote more balanced increases in yields.
    *   Integration of new technologies and equipment into the entire grain production chain using good fields,良种 (seeds), machines, and methods.

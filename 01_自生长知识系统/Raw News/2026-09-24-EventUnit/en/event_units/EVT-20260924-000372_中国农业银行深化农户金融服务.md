## Event Overview
In September 2026, the Agricultural Bank of China (ABC) publicized its strategic implementation of national policies aimed at rural revitalization and common prosperity for farmers. Released in *People's Daily* on September 23, 2026, the report details ABC's efforts to optimize financial products, enhance technological empowerment, and expand service coverage for rural households. The initiative aligns with the State Council's "15th Five-Year Plan for Accelerating Agricultural and Rural Modernization," issued in 2026, which prioritizes consolidating poverty alleviation outcomes, widening farmer income channels, and improving policy effectiveness.

## Core Facts
*   **Strategic Alignment:** ABC is executing the national mandate to support farmers' income growth and rural common prosperity through enhanced financial accessibility, coverage, and convenience.
*   **Loan Portfolio Growth:** As of June 30, 2026, the outstanding balance of ABC’s exclusive rural credit product, "Huinong e-Loan" (Huìnéng e-dài), exceeded 200 billion yuan. This represents an increase of over 260 billion yuan from the beginning of the year, reflecting a growth rate of 14.2%.
*   **Operational Activities:** Coinciding with the 2026 China Farmers' Harvest Festival, ABC participated in the Shanghai main venue event for the Autumn Consumption Season and hosted a "Agricultural Machinery Loan" (Nóngjī dài) product promotion meeting in Changji Hui Autonomous Prefecture, Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region.
*   **Technological Integration:**
    *   ABC utilizes mobile PADs and AI-driven digital lending models to streamline processes.
    *   The bank leverages data from agricultural subsidies, agricultural insurance, and land transfer contracts to improve credit accuracy.
    *   A "On-site + Remote" service model has processed 400,000 rural loans totaling nearly 50 billion yuan as of June 30, 2026.
*   **Cost Reduction:** The weighted average interest rate on newly issued rural loans decreased by 17 basis points compared to the start of the year, alongside targeted preferential rates for eligible farmers.
*   **Infrastructure:** ABC has established over 3,500 "Huinong Service Stations" (Huìníng Fúwù Zhàn), which have assisted in disbursing more than 30 billion yuan in rural loans.
*   **Risk Mitigation:** ABC employs differentiated tools such as principal-free loan renewals and loan extensions to assist agricultural operators. Following a mudslide in Gonggu County, Tibet Autonomous Region, ABC provided repayment grace periods to 33 affected pastoral households after reviewing over 250 loans.

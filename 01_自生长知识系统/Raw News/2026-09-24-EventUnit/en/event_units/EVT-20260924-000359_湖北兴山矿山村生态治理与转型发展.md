# Event Name

Ecological Governance and Transformational Development of Maocaoban Village, Xingshan County, Hubei Province

## Event Overview

This event synthesizes the environmental rehabilitation and socioeconomic transition of Maocaoban Village (茅草坪村) in Shuizisi Town, Xingshan County, Yichang City, Hubei Province. The village, historically burdened by unregulated phosphate mining in the Shukongping Phosphate Mine area, has undergone a structured transformation since 2007. The process involves the consolidation of mining resources under Hubei Xingfa Group Co., Ltd., large-scale ecological remediation, relocation of affected residents, and the development of diversified agricultural industries, specifically walnut cultivation. The case is presented as a model for the transition of resource-dependent rural communities toward green development.

## Core Facts

**Geographic and Historical Context**
*   **Location:** Maocaoban Village, Shuizisi Town, Xingshan County, Yichang City, Hubei Province.
*   **Resource Base:** Located within the Shukongping Phosphate Mine area, characterized by rich phosphate deposits.
*   **Historical Damage (1980s–2006):** Approximately 70+ mine shafts were opened without order. The village developed due to mining but suffered severe ecological degradation, including damaged mountain bodies, extensive goafs (collapsed underground spaces), accumulated waste slag, sediment accumulation in rivers, turbid water quality, and inadequate water supply during dry seasons. Residents lived in earthen houses.

**Intervention and Governance (Starting 2007)**
*   **Consolidation:** Local authorities integrated mining area resources. Hubei Xingfa Group Co., Ltd. (湖北兴发化工集团股份有限公司) was appointed to coordinate protective development, ending extensive/粗放 mining modes.
*   **Investment:**
    *   Nearly 100 million RMB invested in treating goafs.
    *   30 million RMB invested in addressing historical legacy issues.
    *   40 million RMB invested in repairing a 2.3 square kilometer geological disaster area.
*   **Technical Standards:** Implementation of "full-seam mining, separate mining and transportation" (全层开采、分采分运) to achieve "waste rock stays in the well, tailings are not piled externally," preventing new destruction at the source.
*   **Recognition:** Shukongping Phosphate Mine was selected for the Ministry of Natural Resources' "National Green Mine Directory."

**Infrastructure and Environmental Remediation**
*   **Water Management:** Treatment of the Confucius River (孔子河) micro-watershed. Construction of ecological dams, treatment plants ensuring compliant discharge, and ecological slope protection. The river transformed from a "mine water drainage" channel to a "village-nourishing" water source.
*   **Roads:** All village-to-village and group-to-group roads were hardened. Asphalt roads replaced concrete roads in the relocation resettlement area (upgraded two years prior to the report date).
*   **Landscaping:** Exposed岩土 were covered with vegetation; slopes were reinforced to blend with surrounding forests.

**Social and Economic Transition**
*   **Relocation:** Beginning in 2015, residents from mining impact zones were分批 (in batches) relocated to centralized resettlement communities, moving from earthen/dilapidated houses to concrete brick-concrete structures.
*   **Employment:** Over 150 local villagers now work in the mining area.
    *   *Case Study:* Xu Jinlan (许金兰), a resettled resident, previously worked in Guangzhou. She now works as a central control operator in the mineral processing plant. Her husband works in mine transport. Their combined annual income is approximately 200,000 RMB.
*   **Industry Diversification:**
    *   Introduction of fragrant thin-shell walnuts in 2009.
    *   Improvement to amber walnuts (thinner shells, fuller kernels, higher price).
    *   Current Status: 110 out of 216 households in the village plant walnuts, producing approximately 80,000 *jin* (40 tons) annually.

**Key Personnel**
*   **Zhao Jihai (赵家海):** First Secretary stationed in the village, dispatched by Hubei Xingfa Group. This was his third term as of September 2026.

## Known Current Impact

*   **Productive:** The village has reduced dependency on single-resource extraction by establishing a walnut industry involving over 50% of households.

## Event Conclusion

The ecological governance and transformation of Maocaoban Village represents a successful case study in the remediation of legacy mining damage and rural industrial transition. By integrating state-led resource consolidation (Hubei Xingfa Group), significant capital investment in geological and hydrological infrastructure, and the diversification of local agriculture (walnuts), the village has mitigated the negative externalities of its phosphate mining history. The outcome includes restored ecological integrity, improved housing and living standards for relocated residents, and stable local employment, offering a replicable model for other resource-dependent rural communities in China.

# Event Name
False Advertising and Aggressive Sales Tactics Targeting Elderly Consumers in the Water Purifier Market; China Consumers Association (CCA) Issuance of Consumer Warnings

## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, the China Consumers Association (CCA) issued a five-point consumer warning regarding the prominent issues of false advertising and high-price sales tactics for water purifiers, specifically targeting elderly consumers in rural areas and communities. The warning was prompted by a sharp rise in complaints, with data from the National Consumers Association Smart 315 Platform indicating that投诉 volumes in the first seven months of 2026 had already surpassed the total for all of 2024. A report by Qi Zhiming in the *People's Daily* (September 23, 2026, p. 17) detailed these trends and the CCA's specific guidance.

## Core Facts
*   **Source of Warning:** China Consumers Association (CCA / 中消协).
*   **Date of Publication:** September 23, 2026.
*   **Target Demographic:** Elderly consumers in rural villages and urban communities.
*   **Problematic Practices:**
    *   Use of "free gifts" or "health science popularization" events to attract elderly attendees.
    *   Exaggeration of product efficacy, including claims that water purifiers can "treat diseases," "maintain health," or "improve chronic conditions."
    *   Fabrication of water quality safety issues (e.g., using electrolysis experiments to制造 panic).
    *   High-price sales of low-cost products (priced at several thousand to over ten thousand RMB) under the guise of "rural benefit" or "limited-time offers."
    *   Lack of fixed business premises, use of personal WeChat for payments, and failure to issue formal invoices.
    *   After-sales service abandonment (running away/disappearing after sales).
*   **Statistical Trend:** According to the National Consumers Association Smart 315 Platform:
    *   Complaints in the first 7 months of 2026 exceeded the total complaints for the full year of 2024.
    *   Complaints in July 2026 increased by 40.17% compared to January 2026.
    *   The trend shows a month-on-month increase throughout the year.
*   **CCA Five-Point Advice:**
    1.  **Beware of sales meetings (会销):** Do not believe exaggerated claims; water purifiers only improve water quality and do not treat diseases.
    2.  **Verify channels and qualifications:** Purchase from licensed stores or official online channels; check for CCC certification (for electric products), health permits issued by provincial-level health departments, and product certificates. Beware of "three-no" (三无) products.
    3.  **Understand product performance:** Check water flow, total purified water capacity, and applicable water pressure; consider filter replacement costs and lifespan.
    4.  **Clarify after-sales terms:** Request formal invoices and complete warranty/sanbao (repair, replacement, return) documents; clarify installation and filter fees in writing.
    5.  **Use legal weapons:** Preserve evidence (invoices, chats, transfer records); under Article 55 of the *Law on the Protection of Consumer Rights and Interests*, consumers can claim a refund and punitive damages equal to three times the purchase price in cases of fraud. Report to market regulators or police for significant amounts.

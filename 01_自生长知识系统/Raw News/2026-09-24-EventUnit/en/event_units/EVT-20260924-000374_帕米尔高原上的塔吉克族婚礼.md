# Event Unit: Wedding of a Tajik Border Guard on the Pamir Plateau

## Event Overview
On or before September 22, 2026, the author witnessed a wedding ceremony in Varxidie Village, Tashkurgan Tajik Autonomous County, Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, China. The event centered on the marriage of Xiao Gongni Longji (Xiao Gongni Longjik), a Tajik ethnic minority border guard and deputy director of the Paike Border Police Station stationed in the Wakh corridor. The article documents the cultural customs, the family's multi-generational history of border defense, and specific traditions of the Tajik community in this region.

## Core Facts
*   **Location:** Varxidie Village, Tashkurgan Tajik Autonomous County, Xinjiang, China, at the foot of the Kunlun Mountains.
*   **Groom:** Xiao Gongni Longji (Xiao Gongni Longjik). He is identified as a border guard in the Wakh corridor, deputy director of the Paike Border Police Station, and the fourth generation of his family to serve as a border defender on the Pamir Plateau.
*   **Bride:** Her name is not mentioned in the source. She was residing in her natal home, approximately 10 li (approx. 5 kilometers) away from the groom's residence at the time of the evening festivities.
*   **Groom's Father:** Longji Kader (Longjik Kader). A retired soldier who served for over 30 years in the Pamir Plateau and served as an义务护边员 (volunteer border guard) for 18 years after retirement. He was awarded the title of "Most Beautiful Retired Soldier" nationally in 2021, and his family was named a "Most Beautiful Family" in Xinjiang in 2023.
*   **Family History of Border Defense:**
    *   **1st Generation (Great-grandfather):** Abudukelimu Albike (Abudukeirimu Alpike). Acted as a guide for the People's Liberation Army (PLA) and helped build guard posts after the PLA arrived, becoming the family's first volunteer border guard.
    *   **2nd Generation (Grandfather):** Kader Abudukeelimu (Kader Abudukeirimu). Regarded as the prototype for the character "Amir" in the famous Chinese film *Stars on the Snowy Mountain* (Bingshan Shang De Lai Ke). He joined the PLA in 1951, was wounded in border combat, received a First-Class Merit, and later carved a family motto on a boulder at an altitude of 4,700 meters in the Wakh corridor.
    *   **4th Generation (Current):** Xiao Gongni Longji and his elder brother, Abudujami, both continue the family tradition of border defense.
*   **Cultural Customs Observed:**
    *   **Greetings:** Guests exchanged hand-kissing and cheek-kissing greetings with the host.
    *   **Eagle Dance:** Guests performed the traditional Tajik eagle dance, characterized by shoulder shaking and arm movements mimicking flying eagles.
    *   **Bridal Attire:** Tajik brides require 12 sets of red bridal dresses as part of the betrothal gifts (dowry/bride price context). Cash gifts (li Jin) are not collected. Brides wear these red outfits for one full year after marriage.
    *   **Bridal Veil:** The groom wore a veil under his ceremonial hat during the initial dancing phase.

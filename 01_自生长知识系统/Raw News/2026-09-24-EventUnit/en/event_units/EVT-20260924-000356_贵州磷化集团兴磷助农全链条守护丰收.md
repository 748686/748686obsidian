# Event Unit: Guizhou Phosphate Group's Agricultural Support Initiative

## Event Overview
In September 2026, reports detailed the operational achievements of Guizhou Phosphate Group (贵州磷化集团), a major domestic phosphate fertilizer producer. The group reported fulfilling its mandate to support national food security and agricultural high-quality development through a multi-faceted approach involving supply chain stabilization, technological innovation, green circular economy practices, and rural revitalization efforts. The data covers the period from the beginning of 2026 through August 2026, with specific reference to the Spring Farming (春耕) season.

## Core Facts

### Supply Chain Stability and Food Security
*   **Production Volume:** By the end of August 2026, the group had cumulatively produced 2.49 million metric tons of traditional fertilizers, exceeding time-progress targets. Year-to-date sales of various fertilizers exceeded 4 million metric tons.
*   **Distribution:** During the Spring Farming period, the group supplied over 1.9 million metric tons of phosphate fertilizer to major grain-producing regions in Northeast China, North China, and the Huang-Huai-Hai region.
*   **Daily Logistics:** Daily fertilizer output stabilized at the 10,000-metric-ton level, with approximately 20,000 metric tons dispatched daily to key agricultural zones.
*   **Infrastructure:** The group utilizes 40 central warehouses nationwide to implement a "off-peak storage, peak-season supply, precise scheduling" mechanism. A digital logistics system matches supply and demand in real-time to optimize transportation paths.

### Technological Innovation and Product Optimization
*   **R&D Capacity:** The group operates the industry's only enterprise-level National Key Laboratory. It has accumulated 2,454 authorized patents.
*   **New Fertilizer Output:** In the first half of 2026, the production of new-type fertilizers reached 642,500 metric tons. Products include water-soluble fertilizers, slow-release fertilizers, and crop-specific fertilizers.
*   **Product Launch:** A new compound fertilizer entered mass production in April 2026.
*   **Service Model:** Under the "Pioneer Phosphorus · Red Chain Linkage" (先锋磷・红引链动) party-building brand, 23 party member vanguard teams were deployed to conduct over 500 agricultural assistance sessions, including field guidance and planting training.
*   **Specific Applications:**
    *   In Hebei and Shandong (wheat main producing areas), the group promoted wheat-specific fertilizers using a "one product, one prescription" precision fertilization scheme.
    *   In Guizhou (mountainous terrain), specific fertilizers were customized for characteristic economic crops.

### Green Circular Economy and Ecological Restoration
*   **Process Improvement:** Utilization of wet-process purified phosphoric acid technology to reduce impurities and increase nutrient purity, aiming to lower chemical fertilizer usage and reduce non-point source pollution.
*   **Phosphogypsum Utilization:** The "1468" phosphogypsum decomposition device, which produces sulfuric acid and cementitious materials, is now operational.
    *   Annual capacity to consume phosphogypsum: 1.4 million metric tons.
    *   Annual output: 600,000 metric tons of sulfuric acid and 800,000 metric tons of cementitious materials.
    *   Phosphogypsum is also being tested for land remediation and acidic soil improvement.
*   **Mining Efficiency:** The Chuanyandong Mine in Weng'an County, Qiannan Buyei and Miao Autonomous Prefecture, completed intelligent transformation, achieving an ore utilization rate exceeding 90%.
*   **Ecological Rehabilitation:** The group engages in simultaneous ecological governance and land reclamation during mining operations, converting closed mines and slag heaps into arable land or special planting bases where feasible.

### Rural Revitalization and Social Responsibility
*   **Party Building Integration:** The group has established 6 party-building demonstration villages, 28 party member demonstration fields, and 10 party member vanguard stores nationwide, promoting a model integrating party building, technology, and agricultural chemical services.
*   **Targeted Assistance:** Since establishing a pairing assistance relationship with Rongjiang County in 2015, the group has dispatched over 200 stationed village officials covering 30 villages.
*   **Farmer Benefits:** In 2026, the group continued to implement preferential fertilizer policies in multiple locations within Guizhou Province to reduce production costs for farmers.
*   **Quality Control:** Implementation of a "one bag, one code" traceability system for full-process product quality control.

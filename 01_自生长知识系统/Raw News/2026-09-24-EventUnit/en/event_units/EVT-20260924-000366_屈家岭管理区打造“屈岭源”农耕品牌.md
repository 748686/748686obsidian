## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Hubei section) published a report detailing the strategic development of the regional public brand **"Qulingyuan" (屈岭源)** by the Qujialing Management Area in Jingmen City, Hubei Province. The initiative leverages the area’s 5,000-year history of prehistoric farming civilization and over 70 years of state-run farm heritage to drive agricultural modernization. The brand adopts a "1+5+N" product matrix, integrating agriculture, culture, and tourism, while establishing a scientific innovation framework involving local enterprises and research institutions.

## Core Facts

### Brand Identity and Strategy
*   **Brand Name:** "Qulingyuan" (屈岭源).
*   **Geographic Context:** Qujialing Management Area, rooted in the Jianghan Plain, Jingmen City, Hubei Province.
*   **Core Positioning:** "China Agricultural Valley" core area; high-end agricultural product model.
*   **Slogan/Concept:** "Come to Qujialing to find a rice seed from 5,000 years ago" (来屈家岭寻一粒五千年前的稻种).
*   **Brand Philosophy:** "Agriculture as the foundation, culture as the core, tourism as the body" (农为基、文为核、旅为体).
*   **Motto:** "One ridge connects ancient and modern; five products bless the world" (一岭承古今，五品馈天下).
*   **Structure:** A "1+5+N" modern agricultural product brand system. "1" refers to the parent brand "Qulingyuan"; "5" refers to five sub-brands; "N" refers to supporting local autonomous products.

### Historical and Cultural Basis
*   **Archaeological Evidence:** The Qujialing Site is a key relic proving the origin of prehistoric farming civilization in the middle reaches of the Yangtze River. It yielded carbonized rice grains dated approximately **5,800 years ago**.
*   **Developmental Lineage:** The area traces a continuous lineage from "Ancient Farming" (first farmers domesticating rice) to "Modern State Farming" (Wusan Farm reclamation) to "Contemporary Agricultural Valley" (science and technology empowerment).
*   **Cultural Conversion:** The brand aims to convert the cultural value of archaeological sites into market added value for agricultural products.

### Sub-Brands and Key Products ("The Five")
The report highlights specific sub-brands under the "Wusan" (五三) banner, linked to the heritage of Wusan Farm:
1.  **Wusan Deer (五三鹿):**
    *   Covers ecological breeding, deep processing, and康养 (health/wellness) tourism.
    *   **Achievement:** "Wusan Farm Sika Deer Antler" and "Wusan Farm Sika Deer Meat" were both selected for the **Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs' 2024 Second Batch of Ancient Farming and Agricultural Product Memory Index** (农业农村部2024年第二批农耕农品记忆索引名录).
2.  **Wusan Yellow Peach (五三黄桃):**
    *   Grown in a 10,000-mu standardized base in Changtan Office, leveraging the unique climate of the 30th parallel north.
    *   **Achievement:** Selected for the same Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs index (2024 Second Batch). Rated a Hubei Province High-Quality Agricultural Product (Yellow Peach) Production Base.
    *   **Model:** Full-chain operation including flower viewing (March), peach picking (June), and year-round processing (e.g., peach gum).
3.  **Wusan Fresh Vegetables (五三鲜蔬):**
    *   Utilizes substrate cultivation and water-fertilizer integration.
    *   Products include pickled vegetables, fruit/vegetable crisps, and pumpkin powder.
4.  **Wusan Fungi (五三菌):**
    *   Passed green food sampling tests.
    *   Products include fresh mushrooms, dried goods, and prepared dishes.
5.  **Wusan Fish (五三鱼) / Quling Fish Fresh (屈岭渔鲜):**
    *   Based on a smart recirculating aquaculture base.
    *   Products include live aquatic products and prepared dishes.

### Institutional and Market Development
*   **Enterprise Impact:** The brand has driven the抱团 (grouped/cooperative) development of **22 provincial and municipal-level agricultural leading enterprises**, along with numerous cooperatives and family farms.
*   **Market Presence:**
    *   Participation in the 22nd China International Agricultural Products Trade Fair and the 2025 Hubei Agricultural Expo (since 2025).
    *   Dedicated counters in local supermarkets.
*   **Cultural Tourism Integration:** Festivals such as "Encountering Grain Civilization · Intoxicated in Qujialing" (遇见谷文明・TAO醉屈家岭) and the "Antler Picking Festival" (采茸节) are used to link exhibition, retail, and agri-tourism experiences (picking, study tours, health wellness).
*   **Scientific Innovation:**
    *   Construction of the **Synthetic Biology and Functional Food "Two Academies and One Center"** (两院一中心) project.
    *   Layout of provincial engineering technology centers and provincial/ministerial key laboratories in food processing.
    *   Collaboration between local leaders (Baoderui, Juhui, Tianye) and research bodies (Huazhong Agricultural University, Hubei Academy of Agricultural Sciences).

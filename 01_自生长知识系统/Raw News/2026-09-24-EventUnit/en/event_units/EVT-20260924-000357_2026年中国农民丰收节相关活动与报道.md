# Event Name

**2026 China Farmers' Harvest Festival: National Observance and Regional Implementation**

## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, China celebrated its ninth annual "China Farmers' Harvest Festival." Coinciding with the beginning of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period, the festival carried heightened significance as a marker for rural revitalization and agricultural stability. The event was characterized by a dual focus: national-level commentary on the structural transition from "good harvests" to "good incomes," and localized implementation in Chongqing, where the regional main venue hosted the Third Farmers' Agricultural Skills Competition.

Primary themes included the resilience of agricultural production despite climatic challenges (typhoons, floods, drought), the integration of technology into traditional farming (smart agriculture, genetic conservation), and the expansion of rural industry chains to ensure farmers capture more value.

## Core Facts

*   **Date of Observation:** September 23, 2026 (Autumn Equinox).
*   **Festival Edition:** The 9th China Farmers' Harvest Festival.
*   **National Theme:** "Harvest Opens a New Chapter, Hard Work Promotes Revitalization" (丰收开新局 实干促振兴).
*   **National Main Venue:** Zaoyang City, Hubei Province (specifically Dongjiao Village).
*   **Chongqing Main Venue:** Hezhongdao, Qingjiang Town, Rongchang District, Chongqing Municipality.
*   **Accompanying Event:** The Third Farmers' Agricultural Skills Competition (held concurrently with the Chongqing main venue activities).
*   **Agricultural Production Data (reported for the period leading up to the festival):**
    *   Summer grain yield: 301.49 billion jin (150.745 million tonnes), marking the first time it has exceeded 300 billion jin.
    *   Early rice yield: Stabilized above 56 billion jin (28 million tonnes) for six consecutive years.
    *   Rural resident per capita disposable income (H1 2026): 12,699 RMB, representing a real year-on-year growth of 5.5%.
*   **Key Activities:**
    *   **Hubei (Zaoyang):** Exploration of integrated planting/breeding and rural tourism to extend industrial chains.
    *   **Hubei (Xuan'en County):** Harvest Festival Village Song Competition (Central Region Preliminary).
    *   **Shanghai:** Launch of the Golden Autumn Consumption Season on September 4, 2026, connecting rural products with urban consumers.
    *   **Chongqing (Rongchang):** Opening ceremony, brand ceremony, industrial matchmaking, agricultural experience, cultural-tourism integration, and benefit-oriented exhibitions.
    *   **Fujian (Fuqing):** Local harvest festival activity featuring longan sales.

## Event Conclusion

The 2026 China Farmers' Harvest Festival (9th edition) served as a focal point for discussing the transition of Chinese agriculture from quantity-focused production to quality and income-focused development. The event highlighted a national summer grain yield breakthrough (>300 billion jin) and simultaneously showcased regional technological innovations in Chongqing, where smart agriculture and brand building (animal husbandry) are being used to overcome geographical limitations and drive rural revitalization. The synthesis of national policy observation and regional implementation details confirms a strategic emphasis on "science and technology as the key" and "industrial chain extension" to ensure harvests translate into tangible economic gains for farmers.

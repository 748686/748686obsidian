## Core Facts
Based on the source material from Article #514, the following core facts are established:

1.  **Institutional Identity**: The subject is the Zhejiang University Architectural Design and Research Institute (浙江大学建筑设计研究院).
2.  **Core Philosophy**: The institute adheres to "Li Xin Min Ben" (立心民本 - establishing a people-centered mindset) and seeks "Balanced Architecture" (平衡建筑) by finding the greatest common divisor among diverse demands.
3.  **Design Pathway**: The institute utilizes a path of "在地共生" (Local Symbiosis), ensuring buildings integrate into the historical fabric and natural environment of the city.
4.  **Key Projects Cited**:
    *   **Yiwu Cultural Square**: Removed walls to merge Jiangnan courtyards and stage memories into daily public life, creating an open "cultural living room."
    *   **Yiwu Global Digital Trade Center**: Broke traditional market functional boundaries, integrating commerce, office, and leisure. It served as a venue for the 2026 CCTV Spring Festival Gala sub-venue.
    *   **Shaoxing Ancient City Preservation**:
        *   *Dayu Memorial Hall*: Built at the foot of Kuaiji Mountain, using earth-covering techniques to blend with the山体, symbolizing Dayu's water management philosophy ("疏导而不堵塞").
        *   *Xu Wei Art Museum*: Located next to Qing藤书屋, inheriting garden essence while adapting to modern exhibition needs.
    *   **Zhuji Fengqiao Ancient Town**: Implemented an "overall patchwork" strategy, repairing old residences "as they were" and introducing new formats.
    *   **Bazhi Bridge Area**: Followed "micro-renewal, light intervention," repairing 18 ancient bridges and dredging 5km of rivers, while introducing "15-minute life circle" facilities like community canteens.
    *   **Hangzhou West Lake Museum**: Main structure concealed underground to preserve lake views for citizens.
    *   **Hangzhou Yinglan Center**: Set back 30 meters from Xiangji Temple to preserve the skyline for the ancient monument.
    *   **University Campuses**:
        *   *Zhejiang University Ningbo Campus*: Transformed a former vocational high school into a research university using a "two axes, one ring" structure and 3D-printed pedestrian bridges.
        *   *Westlake University Yun Valley Campus*: Used a ring-shaped water system to connect nature, academics, and life.
        *   *Zhejiang University International Campus (Haining)*: Adopted a residential college model with Neoclassical architecture, integrating with the Jiangnan water town.
    *   **Industrial/Tech Projects**:
        *   *Supergravity Centrifugal Simulation and Experimental Device*: Designed a simple elliptical form to suit precision equipment.
        *   *Hangyang Factory Renovation*: Preserved industrial details while adding office, commercial, and exhibition functions.
5.  **Additional Outcomes**: The article notes that Asian Games venues in Zhejiang have been integrated into citizens' daily lives post-event, and cites other projects like the Wuhan Library and the Yellow River Delta Agricultural Products Trading Service Center.

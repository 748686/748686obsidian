# Event: Yumin County Safflower Industry Whole-Chain Development

## Event Overview
This event documents the transformation of the safflower industry in Yumin County, Xinjiang, into a pillar of local economic development. As reported in *People's Daily* on September 23, 2026, the county has shifted from traditional, small-scale farming to a modernized, full-chain industrial model. This transformation involves technological innovation in seed breeding, the construction of centralized trading infrastructure, deep processing expansion, and integration with the cultural tourism sector. The initiative has stabilized farmer incomes and increased the value of agricultural products, effectively turning a single crop into a comprehensive economic engine for the region.

## Core Facts
*   **Economic Impact:** The entire industry chain of Yumin safflower generates an annual output value exceeding 300 million RMB, benefiting over 5,700 pastoral and agricultural households through stable income increases.
*   **Production Scale:** As of 2026, the safflower planting area in Yumin County is stable at 150,000 mu.
*   **Geographic Advantage:** Yumin County is located on the southern edge of the Ili River Basin (Ta'ei Basin), at the foot of Baleruok Mountain. The region benefits from over 3,000 hours of annual sunlight, a diurnal temperature range often exceeding 15°C, and irrigation using glacial meltwater, which supports high accumulation of pharmacological components like carthamin and linoleic acid.
*   **Seed Innovation:** The county has developed and popularized the "Yumin Thornless Safflower" variety. This thornless variety accounts for 99% of the county's planting area, solving the historical bottleneck of difficult manual harvesting associated with thorny varieties. It is a dual-purpose variety (oil and flower), featuring large buds, early maturity, and water/fertilizer efficiency.
*   **Trading Infrastructure:** In 2025, the county secured 70 million RMB in project funds to construct the China Thornless Safflower Trading Center. The facility covers 91 mu and includes a 3,200-square-meter R&D and testing center and 15,000 square meters of warehouse space.
*   **New Variety Development:** The Shihezi University Safflower Industry Research Institute Yumin Branch has collaborated with an academician team from Wenzhou Medical University to collect nearly 60 safflower germplasm resources. They have bred a new local variety, "Yuhong No. 1," and established a 500-mu seed breeding field. This new variety has increased yield of flower threads and seeds by 20–30% compared to older varieties.
*   **Processing Capacity:** Xinjiang Tianding Safflower Oil Co., Ltd., a local leading enterprise, invested 80 million RMB to build an automated processing line with an annual capacity of 30,000 tons of safflower seeds. The company holds 9 patents and its products have obtained EU organic certification.
*   **Market Expansion:**
    *   **Domestic:** The county holds regular safflower transaction and ordering conferences. The total signed amount for the first three conferences reached 179 million RMB. Through platforms like the Euro-Asia Expo,意向 (intent) contracts totaling approximately 1.55 billion RMB have been signed.
    *   **International:** Leveraging China-Europe freight trains, safflower seed oil and refined safflower threads are exported to Central Asia and Europe.
*   **Tourism Integration:** A 100,000-mu safflower sightseeing belt has been created. The "Yumin Mountain Flower Festival" has become a significant cultural tourism brand, driving growth in local农家乐 (farm stays), homestays, and specialty agricultural product sales.
*   **Policy Support:** In 2026, the county implemented 3 million RMB in interest-subsidized loans to support key enterprises and associations in reducing financing costs for technological upgrades and R&D.
*   **Supply Chain Model:** Since 2024, enterprises have adopted a "free seed supply, seed deduction at harvest" model, where farmers receive seeds in spring and pay in autumn using safflower seeds.

## Known Current Impact
*   **Market Power Shift:** The establishment of the Trading Center has returned pricing power to the origin. Farmers no longer face predatory pricing from small-time traders; instead, they benefit from transparent, quality-based pricing.
*   **Income Stability:** The shift from speculative planting to order-based agriculture (supplying local leading enterprises) has reduced market risk for farmers like Zhu Changjun.
*   **Industrial Upgrading:** The county has moved from selling raw materials to producing high-value goods (organic oil, refined threads, safflower vinegar, honey, and tea).
*   **Self-Sufficiency in Seeds:** The breeding of "Yuhong No. 1" has ended the county's long-term dependence on externally sourced seeds, ensuring adaptability and yield stability.

## Event Conclusion
The development of the safflower industry in Yumin County represents a successful case of rural industrial upgrading driven by technological innovation and institutional reform. By addressing key pain points—such as labor-intensive harvesting (solved by thornless varieties), weak pricing power (solved by the Trading Center), and low附加值 (solved by deep processing and branding)—the county has created a sustainable economic model. The integration of science (Shihezi University/Wenzhou Medical University collaboration), government support (infrastructure and subsidies), and market mechanisms (export and tourism) has allowed a traditional crop to become a modern, high-value pillar industry. The immediate impact is visible in increased farmer incomes and expanded market reach, both domestically and internationally.

## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, *People's Daily* published a report detailing the summer social practice activities of Capital Normal University (首都师范大学). During the 2026 summer vacation, the university organized over 400 practice teams comprising more than 4,000 teachers and students from undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programs. These teams traveled nationwide to conduct practical activities ranging from patriotic education at revolutionary sites to technological innovation support and rural education assistance.

The event is contextualized within the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army. The university leveraged its strength in Marxist studies to guide students in understanding the contemporary significance of the Long March spirit while integrating ideological and political education into real-world social practice.

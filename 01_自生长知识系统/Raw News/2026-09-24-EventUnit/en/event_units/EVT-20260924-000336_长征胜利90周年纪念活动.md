# Event: 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Long March Commemorative Activities (EVT-20260924-000336)

## Event Overview

On September 22, 2026, the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) Central Committee held significant commemorative and directive events marking the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Red Army's Long March. These activities were covered collectively on the front page (Page 04) of the *People's Daily* on September 23, 2026. The events encompass a high-profile thematic exhibition opening in Beijing, political rhetoric linking the Long March spirit to contemporary law enforcement duties, and on-the-ground reporting from former battle sites in Guangxi.

The synthesis of these sources indicates a unified state narrative aimed at reinforcing political loyalty ("Two Establishes"), leveraging historical memory for current social stability, and promoting revolutionary traditions as part of the broader "New Era" campaign toward national rejuvenation.

## Core Facts

**1. Exhibition Opening Ceremony**
*   **Event:** Opening ceremony of the "Great Expedition—Commemoration of the 90th Anniversary of the Victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army Long March" thematic exhibition (often referred to as "Great Expedition").
*   **Date/Time:** Morning of September 22, 2026 (Ceremony started at 10:00 AM).
*   **Location:** Chinese People's Revolution Military Museum, Beijing.
*   **Key Official:** Cai Qi (Member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and Secretary-General of the CPC Central Committee) delivered a speech and announced the opening of the exhibition.
*   **Attendees:** The ceremony was attended by approximately 600 people, including cadres and masses. Other attendees included Yin Li, Li Shulei, Zhang Shengmin, Luosang Jiangcun, and Wang Yong.
*   **Exhibition Content:** The exhibition is divided into 7 sections, featuring 357 photo charts and 1,061 sets of cultural relics and documents.
*   **Protocol:** The ceremony included the performance of the *March of the Volunteers* by the PLA Band, singing of the national anthem, and a moment of silence for revolutionary martyrs who died during the Long March.

**2. Political Directive (Wang Xiaohong)**
*   **Event:** National Public Security System Video Conference.
*   **Date:** September 22, 2026.
*   **Key Official:** Wang Xiaohong (Member of the CPC Central Committee Secretariat and Minister of Public Security) attended and spoke.
*   **Directives:** Emphasized studying the spirit of General Secretary Xi Jinping’s important reply letter to the "Zhangzhou 110" team. Highlighted the need to guard against political security risks, prevent subversion/separatism, manage contradictions, and ensure stability during the upcoming Mid-Autumn and National Day holidays.
*   **Ideological Link:** Explicitly linked the "Long March spirit" to public security duties, framing the police force as a "loyal guardian" tasked with protecting people's happiness and national security.

**3. Field Reporting (Guangxi)**
*   **Location:** Jiuhaiwell Red Army Martyrs Memorial Park, Xinwei Town, Guan County, Guilin City, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region.
*   **Historical Context:** Site of the Xinwei Blockade Battle (November 1934) during the湘江 (Xiang River) Campaign. Over 100 Red Army soldiers died here and were thrown into Jiuhaiwell after the battle.
*   **Current Status:** The site is a National Key Cultural Relic Protection Unit (designated 2006) and a National 4A-level Tourist Attraction. It has been integrated into the Long March National Cultural Park and local anti-corruption education routes.
*   **Artifact:** A faded red flag (copy held at the memorial, original at Guangxi Museum) gifted by a wounded soldier to local civilians (Huang Helin couple) in 1934, preserved by the family and surrendered in 1979.

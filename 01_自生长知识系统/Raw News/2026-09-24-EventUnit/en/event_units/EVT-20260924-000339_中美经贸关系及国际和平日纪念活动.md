# Event: Chinese-US Economic and Trade Relations and the 2026 International Day of Peace Memorial Activities

## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, *People's Daily* published a special report (Pages 05-06) covering two major themes: the stabilization and development of Chinese-US economic and trade relations, and the 2026 International Day of Peace commemorative events held in Wuhan, Hubei Province. The articles emphasize that经贸关系 (economic and trade relations) remain a stabilizer and engine for bilateral ties, while also highlighting international cooperation for peace in the context of complex global challenges.

## Core Facts

### Chinese-US Economic and Trade Relations

**1. Leadership Consensus and Strategic Positioning**
- In May 2026, President Xi Jinping met with U.S. President Donald Trump in Beijing.
- They agreed on a new positioning for building "constructive strategic stability" in China-US relations.
- Key consensus areas: maintaining stable economic/trade relations, expanding pragmatic cooperation across fields, and properly resolving mutual concerns.
- Xi Jinping stated: "The essence of China-US economic and trade relations is mutual benefit and win-win cooperation. Facing differences and friction, equal consultation is the only correct choice."
- Xi emphasized: "China's door for opening up will only open wider. American enterprises are deeply participating in China's reform and opening up, and the Chinese side welcomes strengthened mutually beneficial cooperation with the United States."

**2. Trade and Investment Data (as of H1 2026)**
- China customs statistics: China-US goods trade reached 2 trillion yuan in the first half of 2026, with Q2 growing 13.7% year-on-year.
- As of July 2026, U.S. enterprises have cumulatively established over 84,000 foreign-invested enterprises in China.
- H1 2026: U.S. enterprise investment in China grew 7.8% year-on-year.
- Tesla Shanghai Gigafactory: In December 2025, the 4 millionth vehicle rolled off the line, with 95% of parts sourced locally and over 400 Chinese tier-one suppliers involved.
- General Electric Healthcare: Annual procurement in China exceeds 10 billion yuan with over 1,000 supplier partners.

**3. Business Community Sentiment (2025-2026 Surveys)**
- U.S.-China Business Council (USCBC) survey (2026):
  - 92% of surveyed U.S. enterprises achieved profitability in China in 2025.
  - 95% believe their China operations are critical to global competitiveness.
  - Nearly 60% plan to increase investment in China.
- Shanghai American Chamber of Commerce report:
  - Nearly 80% of member enterprises reported profit levels rising to the highest since 2019 in 2025.
  - 58% of surveyed enterprises are optimistic about China's business outlook over the next 5 years, up 17 percentage points from the previous year.
- South China American Chamber of Commerce survey:
  - 45% of surveyed enterprises rank China as their top global investment destination.
  - Member enterprises' planned profit reinvestment budget in China over the next 3-5 years amounts to approximately $13.79 billion.

**4. High-Level Corporate Cooperation Examples**
- **Zhejiang Rourou Technology**: In late July 2026, participated in business matchmaking in Salt Lake City; achieved initial consensus with 10+ institutions on North American distribution and joint scenario implementation.
- **SAIC-GM**: Signed a joint venture renewal agreement extending cooperation to 2047 (20 additional years).
- **Tesla**: Integrated Doubao voice large model, described as "sparks" of China-U.S. tech cooperation.
- **Qualcomm**: Showcased 50+ innovation cooperation成果 at the 4th China International Supply Chain Innovation Expo, covering AI and 6G.
- **Corning**: Highlighted deep industrial complementarity between China and U.S.

**5. Agricultural Cooperation**
- China is the world's second-largest agricultural importer; the U.S. is the largest agricultural exporter.
- 8th China International Import Expo (CIIE): U.S. Food and Agriculture Pavilion attracted 19 enterprises/organizations; small and medium-sized exhibitors concluded transactions totaling nearly $12 million, up 5% from the previous session.
- 9th CIIE: U.S. enterprises secured the largest签约面积 (contracted exhibition area) among all countries; organizers plan expanded pavilion size and participation.
- China-U.S. Soybean Product Application Value Chain Innovation Center: Established in 2023 by the U.S. Soybean Export Council and Henan Province.
- June 2026: China-U.S. Local Cooperation Dialogue—Zhoukou National Agricultural Hi-Tech Industry Demonstration Zone (Henan) reached a smart agriculture cooperation意向 with Rick Manufacturing Corp (U.S.) for intelligent irrigation and wheat smart planting benchmarks.

**6. Business Exchange Missions**
- Late July 2026: China Council for the Promotion of International Trade organized a 22-enterprise delegation to visit Salt Lake City, Chicago, and Washington, D.C., invited by the U.S.-China Business Council.
- Activities included 10+ business matchmaking events with 120+ U.S. enterprises and institutions.

### 2026 International Day of Peace Commemorative Activities

**1. Event Details**
- **Dates**: September 20-22, 2026.
- **Locations**: Wuhan and Han川 (Hanchuan), Hubei Province.
- **Theme**: "Cooperate to Address Challenges, Build a Peaceful Homeland Together" (合作应对挑战，共建和平家园).
- **Organizers**: Guided by the External Affairs Office of the CPC Central Committee; co-hosted by the China Peace and Disarmament Committee and the Hubei Provincial People's Government.
- **Participants**: 300+ attendees from 60 countries, including political party leaders, peace organization representatives, think tank scholars, civilian friendship figures, foreign diplomats accredited to China, and domestic department representatives.

**2. Key Statements and Perspectives**

**United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres** (video message):
- Described the world as severely lacking in peace, to a dangerous degree.
- Stated: "Peace does not come automatically; it must be actively sought by human society."
- Called for investment in functional communities where peace can take root and in sustainable development.
- Emphasized that every person should enjoy human rights and dignity.
- Urged world leaders to invest in humanity's peaceful future.

**Mauritania National Assembly Speaker (麦盖特)**:
- Described the world as filled with deep divisions, ongoing conflicts, millions of displaced persons, rising extremism, and economic crises consuming vulnerable populations.
- Stated: "We always believe dialogue is the only path serving the interests of all countries' peoples."
- Called the gathering itself "a great deed for peacebuilding."
- Asserted: "Peace is not just the absence of war; it is the prerequisite for development, the guarantee of stability, the foundation of fairness, and the highest manifestation of human dignity."
- Warned: "There is no development amid gunfire; there is no future under the shadow of fear."

**Malaysia Senate Deputy Speaker (贾兹兰)**:
- Stated that strong economic development requires a stable world.
- Emphasized indivisible security, particularly in maritime domains.
- Noted Malaysia's policy framework under Prime Minister Anwar aligns with Xi Jinping's Global Development Initiative, Global Security Initiative, Global Civilization Initiative, and Global Governance Initiative.

**African Union Secretary-General for African Governance Architecture and Peace and Security Architecture (萨拉赫·哈马德)**:
- Stated: "Without peace, there is no development."
- Highlighted that war and poverty disrupt normal life秩序 globally, especially affecting vulnerable groups in Africa.
- Called for transforming shared peace convictions into practical development outcomes through collaboration.

**British Historian/Author Robert Lawrence Kuhn** (American Kuhn Foundation Chairman, author of the International Forum article on Page 05):
- Stated that stable China-U.S. economic and trade relations constitute an important international public good.
- Emphasized元首-level strategic communication has irreplaceable navigational role in reducing strategic miscalculation and enhancing political mutual trust.
- Cited U.S.-China Business Council 2026 survey data (95% and 92% figures above).
- Identified energy cooperation as a typical example of highly aligned interests: China's increased procurement of U.S. crude oil and LNG serves its energy security needs while helping the U.S. expand exports and balance trade.
- Warned against "decoupling" rhetoric:
  - Called it inconsistent with economic laws and objective reality.
  - Noted capital, technology, and production各环节 are deeply intertwined in an inseparable industrial network.
  - Stated full "decoupling" would substantially raise transaction costs, disrupt global supply chains, and harm enterprises and consumers on both sides—fundamentally unworkable.
  - Acknowledged China's industrial advantages in green technology and new energy vehicles as part of the global supply system.
  - Asserted market logic and pragmatic demands will ultimately prevail over ideologically driven "decoupling" claims.
- On AI cooperation:
  - Described AI as a frontier technology with cross-border spillover effects, bringing both benefits and risks.
  - Called for distinguishing risk management from technological competition.
  - Advocated focusing on common issues for dialogue and collaboration, building safety standards and ethical cooperation frameworks.
  - Stated this would set benchmarks for global AI governance and create a stable environment for healthy industrial development in both countries.
- Concluded that while one meeting cannot eliminate structural differences, it can set the tone for bilateral relations, reduce great power strategic miscalculation, and provide stable expectations for global trade, finance, and supply chains.

**Foreign Youths in China**:
- **Jack (American)**: Studied tai chi at Wudang Mountain; deeply influenced by "harmony is precious" (以和为贵); explained it means finding balance within rules, not blind concession, but cultural confidence and China's peace tradition; described true skill as defending traditions and solving problems through negotiation.

**Historical Remembrance Figures**:
- **Anthony Jones** (descendant of British POW rescued during WWII):
  - His grandfather, Thomas Jones, was among 384 British POWs rescued from the SS *Lisbon Maru* by Chinese fishermen in 1942 near Zhoushan.
  - Stated: "We regard the Zhoushan fishermen as family. I am now a music teacher; I hope to teach children through music what love, kindness, and mutual respect are. Let us remember history, cherish peace, and pass this friendship down generation to generation, becoming an eternal bridge connecting our peoples."
- References to "驼峰天使" (Hump Angels) nurse Huang Huanxia, who cared for Flying Tigers patients and was treated as family by the pilots.

**Bulgaria Former Vice Prime Minister (特伦科娃)**:
- Stated: "People's bonds are built not only through political relations but also through education, culture, economic and trade cooperation, and interpersonal exchanges."
- Believed dialogue among peoples and nations is one of the most powerful pathways to building a more peaceful world.
- Noted international cooperation does not mean becoming identical, but respecting differences and collaborating within them.

**Oregon State Pro-Tem Senator James Manning (American)**:
- Called for active participation in international cooperation, keeping communication channels open, preventing misunderstandings, and forming cooperative traditions for constructive problem-solving.
- Stated: "Let people strengthen exchanges, scientists share knowledge, entrepreneurs build trust, and government officials choose dialogue rather than estrangement."
- Emphasized developing peace through development and measuring progress not only by what is created but by whether relationships are strengthened and whether the next generation has ample reason to believe in the power of cooperation.

## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, the *People's Daily* (Theory Section, Page 09) published two significant theoretical articles under the framework of "Deeply Studying and Implementing Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era." The date coincides with the Ninth Chinese Farmers' Harvest Festival (the autumn equinox).

The first article, authored by the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences (CAAS), focuses on using agricultural sci-tech innovation to support the construction of a strong agricultural nation. The second article, authored by Li Hongfeng, analyzes the essential connotations of Xi Jinping's Party-building thought. While published in the same section and sharing the overarching political-theoretical context, these articles address distinct domains: one is technical and economic (agricultural modernization), and the other is ideological and organizational (Party governance).

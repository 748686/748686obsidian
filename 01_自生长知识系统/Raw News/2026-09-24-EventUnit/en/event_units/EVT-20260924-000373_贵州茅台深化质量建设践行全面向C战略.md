## Event Overview

In September 2026, coinciding with China's National "Quality Month," Kweichow Moutai (贵州茅台) announced the deepening of its quality management systems and the strategic integration of quality assurance with its consumer-centric "Comprehensive-to-C" (全面向C) strategy.

The initiative emphasizes a dual approach: preserving traditional craftsmanship and natural brewing cycles while implementing modern, data-driven quality control systems. The core objective is to align product quality and service delivery directly with consumer perceptions, thereby solidifying the brand's market position through long-term quality excellence rather than short-term output.

## Core Facts

### 1. Strategic Alignment
*   **"Comprehensive-to-C" Strategy:** In 2026, Moutai fully launched a strategy centered on consumers. Quality management has expanded from mere production processes to the entire industry chain, linking consumer needs to design, procurement, production, delivery, and service.
*   **Quality Month Initiatives:** During the September 2026 National Quality Month, Moutai proposed optimizing the quality management system across the full industry chain and establishing a "consumer perception-driven mechanism."

### 2. Traditional Craftsmanship and Philosophy
*   **Core Philosophy:** "Quality is the Soul of Life" (质量是生命之魂).
*   **Quality Outlook:** "Reverence for fundamentals, adherence to the Dao, strict observance of craft, sufficient aging of vintage, and sale of no new wine" (崇本守道、坚守工艺、贮足陈酿、不卖新酒).
*   **Brewing Cycle:** Moutai liquor requires at least five years from raw material input to finished product export.
*   **Seasonal Rhythm:** Strict adherence to natural cycles: qu (starter) production in early summer (Duanwu) and sand (sorghum) pouring in autumn (Chongyang).
*   **Reliance on Craftsmanship:** Key nodes in qu-making and liquor-making depend on experienced artisans due to variables such as temperature, humidity, and fermentation microbes.

### 3. Modern Quality Management System
*   **Matrix Management Model:**
    *   **Vertical:** Multi-level quality safety responsibility system led by the Chief Quality Officer system.
    *   **Horizontal:** Full-process quality control from "good seeds" to final product.
*   **Standardization:**
    *   19 key work principles (e.g., manual qu-making, wine-for-wine blending).
    *   22 technical method essentials (e.g., qu selection, grain moistening, wine sorting, pan blending).
*   **Data Integration:** Deployment of four machine-learning-based quality evaluation models for liquor-making engineering to merge data with experience.
*   **Scientific Research:** Utilization of metagenomics, flavor chemistry, and metabolic analysis to study relationships between raw materials, environment, microbes, craft, and timing.

### 4. Recognized Achievements
*   **National Quality Award:** Won in 2003, 2011, and 2023.
*   **EFQM Global Award:** Awarded the Seven-Diamond rating and the "Inspiring Culture" Outstanding Achievement Award in June 2024 for the 2023 cycle.
*   **Methodology Application:** Moutai has systematically introduced the EFQM Excellence Performance Model into corporate governance and production management.

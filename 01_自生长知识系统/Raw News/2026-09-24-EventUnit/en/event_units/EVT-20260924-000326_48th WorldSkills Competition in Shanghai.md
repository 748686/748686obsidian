# 48th WorldSkills Competition in Shanghai

## Event Overview

The 48th WorldSkills Competition is an international vocational skills contest hosted in Shanghai, China. It opened on September 22, 2026, and is scheduled to run through September 27, 2026. The event is organized by WorldSkills International and co-hosted by China's Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the Shanghai Municipal People's Government.

The competition is accompanied by two related events: the WorldSkills General Assembly and the WorldSkills Expo. The theme of this edition is "Skills Change the World, Skills Illuminate the Future." The event has attracted diplomatic attention, with congratulatory messages from Chinese national leadership and the attendance of foreign heads of government.

## Core Facts

**Event Identity**
*   **Name:** 48th WorldSkills Competition
*   **Location:** Shanghai, China
*   **Dates:** September 22–27, 2026
*   **Theme:** "Skills Change the World, Skills Illuminate the Future" (技能改变世界 技能照亮前程)
*   **Organizer:** WorldSkills International
*   **Co-organizers:** Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (China); Shanghai Municipal People's Government

**Diplomatic Engagement**
*   **Xi Jinping (President of China):** Issued a congratulatory message on September 22, 2026. In his message, he noted that the current round of scientific and technological revolution and industrial transformation is having a profound impact on global skills development. He emphasized that the competition closely aligns with industrial needs and provides a platform for技能人才 (skilled personnel) from various countries to hone their skills and learn from one another. He stated that China hopes to use the hosting of the competition as an opportunity to encourage and guide more workers, especially young people, to pursue pathways to success through skills. He expressed willingness to strengthen exchanges and cooperation with all parties to promote the development of world skills movements and better serve global economic growth and improvement of people's livelihoods.
*   **Foreign Leaders:** Malaysia Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and Kyrgyzstan Prime Minister Kasmalyev were invited to attend. Prime Minister Anwar is scheduled to attend the opening ceremony of the WorldSkills Competition and the launch ceremony of the 5th Global Digital Trade Fair. Prime Minister Kasmalyev is scheduled to attend the launch ceremony of the 5th Global Digital Trade Fair.

**Associated Events**

*   **WorldSkills Expo:** To be held during the competition period.
*   **5th Global Digital Trade Fair:** Held concurrently in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, from September 23–27, 2026.

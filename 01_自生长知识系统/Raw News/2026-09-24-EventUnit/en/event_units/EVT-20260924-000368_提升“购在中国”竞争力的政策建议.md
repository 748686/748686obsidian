# Event Unit: Policy Recommendations for Enhancing the Competitiveness of "Shop in China"

## Event Overview
On September 23, 2026, the People's Daily published an editorial titled *"Do Good 'Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division' to Enhance the Competitiveness of 'Shop in China'"* (做好“加减乘除”，提升“购在中国”竞争力) by Qiu Yi and Wei Kai. The article proposes a systematic framework for optimizing China's inbound consumption sector by leveraging the country's ultra-large market advantage. The analysis argues that while recent policy moves (visa waivers, tax refunds, payment facilitation) have strengthened the appeal of Chinese tourism and shopping, significant gaps remain in consumption conversion rates, digital adaptation, and multilingual environments. The authors advocate for targeted "arithmetic" interventions across supply, institutional, business model, and channel dimensions to stimulate inbound consumption momentum.

## Core Facts

### Statistical Baseline (Reported as 2025 Data)
*   **National Tax Refund Growth:** Nationwide outbound tax refund sales grew by nearly 100% (approx. 1x) in 2025 compared to the previous period.
*   **Trend Shift:** "China Tour" and "China Shop" have emerged as new international trends.
*   **Key City Concentration:** In 2025, the five designated International Consumption Center Cities (Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou, Tianjin, and Chongqing) accounted for:
    *   One-third (1/3) of inbound foreign visitors nationwide.
    *   Two-thirds (2/3) of national outbound tax refund sales.

### Policy Framework: The "Four Operations"
The article structures policy recommendations around four operational metaphors:

**1. Addition (Consumption Supply Enhancement)**
*   **Supply Expansion:** Leverage the five key cities to attract international brand flagship/first-store locations. Utilize platforms like the China International Import Expo (CIIE) and China International Consumer Goods Expo (CICE) as hubs for global product launches.
*   **Product & Service Upgrade:** Cultivate "Guochao" (national trend) premium products and high-end cultural tourism experiences to fill gaps in high-end goods and immersive services.
*   **Tax Refund Efficiency:** Promote "refund upon purchase" (即买即退) services across major tourism cities and commercial districts, ensuring cross-regional mutual recognition and real-time fund settlement.
*   **Atmosphere Creation:** Deploy multilingual smart navigation and guidance systems in key consumption zones; collaborate with foreign influencers for live-streamed scenario promotion.

**2. Subtraction (Institutional Optimization)**
*   **Market Access:** Simplify approval processes for high-end consumer goods and fashion services; implement looser trade management measures.
*   **Process Streamlining:** Optimize tax refund procedures and iterate port inspection workflows to align with paperless "Tax Refund 2.0" policies.
*   **Data & Certification:** Establish cross-departmental data-sharing mechanisms for tax refunds and accelerate the construction of a unified domestic-international product certification system.

**3. Multiplication (Business Format Empowerment)**
*   **Integration:** Bind tax refund policies with cultural tourism routes;布局 (layout) tax refund shops and Guochao experience stores in historical blocks and key scenic areas.
*   **Digitalization:** Develop mini-programs or apps integrating tax eligibility queries, merchant navigation, online invoicing, and after-sales support.
*   **First-Store Tax-Free Exploration:** Encourage international brands to open first stores and register as tax refund merchants to attract global fashion enthusiasts.
*   **Word-of-Mouth Marketing:** Incentivize overseas tourists and bloggers to share tax refund shopping experiences to boost global influence through authentic reviews.

**4. Division (Channel Facilitation)**
*   **Barrier Removal:** Eliminate circulation obstacles for "Same Line, Same Standard, Same Quality" (同线同标同质) products, ensuring quality consistency between domestic and international markets.
*   **Cost Reduction:** Support enterprises in optimizing cross-border warehousing, sorting, and distribution to lower logistics costs for both imported goods and exported domestic goods.
*   **Public Services:** Build a full-chain service system covering "eating, living, traveling, touring, shopping, and entertainment"; improve cross-border payment settlement networks; establish efficient consumer dispute mediation mechanisms.
*   **Multi-City Adaptation:** Eliminate rule discrepancies between cities to accommodate multi-city itineraries.
*   **Export Channel Broadening:** Create a "white list" of overseas-acclaimed domestic products to support high-quality goods in expanding global reach.

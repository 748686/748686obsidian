**3. First 10,000-Ton Grain Air-Supported Structure Membrane Blowing Completed**
*   **Date:** Recently reported on September 22, 2026 (completion occurred prior to the report date).
*   **Location:** Changchun, Jilin Province.
*   **Responsible Entity:** China Grain Reserves Group Co., Ltd. (Sinograin).
*   **Technical Specifications:** The project plans to build four grain air-supported structures. Each single structure has a diameter of 30 meters and a height of 29 meters.
*   **Significance:** This is the first application of 10,000-ton grade grain air-supported structure projects in the cold region of Northeast China, setting a new domestic record for the single-unit scale of large-scale air-supported grain storage facilities.
*   **Design Features:** The design accounts for Northeast China's climate, offering improved thermal insulation, airtightness, and moisture resistance. The overall airtightness exceeds national requirements for high-standard grain warehouses, facilitating controlled atmosphere storage to inhibit pests and reduce loss. The interior is beamless and column-free, allowing for large-span, unobstructed storage space suitable for mechanized automated operations.
*   **Construction Efficiency:** Using an inflatable forming process, the warehouse body and internal construction can be completed in approximately 120 days after foundation construction, with simplified procedures and less construction waste.

**4. 2026 International Forum on Grotto Temple Protection**
*   **Dates:** September 21–22, 2026.
*   **Location:** Datong City, Shanxi Province.
*   **Key Themes:**
    *   Evolution of international cultural heritage protection in response to climate change.
    *   New challenges posed by climate change to grotto temple protection.
    *   Topics included: "Protection and Technological Innovation of Grotto Temples under Climate Change," "Archaeological Research and Value Interpretation of Grotto Temples," and "Protection and International Exchange of Grotto Temples."
*   **China's Contribution:** The forum summarized China's achievements in grotto temple protection, noting improvements in institutional systems, protection intensity, scientific and talent support, research interpretation, and international cooperation. China presented its "wisdom and solutions" for world grotto temple protection.

Vocational Education Teacher Inherits Craftsmanship Spirit to Cultivate Talent – Zhao Kongbiao’s Narrative

## Event Overview

On September 23, 2026, *People’s Daily* published an article titled “Where Welding Sparks Fall, There Is Starlight” (Section 14: Education). The piece features Zhao Kongbiao, a welding practical training teacher at Jiangsu Shipping Vocational and Technical College. The article narrates his journey from a vocational school graduate and factory welder to a college instructor dedicated to passing on the “craftsman spirit.” It highlights the significant contribution of vocational education to modern industries, specific achievements in student skill competitions, and the establishment of a master skills studio to tackle technical challenges.

## Core Facts

**Subject Identity and Background**
*   **Name:** Zhao Kongbiao.
*   **Profession:** Welding practical training teacher.
*   **Institution:** Jiangsu Shipping Vocational and Technical College.
*   **Employment Start:** Joined the college in 2016.
*   **Early Career:** After graduating from secondary vocational school, he worked as a welder in a boiler factory and a shipyard.
*   **Mentorship Influence:** His mentor taught him that “welding is not just about steel plates, but also about a person’s mindset/temperament.”

**Professional Philosophy and Methodology**
*   **Core Belief:** He views welding as physically demanding work requiring repeated practice; fear of hardship prevents skill improvement.
*   **Safety Emphasis:** He stresses to students that a single weld seam relates to the safety of all crew members, leaving no room for松懈 (negligence/laxity).
*   **Training Simulation:** While preparing for the National Seafarer Skills Competition, he imported international competition standards into the training room, simulating real shipyard environments including noise, high temperatures, and time limits.
*   **Student Support:** He personally assisted a student who failed to qualify after welding over 10 test plates in a row, analyzing parameters and practicing repeatedly with them.

**Achievements and Outcomes**
*   **Competition Result:** Won first place nationally in the National Seafarer Skills Competition. Students placed the gold medal around his neck during the awards ceremony.
*   **Studio Establishment:** With school support, he founded a “Skill Master Studio.”
*   **Technical Breakthroughs:** The studio addressed “chokepoint” (卡脖子) difficulties, specifically including low-temperature high-strength steel welding.
*   **Talent Output:** Over the years, the studio has produced more than 20 provincial and municipal-level technical experts.
*   **Career Trajectory of Graduates:** His former students are now working in shipyards, ports, and on waterways.
*   **Strategic Alignment:** Zhao states his teaching is closely linked to the “maritime power strategy” (海洋强国战略).

**Industry Context**
*   **Statistic:** In the past five years (relative to the 2026 publication), vocational education has provided over 70% of new high-quality, skilled talents for modern industries.

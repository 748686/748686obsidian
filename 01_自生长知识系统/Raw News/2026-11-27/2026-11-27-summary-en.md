**「Solution」** To justify pausing these values, the author uses an analogy of an ancient sacred lake. Applying this to the modern era, the author argues that death, disease, and war are still rampant threats. Because humanity has not yet won the fight against mortality, the costly sacrifices required to maintain sacredness are not currently justifiable. These non-efficient practices must be deferred until we have successfully defeated these existential dangers.

**「Takeaway」** The author concludes that because humanity is in a persistent state of warfare against death and disease, there will be ample time for sacredness only after these fundamental threats have been defeated.

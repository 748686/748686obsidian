# Event Name
Rural Revitalization and Integrated Development of Shazhou Area, Rucheng County, Hunan Province

## Event Overview
This event documents the "ten villages, one community" (十村一社区) coordinated rural development initiative in the Shazhou area of Rucheng County, Chenzhou City, Hunan Province. The initiative, which began in 2023, aims to transition from single-village success ("One Village Red") to integrated district prosperity ("Area Prosperity") by leveraging the tourism appeal of the historic "Half of a Quilt" Red Army heritage site. The effort involves implementing six major projects focused on industrial development, income growth, environmental improvement, infrastructure, and governance to create a common prosperity demonstration zone.

## Core Facts
*   **Geographical Scope:** The initiative covers the "Shazhou surrounding area" in Wenchang Yao Ethnic Township, Rucheng County, Chenzhou City, Hunan Province. It involves a cluster of 10 villages and 1 community (ten villages, one community).
*   **Historical Origin:** In November 1934, during the Long March, three Red Army female soldiers stayed overnight at the home of villager Xu Jiexiu in Shazhou Village. They cut a quilt in half to leave one half for the villagers, a story known as "Half of a Quilt."
*   **Initiative Start:** The coordinated development strategy was implemented starting in 2023.
*   **Strategic Framework:** The development model is based on six major projects: bottom-line safeguarding, industrial cultivation, common prosperity through increased income, environmental improvement, infrastructure upgrading, and rural governance.
*   **Economic Growth:** The collective economic income of the "ten villages, one community" increased from an average of 115,400 RMB per village to 348,700 RMB per village.
*   **Tourism Statistics:** Over the past three years, the Shazhou area has attracted over 4.3 million tourists.
*   **Recent Event Context:** As of September 2026, the area hosted a Chenzhou City walking activity commemorating the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Red Army's Long March.

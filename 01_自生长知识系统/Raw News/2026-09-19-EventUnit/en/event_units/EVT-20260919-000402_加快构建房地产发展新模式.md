## Event Overview
On September 18, 2026, the State Council Information Office held a thematic press conference under the "Starting the '15th Five-Year' Period" series. The Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD) announced its strategic framework for high-quality development in housing and urban construction for the upcoming "15th Five-Year" period. ## Core Facts

### 1. Policy Framework for the New Real Estate Development Model
The Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD) defined the "New Real Estate Development Model" based on the following structure, reported by Zhang Xuetao, Director of the Real Estate Market Regulation Department:
*   **One Goal:** Ensure people have a place to live ("zhuzuo youju") at a higher level.
*   **Two Systems:** Improve both the guarantee system (public/affordable housing) and the market system, creating a supply structure with clear positioning and functional complementarity.
*   **Three Key Institutions:**
    1.  **Project Company System:** One real estate project corresponds to one independent legal entity (project company). Headquarters are strictly prohibited from misappropriating project company funds.
    2.  **Lead Bank System:** One project is assigned one lead bank (or syndicate). All development, construction, and sales funds must be deposited with this lead bank, which provides financing services.
    3.  **Completed Housing Sales System:** New development projects are required to prioritize selling completed properties ("seen is what is obtained") to fundamentally prevent delivery risks. For projects that must use pre-sales, stricter supervision of pre-sale funds is mandated.
*   **Four Elements:** Linkage among "People, Housing, Land, and Money" to achieve reasonable factor allocation (housing demand dictates housing supply, which dictates land supply, which dictates money supply).

*Status:* It is reported that all three foundational real estate institutions have been issued as of the press conference date.

### 2. Market Context and Data
*   **Market Shift:** The real estate market has entered a "stock stage" (inventory/secondary market focus).
*   **Demand-Supply Changes:** Significant changes in the supply-demand relationship have occurred.
*   **Secondary Market Data:**
    *   2020: Secondary home transaction share was 27%.
    *   2025: Secondary home transaction share increased to 46%.
    *   Jan-Aug 2026: Secondary home transaction share reached 52%, exceeding the 50% threshold.
*   **"14th Five-Year" Achievements:**
    *   Over 11 million sets of affordable housing and resettlement houses were completed, benefiting over 30 million people.
    *   Over 1.78 million rural low-income households had dangerous houses renovated.

### 3. "Good House" Construction Standards
MOHURD identified four keywords for "Good Houses": Safety, Comfort, Green (Ecological), and Smart. Specific standard updates in the latest "Residential Project Norms" include:
*   **Ceiling Height:** Increased from 2.8 meters to 3.0 meters.
*   **Elevator Requirement:** Changed from mandatory for buildings of 7 floors or more to mandatory for buildings of **4 floors or more**.
*   **Safety Rails:** Height of handrails on balconies, exterior corridors, and all open-air areas raised to no less than **1.2 meters** for child safety.
*   **Innovations:** "Pipe separation" (separating water/heating/electricity from the main structure) to facilitate maintenance; modular and adjustable living spaces.

### 4. Urban Renewal Plan ("15th Five-Year" Period)
The "Urban Renewal '15th Five-Year' Plan" includes 10 development indicators, 23 key tasks, and 14 major projects. Key targets for the upcoming period:
*   **Civil Livelihood Projects:**
    *   Start renovation of **115,000** aging urban residential communities.
    *   Implement "complete community" construction in **5,000** communities.
    *   Update or install **575,000** residential elevators.
    *   Construct/renovate **50,000 hectares** of urban green space.
    *   Add **15,000** pocket parks.
    *   Construct/renovate **20,000 kilometers** of urban greenways.
*   **Development Projects:**
    *   Renovate/upgrade **1,500** old urban districts and industrial zones.
    *   Restore/enhance a new batch of national historic and cultural cities.
    *   Renovate/upgrade **300** historic and cultural districts.
*   **Safety/Infrastructure Projects:**
    *   Construct/renovate approximately **770,000 kilometers** of urban underground pipe networks:
        *   Gas: ~200,000 km
        *   Drainage: ~175,000 km
        *   Water Supply: ~175,000 km
        *   Sewage: ~100,000 km
        *   Heating: ~120,000 km
    *   Promote underground utility tunnels where appropriate, with an investment scale exceeding **5 trillion yuan**.

## Event Conclusion
The event represents a formal policy pivot for China's real estate sector into the "15th Five-Year" period. The central theme is the transition from a supply-driven, incremental growth model to a demand-driven, stock-based, and quality-focused model. Key structural reforms include the legalization of the "Lead Bank" and "Project Company" systems to improve financial discipline, and the prioritization of "Completed Housing Sales" to mitigate buyer risk. Simultaneously, the government is directing massive capital (over 5 trillion yuan) into urban infrastructure (underground pipes) and neighborhood renovation (115,000 old communities), positioning urban renewal as a primary economic stimulus and quality-of-life improvement initiative. The data indicating that secondary homes now constitute over 50% of transactions supports the narrative that the market has structurally changed, necessitating these new institutional frameworks.

# Event Name

## Event Overview

On September 18, 2026, the State Council Information Office of China held a press conference to introduce the preparation status of the 140th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair). The event, scheduled to be held in Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, from October 15 to November 4, 2026, will be conducted in three phases. Key themes of the upcoming fair include a shift toward "new, smart, and specialized" characteristics, with a strong emphasis on integrating artificial intelligence and big data into exhibition operations, and achieving full coverage of ten categories of producer services for the first time.

## Core Facts

The following facts are derived from the provided source material:

*   **Event Dates and Location:** The 140th Canton Fair is scheduled to take place from October 15 to November 4, 2026, in Guangzhou, Guangdong Province. It will be held in three phases.
*   **Press Conference:** On September 18, 2026, the State Council Information Office held a press conference to announce details regarding the event.
*   **Participating Enterprises:** Approximately 32,000 enterprises are registered to exhibit. Over 11,000 of these are designated as "little giant," "single-item champion," or other high-quality enterprises.
*   **Technology Adoption:** 64% of participating enterprises are applying new technologies such as the Industrial Internet and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
*   **Product Composition:** New products account for 23.2% of exhibits, and green products account for 26%, both representing all-time highs.
*   **Smart Applications:** For the first time, the fair will feature 22 smart application scenarios and 16 AI-specific applications. These include hall-wide booth-level navigation and augmented reality (AR) information displays for booths.
*   **Service Industry Coverage:** The trade service exhibition area will achieve full coverage of ten categories of producer services for the first time. 36.5% of exhibiting enterprises are implementing a "product + service" model transition.
*   **Buyer Registration:** Over 210,000 buyers from 196 countries and regions have pre-registered. Professional buyers constitute nearly 80% of this total, with significant participation from countries involved in the "Belt and Road" initiative.
*   **Support Measures:** Guangdong Province has launched 29 measures to upgrade service quality, including dedicated lanes at Baiyun Airport and the implementation of a 240-hour transit visa exemption. Guangzhou is coordinating over 2,000 taxis and ride-hailing vehicles daily and establishing dedicated schedules for public transport.

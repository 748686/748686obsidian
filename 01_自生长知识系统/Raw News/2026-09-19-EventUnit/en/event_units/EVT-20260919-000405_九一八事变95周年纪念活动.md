## Event Overview
On September 18, 2026, marking the 95th anniversary of the September 18 Incident (a pivotal event in the Japanese invasion of China), multiple locations across China held commemorative activities to remember the history and honor the martyrs of the War of Resistance against Japanese Aggression. These events included ceremonies at national museums in Beijing, educational activities in Heilongjiang, tribute rituals in Nanjing, and memorial services in Nanchang. The overarching theme of these activities was to "memorize history, honor the martyrs, cherish peace, and create the future."

## Core Facts
*   **Date of Commemoration:** September 18, 2026.
*   **Historical Occasion:** 95th anniversary of the September 18 Incident.
*   **Beijing Activity:**
    *   Location: Hall of the People’s Liberation Monument and Museum of War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression.
    *   Participants: Approximately 200 representatives from universities and museum staff.
    *   Actions: Played the national anthem, observed a moment of silence, offered flowers to martyrs, and presented a lecture based on the exhibition "For National Liberation and World Peace."
    *   Cross-regional Collaboration: A live-streamed joint "cloud ideological and political education class" was conducted with the "September 18" History Museum in Shenyang, Liaoning, under the theme "Rivers and Mountains as Witnesses, War of Resistance Memories."
*   **Heilongjiang/Harbin Activity:**
    *   Location: Museum of War Criminals of the Japanese Kwantung Army Unit 731.
    *   Participants: Over 400 youth representatives from government, schools, fire, medical, state-owned enterprises, and railway sectors.
    *   Organizers: Communist Youth League Committees of Heilongjiang Province and Harbin City; Department of Veterans Affairs of Heilongjiang Province.
    *   Format: Combined ceremonial education and on-site study tours, featuring three segments: "The Alarm Clock Rings: Do Not Forget National Humiliation," "Echoes of Mountains and Rivers: Mourn the Loyalty," and "Youth Temper Their Ambition: Strong Country I Am Responsible."
*   **Nanjing Activity:**
    *   Location: Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre by Japanese Invaders.
    *   Participants: Representatives from various sectors of society.
    *   Actions: Raised the national flag, sang the national anthem, struck the peace bell, and observed silence for victims and heroes. A student representative from Nanjing University of the Arts recited war poetry.
*   **Nanchang Activity:**
    *   Location: Garden Plaza of the New Fourth Army Headquarters Museum (Xihu District, Nanchang).
    *   Participants: Over 100 representatives from society, students, and volunteers.
    *   Actions: Observed silence, offered floral tributes to statues of revolutionaries.
    *   Exhibition: Featured a special exhibition titled "Long March Tempering Original Aspirations, Red Soil Nurturing Loyal Souls – Yu Hua Martyrs Who Came from Jiangxi Land," which included 125 historical photos/documents and 25 physical items/sets related to Jiangxi-originated or active Yu Hua martyrs.

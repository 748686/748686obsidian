# Event Name

**Release of National Fiscal Revenue and Expenditure Data for the First 8 Months of 2026**

## Event Overview

On September 18, 2026, the Ministry of Finance of the People's Republic of China released data indicating that national general public budget revenue for January through August 2026 reached 15.66 trillion yuan, representing a year-on-year increase of 5.7%. During the same period, national general public budget expenditure reached 18.15 trillion yuan, an increase of 1.2% year-on-year. This data was reported on September 19, 2026, by *People's Daily*, citing the Ministry of Finance's release.

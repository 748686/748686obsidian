# West-Kang High-Speed Rail: Completion of Acceptance for Four New Stations

## Event Overview
On September 18, 2026, four newly constructed passenger stations on the West-Kang High-Speed Railway (Xikang High-Speed Rail) line connecting Xi'an and Ankang in Shaanxi Province completed their completion acceptance inspections. The stations involved are Niubeiliang Station, Zuoshu West Station, Zhen'an West Station, and Ankang West Station. Upon operational launch, this infrastructure improvement is projected to reduce the travel time between the two endpoints from the current approximately 3 hours to less than 1 hour. This event was reported by *People's Daily* on September 19, 2026.

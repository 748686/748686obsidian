# Event Name

National Advanced Manufacturing Conference and Development Strategy

## Event Overview

Between September 16 and 17, 2026, the National Advanced Manufacturing Conference was held in Beijing. In conjunction with this event, the General Secretary of the Communist Party of China issued important instructions regarding the development of advanced manufacturing. The instructions emphasize "completely, accurately, and comprehensively implementing the new development concepts" and "coordinating high-quality development with high-level security." The core objective is to accelerate the construction of a modern industrial system with advanced manufacturing as its backbone, thereby consolidating and expanding the foundation of the real economy to support Chinese-style modernization. The "15th Five-Year Plan" recommendations place the construction of a modern industrial system as the top strategic task, with advanced manufacturing identified as the key driver for new quality productivity and economic resilience.

## Core Facts

*   **Event:** National Advanced Manufacturing Conference.
*   **Dates:** September 16–17, 2026.
*   **Location:** Beijing, China.
*   **Key Official:** The General Secretary (referred to as "General Secretary" or "President Xi" in the context of the instruction) issued important instructions on developing advanced manufacturing.
*   **Strategic Positioning:** Advanced manufacturing is described as the "backbone" of the modern industrial system, which is the "strategic support" of China's economy.
*   **Policy Direction:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" recommendations prioritize the construction of a modern industrial system.
*   **Development Paths:** The strategy emphasizes three main directions:
    1.  **Intelligentization (Zhì):** Advancing "Artificial Intelligence + Manufacturing" and digital-intelligent transformation.
    2.  **Greenization (Lǜ):** Promoting green technology and ecological priority, noting that "new quality productivity is essentially green productivity."
    3.  **Integration (Róng):** Fostering cross-boundary integration between advanced manufacturing and modern services, including in fields like information technology, new materials, and biotechnology.
*   **Economic Context:**
    *   China’s manufacturing added value has held the top global position for 16 consecutive years, accounting for nearly 30% of the global total.
    *   Over the past 10 years, high-tech manufacturing added value has grown at an annual average of 10%, and equipment manufacturing at over 8%.
    *   New energy vehicle sales in China have grown by more than 930 times since 2013.
    *   There are over 8,000 national-level green factories.
    *   China has built over 56,000 basic-level smart factories, over 9,000 advanced-level smart factories, over 500 excellence-level smart factories, and 15 leading smart factories.

## Event Conclusion

The National Advanced Manufacturing Conference of September 2026 marked a significant policy consolidation for China’s industrial strategy, reinforcing advanced manufacturing as the backbone of the nation's modern industrial system. The General Secretary’s instructions emphasized a tripartite approach of intelligentization, greenization, and integration to navigate both domestic economic transitions and rising global competition. By anchoring these directives in the "15th Five-Year Plan," the state is signaling a long-term commitment to enhancing industrial resilience, autonomous controllability, and the development of new quality productivity. The event serves as a top-level directive for all economic actors to align with the goal of transforming China’s complete industrial system into a globally competitive, secure, and innovative engine for Chinese-style modernization.

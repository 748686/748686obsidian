## Core Facts

*   **Event Date and Location:** The 20th Asian Games opened on the evening of September 19, 2026, in Nagoya, Japan.
*   **Preparation Status:** As of September 18, 2026, multiple Chinese teams had arrived at competition venues to begin final pre-competition sprint training.
*   **Badminton:** On September 18, the Chinese badminton team conducted adaptive training at Ichinomiya City General Gymnasium, noting distinct headwind/tailwind conditions and adequate lighting.
*   **Table Tennis:** On September 17, the Chinese table tennis team conducted its first pre-competition training at Toyota Sky Gymnasium. The team arrived in Nagoya on September 16. Three Olympic champions on the team are competing in three events each.
    *   **Athlete Quotes:**
        *   Sun Yingsha: Stated she aims to enjoy each match and build form gradually.
        *   Wang Manyu: Emphasized the need to maintain physical condition due to the dense schedule and produce closed-training quality.
        *   Wang Chuqin: Expressed a goal to prepare for a "marathon-style schedule," face challenges bravely, and strive to win every point.
*   **Martial Arts:** The Chinese martial arts team sent 12 athletes to compete in 12 of the 15 available events. On September 17, the team completed its first training session, focusing on recovery, time-zone adjustment, and mock competition procedures.
*   **Diving:**
    *   **History:** Since the 1974 Tehran Asian Games, the Chinese diving team has won all event gold medals for 13 consecutive editions.
    *   **Current Status:** The team held internal tests in Beijing recently. On September 26–30, the Asian Games diving competitions will take place at the Tokyo Aquatics Center. The team sent 12 athletes to compete in all 10 events.
    *   **Athlete Quote:** Chen Yuxi, a 4-time Women's 10m Platform World Champion, stated she is refining her movements and entry effects, aiming to stay mentally composed and compete in every dive.
*   **Wrestling:**
    *   **Performance Context:** In the April 2026 Asian Championships, the Chinese wrestling team won 5 golds, 1 silver, and 8 bronzes. The women's team won 5 golds, 1 silver, and 4 bronzes across all 10 weight classes, securing first place in the team total.
    *   **Preparation:** The team conducted mock opposition training with coaches acting as referees to help athletes handle judgments and emotions. Asian Games wrestling competitions begin on September 30.
    *   **Athlete Quote:** Long Jia noted that recent training intensity exceeded limits, providing a solid foundation for adaptability.
*   **Artistic Swimming:**
    *   **Performance Context:** The team will present a new routine titled *Time* in the collective free choice event.
    *   **Schedule:** Competitions are scheduled for September 26–28 at Hamamatsu, Shizuoka Prefecture.
    *   **Head Coach Quote:** Zhang Xiaohuan stated the team is refining difficult movements, preventing injuries, and converting high-intensity training into targeted competition simulations.
*   **Climbing:**
    *   **Recent Result:** At the September 13 Climbing World Cup Guiyang Station, the Chinese team won 3 golds, 3 silvers, and 1 bronze.
    *   **Athlete:** Deng Lijuan won the women's speed individual gold with a time of 6.14 seconds. She is a silver medalist from the Paris Olympics in this event and is aiming to overcome Indonesian rival Deshaake, the Asian record holder.
*   **Golf:**
    *   **Schedule:** Golf competitions are scheduled for September 30 to October 3.
    *   **Team Composition:** The Chinese team consists of 3 male and 3 female athletes competing in all 4 sub-events (individual and team).
    *   **Preparation:** The Chinese Golf Association conducted pre-competition course trials in July to gather performance data. Athlete Yin Ruoning won the FM Championship at the end of August.
*   **Women's Basketball:**
    *   **Matchup:** On September 19, the Chinese women's team played Thailand in their first group stage match, aiming for a third consecutive Asian Games title.
    *   **Context:** The team recently advanced to the quarterfinals of the 2026 FIBA Women's World Cup. Head Coach Gong Luming noted that the World Cup experience improved team unity.

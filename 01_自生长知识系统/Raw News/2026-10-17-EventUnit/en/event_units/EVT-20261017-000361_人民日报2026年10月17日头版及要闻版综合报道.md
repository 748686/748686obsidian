# Event Name

**People's Daily Comprehensive Reporting: 46th World Food Day, Eighth China International Import Expo (CIIE) Milestones, and National Development Initiatives (October 17, 2026)**

## Event Overview

On October 17, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive front-page and要闻版 (Important News) report covering multiple national and international themes. The reporting centered on the 46th World Food Day (observed October 16) and its associated National Food Security Awareness Week, the outcomes of the 8th China International Import Expo (CIIE), diplomatic engagements, domestic policy implementation, and significant scientific and technological developments in China.

The event represents a coordinated media narrative highlighting China's achievements in food security, agricultural modernization, digital economy integration, foreign relations, and scientific innovation during the opening year of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period.

## Core Facts

### 1. Food Security and Whole Grain Action Plan
- **World Food Day Context**: October 16, 2026, marked the 46th World Food Day, with the National Food Security Awareness Week launching simultaneously under the theme "Implement the Whole Grain Action Plan: Promote Food Saving, Loss Reduction, and Nutritional Health."
- **Main Venue**: The main venue event was held at the University of Science and Technology of China in Hefei, Anhui Province.
- **Policy Framework**: The *National Whole Grain Action Plan (2024–2035)* was released in December 2024.
- **Implementation Results** (as reported by Han Jizhi, Director of the Safety Storage and Technology Department, National Food and Strategic Reserves Administration):
  - Public awareness campaigns: 2,643 themed activities and 1,164 science lectures conducted.
  - Standards system: 18 national/industry standards, 29 local standards, 200 group standards, and 179 enterprise standards established.
  - Research: 435 basic research projects and 657 applied research projects initiated; 70 local innovation platforms built; 495 innovative products developed.
  - Industry capacity: 926 enterprises with whole grain processing capabilities; 29 industrial parks/clusters; 103 regional public brands.
- **Technological Innovations**:
  - Heilongjiang Academy of Agricultural Sciences developed high-protein corn variety "Qianyu 568" with protein content接近12% (3-4 percentage points higher than ordinary corn).
  - Central Reserve Grain Heyuan Direct Depot upgraded nitrogen gas regulation pest control technology using "dual-work nitrogen production" for循环制氮、循环降氧.
  - National Academy of Grain Storage and Technology (Changping R&D Center) developed processing technologies including physical crushing, bio-enzyme preparation, and enzyme inactivation to improve whole grain product taste and shelf life.

### 2. Digital Economy and Real Economy Integration
- **Xi Jinping's Inspection**: On October 16, 2026, General Secretary Xi Jinping inspected Ulanqab City, Inner Mongolia, emphasizing "promoting deep integration of the digital economy and real economy."
- **Case Studies**:
  - Ulanqab: Iron alloy industry transformed from "傻大黑粗" to "高智绿新"; potato and oat industries achieved increased附加值 through full-chain digitalization.
  - Ningbo, Zhejiang: "5G + industrial internet" applied to mold and home appliance industries.
  - Liuzhou, Guangxi: AI and digital twin technologies used to renovate automobile production lines.

### 3. Eighth China International Import Expo (CIIE)
- **Record Numbers**: The 8th CIIE achieved record-high numbers of "first launches, first exhibitions, and first shows" (首发首展首秀品数量创新高).

### 4. Diplomatic Activities
- **Mu Hong Meeting Turkish Delegation**: On October 16, 2026, Mu Hong (Vice Chairman of the CPPCC National Committee) met in Beijing with a senior cadre delegation from Turkey's Justice and Development Party (AK Party), led by Vice Chairman Ileri. Discussions focused on implementing consensus between heads of state, strengthening party-to-party interactions, and promoting China-Turkey strategic partnership.
- **Wang Yi's Diplomatic Engagements**: Foreign Minister Wang Yi met with Palestinian Foreign Minister Mustafa al-Barghouthi and Israeli President Isaac Herzog.
- **Foreign Minister Visits**: Djibouti Foreign Minister Omar and Congo-Brazzaville Foreign Minister Bonda announced visits to China.
- **China-Belgium Business Dialogue**: The 2nd China-Belgium Business Dialogue was held in Brussels on October 15, 2026, with nearly 120 representatives from government, chambers of commerce, financial institutions, and enterprises. Theme: "55 Years of Partnership, Co-creating China-Belgium Future." Focus areas: digital connectivity, supply chain cooperation, green innovation, and corporate localization.

### 5. Poverty Alleviation and International Cooperation
- **International Day for Eradication of Poverty**: October 17 is the International Day for Eradication of Poverty.
- **Global Context**: A UNDP-Oxford University report indicated 1.2 billion people live in severe multidimensional poverty.
- **China's Contributions** (per spokesperson Mao Ning):
  - Historically eliminated absolute poverty, achieving the UN 2030 Sustainable Development Goals poverty target 10 years early.
  - Promoted poverty reduction as the first of eight key areas of the Global Development Initiative.
  - Provided technical assistance through菌草技术 (cordyceps grass technology) and hybrid rice to Global South countries.
  - Sent thousands of experts for technical aid.
  - Founded the Global Poverty Reduction and Development Partnership Alliance.

### 6. Southern South Cooperation for Global Food Security
- **FAO Perspective**: Carlos Aldaco (FAO Representative to China) stated China's whole grain action plan provides an innovative path for reducing food loss, improving nutrition, and ensuring food security.
- **WFP Rural Development Excellence Center**: Established in China in 2016, the center has spent 10 years promoting experience exchange, technical cooperation, and knowledge sharing with developing countries.
- **Case Study - Côte d'Ivoire and Guinea**:
  - Introduced smallholder-adapted grain storage facilities and promoted local equipment production.
  - Connected rice production with school meal procurement systems.
  - Results: Post-harvest loss rate decreased from ~30% to 15%; farmer income increased by ~30%.

### 7. Domestic Development Stories
- **Huining County, Gansu**: High-standard farmland construction (105.53万亩 cumulative);机械化率 exceeding 68%; 2,000-acre potato yield improvement demonstration area; water-fertilizer integration systems saving 1/3 water usage.
- **Dangchang County, Gansu**: Red tourism integration with ecological resources; 12.8567 million tourists in Q1-Q3 2026; over 8 billion RMB cultural tourism revenue; 33,000+ residents benefiting from tourism employment.
- **Traffic Sector Role Models**: Five outstanding representatives from transportation sectors shared experiences at the State Council Information Office press conference on October 16, 2026, themed "Advancing the 15th Five-Year Plan,勇当开路先锋."
  - Sang Zhu (Tibet highway maintenance): 30+ years at 4,500m+ altitude; Tibet highway mileage now 125,200 km (110,000 km class roads).
  - Yin Wenxue (Gansu freight driver): 10+ years helping villagers; founded "Yin Wenxue Party Member Pioneer Fleet."
  - Hu Jian (Shanghai Salvage Bureau): 26 years experience; rescued "Longqing 1" vessel with 3,000+ tons isooctane in 2020.
  - Hu Hang (Three Gorges Navigation): Modernized船闸工业控制系统; 2025 throughput exceeded 170 million tons (historical high).
  - He Xiaoman (World Skills Competition): Won Albert Vidal Award at 48th WorldSkills Competition.

### 8. Ecological Conservation
- **Northeast Tiger and Leopard National Park**: A juvenile Chinese water deer (獐, National Class II protected) was rescued and is being cared for at Tianqiaoling Management Center. A new professional wildlife rescue center in Wangqing County is nearing completion, expected to serve all 10 management centers.
- **Xinyi City, Henan**: Geological workers resolved groundwater contamination in Zhengjiazhuang Village through "repair instead of seal, intercept pollution and protect clarity" approach. 126 abandoned coal mines investigated; 21 high-risk mines treated. Clean water restored to two village wells by 2022.

### 9. Administrative Appointments
- **Appointments**: Liu Shuang as Vice Minister of Veterans Affairs; Hao Liping (female) as Deputy Director of China Meteorological Administration.
- **Removals**: Wang Gang (Vice Minister of Transport); Xu Yao (Vice Minister of Veterans Affairs); Bi Baogui (Deputy Director of CMA); Wang Yunpeng (President of Beihang University).

### 10. Science and Technology Achievements
- **Plant Genome Initiative**: China-led "Plant Planet" initiative launched in February 2026, involving 40+ research institutions from 15 countries. Goal: reconstruct the "Tree of Life for Land Plants" by sequencing genomes of 450,000 land plant species (currently >99% lack high-quality reference genomes).
- **AI Traffic Systems**: Beijing piloting AI-powered traffic signal systems showing 21% speed increase and 19% congestion reduction in pilot intersections. 7,900 of 12,000 city signals networked; 100% coverage in 5th Ring Road and sub-center.
- **Nuclear Reactor Materials**: China Institute of Atomic Energy achieved breakthrough in nickel-based alloy volatile behavior research under high-temperature ultra-high vacuum conditions.
- **Cosmology**: Huazhong University of Science and Technology team discovered explanation for disappearance of "red dots" in early universe; published in *Nature Astronomy*.
- **Nuclear Power + Ecology**: Hainan Changjiang Nuclear Power Plant enabling white pearl (澳白珍珠) cultivation using waste heat water at optimal 25°C temperature.
- **Quantum Materials**: USTC team achieved atomic-scale spin imaging in interlayer magnetic materials; published in *Nature*.

### 11. Sports and Industry
- **2026 China Outdoor Sports Industry Conference**: Held in Beijing on October 16, 2026. Theme: "Beijing Outdoor, Sci-Tech Future." Report showed 500 million+ participants; industry scale ~3.17 trillion RMB.北斗 (Beidou) outdoor sports event management system launched.
- **Global People Magazine Annual Ceremony**: Held in Bazhong, Sichuan on October 16, 2026, commemorating the 90th anniversary of the Long March victory.

### 12. Mutual Aid Elderly Care
- **Policy**: 11 departments issued opinions on promoting mutual aid elderly care services, targeting 70% coverage of community elderly care facilities with mutual aid functions by 2030.
- **Nanjing Model**: Time-banking platform with 95,000 volunteers, 310,000 service recipients, 1.5 million service orders, 943,000+ hours stored.
- **Guangyuan Chao Tian District**: "Blueberry silver-haired workshop" providing elderly employment; "knock-on-door" volunteer program pairing 1,500+ party members with solitary elderly.
- **Zhangye Ganzhou District**: "Pastoral mutual aid elderly care" model with shared gardens and paired assistance.

### 13. Elderly Care Pricing Policy
- **Three Departments Directive**: NDRC, Ministry of Civil Affairs, and National Health Commission issued guidance on improving price formation mechanisms for inclusive elderly care services.
- **Institution Classification**:
  - 兜底保障型 (Safety-net): Government-guided pricing; free/subsidized for impoverished/disabled elderly.
  - 普惠支持型 (Inclusive support): Government-guided or reference price intervals.
  - 完全市场型 (Fully market): Market-regulated pricing.
- **Community/Home Care**: <10 beds = market pricing; ≥10 beds = institution management. Home care = market pricing.

### 14. Bohai Sea Communications
- **Bohai Communications Sensing Network**: Officially opened October 15, 2026.
- **Infrastructure**: 48 5G base stations, 8 VHF stations, 6 AIS stations, 4 hydrological observation stations.
- **Coverage**: 95% comprehensive signal coverage; >80% 5G coverage; full coverage at major ports, anchorages, and waterways.
- **Benefits**: Expected to serve 300,000 vessel calls and 5 million passenger trips annually.

### 15. CCUS Project
- **First Million-Ton Low-Concentration CCUS**: Construction began October 16, 2026, at Shengli Oilfield. Expected operational by end of 2028.
- **Technology**: First use of green electricity direct-supply carbon capture system; 98.8 MW wind power capacity; 217 million kWh annual green electricity generation.

### 16. Floating Air Vehicle
- **Sichuan Rongxian Test**: October 13-16, 2026, a domestically developed all-weather floating air vehicle completed first flight series tests. Diameter 21m, length 24m, integrating communication relay, navigation enhancement, and meteorological detection.

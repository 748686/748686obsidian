# Event Name
Mount Baegak Opening

## Event Overview
On October 17, 2026, the Mount Baegak trail in Seoul, South Korea, officially opened to the public for the first time after a restoration period. The trail, which had been closed since a rockfall incident in 2019, was restored through a two-year project aimed at stabilizing the cliff and clearing overgrown vegetation. The opening is part of broader efforts by the Seoul Metropolitan Government to revitalize urban green spaces and promote hiking and outdoor activities among residents.

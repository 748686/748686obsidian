## Event Overview

On October 25, 2026, the *People's Daily* published a comprehensive series of articles centered on the commemoration of the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army's Long March. The coverage spans political directives, military mobilization, educational initiatives, cultural publications, international diplomacy, and provincial development narratives. The core theme links the historical Long March spirit to contemporary tasks, including military modernization, youth education, global governance reform, and regional high-quality development.

The event is situated within a broader political cycle that includes the upcoming Third Plenum of the 20th Central Committee (scheduled for October 26–29, 2026) and the celebration of the 105th anniversary of the Founding of the Communist Party of China (July 1, 2026).

## Core Facts

**1. Leadership and Diplomatic Activities**
*   Li Xi, member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and head of the Central Inspection Work Leading Group, presided over the mobilization meeting for the eighth round of inspections by the 20th Central Committee on October 24, focusing on the Ministry of Education and centrally-administered universities. (Source: Article #383)
*   Wang Huning, member of the Political Bureau Standing Committee, accompanied North Korean leader Kim Jong Un in a visit to Dandong. (Source: Article #382/#383 metadata)
*   Xi Jinping delivered a speech on October 22 at the commemoration conference for the 90th anniversary of the Long March victory, emphasizing the "Great Long March Spirit" and outlining five requirements for the new era. (Source: Article #384, #388)

**2. Military Response and Mobilization**
*   Following Xi’s speech, the PLA and armed police forces engaged in study sessions. Key themes included building a world-class military, adhering to the Party's absolute leadership, and achieving the centennial goal of building the military by 2027. (Source: Article #384)
*   Specific units highlighted include the "Red First Company" (Hong Yilian) of the 83rd Group Army, which conducted root-seeking activities in the Riganqiao Wetlands and Huili. (Source: Article #407)
*   The submarine force named vessels "Long March" to symbolize penetrating deep seas. (Source: Article #384)

**3. Educational and Youth Initiatives**
*   An article titled "Understanding the 'Monument': A Vivid Lesson for Youth" detailed how Long March stories (e.g., "The Monument," Chen Shuxiang's sacrifice, the "Half a Quilt" story) are integrated into textbooks to inspire students. (Source: Article #388)
*   The Third New Era Youth "Retracing the Long March" activity was launched in Zunyi, Guizhou, on October 24. (Source: #408)
*   Xi Jinping’s past interactions with students in Rucheng County, Hunan, regarding the "Monument" story were cited. (Source: Article #388)

**4. Cultural and Media Outputs**
*   Starting October 24, the China International Television Corporation released commemorative audio-visual publications, including:
    *   60-episode TV drama *The Great Long March*.
    *   TV gala *Ode to the Long March*.
    *   7-episode documentary *The Great Expedition*.
    *   12-episode documentary *Wan Shui Qian Shan*. (Source: Article #392)
*   The archive collection *Selected Archives of the Red Army Long March* was also published. (Source: Article #385/400)

**5. Global Governance and Diplomacy**
*   The 2026 Global Governance Forum was held in Xiong'an, Hebei, on October 23–25. (Source: Article #390)
*   Xi Jinping sent a letter to the forum. The Global Governance Initiative, proposed in September 2025, has been supported by over 160 countries and international organizations. (Source: Article #390)
*   China commemorated the 55th anniversary of its restoration of lawful seats in the United Nations (October 25, 1971). (Source: Article #391)
*   China announced 8 practical actions, including hosting the APEC Leaders' Meeting and strengthening the "Friends of Global Governance" group. (Source: Article #390)

**6. Provincial Development (Guizhou)**
*   A feature article highlighted Guizhou’s high-quality development as a "new Long March." Key metrics cited for January–August 2026 include:
    *   Industrial value-added growth of 6.4%.
    *   Data center computing power exceeding 176 EFLOPS (as of August 2026), with over 98% AI computing share. (Source: Article #411)
*   Guizhou’s "Village Super League" (Cun Chao) had attracted nearly 40 million tourists and generated over 41.8 billion RMB in comprehensive income. (Source: Article #411)

**7. Personnel Changes**
*   Wang Zhijun appointed Party Secretary of Hunan Province; Shen Xiaoming relieved. (Source: Article #399)
*   Mo Gaoyi appointed Party Secretary of Yunnan Province; Wang Ning relieved. (Source: Article #399)
*   Tang Dengjie elected Party Secretary of Shanxi Province. (Source: Article #400)
*   Chen Xiaojing elected Party Secretary of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region. (Source: Article #401)

### [China and Switzerland Upgrade Free Trade Agreement](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/15/content_30181037.html) ⭐️ ?/10

China and Switzerland concluded the upgrade negotiations for their free trade agreement, signing a memorandum of understanding that represents the first upgraded FTA China has reached with a top-20 global economy and an EU-adjacent state. The upgrade, which began in September 2024 and spanned five rounds of consultations, establishes that over 99% of goods from both countries will be imported at zero tariffs. The new framework extends high-level, two-way liberalization to services and investment, and establishes detailed cooperation rules in digital trade, artificial intelligence, sustainable development, supply chains, pharmaceuticals, mechanical industries, and watchmaking. The agreement incorporates advanced institutional rules covering competition policy, intellectual property, government procurement, and labor standards to provide a transparent and predictable operating environment for businesses in both nations.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 15, 00:26

**「Background Context」** The original bilateral free trade agreement was signed in 2013, marking a pioneering move for Switzerland at a time when Western partners were generally cautious about establishing FTAs with China. This initial agreement significantly boosted bilateral commerce, elevating China to the position of Switzerland&\#x27;s third-largest trading partner.

**「Significance」** The upgraded agreement serves as a structural "pressure release valve" for Swiss firms facing sustained trade policy pressure from the United States, while demonstrating to broader European economies that mutual economic integration with China remains a viable strategic alternative to confrontation. The new institutional framework provides a stable, predictable regulatory environment for Chinese and Swiss enterprises that actively operate in or plan to enter the respective markets.

### [Hebei Province Boosts Land Security for 747 Key Projects](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/15/content_30181038.html) ⭐️ ?/10

As of August 2026, the Hebei Department of Natural Resources approved 120,400 mu of construction land, ensuring 93.98% security for the province&\#x27;s 747 key projects. The province achieved this by activating inter-departmental coordination and simplifying approval workflows, including a linked review system for land, forest, and sea use. Specific policy measures were introduced to compress project implementation timelines, such as reducing correction deadlines to ten working days. This coordinated administrative effort supports major infrastructure developments, including the Qinshen Expressway and the Dongguang wind power project.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 15, 00:26

**「Background」** In China&\#x27;s administrative framework, land is a strictly regulated element required for all economic and social development. The Hebei department is integrating fragmented approval processes for land, forest, grassland, and sea resources to reduce bureaucratic bottlenecks.

**「Impact」** Developers and infrastructure enterprises in Hebei benefit from a 4.34-day average approval turnaround, which significantly accelerates the pre-construction phases of major transportation and energy projects. This improved administrative efficiency reduces the time lag between project planning and physical construction.

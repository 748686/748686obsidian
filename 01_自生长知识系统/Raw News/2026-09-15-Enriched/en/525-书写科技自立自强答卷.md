The article outlines the &\#x27;three words&\#x27; \(three key actions\) framework: constant attention to daily work \(抓常\), focusing on details \(抓细\), and ensuring long-term sustainability \(抓长\). Citing data from July 2026, the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection noted that 27,000+ violations of the Central Eight-Point Regulations were handled nationwide, marking the 155th consecutive month of such reports. rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 15, 00:26

**「Background」** The Central Eight-Point Regulations \(中央八项规定\) are a set of anti-corruption and work-style directives introduced by the Chinese Communist Party in 2012, aimed at curbing extravagance and formalism. It serves as the cornerstone of the party&\#x27;s ongoing &\#x27;style-building&\#x27; \(作风建设\) campaign.

**「Impact」** For Chinese government officials and local cadres, the article serves as a formal directive reinforcing that adherence to anti-corruption standards and anti-formalism measures remains an ongoing, high-priority mandate rather than a one-time initiative.

### [Cultural essay outlines the evolving role of Chinese folk songs](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/15/content_30181056.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A cultural essay published in People&\#x27;s Daily argues that Chinese folk songs, or &\#x27;minge&\#x27;, remain vital and are not inherently obsolete despite rapid social changes. The author highlights recent viral successes on social media, such as the Northwest &\#x27;Hua&\#x27;er&\#x27; song &\#x27;Galian Shou&\#x27; and the Jingpo ethnic &\#x27;Munao Song&\#x27;, which have garnered hundreds of millions of plays and demonstrated the renewed appeal of traditional music. The essay uses the twenty-year resurgence of Mongolian long songs on the Xilin Gol grassland as a case study, noting that younger generations are increasingly adopting and adapting the art form in both traditional settings and modern urban contexts. It cautions that successful innovation requires a deep understanding of a song&\#x27;s historical, geographic, and social roots, warning against superficially appropriating traditional musical symbols without grasping their underlying cultural context.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 15, 00:26

**「Background」** Chinese folk songs are diverse traditional musical forms that are primarily transmitted through oral tradition and vary significantly by region and ethnic group. As China undergoes rapid urbanization and modernization, many of the everyday and ceremonial environments that historically supported these songs have diminished, leading to a loss of their traditional living context. However, contemporary media, educational institutions, and modern performance stages have created new avenues for their preservation and widespread dissemination.

**「Impact」** For contemporary musicians, cultural practitioners, and the public, the essay advocates that authentic creative transformation of folk music requires deep historical and cultural research to preserve the original traditions and prevent the loss of foundational knowledge. It highlights the importance of maintaining the practice of listening to traditional music within its specific geographic and lifestyle context to avoid reducing complex cultural heritage to superficial pop adaptations.

党的作风就是党的形象，关系人心向背，关系党的生死存亡。“坚持推进作风建设常态化长效化”是习近平党建思想的内涵要义之一，是我们党历经千锤百炼而永葆生机活力的经验总结。

新时代以来，党中央从制定实施中央八项规定开局破题，党风政风焕然一新，社风民风持续向好。作风建设永远在路上，永远没有休止符，一刻也不能松懈。习近平总书记深刻指出：“关键是要在抓常、抓细、抓长上下功夫。”

抓常，就是经常抓、见常态。

前不久，中央纪委国家监委公布了2026年7月全国查处违反中央八项规定精神问题汇总情况。当月，全国共查处违反中央八项规定精神问题2.7万余起，批评教育和处理3.4万余人，这是连续第155个月公布月报数据。

习近平总书记曾引用“快活三里”的故事。“快活三里”是泰山半腰的一段平路，游客喜欢在此歇脚，挑山工却不敢停留太久，怕腿“发懒”，难上“十八盘”。作风建设如同登山，人到半山路更陡。紧要关头，精神一旦懈怠，思想一旦放松，“四风”问题就可能卷土重来。

风气养成重在日常教化，作风建设贵在常抓不懈。把作风建设时刻摆上位置、有机融入日常工作，做到管事就管人，管人就管思想、管作风，才能让崇廉拒腐的价值理念逐渐成为深入人心的思想共识，让优良党风化风成俗。

抓细，就是深入抓、见实招。

中央八项规定实施后第二天，习近平总书记到原第二炮兵机关视察，特意叮嘱不要封路，坐的车和社会车辆一起走；党的十八大后首次离京考察，习近平总书记下部队视察，到水兵餐厅，拿着餐盘同战士们一起排队打饭菜，一张桌子吃饭。

后来，提起这两次考察，习近平总书记语重心长：“虽然在我们看来这是小事，但战士们会认为这是大事，甚至是他们终生难忘的事情。作风好不好就体现在这些细节当中，广大官兵就是通过这样一点一滴的事情观察我们。”

抓细体现认真，认真才能具体，具体才能深入。

细节之所以重要，就是因为其连着民心、系着党性。重温习近平同志在《之江新语》中写下的话，启示更加深刻：“小事小节是一面镜子，能够反映人品，反映作风。小事小节中有党性，有原则，有人格。”

抓长，就是持久抓、见长效。

“我们抓八项规定、抓作风建设，为什么要扭住不放？就是要落到地，巩固下来，长效化，那么成绩就很了不起，才有集中精力办一些新的事的前提条件。”

久久为功，靠的是向制度建设要长效。党的十九大、党的二十大后召开的第一次中央政治局会议，都列有审议《中共中央政治局贯彻落实中央八项规定实施细则》的议程。

绳锯可断木，滴水能穿石。2025年国家统计局调查显示，95.5%的受访群众对中央八项规定精神贯彻成效表示肯定，92.2%的受访群众对党风政风转变带动社会风气好转的效果表示满意。这是人民群众给我们党投下的信任票。

中央八项规定是“长期有效的铁规矩、硬杠杠”。新征程上，保持打攻坚战、持久战的决心和恒心，锲而不舍落实中央八项规定精神，推进作风建设常态化长效化，我们定能以优良作风凝心聚力、真抓实干，不断开创事业发展新局面。

### [Inner Mongolia&\#x27;s &\#x27;Couple&\#x27;s Police Station&\#x27; Marks Fourth Year on Frontier](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/17/content_30181577.html) ⭐️ ?/10

People&\#x27;s Daily reports that the &\#x27;couple&\#x27;s police station&\#x27; \(夫妻警务室\) established in February 2022 on the Alxa Left Banner frontier of Inner Mongolia has now operated for four years. Officers Xu Naichao and his wife Li Wenna, a police officer and auxiliary police worker, jointly patrol a 3,145-square-kilometer area 15 kilometers from the China-Mongolia border. The station has seen significant infrastructure upgrades, including piped water, 4G network coverage, a paved road, and improved insulation, replacing the initial reliance on solar panels and water collected from over 10 kilometers away. Modern tools such as smart surveillance, drones, and mobile police terminals have transformed their border security operations. In recognition of their service, the couple has received multiple awards between 2022 and 2025, including the May Fourth Medal and the &\#x27;Most Beautiful Family&\#x27; honor.

**「Background」** The &\#x27;frontier police station&\#x27; \(抵边警务室\) model is a Chinese public security initiative where small police offices are deployed directly into remote border areas to enhance local surveillance and control. This station was created under the Alxa Frontier Management Detachment to strengthen patrols in a vast, isolated desert and Gobi region near the Mongolia border.

**「Impact」** The integration of modern technology and infrastructure upgrades into isolated border postings demonstrates how China is modernizing its remote frontier management systems, improving operational capabilities and living conditions for officers deployed in extreme environments.

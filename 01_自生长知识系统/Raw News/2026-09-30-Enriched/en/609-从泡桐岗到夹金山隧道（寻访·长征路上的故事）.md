### [AI Agents&\#x27; Willingness to Avoid Killing Animals in HarvestBench](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2dg5F9iWunz2k2oZb/will-ai-agents-pay-to-avoid-killing-animals-1) ⭐️ ?/10

HarvestBench is a new benchmark from Compassion Aligned Machine Learning \(CaML\) that tests whether LLM agents will pay a cost to avoid harming animals. In the "Harvest Rush" game, agents driving tractors must choose between driving over obstacles or swerving at a fuel cost. Nine models were tested, with hit rates varying dramatically from 0.4% to 98.8% when moral instruction was added.

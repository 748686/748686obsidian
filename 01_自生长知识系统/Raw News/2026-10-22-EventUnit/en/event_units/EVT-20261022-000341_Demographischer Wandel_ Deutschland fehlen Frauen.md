## Core Facts

*   **Comparative Scale:** This gender gap exceeds the total current population of Germany, implying a systemic demographic surplus of women in Russia that is not mirrored in Germany.
*   **Migration Statistics:** In the year 2000, approximately 300,000 women migrated from Russia to Germany.

*   **Outcome Assessment:** The migration policy from 1990–1993 successfully reduced the overall population decline in Germany but failed to achieve the intended demographic balance or reverse the low birth rate.

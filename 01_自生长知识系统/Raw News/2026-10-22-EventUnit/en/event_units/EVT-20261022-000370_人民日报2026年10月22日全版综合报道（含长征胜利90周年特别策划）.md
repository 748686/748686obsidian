# Event: People's Daily Comprehensive Report on October 22, 2026 (90th Anniversary of the Long March Victory)

## Event Overview
On October 22, 2026, *People's Daily* published a comprehensive special report commemorating the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March. The coverage spanned from the front page to the supplementary section, including a theoretical editorial, diplomatic news, policy documents, economic analysis, and cultural features. The central theme linked historical reflection on the Long March with current national strategies, including the start of the "15th Five-Year Plan," technological innovation, and diplomatic initiatives.

## Core Facts

### 1. Commemoration of the Long March Victory (90th Anniversary)
*   **Central Theme:** The editorial "Work Together to Walk the New Long March Well" (Pages 1 & 3) emphasized that the Long March represents a "great turning point" from defeat to victory and serves as a spiritual beacon for the new era.
*   **Key Historical References:**
    *   The Red Army crossed nearly 100 rivers and climbed over 40 high mountain passes.
    *   Fought more than 600 battles.
    *   Specific events highlighted include the Battle of Xiang River, the Zunyi Conference (the "turning point"), crossing the Snowy Mountains and Grasslands, and the Three Forces' Convergence in the Northwest.
    *   Stories featured: Chen Shuxiang's "severing intestines to show loyalty," the "half a quilt" story in Rucheng, Hunan, and the "half a belt" story in the Grasslands.
*   **Cultural Events:**
    *   **TV Gala:** "Ode to the Long March" (《长征颂歌》) premiered on October 22, 2026, at 8:00 PM on CCTV, featuring five chapters: "Road of Bloody Battle," "Road of Turning Point," "Road of Connecting Hearts," "Road of Faith," and "Road of Victory."
    *   **Commemorative Coins:** The People's Bank of China issued a set of gold and silver commemorative coins on October 22, 2026, marking the 90th anniversary. The gold coin (8g) features the Red Army crossing snow mountains and grasslands; the silver coin (30g) features the sculpture of the Red Army arriving in Northern Shaanxi and the Baota Mountain in Yan'an.
    *   **Exhibitions:** The "Great Expedition" theme exhibition at the Military Museum of the Chinese People's Revolution was widely visited.

### 2. Diplomatic Activities
*   **China-Russia Relations:** Premier Li Qiang co-chaired the 31st Regular Meeting of China-Russia Prime Ministerial Cooperation with Russian Premier Mikhail Mishustin in St. Petersburg on October 21, 2026.
    *   Both sides stated that the comprehensive strategic partnership of coordination between China and Russia has reached a historic high level.
    *   Agreed to deepen cooperation in trade, energy, aerospace, AI, and the Arctic.
    *   Russia reaffirmed its adherence to the One-China principle.
    *   Signed the Joint Communique of the 31st Regular Meeting of China-Russia Prime Ministerial Cooperation.
*   **China-Djibouti Relations:** Foreign Minister Wang Yi met with Djibouti's Foreign Minister Omar in Beijing on October 21, 2026.
    *   Issued a joint press communique on maintaining peace and stability in the Horn of Africa and safety of navigation in the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden.
    *   Both sides supported the "two-state solution" for Palestine and called for de-escalation in the Middle East.
    *   Opposed any attempt to recognize "Somaliland" as an independent entity.
*   **Other Diplomatic Engagements:**
    *   Wang Yi will attend the opening ceremony of the 2026 Global Governance Forum (Xiong'an) on October 23, 2026.
    *   Zhang Qingwei attended the opening ceremony of the 2026 China International Friendly City Conference in Xi'an, Shaanxi.
    *   Liu Haixing (Minister of Foreign Affairs of the CPC Central Committee) met with a delegation from the Rwandan Patriotic Front led by Secretary-General Bazigagara.

### 3. Domestic Policy and Economic Indicators
*   **Sci-Tech Innovation Policy:** Issued the "Opinions of the CPC Central Committee General Office and the State Council General Office on Promoting the Deep Integration of Scientific and Technological Innovation and Industrial Innovation." Key points include:
    *   Building a innovation system led by enterprises.
    *   Breakthroughs in key core technologies (IC, industrial母机, advanced materials).
    *   Development of future industries: quantum tech, biomanufacturing, hydrogen/nuclear fusion, brain-computer interfaces, embodied intelligence, and 6G.
    *   Implementation of the "AI+" action plan.
*   **Economic Data (Q3 2026):**
    *   GDP exceeded 107 trillion RMB, with a year-on-year growth of 4.6%.
    *   Total import and export of goods reached 39.94 trillion RMB, up 18.8% year-on-year (fastest growth in nearly 5 years).
    *   Equipment manufacturing and high-tech manufacturing growth accelerated.
    *   New energy vehicle (NEV) sales accounted for 50.8% of total auto sales.
*   **Climate Change Report:** The "2026 Report on China's Policies and Actions on Climate Change" was released.
    *   CO2 emissions per unit of GDP dropped 17.7% compared to 2020.
    *   Non-fossil energy consumption accounted for 21.7% of total energy consumption.
    *   Wind and solar power installed capacity totaled 1.84 billion kilowatts.
    *   Carbon emission trading market covers ~8.3 billion tons of CO2 emissions.
*   **Emergency Management:** The "15th Five-Year Plan" for modern emergency system construction was introduced. Goals include building a comprehensive rescue force system by 2030 and improving earthquake monitoring and early warning capabilities.

### 4. Social and Cultural Features
*   **Rural Revitalization Cases:**
    *   **Yongping Town, Yanchuan County, Shaanxi:** Transformed from abandoned land to mulberry plantations and greenhouses, boosting sericulture and vegetable industries.
    *   **Qingshi Primary School, Guyuan, Ningxia:** Created "Red Classrooms" to teach Long March history, including the Battle of Qingshizui.
    *   **Wuqi County, Shaanxi:** Developed apple industry on loess plateaus, leveraging climate and terrain for economic growth.
    *   **Guangzha County, Qinghai:** Guoluo Zanggongma Village achieved ethnic unity and poverty alleviation through pastoral settlement, photovoltaic dividends, and tourism.

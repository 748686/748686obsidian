## Core Facts

### 1. Domestic Economic and Green Shipping Policy
- **Green Shipping Action Plan**: The Ministry of Transport and two other departments jointly issued the *"Action Plan for the Promotion and Application of Inland New Energy Ships (2026–2030)"* (《内河新能源船舶推广应用行动方案（2026—2030年）》).
  - **Targets**: By 2028, the number of inland new energy ships will double compared to 2025 levels; by 2030, large-scale application of new energy ships will be initially achieved.
  - **Technical Focus**: Priority is given to electric and methanol-powered ships. Electric ships for short-distance (<300 km) and light-load (<3,000 tons) scenarios; methanol power for long-distance and heavy freight.
  - **Infrastructure**: Development of a "Zero-Carbon Water Transportation Channel" network ("Two Horizontals, One Vertical, Two Networks"), covering the Yangtze River Mainstream, Xijiang Waterway, Beijing-Hangzhou Grand Canal, and the Yangtze and Pearl River Delta water networks.
  - **Current Status**: As of 2026, China has over 1,200 inland new energy/clean energy ships, leading globally in scale and application, though they represent less than 2% of total inland vessels.
  - **Key Projects**: The "Jianglong 1605" (first methanol-diesel dual-fuel hazardous chemical demo ship) made its maiden voyage in Fuling, Chongqing; the "Gezhouba" (world’s first inland 10,000-ton pure electric bulk carrier) was launched in Yichang, Hubei; the "Chuangxin 19" (first single-methanol fuel江海直达 ship) operates between Ningbo Zhoushan Port and Yangtze ports.
  - **Expert Commentary**: Liu Xin (Director, Transport Planning and Research Institute) and Feng Yue (Deputy Director, Waterborne Transport Research Institute) emphasize the shift from demonstration to scaled application and the need for full-chain support (fuel production, manufacturing, port facilities, standards).

- **Q3 Economic Indicators**: Reports indicate that China’s consumption market expanded steadily in the first three quarters of 2026, and the China-Europe Railway Express exceeded 140,000 trains operated. - **Meeting Details**: Held from October 26–29, 2026, in Beijing.
- **Key Outcomes**:

  - Systematically deployed the continuous promotion of comprehensive strict Party governance.
  - Reviewed and adopted the *"Decision of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China on Persisting in Comprehensively Advancing Strict Governance of the Party"* (《中共中央关于持之以恒推进全面从严治党若干重大问题的决定》).
- **International Reaction**: Experts from Jordan, Latin America, Egypt, Spain, Kenya, Pakistan, and Rwanda praised the Party’s leadership, anti-corruption efforts, and contribution to global governance models. They highlighted the link between Party governance and improved public services, social fairness, and people’s livelihoods.

### 4. International Cultural and Ecological Cooperation
- **Youth Olympic Games (YOG) in Senegal**: The 4th Youth Olympic Games opened on October 31, 2026, in Dakar, marking the first time the YOG flame lit up the African continent.
  - **Wushu Debut**: Wushu made its debut as an official sport in the Youth Olympics and the Olympic family, symbolizing China-Africa cultural exchange.
  - **China-Senegal Relations**: Senegal is the first West African country to sign a Belt and Road cooperation document. Key projects include the Senghor Stadium, Museum of Black Civilizations, and National大剧院 in Dakar. China dispatched 21 medical teams since 1971, treating over 3.5 million patients. The PLA Navy hospital ship *吉祥方舟* (Auspicious Ark) provided medical services to over 6,000 patients in its first visit to Senegal.
- **China-Latin America Dialogue**: The 9th China-Latin America Civilization Dialogue Forum was held in Mexico City.
  - Participants: Over 100 officials, scholars, and media representatives from 12 countries and 2 international organizations.
  - Themes: Governance experience exchange, digital cooperation, education.
  - Outcome: Release of the second batch of joint research topics under the "Global Civilization Dialogue Academic Partner Network" and publication of the book *"Five Major Projects: A New Paradigm for China-Latin America Cooperation."*
- **China-Africa Media Cooperation**: At the 2026 African Television Festival in Cape Town (opened October 28), the "China Joint Exhibition Stand" showcased 50+ high-quality audio-visual programs and technologies from 16 Chinese institutions. Focus on co-production, distribution, technology sharing, and talent training.
- **Ecological Recognition**: China’s Blue Bay Restoration Action (蓝湾行动), led by the Ministry of Natural Resources and the Ministry of Finance, was selected as a 2026 UN "Flagship Project for Ecosystem Restoration."
  - Significance: This is the second time a national ecological restoration project from China has received this recognition (after the Mountains-Rivers-Forests-Farmland-Lakes-GRASSLANDS-Sandification Governance Project in 2022). China is the first country to have two national-scale actions honored as flagship projects.

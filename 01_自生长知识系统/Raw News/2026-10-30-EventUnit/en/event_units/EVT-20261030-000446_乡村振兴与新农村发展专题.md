### 2. Party Building in New Economic Organizations
Article #564 highlights the integration of Party building and business operations in China's new economic sector.
*   **Private Economy Data:** As of May 31, 2025, there were 185 million private economic organizations in China, accounting for 96.76% of all market entities. From January to August 2025, 15.502 million new market entities were established.
*   **Case Study: Cixing Cross-Border E-Commerce (Zhejiang):** The Haishu District Cross-Border E-Commerce Industry Party Committee was established in May 2025. It operates a "monthly meeting, quarterly consultation, annual summary" mechanism. A specific case involved helping a small enterprise pivot its product line to electric gardening tools, leading to a million-yuan order from a Japanese client in May 2026. The incubation class covered over 30 enterprises with a 60% order success rate.
*   **Case Study: Chuan Shen Yu Lian (Hubei):** The company developed "Rendu," China's first "root original" large model, through a "Li Ren Large Model Pioneer Team" composed of 9 Party members out of 13. *   **Case Study: Chongqing Rili Technology (Chongqing):** The company uses a "Three Aggregations Working Method" to integrate Party building with X-ray microscopy R&D. Their equipment achieves 500-nanometer detection precision. Party members accounted for over 70% of core management and key technical roles, contributing to 10 million yuan in cost savings/benefits over three years.

### 3. Yongzhou Railway Construction (Zhejiang)
Article #565 reports on the progress of the Yongzhou Railway, a major infrastructure project.
*   **Status:** As of late October 2026, with the placement of the first steel box girder at the Taoyao Gate Bridge, all bridges on the line have entered the girder erection construction phase.
*   **Specifications:** The railway runs from Ningbo East Station to Zhoushan Main Island, with a total length of 76.4 km.
*   **Three "World's Firsts":**
    1.  **Xihoumen Bridge:** Main span of 1,488 meters, the world's largest span for a road-rail两用 bridge.
    2.  **Taoyao Gate Bridge:** Main span of 666 meters, the world's largest span for a road-rail hybrid beam cable-stayed bridge.
    3.  **Fuchi Gate Bridge:** Main span of 388 meters, the world's largest span for a high-low tower road-rail same-level cable-stayed bridge.
*   **Engineering Innovation:** To withstand typhoons and strong winds, the bridges use a "same-level" design (rail and road on the same deck) rather than a double-deck design. The Fuchi Gate Bridge features a "beam passing through tower" design with a width of 56.5 meters, the widest cantilever steel box girder in the world.
*   **Equipment:** The "Iron Construction Bridge Crane 2" (a fully rotating crane ship) with a 1,300-ton lifting capacity was used for precise installation of the 467.2-ton steel box girders.
*   **Impact:** Upon completion, Ningbo to Zhoushan travel time will be reduced to 26 minutes, and Hangzhou to Zhoushan to approximately 1.5 hours, completing Zhejiang's "city-to-city high-speed rail" network.

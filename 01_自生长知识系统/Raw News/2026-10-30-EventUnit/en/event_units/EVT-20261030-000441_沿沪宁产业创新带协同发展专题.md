# EVT-20261030-000441: Collaborative Development of the Shanghai-Nanjing Industrial Innovation Belt

## Event Overview

On October 30, 2026, *People's Daily* (Page 11) published a special thematic report titled "Collaborative Innovation Strengthens the Shanghai-Nanjing Industrial Innovation Belt" (协同创新做强沿沪宁产业创新带). The report focuses on the institutional mechanisms facilitating cross-regional technology transfer and resource allocation between Shanghai and cities along the industrial innovation belt in Jiangsu Province. A key highlight is the emergence of "Science and Technology Matchmakers" (科技红娘), officially known as technology managers, who act as intermediaries to match Shanghai’s technological capabilities with the industrial application scenarios found in along-belt cities.

## Core Facts

**1. Institutional Mechanism: The "Science and Technology Matchmaker"**
*   **Definition:** The term "Science and Technology Matchmaker" refers to technology managers working within the framework of the Shanghai-Nanjing collaborative innovation initiative.
*   **Function:** Their primary role is to bridge the gap where Shanghai possesses technology but lacks application scenarios, while cities along the Shanghai-Nanjing belt have industrial demand but lack technology.
*   **Location:** The Shanghai-Nanjing Collaborative Innovation Center is located on the 42nd floor of Xintai Center in Putuo District, Shanghai, near the G2 and G42 highway entrances. Officials from cities such as Suzhou and Nantong work in this open-space hub.
*   **Key Figure:** Pu Zhengyu (浦征宇), a staff member at the Shanghai Chemical Industry Research Center and a seconded cadre at the Putuo District Regional Collaborative Development Office, serves as a practicing example of this role.

**2. Case Studies of Successful Matching**
*   **Carbon-13 Isotope:** Previously facing difficulties in promoting a domestically substituted isotope developed at the Shanghai Chemical Industry Research Center, the technology now benefits from the collaboration platform. It has achieved a cycle of R&D in Shanghai Putuo District, production in Anhui, and application expanding from the Yangtze River Delta to the whole country.
*   **Automotive Parts:** The Shanghai Electrical Apparatus Research Institute reached a technology transfer cooperation意向 (intent) valued at over 8 million RMB with an automotive parts enterprise in Jiangsu.
*   **Marine Technology:** Shanghai Haihuan Technology Co., Ltd. is cooperating with a special equipment company in Jiangyin City, Wuxi, on the R&D and business expansion of hull cleaning robots.
*   **Power Engineering:** The East China Electric Power Design Institute and Nanjing Tech University are jointly developing an integrated "wind-solar-thermal-storage-transmission" project, seeking further对接 (docking/matching) with enterprises and institutes along the belt for photovoltaic power generation.

**3. Organizational Structure in Putuo District**
*   **Innovation Consortiums:** Putuo District is promoting the construction of innovation consortiums involving enterprises, universities, and research institutes along the Shanghai-Nanjing belt.
*   **Scale:** The first batch of 10 consortiums covers frontier sectors including biomedicine, new energy, and new materials. The technology manager team covers universities, institutes, parks, and key enterprises along the route.
*   **Digital Platform:** Putuo District launched the "Shanghai-Nanjing Collaborative Innovation Market" (沿沪宁协同创新集市). This platform integrates sci-tech policies, carrier spaces, and technology demands from cities along the belt, enabling resource sharing and supply-demand matching across regional boundaries.

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives

The report highlights a specific **Shanghai-Jiangsu inter-provincial perspective**:
*   **Shanghai's Position:** Positioned as the source of technological innovation and R&D capacity but constrained by a lack of local application scenarios and space for industrialization.
*   **Jiangsu Belt Cities' Position:** Positioned as having strong industrial bases and clear market demands (e.g., auto parts, special equipment, photovoltaics) but lacking access to cutting-edge technologies or the direct channels to Shanghai research institutions.
*   **Synergy:** The "matchmaker" mechanism is presented as a solution to this asymmetry, facilitating a division of labor where R&D remains in Shanghai (e.g., Putuo), production moves to neighboring provinces (e.g., Anhui, though the belt primarily focuses on Jiangsu cities like Suzhou and Nantong in the office description), and applications spread nationally.

## Event Conclusion

The October 30, 2026, *People's Daily* report confirms the operationalization of the "Science and Technology Matchmaker" (technology manager) mechanism within the Shanghai-Nanjing Industrial Innovation Belt. Based in Putuo District, Shanghai, this initiative directly addresses the mismatch between Shanghai's R&D output and the industrial application needs of Jiangsu cities. Early results include specific high-value technology transfers and the successful market entry of previously stalled technologies like the Carbon-13 isotope. The establishment of the "Shanghai-Nanjing Collaborative Innovation Market" digital platform represents a structural innovation aimed at permanent resource integration across the region.

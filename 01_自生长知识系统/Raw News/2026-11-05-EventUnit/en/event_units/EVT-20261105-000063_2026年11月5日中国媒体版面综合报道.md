### Judicial and Legal Affairs

8. **People's Assessor System** (Article #356)
   - As of June 2026, China has 340,000 people's assessors, a 61.9% increase since the People's Assessor Law took effect in 2018.
   - Over 50% are grassroots cadres, farmers, community workers, and flexible employment personnel.
   - 37,000 ethnic minority assessors.
   - 124 Hong Kong/Macau and 180 Taiwan compatriot assessors.
   - 831 active-duty military personnel newly selected as assessors in 2026.
   - Since 2018, assessors have participated in concluding 15.5 million cases.
   - 33,000 assessors with professional knowledge participated in 2.16 million cases involving intellectual property, ecology, finance, maritime, and foreign-related disputes.
   - Appeal rates and retrial application rates for cases with assessors were 2.19 and 1.24 percentage points lower, respectively, than cases without assessors since 2018.

9. **Intellectual Property Protection** (Article #356)
   - From January 2021 to September 2026, procuratorates handled 134,000 intellectual property cases:
     - Criminal prosecution: 118,000 cases
     - Civil prosecution: 7,514 cases
     - Administrative prosecution: 5,393 cases
     - Public interest litigation: 2,864 cases
   - 873 persons prosecuted for trade secret infringement and patent counterfeiting.
   - 24,000 persons prosecuted for data security crimes involving AI and e-commerce.
   - 80,200 persons prosecuted for trademark-related crimes.
   - 1,386抗诉 and retrial prosecution recommendations made for malicious civil litigation.

### Cultural & Aesthetic Analysis (Per Jing Xiaoyong)
- **Core Appeal:** The drama succeeds because it aligns with the contemporary Chinese desire to tell their own stories proactively and calmly, touching on cultural confidence and national identity.
- **Aesthetic Identity:** Represented by "Only This Green" (*Zhi Ci Qing Lü*), symbolizing a highly summarized cultural aesthetic that is inclusive, restrained, and characterized by "Chinese romance" and Eastern aesthetic implications.
- **Cross-Cultural Reception:**

  - **Music:** Musical elements have achieved broader resonance internationally than thematic narratives. Plans are underway to collaborate with foreign symphony orchestras to perform the score.

### Production Details
- **Collaborators:** Co-produced by the Palace Museum (Forbidden City), China Eastern Performing Arts Group, and People.com.cn.
- **Artifact Integration:** The original 2019 premiere did not feature the actual *A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains* (*Qian Li Jiang Shan Tu*) scroll. - **Physical Space:** The "Qing Lü Space" (*Qing Lü Kong Jian*) was created using the group’s storefront rooms, integrating the theme of *A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains* into daily life.

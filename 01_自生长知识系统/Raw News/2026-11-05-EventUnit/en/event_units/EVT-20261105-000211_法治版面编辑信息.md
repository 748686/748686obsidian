3.  **Published Report Content (Section 18):**
    *   The article titled *"Responding to Private Enterprise Concerns with the Rule of Law"* (以法治回应民营企业关切) was published on **November 5, 2026**, on **Page 18**.
    *   The report author is identified as **Shi Yu** (施 钰).
    *   The report details the implementation of the *"Quanzhou Municipality Ordinance on Inheriting, Carrying Forward, and Innovatively Developing the 'Jinjiang Experience'"* (《泉州市传承弘扬创新发展“晋江经验”条例》), hereinafter referred to as the "Ordinance."
    *   The Ordinance was approved by the Standing Committee of the 14th Fujian Provincial People's Congress on **May 26, 2026**, and came into effect on **August 20, 2026**.
    *   The Ordinance consists of **7 chapters and 56 articles**.

4.  **Legislative Context:**
    *   The "Jinjiang Experience," summarized by Xi Jinping during his tenure in Fujian, includes "Six Always Adherences" and "Correctly Handling Five Major Relationships."
    *   In March 2026, "Innovatively Developing the Jinjiang Experience" was incorporated into the National "15th Five-Year Plan."
    *   Quanzhou's private economy contributes approximately 70% of tax revenue, 80% of GDP, 90% of technological innovation results, 90% of urban employment, and 90% of enterprise count.

5.  **Specific Legal Provisions and Outcomes Reported:**
    *   **Financial Services:** The Ordinance codifies "no-principal-repayment renewal loans" (无还本续贷). Quanzhou Bank reported processing 61,600 such loans totaling 18.1 billion RMB since 2013, saving enterprises 1.059 billion RMB in financing costs.
    *   **Land Use:** The Ordinance supports the conversion of inefficient industrial land to commercial use ("industry-to-commerce" policy). A specific case in Shishi City involved a company paying over 8 million RMB to convert industrial land for hotel development.
    *   **Talent Recognition:** The Ordinance allows private enterprises to autonomously evaluate and recognize high-level talents, consistent with the *Fujian Provincial Regulation on Promoting the Private Economy*.
    *   **Intellectual Property:** Article 49 establishes a quick collaborative protection mechanism for intellectual property, linking administrative and judicial protection.

Captain Samual Shaw noted that their status as citizens of an independent nation was recognized by all.

    *   **Jian Zhen:** Successfully crossed to Japan for the sixth time after five failures and losing his sight.
    *   **Japanese Envoys to Tang:** Used a strategy of three ships sailing together to ensure at least one reached Chang'an.
    *   **Yang Liangyao:** A Tang Dynasty envoy who sailed from Guangzhou to Ababilas (core area in modern Iraq).
5.  **Production Methodology:**
    *   Unlike *How We Became China* (《何以中国》), which had limited raw materials, *Guangji Canghai* had abundant historical data serving as a "huge historical treasury."
    *   The production used animation to recreate historical scenes.
    *   Over 80% of the visuals are based on direct historical materials.
    *   Animation keyframes were hand-drawn by artists referencing historical data and expert verification.
6.  **Archival Discovery:** The production team located the stele inscription *Stele of the Sacred Way of Master Yang of the Tang Dynasty* (《唐故杨府君神道之碑》) in Jingyang County, Shaanxi, near the end of filming. This inscription contains the poetic description of Yang Liangyao’s voyage: "At night, the divine lamp indicates the way; by day, immortal beasts lead the drive," referring to lighthouse lights and dolphins.

## Event Overview
This event synthesizes reports regarding the five-year touring history and cultural IP commercialization of the dance poetry drama *The Journey of a Legendary Landscape Painting* (Chinese: 《只此青绿》; also referred to as "Zhi Ci Qing Lv"). The drama, which reinterprets the famous Chinese painting *A Panorama of Rivers and Mountains* (《千里江山图》), has been widely recognized for its aesthetic success and innovative approach to intangible cultural heritage. It has conducted multiple tours across China and internationally, engaging diverse audiences through both stage performances and media adaptations.

## Core Facts
- **Subject**: Dance poetry drama *The Journey of a Legendary Landscape Painting* (《只此青绿》).
- **Timeline**: A five-year period of touring and operation (2021–2026).
- **Touring Scope**: The drama has performed extensively across China and internationally.
- **IP Operations**: The production has been noted for its successful cultural IP commercialization and brand extension strategies.
- **Key Figure**: Zhang Guangqian (张国立) is cited as a commentator/observer of the phenomenon.

## Cross-Source Verification
- **Fact**: Both source articles confirm that *The Journey of a Legendary Landscape Painting* has undergone significant touring and IP development over a five-year period.

**Source 2 (People’s Daily)**

## Different Country / Regional Perspectives
- **Domestic (China)**: Both sources frame the drama as a pinnacle of “cultural confidence” and a model for integrating traditional culture with modern technology and market mechanisms. It is viewed as successfully making classical art accessible to young, urban audiences (“Guochao” trend).
- **International**: The drama’s debut at the National Theatre in London is cited as a significant milestone, indicating its reception as a high-level cultural export and its ability to communicate Chinese aesthetics to global audiences.

The dance poetry drama *The Journey of a Legendary Landscape Painting* represents a significant success in the contemporary Chinese cultural sector.

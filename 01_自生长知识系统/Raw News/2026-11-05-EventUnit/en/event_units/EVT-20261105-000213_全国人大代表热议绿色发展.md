## Core Facts

Based on Article #414 (Source: *paper.people.com.cn*, Date: 2026-11-05):

*   **Subject:** The article reports on how Chongqing municipalities are innovating channels for public opinion expression in lawmaking, implementing the concept of "Whole-Process People's Democracy."
*   **Key Mechanism:** Utilization of "Grassroots Legislative Contact Points" (基层立法联系点) established by the Legislative Affairs Work Committee of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.
*   **Case Study 1: Social Assistance Law**
    *   Residents of Zhongxinwan Community, Shijiping Street, Shapingba District, participated in drafting consultations for the *Social Assistance Law*.
    *   A resident, Wang Fulan, held the newly enacted law and expressed surprise that community suggestions became legal clauses.
    *   Legislative Information Officer Huang Xuelli collected suggestions during a session at the community's "Yishiyuan" (Council Courtyard).
    *   Specific suggestion adopted: When reducing or terminating social assistance, authorities must not only provide written notice but also **explicitly state the reasons**, better protecting citizens' rights to material assistance.
*   **Case Study 2: Chongqing Youth Entrepreneurship Promotion Regulations**
    *   Located in Panlong Town, Rongchang District (at the Sichuan-Chongqing border).
    *   Consultation held for the draft regulations in the first half of 2026.
    *   Zhu Wanming (Panlong Town Information Collector) suggested classifying "innovation-driven entrepreneurship" and "employment-oriented entrepreneurship" with distinct policy focuses.
    *   A returning youth from Jieshi Town, Longchang City, Sichuan, suggested adding local特色 industries (such as automobile manufacturing) and guiding youth toward smart connected vehicles, biomedicine, and green/low-carbon industries.
    *   Result: 2 suggestions were adopted by higher-level people's congresses.
*   **Infrastructure & Tools:**
    *   Shapingba District has created over **220** legislative information collection stations at residents' doorsteps.
    *   Development of the **"Shaci Minyiting" (沙磁民议厅)** mini-program for 24-hour legislative consultation.
    *   Integration with "Bayu Practice Stations" for Whole-Process People's Democracy.
*   **Other Models Mentioned:**
    *   Dadukou District: "Shajie" Grassroots Legislative Contact Point focusing on minor protection.
    *   Shizhu Tujia Autonomous County: "135" working model for targeted opinion collection.
    *   Tanghe Town, Jiangjin District: "1+8+N" linkage mechanism with a 500-person think tank.

**3. Artistic Reflection: “Let Chinese Aesthetics Take Root in Life”**
*   **Author:** Huang Yingxiang (黄颖湘), director of the TV drama《Lan Xiang Ru Gu》(《兰香如故》).
*   **Core Thesis:** Chinese aesthetics in contemporary television should not be limited to visual reproduction of traditional elements but must understand their spiritual connotations and transform them into contemporary narrative language and emotional expression.
*   **Visual Construction:** The production team conducted field research in traditional gardens in Nanjing and Suzhou, Jiangsu Province. They focused on cultural concepts and narrative possibilities rather than just architectural forms.
*   **Symbolism:**
    *   **Balustrades:** Used as a medium connecting humans and nature; inside represents unavoidable reality, outside represents a broader world.
    *   **Life Texture:** Juxtaposition of servants’ mottled walls and tea-room steam against exquisite courtyards to reflect character status and circumstances.
    *   **Plum Trees:** Symbolize resilience; branches may be broken by wind and snow, but life grows upward, reflecting human vitality in困境.
*   **Character & Emotion:**
    *   The drama avoids simple善恶 (good/evil) divisions, presenting complex human nature shaped by fate, environment, and personality.
    *   A specific scene involving the separation of the protagonist Xu Lanxiang and her adoptive parents drew on the director’s personal experience of parting from her own parents while studying abroad.
    *   The theme emphasizes “living, and living better,” finding strength in small daily joys (meals, scenery, affection) rather than only heroic acts.
*   **Philosophical Conclusion:** “Love given returns; fortune offered returns” (Ai chu zhe ai fan, fu wang zhe fu lai). Traditional aesthetics’ contemporary value lies in understanding human emotion and life through formal beauty.

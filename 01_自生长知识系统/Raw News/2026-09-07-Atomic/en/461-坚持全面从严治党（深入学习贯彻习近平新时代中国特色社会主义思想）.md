### [China advocates inclusive AI governance at 2026 World AI Conference](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/07/content_30179438.html) ⭐️ ?/10

On September 7, 2026, People&\#x27;s Daily published a special commentary by Zhang Xiao, deputy director of the China Internet Network Information Center, outlining China&\#x27;s approach to global artificial intelligence governance. The article highlights President Xi Jinping&\#x27;s address at the 2026 World AI Conference, which called for AI to become a driving force for common prosperity and security while building a fair global governance system. China has established the World AI Cooperation Organization as the first intergovernmental AI organization, launched capacity-building programs for developing nations, and pledged 5,000 training slots over five years to bridge the digital divide. The piece emphasizes multilateral cooperation, open technology sharing, and inclusive development as alternatives to technological exclusion and hegemony.

**「Background」** Global AI governance has become a critical challenge as artificial intelligence rapidly evolves faster than regulatory frameworks can adapt. **「Impact」** China&\#x27;s establishment of the World AI Cooperation Organization and capacity-building initiatives provides developing countries with institutional pathways to participate equally in AI governance and benefit from technological advancement.

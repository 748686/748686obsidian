### [Competence Requires Maintenance Under AI Offloading](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Sj5x4we53LigxFsxp/competence-has-a-maintenance-requirement) ⭐️

rss · LessWrong AI风险社区 · Sep 6, 23:22

**「Background」** Organizations increasingly rely on AI automation to reduce cognitive load, creating an assumption that humans can seamlessly reclaim control when needed. This assumes competence is static, ignoring that offloaded skills silently erode without deliberate upkeep.

**「Solution」** The author builds on Lisanne Bainbridge’s 1983 ‘Ironies of Automation,’ which identified how automation can degrade human operator situational awareness and manual skills. The essay argues that cognitive offloading to AI generates similar silent organizational exposure: as AI handles more tasks, human operators lose the practiced competence required to intervene during failures. However, the piece offers no concrete mechanisms, empirical evidence, or implementation guidance beyond restating this premise.

**「Takeaway」** Competence gained through direct human involvement has a maintenance requirement; without deliberate practice, offloading to AI creates hidden fragility in systems where human oversight remains essential.

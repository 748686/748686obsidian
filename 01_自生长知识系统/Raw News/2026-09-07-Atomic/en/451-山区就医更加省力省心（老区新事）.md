On September 6, 2026, the first direct Fuxing high-speed train, G3998, departed Ruijin in Jiangxi for Yan&\#x27;an in Shaanxi, completing the 1,800-plus-kilometer journey in 11 hours 32 minutes. The service connects the Central Red Army&\#x27;s Long March departure point with its final destination, routing through 13 stops across seven stitched railway lines including the Gan-Ruilong, Beijing-Kunming, and Xi&\#x27;an-Yan&\#x27;an high-speed corridors. By enabling same-day travel between the two historically significant cities, the route provides a dedicated transportation backbone for red-themed educational trips, tourism cooperation, and coordinated development of the revolutionary base areas.

**「Background」** The Central Red Army began its Long March from Ruijin in 1934 and reached Yan&\#x27;an in northern Shaanxi after more than a year of movement. Today, those routes are linked by China&\#x27;s expanding high-speed rail network, which often combines multiple regional lines to serve cities that previously required longer, indirect journeys.

It also strengthens practical links for educators, tour operators, and local governments coordinating red tourism and regional development along the former Long March corridor.

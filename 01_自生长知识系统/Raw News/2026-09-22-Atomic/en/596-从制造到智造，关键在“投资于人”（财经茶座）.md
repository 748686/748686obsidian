### [China achieves second lunar laser communication breakthrough](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/21/content_30182147.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A Chinese scientific team successfully completed a two-way high-speed laser communication trial between Earth and the Moon, becoming the second country globally to master deep-space laser communication technology. The experiment, led by researchers at the Chinese Academy of Sciences&\#x27; Space Application Engineering and Technology Center, achieved a downlink rate of 100 Mbps and uplink rate of 1.25 Mbps over approximately 400,000 kilometers, enabling an uncompressed 8K lunar panoramic photo to transfer in just 12 seconds compared to 4-5 minutes via traditional microwave. The trial overcame three major technical hurdles: precise beam aiming at ultra-long distances, detecting extremely weak signals that attenuate to just a few photons, and processing high-speed data. This breakthrough marks China&\#x27;s transition from near-Earth orbit laser communications into lunar space, supporting future manned lunar missions and deep-space exploration.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 21, 00:26

**「Background」** China&\#x27;s deep space exploration has long relied on microwave communication for data transmission, but this technology suffers from limited bandwidth—a critical bottleneck for returning high-resolution images and scientific data from lunar and deep-space missions. Laser communication offers significantly higher data rates with better security and smaller, lighter equipment, but requires solving extreme precision targeting and signal detection challenges across hundreds of thousands of kilometers. The Chinese team conducted over two years of in-orbit testing after the payload was delivered in December 2023, weathering setbacks including satellite solar panel damage and persistent failure through late 2025 before achieving success.

**「Impact」** This achievement positions China among the first tier of nations capable of interplanetary laser communication, directly supporting planned crewed lunar landings and lunar research station construction by providing a reliable high-bandwidth data relay. Future developments aim to increase communication rates to match international leading standards and enable daytime operation in complex weather conditions, ultimately building a complete Earth-Moon information network rather than isolated point-to-point links.

### [Chinese researchers synthesize mesoporous carbons and win Future Science Prize](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202609/21/content_30182148.html) ⭐️ ?/10

A research team led by Chinese Academy of Sciences academician Zhao Dongyuan has been awarded the 2026 Future Science Prize for Physical Science for three decades of work on mesoporous materials. By developing a "stepwise" method—first self-assembling then polymerizing organic precursors—the team successfully synthesized mesoporous polymers and, through carbonization, mesoporous carbons. These materials, with pore sizes between 2 and 50 nanometers, offer high specific surface areas and ordered channels that accommodate large molecules. Practical applications include using mesoporous carbon as a silicon-host framework to mitigate volume expansion in battery electrodes, and mesoporous–zeolite composite catalysts co-developed with Sinopec that have reached kiloton-scale production for upgrading heavy oil and residuum into high-grade fuels.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Sep 21, 00:26

**「Background」** Porous materials have been studied for over a century; zeolite molecular sieves and activated carbon are long-standing examples. Mesoporous materials fill the gap between microporous solids \(pores under 2 nanometers\) and macropores, enabling transport and conversion of larger molecules in catalysis, energy storage, and adsorption.

**「Impact」** The stepwise organic–organic self-assembly pathway expanded mesoporous materials beyond inorganics into polymers and carbons, enabling commercial catalyst production and improved lithium-ion battery silicon anodes. Remaining limitations include lack of atomic-scale precision in current mesoporous synthesis and the need for biocompatible, functional variants in biological systems.

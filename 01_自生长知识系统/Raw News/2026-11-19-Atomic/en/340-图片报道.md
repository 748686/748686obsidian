### [Commemoration delegation visits Guangdong for Sun Yat-sen anniversary](http://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202611/19/content_30192026.html) ⭐️ ?/10

The guest delegation for the 160th anniversary of Sun Yat-sen&\#x27;s birth conducted commemorative activities in Guangdong on November 17 and 18, 2026. Delegates, including overseas relatives and representatives from Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and overseas Chinese, visited the Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall in Guangzhou and the Sun Yat-sen Former Residence Memorial in Zhongshan. During visits to both sites, they observed minute-long silence, presented flower baskets, and bowed three times before Sun Yat-sen&\#x27;s statue. The Guangdong leg followed earlier events in Beijing, Nanjing, and Shanghai, marking the final stop of the commemorative tour.

rss · 人民日报\(People&\#x27;s Daily\) · Nov 19, 00:26

**「Background」** Sun Yat-sen \(1866–1925\) was a Chinese revolutionary and political leader who played a leading role in overthrowing the Qing dynasty and establishing the Republic of China. He is honored in mainland China as a founding father of modern China, and his birth anniversaries are marked by official commemorative activities across sites associated with his life and revolutionary work.

**「Impact」** The visit reinforces state-led commemoration of Sun Yat-sen in his native Guangdong province as part of nationally coordinated anniversary events. It serves to highlight Guangdong&\#x27;s role as Sun Yat-sen&\#x27;s hometown and a key base for his revolutionary activities.

## Event Overview
On October 1, 2026, two distinct but related government initiatives in South Korea were identified as part of a broader technology and AI development strategy. The first involves a collaborative supply initiative between the government and private sector to deploy 200,000 robots by 2030. The second involves the Ministry of Science and ICT drafting a comprehensive National AI Safety Ecosystem Plan, with a deadline set for December 2026.

## Core Facts
*   **Robot Supply Initiative:** The South Korean Prime Minister announced that the government and private sector will jointly supply 200,000 robots through the year 2030 (Source: Article #280).
*   **AI Safety Planning:** The Science Ministry (Ministry of Science and ICT) intends to draw up a National AI Safety Ecosystem Plan (Source: Article #295).
*   **Timeline:** The AI Safety Ecosystem Plan is scheduled to be completed by December 2026 (Source: Article #295).
*   **Scope:** The initiatives cover both physical robotics deployment and digital/AI safety infrastructure (Synthesis of Articles #280 and #295).

# Event Name

The 77th Anniversary of the Founding of the People's Republic of China: Official Ceremonies, Diplomatic Engagements, and Concurrent National Development Events (October 1, 2026)

## Event Overview

On October 1, 2026, the People's Republic of China celebrated the 77th anniversary of its founding. The official observance included two primary solemn ceremonies held on September 30—the Martyrs' Day wreath-laying ceremony and the National Day Reception—followed by a comprehensive series of domestic policy announcements, diplomatic exchanges, and cultural events spanning sports, international forums, and industrial development. The celebrations were framed by the dual historical milestones of the 105th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China and the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March, while simultaneously marking the opening of the "15th Five-Year Plan" period.

## Core Facts

**Martyrs' Day and National Day Ceremonies (September 30, 2026)**

*   **Martyrs' Day Wreath-Laying Ceremony:** Held at 10:00 AM at the Monument to the People's Heroes in Tiananmen Square, Beijing. Top national leaders, including Xi Jinping, Li Qiang, Zhao Leji, Wang Huning, Cai Qi, Ding Xuexiang, Li Xi, and Han Zheng, attended the ceremony alongside representatives from various sectors. The event featured the playing of the "Martyrs' Day Bugle Call," the national anthem, a moment of silence, and the laying of nine large flower baskets. Xi Jinping adjusted the red silk ribbon on the central flower basket inscribed with "Eternal Glory to the Heroes of the People." The ceremony was presided over by Yin Li, Municipal Party Secretary of Beijing.
*   **National Day Reception:** Held at 5:30 PM at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing. Xi Jinping delivered a speech celebrating 77 years of the PRC. Li Qiang presided over the reception. Approximately 800 Chinese and foreign guests attended. The speech highlighted the "two miracles" of rapid economic development and long-term social stability, the completion of the first centenary goal (moderately prosperous society), and the pursuit of the second centenary goal (modern socialist powerhouse). Xi emphasized the importance of the "15th Five-Year Plan," the "One Country, Two Systems" policy for Hong Kong and Macau, cross-strait reunification, and the implementation of the Four Global Initiatives (Global Development, Security, Civilization, and Common Security) to build a community with a shared future for mankind.
*   **Editorial Theme:** The official editorial in *People's Daily* framed the day as the beginning of the "15th Five-Year Plan" era, citing achievements in AI, human robots, new energy vehicles, quantum information ("Jiuzhang No. 4"), and aerospace (reusable rockets). It reaffirmed commitment to "New Quality Productive Forces" and the guidance of the "Two Establishments."

**Diplomatic Engagements**

*   **China–Brunei Relations:** On September 30, President Xi Jinping exchanged congratulatory messages with Hassanal Bolkiah, Sultan of Brunei, to commemorate the 35th anniversary of diplomatic relations. Xi highlighted the establishment of a China-Brunei community with a shared future during the Sultan's state visit the previous year.
*   **Foreign Expert Awards:** The 2026 Government Friendship Award ceremony was held at the Great Hall of the People on September 30. State Councilor Chen Yiqin presented awards to 50 foreign experts, recognizing their contributions to China's development and Sino-foreign cooperation during the opening year of the "15th Five-Year Plan."
*   **Sichuan University Correspondence:** President Xi Jinping issued a reply letter to faculty and students at Sichuan University on the occasion of the university's 130th anniversary, encouraging them to serve national strategies and act as pioneers in education, science, and talent development.

**International Forums and Multilateral Engagement**

*   **Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB):** The 11th Council of Governors was held in Doha, Qatar, on September 28–29, under the theme "Infrastructure for the Future: Impact and Innovation." AIIB President Zou Jiayi announced the bank's first project in South Africa and a "Special Fund" for energy and food crises. Governor Ali bin Ahmed Al Kuwari and Egypt's Ahmed Koushock emphasized the role of multilateral development banks as catalysts for sustainable infrastructure.
*   **Global South Modernization Forum:** Held in Cairo, Egypt, on September 30, the forum focused on "Artificial Intelligence: Opportunities and Challenges for Global South Modernization." Co-hosted by multiple Chinese and Egyptian institutions, it released the "Academic Research Atlas of Global South Modernization" and the "Top Ten Buzzwords of Global South Modernization Research 2026."
*   **UN Human Rights Council:** On September 30, Jia Guide, China's Permanent Representative to the UN in Geneva, delivered a joint statement on behalf of nearly 80 countries at the 63rd session of the Human Rights Council. The statement called for correcting international human rights governance based on the Global Governance Initiatives, advocating for people-centered governance, sovereign equality, justice, and international rule of law.
*   **Indonesia International Book Fair:** A Chinese publishing delegation comprising over 20 institutions, including Peking University Press and Tsinghua University Press, debuted at the 46th Indonesia International Book Fair in Jakarta on September 30, displaying over 1,000 titles and seeking copyright cooperation.

**Domestic Policy and Development Initiatives**

*   **"15th Five-Year Plan" Opening:** The publication of Xi Jinping's important article, "Strengthening Inclusive, Basic, and Safety-Net Livelihood Construction" (covering 2012–2026 excerpts), appeared in the 19th issue of *Qiushi* Journal. The article stressed balancing economic development with livelihood security, focusing on education, employment, social security, and healthcare.
*   **Water Network Construction:** At a September 30 State Council Information Office press conference, Minister of Water Resources Li Guoying outlined "15th Five-Year Plan" goals, including achieving 82% national water network coverage by 2030, adding 15 billion cubic meters of reservoir capacity, and raising important dike compliance to 91%. YTD water conservancy investment reached 795 billion RMB.
*   **Sports Policy:** The General Office of the State Council issued opinions on developing sports events to stimulate consumption, targeting a modern event system by 2030 with 17 key tasks spanning supply, integration, and marketization.
*   **Service Industry Growth:** A *People's Daily* commentary highlighted that the service sector contributed 66.1% to economic growth in the first half of 2026, with a target to reach 100 trillion RMB in total scale by 2030.
*   **Industry-Education Integration:** Four national industry-education integration consortia (industrial machine tools, grain, railway construction, new energy vehicles) were established on September 29, pledging 600 million RMB annually for joint tech R&D and talent training.
*   **Shanghai Leadership:** Zhu Zhongming was elected Mayor of Shanghai on September 30.

**Cultural and Commemorative Activities**

*   **Long March Spirit & Rural Revitalization:** A report from Huamao Village, Zunyi, Guizhou, illustrated the integration of Red culture (linked to the 1935 Zunyi Campaign and 2015 Xi inspection) with rural revitalization, noting village collective income exceeded 14.81 million RMB by end-2025.
*   **Martyrs' Spirit Commentary:** An editorial op-ed called for honoring martyrs through action and national strength, citing contemporary exemplars like Xu Zhenchao, Huang Danian, and Zhang Guimei.
*   **Liu Huaqing Commemoration:** The General Office of the CMC published a lengthy article commemorating the 110th anniversary of Liu Huaqing's birth, detailing his roles in naval modernization, defense industry development, and the "Near-Sea Defense" strategy.

**Sports Achievements (Nagoya Asian Games)**

*   **Overall Standings:** As of September 30, the Chinese delegation led with 151 gold, 67 silver, and 59 bronze medals.
*   **Diving:** The Chinese diving team won its 100th Asian Games gold medal (Lian Junjie in men's 10m platform) and swept all diving events for the 14th consecutive Games.
*   **Athletics:** China won 17 gold medals in athletics, its best overseas performance since 1998. Highlights included Chen Yujie's historic mixed 4x100m relay gold and Shi Shengji's race walking victory.

**Public Service and Education**

*   **Macau Patriotic Education:** A sharing session for the "Salute to the National Flag" Macau youth delegation was held on September 30, featuring reflections from students who visited Beijing.
*   **Public Service Ads:** The National Radio and Television Administration issued opinions on promoting high-quality public service advertising in broadcast and online media.

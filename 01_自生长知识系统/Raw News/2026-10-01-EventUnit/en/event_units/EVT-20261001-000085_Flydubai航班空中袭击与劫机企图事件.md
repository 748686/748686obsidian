# Event: Flydubai In-Flight Assault and Alleged Attempted Crash

## Event Overview
On or around October 1, 2026, an incident occurred aboard a Flydubai flight (Flight 1073) traveling from Tel Aviv toward Israel. During the flight, one pilot stabbed another pilot in the cockpit. Passengers intervened to subdue the attacker. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu stated that the attacker appeared to have attempted to crash the plane. Reports indicate the aircraft entered a significant dive, which passengers helped reverse. The plane subsequently landed safely, and the surviving travelers arrived at an Israeli airport in a shaken state.

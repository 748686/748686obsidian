# Event Unit: Meeting between Chinese Premier Li Qiang and Tajik President Rahmon

## Event Overview

On the morning of October 19, 2026, Chinese Premier Li Qiang met with Tajik President Emomali Rahmon in Dushanbe. The meeting was reported in *People's Daily* on October 20, 2026. Both leaders reaffirmed their commitment to the strategic partnership between China and Tajikistan, discussing political trust, economic cooperation, security collaboration, and multilateral engagement. Wu Zhenglong participated in the meeting.

## Core Facts

- **Date of Event**: October 19, 2026 (morning)
- **Location**: Dushanbe, Tajikistan
- **Participants**:
  - Li Qiang (Premier of China)
  - Emomali Rahmon (President of Tajikistan)
  - Wu Zhenglong (attendee)
- **Primary Topics Discussed**:
  - Construction of a China-Tajikistan community with a shared future
  - Bilateral political trust and support for core interests
  - Economic and trade expansion
  - Cooperation in infrastructure, green minerals, and connectivity
  - Collaboration in digital economy and artificial intelligence

  - Support for Tajikistan's hosting of the SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting
  - Multilateral coordination within the UN, Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO), and China-Central Asia mechanism

每天，我都会思考什么让人变得健康。我相信运动对我们的身体非常重要。当你在外面玩耍或和朋友们一起跑步时，你的身体会变得强壮而敏捷。运动和整天坐着看电视是不同的。它还能帮助你在晚上睡个好觉。一个健康的身体是美丽的，因为它能做多件事情。你可以走路、跳跃、爬树。在我看来，每个人每天都应该运动一点。强壮的身体意味着幸福的生活。所以，到外面去，玩耍，活动你的身体吧！

<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
语法知识点
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>一般现在时表观点</strong><br>用 I believe / I think 表达个人观点，后接 that 从句。<br><em>例：I believe exercise is very important for our body.</em><br><br><strong>When 时间状语从句</strong><br>When 引导时间条件，主句描述结果。<br><em>例：When you play outside, your body gets strong and fast.</em><br><br><strong>情态动词 can 表能力</strong><br>can 后接动词原形，表示能够做某事。<br><em>例：You can walk, jump, and climb trees.</em><br><br><strong>祈使句表号召</strong><br>句子以动词原形开头，发出建议或号召。<br><em>例：Go outside, play, and move your body!</em><br><br><strong>different from 搭配</strong><br>different 必须与 from 搭配，表示与某事物不同。<br><em>例：Exercise is different from just sitting and watching TV all day.</em>
</div>

<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
重点句型
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>I believe + clause</strong><br>表达明确观点<br><em>原文：I believe exercise is very important for our body.</em><br><br><strong>When + clause, main clause</strong><br>描述条件与结果<br><em>原文：When you play outside or run with your friends, your body gets strong and fast.</em><br><br><strong>A + noun + is + adj + because...</strong><br>陈述原因<br><em>原文：A healthy body is beautiful because it can do many things.</em><br><br><strong>everyone should + verb</strong><br>提出建议<br><em>原文：Everyone should exercise a little every single day.</em><br><br><strong>So, + imperative</strong><br>总结号召<br><em>原文：So, go outside, play, and move your body!</em>
</div>

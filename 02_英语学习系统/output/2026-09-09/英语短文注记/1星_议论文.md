# Why Healthy Living Matters

## English Article

Some people think that looking beautiful is the most important thing. I strongly disagree. I believe that being healthy is far more important than looking beautiful. Everyone is different, but we all need good sleep and daily exercise to stay strong. Exercise makes your body healthy and strong. Good sleep helps your brain think clearly. Without enough sleep, you cannot study well or work well. I think a healthy body and mind are more important than a beautiful face. We should exercise every day and sleep eight hours. These small habits make a big difference in our lives.

---

## 中文翻译

有些人认为看起来美丽是最重要的事情。我强烈不同意。我认为保持健康远比看起来美丽重要。每个人都是不同的，但我们都需要良好的睡眠和每日锻炼来保持强壮。锻炼让你的身体变得健康而强壮。良好的睡眠帮助你的大脑清晰地思考。没有足够的睡眠，你无法好好学习或工作。我认为健康的身体和心灵比一张美丽的脸更重要。我们应该每天锻炼并睡八个小时。这些小习惯在我们的生活中产生巨大的不同。

## 学习重点

**添加的词汇**  
word: disagree；meaning: 不同意<br>word: brain；meaning: 大脑<br>word: clearly；meaning: 清晰地<br>word: habits；meaning: 习惯

**重点短语**  
the most important thing<br>far more important than<br>to stay strong<br>think clearly<br>without enough sleep<br>make a big difference

**语法知识点**  
宾语从句：I believe that being healthy is...<br>比较级：more important than / far more important than<br>but 引导的并列转折句：Everyone is different, but we all need...<br>介词短语作条件：Without enough sleep, you cannot...<br>情态动词 should 表建议：We should exercise every day.

**知识结构**  
议论文结构：提出对方观点 → 反驳 → 阐述己方理由 → 总结建议<br>通过 beautiful vs healthy 的对比展开论证<br>用 but 转折引出核心论点<br>用 Without... 引导让步/条件句加强论证<br>结尾用 should 提出行动建议，收束全文

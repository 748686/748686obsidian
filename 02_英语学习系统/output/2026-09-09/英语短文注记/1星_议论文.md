<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
语法知识点
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>一般现在时表达观点</strong><br>用 I think / I believe + 陈述句 表达个人看法<br><em>例：I think exercise is very important for everyone.</em><br><br><strong>时间状语从句</strong><br>When 引导时间从句，主句描述结果<br><em>例：When we exercise, our body becomes strong and healthy.</em><br><br><strong>情态动词 should</strong><br>should 表示建议或义务，后接动词原形<br><em>例：I believe everyone should exercise because it makes our life much better.</em><br><br><strong>最高级</strong><br>the most + 形容词 表示三者及以上中的最高程度<br><em>例：A healthy body is the most important thing we can ever have.</em>
</div>

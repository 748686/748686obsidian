## 三、答案解析

- Question 1: The article states "I believe that being healthy is far more important than looking beautiful." So the answer is B (being healthy). The key words 'healthy' and 'important' directly point to option B.
- Question 2: The article says "We should exercise every day and sleep eight hours." The number is clearly eight, so the answer is C.
- Question 3: The article says "I think a healthy body and mind are more important than a beautiful face." The statement in the question reverses this, so it is False.
- Question 4: The sentence "Exercise makes your body healthy and strong" appears almost word-for-word in the article. The missing word is 'healthy'.
- Question 5: The article says "Everyone is different, but we all need good sleep and daily exercise." The missing word is 'different'.
- Question 6: Each word is defined in the target vocabulary list. Students match 'beautiful' to 美丽的, 'exercise' to 锻炼, 'important' to 重要的, 'sleep' to 睡眠, and 'strong' to 强壮的.
- Question 7: The article states "Good sleep helps your brain think clearly." The answer is the phrase 'think clearly'.
- Question 8: The article says "Without enough sleep, you cannot study well or work well." This matches option B exactly.

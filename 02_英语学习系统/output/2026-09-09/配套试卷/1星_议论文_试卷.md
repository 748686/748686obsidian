答案解析

单项选择

1. 根据文章'We are all different people with different ideas.'，答案为B。考查目标词different。
2. 根据文章'I think healthy habits are very important for everyone.'，答案为C。考查目标词important。
3. 根据文章'First, exercise makes our body strong.'，答案为D。考查目标词strong。
4. 根据文章'If we sleep well at night, we can learn better in school.'，答案为B。
5. 根据文章'a healthy life is the best gift for us'，答案为C。

多选题

1. 文章中提到'First, exercise makes our body strong. When we run or play sports outside, we feel good and happy.'，因此A和B正确。C和D是sleep的好处，不是exercise的好处。
2. 文章中提到'Let's exercise every day and go to sleep early.'以及'When we run or play sports outside, we feel good and happy.'，因此A、B、D正确。C与文章建议相反。
3. 文章原文包含'A'、'B'、'C'的内容。D选项与文章'go to sleep early'的建议相矛盾，故不选。

阅读理解

1. 文章标题为'Why Healthy Habits Are Important'，全文围绕健康习惯的重要性展开，故选B。
2. 文章明确指出'First, exercise makes our body strong.'，故选B。
3. 文章说'If we sleep well at night, we can learn better in school.'，故选B。
4. 文章说'a healthy life is the best gift for us'，故选A。
5. 文章最后说'Let's exercise every day and go to sleep early.'，故选C。

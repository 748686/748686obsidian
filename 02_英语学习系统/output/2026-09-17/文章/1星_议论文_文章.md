语法知识点

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>Imperative Sentences</strong><br>Used to give advice or commands.<br><em>例：We must exercise every day.</em><br><br><strong>Reason-Result Clause</strong><br>Explains cause and effect using 'When'.<br><em>例：When you run or jump, your body feels strong.</em><br><br><strong>Comparison Adjectives</strong><br>Uses 'different' to show variety.<br><em>例：We are all different, so some children prefer morning exercise.</em>

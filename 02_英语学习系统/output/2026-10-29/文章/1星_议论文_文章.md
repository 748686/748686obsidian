Is It Kind to Help?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:28px; text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
Some people think it is not nice to help those who do not ask. I do not agree. Yesterday, a young boy who looked sixteen sat on the windy park bench. He held a card with a song on it. I wanted to take his card. I think a small act of help can make the day better. A kind gesture is a good idea. It shows warmth. We should take small steps to be kind every day. Helping others is always a good thing.
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-top:22px; margin-bottom:18px; text-align:center;">
中文翻译
</div>

<div style="text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
有些人认为，帮助那些不主动求助的人是不友善的。我不这么认为。昨天，一个看起来十六岁的年轻男孩坐在有风的公园长椅上。他拿着一张印有歌曲的卡片。我想买他的卡片。我认为一个小小的帮助可以让这一天变得更好。友好的举动是一个好主意。它展现了温暖。我们应该每天迈出小步去表达善意。帮助他人总是好事。
</div>

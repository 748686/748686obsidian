<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
语法知识点
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>观点表达句型 I think / I believe</strong><br>用 I think 或 I believe 开头直接表达个人观点，是议论文常用句式。<br><em>例：I think exercise is very important for every child.</em><br><br><strong>时间状语从句 When + 从句</strong><br>When 引导时间状语从句，说明'当……时'发生的结果。<br><em>例：When you exercise, your body becomes strong.</em><br><br><strong>情态动词 should + 动词原形</strong><br>should 表示建议或义务，后接动词原形，语气温和。<br><em>例：we should play outside for at least one hour every day</em><br><br><strong>定语从句 who + 动词</strong><br>who 指代人，引导定语从句修饰先行词 children。<br><em>例：children who exercise feel good every single day</em>
</div>

<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
重点句型
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>I think ... is very important for ...</strong><br>我认为……对……非常重要<br><em>原文：I think exercise is very important for every child.</em><br><br><strong>When you ..., your ... becomes ...</strong><br>当你……时，你的……会变成……<br><em>原文：When you exercise, your body becomes strong.</em><br><br><strong>In my opinion, we should ...</strong><br>在我看来，我们应该……<br><em>原文：In my opinion, we should play outside for at least one hour every day.</em><br><br><strong>A + 形容词 + 名词 makes ...</strong><br>一个……的……使……<br><em>原文：A strong body makes life truly fun!</em>
</div>

<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
文章结构
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px;">

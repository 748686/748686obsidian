Why Exercise Is Good for Us
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:28px; text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
I think everyone loves being healthy. Yes, it is very important to keep our bodies strong. We can stay strong by doing simple exercise. We can run, jump, or play games. Every body is different, but all bodies need good care. If we do not sleep well, we feel tired. Healthy sleep helps us feel better the next day. Some days are beautiful, and that makes us happy to go outside and move. We love the sun and the fresh air. Exercise makes us feel great inside. It helps our hearts and our muscles. We should exercise every day. Even ten minutes is enough. A healthy life is a happy life. Let's start today and never stop. We all want to be strong and ready for the day. So yes, please try to exercise now.
</div>

</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-bottom:20px; text-align:center;">
Why a Letter to Grandma Is the Best Visit
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:28px; text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
I think a letter is better than a short visit. Why? I am thirteen and a little shy. My grandparent lives far away. I can travel for two days only. A long trip makes me tired. But a letter does not make me tired. I can write it at home. I think of her and write. I use a fun pea to test my pen. I practice writing well. I say, 'Dear Grandma, I see your smile in my mind.' A visit is nice, but a letter shows my love. It is easy and sweet.
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-top:22px; margin-bottom:18px; text-align:center;">
中文翻译
</div>

<div style="text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
我认为一封信比一次短暂的拜访更好。为什么？我今年十三岁，有点害羞。我的祖父母住得很远。我只能旅行两天。长途旅行会让我累。但写信不会让我累。我可以在家写。我会想她，然后写。我用一颗有趣的豌豆来测试我的笔。我练习写得好。我说：‘亲爱的奶奶，我在脑海中看到了你的笑容。’拜访是美好的，但一封信表达了我的爱。它简单而甜蜜。
</div>

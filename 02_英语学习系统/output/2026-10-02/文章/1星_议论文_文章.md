Writing to a Shy Grandparent
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:28px; text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
I think writing is a good way to meet a shy person. If you have a shy grandparent, try writing a letter. A Pea in a pot is a small thing, but food makes everyone well. This can be a fun idea to start a chat. Dear Grandma, I want you to see that I care. My family has thirteen pets. I will visit you on Sunday. A kind letter makes a shy heart feel safe. It is a good rule for all kids.
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-top:22px; margin-bottom:18px; text-align:center;">
中文翻译
</div>

<div style="text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
我认为写作是结识害羞的人的好方法。如果你的祖父母很害羞，试着写封信。锅里的豌豆是小事，但食物能让人身心舒畅。这是开始聊天的有趣想法。亲爱的奶奶，我想让你看到我在乎你。我家有十三只宠物。我周日会去看你。一封善意的信能让害羞的心感到安全。这是对孩子们有益的一个好准则。
</div>

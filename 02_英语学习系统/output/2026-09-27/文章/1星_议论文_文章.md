How to Stay Happy and Healthy
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:28px; text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
Hello, my friend. Do you like to be healthy? Yes, I know you do. It is very important to have a good body. How can we be strong and healthy? First, we need to sleep well. Sleep helps our body grow. Second, we should exercise. Exercise makes us strong and beautiful inside. We all love different kinds of exercise. Some like swimming, and some like running. This is good for our heart. A healthy body is very important. So, let's sleep well and exercise. Then we can be happy and strong.
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-top:22px; margin-bottom:18px; text-align:center;">
中文翻译
</div>

<div style="text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
你好，我的朋友。你喜欢健康吗？是的，我知道你喜欢。拥有一副好身体非常重要。我们怎样才能变得强壮和健康呢？首先，我们需要睡好觉。睡眠有助于我们的身体成长。其次，我们应该锻炼。锻炼让我们内心强壮而美丽。我们都喜欢不同种类的锻炼。有些人喜欢游泳，有些人喜欢跑步。这对我们的心脏有好处。健康的身体非常重要。因此，让我们睡好觉并锻炼身体。这样我们就能快乐且强壮。
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-bottom:20px; text-align:center;">
Staying Healthy and Happy
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:28px; text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
Everyone has different ways to stay healthy. I think it is important to exercise every day. When you exercise, your body becomes strong and healthy. For example, you can run or play sports. This makes your heart beat faster. You also need enough sleep at night. A beautiful morning starts with good rest. Doing different things helps you grow up well.
</div>

<div style="font-size:1.25em; font-weight:700; line-height:1.5; margin-top:22px; margin-bottom:18px; text-align:center;">
中文翻译
</div>

<div style="text-align:left; white-space:pre-wrap;">
每个人都有不同的保持健康的方式。我认为每天锻炼很重要。当你锻炼时，你的身体会变得强壮和健康。例如，你可以跑步或做运动。这会让你的心跳加速。你晚上还需要充足的睡眠。美丽的早晨从良好的休息开始。做不同的事情能帮助你健康成长。
</div>

语法知识点

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>It is + adj. + to do</strong><br>用it作形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式。<br><em>例：It is important to stay healthy.</em><br><br><strong>Modal verb should</strong><br>表示建议或责任。<br><em>例：We should exercise every day.</em><br><br><strong>Verb + object</strong><br>动词后接宾语构成动宾结构。<br><em>例：Eating vegetables is beautiful for our body.</em>
</div>

语法知识点
</div>

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
<strong>It is + adj. + to do</strong><br>用it作形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式。<br><em>例：It is important to have healthy habits.</em><br><br><strong>Subject + verb + object</strong><br>简单的主谓宾结构。<br><em>例：Exercise makes our bodies strong.</em><br><br><strong>Modal verb 'need'</strong><br>表示需求或必须。<br><em>例：We need enough sleep every night.</em>
</div>

<div style="font-weight:700; margin-top:12px;">
重点句型

<div style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:18px;">
